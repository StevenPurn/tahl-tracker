# Ice Breakers Keep the Streak Alive in a Wild 6-5 Barn Burner

**By: Your Hometown Rink-Side Reporter**
**Date: May 17, 2026**

If you didn’t have a heart rate monitor strapped to your chest at the arena tonight, you missed out on one heck of a workout. In a game that saw more momentum swings than a playground seesaw, the Ice Breakers managed to hold off a desperate Mafia squad in a 6-5 thriller that had the fans on their feet until the final buzzer echoed through the rafters.

The Ice Breakers entered the night sitting pretty at 4 points, while The Mafia were looking to shake off the goose egg in the standings. What we got was an absolute defensive scramble that kept the goal judges busier than a one-armed wallpaper hanger.

### Period 1: The Ice is Broken
The Breakers came out of the tunnel with their skates sharpened and their sights set high. Daniel Reyes wasted no time, tickling the twine at 4:55 to draw first blood. The Mafia, perhaps feeling the pressure of their winless start, got a little handsy, leading to a tripping call on Cameron Green. But just as the Breakers looked to capitalize on the man advantage, Daron Ross snatched a shorthanded gift at 9:06 to silence the home crowd. Another hook by Brian Maher shortly after kept the special teams units working overtime, but we headed into the intermission knotted up.

### Period 2: Total Chaos
If the first period was a warm-up, the second was a full-blown hockey circus. The Mafia came out with fire, as Daron Ross buried his second of the night, followed quickly by a Joshua Hendrickson snipe that made it look like The Mafia might finally flip the script.

But you can’t keep a team like the Ice Breakers down for long. Joshua Pula decided it was time to put on a show, igniting a flurry of offense. Pula started the rally, followed by a beautiful connection between Justice Dick and Luke Morley, and another tally from Pula himself. The Breakers flipped the deficit into a lead in what felt like the blink of an eye, sending us into the final frame with the momentum shifted entirely to the home side.

### Period 3: Hanging On by a Thread
The third period began with the Breakers looking to ice the cake. Joshua Pula capped off an incredible performance by completing a hat trick, set up by Morley and Reyes, before Connor Unruh added what seemed to be the dagger at the 1:10 mark.

The Mafia, however, refused to pack their bags. Brett Lamb found the back of the net to give his team a glimmer of hope, and when Hendrickson notched his second of the night at the 5:01 mark, the Breakers’ bench suddenly looked a lot more nervous. The final minutes were pure "traffic in front of the net" chaos, with bodies flying and pucks hitting shin pads, but the Ice Breakers’ backline held firm to secure the 6-5 victory.

### The Big Picture
With this win, the Ice Breakers march forward with 6 points, proving they have the grit to grind out a win even when the defensive zone turns into a pinball machine. For The Mafia, it’s a tough pill to swallow. They showed incredible heart coming back late, but they remain at 0 points in the standings. 

Tonight proved one thing: in this league, you’re only as safe as your lead, and tonight, the lead was thinner than a fresh sheet of Zamboni ice. Catch you at the next one!
