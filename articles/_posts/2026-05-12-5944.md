# Goat-a-Palooza: Killer Goats Pummel Badgers in 10-4 Rout

**By: "Slap-Shot" Sammy, Hometown Beat Reporter**
**Date: May 12, 2026**

If you were looking for a quiet Tuesday night at the rink, you picked the wrong zip code. Last night’s showdown between the Honey Badgers and the Killer Goats was less of a hockey game and more of a clinic on how to turn the ice into a personal playground. When the final horn mercifully sounded, the scoreboard read a staggering **10-4 in favor of the Killer Goats**.

It was a barn burner from the opening puck drop, but the Badgers quickly found out that when you stare down a Goat, you’re bound to get the horns.

### Period 1: Trading Punches
The game started at a blistering pace. Aaron Quinton got the party started for the Goats at 13:42, finding the back of the net off a silky feed from Michael Doyle. The Badgers, looking to show some grit, answered back at the 4:00 mark when Adam Carter converted a pass from Drew Sleezer to knot it up.

But the Goats weren't interested in a tight affair. Michael Doyle decided he was done setting the table and served up a solo dish, putting the Goats back on top 2-1 before the period break.

### Period 2: The Floodgates Open
If the first period was a warm-up, the second was a deluge. The Goats came out of the locker room with their skates laced with rocket fuel. Aaron Quinton added another one just seconds into the frame, and from there, it was a highlight-reel parade.

The Badgers tried to find a spark, but the Goats’ transition game was surgical. Every time the Badgers tried to generate traffic in front of the net, they were pinned against the boards. Even with a penalty kill attempt following a Drew Sleezer hooking minor, the Badgers couldn't stop the bleeding. Dustin Giebel, Lucas Blanchard, Erik Chillingworth, and Dylan Criquet Danielson all found their way onto the scoresheet. 

The Badgers did manage to scrape together a few goals—including a crafty finish from Jonathan Houde—but by the end of the second, the Goats had effectively put this one on ice, leading 9-3.

### Period 3: Closing the Barn Door
The third period was a bit of a victory lap for the visitors. Samuel Michaud added the final exclamation point for the Goats at 3:37, burying a beauty top-shelf where mom keeps the cookies. Jacob Whalen grabbed a late consolation goal for the Badgers at 8:17 off a nice setup from Houde, but the writing had been on the wall for twenty minutes. 

When the dust settled, the Killer Goats had cemented a dominant 10-4 victory.

### The Aftermath
For the Killer Goats, this is a statement win. They improve their standing to 4 points on the young season, proving that their offense is firing on all cylinders. Michael Doyle and Aaron Quinton were the undisputed stars of the night, orchestrating a masterclass in puck distribution.

As for the Honey Badgers, they’ll be looking to hit the reset button. Stuck at 0 points, they’ll need to tighten up their defensive zone coverage and find a way to keep the puck out of their own crease if they want to turn this season around. 

The Badgers have plenty of heart, but tonight, the Goats were simply the Greatest Of All Time. We’ll see if the Badgers can regroup before their next trip to the ice. Stay tuned, folks—it’s going to be a long season!
