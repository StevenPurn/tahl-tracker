# RACHAL’S RAMPAGE: Dirty Birds Soar Past Wolfpack in Seven-Goal Thriller

**By: Your Hometown Rink-Side Reporter**  
**Date: May 17, 2026**

If you weren’t at the arena tonight, you missed a classic. The ice was smoking, the tension was thick enough to cut with a skate blade, and by the time the final buzzer echoed through the rafters, the scoreboard read: **Dirty Birds 7, One Man Wolfpack 5.**

It was a wild, high-octane affair that served as a rude awakening for a Wolfpack squad still looking for their first point of the season. For the Dirty Birds, it was a display of offensive chemistry that would make a chemist blush.

### Period 1: The Rachal Express Leaves the Station
The Dirty Birds wasted absolutely no time setting the tempo. Just 10:30 into the opening frame, Nicklas Clark found the back of the net off a sweet feed from Joel Thorson and Gabriel Kierath. That goal seemed to act like a shot of adrenaline for Kyle Rachal. 

Rachal decided he was going to take over the night, potting two goals in quick succession (1:02 and 2:02 left in the period). By the end of the first, the Wolfpack looked like they were trying to bail out a sinking ship with a teaspoon, while the Birds were flying high with a 3-0 cushion. Physical play flared up late, with penalties to Steven Ramos and Todd Thompson proving that there was no love lost between these two.

### Period 2: Momentum in the Mud
The middle frame saw the Dirty Birds extend their lead early when Gabriel Kierath tucked one home, assisted by the ever-dangerous Rachal. Down by four, you could see the Wolfpack digging in, trying to find some grit. They finally broke their drought at the 9:42 mark when Brandt Mueller took matters into his own hands—literally—with an unassisted effort that finally gave the Wolfpack faithful something to cheer about. 

Despite the goal, the Wolfpack struggled with discipline, forcing themselves to kill off penalties to Jared Zellers and John Digges. It was a grind-it-out period that left the Wolfpack trailing 4-1 heading into the final frame.

### Period 3: The Scoring Carousel
If you went to the concession stand to grab a hot dog, you missed the wildest twenty minutes of hockey we’ve seen all season. 

Kyle Rachal continued his absolute masterclass at 11:12, but the Wolfpack refused to go quietly into the night. They ignited a late-game fire, with Jared Zellers and Brandt Mueller scoring back-to-back within a minute of each other. The energy in the building shifted—suddenly, the Wolfpack smelled blood in the water. 

However, Rachal wasn’t done. Just as the Wolfpack momentum reached a fever pitch, Rachal answered back (2:13) to restore order. The teams traded blows in a frenetic flurry—Michael Bergstrom and James Whitacre clawed two more back for the Wolfpack—but Rachal put the final nail in the coffin at the 6:46 mark, effectively silencing the comeback bid.

### The Aftermath
When the dust settled, Kyle Rachal had put on a legendary performance, and the Dirty Birds walked away with their second victory of the season, moving to 4 points in the standings. 

For the One Man Wolfpack, the 0-point hole in the standings is deepening, and they’ll need to tighten up the defensive zone if they want to avoid another long night like this. But if tonight showed us anything, it’s that this team has a high-scoring gear once they find their flow.

The Birds fly home with the W, but keep an eye on the Wolfpack—they’re hungry, and they’re looking for their first win of the season. We’ll see you at the rink!
