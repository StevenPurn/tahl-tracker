# Lodge Hangs On in Wild Sunday Morning Barn Burner, Edge Laser 5-4

**By: "Rinkside" Rick Henderson, Lead Beat Writer**

**MAY 10, 2026** — If you’re a fan of high-octane hockey and early-morning adrenaline, you should have been at the rink this morning. The ice was still fresh, but the action was scorching as The Lodge narrowly edged out Laser in a 5-4 thriller that had a little bit of everything: defensive lapses, desperate rallies, and enough heart-stopping moments to keep the coffee drinkers wide awake.

### Period 1: Feeling Out the Frost
The game began with a bit of a stalemate, as both teams traded heavy checks and navigated the neutral zone like they were moving through molasses. The only real drama of the opening frame came at the 14:25 mark when Laser’s Timothy N. See found himself in the sin bin for hooking. The Lodge looked to capitalize, but Laser’s penalty killers held firm, keeping the score knotted at zeroes heading into the first intermission.

### Period 2: The Goal-Scoring Carousel
If the first period was a slow burn, the second was a forest fire. 

The Lodge drew first blood at 12:50 when Jason Bahr buried a feed from Mark Stacy. But that goal seemed to wake a sleeping giant. Laser roared back with a vengeance, rattling off three unanswered goals in a dizzying sequence. Ronald Herwig got the party started at 4:08, and just 15 seconds later, Ben Castagna hammered home a helper from Robert Verity to snatch the lead. Castagna wasn't done, adding his second of the period at 6:58 to put Laser up 3-1.

Just when it looked like Laser might skate away with it, The Lodge showed their grit. Braden Trinka answered back almost immediately (7:11), and Jered Pieschke finished off a beautiful passing play from Trinka and Jonathon Anderson at 9:22 to tie things up at 3-3. 

### Period 3: The Clutch Factor
Tensions were high as we entered the final frame. The Lodge took a costly high-sticking penalty at 3:38, but they defended the zone like men possessed. 

With the clock ticking down and the tension thick enough to cut with a skate blade, Laser’s Jeremy Kuzmarov managed to find the back of the net unassisted with just 44.5 seconds left in the period, breathing new life into his squad. But the real dagger came from the man of the hour: Jason Bahr. With the clock winding down, Bahr delivered the final blow at the 6:44 mark (late-period adjusted scoring), netting his second of the day off a dish from Stacy and Dorough to seal the 5-4 victory for The Lodge.

### The Aftermath
It was a heartbreaking loss for Laser (0 pts), who fought back valiantly but couldn't keep the door shut in the final minutes. They’ll head back to the drawing board searching for their first win of the season. 

For The Lodge (4 pts), this win is a massive momentum builder. They’ve proven they can handle a back-and-forth track meet and come out on top. They’ll look to keep this winning feeling alive as they move forward in the standings, while Laser will surely be looking to tighten up the traffic in front of their own net for the next outing.

**Final Score:** The Lodge 5, Laser 4.
