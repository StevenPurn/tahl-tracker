# A Stink in the Rink: Hot Garbage Overpowers Fossil Hockey in 6-3 Thriller

**By: "Rusty" Blades, Hometown Hockey Beat Writer**
*May 10, 2026*

If you like your hockey served with a side of high-octane hustle and a dash of unpredictability, the 2:00 PM matinee at the local barn was exactly the prescription. In a matchup that pitted the winless Fossil Hockey against the seasoned squad from Hot Garbage, it was the "Garbage" men (and women) who showed they know how to take out the competition, skating away with a decisive 6-3 victory.

### Period 1: The Garbage Men Set the Pace
From the drop of the puck, Hot Garbage looked like a team that had finished their morning coffee while Fossil was still trying to find their skates. Aaron Whisner set the tone early, buzzing around the Fossil net and putting one home at the 12:43 mark to draw first blood. 

Fossil Hockey tried to settle into a rhythm, but the chemistry for Hot Garbage was undeniable. Just minutes later, Nicole Baker tapped home a beautiful feed from Whisner and Alan Dyke. Fossil’s Bryan Simms found himself cooling his heels in the sin bin for a hooking minor at 8:36, but the Fossil defense held firm, ensuring they didn't go into the intermission staring at a deeper hole.

### Period 2: Digging Out
The second frame saw the intensity ramp up. Nicholas Brewer extended the Hot Garbage lead to 3-0 at the 6:07 mark, finishing off a slick pass from Baker. It looked like the game might turn into a blowout, but Fossil Hockey wasn’t ready to become fossils just yet. 

Zach Cox finally broke the seal for the home side at 7:32, jamming in a rebound off assists from Daron Ross and Michael Hoey. It was a gritty, "blue-collar" goal—the kind that gives a team hope that the momentum is finally swinging their way.

### Period 3: The Scoring Carousel
The final period was nothing short of a barn burner. Both teams abandoned the trap and opened up the ice. Joshua Hall struck quickly for Hot Garbage at 1:07, but Fossil’s Ethan Hilton answered back at 4:38 off a smart dish from Dave Griffiths to keep the pressure on.

Then, things got chaotic. In a span of barely twenty seconds, the red light went off three times. Alan Dyke restored the multi-goal cushion for Hot Garbage at 5:00, and Hall tacked on his second of the game at 5:11 to put the game effectively out of reach. Daron Ross brought one back for Fossil at 5:22, a valiant effort that went top shelf where mom keeps the cookies, but the damage was already done.

Tempers flared slightly late in the third when Ethan Peterson (Fossil) and Joshua Hall (Hot Garbage) were both sent to the box for tripping, but the whistle-heavy end didn't change the destiny of the scoreboard.

### The Takeaway
With the final horn, Hot Garbage moves to a solid 4 points in the standings, proving they have the depth and the offensive engine to contend this season. For Fossil Hockey, the 0-point hole is starting to look a bit daunting, but there’s silver lining to be found in the grit shown by Ross and Cox late in the game. 

Fossil needs to find a way to stop the bleeding in their own end, while Hot Garbage just needs to keep doing what they’re doing—because right now, they’re playing anything but trash. 

**Final Score: Hot Garbage 6, Fossil Hockey 3**
