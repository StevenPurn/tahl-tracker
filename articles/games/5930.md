# Hot Garbage Cleans Up: Fossil Hockey Stumbles in Sunday Matinee

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
**May 10, 2026**

If there’s one thing we know about hockey, it’s that the ice doesn’t care about your resume—it only cares about who puts the biscuit in the basket. Sunday afternoon at the arena, the atmosphere was thick enough to cut with a skate blade as Fossil Hockey looked to shake off the early-season rust against the boys and girls in the Hot Garbage sweaters. 

When the final horn sounded, it was clear: Hot Garbage came to play, and they left with a definitive 6-3 victory that sends them climbing the standings.

### First Period: The Garbage Grinders Set the Tone
The opening frame was a masterclass in establishing dominance. Hot Garbage didn’t wait for the ice to settle, with **Aaron Whisner** finding the back of the net unassisted at the 12:43 mark. It was a wake-up call for Fossil, but before they could answer, **Nicole Baker** capitalized on a gorgeous tic-tac-toe sequence with Whisner and Alan Dyke to extend the lead. 

Fossil Hockey tried to find some grit, but Bryan Simms’ trip to the sin bin for hooking at 8:36 forced them to spend more time defending than dictating, leaving them chasing the game for the remainder of the period.

### Second Period: Trading Sparks
The middle frame saw the pace kick up a notch. **Nicholas Brewer** pushed the lead to three for Hot Garbage, burying a helper from Baker that looked like it had "top shelf where mom keeps the cookies" written all over it. 

Fossil finally found a pulse midway through the period. **Zach Cox** hammered one home, set up by Daron Ross and Michael Hoey, providing a glimmer of hope. It was a gritty goal, the kind that happens when you create traffic in front of the net and wait for the garbage to spill out. The Fossil bench erupted, believing they had finally found the spark to ignite a comeback.

### Third Period: The Scoring Carousel
If the first two periods were a chess match, the third was a chaotic, beautiful barroom brawl of hockey. 

**Joshua Hall** opened the third with a strike, but Fossil refused to go quietly, with **Ethan Hilton** answering back at 4:38. However, the next 60 seconds of game time were pure insanity. Hot Garbage turned on the afterburners, netting two quick ones from Alan Dyke and a second from Joshua Hall to put the game effectively on ice. 

**Daron Ross** managed to snag one more for Fossil late, but the mountain was simply too high to climb. The late-game antics culminated in a double-minor sequence at 7:48, with Ethan Peterson and Joshua Hall both heading to the box for tripping, but the damage was already done.

### The Takeaway
With this 6-3 result, Hot Garbage moves to 4 points on the season, proving they aren't just here to take up space—they’re here to make a mess of their opponents' plans. Fossil Hockey remains at 0 points, and while they showed flashes of chemistry in the third period, they’ll need to tighten up their defensive zone coverage before their next outing.

It’s early, the ice is still fresh, and as any vet will tell you: there’s plenty of hockey left to play. But for today? Tip your caps to Hot Garbage—they certainly cleaned up.
