# A Hat Trick and a Heatwave: The Rookery Absolutely Tusk-led at Home!

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: May 17, 2026**

If you didn’t make it down to the barn tonight, you missed a masterclass in puck possession. The Rookery turned the ice into their own personal playground, hanging a decisive 7-1 drubbing on the Tulsa Tuskens in a game that felt like a statement of intent from the opening faceoff.

After dropping their last outing, The Rookery came out hungry. They didn't just want the win; they wanted to make the Tuskens pay for every inch of ice.

### First Period: The Caldron Effect
James Caldron decided early on that he was going to be the main character tonight. At the 12:41 mark, he kicked off the scoring with a solo effort that set the tone. He wasn’t done there, either. With 2:23 remaining in the frame, he doubled down, burying a beautiful feed from Donnie Buehrig to put The Rookery up by two.

The Tuskens did manage to stop the bleeding briefly when Dylan Walker found the back of the net at the 7:10 mark, but that was about the only time Tulsa had any reason to celebrate. The Rookery took a 2-1 lead into the intermission, looking sharp and hungry for more.

### Second Period: Turning the Screws
Coming out for the second, the energy in the building was electric. Chris Olsen—who would go on to have a monster night—found twine at 11:35, set up by some gorgeous tic-tac-toe passing from Christopher Guffey and Chad Olson. 

The Rookery’s depth was on full display when Gage Walker hammered home a helper from Glenn Prouse with just 1:30 left in the second. While Glenn Prouse found himself cooling his heels in the box for a tripping minor, the Tuskens couldn't find a way to capitalize. Garrett Caraway tried to get physical for Tulsa, picking up his own tripping penalty, but the Tuskens just couldn't find a rhythm against a stifling Rookery defense. 

### Third Period: The Floodgates Open
If the second period was a warning, the third was an absolute deluge. James Caldron completed his hat trick at 11:20, capitalizing on a crisp setup from Chris Olsen and Tyler Waller—a goal so pretty it belonged in a museum. 

Christopher Guffey joined the party at 1:06, and Chris Olsen notched his second of the night at 9:29 off another Tyler Waller assist. By the time Cody Springfield caught a roughing minor for the Tuskens in the final minute, the game had long since been put to bed. 

### The Bottom Line
Tonight wasn't just a win; it was a demolition. James Caldron’s four-goal masterpiece serves as a wake-up call to the rest of the league. With this win, The Rookery moves to 4 points on the season, establishing a solid foundation to build upon as they shake off that recent slump. 

As for the Tulsa Tuskens, they remain anchored at the bottom of the standings with 0 points. They’ll need to head back to the drawing board and find a way to tighten up the defense before their next tilt.

For the faithful in the stands tonight, there’s nothing quite like seeing your squad fire on all cylinders. See you at the next one—and don't forget to bundle up, it’s cold near the glass!
