# A Hat-Trick of Magic: The Rookery Demolish Tuskens in 7-1 Rout

**By: "Slapshot" Sally, Beat Reporter**
*May 17, 2026*

If you weren’t at the arena tonight, I’m sorry to tell you that you missed a masterclass in ice dominance. The Rookery walked into the building looking to shake off that recent loss, and boy, did they shake it off—they practically turned the Tulsa Tuskens into a dusting of snow. Final score: 7-1. It wasn’t just a win; it was a statement.

### Period 1: The Caldron Effect
The ice was barely frozen before James Caldron decided he was going to be the main character tonight. At the 12:41 mark, he snatched the puck and went coast-to-coast, burying it to put The Rookery on the board. 

Tulsa tried to find their footing, but they were skating into a headwind. With 2:23 remaining in the first, Donnie Buehrig fed a beautiful saucer pass to Caldron, who tucked it away for his second of the night. The Tuskens managed a pulse at the 7:10 mark when Dylan Walker snuck one past our defense, but that felt more like a "pity goal" than a momentum shift. 

### Period 2: Putting the Foot Down
Coming out of the locker room, The Rookery smelled blood in the water. We saw some serious traffic in front of the net, and Chris Olsen capitalized at 11:35, redirecting a dish from Guffey and Chad Olson. 

The highlight of the middle frame? Gage Walker at 1:30. Taking a feed from Glenn Prouse, Walker hammered a wrister that went top shelf where mom keeps the cookies. Even with a few trips to the sin bin—Prouse and Caraway both cooling their heels for tripping—The Rookery’s PK unit looked like a brick wall.

### Period 3: The Rout is On
Up 4-1 heading into the final twenty, you might have expected The Rookery to sit back and play prevent defense. Instead, they hit the gas. 

James Caldron completed his hat trick at 11:20, showing off the kind of hands that make goalies lose sleep. Christopher Guffey added a solo effort at 1:06, and just when the Tuskens thought they could limp to the final whistle, Chris Olsen lit the lamp again at 9:29 off a beautiful setup by Tyler Waller. By the time Cody Springfield caught a roughing penalty in the waning seconds, the Tuskens looked like they were ready to call an Uber back to Tulsa.

### The Big Picture
Let’s look at the numbers. The Rookery moves to 4 points in the standings, firmly establishing themselves as a force to be reckoned with after that midweek stumble. They played a disciplined game, kept the pressure high, and proved that their offense can fire on all cylinders when they commit to the forecheck.

As for the Tuskens? They drop to 0 points and have some serious soul-searching to do. They were out-skated, out-muscled, and out-classed for sixty minutes. 

It was a real barn burner of a night, folks. The Rookery looks like they’ve found their groove, and if they keep skating like this, the rest of the league better lace up tight—because these guys are coming for the top spot.
