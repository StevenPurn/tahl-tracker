# A Wild One in the Wake: Riveters Outlast Valkyries in 12-Goal Thriller

**By: "Slap-Shot" Sammy, Hometown Beat Reporter**
*May 17, 2026*

If you like your hockey served with a side of chaotic energy and a dash of “anything goes,” you should have been at the rink this morning. The ice was still fogging up at 8:45 AM, but the Valkyries and the Tulsa Riveters didn't need a double espresso to get the blood pumping. By the time the final buzzer sounded on this 5-7 barn burner, the scoreboard operator had likely filed for overtime pay.

### Period 1: The Valkyries Take the Reins
The Valkyries came out looking like they’d had their morning Wheaties, skating with a hunger that defied their goose-egg in the standings. **Jessica Hopwood** drew first blood at the 10:16 mark, dancing through the neutral zone before uncorking a wrister that beat the keeper clean. When **Makenzie Dillard** hammered one home at 4:17, the Valkyries held a 2-0 lead and were looking like the team to beat.

However, the Riveters aren’t a squad that stays down for long. **Ivy Emerson** reminded everyone why Tulsa is a threat, burying a feed from Stacey Burgess at 7:42 to cut the deficit and steal a bit of the Valkyries' early momentum.

### Period 2: The Momentum Pendulum
The second frame was a tactical tug-of-war. **Kalea Maxwell** put the Valkyries back up by two early on, finding a soft spot in the defense and burying it. But just when it looked like the Valkyries might pull away, the Riveters’ engine room kicked into high gear. **Gabrielle Gibson** started her personal highlight reel at the 14:33 mark, and just as the period was gasping its final breaths, **Hannah Atkins** cashed in a helper from Taylor Soucy with a mere 22.4 seconds left on the clock. Suddenly, a 3-1 game was a 3-3 deadlock, and the atmosphere in the arena was electric enough to light up the city grid.

### Period 3: The Gibson-Long Show
If the second period was a tight contest, the third was pure, unadulterated madness. The Riveters emerged from the locker room with fire in their eyes. **Gabrielle Gibson** and **Amanda Long** began a dizzying two-man game that resulted in back-to-back goals in just thirty seconds. 

The Valkyries refused to go quietly into the morning air. **Makenzie Dillard**—who deserves a game ball for her persistence—sniped one home at 12:47, but Gibson answered back at 1:27 for her hat-trick goal. Dillard completed her own hat trick at 5:52, pulling the Valkyries within striking distance, but Amanda Long put the final nail in the coffin at 6:43, capitalizing on a crisp passing play from Burgess and Atkins to cement the 7-5 victory for the visitors.

### The Morning After
Look, a loss is a loss, but for the Valkyries, there’s a lot to like here. Dillard’s offensive outburst proves that they have the firepower to keep up with the league's best. However, defensively, they’ll need to do a better job clearing the traffic in front of the net if they want to climb out of that zero-point hole.

For the Tulsa Riveters, this is a massive statement. They climb to 2 points, proving that even when they’re down, they have the grit to turn a morning skate into a celebration. They handled the pressure, weathered the Valkyries' early storm, and played the kind of "cookie-jar" hockey that wins championships. 

It’s early in the season, folks, but if this morning’s game is any indication of where we’re headed, grab your seat early. You don't want to miss a second of this action.
