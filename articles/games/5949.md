# Whitacre’s Wild Night: Jackalopes Hop All Over the Knights in 5-0 Shutout

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
*May 18, 2026*

If you were looking for a high-octane offensive clinic in the wee hours of Monday morning, you certainly found it. In a game that served as a rude awakening for the home crowd, the Jackalopes rolled into the arena and completely dismantled the Knights, leaving the scoreboard reading a lopsided 5-0 in favor of the visitors.

### First Period: A Quick Jab
The Jackalopes didn’t bother with a feeling-out process. Barely two minutes into the contest (2:03), James Whitacre signaled his intentions early. With a crisp feed from Donald Schleicher, Whitacre buried the biscuit, setting a tone that the Knights would never quite overcome. 

The Knights looked to capitalize when Schleicher found himself in the sin bin for a holding minor at 9:08, but the power play was as stagnant as a mid-July pond. The Knights couldn't find any traffic in front of the net, and the Jackalopes’ PK unit swatted away every attempt to break their perimeter.

### Second Period: The Whitacre Show
If the first period was a warning shot, the second was a full-blown cannonade. James Whitacre continued his masterclass, doubling the lead at 12:04—once again off a beautiful setup from Schleicher. 

Just over two minutes later, at 14:39, Whitacre decided he didn’t even need an assist to find the twine. He put on a dazzling display of individual skill, deking through the neutral zone and snapping one top-shelf where mom keeps the cookies. It was a demoralizing blow that effectively sucked the air out of the building. Even when the Jackalopes tripped over their own skates—taking a "too many men" penalty at 3:06—the Knights looked more interested in staring at the rafters than finding the back of the net.

### Third Period: Closing the Vault
Trailing by three, the Knights came out with a desperate energy in the third, but the Jackalopes’ defense was playing with the grit of a team trying to protect a lead in the seventh game of a series. 

Donald Schleicher, tired of just playing the role of setup man, decided to join the scoring party himself at 13:14, hammering home an unassisted beauty to make it 4-0. The final nail in the coffin came at 8:42, when Elias Elias finished a textbook passing play from Tyler Wick and Christopher King. 

The Knights managed to land themselves in the box late when Toby Temple was whistled for tripping, but at that point, the outcome was academic. The Jackalopes skated out of town with a dominant 5-0 victory and a clean sheet for their netminder.

### The Morning After
For the Jackalopes (2 pts), this win is a massive morale booster after their recent skid. They moved the puck with purpose and capitalized on every defensive lapse the Knights offered. 

As for the Knights (0 pts), it’s back to the drawing board. Being held scoreless is a bitter pill to swallow, and the coaching staff will certainly be looking at the lack of offensive chemistry. If they want to climb out of the basement of the standings, they’ll need to find a way to generate more traffic in the slot and stop chasing the play.

The ice might have cooled down by 12:45 AM, but the message from the Jackalopes was loud and clear: they aren't here to just participate—they're here to run the table.
