# The Abominable Onslaught: Yetis Bury Jackalopes in 7-2 Rout

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
*May 13, 2026*

If you were looking for a quiet Tuesday night at the arena, you picked the wrong zip code. Last night’s showdown between our hometown Yetis and the visiting Jackalopes was less of a hockey game and more of a total demolition. When the final horn sounded, the scoreboard read 7-2 in favor of the home team, and let me tell you—the ice was absolutely buzzing.

### Period 1: The Yetis Come Out Frosty
The Yetis didn’t just wake up; they came out swinging like they had a point to prove. The tone was set early at the 15:50 mark when Denis Blaquiere hammered one home, set up beautifully by Jason Fennell and Skylar Miller. 

Before the Jackalopes could even catch their breath, Miller added a goal of his own at 1:15, and Jason Fennell joined the party at the 55-second mark. It was an offensive clinic, moving the puck with the precision of a Swiss watch. The Jackalopes finally broke the silence at 7:27 thanks to Kyle Foster, but the damage was already done. The Yetis headed to the locker room with all the momentum in the world and a clear lead.

### Period 2: Fennell’s Finesse
If the first period was a barrage, the second was a surgical strike. Jason Fennell continued his masterclass, burying his second of the night at the 7-second mark—yes, you read that right, he didn't waste any time. 

Brendan Burkman got in on the action at 12:39 to keep the pressure cooker turned up to high. We saw a bit of grit when Robert Verity headed to the sin bin for cross-checking at 3:50, but the Jackalopes managed to kill it off. Unfortunately for them, they couldn't stop the inevitable; Fennell completed his scoring spree at 7:58, finding the back of the net to put the game firmly out of reach.

### Period 3: Closing the Curtain
The final frame saw the pace settle, but the Yetis remained in the driver’s seat. Lauchlin Cook served two minutes for hooking, which opened the door for Kyle Verity to snag a consolation goal for the Jackalopes at 2:29, but it was too little, too late. 

Brendan Burkman put an exclamation point on the evening with his second goal of the night at 5:20, courtesy of a slick feed from Jason Bahr and the ubiquitous Jason Fennell. 

### The Bottom Line
When you look at the stats, this was the Jason Fennell show—he was everywhere, acting as the primary architect for this 7-2 shellacking. For the Yetis, this win is a massive confidence booster, moving them to 2 points and establishing a dominant early-season footprint. 

As for the Jackalopes, they’re heading back to the drawing board with 0 points and a lot of questions to answer. The Jackalopes looked a step behind all night, constantly battling traffic in front of their own net. 

The Yetis proved tonight that when they’re clicking, they’re top-shelf material—and I don’t just mean where mom keeps the cookies. Keep your eyes on this squad, folks. If they play like this all season, we’re in for one heck of a ride.
