# Ice Breakers Edge Out The Mafia in a Sunday Night Barn Burner

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
*May 17, 2026*

There’s nothing like the smell of fresh ice and desperation on a Sunday night. At 8:45 PM, the Ice Breakers and The Mafia collided in a contest that saw enough momentum shifts to give a Zamboni driver whiplash. When the final horn sounded, the Ice Breakers walked away with a 6-5 victory, keeping their perfect start alive and leaving The Mafia searching for their first spark of the season.

### First Period: A Tactical Tug-of-War
The Breakers drew first blood at the 4:55 mark when **Daniel Reyes** danced through the neutral zone, unassisted, and buried one to set the tone. However, The Mafia wasn't about to roll over. Despite a flurry of penalties—including a pair of hooking calls against Cameron Green and Brian Maher—The Mafia found their footing. **Daron Ross** silenced the crowd at 9:06, shorthanded, proving that even when the chips are down, the "family" business is still very much in session.

### Second Period: The Pula Party
If the first period was a tactical chess match, the second was a back-alley brawl on blades. The Mafia stormed out of the gates, with Ross netting his second of the night and **Joshua Hendrickson** finding the twine shortly after to put The Mafia up 3-1. 

But just as the Ice Breakers looked like they were slipping on a banana peel, **Joshua Pula** and **Luke Morley** decided they’d had enough. Pula ignited the comeback at 14:20, and Morley followed up quickly to narrow the gap. By the time Pula struck again at 6:16, the momentum had shifted entirely to the Breakers' bench. The pace was electric, the hits were heavy, and the traffic in front of the net was thick enough to park a truck in.

### Third Period: Holding the Line
Entering the final frame, it was anyone’s game. The Ice Breakers showed the poise of a team that already had a win in the bank. Pula completed his massive night by lighting the lamp at 12:40 for the hat trick, and **Connor Unruh** followed it up with a beautiful finish off a feed from Tobias Roeding to make it 6-3.

The Mafia, true to their name, showed a gritty resolve in the final minutes. **Brett Lamb** and **Joshua Hendrickson** both beat the netminder to trim the lead to a single goal, turning the final minutes into a high-stakes scramble. But the Ice Breakers clamped down on defense, effectively "parking the bus" to survive the late-game surge.

### The Aftermath
For the Ice Breakers (4 pts), this win is a statement. They’ve proven they can handle a punch to the gut and come back swinging. They currently sit comfortably at the top of the standings, maintaining their momentum with back-to-back victories.

For The Mafia, it’s a tough pill to swallow. Moving to 0-4, the goose egg in the win column is starting to feel heavy. However, if their offensive production in the final frame is any indication, they have the firepower to turn the tide. They just need to tighten up the defensive zone before the next drop of the puck.

The rink was loud, the competition was fierce, and if this is a sign of what’s to come this season, keep your helmets strapped on tight—we’re in for a wild ride.
