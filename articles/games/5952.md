# BONE ZONE SURVIVES THE MOOSEKNUCKLES ONSLAUGHT IN 6-5 THRILLER

**By: Hockey Beat Reporter**
**Date: May 17, 2026**

If you didn’t have a heart monitor strapped to your chest at the rink this afternoon, you might want to look into one. In a game that featured more momentum swings than a playground teeter-totter, The Bone Zone narrowly escaped a ferocious Mooseknuckles rally to secure a 6-5 victory in what can only be described as a bona fide barn burner.

### First Period: The Wimpee Show
The Bone Zone wasted no time setting the tempo. Tyler Wimpee came out flying, lighting the lamp early at the 12:46 mark to draw first blood. However, the man was clearly playing on the edge; just a few minutes later, he headed to the sin bin for an unsportsmanlike conduct penalty. 

The Mooseknuckles, desperate to put points on the board, finally cracked the code at 6:55 when Thomas Howe beat the keeper to breathe some life into the visitors. But The Bone Zone wasn’t about to let the momentum shift for long. Bryce Perkins buried a beautiful feed from Wimpee and Eric Roepke at 1:16, followed shortly by Braden Trinka, who cleaned up the trash off a Timothy N. See helper to send The Bone Zone into the intermission with a healthy cushion.

### Second Period: Penalties and Precision
The second frame was a masterclass in organized chaos. A "Too Many Men" penalty on James DePriest threatened to open the floodgates for the Mooseknuckles, but The Bone Zone’s penalty kill stood tall. 

Once back at full strength, Tyler Wimpee decided he wasn’t done, finding the back of the net at 11:58 off a crisp sequence from Perkins and See. Jeffrey Kleehammer managed to sneak one past for the 'Knuckles at the 5:09 mark, but Lance Levasseur immediately answered back with a solo effort just 37 seconds later, reminding everyone that The Bone Zone had an answer for every question asked.

### Third Period: The Hodges Heroics
If the first two periods were a sprint, the third was a marathon of nerves. Mark Hodges decided he was going to single-handedly put the Mooseknuckles on his back, orchestrating an offensive explosion. He found the twine twice in the span of five seconds at the 12:47 and 12:52 marks—a sequence that left the crowd absolutely buzzing.

Just when it looked like the game was slipping away, James Whitacre played the role of savior, rifling home a goal at 13:29 that ended up being the eventual game-winner. Hodges completed his hat trick late in the period at 8:45, bringing the Mooseknuckles within one, but The Bone Zone’s defensive shell held firm in the final minutes, sealing the 6-5 victory.

### The Aftermath
With this hard-fought result, The Bone Zone moves to a solid 2 points on the season, proving they have the grit to close out tight games even when the opposition is breathing down their necks. For the Mooseknuckles, it’s a tough pill to swallow—they sit at 0 points, but tonight’s performance showed they have the offensive firepower to hang with anyone. If Hodges can keep playing at this level, it won't be long before the 'Knuckles find themselves in the win column.

The Bone Zone lives to fight another day, but they’ll be looking to tighten up the back end before their next outing. You can’t win them all by trading haymakers, but for today, the house was rocking, the puck was moving, and The Bone Zone remains the team to beat.
