# BONE ZONE SURVIVES MOOSEKNUCKLES ONSLAUGHT IN A SUNDAY AFTERNOON BARN BURNER

**By: "Rusty" Blades, Rinkside Reporter**

**MAY 17, 2026** — If you weren’t at the arena this afternoon, you missed a classic. We saw speed, we saw grit, and we saw enough rubber flying to keep the local tire shops in business for a month. In a game that had more twists than a pretzel factory, The Bone Zone narrowly escaped with a 6-5 victory over the Mooseknuckles in what can only be described as a pure, old-fashioned, high-octane hockey circus.

### The First: Wimpee Sets the Tone
The Bone Zone didn’t wait for the ice to settle. Just over two minutes in, **Tyler Wimpee** took matters into his own hands, finding the back of the net unassisted to kick off the scoring. Though Wimpee later found himself in the sin bin for an unsportsmanlike conduct penalty, the momentum stayed with the boys in the home colors. **Bryce Perkins** buried a beautiful feed from Wimpee and Eric Roepke, and despite a gritty response from the Mooseknuckles' **Thomas Howe**, **Braden Trinka** ensured The Bone Zone headed to the locker room with a commanding lead, courtesy of a slick setup from Timothy N. See.

### The Second: A Mid-Game Marathon
The second frame was where the real chaos unfolded. After a hiccup that saw The Bone Zone whistled for "Too Many Men"—a classic case of having too many cooks in the kitchen—Wimpee struck again on a gorgeous passing play involving Perkins and See. 

The Mooseknuckles weren't going home without a fight, though. **Jeffrey Kleehammer** caught the defense napping to put the 'Knuckles on the board, but The Bone Zone’s **Lance Levasseur** answered back almost immediately with a solo effort that proved to be a real back-breaker for the visitors. By the end of the second, it felt like the game was firmly in the Bone Zone’s pocket, but as any seasoned fan knows: never count out a team with "Knuckles" in the name.

### The Third: The Hodges Heroics
The third period was the Mark Hodges show. The Mooseknuckles forward decided to turn the final frame into his own personal highlight reel. With the game slipping away, Hodges went on a heater, scoring twice in the span of five seconds early in the period, followed by another beauty late in the frame to complete the hat trick. 

However, The Bone Zone had the answer when it mattered most. **James Whitacre** managed to find the twine in the midst of the Mooseknuckles' comeback attempt, providing the crucial insurance marker that ultimately sealed the fate of this 6-5 thriller. 

### The Aftermath
When the final horn sounded, the air in the rink was thick with the scent of spent adrenaline and sharpened edges. 

For **The Bone Zone (2 pts)**, this victory proves they can handle a punch-for-punch shootout. They’ve established themselves as the team to beat in these early standings, showing resilience even when the opposition decides to turn on the afterburners.

For the **Mooseknuckles (0 pts)**, while the scoreboard wasn't in their favor, the way they clawed back in the third proves they aren't afraid to get their noses dirty. They’ll be looking to turn that offensive chemistry into a full 60-minute effort next time out.

The Bone Zone walks away with the bragging rights, but if this afternoon is any indication, the rest of the season is going to be a white-knuckle ride. Keep your sticks on the ice, folks—this league is just getting warmed up!
