# Barn Burner at the Rink: The Lodge Edges Out Laser in 5-4 Thriller

**By: "Rusty" Blades, Hometown Beat Writer**
**May 10, 2026**

If you weren’t at the arena this morning at 11:15 AM, you missed a classic. We saw end-to-end action, a flurry of goals that would make a goal judge dizzy, and enough intensity to melt the ice. In a back-and-forth affair that kept the crowd on the edge of their seats, The Lodge narrowly escaped with a 5-4 victory over Laser, proving that while Laser is hungry, The Lodge knows how to feast.

### First Period: A Defensive Chess Match
The opening frame was a physical affair, with both squads feeling each other out. The only smudge on the ledger was an early hooking call on Laser’s Timothy N. See at the 14:25 mark. Laser’s penalty kill stood tall, neutralizing the power play and sending us into the intermission scoreless. It was the calm before the storm, folks.

### Second Period: The Scoring Spree
If the first period was a chess match, the second was a backyard brawl. Jason Bahr broke the seal for The Lodge at 12:50, burying a beauty off a setup from Mark Stacy. But Laser wasn't about to go quietly into the morning air.

Ronald Herwig ignited the Laser bench at 4:08, and before the ice could even settle, Ben Castagna decided he wasn't done yet. Castagna went "top shelf where mom keeps the cookies" just 15 seconds later to give Laser the lead. When Castagna tickled the twine again at 6:58—his second of the period—it looked like Laser had all the momentum.

Not so fast. The Lodge answered with a blitz of their own. Braden Trinka found the back of the net 13 seconds later, and Jered Pieschke cleaned up some traffic in front of the net at 9:22 to knot the game up. It was a high-octane frenzy that left us breathless heading into the final frame.

### Third Period: The Drama
The tension in the building was palpable. Pieschke, fresh off his goal, found himself in the sin bin for high-sticking at 3:38. Laser couldn't capitalize on the man advantage, but they kept the pressure high. With the clock ticking down and the fans screaming for a hero, Jeremy Kuzmarov stepped up at the 44.5-second mark, unassisted, to give Laser the lead once more.

But the "never-say-die" spirit of The Lodge shone through. With 6:44 remaining, Jason Bahr played the role of the spoiler, sniping his second of the game off a dish from Stacy and Scott Dorough to seal the 5-4 victory. 

### The Bottom Line
For The Lodge, this win is massive. They improve to 4 points on the season, firmly establishing their presence near the top of the leaderboard. They showed they can handle a punch in the mouth and keep skating.

As for Laser, they move to 0 points, and while the result stings, there’s no reason to hang their heads. They traded blows with a tough squad and proved they can fill the net. If they can tighten up the defensive zone and clear the traffic in front of their crease, that first win of the season is surely right around the corner.

Stay tuned, folks. If this game is any indication, the rest of the season is going to be a wild ride.
