# Garden Variety Thriller: Average Hoes Edge Out The Rookery in 5-4 Barn Burner

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: May 10, 2026**

If you weren’t at the arena this morning at 11:45 AM, you missed a classic. We saw everything from defensive masterclasses to a third-period surge that had the glass rattling and the fans on their feet. When the final horn sounded, the Average Hoes walked away with the two points, hanging a heartbreaking 5-4 loss on The Rookery in a game that felt like a heavyweight title fight disguised as a weekend skate.

### Period 1: The Hoes Set the Tone
The Average Hoes came out of the gate looking like they’d had an extra shot of espresso in their pre-game coffee. Taylor Skorupski opened the account early, burying one at the 14:25 mark off some nifty setup work from Ashley Ivanoff and Suzanne Hawkins. 

The Hoes kept the pressure in the offensive zone, creating heavy traffic in front of The Rookery’s crease. With just over a minute left in the frame, Ivanoff—who was clearly playing with a magnetic stick today—potted her own goal to extend the lead. By the time the Zamboni signaled the intermission, the Hoes were sitting pretty with a 2-0 cushion.

### Period 2: Momentum Swings and Guffey’s Grit
The Hoes didn’t take their foot off the gas to start the second. Jordan Hawkins Baker connected with Kristin Brooks to put the Hoes up by three, and shortly after, Ivanoff struck again for her second of the afternoon. 

Just when it looked like the Hoes might cruise to a shutout, The Rookery finally found their legs. Christopher Guffey decided he’d had enough of the defensive shell-game, lighting the lamp off a feed from Tyler McKee at 2:49. That goal wasn’t just a tally; it was a pulse. It breathed life into the Rookery bench and set the stage for a dramatic final frame.

### Period 3: The Comeback That Almost Was
The third period was an absolute fever dream. Aaron Crum slashed the deficit to two with an unassisted beauty at 10:38, followed by Chris Olsen, who went top shelf where mom keeps the cookies off a Guffey pass just minutes later. Suddenly, we had a one-goal game.

But just as The Rookery looked poised to complete the comeback, Abigail Nisbet silenced the rally. With only 22 seconds left on the clock, she buried a clutch goal courtesy of Jordan Hawkins Baker. Jerry LaRue added a late-game heroic effort for The Rookery at 2:42, but it was too little, too late. 

### The Aftermath
When the dust settled, the Average Hoes had escaped with a 5-4 victory. It was a gutsy win for the Hoes, who now sit comfortably with 4 points on the season, proving they have the clutch factor needed to close out tight games.

For The Rookery, the scoreboard shows 0 points, but let’s be honest: that third-period surge proved they have the grit to battle back from the brink. They’ll be looking to bottle that late-game intensity for their next outing. 

It was a morning of end-to-end action, missed chances, and pure hockey magic. If this is a preview of the rest of the season, do yourself a favor: clear your schedule and get to the rink. You don't want to miss what happens next.
