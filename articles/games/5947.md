# Danglers Dance Circles Around Berzerkers in 4-0 Shutout

**By: "Slap-Shot" Sammy, Hometown Beat Reporter**
*May 14, 2026 | The Ice Palace*

If you caught the 9:30 PM puck drop on this chilly May night, you know one thing for sure: the Berzerkers might want to check if their skates were on the wrong feet. In a display of pure, unadulterated puck control, the Dirty Danglers marched into the rink and absolutely suffocated the Berzerkers, walking away with a clean 4-0 sheet and a whole lot of swagger.

### Period 1: Setting the Tone
The Berzerkers came out looking for a spark, but the Danglers were busy throwing ice water on any flames of hope. At the 7:09 mark, the dam broke. Joshua Hopwood feathered a beautiful pass to Dylan Calhoun, who didn’t just put the puck in the net—he sent it home with a polite invitation. Calhoun’s goal put the Danglers up 1-0 early, and for the remainder of the period, the Berzerkers were left chasing shadows as the Danglers controlled the tempo with surgical precision.

### Period 2: A Brief Glimmer of Hope Denied
The middle frame provided the Berzerkers with their best chance to claw back into this tilt. When Jonathan McCormick sat for two minutes for roughing at the 2:01 mark, the home crowd was ready to see a surge. Instead, the Danglers’ penalty kill played like a brick wall in skates. 

Once the teams got back to even strength, the Danglers reminded us why they’re the ones with the points. At 4:25, Kalea Maxwell pulled a rabbit out of a hat, scoring an unassisted beauty. Maxwell danced through traffic like she was shopping on a Sunday afternoon, leaving the Berzerker defense looking for their compasses. By the time the horn blew for the second intermission, it was 2-0, and the writing was officially on the wall.

### Period 3: The Danglers Close the Curtain
If the Berzerkers hoped for a third-period rally, they were instead met with a storm. Even with the clock running down in the final seconds of the game, the Danglers were hungry. Charles Webber put a dagger in the heart of the Berzerkers with a buzzer-beater goal that defied physics. 

Not satisfied with just one, Kalea Maxwell struck again at the 1:14 mark, burying her second of the night off a lovely feed from the duo of Calhoun and McCormick. The Berzerkers had a late power-play opportunity after Arthur Baisley was sent to the box for tripping at 4:38, but the Danglers' goaltender stood tall, refusing to let even a consolation goal slip past. When the dust settled, it was a total lockdown performance.

### The Big Picture
The final score of 4-0 says it all. The Dirty Danglers move to 4 points on the season, asserting themselves as the team to beat in this early stretch. They showed grit, discipline, and the kind of finishing touch that keeps goalies up at night.

For the Berzerkers, it’s back to the drawing board. Sitting at 0 points, they’ll need to find some traffic in front of the net and tighten up their defensive zone coverage if they want to stop their early-season slide. It’s early days yet, but as any seasoned fan knows: it’s hard to win games when you’re spending the whole night watching the other team celebrate.
