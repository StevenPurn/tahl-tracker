# Syndicate Swamps Cold Ones in 8-3 Route at the Rink

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
*May 13, 2026*

There’s a smell you only get in a hockey rink at 9:30 on a Wednesday night—stale coffee, sharp skate-sharpening dust, and the unmistakable scent of a team trying to find its legs. Tonight, that search led the Kraken Cold Ones into a buzzsaw, as The Syndicate skated away with a commanding 8-3 victory in a game that felt like a clinic on how to crash the crease.

### Period 1: Syndicate Strikes Early and Often
The Syndicate didn’t just open the door; they kicked it off the hinges. It started at the 11:01 mark when Brett Mooney found the back of the net, courtesy of a slick dish from Travis Moore. Before the Cold Ones could even wipe the condensation off their visors, Daron Ross hammered home a goal off a beautiful tic-tac-toe passing play from Moore and Michael Marshall.

By the time Brett Mecham went coast-to-coast for an unassisted beauty at the 1:43 mark, the Kraken were looking for a life raft. Chase Stubblefield finally put the Cold Ones on the board at 3:20, but the Syndicate’s Mooney wasn't done, adding his second of the period to send the teams into the break with a lopsided 4-1 scoreboard.

### Period 2: The Discipline Dilemma
The second frame saw the Cold Ones try to claw their way back. Randy Hatfield sparked a glimmer of hope just over a minute in, finishing off a feed from Paul Davis to make it 4-2. But momentum is a fickle friend, and it quickly abandoned the Kraken.

Michael Marshall restored the three-goal cushion just thirty seconds later, and Kacy Griffin added another shortly after. To make matters worse, the frustration boiled over for the Kraken. Paul Davis headed to the box for tripping, followed almost immediately by a high-sticking call against Hatfield. The Cold Ones spent more time staring at the sin bin clock than the opponent’s goalie, ending the period trailing 6-2.

### Period 3: Closing the Curtain
The final frame saw Edgar Solorio notch an unassisted tally for the Cold Ones, but by then, the barn was already burning. The Syndicate kept their foot on the gas, with Kalea Maxwell and a second goal from Kacy Griffin putting the game firmly in the "out of reach" column. 

When the final buzzer echoed through the rafters, the scoreboard read 8-3 in favor of The Syndicate. It was a rough night for the Cold Ones' defense, who struggled to clear the heavy traffic forming in front of their crease all evening.

### The Aftermath
With this result, the standings are beginning to take a sharp shape. The Syndicate moves to a perfect 2-0 record, looking like a squad that has fully gelled and is ready to dominate the leaderboard. 

As for the Kraken Cold Ones, they sit at 0 points and have plenty of film to review. Tonight’s performance showed they have the heart to chase the puck into the corners, but they’ll need to tighten up the blue line and find some consistency if they want to climb out of the basement.

The cold truth of hockey? You can't win if you don't keep the puck out of your own net. The Cold Ones will look to thaw out and reset before their next trip to the ice. Until then, the Syndicate stands tall, having proven tonight that they are, quite simply, the ones to beat.
