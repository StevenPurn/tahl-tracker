# Havok Unleash the Storm: A Goal-Scoring Clinic in 8-4 Rout of Blizzard

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: May 11, 2026**

If you weren't at the barn tonight, you missed a prime-time performance that had the ice steaming and the goal horn working overtime. The Havok turned the rink into their personal playground on May 11, dismantling the Blizzard in an 8-4 offensive explosion that was, frankly, a total barn burner. 

With this result, the Havok move to 2 points on the season, leaving the Blizzard still hunting for their first taste of victory.

### Period 1: Shaking Off the Rust
The Blizzard struck first blood at the 11:03 mark, with Gary Hennenfent burying one off helpers from Jerry Bates and Jamie Halverson. For a moment, the Blizzard looked like they had the game plan dialed in. But the Havok? They don’t panic—they adjust.

With the clock winding down in the first, Michael Pasque knotted things up at 18:1, finding the back of the net off a sweet dish from Steven Purn. Just when you thought we were headed to the locker room tied, Matthew Smith ignited the crowd at 2:38, tucking one home to put the Havok up 2-1 heading into the intermission.

### Period 2: The Havok Pull Away
The second period was where the Havok truly planted their flag. The Blizzard found themselves chasing shadows as the Havok’s forecheck turned into a highlight reel. 

Michael Pasque grabbed his second of the night at 21.7, and Robert Verity—who was absolutely cooking out there—added another at 2:27 to stretch the lead. Though Hennenfent grabbed his second of the night for the Blizzard at 5:13, it was a mere speed bump. Verity answered back just over a minute later at 6:15, roofing a beauty "top shelf where mom keeps the cookies." By the end of forty minutes, it was 5-2, and the Havok were firmly in the driver’s seat.

### Period 3: Closing the Curtain
The final frame saw both teams trading haymakers. The Blizzard managed to chip away at the lead with an unassisted goal and a Jerry Bates strike, but the Havok offense was simply too deep tonight.

Robert Verity completed a monster night by securing his hat trick at 11:25, fed beautifully by Logan Singleton and Pasque. When Steven Purn rifled one home unassisted at 4:26 and Zachary Wade hammered the nail in the coffin at 6:45, the rout was officially on. The Blizzard’s frustrated defense led to a flurry of penalties, but the Havok stayed disciplined enough to coast to an 8-4 victory.

### The Breakdown
**Key Performers:**
*   **Robert Verity:** The man of the hour. Three goals and a presence in the offensive zone that the Blizzard defense simply couldn't map. 
*   **Michael Pasque:** Two goals and an assist. He was buzzing like a mosquito at a campsite all night long.
*   **Gary Hennenfent:** A bright spot for the Blizzard, netting two goals in a losing effort.

**What This Means:**
For the Havok, this is exactly the kind of momentum-building win that turns a season around. They’ve proven they can find the twine with regularity and exploit traffic in front of the net. They sit on 2 points now, looking like a team that wants to be at the top of the heap.

For the Blizzard, it’s back to the drawing board. Being outscored 8-4 isn't just about bad bounces; it’s a defensive wake-up call. They’ll need to tighten up their gap control if they want to get their first points of the season on the board.

**Final Score: Havok 8, Blizzard 4.** 

I’m hanging up the skates for the night, but one thing is for sure: if the Havok keep playing like this, you’ll want to get your tickets early for the next one. See you at the rink!
