# HAVOK REIGN SUPREME IN 8-4 OFFENSIVE EXPLOSION OVER BLIZZARD

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: May 11, 2026**

If you wanted a boring, low-scoring defensive struggle, you walked into the wrong arena tonight. The air in the barn was electric, the ice was fast, and the Havok treated the home crowd to an absolute offensive clinic, putting the Blizzard on ice with a commanding 8-4 victory.

### Period 1: Shaking Off the Rust
The Blizzard struck first, with Gary Hennenfent finding the twine at the 11:03 mark off helpers from Jerry Bates and Jamie Halverson. It was a wake-up call for the Havok, who looked like they were skating in mud for the first ten minutes. 

But hockey is a game of momentum, and Michael Pasque turned the tide with a clutch goal at 18:01, burying a feed from Steven Purn. That goal seemed to act as smelling salts for the Havok bench. Despite some extracurricular activities—including a roughing minor for Blizzard’s Garreth Lewis—the Havok didn't blink. Matthew Smith sent the crowd into a frenzy at the 2:38 mark, capitalizing on a beautiful setup from Robert Verity and Zachary Wade to head into the intermission with a 2-1 lead.

### Period 2: The Havok Storm
The second period was a masterclass in puck possession. The Blizzard’s discipline began to fray, and the Havok made them pay. 

Michael Pasque picked up his second of the night late in the frame, followed shortly by a rocket from Robert Verity. While Gary Hennenfent managed to snag a second goal for the Blizzard at 5:13, it was merely a drop in the bucket. The Havok kept their feet moving, and Verity hammered home another one at 6:15 to keep the pressure on. By the time the buzzer sounded for the second break, the Blizzard looked like they were searching for a weather report—because they were officially caught in a storm.

### Period 3: Closing the Door
The final frame saw both teams trading blows, but the Havok remained in the driver’s seat. After a mysterious "Unknown" tally for the Blizzard, Robert Verity completed his rampage with his third of the night at the 11:25 mark, effectively completing a hat trick that will be talked about at the water cooler all week.

Jerry Bates managed to find the back of the net for the visitors, but any hopes of a Blizzard comeback were promptly snuffed out. Steven Purn went top shelf where mom keeps the cookies at 4:26, and Zachary Wade added the icing on the cake with a smooth finish at 6:45. Even a late interference call on Seth Miller couldn't sour the mood; the Havok had already locked this one in the vault.

### The Aftermath
Final Score: **Havok 8, Blizzard 4.**

With this result, the Havok move to 2 points, firmly establishing themselves as a team that can light the lamp with reckless abandon. For the Blizzard, it’s a tough night at the office that leaves them still searching for their first point of the season. 

The Blizzard will need to go back to the drawing board and shore up the defensive zone if they want to survive this league. As for the Havok? If they keep finding traffic in front of the net and burying their chances like they did tonight, they’re going to be a nightmare for every goaltender in the conference.

See you at the next one—bring your earplugs, because the Havok are just getting loud.
