# Pula’s Hat Trick Heaven: Ice Breakers Shred Tuskens in 6-2 Shellacking

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
*May 10, 2026*

If you weren't at the rink this morning, you missed a clinic. The Ice Breakers didn't just walk into the arena; they practically skated on water, leaving the Tulsa Tuskens frozen in their tracks in a 6-2 masterclass that had the home crowd buzzing like a power play unit clicking on all cylinders.

The final scoreline might show 6-2, but for the Tuskens, it felt more like a blizzard that they just couldn't escape.

### First Period: The Pula Party
The opening frame was, for all intents and purposes, the Joshua Pula show. If there were any Tuskens fans hoping for a slow start, Pula had other plans. At the 6:46 mark, Pula buried his first of the day off a dish from Tobias Roeding. Before the scoreboard operator could even finish changing the numbers, Pula was at it again—eleven seconds later, he went coast-to-coast, unassisted, to make it 2-0.

The Tuskens were reeling, and things went from bad to worse when Pula completed a lightning-fast hat trick at 9:50, burying a beauty from Luke Morley. The Ice Breakers were firing on all cylinders, and despite a brief interference penalty by Nathan Klassen, the Breakers’ defensive wall held firm, heading into the intermission with a commanding 3-0 lead.

### Second Period: Ward Adds to the Woes
The Tuskens tried to tighten the screws in the second, but the Ice Breakers were just too slippery. Shawn Ward added his name to the scoresheet at the 10:34 mark, once again off a beautiful feed from the conductor himself, Joshua Pula. Ward put it top shelf, right where mom keeps the cookies, leaving the Tulsa netminder reaching for air. By the end of the second, it was clear: the Breakers were holding all the cards, and they weren’t planning on folding.

### Third Period: Trading Blows, Sealing the Deal
The final frame saw a bit of back-and-forth action. Dylan Walker finally put the Tuskens on the board at 12:20, but the celebration was short-lived. Tobias Roeding, who had been orchestrating play all morning, found the back of the net at 13:08 thanks to help from Morley and Pula.

Even with a brief scare—another interference penalty, this time by Justice Dick—the Ice Breakers remained unfazed. In fact, they doubled down. While shorthanded, the unstoppable Pula struck *again* at 7:55, notching his fourth goal of the game off a Roeding helper. Walker added a late consolation goal for Tulsa, but it was nothing more than a footnote in a lopsided victory.

### The Morning After
When the final buzzer sounded, the Ice Breakers walked away with two points, cementing their place at the top of the standings with a perfect start. For the Tuskens, it’s a tough pill to swallow—a 0-point performance that highlights a clear need to find some traffic in front of the net and tighten up the defensive zone.

For the Ice Breakers, it’s back to the drawing board to keep this momentum rolling. As for Joshua Pula? He’s probably going to need a second bag just to carry the pucks from this four-goal performance home. 

What a game. What a barn burner. Hockey is back, and the Ice Breakers are looking every bit the contender. See you at the next drop!
