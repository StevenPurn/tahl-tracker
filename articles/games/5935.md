# Pula’s Playbook: Ice Breakers Shred Tuskens in 6-2 Masterclass

**By: "Rusty" Blades, Hometown Beat Writer**
**Date: May 10, 2026**

If you weren’t at the rink this morning for the 8:45 a.m. puck drop, you missed a absolute clinic in offensive hockey. The Ice Breakers came out of the gate looking like they’d had three shots of espresso, dismantling the Tulsa Tuskens 6-2 in a game that proved why our boys are the ones to watch this season.

But let’s be honest: we didn’t just watch a hockey game today. We watched the **Joshua Pula Show.**

### First Period: The Pula Hat-Trick Blitz
It’s rare you see a guy walk away with a hat trick in the first period, but Pula decided he didn’t want to wait around. At 6:46, he opened the scoring off a slick feed from Tobias Roeding. Eleven seconds later—yes, you read that right, *eleven seconds*—Pula found the back of the net again, unassisted. 

The Tuskens looked like they were trying to skate through quicksand, and by the 9:50 mark, Pula completed the natural hat trick courtesy of a Luke Morley apple. Despite a minor hiccup when Nathan Klassen sat for interference, the Breakers controlled the pace, heading into the intermission with a suffocating 3-0 lead.

### Second Period: Ward Adds to the Woes
The second period saw the Ice Breakers tighten the screws on the defensive end while continuing to punish the Tulsa goalies. Shawn Ward got in on the action at 10:34, burying a rebound off a beautiful Pula setup. At 4-0, the energy in the building was electric. The Tuskens were scrambling, fighting for every inch of ice, but the Breakers were simply winning the battles along the boards and generating traffic in front of the net that made life miserable for the Tulsa netminder.

### Third Period: A Chaotic Finish
The final frame was a scrappy affair. Dylan Walker finally got the Tuskens on the board at 12:20, but the momentum was short-lived. Tobias Roeding, who had been orchestrating plays all morning, slammed one home at 13:08 thanks to a gorgeous tic-tac-toe passing play from Morley and Pula.

Even when Justice Dick found himself in the sin bin for interference at 7:50, the Breakers refused to break. In a bit of poetic justice, Pula grabbed his *fourth* goal of the game while shorthanded just five seconds into the penalty kill. Walker netted one more for the visitors at 9:28, but at that point, it was just window dressing on a masterpiece.

### The Aftermath
The final buzzer cemented a dominant 6-2 victory for the Ice Breakers, moving them to a perfect 4 points in the standings. They look sharp, they look hungry, and they’re moving the puck with a confidence that’s terrifying for the rest of the league.

For the Tuskens, sitting at 0 points, it’s back to the drawing board. They’ll need to find a way to plug those gaps in the neutral zone, or they’re going to be in for a long, cold season.

As for our Ice Breakers? They’ll head to the locker room smelling like victory and icy-hot. Stay tuned, folks—if this is how they play before lunch, I can’t wait to see what they do when the stakes get higher. 

**Three Stars of the Game:**
1. **Joshua Pula (ICE):** 4 Goals, 2 Assists. A performance for the ages.
2. **Tobias Roeding (ICE):** A playmaker’s playmaker.
3. **Luke Morley (ICE):** Keeping the engine running with two primary assists.
