# Trinka’s Terrific Night: Leafs Skate Past Mojo in 4-2 Barn Burner

**TULSA** – If you were looking for a reason to believe in the Tulsa Leafs this season, look no further than Braden Trinka. On a crisp May 12th evening that had the ice at the arena feeling extra slick, Trinka decided he wasn’t just going to play the game—he was going to own it.

In a 4-2 victory that kept the hometown crowd buzzing long after the final horn, the Leafs proved they’ve got the grit to handle the heavy lifting. While the Mojo showed flashes of brilliance, Tuesday night belonged to a man who clearly had a date with the back of the net.

### First Period: A Solo Act
The game started with the typical feeling-out process, but it didn't take long for Trinka to turn the heat up. At the 11:41 mark, he decided to do it all himself, navigating through the neutral zone like he was weaving through holiday traffic and burying a beauty to put Tulsa on the board. It was a statement goal, the kind that lets the visitors know it’s going to be a long night at the office.

### Second Period: Trading Blows
The Mojo weren’t about to roll over. Early in the second, Andrew Armendariz found a pocket of space and knotted things up at 10:21. But just as the momentum started to tilt toward the Mojo, the connection between Curtis Connors and Trinka proved too much to handle. At 5:22, Connors teed up Trinka for his second of the night, retaking the lead and reminding everyone that the Leafs’ top line was in a rhythm.

### Third Period: The Trinka Show and a Little Dust-Up
The final frame was a roller coaster. Tempers began to flare as the clock ticked down. We saw a parade to the sin bin, starting with Stephen Jeffery’s hooking call, followed by a bit of extracurricular pushing and shoving that sent Austin Lowry and our hero, Trinka, to the box for roughing.

The Mojo tried to claw back, with Austin Stauffer lighting the lamp at the 3:56 mark to pull within striking distance. But any hopes of a Mojo comeback were systematically dismantled when Trinka—yes, him again—completed his staggering performance with his third and fourth goals of the night. His final strike at 14:50 was an absolute laser that found the top shelf where mom keeps the cookies, effectively slamming the door shut on any late-game heroics from the Mojo.

Even a late-game delay-of-game penalty by Paul Atkins couldn’t derail the Leafs, as the defense tightened up the "traffic in front of the net" to secure the two points.

### The Bottom Line
With this win, the Tulsa Leafs move to a solid 2 points on the season, establishing themselves as a force to be reckoned with early on. For the Mojo, it’s a tough road trip back to the drawing board, as they remain at 0 points and will be looking for answers to their defensive gaps.

As for Trinka? Well, he’ll be buying dinner tonight. You don't put up a four-goal performance without earning a few drinks on the house. The Leafs look like a team that’s finding their stride, and if they keep skating like this, the rest of the league had better start checking their rearview mirrors.

**Final Score: Tulsa Leafs 4, Mojo 2.**
