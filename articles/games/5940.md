# Wendigos Howl Under the Bright Lights, Clip Eagles’ Wings in 4-2 Thriller

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: May 10, 2026**

The ice was crisp, the smell of damp gear hung thick in the air, and for the Lite Beer Eagles and the Wendigos, it was time to put up or shut up. When the final horn sounded at 8:45 PM, the scoreboard told the tale of a hard-fought battle: The Wendigos walked away with the W, taking down the Eagles 4-2 in a contest that saw plenty of grit and enough "traffic in front" to keep the goalies sweating through their jerseys.

### First Period: Wendigos Come Out Flying
The Wendigos didn't wait for the ice to settle. Just over ten minutes into the opening frame, **Zac Hughes** found the back of the net off a sweet dish from **Taylor Chinowth**, sending a wake-up call to the Eagles’ bench. Not long after, **Joshua Copeland** decided to do it all on his own, unassisted, to stretch the lead to 2-0. 

The Eagles tried to generate some juice when **Patrick Flannery** sat for a roughing minor at the 9:55 mark, but the Wendigos' penalty kill was as stingy as a goalie who’s seen too many breakaways. The Eagles skated into the locker room down by a pair, looking for answers.

### Second Period: The Eagles Find Their Wings
The second period was all about the tug-of-war. The Wendigos struck first when **Kalea Maxwell** buried one, again off the tape of Chinowth, pushing the lead to 3-0. It looked like the game was starting to slip away, especially after **Jonathan McCormick** sent the Eagles down a man for roughing.

But hockey is a funny game. Shorthanded or not, momentum is a fickle beast. McCormick, clearly looking to atone for his time in the box, snapped a puck home with just 1:35 remaining in the period. Assisted by **Christopher Webber**, the goal was the spark the Eagles desperately needed. Suddenly, it was a 3-1 game, and the energy in the rink shifted from a quiet hum to a buzzing roar.

### Third Period: Too Little, Too Late
The Eagles came out in the third playing with their hair on fire. **Jonathan McCormick** struck again at the 10:15 mark—this time on a helper from **Jeff Horvath**—bringing the Eagles within a single goal. The "barn burner" we were promised was officially in full effect.

The Wendigos took a couple of trips to the sin bin courtesy of **Matthew Palmer**, but the Eagles just couldn't find the equalizer. Just as the clock ticked down and the Eagles were pushing for the tie, **Taylor Chinowth** played the role of the spoiler. He iced the game at 3:49 with an unassisted beauty, burying his second point of the night and sealing the 4-2 victory for the Wendigos.

### The Aftermath
For the Wendigos, this was a statement game. They move to 2 points on the season, showing the league they’ve got the depth to handle the pressure when the Eagles start clawing back. 

For the Lite Beer Eagles, it’s a tough start to the campaign—sitting at 0 points—but if they can bottle the resilience they showed in the second and third periods, they won't be in the basement for long. They proved tonight they can hang in the physical battles; they just need to find that "top shelf" finish a little earlier in the game next time out.

The sticks go back in the bag, the skates get sharpened, and both teams look ahead. If tonight was any indication, the rest of the season is going to be one wild ride.
