# Riders Stampede Over Bombers in Morning Masterclass

**By: "Slapshot" Sully, Beat Reporter**

**May 10, 2026** – If you were hoping for a slow start to your Sunday morning, you picked the wrong arena. The Rohan Riders skated into the rink at 10:15 AM and didn’t just play hockey; they put on a clinic. By the time the final buzzer echoed through the rafters, the scoreboard read 6-1 in favor of the Riders, leaving the Blue Bombers looking like they’d just tried to stop a freight train with a pool noodle.

### Period 1: The Blitzkrieg
The Riders didn’t just open the floodgates; they dismantled the entire dam. James Caldron kicked off the festivities at the 11:00 mark, tucking one home off a crisp feed from Ryan Martinez. It was the kind of goal that makes you adjust your glasses—clean, clinical, and a warning sign of things to come.

Then, Ryan Martinez decided he wasn't just content with being a playmaker. He went on a personal heater, netting an unassisted beauty at 1:54, followed quickly by Sergio Castellanos making it 3-0 at 3:17. Just when you thought the Bombers might get to the locker room to regroup, Martinez added his second of the period at 5:27—this one assisted by Caldron and Aivar Kant. 

Sure, Martinez spent a bit of time in the sin bin for a high-sticking infraction with under a minute to go, but by then, the damage to the Bombers’ morale was already irreversible. 

### Period 2: Coasting and Crashing
Coming out of the break, the Riders kept their foot on the gas. Joshua Hendrickson joined the party at 2:05, dancing around the defense to make it 5-0. James Caldron, arguably the hardest worker on the ice today, notched his second of the game at 5:37, effectively putting this one on ice.

The Blue Bombers finally found a flicker of light at 7:25 when Hayden Sixsmith—fed twice by the relentless Samuel Northup—found the back of the net. It was a well-earned tally for the Bombers, but by the middle of the second, the mountain was simply too high to climb. The energy in the building shifted from "thriller" to "stroll through the park" for the Riders.

### Period 3: Closing the Curtains
The third period was a bit of a defensive slog, characterized by some extracurricular activity. Martinez, clearly feeling a bit feisty, found himself back in the box for another high-sticking penalty at 10:13. To their credit, the Bombers tried to capitalize on the man advantage, but the Riders' blue line was as stingy as a miser with a winning lottery ticket. 

Frank Post tried to ignite a spark for the Bombers with a hooking penalty at 5:11, but the urgency had long since evaporated. The Riders played smart, defensive hockey to kill the clock and walk away with a convincing 6-1 victory.

### The Takeaway
With this win, the Rohan Riders move to a solid 2 points, asserting themselves as the team to beat early in the season. They look sharp, they’re finding the back of the net with ease, and their chemistry is palpable. 

For the Blue Bombers, sitting at 0 points, it’s back to the drawing board. They’ll need to tighten up their defensive zone coverage and clear out that traffic in front of the net if they want to avoid another afternoon of chasing shadows. 

It was a tough day at the office for the Bombers, but as we know in this league: the ice is slippery, the puck is round, and there’s always another shift. 

**Final Score:** Riders 6, Bombers 1. Keep your stick on the ice, folks—we’ll be right back at it next week.
