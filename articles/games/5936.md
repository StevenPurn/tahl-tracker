# Riders Run Wild: Rohan Puts on a Scoring Clinic in 6-1 Rout of Bombers

**By: "Rusty" Blades, Hometown Beat Writer**
**Date:** May 10, 2026

If you weren’t at the rink this morning, you missed a masterclass. The Rohan Riders took to the ice at 10:15 a.m. looking like they’d just mainlined a double espresso, turning what was expected to be a scrappy Saturday morning tilt into a full-blown Rohan highlight reel. The final tally? A lopsided 6-1 drubbing of the Blue Bombers that left the visitors looking for answers in the parking lot.

### Period 1: The Rohan Blitz
From the opening puck drop, the Riders were playing a game of keep-away that would make a veteran circus performer blush. James Caldron opened the floodgates early, finding the back of the net off a beauty of a feed from Ryan Martinez at the 11-minute mark. 

It was clear early on that the Bombers were struggling to clear the traffic in front of their own net. The Riders capitalized on that congestion relentlessly. Martinez decided he wanted some of the spotlight for himself, going coast-to-coast for an unassisted marker at 1:54, followed just seconds later by Sergio Castellanos, who ripped a wrister that likely left smoke trails on its way home. Even a late high-sticking penalty by Martinez couldn't slow the momentum, especially after he redeemed himself by burying another one at 5:27, assisted by Caldron and Aivar Kant. 

By the time the first intermission rolled around, the Riders were up 4-0, and the Bombers looked like they were skating through wet concrete.

### Period 2: Cashing In, Closing Out
The second frame saw the Riders keep their foot on the gas. Joshua Hendrickson joined the party at the 2:05 mark, finding a soft spot in the slot and burying it with authority. Shortly after, James Caldron snagged his second of the game, proving he was the sharpest blade on the ice today.

To their credit, the Blue Bombers finally showed some life midway through the period. Hayden Sixsmith managed to spoil the shutout bid at 7:25, cleaning up a nice dish from Samuel Northup. It was a lone bright spot in a dark tunnel for the Bombers, who were fighting tooth and nail just to keep the Riders from reaching double digits.

### Period 3: Grit and Grime
The third period was less about offensive fireworks and more about the "rough stuff." As the game slowed down, the tempers started to simmer. Ryan Martinez found himself in the box again for high-sticking, and the Bombers’ Frank Post spent two minutes cooling his heels for hooking. The defenses tightened up, the goalies stood tall, and the score remained locked at 6-1 until the final horn.

### The Aftermath
For the Rohan Riders (4 pts), this is a statement win. They didn't just win; they dominated the neutral zone and crashed the net with a ferocity that will keep opposing goalies up at night. 

The Blue Bombers (0 pts) leave the rink with a lot of tape to review. While Sixsmith’s goal showed they have the talent to find the net, they’ll need to figure out how to navigate the traffic in front of their crease if they want to stop the bleeding in the standings.

The Riders are flying high, and if they keep playing this brand of "top-shelf" hockey, the rest of the league had better take notice. Until next time, keep your sticks on the ice and your head on a swivel!
