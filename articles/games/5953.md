# Hitmen Silver Strike Gold: A Clinical Masterclass at the Rink

**By: Your Rinkside Beat Reporter**
**Date: May 17, 2026**

There’s an old saying in this league: you can’t win if you don’t skate, but you sure can’t win if you let the Hitmen Silver turn the zone into their own personal playground. In what started as a scrappy tilt at the 5:15 PM puck drop, the Hitmen Silver eventually transformed a tense matchup into a 6-2 rout of the Puckheads, leaving the home crowd buzzing and the visitors heading back to the drawing board.

### First Period: The Wake-Up Call
The Puckheads came out of the gate with some serious jump. Just over eight minutes into the contest, Cameron Sartin silenced the home faithful, burying a beautiful feed from Richard Retkowski and Jered Pieschke to put the visitors up 1-0. It was a classic "cold shower" moment for the Hitmen, but they didn't panic. 

They responded with the grit we’ve come to expect from this squad. Lukas Vodicka took a gorgeous dish from Nicklas Clark and wired it home just four minutes later, leveling the score and sucking the momentum right out of the Puckheads’ bench. We headed into the first intermission all knotted up at 1-1.

### Second Period: The Silver Surge
The middle frame was where the Hitmen truly found their rhythm, despite an early hiccup. Nicklas Clark found himself cooling his heels in the sin bin for interference at 3:55, giving the Puckheads a golden opportunity to grab the lead back. But instead of folding, the Hitmen’s penalty kill tightened up like a drum.

Barely seconds after Clark stepped out of the box, the Hitmen struck. Calvin Baker, set up by the ever-reliable Christopher Gardner, found the back of the net to seize the lead. From there, it was all Hitmen. Clark redeemed his trip to the penalty box by potting a goal of his own at 9:36, assisted by Brooks Martin and Drew Sleezer. The Hitmen headed into the final stanza carrying a 3-1 cushion and looking like a team that had no intention of looking back.

### Third Period: The Clark Show
If the second period was a surge, the third was a full-blown avalanche. Nicklas Clark put on a clinic, securing his hat trick and then some. He opened the period with an unassisted beauty at 10:01, dancing through the defense and roofing it top-shelf—right where mom keeps the cookies. 

Though William Hofmeister managed a valiant unassisted response for the Puckheads later in the period, the Hitmen were already in cruise control. Calvin Baker added his second of the night off another smooth feed from Gardner, and Clark capped off his stellar evening by tucking away his fourth point of the night off a Brooks Martin assist. By the time the final horn sounded, the scoreboard read 6-2 in favor of the Silver.

### The Big Picture
With this emphatic victory, the Hitmen Silver move to 6 points on the season, cementing their status as a team to watch and extending their winning streak. They played with a level of chemistry that suggests they’re just warming up.

For the Puckheads, it’s a tough pill to swallow. Still searching for their first point of the season, they’ll need to figure out how to stop the traffic in front of their net if they want to turn this ship around. But in hockey, as in life, there’s always the next shift. We’ll see if the Puckheads can bounce back, but for tonight, the town belongs to the Hitmen.
