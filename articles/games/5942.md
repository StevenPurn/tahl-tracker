# Silver Bullets: Hitmen Dominate the Wolfpack in 7-1 Statement Win

**By: Your Rink-Side Correspondent**
*May 11, 2026*

If you were looking for a quiet Monday night at the rink, you definitely walked into the wrong barn. At 9:30 PM under the bright lights, the Hitmen Silver turned the ice into their personal playground, dismantling the One Man Wolfpack in a 7-1 rout that left the scoreboard operator working overtime.

The Hitmen didn't just show up; they brought the heavy artillery. With this win, the Silver move to a clean 2 points on the season, while the Wolfpack find themselves hunting for their first point of the campaign.

### Period 1: The Silver Surge
The Hitmen came out of the gate with their skates sharp and their vision clear. Lukas Vodicka drew first blood at the 11:12 mark, burying a beautiful feed from Eric Guthrie and Nicklas Clark. It was a classic display of "work the cycle, find the open man." 

Bobby Pickett doubled the lead at 6:10, tucking one home thanks to some hard-nosed hustle from Josh Moyer and another helper from Clark. The Hitmen did see some adversity when Moyer sat for a hooking minor at 8:46, but the penalty kill stood tall, neutralizing the Wolfpack’s best efforts to find a rhythm.

### Period 2: The Moyer-Pickett-Clark Show
If the first period was a warm-up, the second was a masterclass. The trio of Moyer, Pickett, and Clark spent the middle frame playing a game of catch that would make an MLB shortstop blush.

Josh Moyer ignited the engine, potting a goal at 3:37 and following it up with another laser just minutes later at 7:14. Not to be left out of the highlight reel, Nicklas Clark joined the party at 7:36, putting a puck "top shelf where mom keeps the cookies" to put the game firmly out of reach. 

The Wolfpack finally clawed one back at 8:20 when Steven Purn, assisted by James Whitacre, found the back of the net to avoid the shutout. It was a gutsy play, but on a night where the Hitmen were humming, it was merely a consolation prize.

### Period 3: Closing the Door
The final frame was all about discipline and finishing the job. While Garrett Williams (Wolfpack) found himself in the box for an unsportsmanlike minor, the Hitmen stayed focused on the task at hand. 

Bobby Pickett grabbed his second of the night at 12:59, courtesy of a gorgeous setup from Clark, and Moyer capped off his massive night with his own second marker at 5:25, assisted once again by the tireless Pickett. By the final buzzer, the Hitmen had effectively put the Wolfpack in the rearview mirror.

### The Aftermath
For the Hitmen Silver, this is exactly the kind of momentum you want to build on. They looked like a well-oiled machine, particularly that top line, which seemed to have an internal GPS leading them straight to the Wolfpack crease.

As for the One Man Wolfpack, it’s back to the drawing board. They struggled with traffic in front of the net and couldn't contain the relentless puck movement of the Hitmen. They’ll look to shake off the rust and get back in the win column as the season progresses.

For now, the Hitmen sit comfortably with their 2 points, proving that while it’s a long road to the championship, they’ve certainly got the engine to handle the hills.

**Final Score: Hitmen Silver 7, One Man Wolfpack 1.**
