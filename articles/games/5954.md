# Blizzard Forecast: A Scoring Storm Buries the Honey Badgers, 7-4

**By: Your Hometown Rink-Side Reporter**
**Date: May 17, 2026**

If you wanted a quiet Saturday night, you certainly didn’t come to the arena tonight. The Blizzard and the Honey Badgers collided in a contest that saw the mercury rise, the tempers flare, and the red light behind the net get more workout than a treadmill in January. When the final horn sounded, the Blizzard had successfully iced the Honey Badgers, 7-4, in an absolute barn burner.

### First Period: The Early Chill
The Blizzard didn't just come out of the locker room; they came out like a Nor'easter. It took less than a blink for Gary Hennenfent to strike, finding the back of the net just 5.6 seconds into the frame off a crisp feed from Garreth Lewis. Before the Honey Badgers could even find their legs, Jerry Bates doubled the lead at the 1:39 mark, assisted by Garrett Williams. The message was clear: The Blizzard weren't just playing; they were dictating.

### Second Period: The Penalty Box Polka
If the first was a tactical clinic, the second was a chaotic, high-octane symphony of whistles and rubber. The middle frame was a parade to the sin bin—Nunneley, Conway, Kwasny, and See all spent quality time reflecting on their life choices in the box. 

The Badgers, showing the grit their name implies, started clawing back. Robert Tett lit the lamp at 5:26 to break the ice for the visitors, but Gary Hennenfent answered shortly after to restore the cushion. The real drama hit around the 13-minute mark, when Cole Rush went on an absolute heater, scoring twice in just 13 seconds. Suddenly, a comfortable lead was looking precarious. But the Blizzard’s Garrett Williams took control, sniping a beauty at 11:07 to steady the ship and keep the Badgers at arm's length.

### Third Period: Closing the Vault
Up 5-3 heading into the final frame, the Blizzard refused to let the game slip. Jerry Bates found his second of the night just 1:15 into the period, and Garrett Williams followed suit at 3:41, burying his second of the game to effectively deflate the Badgers’ momentum. 

While Jonathan Houde grabbed a late consolation goal for the Badgers at 8:38, the Blizzard defense—and a fair bit of traffic in front of the net—held firm to secure the victory. It was a textbook display of "top shelf where mom keeps the cookies" hockey from the Blizzard’s top lines.

### The Aftermath
With this 7-4 victory, the Blizzard improve to 4 points in the standings, firmly putting their recent loss in the rearview mirror. It was the exact response they needed to kickstart their campaign.

For the Honey Badgers, it’s back to the drawing board. They’re still searching for that elusive first win of the season, sitting at 0 points. Tonight, they proved they have the offensive spark to light the lamp, but they’ll need to tighten up the defensive zone if they want to stop the bleeding.

As for the Blizzard? They’re heading home tonight with the wind at their backs and a solid win in their pockets. That’s the kind of momentum that starts a streak, folks. See you at the next one!
