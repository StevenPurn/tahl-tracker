# Blizzard Blow Past Honey Badgers in Seven-Goal Offensive Clinic

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**  
**Date: May 17, 2026**

If you didn’t make it down to the barn tonight, you missed a classic. It was a 7-4 final that had everything: slick passing, bone-jarring hits, and enough twine-tickling to make a goal judge dizzy. The Blizzard finally shook off that recent funk, storming the gates and leaving the Honey Badgers wondering exactly what hit them.

### First Period: The Blizzard Warning
The Blizzard came out of the gate looking like they’d had three extra espressos in the locker room. They set the tone early, putting the Honey Badgers on their heels. With just seconds left in the opening frame, Gary Hennenfent found the back of the net off a beauty feed from Garreth Lewis. Before the Honey Badgers could even catch their breath, Jerry Bates picked his spot and went top-shelf where mom keeps the cookies—a rocket that doubled the lead to 2-0 heading into the first intermission.

### Second Period: The Seesaw Battle
The middle frame turned into a real barn burner. The officiating crew had their work cut out for them, as tempers flared midway through the period. After a parade of whistles and a messy four-on-four situation, Garrett Williams extended the Blizzard lead to 3-0. 

But the Honey Badgers aren’t a team that goes down without a fight. Robert Tett finally broke the seal for the visitors at 5:26, and after a brief tactical reset, Cole Rush put on a clinic. In a span of just 13 seconds, Rush snagged back-to-back goals, suddenly narrowing the gap to 4-3. The air in the building was electric—the Badgers were clawing back into it, but a clutch strike from Gary Hennenfent—his second of the night—restored a two-goal cushion just before the period ended. 

### Third Period: Sealing the Deal
The third period belonged to the Blizzard’s depth. Despite the Badgers looking for that elusive fourth goal, the Blizzard defense tightened the screws. Garrett Williams kept his hot hand alive, burying an unassisted beauty at the 12:01 mark. 

Jerry Bates added some insurance with a late-period laser, and although Jonathan Houde managed to find the back of the net for the Badgers at the 8:38 mark, the Blizzard’s offensive output was simply too much to overcome. When the final horn sounded, the scoreboard read 7-4 in favor of the home team.

### The Takeaway
It was a statement game for the Blizzard (2 pts), who move to .500 on the season and prove that their offense can light the lamp with the best of them. For the Honey Badgers (0 pts), it’s a tough pill to swallow. They showed plenty of heart—especially during that frantic stretch in the second—but defensive lapses proved fatal.

The Blizzard look to carry this momentum into their next tilt, while the Honey Badgers will head back to the drawing board to figure out how to stop the bleeding. One thing is for certain: if tonight was any indication, this league is wide open and the race for the top of the standings is just getting started.

**Three Stars of the Game:**
1. **Garrett Williams (Blizzard):** A hat-trick performance that kept the pressure on all night.
2. **Gary Hennenfent (Blizzard):** Two goals and an absolute engine on the ice.
3. **Cole Rush (Honey Badgers):** A gritty display of talent, providing the only real hope for a Badger comeback.
