# A Barn Burner at the Rink: Chix with Stix Edge Out Mother Puckers in Sunday Thriller

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: May 17, 2026**

If you weren’t at the arena at 11:45 this morning, you missed a classic. It was a classic "dust off the cobwebs" Sunday showdown that turned into an absolute barn burner, with the Chix with Stix skating away with a hard-fought 4-3 victory over the Mother Puckers. 

The ice was fast, the tension was thick enough to cut with a skate blade, and by the time the final horn sounded, the fans were buzzing.

### First Period: A Volley of Goals
The Mother Puckers came out with their hair on fire. Abigail Nisbet drew first blood just 78 seconds into the tilt, burying an unassisted beauty that set the tone early. But the Chix with Stix aren’t known for rolling over. Within the minute, Elizabeth Edwards answered the call, tucking one home off a crisp feed from Heather Cozad. 

Not to be outdone, the Mother Puckers’ Malori Christian went to work, finishing a tic-tac-toe play from Melissa McLauchlin and Emily LaRance to restore the lead. It was a breathless start that left the goalies seeing double.

### Second Period: Rough and Tumble
The intensity ramped up in the middle frame, and the whistle-happy officials were kept busy. Jordan Hawkins Baker headed to the sin bin for roughing, giving the Puckers a chance to extend their lead, but they couldn't capitalize. 

The momentum shifted when Elizabeth Edwards struck gold again at the 1:05 mark, assisted by Ashley Ivanoff, knotting the score once more. After a high-sticking minor sent Malori Christian to the box, she emerged with a vengeance, netting her second of the night at 8:01 to put the Puckers back in front. However, a tripping call on McLauchlin late in the period kept the Puckers on their heels, setting the stage for a dramatic final act.

### Third Period: The Chix Come Clutch
The final frame was a defensive stalemate until the wheels came off for the Mother Puckers. Following a tripping minor to Heather Craig, the Chix with Stix smelled blood in the water. 

With the power play tilting the ice, Heather Cozad found the back of the net off an Ashley Ivanoff pass to tie it up. But the Chix weren't satisfied with a draw. Just 34 seconds later, Makenzie Dillard sent the hometown crowd into a frenzy, lighting the lamp off another Cozad helper to secure the 4-3 lead. The Puckers threw everything but the kitchen sink at the net in the dying minutes, but the Chix defense held firm, standing tall in the "dirty areas" to preserve the win.

### The Aftermath
With this result, the standings are beginning to take shape. The Chix with Stix move to 4 points, firmly establishing themselves as a force to be reckoned with this season. For the Mother Puckers, it’s a tough pill to swallow—they remain at 0 points, but if they play with this level of grit and chemistry moving forward, the "W" column will surely start filling up sooner rather than later.

It was a tough day for the Mother Puckers, but as they say in this game: leave it on the ice, reset, and look to the next shift. We’ll see you at the rink!
