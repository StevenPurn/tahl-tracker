# The Riders Ride High: Tyler Waller Hat-Trick Propels Rohan to First Win of the Season

**By: "Slap-Shot" Sammy, Hockey Beat Reporter**
*June 14, 2026*

If you weren’t at the rink this morning at 8:45 AM, you missed a classic morning barn burner. The smell of cold coffee and fresh Zamboni tracks hung in the air as the Rohan Riders and the Average Hoes squared off in a desperate bid to climb out of the basement of the standings. 

When the final buzzer sounded, the **Rohan Riders walked away with a 6-3 victory**, putting their first tally in the "W" column and proving that sometimes, the early bird really does get the worm—or in this case, the two points.

---

### First Period: Waller Sets the Tone
The Riders came out of the gate looking like they’d had three espressos before warmups. **Tyler Waller** silenced the critics early, opening the scoring at the 12:18 mark with a solo effort that had the Hoes’ defense scratching their heads. 

After Lauren Mincey headed to the sin-bin for unsportsmanlike conduct, the Riders kept the pressure cooking. Waller struck again at 4:05, assisted by Samuel Northup, putting the Riders up 2-0. While Aivar Kant took a trip to the penalty box shortly after, the Hoes capitalized on the power play through **Suzanne Hawkins**, who buried a feed from Lillian Bosque. 

But the Riders had an answer for everything. Just eight seconds later—yes, you read that right, *eight seconds*—**Samuel Northup** answered back to reclaim the two-goal cushion. 

### Second Period: The Tug-of-War
The pace didn't slow down as we entered the middle frame. **Mitchel Pierce** extended the lead at 3:58, threading the needle through some heavy traffic in front of the net. The Hoes weren't going down without a fight, though. Nicole Baker, continuing her torrid scoring streak, helped set up Suzanne Hawkins for her second goal of the game at 5:51. 

A "Too Many Men" penalty on the Riders gave the Hoes a golden opportunity to creep back in, but the Rohan defense hunkered down like a bear in hibernation, keeping the lead intact heading into the final stanza.

### Third Period: The Hat-Trick Heroics
The third period belonged to the man of the hour: Tyler Waller. At the 4:02 mark, he completed the hat trick, tucking one home "top shelf where mom keeps the cookies." 

The Hoes made a late-game push when Hannah Atkins found the back of the net at 7:22 to cut the lead to 5-3, but any dreams of a comeback were squashed just 19 seconds later. **Mitchel Pierce** struck again, unassisted, to ice the game and seal the 6-3 victory.

---

### Standing Tall
With this result, the **Rohan Riders (1-1-0)** finally break their goose egg in the standings, vaulting themselves into the middle of the pack and gaining some much-needed momentum. 

For the **Average Hoes (0-3-0)**, it’s a tough morning at the office. They’re still hunting for that elusive first win, and they’ll need to tighten up the defensive zone if they want to stop the bleeding. While Nicole Baker’s point streak remains a bright spot for the team, the rest of the squad will need to find their rhythm before they sink further toward the bottom of the table.

The ice was fast, the hits were clean, and the Riders proved they have plenty of gas left in the tank. We’ll see you at the rink next week—hopefully, the coffee will be just as strong.
