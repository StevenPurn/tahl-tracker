# Blizzard Storms Back to Freeze the Honey Badgers in 5-3 Thriller

It was a chilly night at the rink, but the temperature on the ice was anything but cold as the Blizzard roared back from a multi-goal deficit to skate away with a 5-3 victory over the Honey Badgers this Sunday. After trailing by two goals midway through the second period, the Blizzard found their thunder, rattling off four unanswered goals to secure their first win of the season.

The Honey Badgers came out of the gate with a serious sting. After Dayne Wall opened the scoring for the Blizzard at the 13:49 mark of the first, the Badgers responded with the tenacity their name suggests. Joseph Pachuta put the Badgers on the board, followed shortly by a Marshall Nunneley strike to take a 2-1 lead into the intermission. When Michael Bergstrom buried an unassisted goal early in the second, the Blizzard found themselves down 3-1 and seemingly staring down the barrel of a long night.

"We were playing like we were skating through wet concrete for the first thirty minutes," admitted one Blizzard teammate, who requested anonymity while enjoying a post-game Gatorade. "But once Moyer got that puck in the net, the energy in the room did a complete 180. It was like we suddenly remembered we brought our sticks to the game."

Joshua Moyer’s goal at 14:46 of the second was the catalyst the Blizzard desperately needed. Heading into the final frame, the momentum shifted entirely. Dayne Wall notched his second of the night at the 10:55 mark, cutting the lead to one. The floodgates then truly opened as Gary Hennenfent leveled the score at 3-3 with just over a minute gone in the third.

The Blizzard’s defensive discipline held firm even when Jason Corcoran headed to the box for interference late in the period. Instead of folding under the pressure of the penalty kill, the Blizzard pressed harder, culminating in Nicklas Clark’s go-ahead goal at the 9:05 mark—a beautiful finish off a feed from Zachary Stidham. 

The Honey Badgers fought to find the equalizer, but the Blizzard backline stood as rigid as an icicle in the dying minutes. By the time the final buzzer echoed through the rafters, the Blizzard had successfully erased the memory of their early-season tie and jumped into the win column.

For the Honey Badgers, it’s a tough pill to swallow after holding the momentum for the first half of the contest. For the Blizzard, this win is a massive shot in the arm. If they can carry the fire they showed in that third period into their next matchup, the rest of the league might want to pack an extra parka.
