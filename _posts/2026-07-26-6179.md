# Stingers Silenced: Valkyries Fend Off Valiant Bad Bees in 2-1 Thriller

**By: "Rusty" Blades, Rinkside Reporter**
**July 26, 2026**

If you didn’t have your heart rate monitor strapped on at the arena tonight, you missed a classic. The Bad Bees (1-5-1) played host to the league-leading Valkyries (6-1-0) in a matchup that looked like a mismatch on paper, but played out like a pure, unadulterated barn burner on the ice. The final score read 2-1 for the visitors, but don’t let the digits fool you—the Bees had the Valkyries sweating through their jerseys until the final horn.

### A Defensive Chess Match
The opening frame was a masterclass in neutral zone neutralizers. Both goaltenders were absolute walls, turning aside every prayer sent their way. We saw plenty of “traffic in front of the net”—the kind where you’re lucky to leave with all your teeth—but the tenders were playing like they had an extra gear. It was a 0-0 stalemate heading into the second, and the atmosphere was thicker than the ice shavings in the crease.

### The Bees Find the Honey
The ice finally cracked in the second period. At the 11:32 mark, the Bees’ enigmatic goal-scoring machine, known simply as "Unknown," lived up to their name in the best way possible. They managed to slip one past the Valkyries' defense unassisted, snapping home a beauty to put the Bees up 1-0. It sent the home crowd into a frenzy, and for a moment, the underdog Bees looked ready to pull off the upset of the season.

However, the Valkyries aren't sitting at the top of the standings by accident. They answered back at 5:55 when Gabrielle Gibson—continuing her hot streak—buried a dish from Makenzie Dillard. The momentum shift was palpable, and despite a sticky situation when the Bees’ Remisha Blation was sent to the box for tripping at 9:07, the Bees’ penalty kill unit stood tall, refusing to let the Valkyries take the lead before the intermission.

### The Third Period Dagger
The third period was a desperate, gritty slog. With the clock ticking down and both teams looking for a hero, the tension was so thick you could cut it with a skate blade. With just seconds left in regulation—a heartbreaking 2.7 seconds on the clock—Makenzie Dillard decided she wasn’t leaving the building with a tie. Dillard went top shelf where mom keeps the cookies, unassisted, to snatch the lead and ultimately the win for the Valkyries. 

A last-ditch effort by the Bees was hampered by a hooking penalty against Elaina Martin at the 2:33 mark, and the Valkyries managed to lock down the defensive zone to clinch the 2-1 victory.

### The Aftermath
For the Valkyries, it’s another two points in the bank, further cementing their status as the team to beat at the top of the standings. They showed the poise of a championship squad, winning a game that could have easily slipped away.

As for the Bad Bees, take a bow. You held the league leaders to the absolute wire. While this loss keeps them sitting at 1-6-1, the effort was there, the intensity was there, and if they keep playing with that kind of defensive grit, they’ll be buzzing their way into the win column again sooner rather than later. 

The Valkyries roll on, but tonight, the Bees proved that in this league, you’re only as good as your next shift. We’ll see you at the rink!
