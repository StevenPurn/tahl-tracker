# Laser Burn Through the Leafs: A Second-Period Shock Therapy in Tulsa

**By: "The Barn Owl" – Rinkside Beat Writer**

**TULSA –** If you’re a Tulsa Leafs fan, tonight’s 5-3 loss to the Laser was a classic case of "the good, the bad, and the ugly." For the first twenty minutes, the Leafs were skating like they had rocket fuel in their skates. By the second intermission? Well, let’s just say the Laser found the switch, and they flipped it to 'high.'

The puck dropped at 8:45 PM under the hum of the arena lights, and the Leafs looked like a team hungry for a change in momentum.

### Period 1: The Leafs’ Golden Hour
The Leafs came out swinging. Even when Colton Taylor headed to the sin bin for a roughing minor at the 2:11 mark, the Leafs didn't just kill the penalty—they looked dangerous. Jason Fennell broke the ice at 4:10, burying a beauty off a feed from Braden Trinka. 

The momentum stayed firmly in the home corner. Even after Douglas Moore caught a holding penalty, the Leafs weren't deterred. Braden Trinka proved he’s the real deal, doubling the lead at 8:31 with an assist from Paul Atkins. Heading into the first intermission, the Leafs held a 2-0 cushion, and you could hear a pin drop in the Laser locker room.

### Period 2: The Taylor Takeover
If the first period was the Leafs' highlight reel, the second was the Colton Taylor show. Trinka kept his personal heater going by burying his second of the night at 2:50, putting the Leafs up 3-0. It felt like a blowout in the making.

Then, the game went haywire. 

Laser’s David Mansfield and Colton Taylor took back-to-back penalties, but instead of the Leafs pulling away, they seemed to go cold. Short-handed, Taylor decided he’d had enough of the scoreboard deficit. At 4:16, Taylor found the back of the net to spark the fuse. 

Suddenly, the ice tilted. The Laser turned up the heat, and the Leafs started playing like they were skating in cement. Taylor hammered home his second of the night with just 48.6 seconds left in the period, and then NICHOLAS POVERUD and Chad Pancoast followed up in a blistering four-minute span. By the time the horn sounded for the second intermission, the Leafs had surrendered a 3-0 lead and were suddenly trailing 4-3. It was a complete defensive meltdown, the kind that makes a reporter want to put down the notepad and hide behind the glass.

### Period 3: Closing the Curtains
The third period was a grind. The Leafs were desperate, throwing everything they had at the Laser net, but the poise had vanished. The Laser defense locked down the neutral zone, effectively bottling up the Leafs' transition game.

Any hope of a Tulsa comeback was effectively snuffed out just seven seconds into the period when ROBERT VERITY—riding a hot streak—tucked one home to make it 5-3. The physicality picked up as James Darnaby and David Mansfield danced in the corner, trading minor penalties at 2:04, but the scoreboard remained frozen in the Laser's favor.

### The Aftermath
For the Laser (4-3-1), this is a massive statement. They’ve proven they can play from behind and keep their cool in a pressure-cooker environment. They sit comfortably in 6th place, looking to climb higher as the season matures.

For the Leafs (1-5-2), it’s a tough pill to swallow. They’ve got the firepower—Trinka was sensational—but they need to learn how to lock the door once they’ve built a lead. They remain mired at the bottom of the standings, and with the playoffs looming on the horizon, the pressure to turn these "almosts" into "wins" is reaching a boiling point.

The fans filed out of the arena with their heads hung low, but hey, that’s hockey. One minute you’re top shelf where mom keeps the cookies, and the next, you’re watching the other team celebrate at center ice. We'll be back at it next week—see you at the rink!
