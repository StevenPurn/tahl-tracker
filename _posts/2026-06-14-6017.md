# A 16-Goal Barn Burner: Leafs and The Lodge Skate to an Epic 8-8 Deadlock

**By: Our Hometown Hockey Correspondent**

If you weren’t at the rink this morning, you missed a absolute masterclass in offensive hockey. The Tulsa Leafs and The Lodge treated the early bird crowd at 11:15 AM to a defensive coordinator’s nightmare and a fan’s fever dream, skating to a wild 8-8 draw that left the scoreboard operator begging for a nap. 

### Period 1: The Leafs Come Out Swinging
The Leafs looked like they’d had their morning espresso, jumping out of the gate with purpose. **Braden Trinka** opened the floodgates at 13:44, assisted by Sean Bowdle, signaling that this wasn't going to be a quiet Sunday morning. **Jason Fennell** decided he was having a career day, potting two goals in the first, while **James Darnaby** added another to keep the pressure on.

But credit where credit is due: The Lodge didn’t pack up the tent. **Dustin Giebel** went on a tear, scoring two rapid-fire goals to drag his squad back into the mix, followed by a **Scott Dorough** rocket that narrowed the gap. By the end of the first, the ice was tilted, the goalies were working overtime, and the pace was breakneck.

### Period 2: Grinding It Out
The second frame tightened up, though not by choice. A "Too Many Men" penalty on the Leafs' **Kenneth Watkins** put a momentary hitch in their giddy-up, but the team killed it off with grit. **Mark Stacy** found the back of the net for The Lodge, showing some individual flair, but the Leafs responded with **Jonathan Lee** lighting the lamp. **Louis Luongo** made sure The Lodge stayed within striking distance, capping off a period that felt like a heavyweight prize fight where both guys forgot to put their hands up.

### Period 3: The Fennell Show and The Late Rally
If you came for the drama, the third period delivered in spades. Jason Fennell was essentially a one-man wrecking crew, completing his multi-goal performance to keep the Leafs ahead. 

However, The Lodge found another gear late. With the clock ticking down, **Jered Pieschke** buried one, and shortly after, **Garrett Williams** sat two minutes for slashing, but that didn't stop the surge. The Lodge rattled off goals from **Scott Dorough** and **Garrett Williams** to knot things up. Fennell answered again for the Leafs, but a back-and-forth scramble ultimately forced the game into the ultimate test of nerves: the Shootout.

### The Shootout: Ice in the Veins
With the game tied 8-8, the goalies stood alone in the spotlight. **Braden Trinka** stepped up for the Leafs and coolly slotted his attempt home—his chemistry with the puck remains top-tier. But Jered Pieschke, showing the poise of a seasoned vet, answered right back to keep it deadlocked. After the dust settled, neither team could find the final edge, and the books will record this one as an 8-8 stalemate.

### Looking at the Standings
What does this mean for the table? Both the Leafs and The Lodge walk away with a point, but they remain log-jammed in the bottom half of the standings. While the Leafs (0-0-2) are still searching for their first W, they showed incredible offensive depth today. The Lodge (0-1-2) proved they have the resilience to climb back from multiple deficits.

One thing is for sure: if these two teams play again, clear your schedule. You won't want to miss the fireworks.
