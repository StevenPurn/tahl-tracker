# Drunks Pour It On: Mighty Drunks Cruise Past Greasers in 5-2 Victory

The Mighty Drunks lived up to their name in spirit if not in libations on Wednesday night, shaking off the Tulsa Greasers with a crisp 5-2 win that keeps them firmly in the hunt near the top of the standings. While the Greasers arrived looking to snap a cold streak, the Drunks were effectively the bartender, cutting off the visitors early and often in a performance that had the local faithful buzzing.

The momentum was established early, with the Drunks turning the opening frame into an offensive clinic. It started at the 10:58 mark when Thomas A. Wolff lit the lamp off a feed from Brett Lamb. Just 50 seconds later, Justen Monroe doubled the lead, burying a slick pass from Glenn Prouse to put the Greasers on their heels. Before the period could even wrap, Brett Lamb decided he wasn't done playing provider and turned finisher himself, netting an unassisted goal at 5:46 to send the Drunks into the intermission with a comfortable 3-0 cushion.

"We were moving the puck like we had eyes in the back of our helmets," said one anonymous Drunks fan behind the bench, who spent the intermission celebrating with a lukewarm pretzel. "When Lamb is skating like that, you might as well just hand him the puck and get out of the way."

The Greasers tried to find a spark in the second period, capitalizing on a Jonathan Liechti tripping penalty when Joshua Hendrickson fired home an unassisted marker at 14:36. But just as the Greasers looked to gain a foothold, the Drunks slammed the door shut. Jared Hallahan answered back shortly after, capitalizing on a power-play opportunity during a roughing stint by Colin Roohan to restore the three-goal lead.

The third period turned into a bit of a grind, but Brett Lamb proved to be the thorn in the Greasers' side all evening. He picked up his second goal of the night at the 10:04 mark, cleaning up a pass from Sean Laube to put the game effectively out of reach. Though Colin Roohan managed a consolation goal for the Greasers with just over a minute left on the clock, the damage had been done.

Tempers flared briefly in the final frame with a coincidental slashing and roughing call against Nathan Fahland and Joshua Hendrickson, but the Drunks managed the chaos with the poise of a team that knows exactly where they sit in the hierarchy.

For the Tulsa Greasers, the 0-2-1 start continues to be a hurdle, and they’ll be looking to head back to the drawing board to find some defensive consistency. As for the Mighty Drunks, they move to 2-0-1 on the season, proving that their early-season form is no fluke. With Brett Lamb extending his point streak and the team’s chemistry looking tighter than a brand-new pair of skates, the Drunks are looking like the team to beat in this young regular season.
