# Mayhem Wreck+ Steamrolls Gold as Ice in Three-Goal Blitz

If you blinked during the second period at the rink last night, you missed the entire story of the game. The Mayhem Wreck+ turned a tight, defensive battle into a total demolition job in the span of just twenty-one seconds, effectively icing the Gold as Ice in a 5-2 victory that puts the Wreck+ squarely in the win column for the first time this season.

The first period was a grit-and-grind affair, the kind where you spend more time scraping the ice than finding the back of the net. Gregory Boyer broke the silence at the 2:41 mark for the Wreck+, a solo effort that served as the only real highlight of the opening frame. It was clean, it was simple, and it set the stage for what looked like a long, low-scoring night.

Gold as Ice managed to punch back early in the second, with Aden Ingram finding the twine off a sweet setup from Dickie Williams and Mark DeSimone to knot things up at 1-1. For a moment, it felt like we were headed for another nail-biter. But then, the Wreck+ offense decided to wake up—and they did it with the ferocity of a Zamboni gone rogue.

The sequence began at 8:29, when John Saltsman found the net. Before the Gold as Ice bench could even finish shouting for a defensive reset, Saltsman was back at it again just thirteen seconds later. And just to put a cherry on top of the madness, Zachary Hughes hammered home another goal only eight seconds after that. Three goals in twenty-one seconds. I’ve seen some frantic shifts in my time, but that was like watching a house fire happen in slow motion. By the time the dust settled, the Wreck+ had turned a stalemate into a commanding 4-1 lead.

To their credit, Gold as Ice didn’t pack up the gear and go home early. Daniel Maxwell clawed one back late in the third, assisted by DeSimone and Jessica Hopwood, but the momentum train had already left the station. Zachary Hughes added an empty-net insurance marker with less than a minute remaining to seal the 5-2 final.

"We just caught a wave," a visibly winded John Saltsman told me near the locker room, leaning on his stick like it was the only thing keeping him upright. "When the puck starts moving like that, you just try not to get in its way."

It was a tough night for Gold as Ice, who now find themselves looking for answers after a stagnant second period. For Mayhem Wreck+, the win is a massive shot in the arm. Moving from a single point in the standings to a statement win puts the rest of the league on notice that this team has a gear they aren't afraid to shift into. If they can replicate that twenty-one-second explosion on a regular basis, they’re going to be a real headache for everyone else in the league.
