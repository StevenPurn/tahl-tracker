# Tuskens Tusk the Riders in Battle of the Basement

The Tulsa Tuskens finally climbed out of the winless column this morning, scraping past the Rohan Riders with a 2-1 victory in a gritty, lunch-pail affair that proved even the teams at the bottom of the standings can deliver some top-tier drama.

Coming into this one, both squads were hungry for their first win of the season, and it showed early. The Riders drew first blood just 6:01 into the opening frame when Gage Walker buried a helper from Nathan Lesnick and Alexander Rowin. It looked like it might finally be the Riders' day, but the Tuskens had other plans.

Midway through the first, Dylan Walker put the Tuskens on his back. He leveled the score at 7:27, proving that sometimes you don’t need an assist to get the job done—just a heavy stick and a refusal to quit. The rest of the first period turned into a whistle-fest, as both teams traded trips and slashes like they were handing out party favors. It was a chaotic stretch that saw tempers flare, but the goaltenders stood tall, keeping the score deadlocked heading into the middle frame.

The second period belonged to the Walker show. Dylan Walker notched his second of the game at the 6:12 mark, firing home a clean feed from Tyler Rathe to give the Tuskens their first lead of the day.

"I just saw the opening, closed my eyes, and hoped for the best," a jubilant Dylan Walker joked in the locker room after the buzzer. "It wasn't pretty, but in this league, a win is a win—even if it’s ugly enough to scare the Zamboni driver."

The Tuskens’ defense locked down the neutral zone for the remainder of the game. While the Riders scrambled to find an equalizer, the third period was defined by disciplined, if somewhat frantic, play. Despite a couple of late-game power play opportunities for the Tuskens caused by Riders’ tripping penalties, the score remained frozen at 2-1.

For the Tuskens, the win is a massive morale boost, pushing them to 1-4-4 on the year. For the Riders, it’s a tough pill to swallow as they continue to hunt for that elusive first victory. But if this morning’s effort was any indication, both of these teams have plenty of fight left in the tank as they try to claw their way up the standings. 

As the ice clears, one thing is certain: in the race to the bottom, the Tuskens officially have a little more breathing room.
