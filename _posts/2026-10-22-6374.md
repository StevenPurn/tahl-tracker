# Outsiders Keep Rolling, Freeze Gold as Ice in 4-1 Victory

If there’s one thing you learn hanging around the boards at the local rink, it’s that momentum is a fickle beast—but right now, The Outsiders are riding it like they’ve got a tailwind. They rolled into tonight’s matchup against Gold as Ice looking to keep their heater alive, and they didn't disappoint, skating away with a convincing 4-1 win that keeps them sitting pretty near the top of the league standings.

The game started with the kind of frantic energy that makes you spill your hot cocoa. The Outsiders came out looking like they’d had an extra cup of coffee in the locker room, burying two quick ones in the first period to set the tone. Logan Pardue got the party started at the 13:42 mark, cleaning up a nice feed from Justice Dick. Not even a minute later, Tyler Waller decided he didn't want the scoreboard operator to get bored, finding the back of the net unassisted to put his squad up 2-0.

Gold as Ice, a team that’s been playing some gritty hockey lately, didn't pack it in. They clawed their way back into the fray in the second period when Aaron McElmeel took a beautiful setup from Joe Baker and Daniel Maxwell to cut the lead in half. For a few minutes there, the atmosphere in the building tightened up—you could feel the tension, the kind that usually results in a few stray elbows and some spirited jawing between the benches.

But The Outsiders have that "it" factor this season. Just as Gold as Ice started feeling the surge of a potential comeback, Enoch Cox snatched it right back. At the 5:24 mark of the second, Cox went unassisted to restore the two-goal cushion, effectively taking the wind out of the visitors' sails. 

The third period turned into a bit of a parade to the penalty box, with both teams trading trips to the sin bin like they were handing out party favors. Between the hooking, interference, and trips, the flow of the game got a little choppy, but The Outsiders’ defense held firm. They kept their cool while the clock ticked down, eventually putting the nail in the coffin at the 8:03 mark. Daron Ross hammered home the final goal of the night, courtesy of a slick pass from Tyler Holmes, to cement the 4-1 final.

For Gold as Ice, it was a tough night at the office, but they’ve got the grit to bounce back. As for The Outsiders, this win just reinforces that they aren't just a flash in the pan; they’re a legitimate force to be reckoned with. They’re playing disciplined, they’re capitalizing on their chances, and they’re having a heck of a lot of fun doing it. If you’re a fan of this team, the beer tastes a little colder and the post-game commute feels a little shorter tonight. Just another night at the rink, folks—and it was a good one.
