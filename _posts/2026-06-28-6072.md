# Blanking the Opposition: Shooting Blanks Prevail in 14-Goal Slugfest!

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
*June 28, 2026*

If you were looking for a defensive masterpiece this morning, you probably stopped at the wrong rink. But if you wanted to see the twine ripple until it practically begged for mercy? Oh, you were in the right place. 

In a clash between the league’s top two titans, the **Shooting Blanks (5-0-0)** extended their reign of terror at the top of the standings, fending off a desperate, late-game charge by the **Renegades (3-3-0)** in an 8-6 thriller that had the ice melting faster than a snowman in July.

### First Period: The Caputo Show
The Blanks came out of the gate looking like they were shot out of a cannon. Hunter Best opened the account at the 13:48 mark, setting the tone for a period that would belong to one man: Phillip Caputo. 

Caputo was an absolute menace, playing like he had a magnet attached to the puck. He tallied a natural hat trick in the opening frame alone, including a buzzer-beater with less than a second on the clock to head into the break. The Renegades finally woke up late in the period with markers from Alex Bateman and Joseph Pachuta, but they were skating uphill against a Blanks squad that was firing on all cylinders.

### Second Period: Trading Blows
The second period was a classic “anything you can do, I can do better” affair. Alex Bateman continued his personal crusade, sniping his second of the game early on to pull the Renegades within striking distance. 

But the Blanks aren’t undefeated by accident. Every time the Renegades clawed back, the Blanks had an answer. When the Renegades pushed to 4-3, Caputo popped back up to hammer home his *fourth* goal of the game. Just as the Renegades narrowed it again, Bret Kurth—who was involved in the play all morning—responded with a gritty finish to keep the momentum squarely in the Blanks' locker room.

### Third Period: The Renegades’ Last Stand
The final frame saw the Renegades try to turn this into a “barn burner” in the truest sense. Hunter Best and Bret Kurth seemed to have put the game on ice with a pair of insurance goals, bringing the tally to 8-4. 

However, credit the Renegades for having the heart of a champion. Trailing by four with the clock ticking down, they ramped up the pressure. With Hunter Best in the box for hooking, Michael Pasque and then the irrepressible Alex Bateman (who finished with a hat trick of his own) lit the lamp in quick succession. The final five minutes were absolute chaos—bodies flying, tape-to-tape passing, and traffic in front of the net that looked more like a morning commute on the freeway. 

Ultimately, the Blanks' cushion was just enough. When the final horn sounded, the scoreboard read 8-6, and the Blanks remained the only undefeated squad in the circuit.

### The Breakdown
*   **The MVP:** Phillip Caputo. Four goals in a single game is the stuff of legend. He was finding the “top shelf where mom keeps the cookies” all morning long.
*   **The Persistence:** Alex Bateman keeps his heater alive, tallying his third straight multi-point performance. The Renegades lost the battle, but they proved they have the firepower to go toe-to-toe with the league leaders.
*   **Standings Shake-up:** With the win, Shooting Blanks remain the undisputed kings of the hill at 10 points. The Renegades sit at 6 points, watching the gap widen slightly, but they’ve sent a clear message: the top of the table isn't safe yet.

It was a real “get your popcorn ready” kind of game. The Blanks look untouchable, but if the Renegades play with that kind of desperation for the rest of the season, the playoffs are going to be a bloodbath. 

Stay tuned, folks. Hockey is alive and well, and it’s hitting the back of the net every 30 seconds!
