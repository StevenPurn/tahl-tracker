# A Barn Burner at the Rink: The Lodge Pulls Off a Thriller Over Tulsa Murder

**TULSA** – If you weren’t at the arena on the evening of June 28th, you missed a classic. The ice was hot, the tempers were flare-up high, and by the time the final horn sounded, the scoreboard told a story of a classic upset. In a high-octane affair that had the fans buzzing from the opening puck drop, the Lodge clawed their way to a 5-4 victory over the surging Tulsa Murder.

### Period 1: Murder Strikes First
The Tulsa Murder came into this one looking to flex their #3-seed muscles, and for the first period, they did exactly that. Despite an early hooking penalty to Spencer Charles that gave the Lodge a power-play look, Tulsa’s defense held firm.

Once back at even strength, the Murder turned on the jets. Pete Fleming kept his hot streak alive, lighting the lamp at 2:22 off a feed from Charles. Not two minutes later, MARK HODGES—a man clearly playing with a magnetic connection to the twine—potted his first of the night thanks to an assist from Luke Brandon. 

The Lodge, however, refused to be buried early. Braden Trinka found the back of the net at 6:17, cutting the lead in half and signaling that this wouldn’t be a one-sided affair. 

### Period 2: The Trinka Show
If the first period was a skirmish, the second was an all-out artillery barrage. The Lodge came out of the locker room with fire in their eyes. JONATHON ANDERSON leveled the score at 10:09, and just 21 seconds later, Trinka struck again to give the Lodge their first lead of the night.

But wait—don't count out the Murder. MARK HODGES proved why he’s on a four-game heater, burying his second of the game at 13:28 to tie it back up. 

The momentum pendulum swung violently again late in the period. Trinka, clearly feeling like he was playing in a video game on 'Rookie' mode, completed his hat trick at 7:49 and added *another* goal just 12 seconds later at 8:01. Suddenly, the Lodge was up 5-3, and the Tulsa bench was looking for answers amidst a sea of "traffic in front of the net."

### Period 3: Too Little, Too Late
The third frame was a grit-and-grind affair. Tulsa Murder turned up the physical intensity, desperate to chip away at that two-goal deficit. MARK HODGES completed a sensational hat trick of his own at 3:39, assisted by Stephen Jeffery and James Darnaby, bringing the Murder within one.

The final minutes were a frantic scramble. Tulsa pulled their goalie in a desperate bid to send this one to the extras, but the Lodge defense played like they were defending the Alamo. When the final buzzer sounded, it was the Lodge celebrating a gritty 5-4 win.

### The Aftermath
This result shakes up the standings in a big way. For the **Tulsa Murder (3-2-0)**, it’s a stinging reality check that even the high-flyers can get grounded if they aren't careful in the defensive zone. 

For the **Lodge (2-1-2)**, this is exactly the kind of "barn burner" win that defines a season. Braden Trinka’s four-goal performance is the stuff of locker-room legend, and it pushes the Lodge into a much tighter cluster in the middle of the pack. 

The standings are tightening up, the ice is getting faster, and if tonight was any indication, the road to the playoffs is going to be paved with sweat, broken sticks, and a whole lot of top-shelf cookies. See you at the rink!
