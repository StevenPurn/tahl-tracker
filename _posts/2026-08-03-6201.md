# Ice Breakers Put The Mafia on Ice: A Cold-Blooded 4-1 Victory at the Rink

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**August 2, 2026**

If you didn’t make it out to the barn tonight, you missed a classic. In a clash that had major implications for the middle of the pack, the Ice Breakers (#4) took the battle to The Mafia (#5) and skated away with a commanding 4-1 victory. It was a night of high-octane hustle, heavy hits, and enough icy intensity to make you reach for an extra layer in the stands.

### First Period: The Breakers Break Out
The Ice Breakers came out of the gate looking like they’d had three shots of espresso instead of pre-game water. At 10:31, **Justice Dick** signaled his intentions early, burying a beautiful feed from **Tobias Roeding** to draw first blood. 

The Mafia looked shell-shocked, and before they could get their legs moving, **Joshua Pula** decided he wasn’t done with his scoring streak. At 14:15, Pula went coast-to-coast, dangling through the neutral zone and snapping one home unassisted. It was the kind of goal that makes goalies question their life choices.

### Second Period: Penalties, Pucks, and Perspiration
The second frame was less of a hockey game and more of a cage match. While the Breakers extended their lead to 3-0 at 10:57 courtesy of a textbook finish by **Justice Dick** (his second of the night!) off a slick dish from **Joshua Sutherland**, the discipline began to fray.

Tensions flared, resulting in a parade to the penalty box. Tobias Roeding sat for two for roughing, but the real fireworks came from **Jason Rosengard**, who found himself in the middle of a chaotic sequence—earning a two-minute minor for checking from behind and a 10-minute misconduct to boot. The Mafia finally found a heartbeat during the ensuing power play, with **Scott Hellard** rifling one past the Breakers' netminder to end the shutout bid at 8:29. 

Despite the pressure, the Breakers’ crease was a fortress, with the goaltending squad standing tall amidst the traffic in front of the net to keep the lead at 3-1 heading into the final frame.

### Third Period: Pula Seals the Deal
The Mafia pushed hard to start the third, desperate to manufacture a comeback, but the Ice Breakers played a "shell" defense that would’ve made a turtle jealous. Any hope of a Mafia rally was squashed at 1:18 when **Joshua Pula** struck again. Sniping one high, glove-side—top shelf where mom keeps the cookies—Pula notched his second of the night and extended his blistering scoring streak to three games.

### The Aftermath
When the final buzzer sounded, the scoreboard read 4-1, a testament to the Breakers' ability to weather the storm (and the penalty box).

**What this means for the standings:**
The Ice Breakers improve to 6-1-1, firmly solidifying their hold on the #4 spot and putting the heat on the teams above them. For The Mafia, the loss stings, dropping them to 5-4-0. They’ve now dropped three of their last five, and they’ll need to tighten up their defensive zone coverage if they want to stop the slide and keep pace in a crowded mid-season field.

The Breakers are playing like a team possessed, and if they keep finding the back of the net with this kind of regularity, the top three squads better start looking in their rearview mirrors. 

**Next up:** The ice is still melting from tonight's action, but you can bet both these teams will be back at it soon. Keep your sticks on the ice, folks!
