# Renegades Run Wild in 14-7 Goal-Fest Against Tornadoes

If you like your hockey served with a side of absolute offensive anarchy, the Renegades provided the feast this morning. In a regular season clash that turned the ice into a total scoring carnival, the league-leading Renegades marched over the Tornadoes with a staggering 14-7 victory, proving once again that their offense isn't just clicking—it’s firing on all cylinders.

The story of the morning was undoubtedly the masterclass put on by Alex Bateman. If there’s a trophy for "Player Who Single-Handedly Makes the Scoreboard Operator Break a Sweat," it belongs to him. Bateman was an absolute force of nature, racking up a flurry of goals that kept the Tornadoes’ defense chasing shadows. By the time the final buzzer mercifully sounded for the visiting side, Bateman had recorded a mountain of offensive production, including a stretch in the first period that effectively put the game out of reach before most fans had finished their first cup of coffee.

"I’m not saying we’re practicing with a beach ball, but the net just looked like it was six feet wider today," Bateman joked in the locker room after the game. 

The Renegades’ momentum was bolstered by the relentless playmaking of Caeleb Burch, who acted as the primary architect for much of the Renegades' early dominance, and Bobby Watson, who seemed to find the back of the net every time he looked in the general direction of the goal. Watson’s chemistry with his linemates turned the second period into a highlight reel, as the Renegades rattled off a string of goals that made the Tornadoes' defensive zone look like an open-air thoroughfare.

To their credit, the Tornadoes didn't just roll over. Despite the deficit, they showed some stubborn grit, particularly through the efforts of Matthew Rankin and Derek Smith, who combined for a few tallies that gave the Tornadoes bench something to cheer about. Christian Alvegran also continued his recent scoring form, finding the twine twice in the third period as the Tornadoes attempted to make the scoreline look slightly more respectable.

However, the Renegades' depth was simply too much to handle. Even with a heated exchange in the second period that saw Elliott Langford sent to the box for an unsportsmanlike conduct penalty, the Renegades barely blinked. Ian Schultz stepped up during the scramble, contributing a pair of goals that ensured the Renegades kept their foot firmly on the gas pedal.

"When you have a night like this, you just try to stay out of the way of the guys who are hot," said an exhausted, yet grinning, Renegades coach. "It was a wild one, but I’ll take the two points and a clean sheet on the injury report any day."

With this result, the Renegades solidify their grip on the top spot in the standings, moving to 4-1-1 on the season. For the Tornadoes, who remain winless, the focus now shifts to tightening up the defensive structure before their next outing. But for today, at least, the Renegades gave us a game for the ages—a 21-goal thriller that proved why we love this sport: because in local hockey, you never quite know when a Tuesday morning game is going to turn into a total goal-scoring circus.
