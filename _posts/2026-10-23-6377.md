# The Mafia Stampede: Tuskens Toppled in 7-2 Rout

If the Tulsa Tuskens were looking for a way to break their cold streak, they certainly didn’t find it at the rink on Thursday night. In a display of pure offensive efficiency, The Mafia improved their record to 3-0-2, steamrolling the Tuskens 7-2 and proving once again that they are far more than just a clever name—they’re a legitimate force to be reckoned with this season.

From the opening puck drop, The Mafia looked like a squad fueled by high-octane espresso. Gage Walker kicked off the festivities just 3:06 into the first period with a clean strike off a David Hardy feed. Not content to let the lead sit, Brian Maher doubled the damage with a helper from Brett Lamb just before the buzzer. While Michael Smith managed to claw one back for the Tuskens late in the opening frame, the momentum had already firmly shifted toward the boys in The Mafia jerseys.

The second period was a clinic in "keep-away." Joshua Hendrickson and Gage Walker—who was clearly playing like a man possessed—both found the back of the net to stretch the lead to 4-1. Even when the Tuskens’ Dylan Walker managed to navigate through traffic for an unassisted tally, The Mafia defense tightened the screws, effectively stifling any hope of a comeback.

"We told the boys in the locker room between periods that if we keep moving the puck like we’re playing keep-away at a playground, the Tuskens would be chasing shadows all night," joked Mafia head coach Vince "The Vulture" Valenti following the win. "Looks like someone was listening!"

The third period served as a victory lap for The Mafia. David Hardy continued his stellar night by burying a shot from Jacob Meyer, followed by a second goal from Joshua Hendrickson. To put the icing on the cake, Gage Walker notched his hat-trick goal—a beautiful solo effort that had the crowd buzzing—completing his dominant performance.

For the Tuskens, who remain winless at 0-2-2, it was a long night at the office. They struggled to find an answer for the relentless forecheck of their opponents, who seemed to have an extra gear every time the puck dropped. 

With this decisive victory, The Mafia keeps their hot streak alive, remaining undefeated in regulation and firmly cemented in the upper echelon of the league standings. They’ve got the wind in their sails and the point streaks to prove it; if they keep playing with this kind of chemistry, the rest of the league might need to start making an offer they can't refuse just to keep up.
