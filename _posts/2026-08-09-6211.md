# Yeti-Sized Comeback: Lodge Stumble as Yetis Charge Up the Standings

**TULSA** – If you were looking for a Saturday morning wake-up call, the ice at the rink provided a triple-espresso shot of high-octane hockey today. In a pivotal clash between the 4th and 5th seeds, the Yetis clawed their way back from the brink to snatch a 6-4 victory over the Lodge, effectively turning the mid-table standings into a absolute blender.

### First Period: Trading Leather
The Lodge came out of the gate with their skates on fire. Braden Trinka opened the dance just over six minutes in, burying a feed from Jess Herd to put the Lodge up 1-0. By the time Louis Luongo doubled that lead at the 3:06 mark, it looked like the Lodge were ready to run away with the barn. 

But never underestimate a Yeti when the scent of blood is in the water. Michael Pacheco responded a mere 18 seconds later to keep things spicy, and Skylar Miller followed up shortly after to dead-lock the score at 2-2 heading into the break. It was a fast-paced, north-south affair that had the few early-rising fans breathless.

### Second Period: The Favier Factor
The second frame was nothing short of a track meet. Ben Gartner gave the Lodge a brief 3-2 cushion early in the period, but the Yetis were finding pockets of space everywhere. 

The momentum swung violently when Corey Favier decided to put the team on his back. Favier netted his first of the night at the 1:49 mark. While Trinka tallied his second of the game for the Lodge shortly after to reclaim the lead, the Yetis caught a massive wave of momentum right before the intermission. With only 51.8 seconds remaining, Favier buried his second goal—assisted by the red-hot Katherine Popoff—to knot the game at 4-4. That goal didn't just tie the game; it sucked the air right out of the Lodge’s bench.

### Third Period: Closing the Deal
The final frame turned into a discipline nightmare for the Yetis—or so we thought. Jason Bahr and Steven Belanger both took seats in the sin bin, handing the Lodge a golden opportunity to take control. But the Yetis’ penalty kill was tighter than a new pair of skates. 

Once they returned to full strength, the Yeti avalanche began. Corey Favier completed his hat-trick at 8:21, finally putting the Yetis in the driver's seat for the first time all morning. With the Lodge pushing hard for the equalizer, Michael Pacheco hammered home his second of the game at 9:58 to seal the deal. 

### The Aftermath
Final score: **Yetis 6, Lodge 4.**

For the Yetis, this win is a massive statement. They climb to 5-4-2, shaking off a two-game slide and planting their flag firmly in the upper half of the bracket. For the Lodge, it’s a tough pill to swallow. Despite a Herculean effort from Jess Herd, who racked up four assists in the losing effort, the Lodge’s defense couldn't contain the relentless Favier-Pacheco combo when it mattered most.

The standings are tightening up, folks. With the Lodge now sitting at 4-4-2, the gap between the middle of the pack and the top contenders is shrinking by the day. Grab your coffee and clear your calendars—if today’s 9:45 AM barn-burner was any indication, the second half of this season is going to be a total slugfest. 

**Stars of the Game:**
*   **Corey Favier (Yetis):** Hat-trick hero. When the game needed a captain, he answered.
*   **Jess Herd (Lodge):** A masterclass in playmaking with four primary assists.
*   **Michael Pacheco (Yetis):** Two goals, two clutch moments.
