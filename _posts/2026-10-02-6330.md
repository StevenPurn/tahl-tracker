# Wreck+ Takes Flight in 6-2 Routine Over Eagles

If you’re looking for a lesson in how to turn a stagnant rink into a highlight reel, look no further than Thursday night’s 6-2 thumping of the Lite Beer Eagles by the Mayhem Wreck+. The Wreck+ looked like they had a bus to catch, systematically dismantling the Eagles to move up the standings in a performance that was as clinical as it was frustrating for the home side.

For the first frame, the game felt like a typical Thursday night slog. The only real excitement was a two-minute trip by the Eagles’ Steven Jordan, which the Wreck+ failed to capitalize on. It was a period defined by dump-ins, shaky passes, and the distinct sound of pucks hitting the glass—the kind of hockey that makes you wonder if the ice is made of magnets.

Then came the second period, and with it, the Zac Hughes show. 

The Wreck+ broke the seal just 1:48 into the middle frame when Jeff Orr buried a feed from Jake Jewson and Douglas Moore. From there, the floodgates didn't just open—they were torn off the hinges. Less than a minute later, Hughes notched his first of the night. By the time Stephen Jeffery and Hughes both added goals before the halfway point of the period, the Eagles looked like they were skating in quicksand.

"We just stopped watching the puck and started watching them celebrate," said one Eagles skater, who wisely declined to give his name while heading for the parking lot. "You can’t give a guy like Hughes that much time in the slot unless you’re trying to inflate his stats on purpose."

Leading 4-0 heading into the third, Mayhem Wreck+ didn't let up. Michael McLaughlin added to the tally early, and despite a pair of minor penalties handed to Spencer Hodgson—proving that even a blowout can’t save you from a trip to the box—the Wreck+ stayed in control. Hughes capped off his dominant night with an unassisted beauty at the 14:04 mark, securing his hat trick and effectively putting the game out of reach.

The Eagles finally showed some pulse late in the third. Jesse Stockley broke the shutout off an assist from Tyler Waller and Kenneth Watkins, and Adam Strange followed it up with an unassisted marker just twenty seconds later. But for the Eagles, it was a case of "too little, too late." It was a valiant effort to save face, but it didn't change the outcome on the scoreboard.

With this win, the Mayhem Wreck+ continue to look like the team to beat, showing the kind of depth and offensive patience that separates the contenders from the middle-of-the-pack teams. The Eagles, meanwhile, will have to head back to the drawing board and figure out how to keep their defensive zone from resembling a turnstile. They’ve got the pieces, but tonight, they were missing the urgency. 

In this league, you’re only as good as your last shift—and tonight, the Eagles spent far too many of them watching the back of the Wreck+ jerseys.
