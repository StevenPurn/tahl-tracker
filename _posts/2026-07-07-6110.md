# A Stix-y Situation: Chix Overcome Early Deficit to Down Power Puck Girls

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
**July 6, 2026**

The ice at the local arena was feeling a bit like a pressure cooker tonight as the winless Power Puck Girls looked to play spoiler against the third-place Chix with Stix. While the standings suggested a blowout, the first period told a completely different story, proving once again that the puck doesn't care about your win-loss record.

### Period 1: The Underdog Awakening
The Power Puck Girls came out of the gate skating like they had rockets strapped to their heels. Just 12:31 into the frame, the mysterious “Unknown” skater put the Girls on the board, cleaning up a beautiful feed from Macy Alford. The momentum stuck, and at 5:45, Suzanne Hawkins decided she didn’t need any help, going end-to-end to bury a solo effort. By the time the horn sounded for the intermission, the Power Puck Girls were sitting pretty with a 2-0 lead, and the Chix looked like they were skating through quicksand.

### Period 2: The Chix Find Their Groove
If the second period were a movie, someone definitely switched the film reels. The Chix with Stix emerged from the locker room with their hair on fire. Catherine Pearsall kicked off the rally at the 11:34 mark, assisted by Jordan Hawkins Baker, but the Girls weren’t ready to roll over just yet—the unstoppable “Unknown” struck again at 15:04 off a dish from Jessica Stoops to extend the lead to 3-1.

Then, the floodgates blew open. 

Ashley Ivanoff started the barrage at 37 seconds, followed by a Carson See special at 3:31. Suddenly, it was a brand-new hockey game. Natalie Myers and Heather Cozad joined the party, finding the twine in quick succession to turn a 3-1 deficit into a 5-3 lead for the Chix. It was a masterclass in offensive pressure, with the Chix turning the zone into a absolute traffic jam in front of the net.

### Period 3: Sealing the Deal
The final frame saw Carson See continue her absolute tear through the league. With a goal at 11:42 and another at 5:44, See completed a hat-trick performance that left the Power Puck Girls’ defense looking for answers in the rafters. Though the relentless “Unknown” netted a third goal for the Power Puck Girls at the 1:16 mark, it was too little, too late. 

The Chix survived a late-game scare when Jordan Hawkins Baker headed to the sin-bin for tripping at 6:54, but the penalty kill stood tall. When the dust settled, the scoreboard read 7-4 in favor of the Chix.

### The Aftermath
For the Chix with Stix, this win is vital. Sitting at 4-2-0, they’ve successfully defended their territory and solidified their hold on the third seed. They are playing with confidence, and with Carson See riding a blistering three-game goal streak, they are a team you do *not* want to face in the playoffs.

The Power Puck Girls, meanwhile, fall to 0-6-0. It’s a tough pill to swallow after leading 2-0 early, but there’s a glimmer of hope: their anonymous offensive weapon—the "Unknown" skater—is clearly a force to be reckoned with. If they can tighten up their defensive zone coverage and keep that energy consistent for a full 45 minutes, that first win is lurking just around the corner.
