# Mayhem Wreck+ Erase Three-Goal Deficit in Stunner Over Dirty Danglers

If you were one of the folks who headed to the parking lot early at the rink on Tuesday night, take this as your official lesson: never turn your back on the Mayhem Wreck+. In a game that shifted from a lopsided affair to a heart-stopping thriller, the Wreck+ stormed back from a 4-0 deficit to snatch a 5-4 victory over the Dirty Danglers.

The evening began with a masterclass in sniping from the Danglers' Jonathan McCormick. If he wasn't wearing a hockey jersey, we might have to check his pockets for a fishing rod, because he was pulling goals out of thin air all night. McCormick netted a hat trick, opening the scoring at the 12:11 mark of the first, adding another just before the buzzer, and completing the trifecta at 13:40 of the second. When Dylan Calhoun buried a helper from McCormick at the 6:24 mark of the second to put the Danglers up 4-0, it looked like the Danglers were set to cruise to an easy victory.

But just as the Danglers were ready to order their post-game pizza, the Wreck+ found their gears. Justin McKinney finally cracked the code at the 8:23 mark of the second, and suddenly, the ice tilted. 

"I don't know what they put in the water during the intermission," said one spectator, still breathless. "But they came out of that locker room looking like they were shot out of a cannon."

The third period was absolute carnage. The Wreck+ turned the offensive zone into their own personal playground. Ivy Emerson sparked the engine at 2:36, and barely thirty seconds later, Jeff Orr tickled the twine to trim the lead to two. Larry Cockrell then sent the crowd into a frenzy with an unassisted laser at the 4:00 mark, pulling the Wreck+ within a single goal.

Despite a brief trip to the sin bin for a tripping call on Mark Smith, the momentum stayed firmly in the Wreck+ corner. Steven Chern played the hero, burying the game-tying—and ultimately game-winning—goal at 6:04, assisted by Jeremy Wentworth and Justin McKinney. 

The Dirty Danglers fought until the final buzzer, but the Wreck+ defense battened down the hatches to protect their improbable lead. John Saltsman, despite a busy night in the penalty box, was instrumental in the comeback, contributing three assists on the night and helping to settle the defensive corps when the heat was at its highest.

With the win, the Mayhem Wreck+ solidify their position near the top of the standings, maintaining their impressive form with a 3-0-1 record. For the Dirty Danglers, it’s a tough pill to swallow after playing 35 minutes of near-perfect hockey, but they’ll look to regroup after seeing that three-goal lead evaporate in the blink of an eye. 

The lesson remains the same, folks: in this league, the game isn't over until the Zamboni hits the ice. And tonight, the Mayhem proved that a little bit of chaos is all you need to turn a disaster into a masterpiece.
