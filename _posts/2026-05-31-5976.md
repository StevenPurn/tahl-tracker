# A Barn Burner in the Heartland: Mother Puckers and Tulsa Riveters Trade Blows in 4-4 Thriller

**TULSA** – If you like your hockey served with a side of high-octane drama and enough momentum swings to give a roller coaster whiplash, then the 3:15 PM matinee on May 31st was the place to be. In a clash that felt less like a mid-season matchup and more like a playoff preview, the Mother Puckers and the Tulsa Riveters skated to a pulsating 4-4 draw that had the crowd buzzing well after the final buzzer.

### Period 1: The Puckers Draw First Blood
The game started with both teams testing the ice, but the Mother Puckers drew the first bit of ire from the officials when Melissa McLauchlin headed to the sin bin for interference at the 4:18 mark. The Riveters couldn't capitalize on the power play, however, and the Puckers used that defensive stand as a springboard. 

At 8:07, **Theresa Rose Rabara** reminded everyone why she’s a threat, burying a beauty off a crisp feed from Kalea Maxwell. It was a classic "garbage goal" effort—plenty of traffic in front of the net, and Rabara finding the space where mom keeps the cookies to put the Puckers up 1-0 heading into the intermission.

### Period 2: The Emerson Express
If the first period was a tactical battle, the second was the Ivy Emerson show. Coming into this one riding a goal streak, Emerson turned the ice into her personal playground. 

She leveled the score at 14:49, set up perfectly by Amanda Long. Then, with just under five minutes left in the period, the duo struck again. Emerson, showing the kind of hands that make goaltenders lose sleep, notched her second of the afternoon to give the Riveters a 2-1 lead heading into the final frame. The Puckers looked stunned, but as any seasoned observer knows, you never count this group out.

### Period 3: Total Bedlam
The third period was an absolute fever dream. 

Emerson completed the hat trick just 81 seconds into the final frame, going unassisted to stretch the lead to 3-1. But the Mother Puckers weren't about to go quietly into the Tulsa afternoon. Taylor Soucy answered back at the 1:46 mark, courtesy of a Rabara setup, and before the crowd could even catch their breath, Rabara went top-shelf herself at 2:47 off a dish from Makenzie Dillard. Suddenly, we were knotted at 3-3.

The back-and-forth continued. Nicole Baker restored the Riveters' lead at 8:10 with a gritty, unassisted effort, but just 96 seconds later, Rabara secured her own personal hat trick—her second goal of the period—to tie it back up at 4-4. The final minutes were pure chaos, with both goaltenders standing on their heads to force a shootout.

### The Shootout Drama
With the game knotted at four after regulation, we headed to the skills competition. The tension was palpable enough to cut with a skate blade. Taylor Soucy stepped up for the Puckers and made no mistake, putting one home with ice in her veins. Not to be outdone, Ivy Emerson—the hero of the day—stepped up for Tulsa and buried hers to match. In a game of such high intensity, a draw felt like the only fitting conclusion for two squads that refused to blink.
