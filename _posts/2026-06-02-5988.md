# A Late-Night Barn Burner: Danglers Edge Out Syndicate in Shootout Thriller

**By: "Slap-Shot" Sammy, Hometown Beat Writer**
*June 2, 2026*

If you were looking for a reason to stay up past 9:15 on a Monday night, the ice at the local rink delivered in spades. In a clash of two squads desperate to climb out of the basement of the standings, the Dirty Danglers and The Syndicate went toe-to-toe in a real beauty that reminded us why we brave the cold rinks and the early morning commutes. 

When the dust finally settled, it was the **Dirty Danglers (4) taking down The Syndicate (3)** in a shootout thriller that had the small, dedicated crowd buzzing until the final buzzer.

### Period 1: The Danglers Come Out Swinging
The Danglers looked like a team with a point to prove. They came out of the gate with more jump than a freshly sharpened blade, pinning The Syndicate back on their heels. 

It didn’t take long for the chemistry to click. At the 11:12 mark, **Kalea Maxwell** found the back of the net, set up beautifully by **Jonathan McCormick**. Just over two minutes later, they reversed the roles, with McCormick burying one of his own off a feed from Maxwell. For a moment, it looked like a runaway, but The Syndicate’s **Michael Marshall**, proving he’s got a nose for the dirty areas, snuck one past the keeper with only 37 seconds left in the frame. It was a momentum-shifting jab right before the horn.

### Period 2: The Grinder’s Goal
The second period was a gritty affair—a classic "heavy hockey" battle. Traffic in front of the net was dense, and both goaltenders were forced to earn their keep. With both teams fighting for every inch of ice, **Juan Padilla** took matters into his own hands at the 6:11 mark. He went the distance on a solo effort, finding a sliver of daylight to extend the lead to 3-1. It was a "highlight reel" effort that brought the Danglers' bench to their feet.

### Period 3: The Syndicate’s Late Rally
As the ice started to get chewed up and the legs grew heavy, The Syndicate found their second wind. **Logan Pardue** ignited the comeback at 12:15 with an unassisted snipe that breathed life into the Syndicate bench. 

The pressure mounted as the clock ticked down. Then, with just 1:30 remaining in regulation, **Travis Moore** lived up to his hot streak, potting an unassisted goal to knot the score at 3-3. The atmosphere shifted from comfortable to absolute mayhem in a heartbeat. Regulation ended with both teams gasping for air and staring down the barrel of the shootout.

### The Shootout: Cold Ice, Cool Heads
After a scoreless overtime period, it came down to the individual showdowns. The Syndicate couldn't find the range, but the Danglers were surgical. **Daniel Maxwell** opened the account with a clinical finish, and **Jonathan McCormick** iced the cake with a move that left the Syndicate netminder reaching for the popcorn. 

### The Aftermath: Looking at the Table
This win is a massive psychological boost for the Dirty Danglers. Moving from the bottom of the heap to a hard-fought two points, they’ve finally got the monkey off their back. For The Syndicate, it’s a tough pill to swallow—they clawed their way back into a tie, only to see it slip away in the skills competition.

Both teams are still sitting in the lower half of the standings, looking up at the likes of Gold as Ice and The Outsiders. However, if tonight proved anything, it’s that the gap between the cellar and the ceiling is smaller than the standings suggest. 

The Danglers leave the rink tonight with their heads held high and a "W" in the column. As for the rest of the league? Consider yourselves warned—the Danglers are hungry.
