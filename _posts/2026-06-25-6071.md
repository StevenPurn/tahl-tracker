# Waller’s Hat Trick Heroics Keep the Mighty Drunks Unblemished in Late-Night Thriller

**By: "Rusty" Blades, Rinkside Beat Reporter**

**June 25, 2026** — If you weren’t at the rink at 10:15 PM last night, you missed a classic. The Mighty Drunks, currently the league’s undisputed boogeyman, kept their perfect record intact, but they didn’t do it without a fight. In a game that saw the Rohan Riders refuse to go quietly into that good night, the Drunks walked away with a 3-2 victory that was closer than a pair of skates two sizes too small.

### Period 1: The Waller Express
The Drunks came out looking to extend their winning streak, and it didn’t take long for the heater of the season, Tyler Waller, to find his rhythm. Just over ten minutes into the opening frame, Waller decided he didn’t need any help, dangling through the neutral zone before burying a wrister that beat the Riders’ netminder clean. It was a statement goal, a “welcome to the party” moment that kept the Drunks firmly in the driver’s seat heading into the first intermission.

### Period 2: A Tale of Two Teams
The second period was a gritty, grease-the-wheels affair. With just 13.9 seconds remaining on the clock—talk about a dagger—Waller struck again. Off a crisp feed from Jared Hallahan, Waller deposited his second of the night, tucking it top shelf where mom keeps the cookies. 

But the Riders, never ones to roll over, shifted the momentum in the second half of the period. With 8:07 on the clock, Sergio Castellanos capitalized on a beautiful setup from Gage Walker, stuffing a loose puck through traffic in front of the net. Suddenly, the tension in the building was thick enough to cut with a skate blade.

### Period 3: The Deadlock and The Decision
The third period was absolute bedlam. The Riders, sensing a massive upset against the #1 seed, pushed the pace. Their persistence paid off at the 1:23 mark when Samuel Northup knotted things up at 2-2, snapping a shot past the Drunks’ keeper after a textbook dish from Joshua Hendrickson. 

For the next eight minutes, it was a barn burner. Both goalies were standing on their heads, turning away high-danger chances and leaving the scoreboard frozen. That was until Tyler “The Closer” Waller took matters into his own hands again. With 9:57 left in the period, he completed the hat trick, weaving through the Riders’ defensive shell to net the game-winner. 

The Riders threw everything but the Zamboni at the Drunks in the final minutes, but the league leaders held the line, closing out the game and maintaining their unblemished 5-0-0 record.

### The Aftermath
For the Mighty Drunks, the beat goes on. They remain the team to beat, sitting comfortably atop the standings and proving that even on a late-night mid-week clash, they have the poise to close out tight games. 

The Rohan Riders, despite the loss, should walk out of that rink with their heads held high. Taking the league leaders to the brink proves that their 1-2-0 record doesn’t tell the whole story. They’ve got grit, they’ve got chemistry, and if they keep playing with this kind of heart, they’ll be climbing out of that #6 spot in no time.

**Final Score:** Mighty Drunks 3, Rohan Riders 2.
