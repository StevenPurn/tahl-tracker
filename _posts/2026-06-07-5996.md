# Jacks Hop Over the Lodge in a Sunday Afternoon Thriller

**By: Rinkside Reporter**
*June 7, 2026*

There’s nothing quite like the smell of fresh ice and the sound of vulcanized rubber hitting the iron on a Sunday afternoon. Today’s matinee between the Lodge and the Jackalopes was exactly what the hockey gods ordered: a high-octane, back-and-forth barn burner that kept the fans on the edge of their seats until the final buzzer sounded at 2:15 PM. 

When the dust settled, it was the Jackalopes who managed to snatch victory from the jaws of defeat, edging out the Lodge 4-3 in a comeback for the ages.

### Period 1: The Lodge Takes Control
The Lodge came out of the gate looking like they had a point to prove. They dominated the early real estate, establishing a forecheck that had the Jacks spinning. 

It didn't take long for the ice to tilt in their favor. Just 1:16 into the opening frame, **JONATHON ANDERSON** continued his blistering hot streak, finding the back of the net off a sweet dish from **CHRISTOPHER FLEMING**. Not satisfied with just one, the Lodge doubled their pleasure at the 2:52 mark. **Braden Trinka**, extending his own scoring streak to three games, capitalized on a feed from **Jered Pieschke** to put the Lodge up 2-0. The Jacks were reeling, and the Lodge looked poised to skate away with it.

### Period 2: The Jacks Start to Hop
The second period was a gritty affair, defined by the "dirty areas." Traffic in front of the net was heavy, and the goalies were earning their keep. 

The Jackalopes finally broke the goose egg at 11:36. **Kyle Foster**, fresh off a goal in his last outing, hammered one home thanks to a beautiful helper from **ELIAS ELIAS**. The momentum shift was palpable, though Todd Thompson didn't help his team’s cause when he was sent to the sin bin for slashing at 6:01. Despite the penalty, the Jacks held firm, keeping the deficit at one heading into the final frame.

### Period 3: A Wild West Finish
If the first two periods were the setup, the third was the blockbuster climax. 

The Lodge extended their lead to 3-1 with 13 seconds left on the clock (yes, you read that right—a buzzer-beating strike from **Seth Miller** off a **MICHAEL GALLE** feed). But hockey is a cruel mistress, and the Jackalopes didn’t get the memo that they were supposed to lose. 

Trailing by two, the Jacks went nuclear. **KYLE VERITY** sliced the lead to 3-2 just 20 seconds later at the 13:20 mark. With blood in the water, **Bradley Fidler** knotted things up at 14:32. Then, with the game hanging in the balance, **ROBERT VERITY** played the hero. At 4:30, off a tic-tac-toe play involving **CHRISTOPHER KING** and **Todd Thompson**, Verity buried the go-ahead goal, silencing the Lodge supporters and securing the 4-3 comeback victory.

### The Aftermath
It was a tough pill for the Lodge to swallow, as they let a two-goal lead vanish in the final minutes of the game. For the Jackalopes, this win is a massive character builder. After a shaky start to the season, they’ve proven they can claw their way back from the brink.

**Standings Impact:**
The Jackalopes leapfrog the Lodge in the standings, gaining their first two points of the season and finally getting into the win column. The Lodge, meanwhile, find themselves stuck at 1 point, looking to regroup after a game that proves that in this league, the lead is never safe until the final horn blows.

The puck drops again soon, and if today was any indication, you’d be foolish to miss a second of it. See you at the rink!
