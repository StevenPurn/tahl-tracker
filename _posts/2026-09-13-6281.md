# Chix with Stix Shut Down Mother Puckers in 4-0 Shutout

The ice was cold at the rink this Sunday morning, but the Chix with Stix were bringing the heat, skating away with a dominant 4-0 victory over the Mother Puckers. In a display of offensive efficiency and a wall-like performance in the crease, the Chix proved that they’ve officially shaken off the rust from their season-opening tie, leaving the Mother Puckers looking for answers.

The momentum began early in the first period when Ashley Ivanoff broke the ice at the 13:09 mark. Assisted by Mckenna N. Allison, Ivanoff’s strike set the tone for the morning. While the Mother Puckers attempted to claw their way back into the game, a minor hiccup in the form of a high-sticking penalty against Heather Cozad at 3:34 threatened to give the Mother Puckers a window of opportunity. However, the Chix penalty kill was nothing short of airtight, and just moments after returning to full strength, Hannah Atkins doubled the lead, burying a feed from Ivanoff to put the Chix up 2-0 heading into the intermission.

The second period saw the Mother Puckers fighting to find their rhythm, but the Chix defense remained as stubborn as a goalie’s glove. At 2:49, Angela Willoughby extended the lead to 3-0, effectively taking the wind out of the Mother Puckers' sails. Atkins, who was involved in the play once again, showed the kind of playmaking vision that turns a good shift into a scoring chance.

By the time the third period rolled around, the Chix were playing with the kind of confidence that borders on swagger. Ivanoff sealed her stellar performance—and the game—at 13:14, netting her second goal of the day off assists from Cozad and Catherine Pearsall. 

"We were just clicking today," said a beaming Ivanoff after the final whistle. "When the passes start connecting like that, it feels less like a game and more like a Sunday skate."

The loss leaves the Mother Puckers still searching for their first win of the season after dropping to 0-1-1. For the Chix with Stix, the win is a massive jump in the standings, moving them to a 1-0-1 record and signaling to the rest of the league that this squad is ready to contend. If they can keep this kind of offensive pressure on the forecheck, they’re going to be a very difficult team to clear out of the zone all season long.
