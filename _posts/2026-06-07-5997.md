# Mooseknuckles Power Through Laser in Mid-Afternoon Barn Burner

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: June 7, 2026**

If you didn’t make it down to the rink for the 3:45 PM puck drop today, you missed a classic shift in momentum that had the crowd buzzing louder than a broken scoreboard. In a pivotal clash between two 1-0-0 squads, the Mooseknuckles pulled off a gritty 5-3 comeback victory over the Laser, proving that you can’t keep a good Knuckle down—not even when they’re chasing a two-goal deficit.

### First Period: The Laser Show
The game started with the Laser looking like they had been plugged into a high-voltage outlet. **Nicholas Poverud** came out firing, putting on an absolute clinic in the early going. Poverud notched the opener at 4:21, and before the Mooseknuckles could even settle their nerves, he was back at it just fourteen seconds later, burying his second of the afternoon off a crisp feed from Bryce Perkins. At 2-0, the Laser looked poised to skate away with the points, but the Mooseknuckles’ locker room had other ideas.

### Second Period: The Turning of the Tide
Hockey is a game of momentum, and the Mooseknuckles grabbed it by the horns in the middle frame. **Mark Hodges** acted as the catalyst, finding the back of the net at 11:03 courtesy of a James Rozell setup. 

Even after Jeffrey Kleehammer headed to the sin bin for slashing, the Mooseknuckles didn’t buckle. With the period winding down and the clock ticking toward the final minute, Hodges struck again—this time assisted by Pete Fleming—to knot the game at 2-2. 

The dam had officially broken. **Michael Pacheco** put the Moose in the lead at 8:57 with a helper from Donny Griffin, and only seconds later, **Pete Fleming** cleaned up the garbage in front to extend the lead to 4-2. By the time the buzzer sounded on the second, the Laser were left wondering how a two-goal cushion turned into a two-goal mountain to climb.

### Third Period: Holding the Line
The final frame saw the Mooseknuckles continue their assault, with Pacheco tallying his second of the night right at the top of the period to make it a commanding 5-2 lead. 

The Laser managed to claw one back at 14:08 when **Colton Taylor** finished off a tic-tac-toe play from Robert and Kyle Verity, but it was too little, too late. Despite Donny Griffin spending a fair amount of time in the box for roughing and interference—turning the final minutes into a nervy affair—the Mooseknuckles’ defensive structure held firm. The goaltenders faced heavy traffic all afternoon, but the Mooseknuckles' netminder stood tall when the heat was turned up, frustrating the Laser’s shooters until the final horn.

### The Aftermath
With this victory, the Mooseknuckles (2-0-0) vault into the upper echelon of the standings, asserting themselves as the team to beat in this early-season sprint. The Laser (1-1-0) will walk away feeling the sting of a game they once held in their hands, but with plenty of hockey left to play, they’ll be looking to recalibrate. 

The Mooseknuckles showed grit, secondary scoring, and a knack for the dramatic today. If they keep playing with this kind of fire, they might just stay parked at the top of the standings for a long time. 

**Final Score: Mooseknuckles 5, Laser 3.**
