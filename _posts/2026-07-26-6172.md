# Mojo Rising: A Stunning Comeback Spoils the Lodge’s Night in 6-5 Thriller

**By: "Hat-Trick" Harry, Senior Hockey Correspondent**

If you didn’t believe in momentum swings, you clearly weren’t at the rink this afternoon. In a game that felt like a heavyweight title fight, Mojo stormed back from the brink to steal a 6-5 victory from The Lodge. It was the kind of barn-burner that leaves the ice surface looking like a blender had been left on high—scratched, chaotic, and absolutely electric.

### Period 1: The Calm Before the Storm
The Lodge entered the tilt as the heavy favorites, sitting comfortably in the #4 spot, and they looked the part early. At the 11:53 mark, Tanner Bemies buried a beautiful feed from Scott Dorough, sending a laser beam home to open the scoring. The Lodge looked poised to coast, though Mojo’s discipline left something to be desired. Matthew Allington and Kyle White both spent time in the sin bin late in the period, but The Lodge couldn’t capitalize on the power plays. 

### Period 2: The Goal-Scoring Carousel
If the first period was a cagey affair, the second period was a full-blown jailbreak. 

Austin Lowry kicked off the carnage early for Mojo, but The Lodge responded with a ferocity that seemed to put the game on ice. Louis Luongo, Jonathon Anderson, and Jered Pieschke all found the back of the net in a flurry, with Anderson later adding his second of the game to stretch the lead. At one point, it looked like The Lodge was going to turn this into a rout.

But never count out a team with nothing to lose. Tyler Mills stopped the bleeding for Mojo, and after a parade to the penalty box by Christopher Fleming, Kyle White capitalized on a Mojo power play. Suddenly, the energy in the building shifted. The Lodge still held the lead, but they were feeling the heat.

### Period 3: The Mojo Miracle
The third period belonged entirely to Mojo. Trailing and desperate, they turned the screws on the Lodge defense. Thomas White started the comeback, Tyler Mills leveled it with his second of the night, and the momentum became a physical force. 

With the Lodge’s Jess Herd sitting for a tripping call, the door opened, and Jarred Butler walked right through it. At 2:53 of the third, Butler punched home the go-ahead goal, completing a comeback that had the Mojo bench erupting like they’d just punched their ticket to the dance. Despite a late penalty against Kyle White, Mojo’s defensive structure held firm, weathering the final storm to walk away with two hard-earned points.

### The Big Picture
For Mojo (#7), this is a massive shot in the arm. Sitting on 6 points entering the day, this win proves that their recent ups and downs aren't a reflection of their ceiling. They showed the grit needed to beat a top-tier contender, and they did it by out-working a team that looked like they had the game in the bag midway through the second.

As for The Lodge, this one is going to sting. They let a dominant multi-goal lead evaporate against an underdog, and while they remain in the thick of the race with 10 points, they’ll be looking for answers in the locker room. In a league as tight as this one, you can't afford to take your foot off the gas—not even for a second.

The scoreboard says 6-5 Mojo, but the story here is the resilience of a team that refused to let their season slide into the abyss. See you at the rink!
