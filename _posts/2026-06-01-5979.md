# Outsiders Crash the Bison Party in High-Octane 7-4 Thriller

**TULSA** – If you weren’t at the arena tonight, you missed a classic. The ice was smoking, the tempers were simmering, and by the time the final horn sounded, the scoreboard looked like a tax return gone wrong. In a battle of two undefeated squads, The Outsiders marched into the Bison’s backyard and walked out with a decisive 7-4 victory that reminded us all why we love this beautiful, chaotic game.

### Period 1: A Gentle Introduction to Mayhem
The Bison came out of the gate with the kind of jump that makes a goalie’s heart skip a beat. Just 3:25 into the frame, **Payton Brewer** found the back of the net off a sweet dish from **Brett Mooney**, putting the home crowd in a frenzy. But this is hockey, and comfort is the enemy. 

The Outsiders didn’t blink. **MICHAEL WIXTED** answered back at 8:09 with a solo effort that silenced the locals, proving that the Visitors were here to work. While ETHAN PETERSON spent a quick two minutes in the sin bin for tripping shortly after, the Bison couldn’t capitalize, and we headed to the intermission deadlocked at one.

### Period 2: The Outsiders Find Their Gear
If the first period was a handshake, the second was a heavy-weight jab. With only 26 seconds left in the period, **TYLER HOLMES** (assisted by LOGAN PARDUE) broke the tie, triggering a veritable offensive avalanche. 

The Bison seemed to lose their footing as **DAVID JOSIAH GUAJARDO** and **ZACH COX** added back-to-back unassisted strikes. Just when the Bison looked like they were heading for the showers, **JUAN PADILLA** ignited a glimmer of hope, burying a feed from Brewer at the 7:44 mark to keep the deficit manageable. But the momentum? It was officially wearing an Outsiders sweater.

### Period 3: Trading Haymakers
The third period was absolute, unfiltered chaos. **DARON ROSS** and **CODY DINSMORE** padded the lead for the visitors, pushing the score to 6-2. Most teams would have rolled over, but not the Bison. 

**Taylor Peterson** notched one off a clever setup from Padilla, and **Brett Mooney** decided to take matters into his own hands with a solo goal at 3:52, pulling the Bison within striking distance at 6-4. The arena was vibrating, the fans were on their feet, and the Bison were swarming the crease looking for a miracle. 

But leave it to **LOGAN PARDUE** to be the ice-water-in-the-veins closer. At 5:44, he blasted home the final nail to shut the door on the comeback, securing the 7-4 victory for The Outsiders. 

### The Aftermath
It wasn’t just a game; it was a statement. The Outsiders showed exactly why they are a force to be reckoned with, turning a tight defensive matchup into a barn-burner that forced the Bison’s defense into some rare, costly errors.

**What does this mean for the standings?**
The Outsiders (2-0-0) have now firmly established themselves as a team on the rise, keeping pace with the league leaders and putting the rest of the pack on notice. The Bison (1-1-0) take a hit in the standings, but they proved tonight they’ve got the grit to fight back when their backs are against the boards.
