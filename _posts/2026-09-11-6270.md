# Kraken Cold Ones Unleash a Flood in 9-2 Rout of Ice Breakers Plus

The Kraken Cold Ones lived up to their name on Wednesday night, turning the ice at the local rink into a total shipwreck for the Ice Breakers Plus. In a regular-season clash that started with a spark and ended in a deluge, the Cold Ones steamrolled their way to a dominant 9-2 victory, leaving no doubt about who currently owns the early-season momentum.

The game began with a flurry of activity that had the fans’ heads spinning. The Ice Breakers Plus actually drew first blood at the 10:21 mark of the first period when Tyler Waller hammered home a pass from Joshua Pula. But if you blinked, you missed the momentum shift. The Kraken responded with a lightning-quick one-two punch just seconds later, with Justin McKinney and Robert Tyree finding the back of the net to flip the script before the game was even twelve minutes old.

"I think we just woke up the Kraken," Ice Breakers captain Sean Widdows joked after the game. "We poked the bear, and then the bear decided it wanted to eat the whole forest."

By the time the first period buzzer sounded, the Cold Ones had built a comfortable cushion thanks to additional tallies from Edgar Solorio and Gregory Boyer. Despite a valiant second-period effort from Jennifer Olson, who put one home for the Ice Breakers, the Kraken were simply operating on a different frequency.

Robert Tyree was the undisputed conductor of the Kraken’s offensive symphony. Tyree finished the night with a hat-trick performance, repeatedly connecting with linemate Chase Stubblefield to slice through the Ice Breakers' defensive zone. Whenever it felt like the Ice Breakers might find a shred of rhythm, Tyree was there to dampen the spirits with another precision finish.

"It’s one of those nights where the puck looks like it’s the size of a beach ball," Tyree said while toweling off after the final whistle. "Chase was putting it right on my tape all night. I just had to make sure I didn't trip over my own skates."

The third period served as a victory lap for the Cold Ones. Ryan Martinez joined the scoring party, and Gregory Boyer added his second of the night in the closing moments, bringing the final tally to a staggering nine goals. Justin McKinney also chipped in his second marker of the evening, capping off a complete team effort that saw the Kraken Cold Ones move to a 1-0-1 record on the young season.

For the Ice Breakers Plus, it was a night to forget, but they’ll have plenty of time to regroup as the season progresses. Meanwhile, the Kraken Cold Ones look every bit like the team to beat in these early standings. They’ve proven they can handle a quick deficit and keep their foot on the gas, a trait that’s going to make them a nightmare for every other squad in the league this year. 

The puck drops again next week, but for now, the Kraken are certainly the ones making waves.
