# Puck Luck Runs Dry: Mother Puckers Silence Power Puck Girls in 3-1 Barn Burner

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
*June 7, 2026*

There’s nothing like the smell of Zamboni exhaust and freshly shaved ice at 11:45 in the morning to get the blood pumping. The Power Puck Girls and the Mother Puckers met on the frozen stage today for a matinee showdown, and while the standings suggested a battle of the cellar-dwellers, what we got was a gritty, grind-it-out affair that proved every inch of ice is contested territory in this league.

### First Period: Griffin Breaks the Ice
The Mother Puckers came out of the gate looking like they’d had an extra cup of espresso, controlling the neutral zone and forcing the Power Puck Girls to play on their heels. It didn’t take long for that pressure to pay dividends. At the 8:20 mark, Abigail Nisbet threaded a needle of a pass to Haley Griffin, who buried it home with the precision of a surgeon. The Mother Puckers held that 1-0 lead into the intermission, having effectively clogged the lanes and keeping the Power Puck Girls’ sticks quiet.

### Second Period: Smith Stops the Bleeding
After a stagnant start, the Power Puck Girls finally found their legs in the middle frame. They started creating the kind of traffic in front of the net that makes goalies lose their religion. With just 32.5 seconds remaining on the clock—talk about a locker-room momentum builder—Suzanne Hawkins found Lindsey Smith open in the slot. Smith didn’t hesitate, rifling one home to knot the game at 1-1. Heading into the third, it was anybody’s game, a classic "next-goal-wins" scenario in the making.

### Third Period: The Griffin and Nisbet Show
If the second period belonged to the Power Puck Girls, the third was a masterclass in opportunistic hockey from the Mother Puckers. 

The deadlock broke at 12:56 when Haley Griffin—playing with the confidence of a player on a mission—struck again, unassisted. She simply refused to be denied, putting the Mother Puckers back in the driver's seat. The Power Puck Girls scrambled to find an equalizer, pulling their goalie in a desperate bid to force overtime, but the strategy backfired. With exactly 1:56 left on the clock, Abigail Nisbet iced the cake, tapping in a beautiful setup from Heather Craig and Haley Griffin to make it 3-1. 

That goal was the final nail, as the Mother Puckers’ defense stood tall, turning away every desperate dump-and-chase attempt in the dying seconds.

### The Breakdown
This was a massive result for the Mother Puckers, who finally find themselves in the win column (1-0-1). Haley Griffin was the undisputed star of the morning, racking up three points and effectively putting the team on her back. For the Power Puck Girls, it’s a tough pill to swallow. Sitting at 0-2-0, the squad is still searching for their first win of the season and will need to find more creative ways to generate offense beyond that lone second-period strike.

**What it means for the standings:**
The Mother Puckers leapfrog the Chix with Stix to take sole possession of fourth place, proving that their early-season "T" wasn't a fluke. The Power Puck Girls remain anchored at the bottom of the table, looking up at the Bad Bees. 

If today proved anything, it's that the bottom half of the league is hungry. We’ve got a long road ahead, but if the Puckers keep playing with this kind of chemistry, the top-tier teams better start checking their rearview mirrors.
