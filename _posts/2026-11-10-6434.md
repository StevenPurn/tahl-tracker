# Blue Bombers Run Wild in 9-3 Rout of Rohan Riders

If you were looking for a quiet Tuesday night at the rink, you picked the wrong game to attend. The Blue Bombers turned the ice into their personal playground last night, hanging nine goals on the Rohan Riders in a 9-3 drubbing that had the folks in the stands wondering if the scoreboard was stuck on "repeat."

The Bombers came into this one riding high, and they didn't waste a second proving why they’re sitting pretty at second place in the standings. Even when Jacob Meyer took a seat in the penalty box for holding early in the first, it didn’t matter. In fact, the Bombers seemed to play better shorthanded than most teams do at full strength. Samuel Northup, extending his point streak to a staggering seven games, opened the scoring while killing that very penalty, fed perfectly by Meyer the second he stepped back onto the ice.

From there, the floodgates didn't just open; they were ripped off the hinges. Abigail Nisbet and the red-hot Aaron West started picking apart the Riders' defense like they were carving a holiday turkey. West, who is currently playing with the kind of confidence that makes a puck feel like it’s attached to his stick by a magnet, notched a pair of goals in the first period alone, bringing his point streak to five games.

Give credit to the Riders’ Christopher Guffey—he was the only one on his squad looking to ruin the Bombers' night, netting two goals in the first, but he was essentially fighting a house fire with a water pistol. The Bombers had an answer for everything, most notably from Dylan Walker. Walker, whose scoring touch has been colder than the ice itself lately, continued his tear by netting a natural hat trick, scoring once in the first and twice more in the second. By the time Walker potted his third, the Riders looked like they were just hoping for the final buzzer to act as a mercy killing.

The second period was a clinic. Aside from a lone effort by Dalton Sirmon for the Riders, it was all Blue Bombers. Nisbet grabbed her second of the night late in the frame, and by the time the dust settled, the Bombers had put the game well out of reach.

For the Riders, it’s a long walk back to the locker room. Sitting at 0-5-2, the season is becoming a real grind, and they’ll be looking to hit the reset button after this one. Meanwhile, the Blue Bombers are looking like the team to beat in this league. They’ve now won three of their last five, and with the way Northup, West, and Walker are moving the puck, they aren't just looking at the top of the standings—they're looking to own it. If you're a goalie in this league, you'd better start praying for some extra padding before you face this lineup.
