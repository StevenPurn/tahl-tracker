# Ice Breakers Put the Chill on Riders in 3-1 Barn Burner

**By: "Slap-Shot" Sammy, Hometown Beat Reporter**
**Date:** June 18, 2026 | **Time:** 8:45 PM Puck Drop

There’s nothing like the smell of fresh ice and the sound of steel digging into the surface on a Tuesday night. Tonight, the Ice Breakers and the Rohan Riders squared off in a clash that had all the makings of a classic, and let me tell you, folks—the atmosphere was electric enough to light up the whole arena.

Coming into tonight with a spotless record, the Breakers were looking to keep the momentum train rolling, while the Riders were hungry to climb the standings. After sixty minutes of high-octane hockey, the Ice Breakers walked away with a 3-1 victory, cementing their status as a team you simply cannot sleep on.

### First Period: Reyes Strikes Early
The game started at a blistering pace, with both sides trading chances like they were going out of style. The deadlock was broken at the 11:41 mark of the first when **Daniel Reyes** decided he didn't need any help. Reyes took the biscuit, went to work, and buried it to give the Ice Breakers the early 1-0 edge. It was a gritty, solo effort that sent the Breakers' bench into a frenzy.

### Second Period: The Riders Answer Back
The middle frame turned into a goaltending clinic, with both netminders flashing the leather to keep things tight. Just when it looked like we were heading into the final intermission at 1-0, the Riders found some daylight. With a mere 44.5 seconds left on the clock, **Mitchel Pierce** cashed in on a beautiful dish from **Samuel Northup**. That goal shifted the momentum, sending us into the third with a brand-new hockey game.

### Third Period: The "Justice" Served
If the first two periods were the appetizer, the third was a full-course, five-star meal. The Ice Breakers came out with their skates on fire, determined to regain the lead. 

At 11:47, **Justice Dick**—playing like a man possessed—found the back of the net off a sweet helper from **Joshua Pula** to put the Breakers up 2-1. Things got chippy shortly after when Nathan Lesnick headed to the sin bin for tripping at 14:03, giving the Breakers a golden opportunity to widen the gap.

Though they didn’t convert on that specific power play, Justice Dick wasn't done. At 5:57 remaining, he struck again, once again assisted by the crafty Joshua Pula. That goal put the nail in the coffin, deflating the Riders' rally. A late trip by Greg Barnes at 7:13 took the wind out of the Riders’ sails, and the Breakers locked down the defensive zone to secure the 3-1 victory.

### The Takeaway
The Ice Breakers keep their perfect streak alive and continue to breathe down the necks of the league leaders. With Joshua Pula extending his point streak and Justice Dick proving to be an absolute menace in the offensive zone, this team is looking like a legitimate powerhouse.

As for the Rohan Riders, it’s back to the drawing board. They played a hard, physical game, but they couldn't overcome the Breakers' persistence in the dirty areas. They’ll need to tighten up the discipline—those two third-period trips proved costly—if they want to jump back into the top three.

The standings are tightening up, and if tonight was any indication, the rest of the season is going to be a wild ride. Keep your sticks on the ice, folks—I’ll see you at the rink!
