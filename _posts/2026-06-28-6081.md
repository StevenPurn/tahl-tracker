# The Outsiders Fossilize the Competition in 5-2 Sunday Morning Showdown

**By: "The Rinkside Reporter"**

If there’s one thing I’ve learned covering this league, it’s that Sunday morning puck drop at 10:15 AM usually separates the real skaters from the ones still dreaming of breakfast burritos. Today at the rink, The Outsiders brought the heat, effectively putting Fossil Hockey on ice with a commanding 5-2 victory that proves they aren't just here to fill out the standings—they’re here to climb them.

### First Period: The Outsiders Set the Pace
The Outsiders wasted absolutely no time getting the blood pumping. JW DePriest opened the books at the 10:42 mark, burying a crisp pass from Kyle Murray to put the early pressure on Fossil Hockey. 

Fossil Hockey’s David Muckala found himself in the sin bin for tripping shortly after, and The Outsiders made them pay. Andrew Lamparter played the role of the ultimate playmaker, setting up Juan Padilla for a rocket that found the back of the net to make it 2-0. 

Fossil Hockey, known for their grit, refused to roll over. Ethan Hilton extended his point streak, finding the twine at 4:27 off a feed from Bryan Simms to cut the lead in half. But the momentum shift was brief; Michael Wixted snatched the wind right out of their sails just 16 seconds later with an unassisted beauty, restoring the two-goal cushion heading into the intermission.

### Second Period: The Padilla Power Hour
The middle frame was all about the chess match between the benches. Padilla was the man of the hour again at 10:25, once again capitalizing on a pinpoint setup from Lamparter to bag his second of the morning. 

The game turned into a bit of a parade to the penalty box, with both squads trading minor infractions. Just when it looked like the period might coast to a finish, Tyler Waller put on a clinic. With just 21.5 seconds left on the clock, Waller found the back of the net off a sweet dish from Logan Pardue, sending The Outsiders into the final frame with a comfortable 5-1 lead.

Fossil Hockey’s Joe Baker managed to light the lamp at 4:44 of the second, courtesy of a Dylan Calhoun helper, but the Fossil attack just couldn’t navigate the defensive traffic jam The Outsiders set up in front of the crease.

### Third Period: Defensive Lockdown
The final period was a masterclass in "defensive posture." With the game well in hand, The Outsiders prioritized heavy legs and clean exits. Fossil Hockey kept pressing—especially after Timothy Willis took an interference minor—but the defensive wall held firm. The clock ticked down, and by the time the final buzzer echoed through the rafters, the scoreboard told the story: a dominant 5-2 performance for The Outsiders.

### The Big Picture
With this win, The Outsiders (4-2-0) make a statement that they are legitimate contenders in the upper crust of the standings, leapfrogging teams and looking to challenge the leaders. Fossil Hockey (3-2-0) sees their momentum stutter after a tough morning, but with a roster that has shown it can score in bunches, don’t expect them to stay down for long.

The ice was cold, the coffee was likely lukewarm, but the hockey? Absolutely scorching. We’ll see you at the rink next week—don't forget your mouthguard.
