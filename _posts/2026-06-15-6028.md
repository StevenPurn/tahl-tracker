# Havok Rocks the Barn: A 4-3 Shootout Thriller Against the Puckheads

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
*June 14, 2026*

If you weren’t at the arena tonight, I’m sorry to report that you missed an absolute barn burner. On a night where the humidity outside was high, the temperature inside the rink was even hotter as Havok and the Puckheads went toe-to-toe in a clash of titans that lived up to every bit of the pre-game hype. When the dust settled, it was Havok walking away with the W, ending the Puckheads' undefeated start in a 4-3 shootout nail-biter.

### Period 1: The Puckheads Draw First Blood
The game opened with the intensity of a playoff final. Both squads were testing the pipes, but it was the Puckheads who broke the ice at 9:47 of the first. **Andrew Retkowski** found some daylight, burying an unassisted beauty to put the Puckheads up 1-0. It was the kind of goal that reminded you why these guys are sitting near the top of the standings—clinical, quick, and opportunistic.

### Period 2: The Havok Avalanche
If the first period was a tactical chess match, the second was a back-alley brawl. Havok woke up with a vengeance, flipping the script entirely. 

It started with a gorgeous tic-tac-toe sequence at the 19:00 mark (rounding into the break), as **Logan Singleton** cleaned up a dish from Michael Bergstrom and Michael Henry to knot it up. Havok didn't let off the gas, and **Zachary Wade**—keeping his impressive three-game point streak alive—sniped one home at 3:16 to give Havok their first lead of the night.

Just seconds later, at 45.9, **Singleton** struck again for his second of the night, sending the Havok bench into a frenzy. But hold your horses—the Puckheads aren't second in the standings by accident. **William Hofmeister** decided he wanted in on the action, scoring at 5:10 and again at 5:23, courtesy of Jamie Halverson, to tie the game at 3-3 before the horn sounded. Talk about a momentum swing!

### Period 3: Penalty Boxes and High Blood Pressure
The third period was a masterclass in defensive grit. With the game deadlocked, both teams tightened their belts. Emil Hinkefent caught a bit too much skate at 14:33 for a tripping call, and when Cameron Sartin returned the favor for the Puckheads at 2:59, the tension was thick enough to cut with a skate blade. Both goaltenders stood on their heads, turning away every bid and refusing to let a single puck cross the paint. We were headed to the shootout.

### The Shootout: Purn Puts It Away
With the points on the line, the stage was set for a hero. After a defensive standoff, it was **Steven Purn** who stepped up to the plate. With the cool confidence of a guy who’s been here a thousand times, Purn snapped a shot past the Puckheads' keeper to seal the deal. 4-3, Havok takes the barn!

### Standing Tall
Tonight’s result is a massive shake-up in the standings. Havok proves that their #3 ranking is a massive understatement—they are a legitimate threat to leapfrog the competition. For the Puckheads, it’s a tough pill to swallow, but taking a point out of a shootout loss against a hungry Havok squad keeps them right in the thick of the title hunt. 

The standings are tightening up, the ice is drying, and the race to the top has never looked more interesting. Stick with us—this season is just starting to boil over!
