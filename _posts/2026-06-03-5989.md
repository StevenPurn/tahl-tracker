# Mooseknuckles Muscle Past Knights in a Mid-Summer Barn Burner

**TULSA** – If you were looking for a reason to put down the remote and head to the rink this Tuesday, June 2nd, the 9:00 PM tilt between the Knights and the Mooseknuckles was the tonic you needed. In a game that saw more grit than a sandpaper factory, the Mooseknuckles finally found their groove, snapping their skid and handing the Knights a 4-2 defeat in a game that felt like a playoff atmosphere.

### The Opening Act: A Chess Match on Ice
The first period was a defensive standoff that could’ve been sponsored by the local bricklayers union. Both goaltenders were putting on a clinic, turning away high-danger chances and keeping the crease as clean as a whistle. It was a classic "who’s going to blink first" scenario, with both teams trading heavy hits along the boards and clogged lanes making life miserable for the forwards.

### Period Two: The Floodgates Open
If the first period was a tactical stalemate, the second was a sheer offensive explosion from the boys in the Mooseknuckles jerseys. 

The dam finally broke at the 2:25 mark when **JEFFREY KLEEHAMMER** continued his hot hand, burying a beautiful feed from Garrett Williams and Michael Randall to light the lamp. The goal clearly gave the Mooseknuckles a shot of adrenaline. Less than two minutes later, at 4:16, **Scott Bartkus** doubled the lead, roofing one top-shelf where mom keeps the cookies, assisted by Jasen Lawwill and Donny Griffin.

By the time **Jess Herd** found the twine at 8:29 off a silky-smooth pass from Pete Fleming, the Knights were reeling. The Mooseknuckles were skating like they were shot out of a cannon, effectively silencing the Knights' bench and carrying a comfortable 3-0 cushion into the final frame.

### Period Three: A Late Rally Runs Out of Runway
Down by three, the Knights decided it was time to wake up. They started generating some serious traffic in front of the net, finally rewarding their patience at 11:28 when **Erik Chillingworth** hammered home a helper from Travis Miller and Thomas Aurilio. 

The momentum shifted palpable. Just moments later, at 1:59, **Joshua Gonya** turned on the jets, going coast-to-coast for an unassisted beauty that brought the Knights within a single goal. The crowd was on their feet, sensing a miracle comeback, but the Mooseknuckles had one last trick up their sleeve. 

With the Knights pressing for the equalizer, **Bradley Fidler** played the role of the spoiler. At 6:09, he connected with Jess Herd to deliver the final dagger, pushing the lead back to two and effectively slamming the door on the Knights' comeback bid. 

### The Aftermath
It wasn’t just a win; it was a statement for the Mooseknuckles, who move forward with a much-needed morale boost after their recent struggles. For the Knights, the loss represents a tough pill to swallow—they fought hard to crawl back, but they simply left their rally too late in the game to overcome the Mooseknuckles' middle-frame blitz.

As we look at the standings, the league remains a shark tank. The Mooseknuckles have proven that when they control the tempo in the second period, they’re a force to be reckoned with. Meanwhile, the Knights will need to tighten the screws and find a way to start their engines before the second period if they hope to climb out of the basement.
