# Undefeated and Unstoppable: Mighty Drunks Keep the Streak Alive in Barn-Burner Against Joes

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: July 2, 2026**

If you wanted to see a masterclass in how to stay perfect in a high-stakes league, the 8:30 PM tilt at the arena was the only place to be. The league-leading Mighty Drunks (6-0-0) faced off against the hungry Average Joes (#4), and while the Joes showed plenty of grit, the Drunks proved exactly why they are currently the boogeyman of this division, skating away with a 6-3 victory.

### First Period: The Joes Strike Early
The game started with the intensity of a playoff final. The Joes, looking to hand the Drunks their first loss of the season, came out buzzing. At the 2:43 mark, **Aidan Caldron** showed exactly why he’s on a scoring heater, finding the back of the net unassisted to put the Joes up 1-0. The Drunks looked momentarily stunned, but as they say in this business, you don't stay 5-0 by panicking. The goalies stood tall for the remainder of the period, turning away quality chances and keeping the crowd on the edge of their seats.

### Second Period: The Drunk Avalanche
If the first period was a tactical chess match, the second was a full-blown street fight. The Drunks kicked down the door at the 13:20 mark when **Nathan Fahland** hammered one home off a sweet dish from Remigiusz Ferenc. 

Once the dam broke, it stayed broken. **Cian Slattery** capitalized on an Omar Awad feed with just 36 seconds left on the clock in the second stanza, but before that, **Daron Ross** and the irrepressible **Tyler Waller** had already padded the lead. Waller’s goal, coming off another beauty assist from Ferenc, extended his personal scoring streak to a ridiculous six games. By the time the buzzer sounded for the second intermission, the Drunks had flipped the script, heading into the locker room with a commanding lead and all the momentum.

### Third Period: Trading Haymakers
The final frame saw the Joes attempt a desperate rally. Aidan Caldron notched his second of the night at the 10:04 mark, trying to spark the comeback, but Daron Ross responded with his second goal of the night just minutes later to dampen the mood.

The Joes caught a break at 6:46 when JW DePriest drew a tripping call. Playing short-handed, the Joes actually managed to find the twine via a **Tracy Wilmes** tally, assisted by the ever-reliable Ryan Martinez. It was a gutsy play, but the Drunks had the final say. With just over a minute left, **Connor Slattery** put the icing on the cake, burying a pass from James Blythe III and Jared Hallahan to seal the 6-3 final.

### The Aftermath
For the Mighty Drunks, this win is a statement. They aren't just winning; they are overwhelming opponents with depth and discipline. Moving to 6-0-0, they sit firmly atop the throne, looking down at the rest of the league. 

For the Average Joes, it’s a tough pill to swallow, but certainly not the end of the road. Despite the loss, the performance of Aidan Caldron—who continues to play at an elite level—remains a bright spot. The Joes remain a dangerous club in that fourth-place slot, but tonight, the Mighty Drunks proved that even when you bring your A-game, sometimes, you just run into a buzzsaw. 

The league rolls on, but make no mistake: the road to the championship runs straight through the Mighty Drunks.
