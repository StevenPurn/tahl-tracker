# Bison Stampede: Tulsa Clinches Championship Thriller in Shootout

The ice at the arena didn’t just melt tonight; it underwent a full-blown existential crisis. In a championship clash for the ages, the Tulsa Bison secured the crown in a heart-stopping 3-2 victory over the Berzerkers, proving once and for all that a herd of Bison is significantly harder to stop than a group of people who simply decided to call themselves "Berzerkers."

The game opened with the intensity of a toaster dropped in a bathtub. The Berzerkers drew first blood at 6:24 of the first period when Stephen Jeffery found the back of the net off a sweet dish from Walter Foddis. It was the kind of goal that made you stand up and spill your popcorn, but the Bison—a team with a winning streak longer than a CVS receipt—weren't about to roll over. JUAN PADILLA continued his scorching heater of a season at 9:53, knotting the score at 1-1 and sending the Bison faithful into a frenzy.

The second period turned into an absolute barn-burner. Zac Hughes showcased his surgical precision for the Berzerkers at 5:28, sniping an unassisted goal to reclaim the lead. But the momentum swung like a pendulum in a hurricane just moments later. At 7:00, DAVID JOSIAH GUAJARDO channeled his inner hockey wizard to equalize for Tulsa, all while Hayden Wheeler was sent to the sin bin for a tripping infraction.

"It was like trying to hold back a tidal wave with a screen door," joked one anonymous Berzerkers staffer while looking at the scoreboard. "We brought the intensity, but the Bison were playing like they had a dinner reservation at the Stanley Cup."

The third period became a masterclass in tension, though neither side could break the 2-2 deadlock. With the Berzerkers struggling to stay disciplined, including another penalty on Hayden Wheeler, and a high-sticking call against the Bison’s SPENCER SHEETS, the game devolved into a high-stakes chess match played with sticks and razor blades. 

With regulation time failing to separate these titans, the game moved to a shootout. With the championship trophy hovering in the chilly arena air, it was the irrepressible Brett Mooney who stepped up. Mooney, currently riding a point streak that has basically turned him into a local folk hero, buried his shot with the cold-blooded efficiency of a tax auditor. That singular strike was all the Bison needed to seal the 3-2 win.

Tonight’s result puts a perfect exclamation point on a dominant season for the Tulsa Bison. Entering the game as the top seed with an unblemished winning streak, they didn't just meet expectations—they skated right over them. For the Berzerkers, it was a valiant effort that proved they belong in the upper echelon of the league, even if they’ll be waking up tomorrow with nothing but memories and a slightly dented pride. As the Bison lift the trophy, the rest of the league is left with one clear takeaway: you can try to cage the Bison, but you’re probably going to end up needing a new goalie.
