# A Sunday Morning Slay: The Mafia Edges Out The Rookery in 6-5 Nail-Biter

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: June 14, 2026**

If you needed a shot of espresso to wake up this Sunday morning, you didn’t need a coffee shop—you just needed to be at the arena for the 10:15 AM puck drop between The Mafia and The Rookery. It was a classic "barn burner" that had the glass rattling and the fans on the edge of their seats from the opening face-off.

When the dust settled on this 6-5 thriller, The Mafia had successfully defended their turf, moving to a pristine 3-0-0 record and proving exactly why they are the team to watch this season.

### First Period: Discipline and Determination
The Rookery came out with fire in their eyes, despite finding themselves in a bit of a parade to the penalty box early on. Travis Shuck, #88, and company were dinged for a string of infractions, forcing the Rookery’s PK unit to work harder than a Zamboni driver on a double shift. 

Despite the man-disadvantage, The Rookery’s #44 struck first at 7:31, silencing the home crowd. But The Mafia isn't a team that stays down for long. Daron Ross, riding a hot streak you could see from the parking lot, answered back at 9:20 with an unassisted beauty to knot it at 1-1 heading into the first intermission.

### Second Period: The Ross & Lamb Show
The middle frame belonged to The Mafia. Brett Lamb, keeping his scoring streak alive, buried a feeder from Tyler Waller at 10:28 to take the lead. Daron Ross doubled down with his second of the game just before the buzzer, showing off some silky mitts to put The Mafia up 3-1.

But just as The Mafia looked to be pulling away, David Hardy found himself in the box for tripping, and The Rookery capitalized. The inevitable #44 struck again on the power play, closing the gap to 3-2. Benjamin Koelsch wasn't about to let the momentum swing too far, however, immediately answering at 8:09 to restore the two-goal cushion heading into the final frame. 

### Third Period: The Wild West
If you think the first two periods were exciting, the third was absolute carnage. Scott Hellard buried a feed from Ross to make it 5-2, but The Rookery showed the heart of a champion. They fought back with a goal from #95, followed quickly by a Brett Lamb response that seemed to seal the deal at 6-3.

But don’t turn the channel just yet! With the clock winding down, The Rookery’s #72 caught the Mafia napping to make it 6-4, and that relentless #44—who seemed to be everywhere—completed a hat-trick performance at 9:12 to pull the visitors within one. The final minutes were pure chaos, with sticks flying, bodies checking, and goalies making desperation saves to keep the puck out of the blue paint.

When the final horn sounded, The Mafia held on for a 6-5 victory in what was quite possibly the most entertaining game of the season so far.

### The Takeaway
The Mafia remains undefeated, sitting comfortably at the top of the heap. They showed today that even when they’re trading paint and playing shorthanded, they have the depth to finish. 

For The Rookery, the loss is a bitter pill, especially with such a heroic effort from their #44. While they sit at 1-2-0, their high-octane offense proves they can hang with the big dogs. They’ll look to bounce back, but for today, the bragging rights belong to The Mafia.

**Three Stars of the Game:**
1. **Daron Ross (Mafia):** A point-producing machine.
2. **#44 (Rookery):** A hat-trick hero who nearly dragged his team to a comeback.
3. **Brett Lamb (Mafia):** Two goals, pure grit, and a constant threat in the slot.
