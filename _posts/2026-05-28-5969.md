# A Barn Burner for the Ages: Goats and Havok Trade Blows in 5-5 Deadlock

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: May 27, 2026**

If you weren’t at the arena tonight, you missed a classic. The 9:30 PM puck drop between the Killer Goats and Havok didn’t just turn into a late-night affair; it turned into an absolute rock fight that left the ice scarred and the fans breathless. When the final buzzer echoed through the rafters, the scoreboard read 5-5, a fair—if heart-stopping—conclusion to a game that felt like a heavyweight title fight where neither side was willing to blink.

### First Period: The Seesaw Showdown
The Goats came out of the gate with their horns sharpened. Michael Doyle drew first blood early, setting the tone at the 14:28 mark. Just before the intermission horn could blow, Nelson Pina doubled the lead, burying one to send the Goats into the locker room with all the momentum.

But Havok? They don’t go down without a scrap. In a whirlwind 16-second span midway through the frame, Emil Hinkefent and Logan Singleton turned the game on its head. Steven Purn was the architect behind both, showing the vision of a man who sees the play three steps before it happens. By the time Travis Miller headed to the box for hooking, the game had transformed from a Goats clinic into a certified barn burner.

### Second Period: The Tactical Tussle
If the first period was a track meet, the second was a trench war. Both teams tightened up their defensive shells, clogging the lanes and making the neutral zone look like a highway at rush hour. 

Havok managed to break the stalemate at 8:14 when Michael Henry found the back of the net off a sweet feed from Robert McWhirt and Zachary Wade. Both goalies settled into a groove here, turning aside chances with high-danger saves that had the benches buzzing. It was a period of grit, grime, and desperation hockey.

### Third Period: The Scoring Frenzy
Buckle up, because the third period was absolute mayhem. The Goats roared back with a vengeance, stringing together three goals in a blistering flurry. Dylan Criquet Danielson started the fire, and Travis Miller—looking to make amends for his earlier trip to the sin bin—found the twine twice, turning a deficit into a two-goal cushion for the Goats.

Just when you thought the Goats had the game tucked away "top shelf where mom keeps the cookies," Emil Hinkefent decided he had other plans. The Havok sniper put his team on his back, lighting the lamp twice in under three minutes to secure the hat trick and pull Havok even at 5-5. The final minutes were pure chaos, with both teams trading rush-for-rush action until the clock finally ran dry.

### The Aftermath
At the end of the night, both the Killer Goats and Havok walk away with a single point to show for their efforts. For the Goats, the late collapse will sting, but they’ll be encouraged by the offensive chemistry between Doyle and Criquet Danielson. For Havok, they’ll celebrate the resilience of a team that simply refuses to stay down.

Both teams entered tonight riding winning streaks, and after tonight’s draw, it’s clear why. Neither side looked like they deserved a loss. As we look at the standings, this result keeps things nice and tight at the top of the heap. If these two meet again in the postseason, you’d be wise to clear your schedule—because tonight proved that when the Goats and Havok clash, the only guarantee is high-octane hockey.
