# Eagles Soar, Garbage Scraps: Lite Beer Eagles Edge Out Hot Garbage in Late-Night Thriller

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: June 18, 2026**

If you weren’t at the rink last night, you missed a classic. While most of the city was hitting the hay, the Lite Beer Eagles and Hot Garbage were busy turning the ice into a pressure cooker. When the dust settled at 10:30 PM, it was the Eagles walking away with a 6-5 victory in a game that had everything: silky mitts, chippy play, and a shootout finish that had the few remaining fans in the stands breathless.

### The First: A Toe-to-Toe Tussle
The Eagles wasted no time letting their presence be known. Just 1:44 into the contest, Jonathan McCormick danced through the zone like he had a map of the defense’s nightmares, burying an unassisted beauty to draw first blood. 

Hot Garbage didn't fold, though. Even after Aaron Whisner found himself cooling his heels in the sin bin for a hooking minor at 8:15, the momentum shifted. Whisner, fresh out of the box, turned playmaker into goal-scorer at 9:19, burying a feed from Nicholas Brewer to knot things up at 1-1.

### The Second: Eagles Take Flight
The middle frame turned into a game of "anything you can do, I can do better." McCormick struck again at 11:12, continuing his torrid point streak and reclaiming the lead for the Eagles. But Hot Garbage’s Joe Baker fired back at 13:54, refusing to let the game slip away. 

The turning point? A tripping call on Alan Dyke gave the Eagles a man-advantage, and they capitalized. Ethan Hilton, fresh off a momentum-building performance, ripped one home off a lovely dish from Kevin Bailey-McGinnis at the 8:31 mark. It was hard-nosed hockey, with both goaltenders seeing a flurry of rubber as the physicality ramped up.

### The Third: Total Chaos
If the first two periods were a chess match, the third was a full-blown bar fight. Charles Webber extended the Eagles' lead early, but Abigail Nisbet sparked a spirited response for Hot Garbage just 34 seconds later. 

McCormick completed his impressive outing by netting his hat-trick goal at 12:45, but Hot Garbage wasn't going down without a fight. Nicholas Brewer put on an absolute clinic in the final minutes, scoring at the 52.8-second mark and again at 6:52 to force a tie. With penalties piling up—including a double-roughing/tripping kerfuffle at 1:02—the game devolved into a desperate, back-and-forth scramble.

### The Shootout: Ice in the Veins
Regulation ended knotted at 5-5, sending the game to the ultimate pressure test. With the tension thick enough to cut with a skate blade, Charles Webber stepped up for the Eagles. He didn't blink, roofing a shot top-shelf where mom keeps the cookies, effectively slamming the door shut on Hot Garbage.

### The Aftermath
It was a gut-check win for the Lite Beer Eagles (2-2-0), who proved that even in the bottom half of the pack, they have the grit to topple anyone on a given night. For Hot Garbage (1-2-0), it’s a tough pill to swallow after a resilient comeback, but the offensive production from Brewer and Whisner suggests they’ll be a thorn in the side of the league’s top teams all season.

As we head into the next slate of games, the standings remain a logjam, but one thing is clear: don't sleep on the late-night action. This league is officially wide open.
