# Bison Stampede: Tulsa Overpowers Fossil Hockey in Late-Night Barn Burner

**TULSA** – If you were looking for a reason to stay up past your bedtime this past Sunday, the ice at the local rink provided exactly the kind of fireworks that make this game so addictive. In a mid-table clash that felt like a heavyweight prize fight, the Tulsa Bison (3-2-0) pulled off a gritty 5-3 victory over the previously unbeaten Fossil Hockey (3-1-0).

The air was thick with tension as the puck dropped at 8:45 PM, and by the time the final horn sounded, the Bison had successfully navigated the Fossil defense to move up the standings, proving that their record is far more impressive than the stat sheet suggests.

### Period 1: The Back-and-Forth Battle
The Bison came out with plenty of vinegar, drawing an early roughing penalty against Fossil’s John White just 14 seconds in. However, it was Fossil Hockey who struck first. At 11:06, Kelly Rundle set up Jose Medina for the icebreaker. 

Tulsa didn’t hang their heads, though. JW DePriest, arguably the Bison’s heartbeat tonight, found the back of the net off a sweet dish from Joe Baker. But Fossil wasn’t ready to fold. Michael Hilton, looking to keep his point streak alive, hammered one home late in the frame. Just as it looked like the Bison might head to the locker room trailing, Brent Brown played hero, redirecting a feed from DePriest and Brett Mecham to knot it up at 2-2 heading into the break.

### Period 2: Special Teams Chaos
The second period was a gritty, grinding affair defined by trips to the sin bin. Fossil reclaimed the lead early when Robert Norris buried a Timothy Willis pass, but the Fossil momentum was soon derailed by a parade to the penalty box.

With Jose Medina serving a tripping minor, the Bison went to work. Juan Padilla—making it five games in a row finding the twine—sniped a beauty off a setup from Justin Phillips to tie the game at 3-3. The Fossil discipline crumbled shortly after, with back-to-back penalties to Ethan Peterson and Ethan Hilton. While they killed off the damage, the Bison had clearly seized the psychological momentum.

### Period 3: Closing the Barn Door
The final frame was all Bison. The defensive corps tightened the screws, forcing Fossil to scramble for scraps. At 11:11, Justin Phillips found the sweet spot to put Tulsa ahead for the first time all night. 

With the Fossil bench pressing for the equalizer, the lanes closed up tighter than a rusted locker room hinge. As the clock wound down to the final minute, JW DePriest iced the cake with an unassisted empty-netter (or a clincher on the rush—either way, it was pure finesse) with just 49.8 seconds remaining. 

### The Bottom Line
For the Bison, this is a massive statement win. Moving to 3-2-0, Tulsa is officially shedding their "middle-of-the-pack" label and knocking on the door of the league's elite. 

For Fossil Hockey, the first loss of the season is a bitter pill, but one they’ll need to swallow quickly. They remain a formidable force, but tonight, they learned the hard way that when the Bison start building "traffic in front of the net," there isn't a goalie in this league who can track the puck through the chaos.

The Bison now look to carry this momentum forward, while Fossil will undoubtedly be looking to hit the reset button after a night of undisciplined play cost them their perfect start. One thing is certain: if tonight’s tilt was any indication, the rest of this season is going to be one wild ride.
