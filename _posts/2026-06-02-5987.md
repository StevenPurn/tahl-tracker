# Rabara’s Double-Tap Dagger: Valkyries Ground the Power Puck Girls in Friday Night Shutout

**By: "Rinkside" Rick Henderson**
*June 1st, 2026*

There’s nothing quite like the smell of fresh ice and the sound of steel biting into the sheet on a Saturday night. The energy at the arena tonight was palpable, but as the dust settled after a hard-hitting 48 minutes, one thing was clear: The Valkyries aren't just here to participate; they’re here to climb the mountain. In a display of tactical discipline and opportunistic finishing, The Valkyries skated away with a 2-0 victory over the Power Puck Girls, putting the rest of the league on notice.

### Period 1: The Icebreaker
The game opened with the intensity of a playoff tilt. The Power Puck Girls came out with heavy skates, looking to silence the critics, but The Valkyries’ defense was playing like a brick wall in a blizzard. 

The breakthrough finally arrived in the dying seconds of the frame. With just 37.5 seconds left on the clock, **Theresa Rose Rabara** found a pocket of soft ice in the high slot. After a crisp feed from **Haley Griffin**, Rabara didn't hesitate, snapping a quick release that whistled past the keeper’s ear. It was the kind of goal that takes the air out of the opponent's tires just as they’re heading to the locker room. 1-0 Valkyries.

### Period 2: The Double-Tap
The second period turned into a classic game of chess. The Power Puck Girls began creating heavy "traffic in front of the net," trying to screen the Valkyries' goalie and fish for rebounds, but the Valkyries' defensive core was clearing the house with authority. 

Just when the Power Puck Girls started to tilt the ice in their favor, the Valkyries struck again. With only 58.7 seconds remaining in the game—a heartbreaker for the Girls—**Barbara Lepe Briones** threaded a needle to Rabara, who tucked her second of the night into the twine. It was a textbook finish, proving once again that if you give Rabara an inch, she’ll take a mile—and the two points that come with it.

### The Backstop Battle
While the scoreboard shows two goals, the real story tonight was the goaltending. Both squads saw stellar efforts between the pipes. The Valkyries' netminder was a vacuum tonight, gobbling up every shot that dared to drift toward the blue paint. Despite the mounting pressure from the Power Puck Girls in the final period, the Valkyries' goalie stood tall, turning away every challenge to secure the clean sheet.

### Standing Tall
With this win, The Valkyries improve to a sparkling 2-0-0 record, firmly cementing their status as a team to fear in the early season standings. They’ve got the momentum, the chemistry, and tonight, they had the finish. 

For the Power Puck Girls, it’s back to the drawing board. They showed grit, but they’ll need to find a way to turn that possession into production if they want to climb out of the basement. As for The Valkyries, they’ll enjoy this one, but with this league, there’s no time to celebrate—the next barn burner is always right around the corner.
