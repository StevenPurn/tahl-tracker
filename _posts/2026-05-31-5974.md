# A Morning Meltdown: Murder Edges Out The Bone Zone in 5-4 Thriller

**TULSA** – If you were hoping to sleep in on this fine May 31st morning, you chose the wrong day. The ice at the local barn was absolutely humming at 9:45 a.m. as the Tulsa Murder and The Bone Zone collided in what can only be described as a greasy, grind-it-out, heart-stopper of a hockey game. 

When the final buzzer sounded, it was the Tulsa Murder skating off with a 5-4 victory, but let me tell you—my heart rate is still trying to recover from the third-period chaos.

### Period 1: Setting the Tempo
The Murder didn't wait for their morning coffee to kick in. Just over five minutes into the tilt, **Spencer Charles** decided he’d seen enough of the neutral zone, taking matters into his own hands with an unassisted blast to open the scoring. It was a cold bucket of water for The Bone Zone, who spent the rest of the frame clogging the lanes and trying to find their rhythm. They finally cracked the code with just 121 seconds left in the period when **Tyler Wimpee** buried one to knot it up at 1-1 heading into the first intermission.

### Period 2: Trading Paint
The second frame was pure trench warfare. **Pete Fleming** put the Murder back on top early, courtesy of a slick dish from Frank Benavides. But The Bone Zone is a resilient bunch; less than a minute later, **Jonathan Lee** capitalized on a Brian Smith feed to erase the lead. 

The physical intensity began to boil over midway through the second. Jason Bigelow headed to the sin bin for hooking, giving the Zone a chance to breathe, but the Murder’s penalty kill stood tall, keeping the game locked in a stalemate.

### Period 3: Total Pandemonium
If you walked away to grab a water, you missed a crime. The third period was a chaotic masterpiece. 

The Bone Zone took their first lead of the morning at the 10:31 mark, with Wimpee bagging his second of the game. But the Murder, clearly playing with a “never say die” attitude, roared back. Pete Fleming notched his second of the day just a minute later off a feed from Jeffrey Pruitt.

Then, things got spicy. With penalties flying—including a massive 10-minute cross-checking misconduct for the Zone's Jerry Bates—the ice opened up. Fleming completed his hat trick at 1:22 remaining, and Jeffrey Pruitt added what would prove to be the game-winner shortly after, set up by Skylar Miller. 

The Bone Zone pulled their goalie and managed one last gasp with a Garrett Williams goal assisted by Wimpee, but the Murder held the blue line like a fortress in the dying seconds.

### Standing Tall
With this result, the Tulsa Murder move to 2-0-0 on the young season, asserting themselves as the team to beat in this early-season sprint. The Bone Zone, despite falling to 1-1-0, showed they have the grit and the high-end talent—particularly in the duo of Wimpee and Smith—to turn any game into a barn burner.

Both squads are leaving the rink with bruised shins and a lot to think about, but one thing is certain: if these two meet again, clear your schedule. You won't want to miss a second of it.
