# Renegades Run Wild in 13-4 Rout of Oklahoma Precast

If you showed up to the rink expecting a tight, defensive battle between the top two teams in the league, you clearly didn't get the memo from the Renegades tonight. In a performance that can only be described as an offensive masterclass, the Renegades bulldozed their way to a 13-4 victory over Oklahoma Precast, extending their winning streak to a blistering six games and proving exactly why they sit atop the standings.

From the opening puck drop, the Renegades moved like they were shot out of a cannon. Caeleb Burch set the tone early with a beautiful strike at the 11:02 mark of the first, and Matthan Hillberg followed suit shortly after. By the time the horn sounded to end the first, the tone for the night had been firmly established.

But the second period? That was pure, unadulterated chaos—the kind that makes you want to buy a season ticket just for the theatrics. The Renegades went on a scoring tear that had the Oklahoma Precast defense looking like they were trying to solve complex calculus on ice skates. Bobby Watson turned the second period into his personal highlight reel, racking up goal after goal with a tenacity that would make a hungry badger jealous. Between Watson’s offensive flurry and clutch contributions from Dominic Najjar and another heater from Hillberg, the scoreboard operator was getting a serious workout.

"I think we just stopped looking at the scoreboard and started looking for open ice," said one Renegade forward between periods, barely catching his breath. "When the chemistry is flowing like this, it feels like the puck is just a magnet for the back of the net."

To their credit, Oklahoma Precast didn't just pack it in. They clawed back in the third with goals from James Stuckey’s playmaking efforts and Matthew Rankin, but the Renegades simply had an answer for everything. Alex Bateman and Elliott Langford continued their impressive point-scoring streaks, adding to the tally and keeping the pressure relentless until the final buzzer.

Bobby Watson was the undisputed king of the castle tonight, putting on a clinic that left the fans cheering and the Precast goaltenders looking for a stiff drink. With this win, the Renegades cement their dominance, moving to 7-1-1 and showing no signs of slowing down. As for Oklahoma Precast, they’ll look to shake off the dust and regroup after a night where, quite frankly, the Renegades were just playing a different sport entirely.

If you’re a fan of high-octane, "turn the red light on" hockey, this was the game of the season. The Renegades are officially the team to beat—and right now, beating them looks like a tall order.
