# Drunks Drink in the Victory in Tight 2-1 Battle Against Joes

If you like your hockey served with a side of grit and a dash of late-game drama, the ice at the local rink was the place to be on November 22nd. In a classic regular-season clash, the Mighty Drunks edged out the Average Joes in a 2-1 grinder that proved once again that in this league, you don't need a touchdown to have a wild night.

The contest was a masterclass in defensive lockdown, with both squads treating the puck like it was radioactive for the better part of two periods. The Drunks’ Omar Awad, who entered the night riding a four-game point streak, shook off an early trip to the sin bin for roughing to play the role of hero. At the 6:51 mark of the second period, Awad found the back of the net off a crisp feed from Connor Slattery, breaking the scoreless deadlock and finally giving the crowd something to cheer about.

"We knew it was going to be a heavy-hitting affair," Awad said with a grin after the game. "I spent enough time in the box earlier to contemplate my life choices, so I figured I’d better make up for it on the ice."

The third period was where the chaos truly kicked into high gear. With the clock winding down, the Average Joes finally broke the Drunks' resistance at 9:21 when Joshua Hendrickson cleaned up a scramble in front of the net, assisted by Jonathan Bransford and James Caldron. Just when it looked like we might be headed for a nail-biting finish, the Drunks showed why they’re sitting pretty at third in the standings.

With just 14.7 seconds left on the clock, Gage Walker played the role of the ultimate heartbreaker. He managed to bury an unassisted beauty, blowing the roof off the rink and snatching the lead right out from under the Joes' noses.

"I think my heart stopped for a second there," joked a Drunks fan near the glass. "One minute we’re looking at a tie, the next we’re heading home with two points. That’s the magic of this league!"

The game was a whistle-heavy affair, particularly in the third, as the referees kept things tight with a series of penalties, including a brief 5-on-3 situation for the Joes that they couldn't quite capitalize on. The disciplined play—or lack thereof—made for a frantic, end-to-end scramble that kept the goaltenders on their toes all night long.

For the Mighty Drunks, the win keeps their momentum rolling as they continue to solidify their spot in the upper tier of the standings. Meanwhile, the Average Joes fall back to a 4-4-1 record, though they proved they can hang with the league’s best. As the saying goes, it’s not how you start, it’s how you finish—and for the Drunks, that finish was worth every drop of sweat.
