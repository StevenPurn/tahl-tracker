# Goat-Horns and Heartbreak: Killer Goats Extend Their Reign Over Hitmen Silver

**Date:** June 28, 2026 | **Arena:** The Cooler | **Final Score:** Killer Goats 4, Hitmen Silver 2

If you were looking for a Saturday morning wake-up call, you didn’t need a double espresso—you just needed a ticket to this morning's tilt between the division-leading Killer Goats and the hungry Hitmen Silver. In a clash that lived up to the hype, the Goats proved exactly why they’re sitting at the top of the food chain, walking away with a 4-2 victory in a game that felt like a heavyweight title fight on ice.

### First Period: The Goats Set the Pace
The morning started with a bit of "chippy" business. Just 24 seconds into the game, Hitmen Silver’s Brooks Martin found himself cooling his heels in the sin bin for hooking. The Goats, looking to capitalize on their league-leading form, moved the puck with the precision of a Swiss watch. 

The pressure paid off at 7:41 when **Joshua Gonya** finally solved the Hitmen netminder. With a gorgeous feed from Anthony Ceci, Gonya buried one to put the Goats on the board. The Hitmen spent the rest of the period trying to find their rhythm, but the Goats’ defensive wall was as solid as a frozen pond in January.

### Second Period: The Trading of Leather
The second frame saw the intensity kick up a notch. The Goats struck first when **Dylan Criquet-Danielson**—who clearly didn't eat his Wheaties for the sake of the Hitmen’s morale—found the back of the net off a beauty of an assist from Garreth Lewis. 

Down 2-0, the Hitmen finally woke up. At 4:03, **Bobby Pickett** proved why he’s on a heater, cleaning up the garbage in front of the net to put the Silver on the board. The crowd roared, the momentum swung, and for a few minutes, it felt like we were headed for a real barn burner. The goalies were the stars here, making several sprawling saves to keep the deficit at just one heading into the final stanza.

### Third Period: The Separation
The final twenty minutes were all about the Goats digging in. Just over seven minutes in, **Dylan Criquet-Danielson** struck again for his second of the day, showing the kind of silky mitts that keep goalies awake at night. 

But these Hitmen aren't the type to fold their laundry early. They kept grinding, putting plenty of traffic in front of the net to make the Goats work for every square inch of ice. However, the Goats' depth proved too much. **Michael Doyle** hammered home a pass from Samuel Michaud and Brian Smith to make it a 4-1 game. 

Pickett tried to stage a late-game miracle with his second goal of the afternoon at the 8:06 mark, courtesy of another Michael Bergstrom setup, but the clock was the Hitmen's biggest enemy. Despite a frantic final push, the Goats slammed the gate shut.

### The Aftermath
With this 4-2 victory, the **Killer Goats** firmly cement their status as the kings of the mountain, extending their lead in the standings and proving that their 3-0-1 record wasn't just a fluke of the schedule. They are playing a disciplined, high-octane brand of hockey that is going to be a nightmare for any team looking to climb into the top spot.

For **Hitmen Silver**, the loss stings, but there’s no shame in losing to the league leaders in a hard-fought battle. They showed heart, and with Bobby Pickett’s continued goal-scoring streak, they’ve got the firepower to bounce back. 

The league table looks a lot clearer tonight, folks. The Goats are moving fast, and the rest of the pack better start skating harder if they want to catch them.

**Three Stars of the Game:**
1. **Dylan Criquet-Danielson (Goats):** Two goals, pure offensive instinct.
2. **Bobby Pickett (Hitmen):** A gritty two-goal effort to keep his squad in it.
3. **The Goats' Defensive Core:** Kept the Hitmen to the outside and silenced a dangerous offense when it mattered most.
