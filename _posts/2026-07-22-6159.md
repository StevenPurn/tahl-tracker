# A One-Sided Shellacking: Mighty Drunks Put on a Skating Clinic Against The Rookery

**By: "Hat-Trick" Harry, Rinkside Reporter**
**July 21, 2026**

If you were hoping for a nail-biter down at the rink this Tuesday night, you were likely in the wrong building. The Mighty Drunks, currently sitting pretty at the top of the standings, reminded everyone exactly why they’re the team to beat. In a game that can only be described as a total dismantling, the Drunks walked all over The Rookery with a commanding 9-0 shutout victory.

### First Period: The Floodgates Open
The Mighty Drunks didn’t just come out firing; they came out with a flamethrower. Remigiusz Ferenc drew first blood at 12:13, and from that moment on, it felt like the Drunks were playing keep-away. 

Omar Awad, who clearly ate his Wheaties this morning, kicked off a monstrous night at 13:54 off a sweet dish from Connor Slattery. Before the period could draw to a close, Awad doubled his pleasure at 2:56, followed by James Blythe III hammering one home at 5:01. By the time the horn sounded for the first intermission, the Drunks had built a 4-0 cushion that left The Rookery looking like they were trying to skate through quicksand.

### Second Period: The Slattery Connection
If you’re a fan of puck movement, the second period was pure art. The Slattery brothers—Cian and Connor—put on a masterclass in chemistry. Cian found the back of the net at 11:43, and just 15 seconds later, Connor returned the favor, burying a goal off a beautiful feed from both Omar Awad and his brother. 

The Rookery’s defense was effectively non-existent, leaving their netminder out to dry against an relentless offensive barrage. Awad capped off his spectacular evening with his third goal of the game at the 3:00 mark, completing a natural-style hat trick that left the rafters shaking. 

### Third Period: The Finishing Touches
With a 7-0 lead heading into the final frame, most teams would have shifted into cruise control. Not the Drunks. Thomas A. Wolff kept the pressure high, cleaning up a rebound from a James Blythe III and Tyler Waller setup at 1:49. Waller then added the final exclamation point with an unassisted laser at 7:51 to make it 9-0.

Special mention goes to the Drunks' defensive corps and goaltending tandem, who turned away every desperate attempt from The Rookery. It was a clean sheet, a shutout, and a statement: "We own this league."

### The Standings Shake-Up
With this win, the Mighty Drunks (7-0-1) extend their lead at the top of the pile, moving to 15 points. They are a runaway freight train, showing no signs of slowing down as they steamroll through the mid-season.

For The Rookery (1-6-0), this is a bitter pill to swallow. They remain stagnant at 2 points, firmly entrenched at the bottom of the table. They’ll need to do some serious soul-searching in the locker room before their next tilt if they want to stop the bleeding. 

As for the Drunks? Keep an eye on those individual streaks. With this kind of offensive output, the scoreboard operators might want to take a nap—because if the Drunks keep playing like this, they’re going to be busy all season long.
