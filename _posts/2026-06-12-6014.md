# A Drunken Masterclass: Mighty Drunks Steamroll The Rookery 11-1

**By: "Slap-Shot" Sammy, Hometown Beat Reporter**

**JUNE 11, 2026** — They say you can’t drink on the job, but someone forgot to tell the Mighty Drunks. Under the lights of a humid Thursday night, the league leaders took to the ice with one mission: to prove why they’re sitting pretty at the top of the standings. What transpired over three periods was less of a hockey game and more of a total dismantling. The final score, a lopsided 11-1 in favor of the Drunks, left the Rookery looking like they’d just tried to stop a freight train with a pool noodle.

### Period 1: The Writing on the Wall
The Drunks didn’t just come out of the gate; they kicked the door off its hinges. Cian Slattery got the party started early, burying a beauty off an Omar Awad feed just 1:36 into the game. From there, the Drunks turned the offensive zone into their own personal playground. 

The Rookery’s defense was caught napping on a few odd-man rushes, and before the crowd could even settle into their seats, Connor Slattery and Jared Hallahan had added two more. By the end of the first, it was 3-0, and the writing was already on the wall: this was going to be a long night for the Rookery faithful.

### Period 2: The Dam Breaks
If the first period was a light sprinkle, the second was a monsoon. The Drunks came out relentless. Tyler Waller—who is playing like a man possessed this season—extended his point streak with a clinical finish at 4:57. Not to be outdone, Awad and Matthew Holland tacked on two more in rapid succession, pushing the lead to 6-0.

The Rookery finally found a pulse midway through the frame when James Caldron managed to solve the Drunks' defensive wall, burying a helper from Travis Shuck. It was a nice bit of individual effort, but for the Rookery, it felt like bringing a squirt gun to a house fire.

### Period 3: The Drunks Keep Pouring
Leading 6-1 entering the final frame, most teams might shift into cruise control. Not the Mighty Drunks. They treated the third period like it was scoreless. 

Remigiusz Ferenc started the final barrage, followed by a highlight-reel tic-tac-toe play from Waller and Hallahan that sent the puck top-shelf where mom keeps the cookies. Hallahan returned the favor to Waller minutes later, and Cian Slattery added insult to injury with his second of the night. Connor Slattery capped off the carnage at the 13:19 mark of the period, rounding out the double-digit scoreline.

### The Aftermath
For the Mighty Drunks, this 11-1 statement win is more than just two points—it’s a declaration. At 3-0-0, they aren't just sitting in first place; they’re sitting on a throne. With players like Waller, Hallahan, and the Slattery brothers all clicking in perfect harmony, the rest of the league has to be looking over their shoulders. 

As for the Rookery, it’s a night to hit the reset button. They’ve shown grit in the past, but tonight, they simply ran into a buzzsaw. They’ll need to tighten up the traffic in front of their net and get back to basics before they slide further down the standings.

**Three Stars of the Game:**
1. **Tyler Waller (Mighty Drunks):** A multi-goal, multi-assist masterclass.
2. **Jared Hallahan (Mighty Drunks):** The engine that wouldn't quit.
3. **Cian Slattery (Mighty Drunks):** Set the tone early and never looked back.

The league standings are starting to take shape, and one thing is clear: the road to the title currently runs straight through the Mighty Drunks’ locker room. We’ll see you at the rink!
