# One Man's Trash is Another Man's Nightmare: Hot Garbage Stuns The Outsiders

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
*June 24, 2026 – 10:30 PM*

If you walked into the rink tonight expecting The Outsiders to cruise past the underdogs of Hot Garbage, you clearly haven't been watching the way the ice tilts when these squads lace up. In a classic "don't read the standings" thriller, Hot Garbage played spoiler under the late-night lights, grinding out a 3-1 victory that proved they aren't just here to take up space on the scoreboard.

### Period 1: Setting the Tone
The game started with both teams testing the perimeter, but the intensity spiked early. Tyler Holmes of The Outsiders found himself cooling his heels in the sin bin for a trip at 10:42, but Hot Garbage couldn't quite capitalize on the man advantage. 

The dam finally broke at the 6:59 mark. Hot Garbage’s talisman Aaron Whisner, riding a scorching hot streak, kept his rhythm alive by finding the back of the net, assisted by the vision of Joshua Hall and Joe Baker. With momentum firmly in their favor, John Bewley decided one goal wasn't enough to satisfy the hunger. At 9:57, after a beautiful dish from Justin Phillips, Bewley sniped one home to put Hot Garbage up 2-0. Despite a late cross-checking penalty by Tyler Mills, Hot Garbage headed to the locker room looking like a team transformed.

### Period 2: Bewley’s Brilliance
The second period saw The Outsiders trying to claw their way back into the fray, but the Hot Garbage defense was playing with a "thou shalt not pass" mentality. The defensive zone was clogged with more traffic than a rush-hour bridge, forcing The Outsiders to settle for low-percentage shots from the blue line. 

At 5:44, the lead stretched to three. John Bewley struck again, cleaning up a rebound off a Brett Mecham shot to notch his second of the night. It was a greasy, blue-collar goal—the kind that makes a coach’s heart flutter—and it sent a clear message to the bench of The Outsiders: tonight was not going to be their night.

### Period 3: The Outsiders’ Late Pulse
The third period was a desperate scramble. The Outsiders turned up the heat, throwing everything they had at the Hot Garbage crease, but they couldn't find the rhythm to crack the defensive shell until the clock started to get real skinny. 

At 12:57, Ethan Peterson finally found a sliver of daylight, unassisted, to bring The Outsiders within two. But it was a case of "too little, too late." Hot Garbage tightened the screws, played keep-away in the corners, and salted the game away to secure the 3-1 upset.

### The Aftermath
For Hot Garbage, this win is a massive shot in the arm. Entering the night at the bottom half of the table, they’ve proven that their 1-2-0 record didn't tell the whole story. They played with the urgency of a team fighting for their lives, and tonight, they looked like the best squad on the ice.

For The Outsiders, it’s a stinging reality check. Sitting pretty at #4, they had a chance to solidify their spot in the upper echelon, but they left their consistency in the locker room tonight. They’ll need to shake this one off quickly if they want to keep pace with the powerhouses like Gold as Ice.

As the ice resurfacer hits the rink, the message is clear: the league is wide open, and Hot Garbage just turned the standings into a giant pile of questions. Stick taps all around—that was a real barn burner.
