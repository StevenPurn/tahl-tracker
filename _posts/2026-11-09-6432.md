# Eagles Soar Past Ice Breakers in High-Flying Friday Night Tilt

The Lite Beer Eagles took flight at the rink this Friday night, securing a gritty 5-3 victory over the Ice Breakers Plus in a matchup that proved that even in the bottom half of the standings, these teams have plenty of fire in their bellies.

The Eagles came into the 8:45 p.m. puck drop looking to find some consistency, and they started the engine early. Dylan Dillard struck first at the 10:49 mark of the opening period, putting the Eagles on the board with an unassisted effort that silenced the Ice Breakers’ bench. While Edward Stern managed to answer back for the Ice Breakers midway through the period, the Eagles responded like a team on a mission. Jeff Horvath and Ethan Hilton—the latter extending his impressive goal streak to three games—found the back of the net in quick succession to close out the first period with a 3-1 lead.

"We told the boys in the locker room before the game, 'If you want to move up the ladder, you have to win the battles along the boards,'" said one rink-side observer. "The Eagles didn't just win the battles; they basically set up permanent residence there."

The second period turned into a defensive tug-of-war. The Ice Breakers, still hunting for their first win of the season, started to tighten up their gaps. However, Horvath proved to be the thorn in their side all night long. At the 8:32 mark of the second, he tapped in his second goal of the game off a beautiful feed from Kevin Bailey-McGinnis, pushing the lead to 4-1 and breathing a little extra life into the Eagles’ bench. Tempers flared briefly at the 9:35 mark with simultaneous slashing penalties, but the Eagles managed to head into the final frame with a comfortable cushion.

But don’t tell the Ice Breakers that the game is over. They came out for the third period playing like their hair was on fire. Edward Stern notched his second goal of the night early in the third, and Aaron West followed it up just 29 seconds later to narrow the gap to 4-3. For a few nervous minutes, it looked like the Ice Breakers might finally climb out of that winless hole.

Just as the momentum began to tilt, Michael Hilton stepped up to play the hero. With 9:33 left on the clock, he buried a pass from Jeff Horvath to ice the game at 5-3. It was a well-deserved point for Horvath, who finished the night with a monster performance.

"It was a bit of a rollercoaster, but that’s local hockey for you," a fan shouted from the bleachers as the buzzer sounded. "One minute you’re comfortable, the next you’re white-knuckling your cold beverage!"

With the win, the Lite Beer Eagles jump to a 3-4-1 record, officially shaking off their recent inconsistent form and finding a bit of rhythm. For the Ice Breakers Plus, it’s a tough result to swallow, but they showed heart mounting that third-period surge. The Eagles will look to carry this momentum forward, while the Ice Breakers head back to the drawing board to hunt for that elusive first win of the season.
