# Puckedelics Keep Rolling as Wilson Powers Past Jackalopes

If you were looking for a high-octane Tuesday night at the rink, the Puckedelics delivered the goods, keeping their undefeated streak alive with a gritty 6-4 victory over a feisty Jackalopes squad. The Puckedelics continue to hunt down the top spot in the standings, moving to 6-0-2 on the season, while the Jackalopes fought hard but couldn't quite contain the offensive barrage in the final frame.

The game started with a literal bang when the Jackalopes’ Todd Thompson tucked one home just 95 seconds in, courtesy of a slick setup from Kyle Foster. But if you’ve been watching the Puckedelics lately, you know they don't panic. Jasen Lawwill responded with a personal highlight reel, netting a pair of quick-strike goals before the first intermission to flip the script and put the Puckedelics in the driver's seat.

The middle period was an absolute barn-burner that saw the momentum shift more often than a Zamboni on a tight turn. TRISTON WILSON, currently hotter than a fresh roll of athletic tape, extended the lead early in the second, but the Jackalopes refused to fold. Joshua Kerr and Ronald Herwig responded with back-to-back goals, both aided by the playmaking vision of Timothy N See, to knot the score at 3-3. 

"We let them back in the kitchen, and they started cooking," joked one Puckedelics veteran after the buzzer. "But we knew we had enough gas in the tank to turn the heat back up."

Cody Kern managed to reclaim the lead for the Puckedelics before the second break, but the third period remained a nail-biter. Joshua Kerr struck again for the Jackalopes at the 13:20 mark, bringing the score to 4-4 and setting up a frantic finish. 

The turning point came when the Puckedelics’ depth proved too much to handle. With under ten minutes remaining, KIRK NIELSEN extended his impressive goal streak to three games, burying a feed from Mark Hodges and Triston Wilson to break the tie. Not satisfied with just the lead, Wilson decided to put an exclamation point on the evening just twenty-four seconds later. Wilson’s second goal of the night—his fourth point of the contest—effectively punched the clock on the Jackalopes’ comeback hopes.

While the Jackalopes will be disappointed to walk away without a point, they showed they can hang with the league's elite. For the Puckedelics, it’s just another night at the office for a team that refuses to lose. With a 5-0-2 record entering the night and now finding another gear, they are looking more dangerous by the shift. They stay firmly glued to the heels of The Bone Zone, proving that this regular season race is far from over.
