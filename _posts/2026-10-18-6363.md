# Valkyries Shut Down the Chix in Cool 2-0 Victory

The ice was crisp, the coffee was likely lukewarm, and The Valkyries were absolutely dialed in this afternoon as they blanked the Chix with Stix 2-0 to keep their momentum rolling in this regular-season clash. While both teams skated hard, it was a tale of two periods that saw The Valkyries flex their defensive muscles and find the back of the net just when the tension was thick enough to cut with a skate blade.

For the first frame, the game had all the feeling of a classic standoff at the O.K. Corral, if that corral happened to be 200 feet long and made of frozen water. Both teams traded possession, but the goalies were acting like they’d made a pact to keep the scoreboards lonely. It wasn't until the second period that the seal finally broke, and it was The Valkyries who grabbed the steering wheel.

The breakthrough came at the 6:03 mark of the second period. Kacy Griffin decided she’d had enough of the stalemate, going coast-to-coast on an unassisted effort that had the Chix with Stix defense scrambling like they’d just seen a ghost. Griffin’s goal was a thing of beauty—a clean, clinical finish that proved once and for all that sometimes, the best way to move the puck is just to take it yourself and say, "I’ll handle this, thanks."

Just over two minutes later, The Valkyries doubled their pleasure. With the Chix with Stix reeling from the first goal, Jessica Hopwood stepped up to bury the second marker of the day at 8:41. With assists from Kacy Griffin—who was clearly having herself a afternoon—and Jessica Hinkefent, the goal effectively took the wind out of the Chix's sails.

"We knew we just needed one to get the dam to break," said a beaming Valkyries team member post-game. "When Kacy tucked that first one away, the whole bench just felt the air change. We weren't looking back after that."

The Chix with Stix didn't go down without a fight, of course. When Cynthia Rozell headed to the box for a tripping penalty with just under ten minutes left in the second, the Chix had a golden opportunity to climb back into the contest. But the Valkyries' penalty kill unit stood tall, turning away every attempt with the kind of collective focus you usually only see when there’s free pizza in the locker room.

With this win, The Valkyries move to 3-0-2 on the season, extending their solid run of form and showing the rest of the league they are a force to be reckoned with. As for the Chix with Stix, they’ll be looking to shake off the rust and find their scoring touch before their next outing. 

It was a tough day for the Chix, but in a league this competitive, you don't need a map to see that every goal is precious, and tonight, The Valkyries simply had the better compass.
