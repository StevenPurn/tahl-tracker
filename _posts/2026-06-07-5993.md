# A Goal-Scoring Gala: Yetis Outgun Knights in 19-Goal Thriller

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: June 7, 2026**

If you wanted a defensive clinic tonight, you walked into the wrong barn. If you wanted to see the twine ripple so many times the goal judges probably developed repetitive strain injuries, well, you were in the right place. In a game that defied logic and shredded the rulebook of conventional hockey, the Yetis emerged from the chaos with an 11-8 victory over the Knights in a bona fide, old-fashioned, barn-burner.

### First Period: Trading Punches
The Knights looked ready to end their skid early, with Justin Eppler lighting the lamp just over ten minutes in off a gorgeous setup from Travis Miller. But the Yetis, playing like a team hungry to climb out of the bottom half of the standings, didn't blink. Skylar Miller—riding a heater of a point streak—answered back shortly after, and by the time the dust settled on the opening frame, we were knotted up. 

Dustin Giebel and Thomas Aurilio both found the back of the net for the Knights, but the Yetis kept pace, with Corey Favier and Michael Pacheco demonstrating that traffic in front of the crease is the best place to be on a Saturday morning. 

### Second Period: The Pacheco Show
If the first period was a skirmish, the second was an all-out bombardment. The Yetis started to pull away, thanks largely to the unstoppable Michael Pacheco. The man was absolutely dialed in, finding the "top shelf where mom keeps the cookies" with alarming regularity. 

The Knights tried to hang tough, with Fuad Shimi snagging a pair of goals—including one on a power play while Skylar Miller was cooling his heels in the box for an unsportsmanlike conduct penalty. But the Yetis’ momentum was a freight train, and Pacheco was the conductor, hammering home back-to-back goals to keep the Knights at arm’s length.

### Third Period: Closing the Curtain
Down by a touchdown and a field goal, the Knights clawed back in the early stages of the third. Matthew Rudick, Dustin Giebel (his second of the night), and Toby Temple all buried shots to bring the Knights within striking distance. It looked for a fleeting moment like we might have a miraculous comeback on our hands.

But the Yetis weren't about to let this one slip away. Jered Pieschke, Jason Bahr, and Jason Fennell responded with a late-period scoring flurry that slammed the door shut on any potential comeback. When the final horn sounded, the scoreboard looked more like a line of code than a hockey score: 11-8.

### The Aftermath
For the Yetis, this win is a massive shot in the arm. Entering the game at #5, they needed these two points to stay relevant in the upper echelon of the standings. Michael Pacheco’s offensive clinic—arguably one of the most productive individual outings we've seen this season—was the difference-maker.

For the Knights, who fall to 0-2-0, it’s a tough pill to swallow. While the offense showed signs of life, the defensive zone was a revolving door. They’ll need to tighten the screws significantly before their next outing if they want to climb out of that #9 spot.

It was a morning of high-octane, pedal-to-the-metal hockey that left the fans breathless and the goalies looking for a nap. That’s the beauty of the sport—sometimes, you don't need a shutout to provide a show. Sometimes, you just need a whole lot of goals.
