# A Seven-Goal Shellacking: The Bone Zone Rattle the Leafs in 7-0 Masterclass

**TULSA** – If you were looking for a quiet Tuesday night at the rink, you picked the wrong zip code. The Bone Zone rolled into the building on the evening of July 8th and proceeded to put on an absolute clinic, dismantling the Tulsa Leafs in a 7-0 blowout that had the home fans looking for the exits long before the final horn sounded.

Coming into tonight, both teams were desperate to climb out of the basement of the standings. The Bone Zone, sitting at 2-3-0, clearly decided that "desperation" was too mild a term. They played with a chip on their shoulder the size of a Zamboni, and the Leafs simply had no answer for the onslaught.

### Period 1: The Early Ambush
The tone was set before the fans had even settled into their seats. With just 27.6 seconds gone, **Jerry Bates** struck first, burying a feed from **Garrett Williams** to put the visitors up 1-0. It was a wake-up call that the Leafs never quite answered.

The Bone Zone continued to pepper the Tulsa crease. By the 6:13 mark, **John White** doubled the lead off a helper from **Timothy N. See**. Before the period could even wrap, Williams decided he was done just setting up his teammates and notched an unassisted marker at 8:21, sending the Bone Zone into the first intermission with a commanding 3-0 cushion.

### Period 2: The Levasseur-Williams Show
If the first period was a tremor, the second was a full-blown seismic event. **Lance Levasseur**—riding a hot stick coming into the night—extended his goal streak with a tally just seven seconds into the frame, once again assisted by the tireless Timothy N. See.

Garrett Williams, clearly fueled by rocket fuel and ambition, notched his second of the night at the 7:55 mark. The Leafs’ frustration began to boil over shortly after, leading to a roughing penalty on **Scott Miller** at 8:23. The Bone Zone didn't just capitalize on the momentum; they suffocated the Leafs. Levasseur hammered home his second of the game at 9:53, pushing the lead to an insurmountable 6-0.

### Period 3: Closing the Vault
With the game firmly in the deep freeze, the third period was mostly about the Bone Zone playing a "keep-away" style of hockey. They continued to clog the neutral zone and limit second-chance opportunities for a Tulsa squad that looked visibly deflated. 

**Jerry Bates** added the final nail in the coffin at 12:34, again assisted by the orchestrator of the night, Garrett Williams. A late tripping minor on Timothy N. See at 6:21 gave the Leafs a glimmer of hope for a consolation goal, but the Bone Zone goaltender stood tall, turning away everything in sight to preserve the clean sheet and secure the shutout.

### The Aftermath
Tonight’s result is a massive statement for The Bone Zone. They move to 3-3-0 on the season, vaulting up the standings and proving that their recent "L-L" stretch was nothing more than a hiccup. For the Tulsa Leafs (1-3-2), this is a "burn the tape" kind of loss. They’ll need to regroup quickly if they want to stop the bleeding and avoid drifting further away from the playoff hunt.

For The Bone Zone, the message is clear: they’ve found their groove, and they’re playing a brand of hockey that’s fast, physical, and currently, impossible to stop. If you’re a goalie in this league, consider yourself warned—The Bone Zone is officially back in the building.
