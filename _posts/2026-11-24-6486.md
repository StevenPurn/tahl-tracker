# Goat-Sized Effort Keeps Hitmen at Bay in 5-5 Thriller

If you were looking for a quiet Tuesday night at the rink, you clearly took a wrong turn at the snack bar. The Killer Goats and the Hitmen Silver absolutely tore the roof off the place, trading momentum shifts like they were playing hot potato with a live grenade. By the time the final buzzer rang, we were staring at a 5-5 stalemate that felt less like a tie and more like a collective exhaustion for both squads.

The Goats came out of the gate with some serious grass in their teeth. Dylan Criquet Danielson was the man of the hour, and frankly, he looked like he was auditioning for a highlight reel. He opened the scoring at the 12:25 mark, setting a tone that would persist all night long. While the Hitmen’s star Nicklas Clark—who is riding a hot hand right now—did his best to dampen the mood with a goal shortly after, the Goats kept pushing back.

The first period saw a flurry of action, with Michael Doyle finding the back of the net to capitalize on a power play. But the Hitmen Silver aren't sitting at second in the standings by accident. Joshua Moyer proved why he’s a focal point of their attack, picking up a goal and two assists on the night. It was a classic "I score, you score" barnburner that had the folks in the stands forgetting to actually drink their coffee.

The middle frame was arguably Criquet Danielson’s masterpiece. He netted two more, including a nifty solo effort that left the Hitmen defense looking like they were trying to catch smoke with a fishnet. But every time the Goats seemed ready to pull away, the Hitmen—led by Andrew Sleezer’s gutsy play—answered the call. Sleezer’s goal in the second period was a testament to the Hitmen’s grit; they simply refused to let the Goats run away with the game.

The third period brought the drama, with Christopher Gardner and Nicklas Clark putting the Hitmen ahead 5-4, putting the pressure squarely on the Goats. With the clock winding down, Criquet Danielson—who by this point probably needed a nap and a steak dinner—completed his massive night by notching his fourth goal of the game at the 7:53 mark to lock the score at 5-5.

When the shootout rolled around, it felt like the only fitting conclusion to a contest this wild. Clark buried his chance with the calm of a man sitting in his own living room, but Steven Purn answered right back for the Goats, keeping the night squared away.

At the end of the day, both teams walk away with a point. For the Hitmen Silver, it’s a chance to keep pace near the top of the standings, even if they’ll be sweating about their defensive coverage tomorrow morning. For the Killer Goats, this performance against the league's #2 team proves that their recent win streak wasn't just a fluke. They’re a scrappy bunch, and after that show, nobody in this league is going to be taking them lightly for the rest of the season.
