# Bone Zone Rallies Late in Absolute Barn Burner to Edge Out Jackalopes

**TULSA** – If you’re a fan of heart-attack hockey and goals coming in bunches, you should have been at the rink on this August afternoon. In a back-and-forth tilt that had the intensity of a playoff game, The Bone Zone narrowly escaped with a 6-5 victory over the Jackalopes, proving once again why they are a top-three force in this league.

### Period 1: Trading Blows
The Jackalopes came out with their fur on fire. Just 1:26 into the tilt, **Ronald Herwig** buried a beauty, set up by some slick passing from Christopher King and Toby Temple. It was the perfect start for the squad looking to climb out of the bottom half of the standings. 

However, The Bone Zone—currently riding a heater of a winning streak—didn't flinch. **JW DePriest** decided to turn the game into his own personal highlight reel, potting two back-to-back goals in the middle of the frame, both assisted by the ever-dangerous Brian Smith. By the time the horn blew for the first intermission, The Bone Zone held a slim 2-1 lead, and you could practically hear the tension humming in the building.

### Period 2: The Goal-Scoring Carousel
If the first period was a warm-up, the second was a full-blown goal-scoring festival. **Todd Thompson** knotted things up early for the Jackalopes, but the lead lasted less than 40 seconds before **Garrett Williams** reminded everyone why he’s on a three-game point streak, firing an unassisted laser to regain the lead.

The Jackalopes weren’t going home without a fight. **James Whitacre** and **Robert Verity** scored back-to-back goals in a span of less than two minutes to pull the Jackalopes ahead 4-3. Just as the crowd started to think the Jackalopes had stolen the momentum, **Brian Smith**—who was playing orchestrator all night—took matters into his own hands with an unassisted marker to square it at 4-4 heading into the final frame.

### Period 3: The Bone Zone’s Late-Game Resilience
The third period began with some desperate hockey. The Jackalopes found themselves in penalty trouble, handing the Bone Zone a golden opportunity. Even at even strength, the pressure was mounting. 

The turning point? A lightning-quick flurry halfway through the third. **John White** snapped home a goal to take the lead, and before the Jackalopes could even catch their breath, **Garrett Williams** tacked on another just 11 seconds later. 

Down 6-4, the Jackalopes scrambled to claw their way back. **Josh Kerr** breathed life into the arena with a goal at the 8:20 mark, but the Bone Zone’s defense—backed by some desperate, scrambling saves in the final minutes—held the line. 

### The Bottom Line
With this 6-5 victory, The Bone Zone (7-3-1) continues their blistering pace, keeping their stranglehold on the #3 spot in the standings and keeping the pressure on the Tulsa Murder and Mooseknuckles above them. 

For the Jackalopes (3-5-2), it’s a tough pill to swallow. They played a gritty, high-energy game, but against a team as hot as The Bone Zone, small defensive lapses end up in the back of the net. They’ll need to tighten up the zone coverage if they want to stop their slide and make a move toward the middle of the pack. 

The Bone Zone keeps rolling, the Jackalopes look for answers, and the rest of the league? Well, they’re just trying to keep up. See you at the rink!
