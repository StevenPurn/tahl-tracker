# Bison Stampede: Tulsa Outlasts Reapers in Late-Night Barn Burner

**TULSA** – If you were looking for a peaceful Tuesday evening, you definitely came to the wrong place. Last night at the BOK Center, the Tulsa Bison and the Reapers decided to turn the ice into a pressure cooker, serving up a 4-3 thriller that had the hometown faithful gripping their seats until the final buzzer sounded at 9:30 PM.

Entering the night, both squads sat deadlocked in the standings with two points apiece. It was a classic "four-point game" energy, and from the opening puck drop, it was clear: nobody was going home early.

### The Opening Salvo
The Bison came out of the gate looking like they’d been shot out of a cannon. Just under eight minutes into the first, **David Josiah Guajardo** decided he didn’t need any help, weaving through the Reapers' defense and burying a beauty to open the scoring. Not long after, at the 9:40 mark, **Juan Padilla** doubled the lead, hammering home a feed from **Spencer Sheets**. The Bison were skating fast, moving the puck with the precision of a Swiss watch, and heading into the first intermission, Tulsa looked to be firmly in the driver’s seat.

### The Reapers Wake Up
Hockey is a game of momentum, and it’s a cruel mistress. In the second period, the Reapers started manufacturing some serious "traffic in front of the net," making life miserable for the Tulsa goaltender. **Edgar Solorio** broke the ice for the visitors at 10:59, and before the Bison could regain their composure, **Kelly Rundle** knotted things up at 5:07. Just like that, a two-goal lead evaporated, and the tension in the building went from "mild concern" to "deafening roar."

### A Chaotic Finale
The third period was absolute bedlam. With the clock winding down, **Justin Phillips** re-established the Bison lead, cleaning up a rebound off a **Brett Mooney** shot to send the home crowd into a frenzy at 12:13. 

The Reapers weren’t finished, though. After a flurry of special teams play—including matching penalties for hooking and tripping—**Kelly Rundle** struck again at 9:19 for his second of the night, bringing the Reapers within one. 

But the story of the night belonged to **Spencer Sheets**. With just 1:07 remaining on the clock, Sheets found the back of the net after a beautiful tic-tac-toe play from Padilla and Guajardo. It was the insurance goal that proved to be the difference-maker. The Reapers threw everything but the kitchen sink at the Bison net in the final minute, but the Tulsa defense stood tall, turning away a desperate late-game surge.

### The Bottom Line
When the dust settled, the scoreboard read 4-3 in favor of the boys in Bison jerseys. It was a gritty, high-octane performance that rewarded the 9:30 PM crowd for their late-night loyalty. 

With this result, the Tulsa Bison leapfrog the Reapers in the standings, moving to four points and establishing a bit of breathing room. The Reapers will head back to the drawing board to iron out their defensive coverage, but for now, the city of Tulsa gets to toast to a hard-earned victory. 

If this is a preview of the intensity we’re going to see for the rest of the season, folks, buckle up—it’s going to be one wild ride.
