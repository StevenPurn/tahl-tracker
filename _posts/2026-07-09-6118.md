# Clark’s Masterclass: Hitmen Silver Put the Havok in the Rearview Mirror

**By: "Rinkside" Rick Henderson**
*July 8, 2026*

If you were looking for a quiet Tuesday night at the rink, you came to the wrong place. Tonight’s tilt between the Hitmen Silver and Havok was less of a hockey game and more of a fever dream featuring 14 goals, plenty of iron, and enough offensive firepower to make the scoreboard operator sweat bullets. When the dust settled on the 9-5 Hitmen victory, the Hitmen had proven that when they find their rhythm, they aren’t just a team—they’re a freight train.

### First Period: The Tone is Set
The Hitmen came out buzzing, looking to climb out of the bottom half of the standings against the third-seeded Havok. **Nicklas Clark** wasted no time establishing his presence, burying an unassisted beauty at the 11:21 mark to open the floodgates. 

**Josh Moyer** followed up with a clinical finish off the tape of Michael Doyle and Eric Roepke, but Havok wasn't about to go quietly into the cold. Steven Purn answered back for the visitors, showing off some silky hands. Moyer would strike again later in the period, but a late tally from Havok’s Michael Henry kept the pressure cooker at high heat. By the time the buzzer sounded for the first break, the Hitmen led 3-2, but it felt like anyone’s game.

### Second Period: The Clark-a-Thon
If the first period was a skirmish, the second was an absolute blitzkrieg. Havok knotted things up early behind another Michael Henry goal, but then the Hitmen decided it was time to take flight.

Nicklas Clark turned into a one-man wrecking crew. He notched two quick goals in under a minute, followed immediately by Michael Doyle adding his name to the scoresheet. In the blink of an eye—a span of just 53 seconds—the Hitmen ripped the game wide open. Havok’s Michael Henry completed his hat trick late in the frame to keep them breathing, but the Hitmen headed into the final frame with a commanding 6-4 cushion. 

### Third Period: The Hitmen Close the Vault
The final period was a masterclass in opportunistic hockey. Despite the Hitmen spending some time in the sin bin—a two-minute hook for Moyer and a trip for Doyle—Havok simply couldn't find the rhythm to mount a comeback.

In a display of pure dominance, Nicklas Clark hammered home his fourth goal of the night while shorthanded, effectively silencing any comeback talk. Christopher Gardner added an exclamation point on a breakaway, and Clark found the back of the net once more—his fifth of the night—to put the game out of reach. Michael Henry managed to snag his fourth goal of the night for Havok, but it was purely consolation prize territory at that point. 

### The Bottom Line
When you see a 9-5 scoreline, you know the defensemen had a busy night at the office, but Nicklas Clark provided the kind of individual performance that headlines sports segments for weeks. With this win, the Hitmen Silver (3-2-0) jump to 6 points, breathing down the necks of the league leaders and signaling to the rest of the pack that they’re finding their groove at exactly the right time. 

Havok (3-2-1) remains locked at 7 points, but after giving up nine goals, they’ll surely be looking to tighten up the D-zone before their next puck drop. 

**Three Stars of the Game:**
1. **Nicklas Clark (Hitmen Silver):** 5 Goals, 1 Assist. An absolute clinic.
2. **Michael Henry (Havok):** 4 Goals. A valiant solo effort in a tough night.
3. **Josh Moyer (Hitmen Silver):** 2 Goals. Provided the momentum that allowed the floodgates to open.
