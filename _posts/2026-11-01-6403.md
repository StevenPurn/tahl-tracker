# Leafs Hang On to Fend Off Late Lodge Surge

If you were looking for a high-octane Saturday evening at the rink, you found it. The Tulsa Leafs and the Lodge treated us to a real barn-burner that kept the heart rates elevated right until the final buzzer, with the Leafs ultimately walking away with a 4-3 victory that felt like it was ten games’ worth of drama crammed into sixty minutes.

The Leafs came out swinging, looking like they were tired of being stuck in the bottom tier of the standings. Braden Trinka set the tone early, netting an unassisted beauty at the 13:56 mark of the first period. Not long after, Hunter Sharpe doubled the lead, finishing off a slick feed from Jonathan Lee. When Sharpe buried his second of the night midway through the second period, assisted by James Darnaby, it felt like the Leafs might just cruise to an easy finish.

But if you know anything about this league, you know that a three-goal lead is the most dangerous score in hockey. The Lodge, who haven't had much go their way lately, started digging in. Richard Retkowski finally broke the seal for the visitors late in the second period, snagging a helper from the red-hot Jered Pieschke to flip the momentum.

The third period was a bit of a scramble. The Leafs managed to pad their cushion when Curtis Connors went coast-to-coast for an unassisted tally with about five minutes left on the clock. It was the kind of goal that makes the beer in the stands taste a little colder and a little better. 

Still, the Lodge weren't about to head home quietly. Louis Luongo turned on the jets, potting a solo effort with just under a minute gone in the third's scoring sequence, and then striking again at the 9:03 mark with a little help from Jamie Halverson. That late surge turned a comfortable evening into a genuine nail-biter, as the Leafs had to sweat through a late penalty from Zac Hughes to keep their lead intact.

"We tried our hardest to make things interesting for the fans," a winded Leafs skater joked in the hallway after the game. "Maybe a little too interesting."

With this win, the Tulsa Leafs climb to 2-3-2 on the season, finally giving their fans something to cheer about after a rough patch. For the Lodge, it’s a tough pill to swallow; they’ll be looking to bounce back after a hard-fought effort that just fell a few inches short of an equalizer. It wasn’t always pretty, but it was exactly the kind of gritty, local hockey we all showed up to see.
