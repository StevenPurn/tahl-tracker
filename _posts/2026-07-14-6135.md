# A Bombing Run at the Rink: Blue Bombers Explode for 11 in High-Octane Rout of The Rookery

Early on, it looked like we were in for a defensive grinder. Chris Olsen struck first for the Bombers, but The Rookery showed some grit, with Joshua Pula bagging two quick ones to give the home crowd a jolt. The lead swapped hands like a hot potato, with Chad Olson adding one for The Rookery and Dylan Walker countering for the Bombers. 

By the time Frank Post buried a feed from Olsen with just seconds left in the opening frame, the score was 3-3. The pace was frenetic—guys were flying, skates were chattering, and there wasn't a breath of air in the building.

### Second Period: The Dam Breaks
The second period was where the "Blue Wave" really started to crest. Abigail Nisbet cleaned up the trash in the crease, and Dylan Walker—looking every bit the veteran—sniped his second of the night on a beautiful setup from Jordan Hawkins Baker. 

By the time Aaron West tickled the twine to push the lead, The Rookery’s defense looked like they were trying to solve a Rubik’s cube in a windstorm. The Bombers headed into the final frame holding all the cards.

### Third Period: An Absolute Goal-Fest
The final frame turned into a highlight reel for the Bombers' offense. Chris Olsen, riding his hot streak, extended the lead, but The Rookery’s Jerry LaRue refused to go quietly, snagging his second of the night.

Even with Gage Walker finding the back of the net for The Rookery, it was a drop in the ocean. The Bombers closed out the night with late markers from West and Hawkins Baker, leaving the final tally at a lopsided 11-6.
