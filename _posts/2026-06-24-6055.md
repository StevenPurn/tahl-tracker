# Mooseknuckles Leave Mojo Spellbound in 6-0 Shutout Masterclass

**TULSA** – If you were looking for a high-octane offensive clinic on this fine Tuesday night, you didn’t need to look any further than the rink. The Mooseknuckles entered tonight’s tilt at 3-0-0, looking to defend their throne atop the standings, and they didn’t just defend it—they reinforced it with steel and a dash of magic. In a total dismantling of Mojo, the 'Knuckles walked away with a 6-0 statement victory that had the hometown faithful cheering from the opening whistle to the final buzzer.

### Period 1: The Lawwill Show
The Mooseknuckles wasted absolutely no time setting the tone. Just 66 seconds into the opening frame, Jasen Lawwill signaled that it was going to be a long night for the Mojo defense. Off a crisp setup from Nicholas Strle and Scott Bartkus, Lawwill buried the biscuit, putting the boys in red on the board early. 

Mojo tried to dig their heels in, but the 'Knuckles were relentless. With 8:34 left in the period, Lawwill struck again—this time finding the back of the net off feeds from Jeffrey Kleehammer and Michael Pacheco. By the end of one, it was clear: the Mooseknuckles weren't just playing hockey; they were conducting a masterclass in puck possession.

### Period 2: The Floodgates Open
If Mojo hoped the intermission would reset the momentum, they were sorely mistaken. The Mooseknuckles came out with their skates on fire. 

At the 6:52 mark, Nicholas Strle turned "traffic in front of the net" into a beautiful piece of art, converting a pass from Frank Benavides and Pacheco to extend the lead to 3-0. Before the Mojo bench could even exhale, Scott Bartkus joined the party just 38 seconds later, firing a rocket home assisted by Kleehammer and Lawwill. By the time the second period whistle blew, the Mojo netminder was fighting to keep the score from becoming a blowout, but the dam had well and truly burst.

### Period 3: Closing the Curtain
Up 4-0, you might expect a team to pack it in and play safe, but that’s not how the Mooseknuckles roll. They kept their foot on the gas, keeping the Mojo offense stifled and their own goalie pristine.

Jess Herd added insult to injury at 2:15, roofing a beauty from a Pete Fleming feed, before Mark Hodges slammed the door shut just under a minute later. With an assist from Strle, Hodges’ goal made it a cool 6-0, proving that tonight was a total team effort where every line was firing on all cylinders.

### The Aftermath
For the Mooseknuckles, this win is pure gold. They remain undefeated at 4-0-0, holding tight to that #1 spot in the standings and proving they are the team to beat in this league. They’ve got the depth, they’ve got the hunger, and tonight, they had the finish.

As for Mojo, it’s a tough pill to swallow. Coming in at 2-1-0, they were looking to make a splash and potentially jump into the top tier, but they ran into a buzzsaw tonight. They’ll need to regroup quickly and find their Mojo again before their next trip to the ice.

It was a real barn burner of a performance by the Mooseknuckles—the kind that makes you think they’ve got their eyes locked firmly on the trophy. We’ll be watching closely as the season progresses, but tonight? Tonight, the town belongs to the 'Knuckles. 

**Final Score: Mooseknuckles 6, Mojo 0**
