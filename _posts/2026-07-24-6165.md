# High-Octane Havoc: Renegades Outgun Precast in 8-6 Barn Burner

The Renegades came out of the gate with the pedal to the metal. Alex Bateman kept his heater of a season going, lighting the lamp early thanks to a slick setup from Michael Pasque and Matthan Hillberg. But Oklahoma Precast wasn’t about to let the Renegades skate away with the momentum. Nicholas "The Powerhouse" Power knotted it up at 14:03, proving that he is currently playing hockey on a different planet.

The Renegades’ top line was buzzing like a swarm of hornets, with Pasque finding the mesh, but once again, Power answered the bell. The second frame began with the Precast boys looking like they were ready to pull away. Marshall Nunneley and Arin Olson—the latter securing his second of the night—stretched the Precast lead to 5-2. At that point, the Precast bench was buzzing, and the Renegades looked like they were stuck in the mud.

Then, the script flipped. 

The Renegades turned the volume up to eleven. Bobby McKinley started the engine, and then the floodgates burst open. Pasque and Pachuta found the back of the net in quick succession, turning a three-goal deficit into a 5-5 tie by the time the horn blew to end the second. The final period was pure chaos. Corey Christian gave Precast a glimmer of hope with a go-ahead goal, but the Renegades had a secret weapon in Bobby McKinley. With the game tied at 6-6, McKinley decided he’d seen enough. He buried a breakaway finish with just seconds remaining in the clock, then added an empty-netter to cap off a massive hat trick. Alex Bateman iced the cake with his own late-game tally to put the final nail in the coffin.

While the goaltenders were likely left feeling like they were playing “dodgeball with cannonballs,” the story of the night was the sheer offensive output from the Renegades’ heavy hitters. Bateman, Pasque, and McKinley turned the ice into their personal playground. For Oklahoma Precast, Nicholas Power and Arin Olson did everything in their power to keep the ship afloat, but tonight, the Renegades’ depth and late-game tenacity proved to be the difference-maker.

### The Road Ahead
With this 8-6 victory, the Renegades (5-3-0) solidify their grip on second place, keeping pace with the league-leading Shooting Blanks. They’ve proven they can score with anyone, but they might want to tighten up the zone coverage before their next tilt.

For Oklahoma Precast (2-7-0), it’s a tough pill to swallow. They showed incredible heart and the ability to strike early, but consistency remains the hurdle. They’ll need to regroup quickly if they want to climb out of the bottom half of the standings.
