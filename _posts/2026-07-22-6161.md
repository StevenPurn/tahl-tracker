# The Lodge Slaps a Reality Check on the Undefeated Mooseknuckles in 10-3 Rout

**TULSA** – If you had "The Lodge hangs ten on the league-leading Mooseknuckles" on your bingo card for this Tuesday night, go ahead and treat yourself to a steak dinner—you’ve earned it. 

Coming into tonight’s 10:15 PM puck drop at the rink, the Mooseknuckles were the undisputed kings of the hill, sitting pretty at 6-0-0 and looking like a freight train with no brakes. But as any seasoned puckhead knows, in this league, the ice is slippery, and tonight, the Lodge treated the Mooseknuckles like a pair of worn-out skates.

### Period 1: A Warning Shot
The Lodge came out of the tunnel with their hair on fire. Braden Trinka opened the account at the 10:55 mark, shaking the dust off the twine with an assist from Jason Bahr. When MARK STACY doubled the lead at 5:20, it felt like the Lodge was sending a message. While Mooseknuckles’ Michael Pacheco managed to silence the Lodge bench briefly with an unassisted snipe at 5:43, the writing was already on the wall. The Lodge wasn’t just here to participate; they were here to renovate the scoreboard.

### Period 2: The Dam Breaks
The middle frame was nothing short of a tidal wave. While Pete Fleming brought the Knuckles within one early, the Lodge responded with a display of offensive clinic work that would make a highlight reel blush. 

Braden Trinka struck again at 11:51, triggering a scoring spree that saw the Lodge bury goal after goal. Between James Whitacre’s absolute clinic—he was finding the back of the net like he had a map—and Jason Bahr’s heavy contribution, the Mooseknuckles’ defensive structure evaporated like mist over the boards. Donny Griffin tried to inject some grit with a roughing penalty at 13:24, but that only seemed to fuel the Lodge’s fire as they put up a crooked number to effectively ice the game before the second intermission.

### Period 3: Closing the Barn Door
By the time the third period rolled around, the Mooseknuckles were looking for a way to salvage some pride. Donny Griffin did manage to light the lamp at 7:23 off a feed from Kyle Foster, but the Lodge’s engine had already shifted into cruise control. Even a late hooking penalty on Jason Bahr couldn't stop the inevitable: a 10-3 statement victory for the Lodge.

### The Aftermath
For the Mooseknuckles, tonight is a massive wake-up call. You don’t get to 6-0 without being a great team, but tonight, their armor was cracked, and their perfect record was unceremoniously handed its first loss. The Lodge, meanwhile, proved that their 3-2-2 record was a lie—this is a team that can skate with the best of them. 

The standings are officially tightening up, folks. The Mooseknuckles remain at the top for now, but the Lodge has just signaled that they are a legitimate force to be reckoned with as we push toward the final stretch of the season. 

One thing is for sure: if you weren’t at the rink tonight, you missed a bona fide barn burner. The Mooseknuckles will look to bounce back, but for tonight, the Lodge owns the town. 

**Final Score:** Mooseknuckles 3, The Lodge 10.
