# The Valkyries Keep Rolling, Stifling the Riveters in 5-3 Victory

If there’s one thing you can count on in this league, it’s that The Valkyries are currently playing hockey like they’ve got a magnet inside the puck. On a brisk Saturday evening at the rink, the league leaders marched into the barn and walked out with a 5-3 win over the Tulsa Riveters, proving once again why they’re sitting pretty at the top of the standings with an undefeated record.

The game started with the kind of frantic pace that makes your coffee go cold before you can even take a sip. Makenzie Giove drew first blood for the Valkyries just over six minutes into the opening frame, but the Riveters weren’t about to roll over. Gabi Gibson answered back exactly sixty-one seconds later, sliding one home off a nifty feed from Theresa Rose Rabara to knot it up. It looked like we were in for a long, grind-it-out affair, but Barbara Lepe had other plans, putting the Valkyries back on top before the first intermission with a helper from the red-hot Giove.

As the second period rolled around, the Valkyries began to turn the screws. They played a brand of hockey that looked less like a league game and more like a well-oiled machine. Kalea Maxwell buried a shot at the 11:39 mark, and before the Riveters could even catch their breath, Elisabeth Schroeder padded the lead. By the time the buzzer sounded on the second period, the Riveters were staring down a 4-1 deficit and looked like they’d just run into a brick wall.

"They're faster than a whisper in a wind tunnel," Riveters coach lamented near the bench after the second period. "You think you've got them bottled up, and suddenly the puck is in the back of your net and they’re already heading back to center ice."

The third period brought a glimmer of hope for the Tulsa faithful. Ivy Emerson, riding a two-game goal streak, found the back of the net to ignite a bit of a rally. In a bizarre sequence of events, a quick follow-up goal—credited to an unknown skater—narrowed the gap to 4-3, and suddenly, the barn was rocking. The Riveters were buzzing, the momentum had shifted, and you could practically feel the ice tilting. 

But the Valkyries are top-of-the-table for a reason. With the Riveters pushing for the equalizer, Elisabeth Schroeder slammed the door shut with her second goal of the night with just over a minute left on the clock, effectively icing the game and sending the Riveters home with a tough loss.

The win extends The Valkyries’ impressive streak and keeps them firmly in the driver’s seat of the league. The Riveters, meanwhile, find themselves in a bit of a slump, dropping their second straight after a promising three-game tear. If you’re a betting person, you’ve learned by now: don't bet against the Valkyries until someone finds a way to throw a wrench in their gears. Until then, they’re just going to keep doing exactly what they’re doing—winning.
