# Ice Cold Perfection: Gold as Ice Keeps the Streak Alive in Shootout Thriller

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: June 28, 2026**

If you wanted a masterclass in defensive grit and high-stakes tension, you weren't at the beach this morning—you were at the rink. In an 11:45 AM matinee that felt more like a playoff tilt than a regular-season clash, the undefeated juggernaut **Gold as Ice** managed to keep their pristine record intact, narrowly edging out the **Dirty Danglers** 2-1 in a nail-biting shootout victory.

### Period One: The Calm Before the Storm
The opening frame was a tactical chess match. Both teams traded heavy hits and played a tight checking game, clogging the neutral zone like a holiday traffic jam on the interstate. Neither goalie wanted to be the one to blink first, and frankly, both were spectacular. If you like “traffic in front of the net” and goalies standing on their heads, this was your brand of hockey.

### Period Two: Ingram Breaks the Ice
The stalemate finally shattered at the 10:48 mark of the second. Gold as Ice’s **Aden Ingram**—the man currently riding a heater of a point streak—found the back of the net after a crafty dish from **Mark DeSimone**. It was a classic snipe that would make any hockey parent beam with pride.

However, Gold as Ice flirted with disaster later in the period. First, DeSimone took a seat for hooking at 2:20, and just minutes later, Taylor Chinowth headed to the box for a trip. The Dirty Danglers threw everything but the kitchen sink at the Gold as Ice netminder, but the penalty kill unit stood tall, clearing rebounds like they were shoveling snow off a driveway.

### Period Three: The Danglers Dig In
The third period was all about the Danglers’ refusal to quit. Down by a goal and fighting for their lives in the standings, they kept the pressure cooker on high. When Tyler Mills took a slashing minor at 14:26, the opportunity finally knocked. 

With just 3:11 left on the clock, the Danglers broke through. **Brett Mooney**, feeding off the hustle of Arthur Baisley, buried the equalizer. The crowd erupted, the momentum swung, and we were suddenly staring down the barrel of a heart-stopping finish. Regulation ended knotted at 1-1, forcing us into the high-stakes theater of the shootout.

### The Shootout: Ingram Seals the Deal
In the shootout, nerves are usually made of brittle glass, but Aden Ingram clearly has veins of ice water. With the game resting on his stick, Ingram deked, dipped, and tucked the puck home to seal the deal. It was the cherry on top of a 2-1 win that was harder fought than a corner battle for a loose puck.

### The Aftermath
For **Gold as Ice (6-0-0)**, this is another notch on the belt—they remain the undisputed kings of the hill. They aren't just winning; they are finding ways to gut out victories when the offense isn't firing on all cylinders. 

As for the **Dirty Danglers (2-4-0)**, while they’ll walk away with a loss, they should hold their heads high. They proved today that even the league’s top dogs can be pushed to the absolute brink. They’re sitting at #8, but with that kind of grit, they’re a team that nobody wants to see on their schedule in the coming weeks.

The sun is shining, the ice is shaved, and the hockey gods have blessed us with another barn burner. Stay tuned, folks—the season is only getting hotter!
