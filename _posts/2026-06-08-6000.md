# Goat-tastic Performance: Killer Goats Pummel the Pack in 7-1 Rout

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
*June 7, 2026 — 8:15 PM*

If you came to the arena expecting a tight, defensive chess match, you were looking at the wrong sheet of ice tonight. The Killer Goats came into this contest hungry for their first win of the season, and boy, did they feast. In a display of offensive firepower that left the One Man Wolfpack howling at the moon in frustration, the Goats secured a dominant 7-1 victory that wasn't just a win—it was a statement.

### First Period: The Avalanche Begins
The Goats didn’t just open the floodgates; they tore the hinges off the door. It all started at the 13:11 mark of the first when Lucas Blanchard tickled the twine, set up beautifully by Travis Miller and Robert Mattison. That goal seemed to act as a dam-breaker. 

Less than forty seconds later, Michael Henry doubled the lead off a feed from Nelson Pina, and before the Wolfpack could even catch their breath, Aaron Quinton went end-to-end for a highlight-reel unassisted beauty. By the time Samuel Michaud and Travis Miller added two more before the buzzer, the Goats had established a 5-0 lead, effectively putting this one on ice before the Zamboni even thought about its second-period break. The Pack’s crease was looking more like a revolving door than a fortress.

### Second Period: Defensive Lockdown
With a comfortable cushion, the Goats shifted their focus to protecting their tender. The intensity remained high, but the scoring cooled off until the dying seconds of the middle frame. With just 23.2 ticks left on the clock, Aaron Quinton struck again—his second of the night—thanks to a crisp setup from Brian Smith. Heading into the third, it was 6-0, and the Goats were playing like a team that had finally found their rhythm.

### Third Period: The Pack Shows Pulse
To their credit, the One Man Wolfpack refused to roll over. They clawed one back at the 12:11 mark when James Whitacre lit the lamp, extending his impressive three-game point streak. Devin Dunn picked up the helper, finally giving the Pack’s supporters something to cheer about. 

However, any hope of a miraculous comeback was squashed exactly 62 seconds later. Lucas Blanchard notched his second goal of the night, burying a feed from Henry and Pina to put the finishing touch on a 7-1 masterpiece.

### The Aftermath and What It Means
The Goats’ goalie was a brick wall for the majority of the night, facing a barrage of chances but standing tall to ensure there was no late-game drama. 

With this decisive win, the Killer Goats jump from 1 point to 3, putting them right in the mix with the top tier of the league. They’ve successfully moved past their early-season jitters and are looking like a team that can dominate the transition game. 

For the One Man Wolfpack, the struggle continues. Sitting at 0-1-0 entering the night and now falling to 0-4-0 overall, they’ll need to do some soul-searching. When you’re caught chasing the game as hard as they were tonight, the ice feels a lot longer, and the legs feel a lot heavier.

The Goats look like they’ve found their groove, and the rest of the league had better take notice: these Goats don't just graze—they hunt. See you at the rink for the next one!
