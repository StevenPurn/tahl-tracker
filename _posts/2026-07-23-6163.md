# A Jurassic Collapse: Mayhem Wreck+ Steamrolls Fossil Hockey in 8-1 Thrashing

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: July 22, 2026**

If you brought a paleontologist to the rink last night, they wouldn't have been looking for fossils—they would have been looking for the remains of a defensive unit. In a game that felt more like a demolition derby than a hockey match, the **Mayhem Wreck+** absolutely dismantled Fossil Hockey, skating away with a dominant 8-1 victory that had the hometown crowd buzzing long after the final horn sounded.

Coming into the 9:00 PM tilt, the Wreck+ were looking to climb the ladder, while Fossil Hockey was desperate to snap a cooling four-game skid. By the time the ice had settled, it was clear: the Wreck+ aren't just playing; they’re hunting.

### Period 1: The Foundation Crumbles
The tone was set early. Just 12 minutes in, **Nick Hatfield**—who was clearly playing with a cheat code tonight—found the back of the net off a sweet dish from **Larry Cockrell**. Fossil Hockey looked like they were skating in mud, and when **Justin McKinney** went coast-to-coast for an unassisted beauty with 33 seconds left in the frame, the "Wrecking Crew" had a comfortable 2-0 cushion. 

Even a tripping penalty on McKinney couldn't derail the momentum; if anything, the Wreck+ seemed to sharpen their skates while shorthanded.

### Period 2: An Absolute Feeding Frenzy
If the first period was a light breeze, the second was a category-five hurricane. Hatfield notched his second of the night at the 9:06 mark (again courtesy of Cockrell), effectively blowing the doors off the arena. 

The dam didn't just break; it vanished. Larry Cockrell joined the party with an unassisted tally, followed shortly by a McKinney-Hatfield connection that turned the Fossil defense into pylons. By the time **Steven Chern** and **Stephen Jeffery** added their names to the scoresheet, it was 7-0. The Wreck+ were firing from every angle, burying pucks top-shelf where mom keeps the cookies, leaving the Fossil goalie looking for a seat on the bench.

### Period 3: A Lonely Goal for the Record Books
To their credit, Fossil Hockey didn’t pack up the gear bags early. They pushed back in the third, finally breaking the shutout bid at the 5:37 mark. **Zach Cox** kept his point streak alive by burying a pass from Timothy Willis and Bryan Simms. It was a valiant effort, but for Fossil Hockey, it was a case of "too little, too late" in a game that had long since been decided by the Wreck+ offensive juggernaut.

### The Aftermath
With this 8-1 drubbing, **Mayhem Wreck+ (6-1-1)** solidifies their spot in the upper echelon of the standings. They are playing with the kind of cohesion that makes goaltenders lose sleep. Their transition game is surgical, and their chemistry is currently off the charts.

For **Fossil Hockey (3-5-0)**, this is a night to bury in the strata and move on from. Five straight losses is a tough pill to swallow, and they’ll need to find some defensive grit if they want to stop their slide down the standings. 

As for the Wreck+? They head home tonight smelling like victory and smelling blood in the water. If they keep playing like this, the teams at the top of the leaderboard better check their rearview mirrors—there’s a Wreck coming through.
