# Ice Cold Revenge: Blizzard Storms Past Wolfpack in 6-3 Thriller

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: June 14, 2026**

The ice at the arena was slick, the coffee was steaming, and the tension was palpable as the One Man Wolfpack and the Blizzard took to the sheet for a classic bottom-of-the-table scrap. Coming into tonight’s 8:45 PM puck drop, both squads were hungry for their first taste of victory. While the Wolfpack were looking to claw their way out of a four-game funk, it was the Blizzard who ultimately found their rhythm, skating away with a decisive 6-3 victory.

### First Period: A Frenzy of Felt Net
If you blinked, you missed a highlight reel. The Blizzard wasted zero time establishing the forecast, with Jamie Halverson opening the scoring at the 10:33 mark, courtesy of a slick dish from John White. 

William Hofmeister then turned the heat up, burying a pair of goals in the span of just under 30 seconds late in the frame. It looked like the Blizzard were going to run the Wolfpack out of the building, but Timothy N. See had other plans. In a gritty display of determination, See went back-to-back, finding the twine twice in just over a minute to cut the deficit to 3-2. Before the period closed, Garrett Williams reclaimed the two-goal cushion for Blizzard, silencing the sudden Wolfpack momentum.

### Second Period: Trading Blows
The second period saw the Wolfpack try to chip away at the fortress. Jared Zellers managed to beat the Blizzard netminder early, keeping the score within striking distance at 4-3. 

However, the discipline started to fray. ROBERT EUSTICE sent the Blizzard to the sin bin for a tripping call, but the Wolfpack couldn’t capitalize on the man advantage. Instead, it was the veteran Gary Hennenfent who hammered home a dagger at 8:24 to regain the two-goal cushion. Shortly after, STEVEN RAMOS headed to the box for another trip, but the Wolfpack's power play remained as cold as the ice itself.

### Third Period: The Blizzard Seals the Deal
The final frame was a chippy, whistle-heavy affair. The Wolfpack, sensing their window closing, took a pair of tripping and unsportsmanlike penalties, which effectively sucked the oxygen out of their comeback bid. 

The icing on the cake came at 8:17, when Nelson Pina redirected a beautiful feed from Hennenfent and Hofmeister, tucking it top shelf where mom keeps the cookies. That final strike put the game firmly on ice, ending the contest at 6-3.

### The Takeaway
For the Blizzard, this is a massive confidence booster. They move off the 0-1-0 mark and into the middle of the pack, proving they have the firepower to handle business when the game gets physical. Gary Hennenfent and William Hofmeister were the clear architects of this victory, dictating the pace and punishing every defensive lapse from the Wolfpack.

As for the One Man Wolfpack, the search for that elusive "W" continues. Despite Timothy N. See’s heroic two-goal effort, the team couldn't overcome the discipline issues and the constant pressure in their own zone. They remain at the bottom of the standings, but if they can bottle the energy they showed late in the first, they won't be winless for much longer.

The league is tightening up, folks—and if tonight was any indication, the path to the top is going to be a cold, hard ride. See you at the next one!
