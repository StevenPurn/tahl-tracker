# GOAT-LIKE GRIT: Killer Goats Edge Honey Badgers in 9-7 Goal-Fest

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
**June 21, 2026**

If you didn’t bring your spare goalie mask to the rink tonight, you were in the wrong building. The Killer Goats and the Honey Badgers turned the ice into a pinball machine on Sunday evening, combining for 16 goals in a 9-7 barn burner that had the fans’ heads spinning faster than a fresh blade on a sharpening stone.

Entering the night as the league leaders, the Killer Goats looked to solidify their perch at the top. The Honey Badgers, still hunting for their first win of the campaign, played with the desperation of a team that wasn’t going to let the first-place squad leave without a fight.

### Period 1: The Fast and the Furious
The scoring started early when Samuel Michaud buried a beauty off a feed from Lucas Blanchard. But the Badgers—defying their record—struck back almost immediately through Adam Carter. By the time the dust settled on a frantic first period, we were deadlocked at 2-2. 

A peculiar moment of brilliance occurred while the Goats were shorthanded; despite Travis Miller sitting in the sin bin for a trip, Dylan Criquet Danielson found the twine to keep the momentum squarely in the Goats' corner.

### Period 2: Trading Paint
The second period turned into a high-stakes game of "you score, I score." Brandon Szuch continued his offensive masterclass for the Badgers, but the Goats’ depth proved to be the difference-maker. 

The highlight reel moment? Samuel Michaud finding the net while *still* shaking off the rust from his own hooking penalty. The Goats leaned on their heavy hitters, with Dylan Criquet Danielson and Travis Miller putting the team on their backs. By the end of the second, the Goats had clawed out a lead, but the Badgers’ Joey Pachuta and Marshall Nunneley weren't ready to let the Goats run away with it.

### Period 3: Closing the Barn Door
Leading 7-5 heading into the final frame, the Goats needed discipline. Nelson Pina and Kyle Foster provided some critical insurance goals to stretch the lead, but Adam Carter wasn't done. He notched his second and third of the night, completing a stellar hat trick that had the Badgers bench buzzing. 

Ultimately, the Goats’ transition game proved too much. Despite the Badgers refusing to roll over—firing pucks at the net until the final buzzer—the Goats held firm to secure the 9-7 victory.

### The Breakdown
*   **Killer Goats (3-0-1):** They remain the team to beat. Dylan Criquet Danielson was the hero of the night, extending his goal streak with a multi-goal performance that kept the Goats in the driver's seat. 
*   **Honey Badgers (0-4-0):** They’re snakebitten, no doubt about it. But if tonight’s offensive output is any indication, they’re finally finding their rhythm. Scoring seven goals against the top team in the league is a moral victory they can hang their hats on.

**The Post-Game Wrap:**
The Goats march on, looking like a juggernaut as they maintain their hold on the #1 spot in the standings. For the Badgers, the climb is steep, but they showed us tonight that they have plenty of bite left.

Keep your sticks on the ice, folks. If the rest of this season is even half as wild as tonight, we’re in for a legendary run.
