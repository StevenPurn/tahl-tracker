# A Midsummer Night’s Stalemate: Laser and Jackalopes Battle to a 4-4 Deadlock

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: June 29, 2026**

If you didn’t have a ticket to the barn tonight, you missed a classic. The 8:45 PM puck drop between the Laser and the Jackalopes might have looked like a mid-table clash on paper, but on the ice, it was a high-octane, back-and-forth tilt that left the goalies catching their breath and the fans on the edge of their seats. When the final horn sounded after a tension-filled shootout, the scoreboard read a knotted 4-4.

### Period 1: Trading Blows
The Jackalopes struck first, and they did it with style. With just seconds remaining before the buzzer, Bradley Fidler found the back of the net, cleaning up a gorgeous setup from the brothers Whitacre, David and James. It was a dagger to the heart of the Laser’s momentum, but the Laser didn't stay down for long.

Coming out for the second half of the period, the Laser flipped the script. Bryce Perkins buried a helper from Chad Pancoast at the 11:34 mark to equalize, and before the Jackalopes could find their footing, NICHOLAS POVERUD went to work. Set up by RONALD HERWIG, Poverud fired a laser of his own to take a 2-1 lead into the break.

### Period 2: The Seesaw Effect
The second period was a gritty affair that lived up to the “barn burner” billing. James Whitacre—showing he’s clearly in form—leveled the score early off a feed from ROBERT VERITY. The Jacks weren't done, as Tyler Wick struck at 7:37 to pull ahead. 

But you can’t keep COLTON TAYLOR down. Extending his red-hot goal-scoring streak to four games, Taylor went coast-to-coast just 35 seconds later, unassisted, to knot it at 3-3. The pace was blistering, the ice was chewed up, and the goalies were doing everything in their power to keep the puck from crossing the red line.

### Period 3 & Shootout: High Stakes Drama
The tension in the third was palpable. With just over eight minutes left, NICHOLAS POVERUD secured his brace, tapping in a beautiful pass from NATHAN HOGUE to put the Laser up by one. The Jackalopes, refusing to go home empty-handed, pushed hard, and Todd Thompson finally cracked the egg with 1:25 remaining, courtesy of another crisp Verity assist.

After a scoreless overtime period that felt like a tactical chess match on skates, we headed to the shootout. The atmosphere was thick enough to cut with a hockey stick. Todd Thompson stepped up for the Jackalopes and delivered, but RONALD HERWIG answered the call for the Laser, keeping the deadlock intact.

### The Aftermath
In the standings, this result keeps things interesting. The Laser (now 2-2-1) cling to their spot, still searching for that consistent winning rhythm, while the Jackalopes (1-3-1) walk away with a hard-earned point that should help them climb out of the bottom tier.

It wasn't a win, but it was a show. As the players headed to the showers, the message was clear: in this league, you’re only as good as your next shift. We’ll be watching to see if the Laser can ignite a winning streak or if the Jackalopes can use this momentum to hop up the rankings next week. 

Stay tuned, hockey fans—the season is only heating up!
