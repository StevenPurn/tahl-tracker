# A Barn-Burner at the Barn: Lodge and Yetis Skate to a Wild 5-5 Deadlock

**By: Your Local Rink-Side Reporter**  
**Date:** May 31, 2026

If you weren't at the rink this morning, you missed a classic. We witnessed a tilt that had everything: end-to-end rushes, heavy traffic in the blue paint, and enough drama to make a goalie’s head spin. When the final buzzer sounded, the Lodge and the Yetis found themselves all knotted up at 5-5, proving once and for all that in this league, the ice is never safe.

### First Period: Trading Punches
The game started with the intensity of a coffee-deprived hockey parent at 11:00 AM. The Yetis struck first at 10:02 when Corey Favier hammered one home off a sweet feed from Jason Fennell. But the Lodge—cool as a glacier—answered back just before the intermission horn when Scott Dorough went coast-to-coast, rifling a biscuit into the twine to tie it up at 17.8 seconds remaining. 

The Yetis regained the lead early, but Garrett Williams kept the Lodge in the thick of it with a gritty individual effort at the 8:01 mark. We headed into the break tied, and the tension in the building was thick enough to cut with a skate blade.

### Second Period: The Lodge Takes Command
Whatever was said in the Lodge locker room during the break, it worked. Braden Trinka, riding high on a scoring streak, tucked one away at the 2:01 mark, and shortly after, Jonathon Anderson doubled the lead, finishing a beautiful setup from Louis Luongo. The Lodge looked like they were putting the Yetis in the deep freeze, heading into the final frame with a two-goal cushion.

### Third Period: The Yetis’ Furious Rally
Hockey is a game of momentum, and it’s a fickle mistress. Trinka notched his second of the game at 12:21, putting the Lodge up 5-2. But the Yetis aren't a team that fades away quietly. After Dylan Calhoun headed to the sin bin for high-sticking, Jason Fennell capitalized on the power play. 

Suddenly, the Yetis caught fire. Jason Bahr buried one at 1:16, and Corey Favier completed a legendary comeback effort with just 41 seconds left on the clock, silencing the Lodge supporters and forcing a deadlock at 5-5. 

### The Shootout: Ice in the Veins
After a penalty-ridden finish—including a late unsportsmanlike call against Lauchlin Cook—we went to the shootout. The nerves were rattling the plexiglass, but Corey Favier stayed cool for the Yetis, sliding his attempt past the twine. Not to be outdone, Jonathon Anderson stepped up for the Lodge, deking the keeper out of his skates to keep the drama alive. 

When the dust settled, both teams walked away with a share of the points in a result that felt like a win for the fans and a "what-if" for the benches.

### The Landscape
With this draw, the Yetis move to 0-1-1 on the young season, showing they have the grit to claw back from the brink of disaster. For the Lodge, it’s a strong showing to open their campaign, proving they have the offensive firepower to hang with anyone. 

As the standings tighten up, one thing is certain: if these two squads meet again, make sure you clear your calendar. This was a 5-5 thriller that reminded us all why we love this beautiful, unpredictable game.
