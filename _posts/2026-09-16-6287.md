# Berzerkers Overpower Danglers in 7-4 Offensive Slugfest

If you enjoy defensive zone coverage, Tuesday night’s contest between the Berzerkers and the Dirty Danglers was likely an exercise in frustration. If, however, you prefer your hockey with the chaotic, high-event energy of a spreadsheet vibrating off a desk, then the 7-4 Berzerkers victory provided plenty to dissect. In a regular-season matchup that saw the momentum shift with the frequency of a faulty light switch, the Berzerkers’ ability to capitalize on unassisted transitions proved the ultimate difference-maker.

The Berzerkers set the tone early. Cody Stephens opened the scoring at the 13:01 mark of the first period, igniting a flurry of action that saw the two teams trade chances. While Dylan Calhoun managed to draw the Dirty Danglers level with 1:36 remaining in the opening frame, the Berzerkers responded with a blistering two-goal outburst from Sage Blubaugh and Evert Wilmeth in under thirty seconds. It was a statistical anomaly that left the Danglers’ bench visibly rattled.

"We were looking at our defensive pairings, and frankly, I think we were still looking at them when the puck hit the back of the net twice," joked a Danglers staffer following the match.

The second period was a testament to the "anything you can do, I can do better" school of hockey. Calhoun struck again for the Danglers at 10:55, but the Berzerkers’ Zachary Hughes answered shortly thereafter, assisted by Nikolas Kemmler. The Danglers refused to fold, with Dylan Dillard burying an unassisted goal at 2:11 to keep the pressure on. However, Hughes—playing with the kind of efficiency usually reserved for a well-oiled machine—notched his second of the game on a setup from Ryan Vidal at the 8:00 mark, effectively stifling any momentum the Danglers were attempting to build.

By the time the third period rolled around, the game had descended into a tactical battle of attrition. Evert Wilmeth secured his second goal of the night at 10:54, assisted by Stephens, to stretch the lead. Despite a late-game penalty for interference, LC Sinor managed to shake off the box-time rust to score a gritty unassisted goal with just 17 seconds left on the clock, putting the final nail in the coffin. Daniel Maxwell added a late consolation goal for the Danglers, but it was a case of too little, too late.

The discipline charts for the evening suggested a game that grew chippy as the deficit widened. Brandon Patocka’s unsportsmanlike conduct penalty in the final minute and Ryan Vidal’s interference call at 6:55 highlighted a frantic closing sequence that saw the Berzerkers successfully weather the storm to secure their first win of the season.

With this result, the Berzerkers vault forward in the standings, moving away from the logjam at the bottom of the table. For the Dirty Danglers, who remain winless, the challenge will be tightening up the transition game that allowed far too many unassisted odd-man opportunities. In a league defined by thin margins, the Berzerkers proved tonight that when the puck is moving, efficiency is the only statistic that truly counts.
