# The Lodge Finally Breaks the Ice with Thrilling Shootout Win Over Mojo

If you’ve been waiting for the Lodge to turn their season around, Tuesday night was the night to be at the rink. In a back-and-forth thriller that had the barn buzzing, the Lodge finally snapped their losing skid, edging out Mojo 6-5 in a high-octane shootout finish.

For a team that had been winless in their last five, the Lodge played like they had a point to prove. Braden Trinka opened the scoring early, but Mojo wouldn't go quietly, answering back through Doug Lowry to keep things knotted up. By the second period, the tension was thick enough to cut with a skate blade. After Brandon Chouinard put Mojo ahead late in the frame, Jonathon Anderson—riding a hot hand with a point streak—dented the twine to keep the Lodge within striking distance.

"We knew we were due for a bounce," said one Lodge player during the intermission, struggling to catch his breath. "The ice was tilted against us for weeks, but tonight, we decided to tilt it back."

The third period was an absolute fever dream of momentum swings. Mojo’s Stephen Jeffery looked like he might play spoiler with a gritty unassisted effort, but the Lodge responded with a fierce two-goal flurry from Trinka and Matthew Seddelmeyer. Just when it looked like the Lodge might run away with it, Mojo’s Austin Lowry decided he wasn't finished, potting a pair of goals in quick succession to reclaim the lead for Mojo.

But the Lodge had one last trick up their sleeve. With time ticking down, Michael Randall—carrying that sweet one-game goal streak momentum—found the back of the net off feeds from Mark Stacy and Gabi Gibson to force the game into the shootout.

Once in the shootout, the Lodge skaters looked cool as a fresh sheet of Zamboni ice. Christopher Brooks and Jered Pieschke both found the back of the net, sealing the deal and securing that elusive win.

For the Lodge, the victory provides a massive shot of confidence as they look to climb out of the basement of the standings. Meanwhile, Mojo sees their recent point streak snapped in a heartbreaker. Both teams proved one thing tonight: in this league, the gap between the bottom and the middle is razor-thin, and the Lodge is officially back in business.
