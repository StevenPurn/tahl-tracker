# A Clinical Masterclass: Mighty Drunks Steamroll Average Hoes in 12-0 Rout

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**  
*August 2, 2026*

If you were looking for a competitive nail-biter on this breezy August evening, you certainly didn’t find it at the rink. In a game that can only be described as a total tactical demolition, the league-leading **Mighty Drunks (9-0-1)** flexed their championship muscles, putting on an absolute clinic against the **Average Hoes (1-8-0)** in a 12-0 shutout that left the scoreboard operator’s fingers tired.

Coming into the night, the Drunks were looking to solidify their grip on the top spot, and they didn’t just knock on the door—they kicked it off the hinges.

### Period 1: The Avalanche Begins
The Drunks didn’t waste any time establishing the pace. **Tyler Waller** set the tone early at 10:22, snapping home an unassisted marker that seemed to take the wind right out of the Hoes’ sails. From there, it was a feeding frenzy. Waller struck again just 36 seconds later off a feed from **Nathan Fahland**, and the floodgates were officially open.

**Matthew Holland** joined the party with a pair of goals, both set up by the playmaker **Jared Hallahan**, who was threading needles all night. Before the period could even catch its breath, **Cian Slattery** (assisted by Connor Slattery), **Remigiusz Ferenc**, and **Daron Ross** all found the back of the net. By the time the horn sounded, it was 7-0. The Hoes' goaltender looked like a man trying to stop bullets with a toothpick, while the Drunks were turning the crease into a high-traffic highway.

### Period 2: Grinding the Gears
The second frame saw the Drunks adopt a more methodical, suffocating style of play. They didn’t let up, though. **Daron Ross** buried his second of the night at 11:12, once again finding space off a Fahland assist. **James Blythe III** put a bow on the period at 13:34 with a silky-smooth unassisted goal, dancing through the defense before picking his corner.

Credit to the Hoes’ defensive corps, who tried to tighten the gaps, but the Drunks’ cycle game was simply too fast, too frequent, and too physical.

### Period 3: The Finishing Touches
With the game well out of reach, the Drunks stayed disciplined, refusing to coast. **Tyler Waller** capped off a monster individual performance by completing his hat trick—and then his "poker" (four goals)—striking at 10:19 and again at 2:48. Sandwiching those tallies was **Nathan Fahland**, who rewarded his teammates with a goal of his own at 12:48, courtesy of a beautiful dish from Matthew Holland.

The Mighty Drunks' netminder had a quiet night at the office, but stayed sharp enough to ensure the "zero" stayed on the board, securing the shutout with a performance that was as much about positioning as it was about stopping rubber.

### The Big Picture
For the **Mighty Drunks**, this win is a statement. With a 9-0-1 record, they are essentially playing a different sport than the rest of the league. They’ve got depth, they’ve got chemistry, and more importantly, they’ve got that "killer instinct" that turns a win into a rout. They remain firmly planted in the driver's seat of the standings.

For the **Average Hoes**, it’s a night to file away in the "forget about it" folder. Sitting at 1-8-0, they’ll need to regroup and find some answers on the defensive end if they hope to climb out of the basement.

The Drunks march on, and if they keep playing with this level of intensity, someone is going to need to bring a bigger scoreboard next week.
