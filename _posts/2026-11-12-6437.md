# Gold as Ice Bronze Shakes Off the Rust in 4-2 Win Over the Lodge

It was a battle at the bottom of the table on Wednesday night, but for Gold as Ice Bronze, the result felt like gold indeed. In a matchup between two teams desperate for a spark, Gold as Ice Bronze found their rhythm to skate past the Lodge with a 4-2 victory, putting a much-needed notch in the win column.

The Lodge came out looking to break their four-game skid early, with Tanner Bemies finding the back of the net unassisted just over five minutes into the opening frame. It was the kind of gritty, lunch-pail goal that usually sets a tone, but Gold as Ice Bronze wasn't about to let this one slip away.

The turning point? A classic Triston Wilson masterclass. Wilson, riding a white-hot four-game goal streak, proved why he’s the man with the golden touch. He leveled the score at 5:45 off a feed from Dylan Dillard, and just over a minute later, the momentum shifted firmly into the Bronze corner. Mark DeSimone cleaned up a beautiful play orchestrated by Thomas Dewey and Lance Levasseur, giving Gold as Ice Bronze a 2-1 lead heading into the first intermission.

"We finally stopped playing like we were wearing lead boots," said a grinning Bronze bench boss after the final buzzer. "We moved the puck, we found the seams, and honestly, we just stopped overthinking the geometry of the rink."

The middle frame was a tighter affair, defined by defensive grit and a lone trip to the sin bin for Wilson, but the real fireworks were saved for the final act. 

The Lodge made things interesting late in the third when Michael Randall fired home a pass from Jonathon Anderson and Jess Herd to cut the lead to 2-2 with just over six minutes left on the clock. For a moment, the tension in the rink was thick enough to cut with a skate blade. 

But Wilson wasn’t done. Just over ten minutes later, he iced his second goal of the night—assisted by the tireless Lance Levasseur—to regain the lead. Moments later, Dylan Calhoun hammered the final nail into the coffin, converting a pass from Daniel Munson to put the game out of reach at 4-2.

Despite a late interference penalty against the Lodge’s Matthew Seddelmeyer, Gold as Ice Bronze stayed disciplined enough to kill the clock and secure the two points. 

For the Lodge, the loss marks a difficult stretch as they search for answers to turn their season around. On the other side, Gold as Ice Bronze leaves the rink tonight with a bit of swagger back in their step, having proven that even at the bottom of the standings, the ice is there for the taking if you’re willing to skate hard enough for it.
