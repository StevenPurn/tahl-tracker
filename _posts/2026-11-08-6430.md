# Valkyries Keep Rolling with Shutout Win Over Mother Puckers

If you were looking for a high-octane, barn-burner full of end-to-end chaos, you probably should’ve looked somewhere other than the rink this Saturday evening. But if you appreciate a clinic in defensive composure and the kind of suffocating forecheck that turns an opponent’s breakout into a headache, then the Valkyries’ 2-0 victory over the Mother Puckers was a masterclass. 

The Valkyries came into this one sitting pretty at the top of the standings, and they played like a team that knows exactly where they want to be. The Mother Puckers, looking to snap their slide, fought hard, but the Valkyries’ defensive shell was tighter than a new pair of skates fresh out of the freezer.

For most of the first two periods, it felt like two prize fighters circling each other, trading jabs but refusing to throw the haymaker. The ice was tilted, but nothing was clicking until the 13:10 mark of the second. That’s when Jessica Hopwood reminded everyone why she’s currently the league’s most dangerous skater. Taking a crisp feed from Makenzie Giove, Hopwood buried it to keep her goal streak alive at three games. It was the kind of goal that feels like a weight being lifted, and it finally broke the tension that had been hanging over the bench like a thick fog.

From there, the Valkyries leaned into their bread and butter: smart, puck-possession hockey. The Mother Puckers tried to ramp up the pressure in the third, but every time they looked like they might find a lane, a Valkyries defender was there to shut the door. 

Just when it looked like the Mother Puckers might pull their goalie and make a desperate scramble for the equalizer, Haley Griffin put the game on ice with only 4.4 seconds left on the clock. It was an unassisted dagger that sent the Valkyries fans home happy and left the Puckers wondering what could have been.

"I think we just stayed patient," said one Valkyries player while unlacing her skates after the final buzzer. "We knew they were hungry, but we just stuck to the script. Sometimes a 2-0 win is a whole lot more satisfying than a 7-6 shootout."

With the win, the Valkyries continue their absolute tear through the regular season, cementing their spot at the top of the table. They’re playing with a confidence that’s hard to ignore, and right now, they look like the team to beat in this league. As for the Mother Puckers, they’ll have to head back to the drawing board to find their offensive rhythm again. But hey, that’s hockey—some nights the puck finds the twine, and some nights, it just finds the boards.
