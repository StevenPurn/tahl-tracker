# A Blistering Morning at the Rink: Shooting Blanks Turn Up the Heat on Renegades

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: June 7, 2026**

If you were hoping for a quiet Sunday morning cup of coffee, you were in the wrong place at 9:45 AM. The ice at the arena wasn't just cold—it was scorched. In a game that started with a frenetic pace and ended with a scoreboard that looked more like a typo than a final tally, the Shooting Blanks put on an absolute clinic, dismantling the Renegades 13-3 to cement their spot at the top of the standings.

### Period 1: The Best Offense is, well, The Bests
The Shooting Blanks came out of the gate like they had a dinner reservation they couldn't miss. Aaron Best kicked things off at the 10:58 mark, fed beautifully by Bobby Watson and Bret Kurth. Elliott Langford and Kurth followed suit in short order, turning the Renegades’ zone into a revolving door.

The Renegades, however, refused to be buried early. Matthew Rankin decided he’d had enough of the one-sided affair, finding the twine twice in less than 30 seconds to breathe some life into the Renegades' bench. But just as the momentum started to shift, the "Best" brothers took the reins. Hunter Best tallied back-to-back goals in the final two minutes of the period, heading into the intermission with the Blanks holding a commanding 5-2 lead.

### Period 2: Tightening the Vise
The second frame was all about the Blanks’ defensive discipline and their ability to capitalize on the smallest of windows. With the Renegades struggling to find traffic in front of the net, Hunter Best picked up his third of the day at 13:45. 

Tensions boiled over briefly at the 3:02 mark when Dominic Najjar headed to the sin bin for interference. It was a costly mistake; Bret Kurth hammered home the power-play goal shortly after, effectively silencing any hope of a Renegades comeback. By the end of the second, it was clear that the Blanks weren't just playing hockey—they were playing keep-away.

### Period 3: The Goal-Scoring Carousel
If the first two periods were a storm, the third was a full-blown hurricane. The Blanks rattled off a barrage of goals, with Bobby Watson turning in a masterclass performance as a playmaker and finisher. Aaron Best added two more to his personal tally, while Elliott Langford and Watson continued to find the back of the net with surgical precision.

Chris Cahill managed to provide a late glimmer of offense for the Renegades at 7:48, but by then, the game had long since migrated into "total domination" territory. 

### The Aftermath
Final Score: **Shooting Blanks 13, Renegades 3.**

For the Shooting Blanks, this is a statement win. They entered the morning as the league leaders, and they’re leaving with a goal differential that would make a statistician blush. Bobby Watson’s playmaking was nothing short of legendary today—the man could pass a puck through a keyhole—and the chemistry between the Best brothers and Kurth is currently the most dangerous weapon in the league.

For the Renegades, it’s a morning to scrub from the memory banks. While Rankin showed flashes of brilliance, the team struggled to find answers for a Blanks offense that was firing on all cylinders from the drop of the puck. 

**Standings Update:** The Shooting Blanks remain the team to beat, extending their lead at the top of the table. The Renegades now have a long week of film study ahead of them to figure out how to stop the bleeding before their next outing. 

*Until next time, keep your sticks on the ice and your head on a swivel.*
