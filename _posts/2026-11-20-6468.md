# Ice Breakers Chill the Joes in 7-4 Offensive Slugfest

The ice at the local rink was less of a frozen surface and more of a demolition derby runway last night as the Ice Breakers out-gunned the Average Joes in a 7-4 thriller. Entering the contest as the third and fourth seeds respectively, both squads skated with the kind of intensity usually reserved for the last slice of pizza at a team banquet, but it was the Ice Breakers who ultimately carved out the win, proving that when it comes to the standings, they have no intention of breaking a sweat while climbing the ladder.

The game started with the subtlety of a freight train. Justin Horn put the Average Joes on the board just over a minute into the first period with a helper from Aric Soule, but the lead was shorter-lived than a budget goalie’s confidence. The Ice Breakers responded with a relentless two-punch flurry from Daniel Reyes and Justice Dick, flipping the script and sending the Joes into the first intermission trailing 2-1.

If the first period was an appetizer, the second period was an all-you-can-eat buffet of goals. Reyes, clearly having a night where he felt the puck was magnetized to his stick, notched his second of the game early on. David Hardy followed suit, extending his point streak to six games and making the Joes’ defense look like they were trying to stop a tidal wave with a screen door. Just as the Ice Breakers seemed to be pulling away, the Joes staged a frantic resurgence. Jonathan Bransford and the ever-reliable Ryan Martinez—extending his impressive goal streak to six games—found the back of the net in quick succession. 

"We had them on the ropes, but they’re like a hydra," Joes captain Jonathan Bransford joked after the game, nursing a bruised shin. "You cut off one scoring chance, and three more appear out of thin air. It’s hard to build a fort when the other guys have a flamethrower."

The momentum shift was brief, however. Nathan Klassen slammed home an unassisted rocket, marking his third consecutive game with a goal and keeping the Ice Breakers’ offensive engine purring. 

The final frame saw both teams trading penalties like unwanted holiday fruitcakes, but the Ice Breakers maintained their composure. Dylan Walker found the net midway through the third, effectively putting the game on ice. While Nicole Baker managed a late-period goal for the Joes—sparked by a beautiful sequence from Martinez and Elizabeth Johnson—Justice Dick capped off his spectacular night with a final goal to secure the 7-4 victory.

With this win, the Ice Breakers cement their position as a legitimate force in the upper echelon of the standings, keeping pace with the league leaders. The Average Joes, meanwhile, see their recent win streak snapped, though they remain firmly in the mix. As both teams head back to the locker rooms, one thing is certain: if this is what these two look like in a mid-November regular season scrap, the rest of the league had better buckle their chin straps.
