# A Bloody Good Time: Tulsa Murder Rallies to Stun Laser in 6-4 Thriller

**By: "Rusty" Blades, Rinkside Beat Reporter**

**TULSA** – If you like your hockey served with a side of grit and a dash of mayhem, Tuesday night at the rink was your kind of party. The Laser came out looking like they were ready to turn the Tulsa Murder into a crime scene, but the Murder had other plans, digging out of a three-goal hole to skate away with a 6-4 victory in a game that felt like it had enough plot twists for a prime-time drama.

### The Laser Blitz
The game started at a blistering pace, and for the first 15 minutes, it was all Laser. Chad Pancoast kicked off the festivities at the 10:06 mark of the first, burying a sweet feed from Bryce Perkins. Then, the man of the hour, Colton Taylor, decided to put on a masterclass. Taylor notched two quick ones—including a snipe that found the "top shelf where mom keeps the cookies"—to push the Laser to a commanding 3-0 lead. 

Just when the Laser faithful were getting comfortable, Spencer Charles breathed some life back into the Tulsa bench, finding the back of the net to make it 3-1 heading into the intermission.

### The Murderous Turnaround
Whatever was said in the Tulsa locker room during the break, they should bottle it and sell it. The Murder came out with their hair on fire in the second period. Adam Hoffdahl trimmed the lead early, and despite a sticky situation involving a hooking penalty on Charles, the momentum remained firmly in the Murder's grasp.

Pete Fleming—who is currently making goal-scoring look like a hobby—kept his heater going with a smooth finish, followed shortly by Nicholas Olson putting one home to complete the comeback. Just like that, a 3-0 deficit had been erased. The atmosphere in the barn was electric, and you could practically feel the condensation dripping from the rafters as the intensity ramped up.

### Penalties, Pucks, and Polish
The third period wasn’t just hockey; it was a street fight on ice. Tempers flared at 2:34 when Nicholas Olson and Laser’s Ronald Herwig earned matching 10-minute misconducts for unsportsmanlike conduct. Not to be outdone, the two went back at it two minutes later, trading minor penalties for a slashing/unsportsmanlike combo.

Christopher Fleming briefly gave the Laser hope with a tally at the 5:51 mark, pulling the game to within one. But the Murder weren't interested in a heart-attack finish. Tom Mortimer struck back almost immediately, and Pete Fleming added his second of the night—a gritty, unassisted effort—to ice the cake. A late interference call on Ben Castagna served as the final exclamation point on a night of absolute chaos.

### The Standing Shake-Up
With this win, the **Tulsa Murder (4-2-0)** leapfrog up the standings, proving that their grit is just as dangerous as their skill. They’re sitting pretty in the upper echelon of the league, and with this kind of resilience, they’re going to be a nightmare for any goalie they face for the rest of the summer.

For the **Laser (2-3-1)**, it’s a tough pill to swallow. They had the gas to start the engine, but they couldn't keep the wheels on the wagon against a relentless Murder squad. They’ll need to tighten up the defense in front of the crease if they want to stop sliding down the table.

**Final Score: Tulsa Murder 6, Laser 4.**

Keep your sticks on the ice, folks. If tonight was any indication, the rest of this season is going to be one wild ride.
