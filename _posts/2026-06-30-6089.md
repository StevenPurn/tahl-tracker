# Bison Stampede: Tulsa Runs Wild Over Lite Beer Eagles in 10-1 Thrashing

**TULSA** – If you were hoping for a defensive chess match at the rink on this fine June evening, you were certainly in the wrong building. In a game that saw the scoreboard light up more often than a Friday night on Bourbon Street, the Tulsa Bison absolutely dismantled the Lite Beer Eagles, skating away with a commanding 10-1 victory that wasn't even as close as the score suggests.

Coming into the night, the Bison (#5) were looking to solidify their standing, while the Eagles (#9) were searching for consistency. By the time the final buzzer rang, the Bison had sent a message loud and clear: they aren't just hungry; they’re starving for a climb up the leaderboard.

### First Period: The Stampede Begins
The Bison didn’t wait for the ice to settle. Just 8.9 seconds into the opening frame, **JW DePriest**—who was clearly playing like his hair was on fire—ripped one home to start the party. Assisted by Geoffery Rash and the ever-dangerous Juan Padilla, it was the fastest statement in town.

Tulsa kept the pressure cooking. **Brett Mooney** doubled the lead at 11:06, and before the Eagles could even find their oxygen, DePriest notched his second of the night at 12:23. By the time Jeff Horvath headed to the box for slashing at the 3:12 mark, the Eagles were reeling. The Bison’s power play was surgical, with **Geoffery Rash** capitalizing just 14 seconds later to send the Bison into the intermission up 4-0. 

### Second Period: A Brief Glimmer
The second period saw the Bison maintain their offensive dominance, with **Lucas Jameson** extending the lead. The Eagles finally found a flicker of life at the 11:34 mark when **Ethan Hilton** went coast-to-coast, burying an unassisted beauty to break the shutout bid. 

But hope is a dangerous thing against a team like Tulsa. Just 17 seconds after an Eagles penalty, **Juan Padilla**—extending his impressive goal-scoring streak—responded immediately. **Geoffery Rash** added his second of the evening shortly after, and the Bison entered the final frame with a mountainous 7-1 lead. 

### Third Period: Closing the Book
With the game well in hand, the Bison refused to take their feet off the gas. **Brett Mooney** tickled the twine twice more in the third, completing a clinical performance, and **JW DePriest** rounded out his hat-trick performance at 12:49, dancing through the zone and putting it right where momma hides the cookies. 

For the Eagles, it was a night to forget, a "burn the tape" scenario where everything that could go wrong, did. The Bison’s puck movement was relentless, cycling through the Eagles’ zone with a frequency that left the Tulsa faithful cheering and the Lite Beer fans reaching for… well, an extra lite beer.

### Looking Ahead
With this 10-1 statement, the **Tulsa Bison (4-2-0)** make a massive statement in the standings. They’ve proven that when their offense is clicking, they can run almost anyone out of the building. They’ll look to carry this momentum forward, especially with the hot hands of DePriest and Padilla anchoring their attack.

For the **Lite Beer Eagles (2-3-0)**, this is a bitter pill to swallow. They’ll need to regroup quickly, shore up the defensive gaps, and find a way to get back in the win column before they slide further down the table. 

It was a barn burner, a blowout, and a bonafide Bison blast. That’s hockey, folks—sometimes you’re the hammer, and sometimes you’re the nail. Tonight, the Bison were doing all the pounding.
