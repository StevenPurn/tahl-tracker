# Berzerkers Bash Their Way Past Fossil Hockey in 6-1 Rout

If you showed up to the rink expecting a polite morning skate on this fine October 18th, you were in the wrong building. The Berzerkers walked into the arena like they owned the place and promptly treated Fossil Hockey to a 6-1 drubbing that left the spectators rubbing their eyes and the Fossil bench looking for answers.

The tone was set early by the Berzerkers’ dynamic duo of Jeff Orr and Stephen Jeffery. Orr, who seems to have a magnetic attraction to the back of the net these days, opened the scoring at the 12:32 mark of the first period, courtesy of a textbook dish from Jeffery. Just a few minutes later, with Brandon Johnson sitting in the box for hooking, the pair decided to run the exact same play back for an encore. It was a beautiful display of chemistry that left the Fossil defense looking like they were stuck in quicksand.

Fossil Hockey finally showed a pulse in the second period when Tyler Waller managed to solve the Berzerkers’ goaltending, tucking one home off an assist from Eron Richards. For a brief moment, it looked like the Fossils might claw their way back into the fray. But the Berzerkers aren't the kind of team that lets you hang around when they’ve got blood in the water.

Zac Hughes, who’s been playing with the kind of intensity that makes your teeth rattle just watching from the glass, responded almost immediately. He went coast-to-coast to bury an unassisted beauty just over a minute after the Fossil goal. That marker seemed to break the dam. Stephen Jeffery added his own name to the scoresheet shortly after, followed by Cody Stephens—who found the twine after a crisp setup from Dylan Calhoun—sending the Berzerkers into the third period with a commanding lead.

"We just kept the feet moving," said one Berzerker after the game, still dripping with sweat and sporting a grin wider than the crease. "When the puck is moving like that, it feels less like hockey and more like geometry."

The third period turned into a chippy affair, with enough penalties to keep the timekeeper busy, but the Berzerkers stayed disciplined enough to keep the Fossils at bay. Hughes put the cherry on top of the sundae with his second goal of the day, an unassisted effort that sealed the 6-1 final.

It’s a tough morning for Fossil Hockey, who couldn't find their rhythm despite their recent win streak. Meanwhile, the Berzerkers look like a team on a mission, climbing the standings and proving that their 2-1-1 start wasn't just a fluke. If they keep playing with this kind of venom, the rest of the league might want to start keeping their heads on a swivel every time they see those jerseys coming down the ice.
