# Bison Stampede: Tulsa Overwhelms Ice Breakers in 12-6 Scoring Frenzy

If you showed up to the rink on Tuesday night expecting a quiet, low-scoring defensive struggle, you were in the wrong building. The Tulsa Bison turned the late-night ice into an absolute track meet, erasing an early deficit to shell the Ice Breakers Plus 12-6 in a game that featured more red lights than a downtown intersection at rush hour.

The Ice Breakers came out of the gate looking like they were ready to snap their winless streak. EDWARD STERN was the early architect of chaos, finding the back of the net twice in the opening period, complemented by a TYLER WALLER strike and a solo effort from GAGE WALKER. By the time the dust settled on a wild first frame, the Ice Breakers had a 4-2 lead, and it looked like the Bison were going to be riding home with a long face.

But whatever was said in the Tulsa locker room during the break, it worked. 

"We basically decided that playing defense was optional for both teams, so we might as well win the race to double digits," joked Bison forward Brett Mecham, who had himself a night for the highlight reels.

The second period was a masterclass in momentum shifts. The Ice Breakers extended their lead to 6-2 early, courtesy of a second goal from WALKER and a tally from TOBIAS ROEDING. Then, the dam broke. The Bison went on an offensive tear that shifted the atmosphere from competitive to clinical. Between the 3:37 and 9:30 marks of the second period, the Bison scored five unanswered goals in a display of offensive efficiency that left the Ice Breakers’ bench looking like they’d just seen a ghost. 

Mecham was at the center of the hurricane, racking up points as if he were collecting stamps, while Geoffery Rash and Brian Cuff turned the Ice Breakers’ zone into their personal practice facility. By the time the buzzer sounded to end the second, the Bison had flipped the script entirely, heading into the final frame with a 7-6 lead.

If the second period was the comeback, the third was a victory lap. The Bison didn't take their foot off the gas, rattling off five more goals to put the game firmly out of reach. DAVID GUAJARDO and Lucas Jameson joined the scoring spree, while the defense—once they tightened their skates—kept the Ice Breakers off the board for the duration of the final period.

For the Ice Breakers, it’s a tough result in a season that has been defined by defensive lapses. For the Bison, this 12-6 win is a massive momentum builder. Moving to 3-3-1, Tulsa proved they have the firepower to climb out of the middle of the pack. They didn't just win; they made a statement that if you’re going to play them, you’d better bring a goalie with a bulletproof vest and a whole lot of conditioning. 

As for the Ice Breakers, they’ll look to regroup. Tonight, the only thing they managed to break was their own defensive structure. Both teams are back at it soon, but for tonight, the Bison are the ones enjoying the spoils of a very, very busy scoreboard.
