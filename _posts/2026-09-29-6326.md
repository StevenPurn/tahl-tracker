# Gold as Ice Thaws Out the Competition in 6-2 Victory Over Kraken

It’s often said that you can’t buy chemistry, but on Sunday night, Gold as Ice looked like they’d just struck gold in the locker room. In a dominant display of grit and offensive flow, Gold as Ice skated away with a 6-2 victory over the Kraken Cold Ones, finally cracking the win column and proving that their early-season record was just a minor frostbite.

The game started with an ironic twist just 13 minutes in when Daniel Maxwell, fresh off a stint in the penalty box for tripping, stepped onto the ice and immediately buried a beauty from Joshua Hass to put Gold as Ice on the board. The Kraken, not ones to let a cold snap linger, answered back later in the first period when Gregory Boyer cleaned up a feed from Robert Tyree to even the score at 1-1.

If the first period was a cautious handshake, the second period was a full-blown wrestling match. Ryan Martinez gave the Kraken a brief 2-1 lead early in the frame, capitalizing on another smart assist from Tyree. But that’s when the momentum shifted permanently into the Gold as Ice corner. Following an interference call on the Kraken, Rachel Colombo knotted things up, sparking a rally that the Cold Ones simply couldn’t extinguish. Before the buzzer signaled the end of the second, Dylan Dillard added a tally to give Gold as Ice a lead they would never relinquish.

"We just stopped skating in quicksand," joked a buoyant Mark DeSimone after the final whistle. "Once the puck started finding the back of the net instead of the post, the bench just exhaled. It felt like we finally remembered how to play hockey."

The third period was an offensive masterclass for Gold as Ice. Even while shorthanded, the team looked dangerous. Aden Ingram proved that his hot streak is no fluke, netting a short-handed goal midway through the final frame off a crafty setup from Dillard. Mark DeSimone kept the pressure on with an unassisted solo effort, and Ingram capped his monster night with his second goal of the game, courtesy of a slick pass from Dickie Williams.

The Kraken Cold Ones, who had been looking to climb the standings, found themselves stymied by a Gold as Ice defense that tightened up significantly as the game progressed. Despite the Kraken's best efforts to generate secondary chances, the Gold as Ice blue line turned the defensive zone into a "no-fly zone."

For Gold as Ice, the win is a massive confidence booster as they look to build some momentum. For the Kraken, it’s a tough night at the office, but with a league this competitive, they’ll have plenty of chances to put the ice back in their drinks and cool down their opponents in the coming weeks. Tonight, however, the glory belongs to Gold as Ice—who proved that when they play with this kind of polish, they’re anything but cold.
