# Mother Puckers Finally Find the Net in 6-0 Rout

If you were looking for a sign that the Mother Puckers were done playing nice, you should have been at the rink this Saturday afternoon. After a tough start to the season, the Puckers finally shook off the rust, the bad bounces, and the early-season jitters, treating the hometown crowd to a dominant 6-0 shutout victory over the Power Puck Girls.

It was the kind of game where everything clicked. The Puckers looked like they’d had their morning coffee injected straight into their skates, buzzing around the zone with a newfound hunger. The tone was set early in the first period when Abigail Nisbet hammered one home at the 13:25 mark, courtesy of a slick setup from Makenzie Giove. It was as if a dam had broken. Before the period was out, Emily LaRance had turned the ice into her personal playground, lighting the lamp twice—once at 14:31 and again late in the frame at 2:56—to put the Puckers up 3-0. 

Watching from the bleachers, you could almost hear the Power Puck Girls’ collective sigh as they headed to the locker room. They tried to adjust, but the Puckers weren’t in a charitable mood. Right at the buzzer to end the second, Melissa McLauchlin added salt to the wound, burying a feed from LaRance to stretch the lead to four.

"I think we just stopped overthinking it," said one Pucker after the game, while wiping a layer of well-earned sweat from her forehead. "We decided to stop counting the losses and start counting the shots."

The final frame was all about the Nisbet show. She notched two more in the third—at 10:47 and 4:08—to secure her hat trick and put the game firmly on ice. The chemistry between Nisbet, McLauchlin, and LaRance was absolute magic, moving the puck with the kind of telepathic precision that usually takes a whole season to develop.

For the Power Puck Girls, it was a day to quickly put in the rearview mirror. They’ve been riding a rocky road lately, and this 6-0 walloping is a tough pill to swallow. As for the Mother Puckers, they’ve finally tasted blood in the water. Sitting at the bottom of the standings coming into today, they proved they aren't about to just roll over and let the rest of the league walk over them. If they can bring this same intensity to their next tilt, that 0-4-1 start might just turn into a very interesting comeback story.
