# See You Later: Carson See Powers Chix with Stix to 6-3 Victory Over Power Puck Girls

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: June 14, 2026**

The ice was crisp, the humidity was low, and the energy at the rink was electric as the Chix with Stix looked to climb out of the basement of the standings against a hungry Power Puck Girls squad. By the time the final horn sounded on this mid-June Saturday, we had witnessed a classic display of offensive firepower. When the dust settled, the Chix with Stix walked away with a convincing 6-3 win, proving they’ve got more than just a clever name.

### Period 1: The Offensive Opening Salvo
The game started with the intensity of a double-espresso at 5:00 AM. The Chix struck first at the 10:21 mark when Rachel Gold found the back of the net, courtesy of a beautiful dish from Catherine Pearsall. It looked like the Chix might run away with it, but the Power Puck Girls answered back quickly to knot things up.

However, the Chix with Stix were not to be denied. Carson See began her personal highlight reel at 6:41, followed shortly by a smooth unassisted strike from Hannah Atkins at 9:10. The Chix headed into the break with a 3-1 lead, having controlled the flow of traffic in front of the net.

### Period 2: Hawkins Strikes Back
The second frame tightened up defensively, but don't tell that to Suzanne Hawkins. Staying hot on her point streak, Hawkins hammered one home at the 1:37 mark, cutting the deficit to a single goal and breathing new life into the Power Puck bench. 

Just when the momentum looked like it might swing toward the visitors, Carson See decided she wasn't done for the afternoon. At 3:02, See capitalized on a loose puck to bury her second of the game, restoring the two-goal cushion and sending the Chix into the final intermission with a comfortable 4-2 advantage.

### Period 3: The "See"-ing Eye Performance
If the first two periods were the appetizer, the third was the main course. Nicole Baker extended the lead to 5-2 at the 2:41 mark, beautifully set up by a crisp pass from Heather Cozad. 

The Power Puck Girls managed to claw one back at 5:59, but that would be the final gasp for the visitors. Showing the poise of a veteran, Carson See completed a spectacular hat trick at 6:34, sealing the 6-3 victory and firing a laser top-shelf where mom keeps the cookies. 

### Standing Tall
With this win, the **Chix with Stix (2-2-0)** shake off their recent inconsistency and leapfrog into the middle of the pack. It was a vital two points that should provide the group with a much-needed morale boost as they hunt down the Mother Puckers. 

For the **Power Puck Girls (0-3-0)**, the search for that elusive first win continues. While the offense showed signs of life—particularly from the ever-dangerous Suzanne Hawkins—the team will need to tighten up the defensive zone if they want to stop the bleeding and climb out of the cellar.

It was a barn burner, folks. The kind of game that reminds you exactly why we lace ‘em up. Until next time, keep your sticks on the ice and your eyes on the scoreboard!
