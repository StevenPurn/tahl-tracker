# Frozen Fireworks: Laser Edge Yetis in a 6-5 Nail-Biter

**By: Your Hometown Rink-Side Reporter**
**Date: May 28, 2026**

If you didn’t have a seat at the arena tonight, you missed a bona fide barn burner. The Yetis and the Laser treated the fans to a display of end-to-end action that felt more like a street hockey game fueled by pure adrenaline than a league matchup. When the final buzzer sounded, the Laser skated away with a 6-5 victory, but the ice was still smoking from the effort both teams put in.

### First Period: A Frenetic Opening
The game started with the throttle wide open. Braden Trinka drew first blood for the Yetis just 2:14 in, catching the Laser cold. But the Laser weren’t about to roll over; Ronald Herwig answered back seventy seconds later, followed by a snipe from James Whitacre to put the Laser up 2-1. Not to be outdone, the Yetis’ Denis Blaquiere hammered one home, knotting the score at 2-2 before the Zamboni even had a chance to think about its second-period duties.

### Second Period: The Tug-of-War
The middle frame was a gritty, grinding affair. Todd Thompson found the back of the net to give the Laser the lead, but the Yetis kept digging. As the penalties began to stack up—including a skirmish that saw both Seth Miller and Aidan Doyle sent to the sin bin for roughing—the intensity hit a boiling point. 

Skylar Miller and Denis Blaquiere connected to tie it up once more, but James Whitacre was feeling it tonight, snagging his second of the game to reclaim the lead. Braden Trinka refused to let the Yetis fade, burying an unassisted beauty to keep the scoreboard tied heading into the final frame.

### Third Period: The Drama Deepens
With the score deadlocked, the defenses tightened their laces. Whitacre completed his hat-trick magic midway through the third, putting the Laser ahead 5-4. With the clock ticking down and the Yetis looking for an equalizer, Brendan Burkman turned into the hero of the hour, burying a feed from Ben Gartner to force the tie. 

Despite a flurry of late-game penalties—a slash here, a trip there—neither side could find the back of the net in the dying minutes of regulation. We were headed to the one place every hockey fan loves to hate: the shootout.

### The Shootout: Cold Ice, Hot Hands
With the tension so thick you could cut it with a skate blade, the shootout came down to nerves. The Laser looked to Nicholas Poverud to provide the spark. Poverud stepped up, iced the nerves, and delivered the deciding goal that sent the Laser bench into a frenzy.

### The Takeaway
Tonight’s game was a testament to the "never-say-die" attitude of these squads. While the Yetis certainly proved they can hang with the best, the Laser showed the kind of clutch composure that wins championships. 

The Laser head home with two points in the bag and a confidence boost that’ll be tough to shake. For the Yetis, it’s a tough loss to swallow after such a gritty comeback effort, but they proved that their offense is firing on all cylinders. If these two keep playing like this, the rest of the league had better keep their heads on a swivel—this season is just getting started, and the race for the top of the standings is shaping up to be a real dogfight.
