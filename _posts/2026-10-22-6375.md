# Eagles Take Flight, Ground the Bison in 7-4 Scoring Spree

If you’re a fan of old-school, wide-open hockey where the goalies spend more time fishing pucks out of the twine than they do stretching their hamstrings, Wednesday night’s tilt at the rink was your kind of party. The Lite Beer Eagles, desperate to climb out of the basement, caught fire early and never looked back, downing the Tulsa Bison 7-4 in a game that felt like a track meet held on ice.

The Eagles came into this one sitting near the bottom of the standings, but they played like a team that had a few extra shots of espresso in the locker room. Corey Phillips broke the seal at 11:04 in the first, and just twenty seconds later, Ethan Hilton—who clearly didn't get the memo that a lead is supposed to be protected—doubled the damage with an unassisted laser. By the time Jesse Stockley added his own tally late in the frame, the Bison looked like they were still trying to find their skates.

"We were skating like we were trying to run through a sandbox in the first," one Bison supporter grumbled near the glass, nursing a lukewarm coffee. They weren't wrong. 

The middle frame didn't offer the Bison much respite. Jonathan McCormick extended the lead, and Ethan Hilton showed up again at the 5:20 mark to bag his second of the night. Joe Baker finally got the Bison on the board late in the second period, but at 5-1, the game felt less like a contest and more like a coronation for the Eagles' offense.

The third period, however, turned into a chaotic scramble. The Bison, realizing they had nowhere to go but up, started throwing the kitchen sink at the Eagles. Joe Baker netted his second of the night, followed quickly by a James DePriest goal that actually made the folks in the stands sit up and take notice. Just when it looked like the Bison might make things interesting, Jeff Horvath answered back for the Eagles to restore a three-goal cushion. 

The Bison clawed back once more through Brian Cuff, but Adam Strange put the final nail in the coffin with a late goal to secure the 7-4 victory. 

For the Eagles, this win is a much-needed breath of fresh air after a rough start to the season. They looked crisp, moved the puck with purpose, and most importantly, they capitalized on the chances they generated. While the Bison will surely be looking to tighten up their defensive zone coverage before their next outing, they have to tip their caps to an Eagles squad that played with the kind of hunger you usually only see when the beer fridge is running low.

It’s a long season, and while one win doesn't punch any tickets to the top of the table, the Eagles proved tonight that they’ve got plenty of flight left in them. As for the Bison, they’ll look to shake this one off and regain the form that had them sitting comfortably in the middle of the pack before tonight’s hiccup.
