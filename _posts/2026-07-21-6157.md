# Puckheads Snap the Skid: Douglas Hat Trick Leads the Charge Over Havok

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
*July 20, 2026*

If you’re a betting person, you probably steered clear of the Puckheads tonight. Coming into this 9:00 PM tilt riding a brutal five-game losing streak, the Puckheads looked like a team searching for an exit sign. But tonight at the arena, the script didn't just flip—it went through the glass. In a wild, penalty-filled affair, the Puckheads silenced their critics and stunned Havok, skating away with a massive 5-3 victory.

### Period 1: Sorting Out the Chaos
The game began with a flurry of whistles, as both teams seemed to have left their composure in the locker room. Robert Verity opened the scoring for Havok at 11:31, cashing in on helpers from Zachary Wade and Michael Henry to put the visitors up early. 

However, the period was defined by the officiating crew getting their cardio in. The Retkowski brothers—Richard and Andrew—found themselves cooling their heels in the sin bin early, creating a parade to the penalty box. Despite the heavy "traffic in front of the net" and a lack of flow, the Puckheads finally found some oxygen when Cameron Sartin buried a feed from Gregory Douglas at 5:30 to knot it up at 1-1.

### Period 2: The Douglas Show
If the first period was a test of patience, the second was a masterclass in opportunistic hockey. Logan Singleton reclaimed the lead for Havok with an unassisted individual effort, but the momentum shifted permanently shortly after.

Gregory Douglas, playing like a man possessed, leveled the score again at 11:17. From there, the Puckheads turned the screws. Richard Retkowski redeemed his earlier penalty minutes by finding the back of the net off a dish from Sartin, and Douglas struck again at 9:11, assisted by the tag-team effort of the Retkowski brothers. By the time the buzzer sounded for the second intermission, the Puckheads had turned a one-goal deficit into a 4-2 lead. The air in the building was thick enough to chew on, and the Puckheads were breathing much easier.

### Period 3: Closing the Barn Door
Havok wasn't going to roll over quietly, of course. Zachary Wade managed to slice the lead to one at 6:49 with a helper from Verity, bringing the Havok bench to life. The arena tension was ratcheted up to an eleven, with every dump-in and every board-battle feeling like a potential season-defining moment.

But just as Havok started to turn up the heat, Gregory Douglas put the exclamation point on his night. With 8:11 remaining, Douglas secured his hat trick, cleaning up a rebound off another Richard Retkowski setup to restore the two-goal cushion. 

The Puckheads’ defense held firm through the final minutes, swatting away Havok's late-game desperation flurries. When the final horn blew, the Puckheads finally broke their five-game drought, walking off the ice with a hard-earned 5-3 win.

### Looking Ahead
For the Puckheads (3-5-0), this win is a massive dose of adrenaline. After languishing at the bottom of the table, they’ve finally grabbed two points to draw even with the Blizzard, signaling that the basement isn't their permanent residence. 

For Havok (3-3-1), it’s a stinging loss that stalls their momentum. They’ll need to reset quickly to keep pace with the Killer Goats and Dirty Birds at the top of the standings. 

Tonight, however, the story belongs to Gregory Douglas. If he can bottle the energy he brought to the rink tonight, the rest of the league had better keep their heads on a swivel. The Puckheads are back in the win column, and in this league, that’s all that matters.
