# The Syndicate Finally Find Their Groove: A 5-1 Masterclass Over the Reapers

**By: "Rusty" Blades, Rinkside Beat Reporter**  
**Date: July 26, 2026**

If you were looking for a high-octane morning matinee at the local barn this Sunday, you surely found it. In a battle of the cellar-dwellers, The Syndicate (1-6-0) finally shook off the mid-season blues, handing the Reapers (1-6-1) a 5-1 reality check that had the building buzzing.

### Period 1: The Reapers Rise Early
The morning air was crisp, but the Reapers were the ones who brought the heat early on. At 7:55, Edgar Solorio showcased some nifty mitts, finding the back of the net unassisted to put the Reapers on the board. It looked for a moment like it might be another long day for The Syndicate, who spent much of the frame skating uphill, unable to solve the Reapers’ defensive shell.

### Period 2: The Momentum Shift
The second period saw the game turn into a bit of a chaotic circus—the kind that makes you spill your hot coffee in the bleachers. Both teams were guilty of having "too many cooks in the kitchen," as Travis Moore (Syndicate) and Tyler Waller (Reapers) both headed to the sin bin for "Too Many Men" penalties within a minute of each other.

The 4-on-4 hockey opened up the ice, and that’s when Michael Marshall decided to turn his personal furnace on. With less than a minute left in the period, Marshall buried a helper from Brett Mooney to equalize. Just when the Reapers thought they’d get to the locker room for a breather, Marshall struck again at 6:15, burying a solo effort that sent The Syndicate into the second intermission with all the momentum in the world. 

### Period 3: The Syndicate Run Wild
If the second was a wake-up call, the third was a full-blown alarm clock for the Reapers. The Syndicate came out skating like they had rockets attached to their skates. Brett Mecham extended the lead at 12:15, firing a laser that rattled the iron before dancing into the cage. 

But the real story of the final frame? Brett Mooney. After picking up an assist in the second, Mooney decided he wanted to be the main character, potting back-to-back goals—one at 1:36 and another at 8:08, the latter beautifully set up by Haley Griffin. The Reapers’ defense, which looked stout in the first, was suddenly giving up more high-quality real estate than a developer in a boomtown.

### The Aftermath
When the final buzzer sounded, the scoreboard read 5-1. For The Syndicate, this win is a massive breath of fresh air. They climb out of a three-way tie at the bottom of the heap, finally putting some distance between themselves and the Kraken Cold Ones. 

For the Reapers, it’s back to the drawing board. They had the early lead, but they couldn't keep up with the Syndicate’s transition game as the ice tilted decisively in the home team's favor. 

As we head into the next slate of games, The Syndicate proved today that the bottom of the standings is a fluid, dangerous place to be. If they keep finding "the top shelf where mom keeps the cookies" like they did this morning, this team might just make a run at the middle of the pack. 

**Final Score:**  
**The Syndicate: 5**  
**Reapers: 1**
