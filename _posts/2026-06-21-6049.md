# Bone Zone Rallies Late, Whips the Jackalopes in 4-3 Thriller

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: June 21, 2026**

If you weren't at the rink this evening, you missed a classic. What started as a frantic, back-and-forth scramble turned into a surgical display of offensive tenacity as The Bone Zone clawed their way back from an early deficit to clip the Jackalopes, 4-3.

The ice was hot at 4:45 PM, and for a moment, it looked like the Jackalopes were going to skate away with the hardware.

### Period 1: The Whitacre Wake-Up Call
The Jackalopes came out of the gate like a team possessed. James Whitacre, evidently feeling his oats, struck first at the 10:46 mark off a crisp feed from David Whitacre. Before the Bone Zone could even find their legs, Matthew Rudick sniped one home just twenty seconds later to put the 'Lopes up 2-0.

The Bone Zone looked wobbly, and after Dan Munson headed to the box for interference, the wheels were threatening to fall off. But hockey is a game of momentum shifts, and the Bone Zone found theirs in the dying minutes of the frame. With 4:12 left, **Tyler Wimpee** decided he wasn’t going to let this one slip away, burying a Bryce Perkins pass to cut the lead in half. 

Thirty-nine seconds later, Wimpee struck again—unassisted—turning the arena into a pressure cooker. Just when the Jackalopes thought they’d reach the break tied, John White shoveled one past the tendy off a nifty dish from Timothy N. See. Suddenly, the Bone Zone held a 3-2 lead, having scored three unanswered goals in less than four minutes of clock time. Talk about a momentum swing!

### Period 2: The Wimpee Hat-Trick Heroics
The second period turned into a defensive clinic with a side of grit. The Bone Zone kept their foot on the gas, and at the 1:58 mark, the "Wimpee Show" reached its crescendo. John White connected with Tyler Wimpee, who completed his natural hat-trick and extended his scorching goal-scoring streak to four games. 

The Jackalopes tried to claw back, but the Bone Zone defense tightened the screws, forcing the 'Lopes into desperation hockey. When Tyler Wick took a seat for hooking at the 7:26 mark, the Jackalopes were forced to play a desperate, man-down style of game to keep the lead from ballooning further.

### Period 3: Closing the Gate
The third period was a heavy-weight fight in a phone booth. Both sides traded hits, and the atmosphere was thick enough to cut with a skate blade. David Whitacre found himself in the sin bin for slashing with 10:23 remaining, putting the Jackalopes on their heels. 

However, the 'Lopes showed serious grit. With the net empty in spirit if not in personnel, James Whitacre netted his second of the night—assisted by Ronald Herwig and Nicholas Poverud—to bring the score to 4-3. The final two minutes were pure chaos, with traffic in front of the net so thick you’d need a bulldozer to clear it, but The Bone Zone held the fort to secure the victory.

### The Aftermath
With this victory, The Bone Zone (2-1-0) vaults forward, proving they aren't just here to fill a roster spot—they are here to climb the standings. They move to 4 points, deadlocking with the Mojo and Tulsa Leafs in the middle of the pack. 

The Jackalopes (1-3-0), meanwhile, fall into a tough spot, searching for consistency as they look to crawl out of the bottom half of the table.

It was a barn burner, folks. The Bone Zone gets the W, Wimpee gets the spotlight, and the fans? We get to go home knowing we just witnessed a high-octane classic. See you at the next drop!
