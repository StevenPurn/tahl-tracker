# The Drunks Pour It On: Mighty Drunks Blank Average Joes in Late-Night Shutout

If you were looking for a high-scoring barnburner on this fine September 24th, you might have been disappointed by the pace, but if you love a goalie clinic, the Mighty Drunks and the Average Joes delivered a masterclass in defensive tension. It took until the third period for the dam to finally break, but the Mighty Drunks eventually pulled away for a 3-0 victory, securing their first win of the season and leaving the Joes searching for answers.

For the better part of two periods, this game felt like a staring contest. Both teams were playing a tight, defensive brand of hockey that made the neutral zone look like a no-fly zone. The goalies were clearly the stars of the show early on, treating the pipes like best friends and shutting down every attempt to find twine.

"We were playing like we were trying to thread a needle with a mitten on," remarked one frustrated spectator near the glass.

The deadlock finally shattered at the 11:00 mark of the third period. Connor Slattery broke the ice for the Drunks, finding the back of the net in a scramble that finally got the momentum swinging in his team's favor. Once the seal was broken, the Drunks hit their stride like a Zamboni driver who just realized he’s late for his coffee break.

Just over eight minutes later, at the 2:24 mark, Glenn Prouse doubled the lead, firing home a beauty set up by Jared Hallahan and Matthew Holland. It was the kind of crisp, clinical passing play that makes you wonder why these two squads didn’t start moving the puck like that back in the first period. With the Joes desperately pulling their netminder to try and force a miracle comeback in the final ticks of the clock, Brett Lamb delivered the dagger, potting an unassisted empty-netter as the final horn sounded.

It was a tough night for the Average Joes, who remain stuck at one point in the standings after this result. On the flip side, the Mighty Drunks jump to four points, shaking off their early-season draw and proving they have the patience to grind out a win when the offense isn't firing on all cylinders.

While it wasn't the goal-fest some might have hoped for, the late-game fireworks were exactly the kind of "refreshment" the Drunks needed to kickstart their campaign. They’ll head into their next matchup with a fresh tally in the win column and a clean sheet, while the Joes will head back to the drawing board to figure out how to put some spark into that offense.
