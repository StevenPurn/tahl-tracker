# Silver Lining for the Hitmen: Blizzard Melted in Late-Night Barn Burner

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
*July 15, 2026*

There’s nothing quite like the smell of fresh ice and the sound of pucks pinging off the iron at 10:15 PM on a Tuesday. Last night’s tilt between the Hitmen Silver and the Blizzard was exactly what the doctor ordered for a mid-week hockey fix. While the thermometer outside might be cooling off, the Hitmen brought enough heat to leave the Blizzard looking more like a puddle in a 6-3 victory.

### First Period: The Hitmen Come Out Swinging
If you stepped away to grab a snack, you missed the Hitmen turning the ice into their personal playground. **Josh Moyer** kicked off the scoring frenzy early at 10:06, setting the tone for a rough night for the Blizzard defense. By the time the dust settled on the first, **Keegan Guthrie** and the in-form **Nicklas Clark** had padded the lead to 3-0. 

The Blizzard finally showed some heartbeat when **Jason Corcoran** went rogue, unassisted, to get his squad on the board, but the period was marred by a parade to the sin bin. With Hennenfent and Conway both spending time in the cooler for the Blizzard, the Hitmen smelled blood in the water.

### Second Period: Maintaining the Pressure
The second frame was all about Hitmen chemistry. Nicklas Clark, clearly playing with a hot stick, buried his second of the night at 10:52 off a nifty feed from Brooks Martin. Even with a slew of penalties—including a rare "Too Many Men" call against the Blizzard and some extracurricular roughing from the Hitmen—the momentum stayed firmly in Silver territory. 

**Skylar Miller** capped off a beautiful passing play from Moyer and Eric Guthrie to make it 5-1. The Blizzard managed a glimmer of hope when the surging **Cole Rush** found the back of the net off a Nelson Pina setup, but the Hitmen defense stayed tighter than a new pair of skates, keeping the Blizzard offense firmly on the perimeter.

### Third Period: Too Little, Too Late
The final frame saw the Blizzard attempt a desperate comeback. **Jerry Bates** added his name to the scoresheet with a gritty goal assisted by Hennenfent and Corcoran, cutting the deficit to three. But the Hitmen weren’t about to let this one slip through their fingers. Josh Moyer, capping off a stellar multi-point night, hammered the final nail into the coffin at the 7:40 mark, courtesy of Sleezer and Clark.

### The Breakdown: Standings and Stars
This was a classic case of the fourth-place team showing exactly why they belong in the upper tier of the standings. 

*   **Hitmen Silver (4-2-0):** They leapfrog the Havok with this win, cementing their status as a legitimate contender. Moyer, Clark, and the crew looked like a well-oiled machine. If they keep this level of cohesion, the teams above them better start looking over their shoulders.
*   **Blizzard (2-4-0):** It’s back to the drawing board. Tonight showed that while they have the individual talent—seeing Corcoran and Rush finding the net is a positive—the lack of discipline in the defensive zone and those early penalties dug them a hole they couldn't climb out of. They remain locked in a tight battle in the middle of the pack with the Puckheads and the Wolfpack.

As we head deeper into the summer, the ice is only getting faster and the stakes are getting higher. If the Hitmen keep playing like this, they’re going to be a tough out for anybody in this league. 

**Until next time, keep your stick on the ice and your eyes on the rafters!**
