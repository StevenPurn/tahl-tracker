# BOMBS AWAY: Blue Bombers Stun The Mafia in High-Octane Thriller

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: June 23, 2026**

If you didn’t believe in miracles, you weren’t at the arena tonight. 

Entering Tuesday night’s tilt at 8:45 PM, the Blue Bombers were sitting in the basement looking up at a mountainous Mafia squad that had yet to drop a single point. But hockey isn't played on a spreadsheet, folks—it’s played on the ice, and tonight, the Bombers proved that even the biggest underdogs can bite back. In a game that saw the scoreboard light up like a pinball machine, the Blue Bombers dismantled The Mafia 7-4 in a performance that will be talked about at the water cooler all week long.

### Period 1: The Early Ambush
The Bombers came out of the gate with their hair on fire. It took less than six minutes for **Tyler Waller** to silence the skeptics, burying an unassisted beauty to put the Bombers on the board. The Mafia, unaccustomed to playing from behind, started chasing the game, and that desperation led to a hook by Brian Maher at 4:14. The Bombers wasted zero time making them pay; **Hayden Sixsmith** shoveled home a feed from Ken Bebak on the ensuing power play, putting the Bombers up 2-0 heading into the intermission.

### Period 2: The Waller Workshop
If the first period was a warning, the second was a full-blown assault. The Bombers turned the defensive zone into a launchpad, catching The Mafia on their heels time and time again. 

**Tyler Waller** notched his second of the night at 10:45, thanks to some slick work by Samuel Northup. Minutes later, **Aaron West** joined the party, finishing a crisp setup from Frank Post. By the time **Connor Slattery** buried an unassisted goal and **Tyler Waller** completed his hat trick—again set up by the tireless Northup—the Bombers had blown the doors off, taking a commanding 6-0 lead into the final frame. The Mafia’s bench looked shell-shocked; the Blue Bombers were flying, and the Mafia’s goaltending was under siege.

### Period 3: The Mafia Rallies Late
You can never count out a team with a nickname like The Mafia. Down by six, they finally found their rhythm. **Daron Ross** continued his blistering goal-scoring streak at 12:16, finally getting one past the Bombers' netminder. **Scott Hellard** followed suit in the dying moments of the period, extending his own point streak and putting a small dent in the deficit.

The Bombers grabbed one final insurance tally from **Waller** (his fourth of the night—a performance for the history books!), but The Mafia refused to go quietly. **Ross** grabbed his second of the night, and **Cian Slattery** found the twine shortly after to bring the final score to 7-4. A late roughing penalty to Frank Post added a little spice to the final buzzer, but the damage was already done.

### The Aftermath
What a statement win for the Blue Bombers. They looked like a team possessed, playing with a level of grit and coordination we haven't seen all season. For The Mafia, this is a wake-up call; their 3-0-0 record meant nothing once the puck dropped, and they’ll need to tighten up the defensive zone if they want to stay near the top of the standings.

**Final Score: Blue Bombers 7, The Mafia 4**

The standings are officially looking a whole lot more interesting, folks. The Mafia remains in the thick of it, but the Blue Bombers have officially served notice: don't sleep on them. Keep your sticks on the ice—we’ve got a season to finish!
