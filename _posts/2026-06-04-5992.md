# Wrecking Ball: Mayhem Wreck+ Steamrolls Hot Garbage in Late-Night Clash

**By: Your Rink-Side Correspondent**
**Date: June 3, 2026**

The ice was still steaming at 9:30 PM, but by the time the final buzzer echoed through the rafters, the only thing truly "hot" about the evening was the fire under Mayhem Wreck+. In a game that saw plenty of grit and a fair share of extracurriculars, Mayhem Wreck+ shook off their early-season rust and dismantled Hot Garbage by a final count of 4-1.

### First Period: A Blitz and a Battle
The game didn’t need a warm-up period. Mayhem Wreck+ came out of the gate like a team possessed, looking to put their first win of the season in the books. 

At the 11:01 mark, Justin DeMoss silenced any doubters with a clinical finish to open the scoring. Before the Hot Garbage bench could even reset their lines, Larry Cockrell doubled the lead just six seconds later, capitalizing on a crisp feed from Nick Hatfield. 

However, hockey is a game of momentum swings. Hatfield found himself in hot water at 13:58, heading to the sin bin for roughing. Hot Garbage pounced on the opportunity. Aaron Whisner showed great vision, setting up Joshua Hall—who extended his goal streak to two games—to cut the lead to 2-1. It was the lone bright spot for Garbage, as Hatfield found himself back in the box just before the period ended, leaving the game teetering on a knife’s edge.

### Second Period: The Tactical Standoff
The middle frame turned into a defensive tug-of-war. Both goaltenders tightened the screws, treating the crease like a fortress. While the sticks were buzzing, neither side could find the back of the net. The physicality ramped up, with players battling for every inch of ice in the corners. It was classic barn-burner hockey—lots of grit, heavy hits, and plenty of traffic in front of the net, but the score remained deadlocked at 2-1 heading into the final frame.

### Third Period: Sealing the Deal
Whatever Mayhem Wreck+ coach discussed in the locker room, it worked. They came out with the pedal to the metal. 

Redemption was the theme of the night for Nick Hatfield. Fresh off a stint in the penalty box earlier in the game, he played the hero at 11:01, burying a goal off a slick assist from Justin McKinney to extend the lead to 3-1. With Hot Garbage forced to pull their goalie and chase the game, Jeff Rhodes delivered the final blow at 1:29, assisted by Jeremy Wentworth. 

### The Aftermath
With this 4-1 victory, Mayhem Wreck+ (1-1-0) finally breaks into the win column, jumping out of the basement and proving they have the offensive firepower to climb the standings. For Hot Garbage, this result puts a damper on their recent form, but in this league, the standings shift faster than a beer leaguer toward the locker room after a loss.

Mayhem Wreck+ looks to carry this momentum into their next tilt, while Hot Garbage will be looking to get back to the basics and clean up their defensive zone coverage. Until next time, keep your sticks on the ice and your skates sharp!
