# Mighty Drunks Liquidate Ice Breakers in Battle for First

**By: "Slap-Shot" Sammy, Hometown Beat Reporter**

**August 6, 2026** — The ice at the local barn was absolutely humming last night as the league’s top two titans collided. We all knew this was going to be a heavyweight bout, but the Mighty Drunks came into the rink with a thirst for two points that the Ice Breakers simply couldn't quench. In a 4-2 final that kept the crowd on the edge of their bleachers, the Drunks proved exactly why they are currently sitting on the throne of the standings.

### First Period: The Waller Wrecking Crew
The Drunks didn’t just come out firing; they came out with a flamethrower. Tyler Waller set the tone early, netting an unassisted beauty at the 10:33 mark that rattled the Ice Breakers’ composure. 

Disaster struck for the Breakers late in the period when Joshua Pula headed to the sin bin for a roughing minor at 1:19. The Drunks, smelling blood in the water, went to work on the power play. Just 19 seconds later, Jared Hallahan threaded a needle to Remigiusz Ferenc, who buried it to make it 2-0. Before the Breakers could even catch their breath, Waller was back at it, putting a dagger in the period with his second of the night at 3:27. The Breakers headed to the locker room down by three, looking like they were trying to skate through a snowdrift in concrete boots.

### Second Period: The Breakers’ Brief Resurgence
You have to give credit where it’s due: the Ice Breakers didn’t pack their bags and head home early. They came out in the second period with a chip on their shoulder the size of a puck bag. 

Joshua Pula, looking to atone for his earlier stint in the box, took matters into his own hands at 10:41, dancing through the zone for an unassisted tally that sparked a pulse in the building. Momentum shifted fully in the Breakers' favor at 1:36 when Daniel Reyes teed up Luke Morley, who snapped a wrister past the Drunks’ netminder to trim the lead to 3-2. The rink was buzzing—you could feel the electricity in the air, the kind that makes your hair stand up. 

However, championship teams have a way of answering back, and the Drunks are nothing if not resilient. At 8:58, Sean Laube set up Tyler Waller, who completed his **hat trick** with a clinical finish. It was a classic "top shelf where mom keeps the cookies" effort that silenced the Breakers’ comeback bid for good.

### The Aftermath
It was a barn burner, folks, but the Mighty Drunks remain the undisputed kings of the hill. With this win, the Drunks extend their remarkable dominance to 10-0-1, officially putting a stranglehold on first place with 21 points. They are playing a brand of hockey that is as disciplined as it is dangerous.

As for the Ice Breakers, they slide to 6-2-1. While they drop a crucial decision, there is no shame in losing to the league’s hottest squad. They’ll need to shake off the rust and regroup quickly if they want to keep the Average Joes and Blue Bombers from breathing down their necks for that second-place spot.

The takeaway from tonight? The Drunks are currently the team to beat, and if you're planning on taking them down, you better bring your best tape-to-tape passing and keep your head on a swivel—because they are playing like a team on a mission.
