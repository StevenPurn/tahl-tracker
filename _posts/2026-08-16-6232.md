# Drunks Keep Rolling: Mighty Drunks Topple Average Joes in Top-Tier Clash

If you wanted to see the best of the best this Saturday morning, the rink at 11:45 AM was the place to be. In a heavyweight tilt between the two titans of the league, the Mighty Drunks flexed their collective muscles and proved why they own the top spot, skating away with a 4-2 victory over the Average Joes.

The Drunks entered the contest with an unblemished 10-0-1 record, and they played like a team that’s forgotten how to lose. Connor Slattery drew first blood late in the opening frame, burying a feed from Omar and Cian Slattery to put the pressure on the Joes early.

The Average Joes, who came in riding a wave of confidence with a stellar recent run, refused to roll over. After killing off a penalty, Frank Smith took matters into his own hands early in the second period, finding the back of the net unassisted to deadlock the game at one apiece. It was a gritty, lunch-pail effort that sparked some much-needed life into the Joes' bench.

"Look, you can't give a guy like Frank an inch," said one rink-side observer, clutching a lukewarm coffee. "He’s got a nose for the net that would make a bloodhound jealous."

But the Mighty Drunks aren't undefeated by accident. Jonathan Liechti silenced the rising Joes momentum just a few minutes later, cleaning up a beautiful setup from Glenn Prouse to reclaim the lead. 

As the third period dawned, Tyler Waller decided he wanted to put this game on ice. Waller extended his impressive goal streak to five games with an unassisted beauty at the 1:26 mark, giving the Drunks a comfortable cushion. The Joes tried to make a late-game surge on the power play—courtesy of a Matthew Holland holding call—and Sergio Castellanos did his part, lighting the lamp with a nice assist from Aidan Caldron to cut the lead to 3-2.

However, the dream of a comeback was short-lived. With the Joes scrambling to find the equalizer, Waller struck again just twenty seconds later, notching his second goal of the period and his fourth point of the day to officially put the game out of reach at 4-2.

"We knew it was going to be a barn-burner," said a very tired-looking Drunks supporter in the stands. "The Joes are tough, but our boys just seem to have that extra gear when the clock starts ticking down."

With this result, the Mighty Drunks move to 11-0-1, remaining the undisputed kings of the standings. The Average Joes drop to 7-3-1, but given their recent form and tenacity, they’re still firmly in the hunt as the season rolls on. For the Drunks, it’s just another Saturday morning at the office—where the office is a frozen sheet of ice and the results are almost always in their favor.
