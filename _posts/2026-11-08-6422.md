# Berzerkers Stave Off Hot Garbage in Wild Saturday Morning Barn-Burner

If you were looking for a calm, low-scoring defensive clinic this Saturday morning, you definitely walked into the wrong rink. The Berzerkers and Hot Garbage traded punches in a 7-6 thriller that had more momentum swings than a playground teeter-totter, ultimately ending with the Berzerkers skating away with the win after an absolute clinic in individual persistence.

For the first two periods, it looked like Hot Garbage had the Berzerkers’ number. Joe Baker kicked off the festivities early with an unassisted marker, and by the time the dust settled on the second period, Tyler Waller had netted a pair, and both John Bewley and Nicole Baker had found the back of the net to give Hot Garbage a formidable cushion. 

But if there is one thing you can’t do in this league, it’s count out the Berzerkers when Zac Hughes decides he’s done playing nice. Hughes was an absolute menace on the ice, embarking on a one-man mission to pull his team back from the brink. He kicked off the Berzerkers’ resurgence late in the second, and by the time he buried his third of the game early in the third, the building was buzzing. 

"I don't know what we put in the water during the second intermission, but the boys just started flying," said one unnamed Berzerker teammate after the game. "When Hughes is dialled in like that, you just try to get out of his way and enjoy the show."

Trailing 6-2 at one point in the second, the comeback seemed like a tall order, but the Berzerkers turned the third period into a total blitzkrieg. Sage Blubaugh proved to be the perfect wingman to the comeback effort, potting two consecutive goals to pull the score within reach. With the pressure mounting and the Hot Garbage defense scrambling, Hughes hammered home his fourth goal of the game at the 6:11 mark to equalize, completing a massive four-goal performance that will be talked about at the post-game coolers for weeks.

Despite a parade to the penalty box—Hayden Wheeler’s hat trick of trips and hooks kept the Berzerkers’ coaches busy chewing their fingernails—the squad managed to keep the game deadlocked through the final horn. 

With regulation offering no winner, it came down to the shootout. In a fitting conclusion to a morning of individual heroics, Sage Blubaugh stepped up and coolly deposited the deciding goal, sealing a 7-6 victory for the Berzerkers. 

It was a tough pill to swallow for Hot Garbage, who played a brilliant offensive game but couldn't quite find the defensive padlock needed to keep the rampaging Berzerkers contained in the final frame. For the Berzerkers, the win is a massive momentum builder as they look to climb out of the middle of the pack in the league standings. If they can channel even half of that third-period energy into their next outing, they’re going to be a scary matchup for anyone in the league.
