# Snowed Under: Yetis Overwhelm Puckedelics in 11-6 Goal Frenzy

If you brought your sunscreen to the rink tonight, you were in the wrong place—but if you brought an abacus, you were golden. The Yetis turned the ice into an absolute launchpad on Wednesday, hanging an eye-popping 11 goals on the Puckedelics in a high-octane 11-6 victory that left the scoreboard operator in desperate need of a nap.

Coming into the night, the Puckedelics were riding a wave of momentum as the number two seed, while the Yetis were looking to find their footing. Whatever the Yetis’ pre-game meal was, it clearly worked, as they snapped the Puckedelics’ impressive form with a display of offensive firepower that can only be described as a blizzard of pucks.

The tone was set early when Jason Bahr struck at the 14:19 mark of the first period, and by the time Skylar Miller buried one with just 33 seconds left in the frame, the Yetis had staked their claim. While James DePriest managed to cut the lead for the Puckedelics just before the buzzer, it was clear that the Yetis were not content with just keeping it close.

The middle frame was pure, unadulterated chaos. The two teams traded punches like a heavyweight title fight, with Richard Retkowski, Brendan Burkman, and Jered Pieschke all finding the back of the net for the Yetis. The Puckedelics tried to claw back through the combined efforts of Brett Mooney, John White, and Joshua Smith, but every time they looked for a foothold, the Yetis simply skated away from them.

But the real fireworks were saved for the third period. Alexander Hajduczok went on an absolute tear, recording a natural-style hat trick that effectively turned the lights out on the Puckedelics. Not to be outdone, Skylar Miller completed his own mission, adding two more goals in the final frame to bring his personal tally to a staggering three on the night. 

"I think we just stopped checking the scoreboard and started checking the back of the net," joked one Yeti in the locker room after the game. Puckedelics coach, who looked like he’d seen a ghost, could only tip his cap to the barrage: "When the puck is jumping like that, sometimes you just have to watch the show."

While the Puckedelics’ Triston Wilson continued his incredible four-game goal streak with another tally in the third, it was a night where the Yetis simply couldn't miss. For the Yetis, this win is a massive shot in the arm as they look to climb up from the middle of the pack. As for the Puckedelics, they’ll look to shake off the snow and get back to the form that made them one of the top teams in the league. 

One thing is certain: if you missed this one, you missed the most high-scoring barn burner of the season. Someone get those goalies an extra-large Gatorade—they earned it.
