# MURDER ON ICE: Tulsa Storms Back to Dismantle Knights in 4-2 Thriller

**TULSA** – If you were looking for a Saturday night barn burner, the BOK Center was the place to be. On a humid July 27th evening, the Tulsa Murder (#3) reminded us all why they are sitting pretty near the top of the standings, overcoming an early deficit to put the Knights (#10) on ice with a 4-2 victory.

It wasn’t the prettiest start for the hometown heroes, but by the final buzzer, the Murder had proven that momentum is a fickle friend—and tonight, it was wearing a Tulsa jersey.

### Period 1: The Rough Patch
The game started with some bad blood. Tulsa’s Nicholas Olson found himself cooling his heels in the sin bin at the 2:28 mark for a roughing minor. While the Murder PK unit looked stout, the Knights managed to silence the home crowd at 7:13 with an unassisted goal. It was a bit of a defensive lapse, the kind that makes a coach lose his hair, and the Knights headed into the first intermission clinging to a 1-0 lead, smelling an upset.

### Period 2: The Tulsa Tsunami
Whatever was said in the locker room during the break, I want a copy of that tape. The Murder came out of the tunnel looking like a team possessed. 

The dam broke at 3:52 when **Luke Brandon** buried one, knotting the score at 1-1. Just 47 seconds later, **Tom Mortimer**—extending his goal streak to an impressive four games—sniped a beauty off a feed from Mark Hodges to take the lead. 

But the Murder weren't done pouring it on. At 7:08, Brandon notched his second of the night, taking a feed from Justin Oxford and burying it top-shelf where mom keeps the cookies. Then, just 28 seconds later, the crowd erupted as **Pete Fleming** kept his historic six-game goal streak alive, hammering home a goal off a Brandon helper. In a span of just under four minutes, Tulsa had transformed a 1-0 deficit into a 4-1 fortress.

### Period 3: Closing the Vault
The third period became a game of "keep away." The Knights managed to find a little life at 4:26 when Anthony Miller beat the tender off a pass from Fuad Shimi to make it 4-2, but the Tulsa defense clamped down like a bear trap for the remainder of the frame. 

The Knights tried to create some traffic in front of the net, desperate for a greasy rebound, but the Murder goaltending held firm, refusing to let the visitors bridge the gap.

### The Takeaway
With this win, Tulsa Murder (6-3-0) holds steady at 12 points, breathing down the necks of the league leaders and proving that their recent "win-loss-win" inconsistency is a thing of the past. For the Knights, it’s a tough pill to swallow; at 2-6-0, they remain near the basement and will need to find some consistency if they want to make a run at the playoffs.

As for the Murder? They’re clicking. With Luke Brandon putting up a three-point performance and Fleming’s streak reaching legendary status, the rest of the league might want to keep their heads up. The Murder are on the prowl.
