# Antlered Ambush: Jackalopes Upset Mooseknuckles in 4-2 Thriller

If you were looking for a quiet Sunday afternoon, you clearly wandered into the wrong rink. The Jackalopes charged into the fray on October 4th and managed to turn the Mooseknuckles’ impressive unbeaten streak into a historical footnote, skating away with a decisive 4-2 victory.

The Jackalopes came out with the intensity of a caffeinated squirrel, setting the tone early. Bradley Fidler struck first at the 11:15 mark of the first period, courtesy of a slick setup from Robert Verity. Not content with just one, Todd Thompson hammered home an unassisted beauty at the 2:18 mark, leaving the Mooseknuckles looking like they were trying to solve a Rubik’s cube in a blender.

"We told the boys to play like Jackalopes with somewhere to be," said a source close to the Jackalopes bench. "And apparently, that destination was the back of the net."

The Mooseknuckles found themselves spending more time in the penalty box than a repeat offender in a small-town courtroom, with Michael Pacheco and Donny Griffin both taking trips to the sin bin in the first period alone. Despite the defensive chaos, the Mooseknuckles refused to roll over.

The second period was a grueling trench war. James Whitacre, looking as sharp as a fresh pair of blades, extended the Jackalopes' lead to 3-0 with an unassisted tally at 13:36. Just when it seemed like the Mooseknuckles were staring down the barrel of a shutout, Benjamin Savory decided he’d had enough of the silence. With a gorgeous feed from Pete Fleming—who is currently on a goal-scoring heater that would make a thermometer blush—Savory buried one with less than a minute remaining in the frame to give the Mooseknuckles a pulse.

The third period brought a flurry of activity, as the Mooseknuckles attempted to chip away at the deficit. However, Robert Verity effectively slammed the door shut at the 10:10 mark, burying a goal off a beautiful tic-tac-toe play involving Matthew Rudick and Whitacre. 

Even while shorthanded due to a Jasen Lawwill tripping penalty, the Mooseknuckles showed the heart of a team that refuses to go down without a fight. Kyle Okurowski snagged a shorthanded goal at 5:57, a desperate, defiant strike that drew a massive roar from the crowd, but the hill proved too steep to climb. 

For the Mooseknuckles, this marks their first stumble of the regular season, putting a dent in their previous 2-0-1 form. Meanwhile, the Jackalopes have proven that they are far more than just a name on the roster, clawing their way to 2-1-1 and showing the rest of the league that they aren’t afraid to kick down the door of the top-tier contenders. 

As the dust settles, the only thing certain is that this local league just got a whole lot more interesting. The Jackalopes leave the ice with their ears held high, while the Mooseknuckles head to the drawing board, likely wondering how a game that felt like a localized blizzard ended in such a clean sweep for the opposition.
