# Drunks Keep Rolling: Mighty Offense Overwhelms Tuskens in 8-4 Thriller

**TULSA** – If you were looking for a quiet Tuesday night, you clearly took a wrong turn and ended up at the rink. In a game that saw the twine rippling like a flag in a hurricane, the Mighty Drunks proved exactly why they’re sitting pretty at the top of the standings, dispatching the Tulsa Tuskens 8-4 in an absolute barn burner.

### First Period: A Frenzied Opening Salvo
The Drunks came out looking to extend their perfect record, and Tyler Waller wasted no time, burying a feed from Jared Hallahan at the 11:48 mark. But the Tuskens, desperate to shake their four-game skid, showed some serious grit. Dylan Walker ignited the Tulsa bench at 14:44, followed by a Gage Walker snipe and a last-second buzzer-beater from Cody Penick. 

Just when the Tuskens thought they had momentum, Tyler Waller decided he wasn’t done. He sniped an unassisted beauty at 5:05, and Daron Ross cleaned up the trash in front of the net off a Matthew Holland dish to pull the Drunks level. By the time the dust settled on the first, the scoreboard looked more like a grocery receipt than a hockey score.

### Second Period: The Waller Show
The middle frame turned into a tactical chess match—if that chess match involved high-speed collisions and heavy breathing. Dylan Walker grabbed his second of the night for Tulsa at 10:59, briefly putting the Tuskens back in the driver’s seat. 

But the Drunks are a veteran squad that doesn't panic. Remigiusz Ferenc, riding a hot streak, knotted it up with a bullet assisted by Nathan Fahland. With just seconds remaining, Tyler Waller completed his hat-trick masterclass, snapping an unassisted goal home to put the Drunks up 5-4 heading into the final frame. 

### Third Period: Closing the Vault
The third period belonged entirely to the boys from the top of the table. Despite Daron Ross heading to the sin bin for a tripping infraction at 10:50, the Drunks’ penalty kill stood tall, suffocating any Tusken rhythm. 

Once back at full strength, Ross redeemed himself with an unassisted marker at 3:50 to provide some breathing room. Then, the Slattery show began. Connor Slattery proved he’s got ice water in his veins, potting one off a slick pass from Omar Awad, and adding a solo effort just 38 seconds later. The Tuskens had simply run out of gas, and the Drunks walked away with a resounding 8-4 victory.

### The Big Picture
For the Mighty Drunks, the win keeps their perfect 4-0-0 record untarnished. They are playing with the kind of cohesion that makes them the team to beat—Tyler Waller is clearly on a mission, and the depth scoring from the likes of Ross and Slattery makes them a nightmare for opposing blue lines.

For the Tulsa Tuskens, it’s back to the drawing board. Sitting at 0-3-0, they showed glimpses of promise—particularly with the Walker brothers’ production—but they’ll need to tighten up the defensive zone if they want to stop the bleeding.

The Drunks continue their march toward the playoffs, looking more like a runaway freight train with every passing game. As for the Tuskens? They’ll need to find a way to bottle that first-period energy for a full sixty minutes if they want to climb out of the basement.

**Final Score:** Mighty Drunks 8, Tulsa Tuskens 4
