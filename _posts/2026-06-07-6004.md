# Eagles Take Flight: A Seven-Goal Statement in Sunday Night Showdown

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
*June 7, 2026*

If you weren't at the barn this Sunday, you missed a masterclass in offensive pressure. The Lite Beer Eagles, hungry for their first win of the season, touched down on the ice with a singular mission: to ground the Kraken Cold Ones. Mission accomplished. In a 7-3 thrashing, the Eagles didn't just win; they practically wrote a textbook on how to crash the crease and bury the biscuit.

### The Hilton Hurricane (First Period)
From the opening puck drop, the Eagles looked like they had been mainlining espresso. Ethan Hilton, playing with the kind of vision that usually requires a telescope, opened the scoring at 11:00, unassisted. But he wasn’t done. Just over two minutes later, he caught the Kraken defense napping again, finding the twine for his second of the night.

The onslaught didn't stop there. Michael Hilton joined the party, taking a feed from brother Ethan to extend the lead, before Adam Strange—looking to keep his goal streak alive—sniped one past the Kraken tender courtesy of a Corey Phillips dish. By the time the buzzer sounded on the first, the Eagles had a four-goal cushion and the Kraken were looking for a life raft.

### The Second Period: A Hilton Hat Trick and More
The Kraken finally found a pulse right at the buzzer ending the first/start of the second with a Ryan Martinez tally, but the Eagles were far from finished. Ethan Hilton turned in a performance for the highlight reels, potting two more goals in a dizzying 21-second span—both assisted by Michael Hilton. When you’re hot, you’re hot, and Ethan was essentially playing at the temperature of the sun tonight.

Jonathan McCormick kept the momentum churning, extending his personal point streak and pushing the lead to 7-1. While the Kraken managed to claw back with a Justin McKinney goal, the game was firmly in the Eagles' talons. 

### Third Period: Steadying the Ship
Down 7-2, the Kraken Cold Ones showed some serious "never-say-die" grit. Chase Stubblefield found the back of the net midway through the frame, cutting the lead to 7-3. But the Eagles' defensive corps, led by a back-end that tightened the screws, refused to let the game slip into "squeaky-bum time." They clogged the lanes, forced turnovers, and effectively turned the remaining minutes into a glorified practice session.

### What It Means for the Standings
Entering tonight at the bottom of the heap, the **Lite Beer Eagles (1-1-0)** have officially announced their presence. This wasn't just a win; it was a momentum-shifting statement that moves them out of the basement and into the middle of the pack. 

The **Kraken Cold Ones (1-1-0)**, meanwhile, will want to put this one in the rearview mirror. They head back to the drawing board to tighten up their defensive rotations after being caught in the "Hilton Hurricane" all night long.

The league table is starting to take shape, and if Sunday’s performance is any indication, the Eagles are ready to fly high. Tune in next week, folks—because if this game proved anything, it’s that the ice is never as cold as it looks.
