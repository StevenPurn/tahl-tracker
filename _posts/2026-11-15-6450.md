# Danglers Blank Reapers in Sunday Morning Shellacking

If you were looking for a high-octane thriller on Sunday morning, you probably should have stayed in bed for another hour. The Dirty Danglers decided that the 11:45 AM puck drop against the Reapers was the perfect time to host a clinic on how to turn a competitive matchup into a lopsided affair, blanking the Reapers 5-0 in a performance that left the folks in the stands wondering if the ice had been tilted toward the Reapers' defensive zone all morning.

The first period was a tactical stalemate, a real "snoozer" where both teams seemed to be shaking off the lingering effects of Saturday night. But come the second period, the Danglers started finding their footing. Even a two-minute roughing minor to Aden Ingram couldn't keep the momentum in the Reapers' favor. In fact, Ingram seemed to take that trip to the sin bin personally. Just moments after serving his time, he made his presence felt in the only way that counts on the scoreboard, burying a feed from Jonathan McCormick at the 7:28 mark to break the ice.

"I just wanted to make sure I wasn't the only one doing all the skating today," Ingram joked after the game, still sweating through his jersey. 

If the second period was the wakeup call, the third was a full-blown alarm clock for the Reapers. The Danglers’ offense ignited like a propane heater in a drafty rink. Ethan Hilton continued his hot streak, finding the back of the net just shy of the 12-minute mark of the final frame, set up nicely by Brett Mooney. That seemed to open the floodgates. Dylan Dillard struck just 29 seconds later, capitalizing on a crisp pass from Hilton to put the game effectively out of reach at 3-0.

The highlight of the afternoon, however, belonged to Brett Mooney. He turned the final portion of the third period into his own personal highlight reel. Even with Dillard cooling his heels in the box for interference, the Danglers weren't deterred; Mooney found the net for his first goal at 4:11, and then, showing some truly greedy hands, he potted a second goal just over two minutes later, assisted by Josh Hopwood and Daniel Maxwell.

The Reapers, who came into this one sitting fourth in the standings, looked uncharacteristically flat, unable to solve the Danglers' defensive shell. With this 5-0 shutout, the Dirty Danglers climb to a 4-4-1 record, officially shaking off the "W-L-W-L-W" inconsistency that has plagued them this season. As for the Reapers, they drop to 4-4-1, sliding back into the pack and likely hoping to forget this morning ever happened as they head into the next week of play. 

It was a tough day at the office for the Reapers, but for the Danglers, it was exactly the kind of momentum-builder a team needs when the season standings start to look like a crowded elevator.
