# Wreck+ Rocks the Rink: Mayhem Blank The Outsiders in Tuesday Night Thriller

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**June 7, 2026**

If you weren’t at the arena tonight for the 8:45 puck drop, you missed a classic game of chess played at Mach speed. Mayhem Wreck+ squared off against The Outsiders in a clash that felt more like a playoff tilt than a mid-season showdown. When the final buzzer sounded, it was Mayhem Wreck+ standing tall with a statement 2-0 victory, snapping The Outsiders' perfect start and signaling that the middle of the pack is hungry for blood.

### First Period: The Foddis Flash
The game started with both squads skating like they had somewhere important to be, but the ice tilted early when The Outsiders’ Andrew Lamparter was whistled for a hold at the 2:54 mark. While the power play didn't yield a direct result, it clearly flipped the switch for the Mayhem.

Just moments after the kill, **Walter Foddis** decided he’d had enough of the stalemate. At 5:36, Foddis picked up the biscuit, navigated through the neutral zone, and buried one past the netminder to draw first blood. It was an unassisted beauty—pure hustle—that put the Mayhem up 1-0 heading into the intermission.

### Second Period: Defensive Masterclass and the Dagger
The second frame was a gritty, grind-it-out affair. Tempers flared at 12:25 when Mayhem’s Stephen Jeffery took a seat for roughing, but the penalty kill unit was absolute iron. They refused to let The Outsiders find any rhythm, collapsing in front of the crease to clear the lanes.

Fresh off the kill, the Mayhem momentum reached a fever pitch. At 13:00, **Spencer Hodgson** played the hero, cleaning up a gorgeous dish from **Larry Cockrell** to light the lamp. That 2-0 cushion felt like a mountain for The Outsiders, who were being suffocated by a Mayhem defense that seemed to have an extra man on the ice at all times.

### Third Period: The Lockdown
If the second period was about grit, the third was about pure desperation. The Outsiders pushed hard, forcing the Mayhem into some tight spots, including back-to-back penalties to Spencer Hodgson (hooking) and Jeff Rhodes (tripping). 

However, the Mayhem crease was a fortress tonight. Even with the man-disadvantage, the defensive corps shut down the passing lanes, making it impossible for The Outsiders to find any "traffic in front of the net." Every time The Outsiders tried to thread the needle, they found a shin guard or a glove waiting to send the puck back the other way. The shutout was total, absolute, and hard-earned.

### The Aftermath
Coming into tonight, The Outsiders were sitting pretty at 2-0-0 and looking like a juggernaut. With this result, they drop to 2-1-0, feeling the heat from a surging Mayhem Wreck+ squad that moves to 2-1-0, finally gaining the traction they needed to climb out of that #4 spot.

For the Mayhem, this is the kind of win that builds a culture. They didn't just win; they frustrated a top-tier opponent, played a disciplined third period, and kept a clean sheet. As for The Outsiders, they’ll have to head back to the drawing board to figure out how to crack the code when teams decide to park the bus.

It’s a long season, folks, but tonight? Tonight, the Mayhem definitely lived up to their name—in the best way possible. See you at the rink!
