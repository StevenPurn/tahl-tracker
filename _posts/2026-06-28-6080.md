# WRECKING BALL: Mayhem Wreck+ Steamrolls Reapers in Sunday Morning Barn Burner

**DATE:** June 28, 2026  
**LOCATION:** The Rink  
**FINAL SCORE:** Mayhem Wreck+ 6, Reapers 3

If you thought an 8:45 AM puck drop meant a sleepy start, you clearly weren’t at the arena this morning. The Mayhem Wreck+ proved they aren’t just morning people—they’re morning predators—charging out of the gate to dismantle the Reapers 6-3 in a high-octane tilt that had the boards rattling from the opening faceoff.

### Period 1: The Early Wake-Up Call
The game began with a dash of drama that would set the tone for the entire morning. Just 12:24 into the first frame, the Reapers’ Chance DeMier found himself cooling his heels in the box for slashing. The Wreck+ wasted zero time capitalizing on the power play, as Jeremy Wentworth hammered home an unassisted beauty to put the Mayhem on the board first.

The Reapers weren't about to roll over, though. Patrick Flannery showed some grit, weaving through the neutral zone to knot the game at 1-1 at the 8:18 mark, silencing the early morning crowd and setting up a nail-biter of a second period.

### Period 2: The Wrecking Crew Takes Over
If the first period was a warm-up, the second was a full-blown demolition project. Despite a couple of trips to the sin bin for the Mayhem—courtesy of Steven Chern and the perpetually involved Ivy Emerson—the Wreck+ offense hit an absolute fever pitch.

Between the 6:48 and 8:46 marks, the Mayhem turned the ice into their personal playground. Jeremy Wentworth grabbed his second of the game off a slick feed from John Saltsman and Stephen Jeffery. That seemed to open the floodgates. Thirty seconds later, Jeffery cashed in his own, and shortly after, Andrew Burns rifled a heater past the Reapers’ netminder, assisted by the ever-gritty Emerson. 

While the Reapers’ Geoffery Rash managed to pull one back at the 9:08 mark to keep the pulse of the game alive, the momentum had firmly shifted into the Wreck+ gear.

### Period 3: McKinney Closes the Vault
The Reapers pushed hard in the third, but they ran into a brick wall named "The Mayhem Offense." Justin McKinney decided it was time to put this one on ice, netting two beauties—one at 10:13 and another at 3:15—the latter again set up by a selfless play from Emerson. 

Things got a little chippy late, with a double-minor scuffle featuring Emerson and Sherry Sanders at the 57-second mark, and a late trip by Emerson, allowing Flannery to net his second of the morning for the Reapers. But it was too little, too late. By the time the final horn sounded, the Wreck+ had firmly established their dominance.

### The Bottom Line
With this 6-3 victory, Mayhem Wreck+ (4-1-0) vaults to 8 points, firmly tightening their grip on the top tier of the standings and keeping the heat on league-leading "Gold as Ice." It’s a statement win for a team that is clearly clicking on all cylinders.

For the Reapers (1-4-0), it’s a tough pill to swallow. Despite Flannery’s two-goal performance and some flashes of brilliance from Rash, the team remains buried in the bottom half of the standings. They’ll need to shore up the defensive zone if they want to stop the bleeding and climb out of that number ten spot.

The Mayhem Wreck+ are proving that when they bring the heat, they’re top shelf where mom keeps the cookies. We'll see if they can keep this momentum rolling into next week!
