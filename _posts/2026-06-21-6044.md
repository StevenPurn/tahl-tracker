# Riveters Derail the Undefeated Express: Tulsa Hands Valkyries First Loss in 5-3 Thriller

**By: "Rusty" Blades, Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: June 21, 2026**

The ice was still shimmering from the morning resurface when the two titans of the league squared off this morning, but by the final horn, it was the Tulsa Riveters who walked away with the bragging rights. In a matchup that pitted the undefeated Valkyries against the surging Riveters, we were treated to a 10:15 AM barn burner that proved once and for all: there’s no such thing as "morning rust" in this league.

The Riveters walked into the building and handed The Valkyries their first ‘L’ of the season, ending a perfect 4-0 run with a gritty, calculated 5-3 victory.

### Period 1: Finding the Range
The Valkyries looked to set the tone early, but a tripping call on Kalea Maxwell at the 14:46 mark forced them into a defensive shell. The Riveters spent the remainder of the period setting up shop in the offensive zone, eventually cracking the seal at 1:59. Amanda Long found the back of the net off a sweet dish from Gabrielle Gibson, sending the Riveters into the locker room with a 1-0 cushion.

### Period 2: The Goal-Scoring Carousel
If the first period was a tactical chess match, the second was an all-out demolition derby. Stacey Burgess doubled the Tulsa lead just two minutes in, burying an assist from Angela Willoughby and Long.

But you don’t get to be 4-0 by folding under pressure. The Valkyries roared back with two quick strikes. Makenzie Dillard ignited the crowd at 3:55 with a finish assisted by Haley Griffin, and just moments later, Griffin took matters into her own hands—literally—unleashing an unassisted rocket to knot the game at 2-2. 

The momentum pendulum swung back to Tulsa almost instantly, however. Just twelve seconds after the equalizer, Gabrielle Gibson restored the Riveters' lead with a goal assisted by McKenna K. Rhodes. A late tripping penalty on Burgess gave the Valkyries a glimmer of hope heading into the final frame, but the Riveters’ defensive structure held firm.

### Period 3: The Gibson Masterclass
The final frame was a high-octane scramble. Haley Griffin, proving she’s playing at a different speed than everyone else today, completed her impressive display by potting her second goal of the game at 11:26, drawing the Valkyries level once more at 3-3.

The drama hit a fever pitch when Amanda Long struck again at 13:16, assisted by the ever-dangerous Gibson, to reclaim the lead for Tulsa. The Valkyries scrambled, throwing everything but the kitchen sink at the net. Even with the Riveters hampered by back-to-back penalties to Remisha Blation and Gibson, the Valkyries couldn’t solve the puzzle. 

Adding insult to injury, Gabrielle Gibson put the nail in the coffin while shorthanded at 9:42—an absolute heartbreaker for the Valkyries’ faithful—assisted by Burgess to seal the 5-3 victory.

### The Aftermath
For the Valkyries, this is a wake-up call. They remain atop the standings, but the cushion has evaporated. They’ll need to regroup before their next shift, as the rest of the league now knows that the "invincible" armor has a chink in it.

For Tulsa? This is a massive statement win. At 3-0-1, the Riveters are breathing down the Valkyries' necks, and if they keep playing with this kind of grit and transition speed, the regular season title race is going to go right down to the wire.

Grab your coffees, folks—if today was any indication, the rest of this season is going to be a wild ride.
