# Berzerkers Unleash the Fury in 7-2 Shellacking of Wendigos

**By: "Slap-Shot" Sammy, Hometown Beat Reporter**

**June 15, 2026** – If you were looking for a quiet Monday night at the rink, you picked the wrong zip code. Last night’s tilt between the Berzerkers and the Wendigos was less of a hockey game and more of a total demolition project. When the final horn sounded at the barn, the Berzerkers had skated off with a dominant 7-2 victory, proving that when this squad clicks, they are a locomotive with no brakes.

### First Period: The Wake-Up Call
The Wendigos actually drew first blood at the 12:06 mark when Taylor Chinowth capitalized on some early-game jitters, silencing the crowd. But if you’ve followed the Berzerkers this season, you know they don't stay down for long. 

Just 29 seconds later, Zac Hughes—riding a hot hand with a goal in his previous game—answered the bell, burying a feed from Stephen Jeffery. That seemed to flick a switch. Jonathan Pixley added his name to the scoresheet with under two minutes to play, and Mik Halgren followed up shortly after. By the time the buzzer sounded on the first, the Berzerkers had flipped a 1-0 deficit into a 3-1 cushion. The Wendigos were left spinning their tires in the mud.

### Second Period: Expanding the Lead
The middle frame was all about the Berzerkers’ depth. EDWARD STERN, fresh off serving a trip to the sin bin for a roughing minor, returned to the ice with a vengeance, finding the back of the net at 11:20. The chemistry between EVERT WILMETH and Zac Hughes was palpable tonight; they were weaving through the Wendigo defense like they were pylons. Wilmeth capped the period with his own tally, extending his impressive point streak to three games and pushing the lead to 5-1.

The Berzerkers’ goaltending was nothing short of a brick wall, stifling any attempt the Wendigos made to get back into the rhythm. The puck movement in the offensive zone was crisp—tape-to-tape passing that made the Wendigos look like they were chasing shadows.

### Third Period: Closing the Vault
The third period was largely a victory lap. NIKOLAS KEMMLER kept his scoring streak alive with a goal at 11:52, courtesy of a beautiful setup from Wilmeth. While Joshua Copeland managed to find a consolation prize for the Wendigos midway through the period, Mik Halgren hammered the final nail into the coffin with his second goal of the night at 6:45. 

It was a masterclass in puck possession, ending with a final score of 7-2.

### The Big Picture
The implications for the standings are massive. The Berzerkers (2-1-0, 4 pts) are shaking off the rust and surging upward, showing that their opening week slump was just a hiccup. With four points on the board, they are firmly back in the hunt, looking like a team that belongs in the top half of the table.

For the Wendigos (0-3-0), tonight was a tough pill to swallow. They’re still hunting for that elusive first win of the season, and tonight proved they have some heavy lifting to do in the defensive zone. 

The Berzerkers are heading home tonight with a lot of swagger, and they should be. They played a complete game, won the battles along the boards, and buried their chances. If they keep playing like this, the rest of the league had better keep their heads on a swivel.
