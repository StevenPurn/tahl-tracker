# Fossil Fuels the Fire: Fossil Hockey Grinds Out a 4-2 Win Over The Syndicate

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date:** May 21, 2026

If you weren’t at the rink last night, you missed a prime-time heater. At 9:30 PM, the ice was still crisp, but the air in the building was thick enough to chew on as Fossil Hockey clashed with The Syndicate in a showdown that felt more like a street fight on skates than a mid-season tilt. Both squads entered the night deadlocked at two points in the standings, and by the final buzzer, it was clear that Fossil Hockey wanted this win just a little bit more.

### A Tale of Two Periods
After a scoreless first period that felt like a tactical chess match played at 90 miles per hour, the second period exploded into a full-blown barn burner. 

Fossil Hockey drew first blood at 11:38 when Kelly Rundle buried one past the Syndicate tender, courtesy of a beautiful setup from Michael Hilton and Timothy Willis. It was the spark the game needed. However, The Syndicate wasn’t about to roll over. Alexander Ogden answered back for the boys in Syndicate colors at 1:53, showing off some silky mitts to even the tally.

But then, the Fossil offense turned into a buzzsaw. Ethan Hilton went rogue at 3:41, unassisted, showcasing some individual brilliance to regain the lead. Moments later, Andrew Lamparter capitalized on a sweet dish from Dave Griffiths at 6:53 to extend the lead to 3-1. 

The middle frame wasn't without its headaches, though. Fossil’s Kelly Rundle found himself heading to the sin bin at 9:24 for a "Too Many Men" call—a classic case of having too many cooks in the kitchen. Fossil survived the kill, but the momentum was beginning to shift.

### Closing the Casket
Entering the final frame down 3-1, The Syndicate started playing like they were double-shifted. Brett Mooney found the back of the net at 3:06, unassisted, slicing the lead in half and turning the pressure up to a level that would make a pressure cooker explode. 

With the clock ticking down and the Syndicate goaltender pulled for the extra attacker, the ice was absolute bedlam. There was enough traffic in front of the net to cause a pileup on the freeway, but Fossil Hockey held the line. With just 42.8 seconds remaining, Ethan Hilton put the icing on the cake, finding the empty cage for his second goal of the night to seal a 4-2 victory.

### The Aftermath
It was a gritty, high-octane performance that saw Fossil Hockey (now 4 points) leapfrog The Syndicate (still sitting at 2 points) in the standings. 

For Fossil, it’s a massive bounce-back win following their recent skid. They proved that even when they’re shorthanded or under siege, they can keep their cool when the game is on the line. For The Syndicate, it’s a tough pill to swallow after coming in with the hot hand, but with plenty of hockey left to play, they’ll be looking to iron out those defensive lapses.
