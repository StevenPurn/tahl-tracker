# KNUCKLES KNOCK OUT YETIS IN A FRIGID FRACAS

**By: Rinkside Reporter**
*August 2, 2026*

If you weren’t at the barn this evening, you missed a classic. The Mooseknuckles and the Yetis took to the ice at 5:45 PM for a collision that lived up to the billing, featuring enough back-and-forth action to give a spectator whiplash. When the final horn sounded, the Mooseknuckles narrowly edged out a 5-4 victory in a game that felt like a heavyweight title fight disguised as a beer league heater.

### Period 1: Knuckles Set the Tone
The Mooseknuckles wasted no time putting the Yetis on their heels. Erik Chillingworth drew first blood at the 13:48 mark, set up beautifully by Garrett Williams, who was essentially playing puppet master all night. Despite a bit of chippy play—including a high-sticking minor for Donny Griffin and a "Too Many Men" blunder by the Yetis—the momentum stayed firmly in the Mooseknuckles' corner. Garrett Williams turned from playmaker to finisher at 9:39, burying a beauty to double the lead before the first intermission.

### Period 2: The Scoring Spree
If the first frame was a tactical skirmish, the second was an absolute explosion. The Yetis woke up early when Denis Blaquiere, riding his goal-scoring heater, found the back of the net just over a minute in. 

But the Mooseknuckles had an answer. James Rozell stepped up to play the role of the night's hero, lighting the lamp twice in the period to restore a comfortable cushion. Between Rozell’s clinical finishing and Pete Fleming’s persistence, the Knuckles pushed their lead to 5-2 heading into the final stages of the frame. Even a late penalty to Katherine Popoff couldn't slow the Knuckles, though Popoff redeemed herself by tallying a goal of her own at 5:18 to keep the Yetis’ hopes on life support.

### Period 3: The Yetis’ Near-Miracle
The third period was a testament to the Yetis' heart. Down by three, they began to chip away at the ice. Skylar Miller capitalized on a helper from Derek Campbell at 13:51, and when Denis Blaquiere notched his second of the night with just over four minutes left, the building hit a fever pitch. 

The officials had their hands full as tempers flared, leading to simultaneous slashing penalties for Jason Fennell and Pete Fleming. With the ice opening up in 4-on-4 play, the Yetis threw everything but the kitchen sink at the Mooseknuckles’ cage, but the defense held fast, surviving the onslaught to preserve the 5-4 win.

### The Aftermath
Garrett Williams was the undisputed architect of this victory, racking up a massive four-assist performance that kept the Yetis’ defense running in circles. 

**What does this mean for the standings?**
The Mooseknuckles (7-2-0) continue to cement their status as the team to beat, keeping the pressure on the Tulsa Murder for that top spot. They’ve proven they can hold a lead, even when the opponent turns up the heat. For the Yetis (4-3-2), it’s a tough pill to swallow, but they proved they have the offensive firepower to hang with the league’s elite. They’ll need to tighten up the defensive zone if they want to climb out of that fourth-place logjam.

The ice may have melted, but the heat of this rivalry is just getting started. See you at the next one!
