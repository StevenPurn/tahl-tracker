# Blanks Remain Unstoppable: Shooting Blanks Decimate Precast in 9-4 Rout

**DATE:** July 12, 2026  
**LOCATION:** The Rink  
**REPORTER:** “The Blue-Line Beat”

If you were looking for a defensive stalemate tonight, you certainly came to the wrong rink. The Shooting Blanks continued their absolute tear through the standings on Friday night, extending their perfect record to 7-0-0 with a dominant 9-4 victory over Oklahoma Precast. 

Entering the game, the Blanks were riding high on a five-game win streak, while Precast was struggling to find their footing. After sixty minutes of high-octane hockey, one thing is clear: the Blanks aren’t just a team; they’re a juggernaut.

### First Period: The Wake-Up Call
The game started with some chippy play, highlighted by a slashing minor assessed to Nicholas Power at the 11:09 mark. However, it was Precast who struck first, with Power redeeming himself by burying a beauty at the 3:15 mark off an Adam Carter feed. 

The Blanks, perhaps annoyed at being poked by the bear, shifted into second gear immediately. Aaron Best knotted it up at 8:52, followed just 18 seconds later by an Austin Tyler snipe that had the twine dancing. The Blanks headed to the locker room with a 2-1 lead, signaling that the floodgates were officially opening.

### Second Period: The "Best" Kind of Offensive Explosion
If the first period was a light breeze, the second was a full-blown hurricane. The Shooting Blanks put on a clinic, tallying five goals in the frame. Austin Tyler and Phillip Caputo were clinical, each finding the back of the net twice in the period. Aaron Best added his second of the night, while Elliott Langford proved to be the ultimate playmaker, setting the table for his teammates with surgical precision.

Nicholas Power managed to stop the bleeding for Oklahoma Precast midway through the period with his second of the night, but the Blanks’ offensive pressure was relentless—they were generating traffic in front of the net like it was rush hour on the interstate. By the time the buzzer sounded for the second intermission, the Blanks had extended their lead to 7-2, leaving the Precast squad searching for answers.

### Third Period: Closing the Curtain
The final frame saw Precast claw back with some grit, as Matthew Rankin and Arin Olson both tallied goals to give the Precast faithful a brief glimmer of hope. But the Blanks, showing the maturity of an undefeated team, slammed the door shut. 

Austin Tyler completed his own personal highlight reel with another strike at the 14:08 mark, and Phillip Caputo put the final nail in the coffin with just over a minute left, roofing one "top shelf where mom keeps the cookies." 

### The Bottom Line
With this 9-4 victory, the Shooting Blanks (7-0-0, 14 points) remain the undisputed kings of the mountain, extending their lead at the top of the standings. For Oklahoma Precast (2-6-0, 4 points), the loss keeps them locked in the middle of the pack, desperately needing to find more consistency in their own zone if they hope to climb back into contention.

The Blanks are playing with the kind of confidence that makes the rest of the league sweat. With the way Austin Tyler, the Best brothers, and Phillip Caputo are filling the net, you have to wonder: is there anyone in this division who can actually stop them?
