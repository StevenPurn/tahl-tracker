# Berzerkers Dismantle Gold as Ice in 7-2 Rout

If there’s one thing I’ve learned from years of staring at rink-side spreadsheets, it’s that a team’s position in the standings is often a fragile illusion. On Wednesday night, the sixth-place Berzerkers proved that rankings are just numbers on a page as they dismantled the second-place Gold as Ice squad, 7-2, in a performance that was as surgical as it was decisive.

The tone for the evening was set early by Mik Halgren, who appeared to have arrived at the rink with a singular, violent agenda against the opposing goaltender. Halgren opened the scoring at 5:53 of the first, then doubled his pleasure—and his team's lead—at 9:19, with Kevin Keller chipping in the primary assist. By the time the buzzer sounded for the first intermission, Gold as Ice looked less like a second-place contender and more like a team trying to solve a Rubik’s Cube while wearing mittens.

The Berzerkers refused to take their foot off the gas as the second period opened. Just as the clock hit 00:00 (marking the transition into the period), Cody Stephens buried a goal that left the Gold as Ice defense in a state of suspended animation. While Dylan Dillard managed to claw one back for Gold as Ice at the 3:15 mark, the momentum was already firmly under Berzerker control. Edward Stern and Stephen Jeffery added two more tallies before the frame concluded, turning a competitive matchup into a lopsided affair.

"We were just trying to maintain our defensive structure," said one Berzerker bench staffer, hiding a smile that suggested he knew full well the offense had been the story. "Though, I suppose when the puck goes in seven times, the structure looks significantly better in hindsight."

The final frame saw the Berzerkers show no mercy. Edward Stern completed his multi-point night with an unassisted marker at 12:59, before Zac Hughes added the exclamation point at 3:50 following an Aden Ingram goal for Gold as Ice. Even a late tripping penalty to Jeffery Rosencutter with less than eight minutes remaining couldn’t dampen the mood for the Berzerkers, who successfully stifled any hope of a late-game resurgence.

For Gold as Ice, the loss is a statistical anomaly in an otherwise strong campaign. Coming in with an 8-1-0 record, they were arguably the hottest team in the building, but hockey has a funny way of refusing to cooperate with past performance trends. 

The Berzerkers, meanwhile, have now signaled that their 5-3-1 record might be drastically under-representing their true talent level. If they can continue to generate offense from the depth of their roster the way they did tonight, the teams currently sitting above them in the standings might want to check their rearview mirrors. The spreadsheet doesn't lie, but tonight, the Berzerkers certainly made it look like a suggestion.
