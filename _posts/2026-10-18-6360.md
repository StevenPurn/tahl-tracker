# Reapers Edge Hot Garbage in Afternoon Thriller

If you showed up to the rink expecting a sleepy Saturday matinee, you clearly haven't been paying attention to the standings. The Reapers and Hot Garbage collided in a mid-table clash that felt less like a regular-season dust-up and more like a grudge match for league supremacy. By the time the final puck hit the back of the net in the shootout, the Reapers walked away with a 2-1 victory, leaving the local faithful breathless and Hot Garbage wondering what might have been.

For the first thirty minutes, the game was a defensive chess match played at breakneck speed. Goaltenders on both ends were seeing the puck like it was the size of a beach ball, turning away every look until the 5:25 mark of the second period. That’s when Brett Mecham finally cracked the code, burying a feed from LC Sinor to put Hot Garbage on the board. It was the kind of goal that usually sucks the air out of the building, and for a long stretch, it looked like that single tally might be all the visitors needed to walk away with the points.

The third period brought a sudden spike in blood pressure. With the clock winding down, things got chippy. Alan Dyke headed to the sin bin for tripping at 10:07, and before the fans could even finish complaining about the officiating, Chance DeMier joined him for slashing. Playing 4-on-4, the ice opened up, and the Reapers started throwing everything, including the kitchen sink, toward the Hot Garbage net. 

The pressure finally paid off with just 1:28 left in regulation. Patrick Flannery, who has been lurking in the shadows all afternoon, found a loose puck and fired it home unassisted to tie the game. It was a classic "don't quit until the buzzer" moment that sent the crowd into a frenzy and forced the game into the shootout.

In the skills competition, it was a battle of nerves. Alan Dyke gave Hot Garbage an early spark with a clinical shootout goal, but the Reapers weren't having any of it. Patrick Flannery showed he wasn’t done for the day, answering back with a beauty, and Paul Sanders stepped up to deliver the dagger, burying his opportunity to secure the 2-1 win for the Reapers.

With the victory, the Reapers (3-2-1) managed to put a little breathing room between themselves and the middle-of-the-pack logjam, showing the kind of resilience that’ll be vital as we move deeper into the season. Hot Garbage, now sitting at 2-2-1, will surely be kicking themselves for letting the lead slip away in the dying minutes, but if today’s intensity is any indication, both of these squads are going to be a thorn in the side of the league leaders for the foreseeable future. Grab your beverage of choice and toast to that—it’s been a hell of a Saturday.
