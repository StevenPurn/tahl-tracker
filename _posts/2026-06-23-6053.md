# Laser Show! Laser Electrifies the Ice in 5-2 Victory Over Gold as Ice Bronze

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: June 22, 2026**

If you were looking for a late-night cure for insomnia, the 10:30 PM puck drop at the rink definitely wasn't it. The Laser came out looking like they’d had an extra shot of espresso, skating with a hunger that proved their 1-2 record was just a mirage. In a battle between mid-table rivals, the Laser managed to tighten the screws and turn the heat up on Gold as Ice Bronze, skating away with a decisive 5-2 win that feels like a season-changing statement.

### First Period: A Frenetic Start
The ice was barely dry before the Laser started firing. At 10:26, **Colton Taylor** reminded everyone why he’s on a scoring heater, burying one off a sweet feed from **Robert Verity** to open the scoring. 

But Gold as Ice Bronze wasn't about to just watch the light show. **Christopher Chapin**, fresh off his own scoring exploits, answered back just 95 seconds later off a setup from **Makenzie Dillard**. It was a back-and-forth affair early on, but **Nathan Hogue** restored the Laser’s lead at the 12:58 mark. Seconds later, a trip by Eric Kolis put Gold as Ice Bronze in the penalty box, and the momentum officially swung toward the Laser. By the time **Nicholas Poverud** found the back of the net late in the frame, the Laser had effectively punched their ticket to the driver's seat.

### Second Period: The Laser Defense Hardens
The second frame saw the Laser turn the "traffic in front of the net" into a defensive fortress. They kept the passing lanes clogged and forced Gold as Ice Bronze to play perimeter hockey. **Jered Pieschke** added the dagger at the 4:03 mark, courtesy of a textbook assist from **Chad Pancoast**. At 4-1, the game started to feel like a runaway train, with the Laser’s forecheck looking sharper than a freshly sharpened pair of Bauer blades.

### Third Period: Closing the Curtains
Gold as Ice Bronze showed some grit in the third. **Christopher Chapin** snagged his second of the night at 11:41, cutting the lead to two and briefly making the Laser faithful sweat in their bleachers. But any hope of a comeback was extinguished with under two minutes remaining. **Nicholas Poverud** hammered his second goal of the night past the keeper, cementing his status as the hero of the evening and putting this one firmly in the "W" column for the Laser.

### The Takeaway
The Laser (now 2-2-0) looked like a team that has finally found its rhythm. By cooling off the streaking Gold as Ice Bronze, they’ve managed to leapfrog into the thick of the standings. 

For Gold as Ice Bronze, it’s a tough pill to swallow. After entering the night in 7th place, this loss leaves them stuck at 3 points in a logjammed middle of the table. They’ll need to stop the bleeding fast if they want to keep pace with the likes of Tulsa Murder and the Mooseknuckles at the top of the heap.

As for the Laser? They played a complete game, moved the puck with purpose, and proved that when their sticks are hot, they can light up anyone in the league. If they keep playing like this, the "real barn burner" might be their next game. 

**Final Score:**
**Laser: 5**
**Gold as Ice Bronze: 2**
