# Dirty Danglers Break the Ice with an 8-2 Rout of the Bison

If you’ve been tracking the standings, you’ve seen the Dirty Danglers struggling to find the back of the net all season. Well, they finally found the net—and they decided to keep feeding it until the Tulsa Bison were left wondering what hit them. In a lopsided 8-2 finish on Sunday evening, the Danglers reminded everyone that even a team stuck in the basement of the standings has a ceiling, and tonight, they blew right through it.

The game started with a bit of a tease for the Bison faithful. Brian Cuff opened the scoring just 68 seconds into the tilt, suggesting a competitive night was in store. While Kalea Maxwell answered back for the Danglers mid-way through the first, Lucas Jameson reclaimed the lead for Tulsa shortly after. At 2-1 heading into the first intermission, it looked like a classic, grind-it-out recreational hockey affair. 

Then, the second period happened. Whatever the Danglers were drinking during the break, I’d like a double. 

The Danglers’ offensive floodgates didn't just open; they were ripped off the hinges. Jonathan McCormick, Stephen Jeffery, and Cameron Richardson rattled off three unanswered goals in the middle frame. By the time the dust settled on the second period, the Bison looked like they were skating in knee-deep slush. The transition game that served the Bison well in their previous outing was non-existent, and their defensive coverage began to resemble a group of tourists trying to navigate a roundabout in a rainstorm.

If the second period was a warning, the third was a full-blown eviction notice. Daniel Maxwell continued his hot streak, potting two goals to pad the lead, while Dylan Dillard and Aden Ingram joined the party to turn a tight game into a blowout. The Bison’s frustrations eventually boiled over with a couple of trips to the sin bin, but frankly, the penalties were just a symptom of a team that had lost its rhythm and its will to contest the blue line.

"We finally stopped overthinking it," said one Dangler in the locker room, likely still catching his breath. "Sometimes you just stop looking for the highlight reel pass and start putting pucks on net. Turns out, the puck goes in more often when you actually shoot it."

For the Bison, this is a night to burn the tape and forget the bus ride home. They entered the night as the sixth-ranked team looking to build momentum, but they were soundly dismantled by a Dangler squad that refused to stop moving their feet. The Danglers, who have been wallowing in the bottom of the league, earned their first win of the season in emphatic fashion, proving that in this league, the gap between the contenders and the bottom-feeders is often just a matter of who decides to show up for the second period. 

The Bison drop to 1-1-1 on the season, while the Dirty Danglers look to turn this momentum into a much-needed climb out of the basement. If they keep shooting like they did tonight, they might just stop being the league’s "dirty" secret and start being a problem for everyone else.
