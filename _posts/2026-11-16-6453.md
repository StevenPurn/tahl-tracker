# Fossil Hockey Turns Back the Clock in 9-5 Rout of Eagles

In a game that featured more goal-scoring shifts than a caffeine-fueled Zamboni driver on an overtime double-shift, Fossil Hockey dismantled the Lite Beer Eagles 9-5 this past Saturday night. If you blinked, you missed a goal—and if you left the arena to grab a hot dog, you likely returned to find the scoreboard had completely rearranged itself.

The Eagles started the contest with the grace of a team ready to soar, grabbing an early 2-0 lead behind unassisted tallies from Adam Strange and Corey Phillips. For a moment, it looked as though the Eagles might fly away with the game. But Fossil Hockey, evidently deciding that defense was merely a suggestion rather than a strategy, began an offensive onslaught that would make a meteor shower look timid. 

By the time the first period buzzer sounded, Fossil had launched a five-goal barrage in just over eight minutes of play. Daron Ross ignited the fuse at the 42.3-second mark of the first, followed by a flurry from Logan Pardue, Cody Dinsmore, and Eron Richards. Enoch Cox capped the frame with a goal of his own, handing the Eagles a deficit so steep it could’ve been a hiking trail.

"We felt like we were playing a game of tag where everyone was 'It' at the same time," joked Eagles captain Tyler Mills after the dust settled. "Every time we looked up, the puck was in our net. It was like trying to stop a waterfall with a kitchen strainer."

While the Eagles attempted to claw back in the second period with a goal from Hunter Neafus—assisted by the gritty passing of Lucas Jameson and Mills—Enoch Cox proved to be the immovable object and the unstoppable force all in one, netting his second of the night to keep the Fossil lead firmly in the triple-digit-deficit range.

The third period turned into an absolute scramble, as Dave Griffiths shook off a tripping penalty to bury a goal, and Enoch Cox secured a hat trick for the ages with his third of the night, fed perfectly by Griffiths and Joshua Hall. Gage Walker and Hunter Neafus managed to pad the scoresheet for the Eagles, but Fossil’s Cody Dinsmore iced the victory with his second goal of the night late in the third, ensuring the Eagles' wings stayed clipped.

The win provides a much-needed morale boost for Fossil Hockey, who move to 4-3-1, climbing out of the middle-of-the-pack logjam. For the Eagles, who slide to 3-5-1, the night was a sobering lesson in the volatility of league play. They’ll head back to the drawing board to tighten up the defensive zone, while Fossil can bask in the glow of a nine-goal performance that proved, at least for one night, that the Fossa-saurus still has a very sharp bite.
