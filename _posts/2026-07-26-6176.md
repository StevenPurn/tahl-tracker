# Outsiders Edge Out Berzerkers in a Saturday Morning Thriller

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
*July 26, 2026*

If you caught the 8:45 AM puck drop this morning, consider yourself lucky. While most of the city was still hitting the snooze button, The Outsiders and the Berzerkers were busy turning the ice into a pressure cooker. When the dust finally settled, The Outsiders walked away with a gritty 4-3 victory, proving once again that in this league, it’s not about how you start, but how you handle the traffic in front of the net.

### Period 1: A Tactical Stalemate
The morning air was crisp, but the tension was already thick. Both squads spent the first period testing the waters, playing a high-stakes game of chess at high velocity. The Outsiders found themselves in a bit of a pickle at 9:33 when a "Too Many Men" call on Zach Cox put them on the defensive. But the Outsiders’ penalty kill stood tall, turning away every Berzerker attempt and heading into the first intermission knotted at zeros.

### Period 2: The Berzerkers Wake Up
The second frame was pure chaos—the kind of hockey that makes you spill your coffee. After a double-roughing and unsportsmanlike dust-up at 10:58, things got greasy. The Berzerkers finally broke the dam at 1:10 when Forrest Beadle, fed by LC III Sinor and Kevin Keller, buried one to open the scoring. 

The Outsiders were still adjusting their goggles when Zac Hughes struck gold just 30 seconds later, extending the lead to 2-0. It looked like the Berzerkers were going to run away with it, but The Outsiders have a knack for the dramatic. Just before the buzzer, Scott Hellard—assisted by the red-hot Tyler Waller and Logan Pardue—found the back of the net, giving the Outsiders a massive momentum swing heading into the break.

### Period 3: The Padilla Show
If the second period belonged to the Berzerkers, the third belonged to Juan Padilla. 

Padilla tied the game at 3:14, and then midway through the period, he put the team on his back, burying a beauty from Andrew Lamparter to take the lead. The Berzerkers weren't going home quietly, though; Zac Hughes netted his second of the game at 14:10, once again deadlocking the score.

With the game hanging in the balance, it was Michael Wixted who stepped up. With less than 30 seconds left on the clock, Wixted fired a laser that found the twine—a highlight-reel finish that sent the Outsiders' bench into a frenzy. Despite a late-game roughing penalty, The Outsiders held the fort to seal a 4-3 victory.

### The Bottom Line
This win is a massive statement for The Outsiders. By taking down a top-tier rival in the Berzerkers, they solidify their position at 7-2-0 with 14 points, breathing right down the necks of Gold as Ice and Mayhem Wreck+. 

For the Berzerkers, who fall to 5-3-1, it’s a tough pill to swallow. They played with intensity, but today they just couldn't solve the late-game puzzle. If this game taught us anything, it’s that the standings are tighter than a goalie’s leg pads, and nobody is safe until the final buzzer sounds.

**Game Notes:** 
*   **Juan Padilla** continues his goal-scoring tear, proving he’s the guy you want with the puck when the game is on the line. 
*   **Zac Hughes** was the standout for the Berzerkers, showcasing a pair of "snipes" that reminded everyone why he’s a threat to score from anywhere in the zone.
*   **Next Up:** Both teams will look to rest their legs and prep for their next tilts as the race for the top spot heats up!
