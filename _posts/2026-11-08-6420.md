# Mayhem Wreck+ Keeps the Steamroller Moving with 4-1 Victory Over Fossil Hockey

If there were any doubts about who currently holds the keys to the kingdom in our local league, the Mayhem Wreck+ arrived at the rink this morning at 8:15 AM with a very clear, very loud, and very effective message: they aren't planning on slowing down anytime soon. In a morning tilt that saw the league-leading Wreck+ lock horns with Fossil Hockey, Mayhem asserted their dominance once again, skating away with a commanding 4-1 victory to extend their unbeaten streak and solidify their place at the top of the heap.

The morning chill couldn't freeze the Wreck+ offense, which opened the scoring early when Larry Cockrell buried a beauty at the 5:00 mark of the first period, assisted by the ever-reliable duo of Jeff Orr and Walter Foddis. It was the kind of goal that feels like a rhythmic drumbeat, signaling to the opposition that they were in for a long, uphill climb.

Fossil Hockey, desperate to break the rhythm, dug in their cleats during the second period. After surviving a messy "too many men" bench penalty—which, let's face it, is the hockey equivalent of tripping over your own shoelaces in public—they struck back. Eron Richards found the back of the net with just 14.1 seconds remaining in the period, set up perfectly by Enoch Cox. That goal acted as a massive jolt of espresso for the Fossil bench, sending them into the intermission feeling like they had found a crack in the Wreck+ armor.

But if you’ve been watching the Mayhem Wreck+ this season, you know they don’t just play the game; they manage the atmosphere. Andrew Burns took the air right out of the Fossil sails early in the third, burying his first of two goals at the 04.9-second mark of the period. He wasn't finished there, either. With the clock winding down, Burns struck again at 12:07, effectively putting the game on ice before John Saltsman followed up seconds later to put the final nail in the coffin.

"We just tried to keep the engine humming," a clearly caffeinated John Saltsman joked after the final whistle. "When the Wreck+ starts rolling, it's like a Zamboni that’s forgotten how to hit the brake. We’re just trying to stay on the ride."

The third period was not without its extracurricular activities, as both teams traded trips to the sin bin. Fossil’s Alan Dyke and Mayhem’s duo of Steven Chern and John Saltsman all spent time reflecting on their life choices in the penalty box, but Fossil could never quite find the momentum needed to mount a serious comeback against a Mayhem defense that looked as impenetrable as a brick wall in a hurricane.

For Fossil Hockey, the loss serves as a tough reality check, moving them to a 3-3-1 record and proving that the gap between the mid-pack and the league leaders is still significant. Meanwhile, the Mayhem Wreck+ improve to a blistering 6-0-1, proving once again that they aren't just here to play—they're here to dictate the terms of the season. If you're looking for the team to beat in this league, you don't need a map; just look for the top of the standings. The Wreck+ are still firmly in the driver's seat, and they have no intention of pulling over.
