# Renegades Run Riot: Oklahoma Precast Stunned in 12-3 Barn Burner

**OKLAHOMA CITY** – If you blinked during this evening’s tilt at the rink, you missed a goal. And if you blinked twice, you probably missed two more. 

In a Sunday evening showdown that felt more like a track meet than a hockey game, the Renegades stormed into the arena and hung a crooked number on the scoreboard, dismantling Oklahoma Precast 12-3. Coming into the game, Precast sat comfortably in second place, but the Renegades—looking to shake off a rough start to their campaign—arrived with their sticks greased and their sights set on the top shelf.

### The First Period: A Frenetic Opening
The Renegades wasted absolutely no time setting the tone. Just over ten minutes into the opening frame, the floodgates flew wide open. Austin Tyler, riding the momentum of a one-game goal streak, struck first with an assist from Hunter Bemies. Nine seconds later, Caeleb Burch added another, leaving the Precast defense looking like they were skating in quicksand.

Marshall Nunneley provided a brief spark of hope for Precast at the 12:56 mark, but the Renegades simply refused to tap the brakes. By the time the buzzer sounded on the first, the Renegades had piled on goals from Alex Bateman and Tyler Roepke, turning a competitive matchup into a lopsided affair. James Stuckey managed to tickle the twine for Precast, but the period ended with a barrage of Renegades offense that had the Precast netminder searching for answers.

### The Middle Frame: Bateman Takes Over
If the first period was a squall, the second was a full-blown hurricane. The Renegades’ puck movement looked like a well-oiled machine, dissecting the Precast zone with surgical precision. 

Tyler Roepke added his second of the night early on, followed by a clinical solo effort from Alex Bateman. Bateman, who entered the night on a goal streak, didn’t just add to it—he put on a masterclass in puck protection. Every time the Renegades transitioned through the neutral zone, the Precast defenders looked overmatched, struggling to clear the heavy traffic forming in the crease.

### The Final Push: Finishing the Job
The third period served as a victory lap for the boys in Renegade colors. Austin Tyler completed his rampage with another tally, and Bateman kept the highlight reel rolling. Even with a late consolation goal from Precast’s Chris Cahill—a gritty, hard-nosed effort assisted by Stone Baulch—the writing had long been on the wall.

By the time Ethan Charny and Matthan Hillberg hammered home their final goals, the Renegades had successfully turned this game into a statement win. 

### What This Means for the Standings
For Oklahoma Precast, this is a night to burn the game tape. Falling from second place and coming off a momentum-killing loss, they’ll need to tighten up their defensive structure before their next skate. 

Meanwhile, consider the league officially put on notice. The Renegades (1-1-0) have proven they can score in bunches, and if they carry this offensive rhythm into their next outing, they’ll be climbing the standings faster than a breakaway winger on a breakaway.
