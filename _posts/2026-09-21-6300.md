# Hitmen Silver Strike Gold in Double-Digit Offensive Clinic Against Honey Badgers

If you were looking for a quiet, defensive chess match on a Saturday night, you certainly didn't find it at the rink. The Hitmen Silver reminded the rest of the league why they’re sitting at the top of the standings, turning in an absolute offensive masterclass to dismantle the Honey Badgers by a final score of 10-4.

From the opening puck drop, the Hitmen looked like they were auditioning for a spot on a highlight reel. Bobby Pickett got the party started just 55 seconds into the first period, and by the time the final buzzer rang, the Silver had orchestrated a symphony of scoring that left the Badgers searching for answers.

The Hitmen’s top-tier synergy was on full display, with the duo of NICKLAS CLARK and JOSHUA MOYER acting like they shared a single brain on the ice. Clark walked away with a massive night, racking up multiple goals and showing off the kind of vision that makes beer-league goalies lose sleep. Moyer was equally lethal, proving that his recent scoring streak isn't just a fluke—it’s a lifestyle.

"We told the boys to keep the pedals down and the puck moving," said a grinning Hitmen captain after the game. "When the passing lanes are opening up like that, you just try not to get in the way of the magic."

To their credit, the Honey Badgers didn’t go down without a fight. Despite being overwhelmed by the Hitmen’s relentless transition game, they managed to find the back of the net four times. JOSEPH PACHUTA and MICHAEL MOSCA showed some real grit, forcing the issue and capitalizing on the few defensive lapses the Hitmen allowed. Justin Houde provided a spark as well, finding the back of the net in the third period to keep his team’s spirits high, but the deficit proved too tall a mountain to climb.

The second period was the real turning point. Even with a trip to the penalty box, the Hitmen seemed to find an extra gear, reeling off three goals in under five minutes midway through the frame. Lukas Vodicka, who was spending some time in the sin bin for tripping, made up for it immediately upon his return, burying one of his own to cap off a flurry of activity that effectively put the game on ice.

The Hitmen’s goaltending remained steady throughout the chaos, making the routine saves look easy and preventing the Badgers from ever stringing together a momentum-shifting run.

With this win, the Hitmen Silver solidify their status as the team to beat, moving to a 2-0-1 record and staying firmly planted at the summit of the standings. For the Honey Badgers, it’s a tough result, but they’ll have to shake off the dust quickly if they want to climb out of the bottom half of the table.

One thing is for sure: if you're a Hitmen defender, tonight was a treat. If you're a Honey Badger, you're probably just happy to be heading home to grab a cold one and forget about the scoreboard. Either way, it was a wild night of local puck that lived up to the hype!
