# Honey Badgers Finally Break the Ice: A 9-5 Goal-Fest Over the Puckheads

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: June 28, 2026**

If you’re a fan of high-octane, defense-optional hockey, the 7:45 PM puck drop at the arena was exactly what the doctor ordered. Heading into tonight, the Honey Badgers were starving, sitting at 0-4-0 and looking for a spark. The Puckheads, meanwhile, were hoping to climb out of the middle-of-the-pack muck. 

What we got instead was a straight-up barn burner that saw the Badgers finally turn their season around with a resounding 9-5 victory.

### First Period: Finding the Rhythm
The Puckheads drew first blood when Michael Bergstrom sniped one home at 10:37. It looked like the Puckheads might dictate the pace, but the Honey Badgers weren’t having any of it. Adam Carter, continuing his scorching hot streak, answered back at 14:28 with a helper from Joey Pachuta. Marshall Nunneley joined the party just minutes later, burying one to give the Badgers a 2-1 lead heading into the break. You could feel the tide shifting—the Badgers weren't just playing; they were hunting.

### Second Period: The Period of Pure Chaos
If the first period was the appetizer, the second was an all-you-can-eat buffet of goals. It was absolute madness. 

The Puckheads knotted it up early thanks to Richard Retkowski, but Matthan Hillberg restored the lead for the Badgers shortly after. Then, Gregory Douglas pulled the Puckheads even once more. But then, the Badgers flipped the switch. 

Between the 44-second mark and the 6:22 mark of the period, the goals came fast and furious. Adam Carter notched his second and third of the night, completing a natural scoring rhythm while Marshall Nunneley and Matthan Hillberg turned the heat up. The Puckheads tried to stay in the fight with two quick strikes from Douglas and Cameron Sartin, but the Badgers’ transition game was simply too fast, leaving the Puckheads' goaltender feeling like he was trying to stop water with a screen door. By the time the dust settled on the second, the Badgers had seized a commanding lead.

### Third Period: Closing the Kennel
Leading 7-5, the Badgers didn't sit back on their heels. Timothy N. See and Hillberg (who secured a massive hat trick performance) tacked on two more insurance goals in the opening minutes of the third. 

The physicality picked up as the frustration set in for the Puckheads. We saw some fireworks in the closing minutes, with a flurry of penalties—including a matching roughing call between Adam Carter and Cameron Sartin at 7:26—that proved there was no love lost on the ice tonight. The refs were kept busy, but the Badgers stood tall, playing sound, gritty hockey to protect their lead until the final horn.

### The Aftermath
For the Honey Badgers, tonight was the breakthrough they’ve been clawing for since day one. They finally get their first two points on the board, and the energy in that locker room must be electric. They proved that even a winless team can turn into a buzzsaw when the passing lanes open up. 

For the Puckheads, this is a tough pill to swallow. They’ll need to tighten up the traffic in front of their net if they want to bounce back and stay relevant in the standings. 

**Standings Update:**
The Honey Badgers finally vacate the basement floor, breathing down the necks of the Blizzard, while the Puckheads are going to have to do some soul-searching to avoid sliding further down the ranks.
