# The Puck Stops Here: Mother Puckers Sting the Bees in 5-2 Barn Burner

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: June 28, 2026**

The ice was crisp, the temperature was perfect, and the smell of Zamboni exhaust was in the air—it was a beautiful night for some hockey. In a mid-table clash that felt more like a playoff preview, the Mother Puckers (1-2-1) looked to put some distance between themselves and the bottom of the pack, facing off against a gritty Bad Bees (1-3-0) squad hungry for a climb in the standings.

When the dust settled at the final buzzer, it was the Mother Puckers who skated away with the two points, downing the Bees 5-2 in a game that featured everything from silky smooth passing to a third-period penalty parade.

### First Period: A Tactical Tug-of-War
The Mother Puckers drew first blood at the 10:19 mark when **Mckenna N Allison** found the back of the net, courtesy of a crafty setup from **Taylor Soucy**. But the Bees weren’t about to roll over. Just minutes later, the "Unknown" skater for the Bees—who clearly prefers to let their stick do the talking—sniped one home at 14:28 to knot it up.

However, the Puckers regained the lead before the intermission. With 8:27 left on the clock, **Abigail Nisbet** capitalized on a gorgeous tic-tac-toe sequence orchestrated by **Malori Christian** and **MAKENZIE DILLARD**. It was a back-and-forth affair that kept the fans on their feet.

### Second Period: The Christian Connection
The middle frame was all about the Mother Puckers finding their rhythm. At 3:47, **Malori Christian** buried a beauty, with Abigail Nisbet turning playmaker to pick up the assist. The Bees’ goaltender stood tall against a barrage of rubber, making several acrobatic glove saves, but the Puckers’ forecheck was relentless, consistently creating traffic in front of the net to disrupt the sightlines.

### Third Period: Penalties, Pucks, and Persistence
If the first two periods were a chess match, the third was a full-blown street fight. The Mother Puckers widened the gap early with a goal from **JESSICA HINKEFENT** (assisted by Nisbet and **Jordan Hawkins Baker**) followed shortly by a dagger from **Abigail Nisbet**—her second of the night—at the 3:09 mark.

Then, things got, well... *sticky*. 

A flurry of high-sticking and roughing calls put the Puckers in a defensive shell late in the game. The Bees finally capitalized on the chaos at 9:58, when **SUZANNE HAWKINS** hammered home a pass from **Elaina Martin** to add a late tally for the home side. Despite the late push, the deficit was simply too high to climb.

### The Aftermath
For the Mother Puckers, this 5-2 victory is a massive statement. They leapfrog out of the bottom tier, proving they have the offensive firepower to hang with the league leaders if they can keep their discipline in check—they’ll certainly want to clean up those high-sticking penalties before their next matchup!

As for the Bad Bees, it’s back to the drawing board. They showed flashes of brilliance and a never-say-die attitude in the final frame, but tonight belonged to the Puckers, who proved that when they move the puck effectively, they’re a tough squad to stop.

**Current Standings Outlook:**
The Mother Puckers climb to 5 points, putting some breathing room between themselves and the bottom of the table, while the Bad Bees remain at 2 points, currently looking up at the rest of the league from the #5 spot.
