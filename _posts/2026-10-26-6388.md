# Mighty Drunks Put on a Scoring Clinic in 7-1 Rout of Rohan Riders

It was a cold night at the rink, but the Mighty Drunks were absolutely scorching, turning Friday night into a full-blown offensive exhibition as they dismantled the Rohan Riders 7-1. While the Riders came into the game looking to snap a skid, they instead found themselves caught in a Drunks-induced cyclone that showed exactly why the Mighty Drunks are firmly locked in the upper tier of the standings.

The scoring floodgates opened early when Omar Awad struck just over eight minutes into the first period, setting a tone that the Riders simply couldn’t shake. From that point on, it felt like the Drunks were operating with an extra skater on the ice, even when they weren't on the power play.

If the first period was a warning shot, the second was a full-on barrage. Cian Slattery picked up right where he left off in his recent scoring tear, burying a beauty to extend his goal streak to three games. Before the Riders could even catch their breath, Sean Laube cleaned up a crisp pass from Jared Hallahan to push the lead further. By the time the dust settled on the second, the Riders were reeling, hampered by a pair of penalties—including a classic "too many men" hiccup—that effectively killed any hope of a comeback. Awad added his second of the night late in the period, effectively putting the game on ice before the Zamboni even considered its final lap.

The Drunks showed no mercy in the third, with Slattery notching his second of the evening and Awad completing a brilliant hat trick in the final minute of play. Thomas Wolff joined the party midway through the frame, set up by a crafty feed from Justen Monroe. 

To their credit, the Riders refused to head to the locker room completely empty-handed. Alexander Rowin managed to break the shutout bid with just over ten minutes remaining, burying a pass from Mitchel Pierce to give the visiting faithful something to cheer for, but it was far too little, too late against a Drunks squad that looked like a well-oiled machine.

"We were just finding the lanes tonight," joked Awad in the hallway after the game, clutching the puck from his hat-trick performance. "I think the ice might have been tilted in our favor for about forty-five minutes straight."

With this dominant win, the Mighty Drunks continue to stake their claim as the team to beat in the local circuit, moving to 4-1-1 on the season. For the Rohan Riders, it’s back to the drawing board to find a spark as they continue to search for their first win of the campaign. The Drunks, meanwhile, look like a team primed to keep the party going as they chase down the frontrunners in the standings.
