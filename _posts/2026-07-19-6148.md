# Bee-witched and Riveted: Bad Bees Rally to Force Stalemate in 5-5 Thriller

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: July 19, 2026**

If you didn’t believe in miracles on ice, you clearly weren’t at the barn this morning. The underdog Bad Bees, looking to climb out of the bottom of the barrel, played host to the high-flying Tulsa Riveters, who strolled in boasting an undefeated 5-0-1 record. On paper, it looked like a mismatch; on the ice, it was a beautiful, chaotic, absolute barn burner.

When the dust settled, the scoreboard read 5-5. While the official books might call it a draw, the Bees walked away feeling like they just conquered Everest.

### Period 1: The Riveters’ Opening Salvo
The Riveters came out skating like they had somewhere important to be, putting the Bees’ defense under immediate pressure. **Hannah Atkins** opened the scoring at 11:11, set up by a sharp feed from **Barbara Lepe Briones**. Just two minutes later, **Amanda Long** extended her point streak, burying a helper from Atkins to make it 2-0.

The Bees looked rattled, but **Suzanne Hawkins**—the heart and soul of this squad—refused to let the hive collapse. She took matters into her own hands at 3:51, dancing through the zone unassisted to put the Bees on the board. However, the "Gigi" show was just getting started. **Gabrielle Gibson**, riding a heater of a four-game goal streak, restored the Riveters' two-goal cushion just before the intermission. 

### Period 2: Grinding It Out
The second frame turned into a blue-collar wrestling match. The Bees found some rhythm when the mysterious **Unknown**—who seems to be living up to that moniker by playing out of their mind—found the back of the net at 12:01. 

The Riveters’ **Stacey Burgess** found herself in the sin bin for holding at 9:03, giving the Bees a prime chance to equalize, but the Tulsa penalty kill was as stingy as a mid-summer swarm. Despite the mounting tension, the goaltending remained top-notch, forcing both sides to battle for every inch of ice.

### Period 3: The Rollercoaster Finish
If the first two periods were a stiff drink, the third was a double shot of espresso. 

Down by one, the Bees suddenly found their wings. **Grace Wolff** sparked a fire at 13.8, tying the game, and moments later, **Unknown** struck again at 14:21 to give the Bees their first lead of the morning. The home crowd was losing their minds, but you can’t keep a juggernaut down for long.

Gabrielle Gibson completed her brace at 5:28, and a lightning-quick response from Hannah Atkins just 25 seconds later put the Riveters back in front. But hold your horses! **Elaina Martin**, fed perfectly by Wolff, knotted it up once more at 6:58. 

### The Shootout: Cold Nerves, Hot Hands
After a frantic regulation, we headed to the shootout. It was a classic "who blinks first" scenario. Suzanne Hawkins stepped up with ice in her veins and deposited a beauty to give the Bees the edge. But Gabrielle Gibson, proving why she’s arguably the most dangerous skater in the league right now, answered right back for Tulsa. 

In the end, the 5-5 tie stands. For the Riveters, a single point keeps them breathing down the necks of The Valkyries at the top of the standings. For the Bad Bees? They just went toe-to-toe with a giant and didn't back down. If this is the start of a momentum shift for the bottom-dwellers, the rest of the league had better start checking their rearview mirrors.

**Final Score: Bad Bees 5, Tulsa Riveters 5**
