# A Scoring Spree: Shooting Blanks Turn Up the Heat in 12-4 Rout of Oklahoma Precast

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: May 18, 2026**

If you blinked during Monday night’s tilt at the local barn, you missed a goal—or three. In a game that saw more rubber flying than a tire factory, the Shooting Blanks (2-1-0) absolutely dismantled Oklahoma Precast (0-1-0) in a 12-4 offensive clinic that will be talked about at the water cooler all week.

The Blanks, fresh off a stinging loss, looked like a team possessed, finding the back of the net with the frequency of a metronome set to "panic."

### Period 1: A Wake-Up Call
Oklahoma Precast actually drew first blood. At 10:45, Joseph Pachuta tapped home a feed from Chris Cahill to silence the home crowd. But as any veteran of the ice knows, you don’t poke the bear. 

The Blanks responded with a flurry that would make a blizzard blush. Bobby Watson tied it up at 8:25, and before the PA announcer could even finish his coffee, Austin Tyler lit the lamp just 18 seconds later to put the Blanks up 2-1. It was a 40-second sequence that completely sucked the wind out of the Precast sails.

### Period 2: The Dam Breaks
If the first period was a light sprinkle, the second was a monsoon. The Blanks went on an absolute tear, scoring seven unanswered goals. 

Phillip Caputo started the carnage, followed by Steven Purn, and then the Bobby Watson show hit full stride. Watson completed a natural hat trick in the span of roughly five minutes, showing the kind of hands that belong in a jewelry store. By the time Aaron Best, Caputo (his second), and David Best joined the party, the scoreboard was looking more like a phone number than a hockey score. 

The frustrations boiled over for Oklahoma Precast just before the intermission, with Zachary Wade cooling his heels in the box for tripping, trying desperately to slow down a freight train that had already left the station.

### Period 3: Closing the Curtain
Despite the mountain of a deficit, Oklahoma Precast showed some grit in the third. Bobby Watson snagged his *fourth* goal of the night early on, and Hunter Best tacked on another to hit double digits. 

To their credit, Oklahoma Precast refused to fold. Nicholas Power, Nixon Bartlett, and Matthew Rankin managed to find twine in the final frame, adding a bit of respectability to the scorecard, but it was far too little, far too late.

### The Takeaway
For the Shooting Blanks, this is exactly the "get-right" game they needed. Their transition game was crisp, and the passing chemistry—specifically between Bobby Watson and Austin Tyler—was borderline telepathic. When you're putting 12 goals past an opponent, you’re doing something right in the offensive zone. 

For Oklahoma Precast, it’s a tough night at the office. They’ll need to shore up their defensive coverage in the house; tonight, the slot was essentially a drive-thru lane for the Blanks. 

**Stat of the Night:** Bobby Watson. Four goals, two assists. A six-point night is the stuff of beer-league legends, and he absolutely dictated the tempo from the opening puck drop.

With this result, the Shooting Blanks move to 4 points on the season, firmly establishing themselves as a force to be reckoned with. Oklahoma Precast will head back to the drawing board, looking to find their footing as the season heats up.
