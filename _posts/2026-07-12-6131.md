# A Total Meltdown: Laser Obliterate Knights in 10-0 Blinding Victory

**Date: July 12, 2026**

David Mansfield joined the party just over a minute later, followed immediately by another strike from Verity. By the time the horn sounded to end the first, the Laser had already hung a "Touchdown and a Field Goal" on the board. The Knights looked like they were skating in cement, offering zero resistance to the Laser’s relentless cycle game.

### Second Period: Adding Insult to Injury
If the Knights had any hopes of a "locker room reset" speech paying off, they were shattered before the second period ice had fully hardened. Mansfield picked up right where he left off, and Colton Taylor continued his legendary hot streak, extending his goal-scoring tear to a mind-boggling six games. 

The Knights’ goaltending was left hung out to dry, facing a barrage of odd-man rushes and unchallenged back-door feeds. It wasn’t just a game; it was a clinical dissection. The Laser were passing the puck like they were on the power play even at 5-on-5, finding "the top shelf where mom keeps the cookies" with surgical precision.

### Third Period: Closing the Book
With the game firmly in the deep freeze, the Laser didn’t ease up on the pedal. An uncredited goal early in the frame kept the momentum going, and despite a late holding penalty by Ronald Herwig—a rare moment of Laser indiscretion—the Knights couldn't even find the back of the net to save their pride. Bryce Perkins capped off the misery at the 9:45 mark, rounding out the double-digit scoreline.

Let’s be honest: this wasn’t a barn burner; it was a total demolition.
