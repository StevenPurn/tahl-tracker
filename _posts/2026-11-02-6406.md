# Average Joes Send The Mafia Packing in 6-2 Rink-Rattler

If you wanted to see a team shake off the mid-season doldrums, you should’ve been at the barn this Friday night. The Average Joes looked like they’d downed a double espresso before the puck dropped, dismantling The Mafia 6-2 in a regular-season clash that proved the Joes aren't just here to fill out the league standings.

The Mafia came in sitting pretty at fourth place, but the ice tilted early and never really leveled out. It wasn’t exactly a clean start for the Joes—Brian Maher lit the lamp for The Mafia at the 10:21 mark of the first—but that seemed to be the wake-up call the Joes needed. Once they got the engine running, they didn't look back.

Ryan Martinez, who apparently thinks he’s allergic to scoreless games, extended his goal streak to four with a beautiful solo effort at 14:42. JOSHUA HENDRICKSON followed suit, keeping his own streak alive, and by the time SERGIO CASTELLANOS buried a feed from Hendrickson, the Joes were skating with a swagger that had The Mafia’s bench looking like they were trying to solve a quadratic equation.

"We just decided to stop overthinking it and start crashing the crease," said one Joes skater near the exit, smelling faintly of deep-heat rub and victory. "Turns out, when the puck hits the twine, it’s a lot more fun than chasing it around the boards."

The middle frame was a gritty, grinding affair. The Joes did hit a speed bump when Martinez headed to the box for "Too Many Men"—a classic local league blunder that usually involves someone jumping off the bench before their teammate has fully vacated the zip code—but their defensive unit held the line like they were protecting the crown jewels.

The third period was all about the Nicole Baker show. She was an absolute menace, finding the net twice—once on a clever assist from Tracy Wilmes, and again off a beauty of a setup from Martinez. Between her goals, Gage Walker managed to squeak one past for The Mafia off a Scott Hellard helper, but at that point, it was just window dressing on a house that had already been remodeled.

For The Mafia, it’s a tough pill to swallow after a solid run of form, and they’ll be looking to scrub this one from the memory banks before their next tilt. For the Average Joes, this 6-2 beatdown is exactly the kind of momentum builder that turns a mediocre season into a potential run. They move to 3-2-1, proving that even if they aren't at the top of the mountain yet, they’re definitely starting to climb. 

If they keep playing like this, the league leaders might want to start checking their rearview mirrors.
