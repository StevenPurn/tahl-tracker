# Bison Stampede Through the Ice: Tulsa Edges Out Kraken in Late-Game Thriller

**By: "Rinkside" Rick Henderson**
*July 20, 2026 | 10:30 PM*

The air inside the barn was thick enough to chew on tonight, and the temperature on the ice was, ironically, plenty warm for a mid-summer tilt. Coming into tonight’s matchup, the **Kraken Cold Ones** were looking to stop the bleeding on a five-game slide, while the **Tulsa Bison** rolled in with the swagger of a team firmly planted in the upper echelon of the standings. 

When the final horn sounded, it was the Bison who skated away with a 3-2 victory, though the Cold Ones gave them a heart-stopping scare in the closing minutes.

### First Period: Wix-ted Whispers
The game started as a tactical game of chess, but it didn't take long for the Bison to find the back of the net. At the 4:00 mark, **Michael Wixted** took matters into his own hands, firing a laser that caught the Kraken netminder leaning. With no helpers on the sheet, it was a pure display of individual hustle. 

The Kraken looked to respond when Tulsa’s Alan Dyke headed to the sin bin for a tripping minor at 9:58, but the Cold Ones’ power play was about as effective as a screen door on a submarine, leaving the Bison net unbothered heading into the intermission.

### Second Period: The Bison Stampede
If the first period was a skirmish, the second was an all-out bombardment. Alan Dyke, looking to make amends for his trip earlier in the night, buried one at the 04.0-second mark of the period, set up perfectly by **Tyler Holmes** and the red-hot **JW DePriest**. 

The momentum turned into a tidal wave for Tulsa shortly after. When the Cold Ones’ **Justin McKinney** was whistled for interference, the Bison smelled blood in the water. Just nineteen seconds into the power play, **Joe Baker**—fresh off his goal streak—poked one home off a nifty feed from Dyke. Suddenly, the Kraken were down 3-0, and it looked like the rout was on.

### Third Period: The Kraken Wake Up
Never count out a team with nothing to lose. The Kraken Cold Ones, playing with a "sink or swim" mentality, finally started putting some traffic in front of the net.

The comeback bid sparked at 4:17 when **Robert Tyree** kept his goal streak alive, converting a clean pass from McKinney. The energy in the building shifted—you could feel the breeze from the skates picking up intensity. Then, at 8:58, **Paul Davis** sent a wobbler past the Tulsa keeper off a beautiful setup from **Shon Rogers** and McKinney again. 

Down 3-2 with time ticking away, the Kraken threw the kitchen sink at the Bison. They pinned the Tulsa squad in their own end, looking for that "top shelf where mom keeps the cookies" equalizer, but the Bison defense held the line, blocking shots and eating pucks until the final buzzer rang.

### The Aftermath: What It Means
For the **Tulsa Bison**, this victory is a statement. Sitting at #4 in the league, they showed the discipline needed to hold a lead under pressure. They continue to climb, keeping pace with the heavy hitters at the top of the table.

As for the **Kraken Cold Ones**, the record drops to 1-6-0, but they should walk out of that locker room with their heads held high. A comeback bid against a top-four team is a massive confidence builder. They have the pieces; they just need to stop digging that 3-0 hole before they start playing their game.

The league standings remain a pressure cooker, and if tonight was any indication, the ice is only going to get faster from here. Stick taps all around—that was a real barn burner.
