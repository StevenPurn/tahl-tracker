# Outsiders Keep Rolling With 8-3 Rout of Berzerkers

If you wanted to see a masterclass in how to stay undefeated, you should have been at the rink this morning. The Outsiders, currently sitting pretty at the top of the standings, showed exactly why they’re the team to beat, dismantling the Berzerkers by a final score of 8-3. It wasn't just a win; it was an early morning wake-up call for the rest of the league.

The Outsiders came out of the gate swinging, clearly fueled by a massive pot of coffee and an unstoppable hunger for the net. Tyler Waller set the tone early, netting his first of two goals just over two minutes into the game, followed shortly by a Logan Pardue strike at 5:25. By the time the horn sounded for the first break, the Berzerkers looked like they were trying to solve a riddle written in invisible ink.

The second period was where the wheels really started to wobble for the Berzerkers. Waller struck again at 10:16, showing the kind of hands that make goaltenders retire early, and Scott Hellard and Juan Padilla joined the party soon after. While Zac Hughes finally managed to get the Berzerkers on the board at 5:51, the Outsiders’ momentum was about as easy to stop as a runaway freight train. Tyler Holmes added his own tally just under a minute later to keep the pressure firmly in the red zone.

"We just tried to keep our feet moving," said a grinning Outsider after the game, though the truth is they were playing like they had an extra skater on the ice for most of the morning. The Berzerkers did show some grit in the third, with Hughes bagging his second of the game and Mik Halgren finding twine late in the frame, but it was far too little, far too late. 

Logan Pardue and Juan Padilla each added their second goals of the game in the final period to put the icing on an absolute blowout. Even a bit of late-game extracurriculars—a double-minor trip to the box for Enoch Cox and Hayden Wheeler after some third-period jawing—couldn't dampen the Outsiders' mood. 

For the Outsiders, this win keeps their perfect streak alive, moving them to a blistering 6-0-1 record. They aren't just winning games; they’re turning them into statement pieces. The Berzerkers, now sitting at 3-3-1, will have to head back to the drawing board and figure out how to tighten the screws before their next outing. 

As for the Outsiders? They’re leaving the rink looking like the kings of the mountain. If they keep playing like this, the only thing they’re going to be worried about is finding enough space in the trophy case at the end of the season.
