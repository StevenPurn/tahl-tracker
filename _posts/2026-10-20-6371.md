# Killer Goats Graze on Hitmen Silver in 4-2 Upset

In a clash that proved the league table is more of a suggestion than a prophecy, the Killer Goats stomped their way to a 4-2 victory over the previously undefeated Hitmen Silver on Monday night. While the Hitmen came into the rink sitting pretty atop the standings, the Goats showed them that reputation doesn’t help you when you’re pinned against the boards.

The Hitmen Silver opened the festivities early in the first period when Andrew Sleezer found the back of the net off a helper from Eric Guthrie at the 5:32 mark. It was a cold splash of water for the Goats, but the team didn't buckle. Instead, they spent the intermission sharpening their horns, returning to the second period with a hunger usually reserved for an all-you-can-eat buffet.

Samuel Michaud leveled the score at 12:01, assisted by the ever-reliable Michael Bergstrom. Momentum shifted like a pendulum in a hurricane, and just six minutes later, Lucas Blanchard proved he’s playing for keeps, finding the net unassisted to put the Goats ahead. Even a trip to the sin bin for Joshua Gonya couldn’t sour the mood, as the Goats successfully navigated the penalty kill to head into the final frame with a narrow lead.

The third period was an absolute barn burner. The Hitmen clawed their way back to a 2-2 tie at 12:57 thanks to Sleezer’s second goal of the night, assisted by Calvin Baker and Christopher Gardner. For a moment, it looked like the Hitmen might complete their typical late-game rally, but the Goats had other plans. 

Redemption came quickly for Joshua Gonya, who buried a puck off a Bergstrom feed at 1:38, followed by a dagger from Lucas Blanchard—his second of the night—at 3:54, with Nelson Pina picking up the helper. Despite a late penalty scare involving Aaron Quinton, the Goats’ defense held firm, effectively turning the Hitmen’s offense into a group of aimless wanderers.

"We knew they were the kings of the mountain," said a triumphant (and slightly out of breath) Goats locker room source after the buzzer. "But tonight, we decided we were the ones who owned the mountain. Also, we had a really good pre-game snack. Never underestimate the power of a quality electrolyte gummy."

The loss marks the first blemish on the Hitmen Silver’s record, snapping a streak of dominance that had the rest of the league checking their mirrors. For the Killer Goats, the win is a massive momentum builder, proving that their steady climb in the standings isn't just a fluke. As the league heads deeper into the regular season, one thing is certain: nobody is sleeping on these Goats anymore.
