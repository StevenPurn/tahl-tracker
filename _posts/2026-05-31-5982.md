# A Sunday Morning Shellacking: The Rookery Soars Past the Riders in 5-1 Masterclass

**By: "Slap-Shot" Sammy, Hometown Beat Reporter**
**Date: May 31, 2026**

If you were looking for a casual morning skate to shake off the weekend cobwebs, you picked the wrong arena. The ice at the barn was absolutely humming this morning as The Rookery squared off against the Rohan Riders. By the time the final horn sounded, The Rookery had firmly established themselves as a team to watch, skating away with a commanding 5-1 victory that had the home crowd buzzing.

### First Period: The Blitz
The Rookery didn’t just wake up; they showed up to work with a lunch pail and a bad attitude. Barely 13 minutes in, **James Caldron**—riding high on a hot streak—opened the floodgates, burying a beauty assisted by Tyler McKee and Cody Turner. 

Before the Riders could even find their footing, **Aaron Crum** decided one goal wasn’t enough, putting on a solo clinic just over a minute later to make it 2-0. The Riders managed to stop the bleeding with a gritty, unassisted effort from **Samuel Northup** with less than a minute left in the frame, but the momentum was undeniably leaning toward The Rookery’s corner.

### Second Period: The Grinder’s Delight
The middle frame turned into a blue-collar slog. The Riders tried to muck it up, but The Rookery kept their cool while the Riders lost theirs. **Tyler Waller** extended the lead to 3-1 at the 13:17 mark, showcasing some silky mitts to find the twine. 

Things got chippy as the period waned. Aivar Kant spent two minutes in the sin bin for a trip, and Nathan Lesnick joined him shortly after for a high-sticking infraction. The Rookery’s power play didn’t convert, but they did something more important: they kept the Riders trapped in their own zone, forcing them to chase the puck like dogs at a park.

### Third Period: Closing the Book
The final period belonged to the men in The Rookery jerseys. They played a textbook game—"clogging the lanes," "keeping the biscuits in the basket," and ensuring there was heavy traffic in front of the Riders' net. 

**Chris Olsen**, continuing his multi-game scoring tear, stretched the lead to 4-1 with an assist from Connor Slattery. Not content to just cruise to the finish, Caldron hammered the final nail into the coffin at 9:10, notching his second of the game. A late penalty on Lesnick for tripping essentially signaled the end of the Riders' hopes. 

### The Bottom Line
The Rookery enters the fray with a statement win, showing the rest of the league that they aren't just here for the coffee and the company. The Rohan Riders, despite their previous form, looked uncharacteristically disjointed against a hungry opponent. 

With the Mighty Drunks currently setting the pace at 2-0-0, the rest of the pack is officially on notice. The Rookery has arrived, and if today’s performance was any indication, they’re looking to climb that leaderboard one shift at a time.

**Stars of the Game:**
*   **James Caldron:** Two goals, pure class.
*   **Tyler Waller:** A goal and an assist, dominated the neutral zone.
*   **The Rookery Defense:** Kept the crease clean and made life miserable for the Riders' transition game.
