# RATTLED THE CAGE: The Bone Zone Turns the Yetis Into Slush in 8-2 Rout

**TULSA** – If you were looking for a quiet Thursday night at the rink, you picked the wrong zip code. On August 6th, the arena was buzzing, the ice was fast, and The Bone Zone looked like a team possessed, dismantling the Yetis in a dominant 8-2 performance that will have the rest of the league checking their mirrors.

Coming into tonight, both squads were neck-and-neck in the standings—The Bone Zone at #3 and the Yetis right on their heels at #4. But what was expected to be a trench-warfare battle for positioning turned into a clinic in clinical finishing.

### First Period: The Zone Claims Their Territory
The Bone Zone didn’t just knock on the door; they kicked it off the hinges. It started at 11:52 of the opening frame when **John White** buried his first of the night, set up by a pristine feed from Dan Munson and JW DePriest. 

Once the dam broke, the flood followed. **Bryce Perkins** went solo at 4:56, dancing through the neutral zone before uncorking a wrister that beat the Yeti tender clean. Before the intermission, **Garrett Williams** joined the party at 8:21, courtesy of a Timothy N. See setup, putting The Bone Zone up 3-0. The Yetis were left looking like they were skating through quicksand while the visitors were running on rocket fuel.

### Second Period: Maintaining the Pressure
If the Yetis hoped for a reset, they were sorely mistaken. **Jerry Bates** continued his hot streak, finding the back of the net at 10:48, with Williams and Perkins grabbing the apples. 

Then, John White decided one goal simply wasn't enough. With 12:31 remaining, he sniped his second of the evening, again off a crisp helper from Timothy N. See. At 5-0, the Bone Zone was putting on a passing exhibition that would make a tiki-taka soccer coach weep with joy. The Yetis’ goaltending was under siege, facing a relentless barrage of traffic in front of the crease.

### Third Period: The Goal Parade
The final frame saw the floodgates stay wide open. Garrett Williams notched his second of the night at 14:38, and just as the clock was ticking toward the final buzzer, **Robert Verity** hammered one home at 17.7 seconds remaining, assisted by the unstoppable duo of White and See.

To their credit, the Yetis didn’t pack it in. They found some late-game life when **Dillon King** (assisted by Derek Campbell) put them on the board, followed by a gritty, unassisted effort from **Katherine Popoff** at the 4:16 mark. But sandwiched between those tallies was another **Jerry Bates** goal, his second of the contest, proving that there was no way to dampen the Bone Zone’s spirits tonight.

### The Takeaway
When the dust settled on this 8-2 barn burner, the message was clear: The Bone Zone is firing on all cylinders. 

With this statement win, The Bone Zone firmly cements their hold on the #3 spot, distancing themselves from the mid-table clutter. The Yetis will need to regroup and lace their skates up tighter; tonight was a tough pill to swallow, but with their talent, they have plenty of runway left to climb back up the ladder.

As for the fans? We got a masterclass in puck movement and a highlight reel that’ll keep the locker room buzzing all weekend. If you weren't there, you missed a classic. See you at the next drop!
