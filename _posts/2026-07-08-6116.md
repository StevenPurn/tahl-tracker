# Valkyries Soar, Puckers Stumble: A Tuesday Night Tussle at the Rink

**DATE:** July 7, 2026  
**LOCATION:** The Barn  
**FINAL:** The Valkyries 4, Mother Puckers 1

If you didn’t make it out to the rink this Tuesday, you missed a clinic in momentum management. In a classic clash between the second-seeded Valkyries and the middle-of-the-pack Mother Puckers, the Valkyries proved exactly why they are currently breathing down the necks of the league-leading Riveters. 

It was a brisk night on the ice, but for the Mother Puckers, the temperature didn’t climb nearly high enough to thaw out the Valkyries’ white-hot offense.

### First Period: A Lightning Strike
The Valkyries didn't just come to play; they came to set the tone. From the opening puck drop, they looked like they’d had an extra shot of espresso in the locker room. Just 3:05 into the contest, the Valkyries’ #87 found the back of the net off a beauty of a feed from #97, silencing the home crowd early.

Before the Mother Puckers could even lace up their mental skates, the Valkyries struck again. At the 4:04 mark, #7 capitalized on a crisp pass from #45, depositing the biscuit in the basket to put the Valkyries up 2-0. It was a textbook start for the visitors, leaving the Puckers looking like they were skating through wet cement.

### Second Period: A Brief Flicker of Hope
Trailing by two, the Mother Puckers finally started finding some traffic in front of the net in the middle frame. At 11:22, the drought ended. Elizabeth Johnson, assisted by the gritty duo of Emily LaRance and Amanda Gonya, finally solved the Valkyries’ netminder, rifling a shot that put the Puckers on the board. The energy in the building shifted—you could feel the momentum swinging like a pendulum.

But the Valkyries are a veteran squad for a reason. Instead of folding, they hunkered down. At 6:05, #45 struck gold, burying a pass from #87 to restore the two-goal cushion and effectively deflate the Puckers' rising balloon. 

### Third Period: The Valkyries Seal the Deal
The final frame was all about defensive fortitude for the visitors. While the Puckers pushed hard, desperate to claw their way back, they found themselves hitting a wall. The Valkyries stayed disciplined, waiting for their opening. 

With 12:04 left on the clock, #7 notched her second goal of the night, again assisted by the seemingly omnipresent #97. That 4-1 dagger proved to be the final nail in the coffin. The Valkyries’ goaltending was nothing short of a brick wall tonight, flashing the leather and shutting down the lanes with surgical precision to ensure the result remained lopsided.

### The Aftermath: What It Means
For The Valkyries, this 4-1 victory is a massive statement. They climb to a 5-1-0 record, keeping the pressure firmly on the Tulsa Riveters at the top of the standings. They are playing a disciplined, "no-nonsense" style of hockey that is going to make them a nightmare for any opponent in the postseason.

As for the Mother Puckers, tonight is a "back to the drawing board" situation. Sitting at 2-3-1, they have the talent to keep pace, but they’ll need to figure out how to stop the bleeding in the first period before they find themselves staring at a scoreboard they can't climb back from.

The season is heating up, and if tonight was any indication, the road to the championship is going to be paved with broken sticks and hard-fought battles. We’ll see you at the rink next time—don’t forget your parka.
