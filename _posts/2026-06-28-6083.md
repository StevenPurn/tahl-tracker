# Riveters Rivet the Competition: Tulsa Dominates Power Puck Girls in 7-3 Offensive Showcase

**TULSA** – If you were looking for a quiet Sunday evening at the rink, you clearly weren't at the BOK Center. The Tulsa Riveters turned the ice into their personal playground on June 28th, steamrolling the Power Puck Girls 7-3 in a display of offensive firepower that left the visitors’ defense looking like they were trying to solve a Rubik’s cube in the dark.

With this win, the Riveters (now 4-0-1) keep their undefeated regulation streak alive and sit firmly on the heels of the league-leading Valkyries. Meanwhile, the Power Puck Girls continue to search for their footing in a season that has been nothing short of a uphill climb.

### Period 1: A Blitzkrieg at the Blue Line
The Riveters didn't just come out of the gate; they shattered it. In a span of less than two minutes, Tulsa turned the game into a blowout before the first period even reached the midway point. 

**Gabrielle Gibson** opened the floodgates at 2:28, followed eight seconds later by an **Ivy Emerson** strike that caught the goalie napping. Before the Power Puck Girls could even regroup, **McKenna K. Rhodes** buried one off a beautiful feed from Emerson at 4:18. By the time the buzzer sounded for the first break, the Riveters were up 3-0, and the scent of a blowout was thick in the arena air.

### Period 2: Battling Through the Traffic
The Power Puck Girls finally found some rhythm in the second, breaking the shutout at 14:48 when a mystery sniper found the back of the net off a **Suzanne Hawkins** helper. But the Riveters weren’t interested in a comeback story. **Kim Wilmes**, flanked by the tandem of **Remisha Blation and Holly Hutto**, hammered home a gritty goal at 4:53, navigating the heavy traffic in front of the crease to restore the three-goal cushion. 

### Period 3: Goal-a-Palooza
The final frame was absolute chaos. Suzanne Hawkins, playing like a woman possessed, grabbed two more for the Power Puck Girls, trying to drag her team back into the conversation. 

But Tulsa’s response was clinical. **Gabrielle Gibson** netted her second of the night at 12:29, and **Ivy Emerson**—having the game of her life—followed up just a minute later to seal the deal. **Stacey Burgess** added a cherry on top with a silky finish at 6:45 off an Emerson assist, extending her personal goal streak to two games. 

The only blemish on the Riveters’ ledger was a late tripping penalty on Burgess at 9:15, but by then, the game was already destined for the highlight reels.

### The Aftermath
For the Riveters, this result is a loud statement to the rest of the league. With Gibson and Emerson clicking on all cylinders, the offense is firing from everywhere—top shelf where mom keeps the cookies, and down low where the dirt is kicked up.

**Standings Check:**
*   **Tulsa Riveters:** They stay cemented in the #2 spot, but their 4-0-1 record suggests they are the team to beat. They’re playing with the confidence of a club that knows a championship is within reach.
*   **Power Puck Girls:** Still winless, they’ll look to the tape of tonight’s final period, where Suzanne Hawkins proved they have the heart to hang with the big dogs. They’ll need to tighten the screws defensively if they want to escape the basement.

The ice may have thawed, but the Riveters are just heating up. Keep your sticks on the ice, Tulsa—this team is worth the price of admission.
