# Gold as Ice Pours It On in 9-2 Rout of Ice Breakers Plus

If you’re looking for a lesson in how to break the game wide open, look no further than the late-night tilt between Gold as Ice and the Ice Breakers Plus. In a game that started as a tight, defensive affair, Gold as Ice eventually turned the ice into their own personal playground, skating away with a commanding 9-2 victory that left the scoreboard operator begging for a coffee break.

The first period was a cautious game of chess, with the only notable friction being a tripping penalty assessed to Gold as Ice’s Daniel Hanning. But the tension shattered early in the second. Mark DeSimone broke the seal with an unassisted beauty at 2:36, signaling that he had brought his "A" game. While the Ice Breakers briefly answered back just two minutes later through their own mysterious number 21, the momentum was short-lived. Daniel Maxwell quickly restored the lead, taking a crisp feed from Dickie Williams to keep his personal two-game goal streak alive and kicking.

If the second period was a warning shot, the third period was a full-on artillery barrage. 

Gold as Ice simply wouldn't take their foot off the gas. DeSimone added his second of the night, followed by a flurry of offensive wizardry that saw Aden Ingram, Dylan Dillard, Dickie Williams, and John Bewley all finding the back of the net in quick succession. DeSimone was clearly the engine room for the team, racking up three assists in the third period alone to go along with his two goals. 

"I think we just stopped overthinking it," said one exuberant Gold as Ice teammate after the buzzer. "Once the puck started finding the back of the net, it was like the floodgates just forgot they were supposed to stay closed."

Even a brief power play for the Ice Breakers couldn't stifle the Gold as Ice machine, which saw Daniel Maxwell net his second of the evening while the team was shorthanded. While the Ice Breakers managed a late-period consolation goal from their number 1 to make it 9-2, the result was never in doubt once the third-period offensive clinic began.

The win is a huge boost for Gold as Ice, who improve to 2-1-1 on the season and look to carry this massive momentum shift into their next matchup. For the Ice Breakers Plus, it’s a tough night at the office as they continue to hunt for their first win of the campaign. 

If this performance is any indication, Gold as Ice has finally found their rhythm, and they’re playing like a squad that’s ready to make some serious waves in the standings. You can bet the rest of the league is going to take notice—this wasn't just a win, it was a statement.
