# A Frozen Nightmare: Hitmen Silver Put the Blizzard on Ice in 7-0 Shutout

**By: "Slap-Shot" Sammy, Hometown Beat Reporter**
*July 28, 2026 | 9:00 PM Puck Drop*

If you were looking for a little heat on a Sunday night, you definitely didn't find it on the Blizzard’s side of the ice. In what was supposed to be a standard mid-season tilt, Hitmen Silver walked into the rink and delivered a clinic, effectively putting the Blizzard in a deep freeze with a dominant 7-0 victory. 

The Hitmen are currently playing hockey like they’ve got magnets in their sticks, and tonight, the Blizzard were merely spectators to a masterclass in puck possession.

### Period 1: The Hitmen Start Their Engine
The game didn't exactly start with a clear path for the Hitmen. Just over a minute into the frame, Eric Guthrie sent the Hitmen to the box for hooking, giving the Blizzard an early chance to establish some rhythm. But instead of folding, the Hitmen’s penalty kill looked like a brick wall wearing jerseys. 

Once they returned to full strength, the floodgates opened. Lukas Vodicka kicked off the party at 2:23, burying a feed from the hottest hands in the league, Nicklas Clark and Josh Moyer. Less than a minute later, Moyer decided he wanted his own highlight reel, finding the twine to stretch the lead to 2-0. By the time the horn sounded for the intermission, the Hitmen had the Blizzard skating in quicksand.

### Period 2: The Moyer-Clark Show
If the first period was a warning, the second was a declaration of war. Josh Moyer added his second of the night at the 4:59 mark, with Eric Guthrie redeeming his earlier penalty with a crisp assist. 

Just a minute later, the inevitable happened: Nicklas Clark, riding a scorching three-game goal streak, proved why he’s a nightmare for opposing blue-liners. Assisted by Vodicka and Moyer, Clark hammered one home to make it 4-0. The Blizzard defense was scrambling, and the Hitmen were playing "keep away" with the intensity of a pack of wolves.

### Period 3: Finishing the Job
The final frame was all about padding the stats and maintaining the goose egg. The Hitmen showed no signs of taking their foot off the gas. Moyer completed a massive night by sniping his third goal of the game at 13:29, with Clark continuing his personal crusade on the scoresheet. 

Vodicka followed that up with his second of the night just under a minute later, and Christopher Gardner added the exclamation point at 6:53 off a beautiful setup from Skylar Miller. The Blizzard netminder was under siege all night, facing a barrage of high-danger chances that would make any goalie’s head spin.

Tonight’s result was a stark reminder of the gap between the contenders and the bottom-feeders.
