# RENEGADES SURVIVE A WILD WEST SHOOTOUT: 9-7 THRILLER OVER TORNADOES

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: July 26, 2026**

If you didn’t come to the rink with your heart medication this morning, you probably should have. In a 8:15 AM puck drop that felt more like a caffeine-fueled fever dream than a standard regular-season tilt, the Renegades and the Tornadoes turned the ice into a total pinball machine. When the dust finally settled and the Zamboni took the ice, the scoreboard read 9-7 in favor of the Renegades. 

It was a barn burner, a fire drill, a defensive coordinator's nightmare, and an absolute treat for anyone who loves the red light flashing.

### Period 1: The Tornadoes Start Whirling
The Renegades wasted zero time, with **Matthew Rankin** finding the twine immediately—literally at the 00.0 mark—to set the tone. But the Tornadoes, desperate to snap a five-game slide, responded with a flurry. **Nicklas Clark** continued his hot streak, knotting it up, followed by a **Tobias Alvegran** strike and a **Josh Moyer** cleanup job. By the time **Bobby McKinley** snagged a greasy one back for the Renegades at 3:13, the Tornadoes had successfully turned the momentum into a whirlpool.

### Period 2: The Rankin-Bateman Show
Whatever Renegades coach said in the locker room between periods, they need to bottle it. The Renegades came out playing like their skates were greased with lightning. 

**Matthew Rankin** turned into a one-man wrecking crew, sniping two more to complete a natural hat trick, while **Alex Bateman** reminded everyone why he’s on a six-game heater, burying two beauties of his own. The Tornadoes looked like they were trying to stop a tidal wave with a screen door. By the time the horn sounded for the second intermission, the Renegades had seized control of the scoreboard, leaving the Tornadoes looking for a life raft.

### Period 3: Hold On To Your Helmets
The third period was absolute carnage. Rankin added *another*—yes, a fourth goal for his personal collection—before Bateman grabbed his hat trick goal just 28 seconds later. The Renegades were up by a mile, but hockey is a funny game. 

The Tornadoes weren’t ready to head to the showers yet. **Nicklas Clark** netted two quick ones to complete his own hat trick, and **Josh Moyer** and **Christopher Cieslak** pushed back hard. **Nicholas Power** provided the critical insurance marker for the Renegades at 6:36, which proved to be the difference-maker as the Tornadoes scrambled late in the final minutes.

### The Aftermath
It was a defensive collapse on both sides, sure, but goodness, it was entertaining. 

**What this means for the standings:**
The Renegades (6-3-0) tighten their grip on the second seed, keeping pace with the league-leading Shooting Blanks and proving they have the offensive firepower to hang with anyone. As for the Tornadoes (2-7-0), the losing streak extends to six. They showed plenty of heart—and clearly, they can fill the net—but they need to find a way to stop the bleeding in their own zone if they want to climb out of the basement.

The Renegades leave the ice with the W, but the Tornadoes proved that if you give them an inch, they’ll take a mile—or in this case, seven goals. 

*See you at the next drop, folks. Keep your stick on the ice.*
