# A Drunk-en Shellacking: Mighty Drunks Run Tuskens Out of the Building

If you stepped out for a refill on your popcorn during Thursday night’s tilt at the rink, you probably came back to find the scoreboard looking like a misprinted grocery receipt. The Mighty Drunks put on a clinical display of offensive firepower, dismantling the Tulsa Tuskens with an 8-1 victory that wasn't even as close as the lopsided score suggests.

The Drunks came into the night sitting comfortably in third place, and they played like a team that hadn't just eaten dinner, but was looking to devour the entire buffet. The Tuskens, still searching for that elusive first win of the season, simply had no answer for the Slattery brothers, who turned the offensive zone into their own private playground.

Connor Slattery kicked off the festivities at 11:46 in the first period, courtesy of a setup from his brother, Cian. Before the Tuskens could even find their legs, Cian Slattery decided he wasn’t done helping his sibling and proceeded to light the lamp twice himself—once off a slick pass from Omar Awad, and then a solo effort that had the Tuskens’ defense looking like they were trying to catch smoke with a butterfly net. By the time the first intermission hit, the Drunks held a commanding 3-0 lead.

The second period was more of the same, with the Drunks clicking with the kind of chemistry that usually takes a decade of marriage to achieve. Connor Slattery proved to be the bane of the Tuskens’ existence, netting two more goals, both assisted by the ever-dangerous Omar Awad. Toss in a solo highlight-reel strike from Gage Walker, and the Drunks headed into the final frame holding a 6-0 lead that sent the Tuskens’ bench into a state of visible disbelief.

"We were just trying to keep the puck on our sticks and off the glass," said one Drunks player in the locker room, grinning behind a post-game beverage. "When the chemistry is flowing like that, you just point your stick at the net and hope the goalie is feeling generous."

The Tuskens finally found a flicker of light at the 9:14 mark of the third when Cody Springfield buried a feed from Luke Morley to spoil the shutout bid. It was a lone bright spot in an otherwise dark night for Tulsa. The Drunks weren't finished, though, as Nathan Fahland and Sean Laube tacked on two more to put a firm exclamation point on the 8-1 drubbing.

The victory pushes the Mighty Drunks to 5-1-2, solidifying their spot as a legitimate force in the league standings. As for the Tuskens, they’ll have to head back to the drawing board to break that winless skid. For the Drunks, it was a night of pure, unadulterated dominance—the kind of performance that leaves the opposing goalie wondering if they should have just taken up golf instead.
