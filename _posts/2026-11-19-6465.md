# Outsiders Run Wild in 10-1 Shellacking of Kraken

If you were looking for a quiet, low-scoring Tuesday night affair, you picked the wrong barn to park your skates in. The Outsiders descended upon the ice at 9:30 p.m. last night and proceeded to treat the Kraken Cold Ones’ defensive zone like their own personal buffet, walking away with a lopsided 10-1 victory that had the scoreboard operator double-checking their math.

The Outsiders, currently sitting in the second seed and hungry for a climb to the top, looked like they were skating on rocket fuel. They set the tone early and often, burying five goals in the first period alone. Brett Lamb opened the floodgates just over a minute into the frame, and by the time the buzzer sounded to end the first, the Kraken were looking for a life raft. Lamb, who finished the night with a hat trick, was a blur of motion, consistently finding soft spots in the coverage.

"We told the boys to keep the feet moving," said an Outsiders bench hand while drying off his gear after the game. "I don’t know if the Kraken forgot their coffee or if we just showed up with an extra gear, but once that first one hit the twine, it felt like the dam burst."

To their credit, the Cold Ones didn't just fold the tent. Despite the mounting deficit, Justin McKinney managed to break the shutout bid in the second period, burying a nice feed from Robert Tyree. For a fleeting moment, the Kraken bench showed some life, but the Outsiders’ momentum was a runaway freight train. Tyler Waller, in particular, was on an absolute tear, racking up a hat trick of his own and showing the kind of chemistry with Logan Pardue that makes defenders lose sleep. 

Pardue was the quiet architect of the night, racking up helpers like he was handing out business cards at a convention. Every time the puck found his stick, disaster seemed to follow for the Kraken.

By the third period, the Outsiders were playing keep-away, adding two more goals—including Lamb’s third of the night—to salt away the 10-1 final. For the Kraken, it’s a tough pill to swallow after a difficult run of games, but they’ll have to wash the taste of this one out of their mouths before their next tilt.

As for The Outsiders, this win keeps them locked into that second-place spot, breathing down the necks of Mayhem Wreck+. They are playing with the kind of swagger that only a four-game heater provides, and if they keep putting up double-digit tallies, the rest of the league might want to start investing in some extra defensive padding.
