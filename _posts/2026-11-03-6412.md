# Hitmen Silver Strike Fast in High-Octane 8-5 Win Over Havok

If you like your hockey served with a side of absolute bedlam and a dash of defensive desperation, Monday night’s tilt at the rink was exactly what the doctor ordered. In an offensive explosion that left the scoreboard operator sweating, the Hitmen Silver clawed their way past Havok in an 8-5 thriller that reminded everyone why we love this local game.

The first period was a frantic, back-and-forth affair that set the tone for the evening. Calvin Baker opened the account for the Hitmen Silver, but Havok proved they weren't interested in being wallflowers at their own dance. A late-period rally saw Zack Wade, Dominic Najjar, and Robert Verity rattle off three consecutive goals to give Havok a promising edge. However, Nicklas Clark’s late-period response for the Hitmen acted like a splash of cold water on the Havok bench, cutting the lead and signaling that the Hitmen had plenty of firepower left in the tank.

The momentum turned decisively in the second period as the Hitmen Silver began to resemble a well-oiled machine. While the referees were busy keeping the peace—handing out a handful of roughing and slashing penalties as tempers flared—Joshua Moyer and Nicklas Clark took control of the ice. Moyer, who had a night that will surely be the talk of the locker room, put on a masterclass in playmaking. He tallied three assists in the middle frame alone, fueling a three-goal surge that flipped a deficit into a commanding Hitmen advantage. 

By the time the third period rolled around, the Hitmen were firing on all cylinders. Lukas Vodicka joined the party with a pair of goals, further extending the lead and effectively putting the game out of reach for Havok. While Havok showed some late-game gumption with goals from Michael Bergstrom and Matthew Rudick, the deficit proved too steep to climb against a Hitmen squad that had truly found its groove.

"We just stopped watching the puck and started chasing it," said one frustrated Havok bench warmer between periods. Meanwhile, over on the Hitmen side, the mood was electric. "When we move the puck like that, we’re tougher to stop than a Zamboni on a mission," joked a Hitmen defender while icing his knuckles.

The win is a massive confidence booster for the Hitmen Silver (now 5-2-1), helping them solidify their spot at third in the standings and snapping a two-game skid. For Havok, the loss is a tough pill to swallow, especially given their offensive output. However, in a league where the parity is razor-thin, they’ll look to shake off the defensive lapses and build on the three-goal burst they showed early on. 

It was 13 goals, a whole lot of heart, and enough action to keep the local crowd buzzing until the final horn. If this is how these two teams play when they’re just getting started, I can’t wait to see what happens the next time they lace ‘em up.
