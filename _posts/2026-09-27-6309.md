# Valkyries Soar Past Mother Puckers in 6-2 Victory

It’s often said that in hockey, the only thing harder than scoring is stopping the guy—or in this case, the gal—who’s already found their rhythm. The Mother Puckers learned that the hard way on Sunday morning, falling 6-2 to a Valkyries squad that looked like they’d finally had their morning coffee and decided to take the game over.

The Valkyries entered the rink holding two draws in two games, clearly hungry to turn those points into a "W." They didn't waste much time signaling their intent. Taylor Skorupski opened the account at the 5:21 mark of the first period, and while the Mother Puckers tried to stabilize the ship, they were immediately forced to fend off a power play after Angelika Copp was whistled for hooking. 

While the Mother Puckers managed to keep the damage minimal during that early shorthanded stretch, the momentum stayed firmly in the Valkyries' corner. By the time the second period rolled around, the Valkyries' skaters were moving with the kind of cohesion that usually suggests they’ve been spending extra time on the whiteboard. The Valkyries' number 45 became a one-person wrecking crew, burying a goal at 12:47 and another at 6:57. 

The Mother Puckers showed a flash of life in the second when Suzanne Hawkins capitalized on a solo effort to find the back of the net at 14:25, but it was a lonely spark in a game otherwise dominated by the visitors' relentless forecheck.

If the second period was about control, the third was about pure attrition. Skorupski picked up her second goal of the day just over three minutes into the final frame, and while the Valkyries took a roughing penalty shortly thereafter, they didn't slow down. Number 31 rubbed salt in the wound with an unassisted marker at the 3:45 mark to stretch the lead further. 

The Mother Puckers did manage to claw one back midway through the third, with an assist from Abigail Nisbet setting up an unassigned skater, but the Valkyries responded with the kind of defensive resolve that turns a close game into a laugher. Skorupski completed her hat trick at 8:26, effectively slamming the door shut on any hopes of a late-morning comeback.

"We just stopped chasing the game and started playing it," said a Valkyries bench staffer after the final whistle, likely referencing the fact that they simply outworked their counterparts in every zone.

For the Mother Puckers, it’s back to the drawing board as they remain winless in league play. Meanwhile, the Valkyries secure their first victory of the season, moving to 1-0-2. They finally found the back of the net with authority, and for a team that has been flirting with the win column all season, this performance should provide a healthy dose of confidence as they look toward their next tilt.
