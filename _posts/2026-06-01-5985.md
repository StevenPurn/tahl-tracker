# Puckheads Howl Over the Pack in Sunday Night Barn Burner

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
*May 31, 2026*

The ice was crisp, the tension was high, and the smell of fresh Zamboni fumes was in the air as the One Man Wolfpack and the Puckheads squared off at 7:15 PM. If you were looking for a casual stroll on the ice, you were at the wrong rink—this was a gritty, high-octane affair that had the boards rattling from the first whistle to the final buzzer. 

In the end, it was the Puckheads who walked away with the bragging rights, handing the Wolfpack a 4-1 loss in a game that felt much closer than the final tally suggests.

### Period 1: Discipline Derailed
The Wolfpack came out of the gate looking to break their recent funk, but discipline became an unwanted guest in their dressing room early on. James Whitacre, usually the engine of the Pack, found himself in hot water with a pair of trips to the sin bin—first for delay of game, then a roughing minor that put the team on their heels. 

The Puckheads smelled blood in the water. At 7:52, Michael Doyle finally capitalized on the chaos, burying a beauty off a crisp feed from Gregory Douglas to break the seal. The Wolfpack spent most of the frame playing defense, unable to establish any meaningful rhythm.

### Period 2: Grinding It Out
The middle frame was a parade to the penalty box, with both teams trading shoves and slashes. Between the Puckheads’ roughing and slashing minors and a tripping call on John Digges, it felt like the game was being played on a power-play merry-go-round.

Despite the back-and-forth, the Puckheads showed better cohesion. At 4:37, the Doyle-Douglas connection struck again. This time, they swapped roles, with Gregory Douglas sliding one past the Wolfpack keeper to extend the lead. The Wolfpack’s goaltender stood on his head to keep the score from ballooning, but the Puckheads were relentless with their forecheck, consistently generating "traffic in front of the net" that made every save a scramble.

### Period 3: Closing the Curtains
The third period was all about the Puckheads putting their foot on the gas. James Rozell padded the lead at 14:01, cleaning up a gorgeous setup from William Hofmeister and Cameron Sartin. 

Down by three, the Wolfpack finally showed some bite. With the clock winding down, Devin Dunn managed to snap the shutout bid, finding the twine off a feed from Whitacre. It was a highlight-reel play—Dunn went "top shelf where mom keeps the cookies"—but it was ultimately too little, too late. Douglas hammered the final nail in the coffin at 1:31 with his second goal of the night, sealing the 4-1 victory.

### Looking Ahead
For the Puckheads, this win is a massive momentum builder. They looked sharp, clinical, and disciplined when it mattered most. Their ability to turn transitional play into high-danger scoring chances is going to make them a team to watch as the standings begin to take shape.

For the One Man Wolfpack, the "L" column is starting to feel heavy. They’ve got the talent—Whitacre and Dunn showed sparks of brilliance—but they need to curb the penalty minutes and tighten up the neutral zone to stop the bleeding. 

The boys are back at it soon, and in this league, a win can turn a season around faster than a breakaway on an empty net. Stay tuned, puck fans—the season is just heating up!
