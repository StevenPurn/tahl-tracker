# Mayhem Wreck+ Steamroll Cold Ones to Keep Unbeaten Streak Alive

If the Mayhem Wreck+ were looking for a way to stay hot, they certainly found it on Wednesday night. Coming into the matchup with a pristine 5-0-1 record, the Wreck+ absolutely dismantled the Kraken Cold Ones in a 5-0 shutout, proving that their spot near the top of the standings is no fluke.

The game started with a literal bang early in the first period. Just 2:23 into the opening frame, Jeff Orr buried a feed from Ethan Hilton to set the tone for the night. From that point on, it felt as though the Wreck+ were playing a game of keep-away, consistently keeping the puck in the offensive zone and suffocating the Cold Ones’ breakout attempts.

While the first period was a measured affair, the Wreck+ really started to pull away as the clock wound down. Following a gritty middle frame that featured a tripping penalty by Kraken’s Chase Stubblefield—which the Mayhem defense managed to neutralize with ease—the game turned into an absolute clinic in the third.

Ethan Hilton struck again at 13:11 of the third, assisted by Corey Phillips, to push the lead to 2-0. From there, the dam truly broke. Michael McLaughlin, fresh off his goal in the previous outing, doubled his pleasure with two massive tallies. His first came with just 35.7 seconds remaining on the clock (an absolute heartbreaker for the Kraken bench), and he followed it up with an unassisted beauty at the 7:32 mark of the third.

"We just tried to keep our feet moving," said an exhausted but grinning McLaughlin after the buzzer. "When you've got guys like Gregory Boyer feeding you pucks, you just have to find the open ice and hope for the best. Tonight, the ice was pretty friendly."

Adding to the Cold Ones' misery, Ethan Hilton completed his monster night by sniping his second goal of the game at 8:39 of the third, capitalizing on a power play opportunity after a Ryan Martinez tripping infraction. Stephen Jeffery and Gregory Boyer picked up the apples on the play, with Boyer’s assist extending his impressive point streak to two games.

The Kraken Cold Ones, who entered the night looking to find some rhythm after a string of tough losses, just couldn't solve the Mayhem defensive shell. Despite some desperate hustle from the Kraken skaters, the Wreck+ goaltending stayed disciplined, refusing to give up even a sniff of a rebound.

With this commanding 5-0 victory, the Mayhem Wreck+ improve to 6-0-1, cementing their position as the team to beat in the local league. For the Kraken, it’s back to the drawing board as they look to snap their slide and find the back of the net in their next outing. 

As for the Wreck+? They aren't just wrecking opponents; they’re turning the league standings into their personal playground. If they keep playing with this kind of synergy, the rest of the pack is going to have a long winter ahead of them.
