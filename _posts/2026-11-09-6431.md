# Storer’s Hat Trick Leads Bad Bees Past Chix with Stix

The Bad Bees were buzzing with absolute intensity on Friday night, stinging the Chix with Stix in a 4-2 victory that proved just how dangerous this team can be when they’re clicking. At the center of the chaos was Liliena Storer, who turned in a performance for the highlight reels, netting a brilliant hat trick to keep the Bees firmly in the hunt near the top of the standings.

From the drop of the puck, the Bees looked like a team on a mission to get back into a rhythm. Storer set the tone early, opening the scoring just 4:11 into the first period with an unassisted laser that caught the Chix with Stix defense flat-footed. Despite an early power play opportunity for the Bees following a trip by Hannah Atkins, the Chix managed to hang tough, heading into the intermission trailing by just the lone marker.

The middle frame was where things got gritty. Hannah Atkins redeemed her earlier penalty by finding the back of the net two minutes into the second period to tie it up, momentarily stalling the Bees' momentum. However, Storer wasn’t about to let the game slip away. After a brief parade to the penalty box interrupted the flow of play—including an interference call on Lauren A Mincey—Storer struck again at the 6:24 mark to restore the Bees' lead. 

"Liliena was hunting goals like they were going out of style," said one Bees teammate after the final whistle. "Every time the puck touched her stick, you just knew something special was about to happen."

Entering the final period with a slim 2-1 lead, the Bees decided to turn up the heat. Grace Wolff, looking to extend her point streak to an impressive five games, extended the lead with a clutch goal at the 12:58 mark. Just a few minutes later, Storer completed the hat trick, putting the game out of reach for the Chix.

The Chix with Stix didn't fold, though. Jessica Hopwood managed to snap one past the Bees' goaltender with an assist from Heather Cozad at the 8:39 mark, bringing the score to 4-2 and injecting a little late-game drama into the building. But the Bees' defensive corps locked down the remaining minutes to secure the win.

For the Bad Bees, the win is a massive confidence booster as they look to distance themselves from the middle of the pack, moving to a solid 5-2-1 record. The Chix with Stix, meanwhile, will be looking to head back to the drawing board after a tough night, searching for a way to break their current cold streak. 

With Storer playing at this level, the Bad Bees are proving they aren't just here to participate—they're here to run the hive.
