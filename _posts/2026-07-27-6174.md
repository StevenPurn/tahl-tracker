# Jackalopes Break the Ice: A 5-1 Masterclass Over the Tulsa Leafs

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: July 26, 2026**

There’s an old hockey adage that says you’re never as good as your last win, but for the Jackalopes, tonight felt like a fresh start to a season that was starting to get a little chilly. Coming into tonight’s tilt at the bottom of the heap, the 'Lopes finally let the hounds off the leash, dismantling the Tulsa Leafs 5-1 in a performance that had the home crowd shaking the glass.

For the Leafs, the misery continues—that’s now six straight losses—but for the Jackalopes, this was the shot of adrenaline they desperately needed.

### Period 1: Setting the Tone
The Jackalopes didn’t just wake up; they arrived at the rink ready to put in the work. At the 10:03 mark, the deadlock was broken when **James Whitacre** found the back of the net, courtesy of a slick setup from **Bradley Fidler**. 

The Jacks kept their boots on the pedal, creating plenty of traffic in front of the Leafs' crease. With just over two minutes left in the frame, the pressure paid off. After SCOTT MILLER headed to the sin bin for a trip, **ROBERT VERITY** capitalized on the advantage, burying a feed from **Kyle Foster** to send the 'Lopes into the locker room with a commanding 2-0 lead.

### Period 2: The Whitacre Show
If the first period was a statement, the second was a manifesto. The Jackalopes turned the middle frame into a clinic on offensive zone possession. **Kyle Foster** kept his hot streak alive, lighting the lamp at 10:30 with **CHRISTOPHER KING** picking up the helper.

But the star of the night was undoubtedly James Whitacre. He wasn’t satisfied with his opening goal; he decided he wanted the hat trick. At 5:39, he cleaned up a rebound off a Tyler Wick blast, and just three minutes later, he iced his masterpiece by redirecting a beautiful pass from Verity. By the time the buzzer sounded on the second period, the 'Lopes were up 5-0, and the Tulsa Leafs looked like they were skating through quicksand.

### Period 3: A Bitter Shutout Bid
The third period was largely a defensive lockdown for the Jackalopes, who were clearly looking to preserve the goose egg for their netminder. They managed to kill off a late minor penalty taken by Toby Temple at 5:16, keeping the crease clean until the dying moments of the game.

With just 18.6 seconds remaining, the Leafs finally broke the seal. **Jason Fennell** managed to solve the Jackalopes' defense, assisted by Tanner Bemies and Dillon King, to play spoiler on the shutout. It was a mere consolation prize, though, as the damage was already done.

### The Aftermath: What it Means
The Jackalopes (2-4-2) finally climb off the mat, leapfrogging the Knights in the standings with this two-point haul. They looked hungry, they looked organized, and most importantly, they looked like a team that remembered how to finish. 

As for the Tulsa Leafs (1-6-2), they remain buried at the basement of the standings. Tonight was a rough outing where the puck seemed to bounce against them at every turn. They’ll have to head back to the drawing board and find a way to stop the bleeding before their season slips out of reach.

For tonight, though, the Jackalopes fans can head home with a smile—the ‘Lopes are officially back in the hunt. See you at the next barn burner!
