# Chix with Stix Catch Fire Early to Topple Power Puck Girls

If you blinked during the first period on Friday night, you missed the entire game. The Chix with Stix came out at the 7:15 puck drop like they’d swapped their skate blades for rocket boosters, effectively burying the Power Puck Girls under an avalanche of offense before the Zamboni driver had even finished his first coffee. By the time the final buzzer echoed through the rink, the Chix had skated away with a commanding 5-2 victory.

The narrative of the night was written in a frantic, six-minute span in the first period that saw the Chix with Stix hang four unanswered goals on the board. Angela Willoughby opened the floodgates at the 10:03 mark, setting the tone with a slick helper from Nicole Baker. Not seventeen seconds later, Catherine Pearsall doubled the lead, leaving the Power Puck Girls’ defense looking like they were trying to catch smoke with a butterfly net.

Hannah Atkins proved to be the wrecking ball of the evening. She notched a pair of goals—one assisted by Amanda Gonya and an unassisted beauty just 47 seconds later—to put the Chix firmly in the driver’s seat. 

"We decided that passing the puck was a pretty good strategy tonight," joked Chix with Stix head coach 'Steady' Sarah Miller after the game. "I told them if they didn't start skating fast, I was going to make them practice in parkas. Apparently, they prefer playing hockey to sweating in winter gear."

The dominance continued into the second period when Ashley Ivanoff added the fifth nail in the coffin at the 3:11 mark, courtesy of a textbook setup from Pearsall. At 5-0, it looked like a total shutout was in the cards, but the Power Puck Girls refused to pack it in. To their credit, they battled through the frustration of a scoreless first two periods and finally found some daylight in the third.

The Power Puck offense finally sparked midway through the final frame. An unassisted tally at 14:47 broke the goose egg, followed by a Whitney Beeler goal at 9:29—assisted by Michelle Karjadi—that gave the PPG faithful something to cheer about. While the late surge provided a glimmer of momentum, the deficit proved too steep to overcome.

For the Chix with Stix, this 5-2 win is a massive breath of fresh air. Entering the night mired in a slump, they looked like a completely rejuvenated squad, utilizing crisp passing and high-energy forechecking to snap their recent skid. They now climb to 2-4-1 on the season, drawing level in the standings with the Power Puck Girls, who fall to 1-5-1. With the standings tightening up in the middle of the pack, Friday’s performance is exactly the kind of "W" that could get the Chix back on track for the remainder of the regular season.
