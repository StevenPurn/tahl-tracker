# Ice Cold Dominance: Gold as Ice Melt the Eagles in 7-3 Rout

**By: Your Hometown Rink-Side Reporter**
**Date: May 31, 2026**

If you wanted to see a masterclass in puck possession, you should’ve been at the barn tonight. The **Gold as Ice** squad rolled into the rink with a singular mission, and by the time the final horn sounded, they had effectively turned the **Lite Beer Eagles** into a puddle. With a dominant 7-3 victory, Gold as Ice proved that their early-season momentum is no fluke.

### First Period: A Cold Front Hits the Rink
Gold as Ice came out skating like they had rockets strapped to their boots. They didn’t just knock on the door; they kicked it off the hinges. **Daniel Maxwell** opened the floodgates at 13:54, unassisted, setting a tone that the Eagles simply couldn't match.

Just 33 seconds later, **MARK DESIMONE**—riding a hot hand after his performance in the season opener—doubled the lead off a sweet feed from **DANIEL HANNING**. The Eagles were reeling, and things went from bad to worse late in the period when **ADEN INGRAM** buried a beauty, extending his personal point streak to two games. Heading into the intermission, it was 3-0, and the Gold as Ice defense was looking like an impenetrable fortress.

### Second Period: Hanning’s Heavy Lifting
The second frame was all about control. The Eagles tried to muck it up, creating some "traffic in front of the net," but the Gold as Ice goaltending stood tall, absorbing everything thrown their way. With time ticking down to just three seconds remaining, **DANIEL HANNING** added insult to injury, rifling one home to make it 4-0. 

Just minutes into the start of the period, **DESIMONE** struck again, collecting his second of the night thanks to a helper from the tireless **Taylor Chinowth**. At 5-0, it looked like a runaway freight train.

### Third Period: The Eagles Attempt a Late Surge
Hockey is a funny game—the momentum can swing like a pendulum in a hurricane. To their credit, the Lite Beer Eagles refused to roll over. **Jesse Stockley** finally solved the Gold as Ice netminder at 10:19, with **Jeff Horvath** picking up his second point in as many games. 

**Adam Strange** added another shortly after, and **Jakob Morehouse** followed suit at 14:45, suddenly making it a 5-3 nail-biter. The crowd was buzzing, sensing a potential comeback for the ages. But Gold as Ice had the answer. **Taylor Chinowth** hammered the final nail in the coffin with a clutch goal at the 45-second mark, and **DANIEL HANNING** iced the cake with his second goal of the night at 6:01.

### The Bottom Line
When the dust settled, Gold as Ice walked away with a resounding 7-3 win. For the Eagles, it’s back to the drawing board to tighten up the defensive zone. For Gold as Ice, this result cements their spot in the upper echelon of the standings. They’ve got the firepower, they’ve got the chemistry, and right now, they’re looking like the team to beat in this division.

As we look at the standings, Gold as Ice is firmly entrenched in the lead pack. With the offense firing on all cylinders—shoutout to **DANIEL HANNING** for a monster multi-point night—the rest of the league has officially been put on notice.
