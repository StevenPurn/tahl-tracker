# Berzerkers Run Wild in 11-3 Rout of Ice Breakers Plus

If the Ice Breakers Plus were looking for a rebound performance to kickstart their season, they picked the wrong night to face the Berzerkers. In a display of offensive efficiency that bordered on the clinical, the Berzerkers dismantled the league’s 13th-ranked squad 11-3 on Saturday night, cementing their place near the top of the standings.

From the drop of the puck, the statistical narrative was clear: the Berzerkers were playing a different game entirely. Zac Hughes was the architect of the evening, putting on a masterclass of individual production. Hughes didn’t just record a hat trick; he finished with four goals and an assist, effectively solving the Ice Breakers’ defensive zone coverage before the first period even reached its midway point. 

Hughes opened the scoring just 2:12 in, followed by a second marker with less than a minute left in the opening frame. When Cody Stephens added another just seconds later, the Ice Breakers found themselves in a 3-0 hole early. Though RYAN MAJOR managed to pull one back for the Breakers following a feed from the streaking TYLER WALLER, it proved to be a solitary flicker of hope.

"When you look at the shot distribution, we were just chasing ghosts all night," said one visibly exhausted Ice Breakers bench staffer, who spent much of the game rearranging lines that simply couldn't find an answer for the Berzerkers' transition game.

The second period saw the Berzerkers turn the dial from "aggressive" to "relentless." While GLENN PROUSE managed an unassisted tally to open the second, the Ice Breakers' brief momentum was swallowed whole by a four-goal barrage from the Berzerkers. Hughes added two more to his tally in the frame, while LARRY COCKRELL and Cody Stephens joined the party, pushing the lead to an insurmountable margin by the second intermission.

The third period served as a victory lap for the Berzerkers. SAGE BLUBAUGH—who quietly dominated the peripheral stats with three assists on the night—found the back of the net himself, followed by a quick brace from Stephen Jeffery. NIKOLAS KEMMLER capped the scoring for the visitors with less than five minutes remaining, keeping his individual point streak alive in the process.

While the scoreboard was lopsided, the final minutes provided a moment of friction. A double-minor for unsportsmanlike conduct involving JERRY LARUE and Cody Stephens hinted at some lingering frustration from the Ice Breakers. Larue would eventually net a consolation goal with less than two minutes remaining, set up by DANIEL REYES, but the damage had long since been done.

Statistically, the Berzerkers are now firing on all cylinders. Entering the night with momentum, they proved that their current 2-0-1 standing is no fluke. For the Ice Breakers Plus, sitting at 0-2-1, the data suggests a need for a drastic defensive recalibration. As for the Berzerkers, they leave the rink with a statement win, reminding the rest of the league that when their top lines are clicking, they aren't just winning games—they’re rewriting the ledger.
