# Riders Ride the Wave, Shut Out Tuskens in Tuesday Night Tilt

**By: "Slap-Shot" Sammy, Beat Reporter**
*July 27, 2026 | The Rink*

If you were looking for a high-octane offensive explosion, you might have been looking at the wrong rink tonight. But if you’re a fan of old-school, gritty, "keep the puck out of the kitchen" hockey, then the Rohan Riders and the Tulsa Tuskens provided exactly the brand of trench warfare that leaves the ice scarred and the fans buzzing.

In a clash between two squads desperate to shake off the rust of long losing streaks, it was the Riders who finally found the win column, blanking the Tuskens 2-0 in a contest that felt more like a chess match played with sledgehammers.

### First Period: The Calm Before the Storm
The opening frame saw both teams skating with the intensity of a pack of wolves fighting for the last scrap of steak. The Tuskens were looking to establish a forecheck, but the Riders’ defensive shell held firm. The only real drama of the period came at the 4:48 mark when Rohan’s Glenn Prouse decided to play a little too rough for the referee’s liking, heading to the sin bin for a two-minute roughing minor. Tulsa couldn’t capitalize on the power play, though—the Riders’ penalty kill was tighter than a goalie’s glove hand on a breakaway.

### Second Period: The Riders Break the Silence
The middle frame is where the Riders finally pulled the trigger. After a penalty to Tulsa’s Cody Springfield for hooking, the Riders smelled blood in the water. 

At 5:46, the deadlock was finally broken. Glenn Prouse, fresh off his stint in the box earlier, redeemed himself by burying a beauty off a crisp feed from Aivar Kant. Just over a minute later, at 6:58, the Riders doubled down. Samuel Northup, riding a hot streak, found the back of the net off a sweet dish from Mitchel Pierce and Nathan Lesnick. That 2-0 cushion proved to be the magic number, as the Riders locked the door and threw away the key for the remainder of the game.

### Third Period: Disciplined D and Discipline Issues
The final frame turned into a bit of a chaotic parade to the penalty box. As the Tuskens pushed harder to solve the Riders' defense, frustration began to boil over. Matthew Springfield took a slashing minor, followed by a risky checking-from-behind call on Michael Smith and another roughing call on Cody Springfield. 

The Tuskens were essentially playing a man down for most of the period, making it impossible to mount a comeback. Even with Aivar Kant taking a slashing penalty for the Riders at the 9:43 mark, the Tuskens just couldn't find the "top shelf where mom keeps the cookies." The Riders' goaltending remained unflappable, turning away every attempt and securing the clean sheet.

### The Big Picture
For the Rohan Riders, this 2-0 victory is a massive exhale. Moving to 2-6-0, they finally climb off the bottom rung of the standings, leapfrogging the struggling pack to sit at #6. It’s a "W" that builds confidence for a squad that’s been looking for a spark all season.

As for the Tulsa Tuskens, sitting at 0-7-0, it’s back to the drawing board. Tonight showed they have the grit, but they need to find a way to navigate traffic in front of the net and turn that zone time into twine-ticklers. 

The breeze from the skates tonight was chilly, but for the Rohan Riders, the future is finally starting to look a little warmer. We’ll see if they can carry this momentum into their next outing, but for tonight, the Riders head home with two points in their pocket and a shut-out to brag about at the pub.
