# A Nine-Goal Night: Berzerkers Unleash Absolute Chaos on the Reapers

**By: Your Hometown Rink-Side Reporter**
**Date: May 31, 2026**

If you were looking for a quiet Friday night at the rink, you definitely walked into the wrong barn. Under the Friday night lights of May 31st, the Berzerkers decided that the scoreboard was merely a suggestion, turning tonight’s tilt into a 9-2 demolition of the Reapers. 

The Reapers came in desperate to shake off an 0-1 start, but instead, they ran headlong into a buzzsaw. 

### First Period: The Hughes Hat-Trick Extravaganza
The opening frame belonged to one man: Zac Hughes. The man was playing like he had magnets in his stick blade. He opened the scoring at the 2:21 mark off a crisp feed from Evert Wilmeth, setting the tone early. 

With just 39.7 seconds left in the period, Hughes was back at it, burying a rebound off a scramble involving Edward Stern and LC III Sinor. But he wasn’t done. Nestled between those, at the 9:57 mark, Hughes completed a lightning-fast natural hat trick, again linking up with Wilmeth. The Berzerkers headed to the locker room with a 3-0 lead and a Reapers squad looking for answers that weren't on the ice.

### Second Period: The Floodgates Burst Open
The middle frame turned into an absolute track meet. The Reapers finally found a heartbeat when Corey Phillips tickled the twine at 12:06, assisted by the streaking Kelly Rundle. But the Berzerkers’ response was swift and brutal.

Stephen Jeffery restored the cushion shortly after, followed by a gritty marker from Cody Stephens. The Reapers were reeling, and the Berzerkers smelled blood in the water. We saw a parade of offense: Alan Dyke went coast-to-coast for an unassisted beauty, followed by Kevin Keller and Evert Wilmeth tacking on tallies that left the Reapers’ netminder shaking his head. 

Despite a minor hiccup—a tripping penalty to Tyler Holmes—the Berzerkers remained in the driver’s seat. By the time the horn sounded for the second intermission, the scoreboard read 8-1. It was an offensive clinic, moving the puck with the kind of tic-tac-toe precision that makes a hockey purist weep with joy.

### Third Period: Closing the Books
The third period saw the Berzerkers ease their foot off the gas, but Zac Hughes managed to find the back of the net one last time at 14:15, marking a stellar four-goal performance on the evening. 

The Reapers did manage a consolation prize with a gritty unassisted goal from Matt Burkhart in the dying seconds (36.9), but it was a case of "too little, too late." The final score of 9-2 tells the story: this was a masterclass in puck possession and finishing.

### The Aftermath: What it Means
For the **Berzerkers**, this win is a massive statement. After a tough loss recently, they proved that their offensive engine is firing on all cylinders. They’ve announced themselves as a team that can turn any game into a track meet if you give them an inch of open ice.

For the **Reapers**, it’s a long bus ride home. Sitting at 0-2, they are currently languishing near the bottom of the table. They’ll need to tighten up the neutral zone coverage and find some defensive structure if they want to climb out of the basement and start chasing the likes of *Gold as Ice* and *The Outsiders*.

The breeze from the skates has settled, the ice is being resurfaced, and the Berzerkers are heading home feeling like kings of the rink. That’s the beautiful, chaotic game we love. See you at the next one!
