# Garbage Time? Not Tonight: Hot Garbage Edges Berzerkers in 5-4 Thriller

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**

**July 21, 2026** — If you weren’t at the rink late last night, you missed a prime-time barn burner that reminded us exactly why we pack the stands for these 10:15 PM puck drops. In a clash that had more lead changes than a malfunctioning scoreboard, **Hot Garbage** proved they aren’t just a catchy name—they’re a legitimate problem for the upper echelon of the standings, knocking off the fourth-place **Berzerkers** in a 5-4 classic.

### First Period: Hot Garbage Comes Out Swinging
The ice was barely frozen before Hot Garbage started digging in. John Bewley drew first blood at the 12:14 mark, cleaning up the trash in front of the net with a beauty of a setup from Nicholas Brewer and Jordan Hawkins Baker. 

Brewer, clearly feeling his oats, decided he didn't need the assist column this time, burying an unassisted heater with less than a minute left in the frame to double the lead. The Berzerkers finally answered back when Brandon Patocka snapped a quiet start with an unassisted snipe, but Joe Baker restored the two-goal cushion just before the buzzer. At 3-1, the momentum was painted in Garbage colors.

### Second Period: The Power Play Pivot
The Berzerkers, known for their tenacity, didn’t roll over. Cody Stephens clawed one back early in the second, breathing life into the Berzerker bench. 

However, discipline proved to be the Berzerkers’ undoing. When Hayden Wheeler headed to the sin bin for a trip at 1:41, Hot Garbage wasted absolutely zero time. Eight seconds later, Brett Mecham hammered home a power-play goal that sent the crowd into a frenzy. It was the kind of clinical finish that makes you realize why these guys are climbing the standings.

### Third Period: The Berzerkers’ Last Stand
Down 4-2 heading into the final frame, the Berzerkers turned the intensity up to eleven. Justin Demoss ignited the rally at the 10:07 mark, taking a tidy feed from Edward Stern and Stephen Jeffery to cut the lead to one. 

But every time the Berzerkers sniffed an equalizer, Hot Garbage slammed the door. Jordan Hawkins Baker—who had been a menace all night—picked up a dish from Brewer and found the twine at 13:28 to make it 5-4. Stern answered for the Berzerkers late, but time ran out on their comeback bid, leaving them inches short of the equalizer.

### The Aftermath
It was a rough night for the Berzerkers, who drop to a 5-2-1 record and will have to look in the mirror after failing to neutralize a potent Garbage attack. For Hot Garbage, this win is a massive statement. Climbing to 5-2-0, they’ve now cemented themselves as the team no one wants to see on the schedule. 

Entering the night, the Berzerkers were the fourth-seed favorites, but tonight, the standings just got a whole lot tighter. If Hot Garbage keeps playing with this kind of grit and "traffic in front" tenacity, the top three teams better keep checking their rearview mirrors.

**Final Score: Hot Garbage 5, Berzerkers 4.**
