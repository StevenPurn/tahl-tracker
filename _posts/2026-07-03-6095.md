# Deadlock in the Rink: Mayhem Wreck+ and Berzerkers Skate to a Gritty Draw

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: July 2, 2026**

If you weren't at the barn tonight, you missed a classic chess match played on frozen water. In a battle of two teams looking to make a statement in the upper echelon of the standings, the Mayhem Wreck+ and the Berzerkers played a game so tight, you could’ve squeezed a piece of coal into a diamond. When the final buzzer sounded on this 1-1 thriller, the only thing thicker than the tension was the traffic in front of the crease.

### Period 1: The Wrecking Crew Strikes First
The ice was barely dry before the Mayhem Wreck+ signaled their intentions. At the 8:53 mark of the opening frame, the puck found the stick of **Justin DeMoss**, who didn’t hesitate to bury it past the Berzerkers’ netminder. With a crafty assist from **Forrest Beadle**, the Wreck+ drew first blood, sending the hometown faithful into a frenzy. It was a textbook play: simple, efficient, and lethal. The Berzerkers spent the rest of the period trying to find their rhythm, but the Wreck+ defense was playing like a locked gate, forcing the Berserkers to fire from the perimeter.

### Period 2: The Wilmeth-Hughes Connection
Whatever the Berzerkers coaching staff said in the locker room between periods, it worked. The second period saw a surge in energy from the visitors, who began grinding down the Wreck+ defense with relentless forechecking. 

At 7:55, the dam finally broke. **EVERT WILMETH**, riding high on a four-game point streak, orchestrated a beautiful setup that allowed **Zac Hughes** to light the lamp. Hughes, extending his goal streak to three games, snapped a wrister that caught the twine, effectively nullifying the Mayhem’s lead. Both goalies stood tall for the remainder of the period, turning away quality chances and keeping the score locked at 1-1 heading into the final frame.

### Shootout: High-Stakes Heroics
Regulation and overtime couldn't separate these two squads, leading us to the ultimate test of nerves: the shootout. 

The pressure was palpable. **Justin DeMoss** stepped up for Mayhem Wreck+, cool as a cucumber, and sent a laser beam home to put the pressure squarely on the Berzerkers’ shoulders. But don't count out a team named the "Berzerkers." **Cody Stephens** answered the bell immediately, flashing some slick mitts to tuck his own tally into the net. Ultimately, with both sides trading blows in the skills competition, the deadlock held firm.

Both teams walk away with a point, but tonight felt like a victory for the fans who love the grind. It was a real barn burner, a tactical masterpiece that leaves us wondering—if these two meet again, who will finally break the ice?
