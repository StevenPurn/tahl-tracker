# High-Octane Fireworks: Hitmen Silver Outgun Honey Badgers in 9-6 Thriller

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: July 19, 2026**

If you were looking for a defensive masterclass, you wandered into the wrong barn on Sunday night. But if you were looking for a pure, unadulterated, goal-scoring circus? Then you got your money’s worth and then some. The Hitmen Silver flexed their offensive muscles to dismantle the Honey Badgers 9-6 in a game that felt less like a hockey match and more like a pinball tournament on ice.

### First Period: A Tit-for-Tat Start
The Hitmen, sitting comfortably in third place, came out firing, with Josh Moyer opening the floodgates at 11:41. But the Badgers—scrappy as ever—weren't going to roll over. Matthan Hillberg knotted it up before Nicklas Clark and Marshall Nunneley traded blows. 

However, the real story of the first frame was the "Keegan Guthrie Show." With the period winding down, Guthrie went on a personal tear, finding the back of the net twice in quick succession to give the Hitmen a cushion. By the time the horn sounded for the intermission, the ice was still smoking, and the Hitmen held a 4-2 lead.

### Second Period: The Hitmen Pull Away
The Badgers tried to find their rhythm, but the Hitmen’s top-line chemistry proved to be the difference-maker. Christopher Fleming’s hooking penalty at 11:17 put the Badgers in a hole, and the Hitmen made them pay. Nicklas Clark and Bobby Pickett turned the screws, pushing the Hitmen’s lead to 6-2. 

Marshall Nunneley grabbed his second of the night for the Badgers, but it was just a drop in the bucket. Before the second intermission, Keegan Guthrie completed a natural-style hat trick, burying his third of the night off feeds from Moyer and Clark. That goal effectively took the wind out of the Badgers’ sails, pushing the score to 7-3 heading into the final frame.

### Third Period: The Frantic Finish
If you think the third period was a quiet affair, you haven't been paying attention to this league. With tensions simmering, the whistles started blowing—Brooks Martin sat for roughing, and Matthan Hillberg spent significant time in the box for the Badgers. 

The Badgers launched a desperate comeback bid. Cole Rush, Joey Pachuta, and Drew Sleezer all lit the lamp in the third, showing the grit that defines this team. But every time the Badgers clawed closer, the Hitmen had an answer. Josh Moyer and Bobby Pickett slammed the door shut with late goals, ensuring the Hitmen walked away with the two points.

### The Breakdown
**Final Score: Hitmen Silver 9, Honey Badgers 6**

This game was a testament to the "traffic in front of the net" strategy. The Hitmen exploited the high-slot relentlessly, with Josh Moyer and Nicklas Clark putting on an absolute clinic in playmaking. For the Honey Badgers, the loss is a tough pill to swallow, but they proved they can find the back of the net against anyone. They just need to tighten the screws in the defensive zone if they want to climb out of the basement.

**What it means for the standings:**
With this win, **Hitmen Silver** (5-2-0) continues their dominant trend, breathing down the necks of the Dirty Birds and the Killer Goats at the top of the table. They look like legitimate title contenders. The **Honey Badgers** (1-6-0) remain at the bottom, and while their offensive spirit is commendable, they’ll need to figure out how to stop the bleeding if they hope to climb back into the playoff hunt.

The puck drops again next week—if this game was any indication, you’d be wise to bring your scorecard and an extra bottle of aspirin. It’s going to be a bumpy ride!
