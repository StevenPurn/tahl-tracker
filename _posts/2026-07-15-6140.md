# Mooseknuckles Stampede Past Murder in Battle of the Unbeatens

**TULSA** – If you were looking for a defensive stalemate tonight at the rink, you definitely came to the wrong place. In a highly anticipated showdown between the league’s top two squads, the Mooseknuckles arrived with a clear message, dismantling the Tulsa Murder 7-2 and proving once and for all that they aren't just sitting at the top of the standings—they’re building a penthouse suite there.

Coming into the July 14th clash with a pristine 5-0-0 record, the Mooseknuckles didn’t just play hockey; they orchestrated a masterclass in puck possession and punishing efficiency.

### Period 1: A Lesson in Special Teams
The Murder entered this one looking to close the gap on the league leaders, but the game started on the wrong foot before anyone could even get a sweat going. Just 90 seconds in, Spencer Charles headed to the sin bin for tripping. The Mooseknuckles’ power play—a unit that moves the puck like it’s hooked to a magnet—capitalized immediately, with **Nicholas Strle** lighting the lamp off a dish from Garrett Williams. 

Tulsa never regained their footing. Another tripping call on Adam Hoffdahl opened the floodgates further, with **Garrett Williams** burying a beauty. Before the period could even wrap, **Jasen Lawwill** went coast-to-coast, and **Strle** snagged his second of the night. By the first intermission, the Mooseknuckles held a commanding 4-0 lead, leaving the Murder looking like they were trying to skate through quicksand.

### Period 2: The Murder Shows a Pulse
Credit where it’s due: the Murder came out for the second frame with a bit more grit under their fingernails. They managed to break the shutout bid at the 11:16 mark when **Tom Mortimer** found the back of the net, thanks to a crisp feed from Luke Brandon. It was a glimmer of hope, but the Mooseknuckles' defensive wall remained largely unbreached, efficiently killing off a Donny Griffin hooking penalty to keep the momentum squarely in their corner.

### Period 3: The Knuckles Put the Game on Ice
The final period was a display of pure, unadulterated dominance. **Jess Herd** decided to join the party, putting on a clinic with back-to-back goals in the span of just over a minute. 

Tulsa’s **Pete Fleming** kept his hot streak alive with a goal at the 3:01 mark, assisted by Nicholas Olson, but it was too little, too late. The final dagger came from **Strle**, who completed a gargantuan performance by sealing his hat trick and capping the 7-2 scoreline.

### The Aftermath
For the Mooseknuckles (6-0-0), this wasn't just a win—it was a statement of intent. They remain the undisputed kings of the hill, looking untouchable as they march toward the midway point of the season. 

The Murder (4-3-0) will head back to the drawing board. While they currently hold steady in the standings, tonight’s loss proves there is a significant delta between "good" and the "elite" level that the Mooseknuckles are currently operating at. The Murder have the talent to bounce back, but after tonight’s shellacking, they know exactly how steep the mountain is if they want to claim the top seed.

**Stat of the Night:** Nicholas Strle’s hat trick performance. When a guy is finding the soft spots in the coverage that easily, you’re going to have a long night at the office. 

The Mooseknuckles keep rolling. The rest of the league? They’re officially on notice.
