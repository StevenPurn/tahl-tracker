# Mayhem Wreck+ Hold Off Gritty Ice Breakers in 5-4 Thriller

If you were looking for a blowout at the rink this Saturday afternoon, you were in the wrong building. The league-leading Mayhem Wreck+ walked into the arena expecting a routine two points against the winless Ice Breakers Plus, but they left with their collective hearts in their throats after a 5-4 victory that was far closer than the standings suggested.

The Wreck+ came in riding a wave of momentum, looking every bit the first-place team early on. Jeff Orr extended his goal-scoring streak to three games just 45 seconds into the opening period, courtesy of an assist from Ivy Emerson. When John Saltsman potted his first of the day at 10:41 of the second, it looked like the script was written: another easy day at the office for the favorites.

But the Ice Breakers, currently sitting in the basement, clearly didn't get the memo that they were supposed to roll over. Trailing by two in the second period, the Breakers’ offense suddenly woke up. Tyler Waller struck at 12:53, and before the Wreck+ could catch their breath, Edward Stern leveled the score at 6:23. 

The game turned into a frantic, back-and-forth affair that would have made a defensive-minded coach pull his hair out. Saltsman regained the lead for Mayhem before the second frame concluded, but Stern was a man possessed, completing a hat trick with two more goals in the third period—a performance that certainly deserved a better result for his team.

"We kept waiting for them to pack it in," admitted one Wreck+ veteran while nursing a cold beverage after the game. "Instead, they kept crashing the net. It wasn't pretty, but it was honest work."

The difference ultimately came down to the depth of the Wreck+ lineup. Justin McKinney added his own mark to the ledger early in the third, and Spencer Hodgson provided what proved to be the game-winner at the 6:32 mark. Even when the Wreck+ went down a man following a hooking penalty by Saltsman late in the game, the Ice Breakers couldn't quite find the equalizer to force overtime.

The win keeps Mayhem Wreck+ firmly atop the standings, proving that even when they aren't playing their sharpest, they have the firepower to escape with the win. For the Ice Breakers Plus, the search for that first win of the season continues, though if they play with the same tenacity they showed today, they won't remain at the bottom of the pile for long. It was a refreshing reminder that in this league, the guys at the top have to earn every inch of ice they take.
