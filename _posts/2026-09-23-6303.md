# Gold as Ice Bronze Shines Bright in 8-5 Goal-Fest Over Yetis

If you like your hockey served with a side of high-octane offense and a sprinkle of absolute madness, then Tuesday night’s barn-burner at the rink was the place to be. Gold as Ice Bronze finally shook off the early-season rust, putting on an offensive masterclass to take down the Yetis with a final score of 8-5.

The story of the night was undoubtedly Triston Wilson, who didn't just show up to play; he showed up to put on a clinic. Wilson was a one-man wrecking crew, lighting the lamp four times and serving as the primary architect for an offense that found the back of the net early and often.

Gold as Ice Bronze set the tempo in the first period, with Wilson netting his first at 14:32. While the Yetis’ Brendan Burkman managed to keep his goal streak alive with a slick equalizer, it was short-lived. Gold as Ice Bronze responded with a flurry of activity, as Josh Hopwood, Wilson again, and Lance Levasseur rattled off three unanswered goals to close the period with a commanding 4-1 lead.

The second period saw the Yetis try to climb out of the frozen tundra. Levasseur notched his second of the night early on, but the Yetis began to chip away at the deficit. Jason Bahr, Skylar Miller, and Jered Pieschke all found the scoresheet in the middle frame, narrowing the gap and putting a genuine scare into the Bronze bench. However, every time the Yetis threatened to claw their way back into the game, Wilson was there to act as the ultimate spoiler, scoring his third of the night at the 9:18 mark of the second to keep the momentum squarely in his team’s corner.

"I think we just stopped overthinking it," said one player from the bench after the final whistle. "When Triston is feeling it like that, you don't over-coach—you just get him the puck and get out of his way!"

The third period turned into a back-and-forth battle of attrition. Mark DeSimone extended the Bronze lead, but Katherine Popoff answered right back for the Yetis to keep the hopes of a comeback flickering. Ultimately, the penalty box doors swung open a bit too often for the Yetis’ liking, and Wilson capitalized on the late-game chaos to bag his fourth goal of the night, effectively slamming the door shut on any hopes of a Yeti resurgence.

The victory marks a massive step forward for Gold as Ice Bronze, who entered the night looking to change their fortunes after a slow start to the season. For the Yetis, it’s a tough pill to swallow after a solid start to their campaign, but the offensive output proves they’ve got the firepower to stay in any scrap. 

The league standings are starting to tighten up, and if Tuesday night’s performance is any indication, Gold as Ice Bronze is officially ready to stop digging for gold and start cashing in.
