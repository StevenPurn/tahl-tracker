# Bison Stampede: Tulsa Takes Down the Berzerkers

The Berzerkers walked into the rink on Friday night looking to protect their spot near the top of the standings, but the Tulsa Bison had other plans, charging to a gritty 3-1 victory that proved they aren't afraid of the league’s heavy hitters.

It was a night where James DePriest effectively turned the ice into his own personal playground. The Bison forward set the tone early in the first period, finding the back of the net at the 14:56 mark off a crisp feed from Amanda Gonya. That opening strike seemed to rattle the Berzerkers, who spent much of the first half of the game playing catch-up against a disciplined Tulsa defensive shell.

The Berzerkers finally showed signs of life in the second period when Zac Hughes capitalized on a beautiful setup from Stephen Jeffery to knot the score at 1-1. For a moment, the Berzerkers looked poised to mount one of their patented rallies. However, the momentum shifted back to Tulsa in a hurry, thanks in large part to the Berzerkers’ own discipline issues.

Hayden Wheeler found himself in the box for the second time in the period at the 14:04 mark, and the Bison didn’t waste the opportunity. Just 50 seconds into that power play, DePriest struck again—this time unassisted—to reclaim the lead for Tulsa. If that goal was the dagger, Michael Wixted provided the insurance policy shortly after, burying a helper from Payton Brewer at the 8:15 mark to extend the lead to 3-1.

"We knew we were facing a team that had the wind in their sails," said one Bison skater after the buzzer. "But tonight, we just decided to be the ones throwing the weight around. DePriest was an absolute vacuum out there, just sucking up every loose puck and putting it home."

The third period turned into a defensive chess match. The Berzerkers tried to claw their way back, but the Bison clamped down, allowing very little clean entry into their zone. A hooking penalty against Cody Stephens late in the frame effectively neutralized the Berzerkers' last-ditch effort to spark a comeback, allowing Tulsa to coast through the final minutes.

The loss puts a temporary damper on the Berzerkers’ impressive start to the season, as they drop a crucial decision to a lower-ranked squad. Meanwhile, the Bison jump into the win column with confidence, proving that even the most feared teams in the league can be brought down to earth when they get caught in a stampede. For the Berzerkers, it’s back to the drawing board—and perhaps a quick reminder to stay out of the penalty box.
