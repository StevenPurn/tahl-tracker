# Ice Cold Redemption: Gold as Ice Edges Out Mayhem Wreck+ in Thrilling Shootout

**Date:** May 19, 2026 | **Arena:** The Rink | **Attendance:** Electric

If you were looking for a Saturday night barn burner, you clearly saved your seat at the right place. Under the bright lights this evening, Gold as Ice shook off their recent slump and ground out a gritty, heart-stopping 2-1 victory over Mayhem Wreck+. It wasn't always pretty, and it certainly wasn't easy, but in this league, a win is a win—and this one had enough drama to satisfy even the most cynical rink rat.

### Period 1: The Wrecking Ball Hits Early
Mayhem Wreck+ came out of the gate looking like they’d had three shots of espresso before warmups. They established a physical presence early, clogging the lanes and making life miserable for the Gold as Ice defensive corps. 

The breakthrough came at the 14:31 mark of the first. **Zac Hughes** found himself in the right place at the right time, picking up a loose puck and burying it with the kind of composure that makes goalies lose sleep. Mayhem Wreck+ took that 1-0 lead into the intermission, leaving the Gold as Ice faithful wondering if they were in for another long night.

### Period 2: The Goalie Duel
The middle frame turned into a blue-collar defensive battle. Both netminders were standing on their heads, turning aside everything that came their way. There was plenty of "traffic in front of the net," with enough elbows and shoves to keep the refs busy, but neither team could crack the code. The puck was pinging off posts and catching leather, keeping the tension thick enough to cut with a skate blade.

### Period 3: Finding the Groove
Down by one and desperate for a spark, Gold as Ice finally woke up in the third. They shifted the momentum, spending long, agonizing shifts in the offensive zone. Their persistence paid off at 12:27 when **Aden Ingram** cleaned up a beautiful dish from **Dickie Williams** and **Mark DeSimone**. Ingram didn't just tuck it in; he put that biscuit top shelf where mom keeps the cookies, knotting the game at 1-1.

Mayhem Wreck+ found themselves in hot water at the 6:06 mark when **Spencer Hodgson** was sent to the sin bin for slashing. However, the Gold as Ice power play couldn't capitalize, and despite a flurry of late-game chances, we headed to the shootout.

### The Shootout: Ingram and DeSimone Seal the Deal
If you’re a fan of high-stakes theater, the shootout is your main stage. With the game on the line, the ice felt like it was shrinking under the skaters’ blades.

Moments later, **Mark DeSimone** stepped up with ice in his veins, dancing through the shootout and sealing the deal with a crafty finish. The Gold as Ice bench erupted, and the visitors walked away with the extra point.
