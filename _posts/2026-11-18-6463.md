# Berzerkers Power Past Gold as Ice in 6-3 Rink Showdown

If you like your hockey served with a side of clinical finishing and a bit of blue-collar hustle, Monday night’s tilt at the rink was right up your alley. The Berzerkers walked away with a 6-3 victory over Gold as Ice, proving that sometimes, even in this league, the guys who hit the open man end up taking home the points.

The Berzerkers opened the scoring early and rarely took their foot off the gas. Jonathan Pixley was the architect of the first period, netting two goals—one off a crisp feed from Hunter Neafus and another courtesy of a smart play by Cody Stephens. While Kalea Maxwell managed to put Gold as Ice on the board late in the first, courtesy of a nice setup by Aden Ingram and Mark DeSimone, the Berzerkers had already established a rhythm that suggested they weren't interested in a tight game.

Cody Stephens added his own marker at the 2:45 mark of the opening frame, effectively acting as the team's entire offensive engine for a three-minute stretch. 

The second period was where the "Gold as Ice" moniker took on a bit of irony. While Hunter Neafus extended the lead, the Gold defense finally decided to wake up, sparking a brief mid-period frenzy. Taylor Chinowth and Aden Ingram both tickled the twine in quick succession to narrow the gap to 4-3, creating a sudden, palpable tension in the building. It’s the kind of momentum shift that usually makes a coach grow grey hair in real-time, but the Berzerkers managed to weather the storm without resorting to panic or, thankfully, too much time in the sin bin—despite Sage Blubaugh’s brief vacation for interference.

"We just stopped watching the puck and started playing it," said one Berzerkers veteran after the game, clearly grateful the team found their defensive structure again before things got out of hand.

The final frame belonged entirely to the Berzerkers. They tightened their grip on the neutral zone, effectively turning the game into a perimeter-heavy affair that left Gold as Ice chasing shadows. Hunter Neafus notched his second of the night, followed by a late insurance marker from Evert Wilmeth, who was nicely set up by the aforementioned Blubaugh and Ryan Vidal.

For the Berzerkers, the win is a welcome correction to their recent "L-W-L-L-W" form, helping them maintain their footing in a crowded middle-pack of the standings. Gold as Ice, meanwhile, will be looking to get back to the drawing board after failing to capitalize on their mid-game push. 

At the end of the day, it was a solid night of hockey—the kind where the better-executed strategy usually wins out. The Berzerkers showed up, finished their chances, and reminded the rest of the league that they aren't about to slide quietly into the basement.
