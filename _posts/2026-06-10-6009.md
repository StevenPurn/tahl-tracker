# Mob Rule: The Mafia Flexes Muscle in 5-1 Drubbing of Average Hoes

**By: "Slap-Shot" Sammy, Hometown Beat Reporter**
**Date: June 9, 2026 | 10:30 PM Puck Drop**

If there’s one thing we learned at the rink tonight, it’s that you don’t cross The Mafia—especially when they’ve got their skates sharpened and their sights set on the top of the standings. In a late-night showdown that felt more like a masterclass in puck possession, The Mafia dismantled the Average Hoes with a clinical 5-1 victory.

### Period 1: The Ross Reckoning
The Hoes barely had time to adjust their helmets before the dam broke. Daron Ross came out of the gate like a man possessed, lighting the lamp just 4:05 into the opening frame. He wasn’t finished, though. Just 34 seconds later, Ross doubled the lead, finishing off a beautiful tic-tac-toe play from Scott Hellard and Brett Lamb. 

By the time Aaron West buried a third goal at 7:39—again off the creative sticks of Hellard and Lamb—the Hoes looked like they were skating in cement. The Mafia was winning every puck battle along the boards, effectively turning the offensive zone into their own personal playground.

### Period 2: Locking Down the Ice
The second period was a defensive stalemate, but with the Mafia firmly in the driver’s seat. Brett Lamb, who had been orchestrating the offense all night, finally grabbed a goal of his own at 1:05, assisted by Nathan Garcia. The Hoes’ netminder faced a barrage of rubber, and while they stood tall against several high-danger chances to keep the score from ballooning further, the momentum was undeniably one-way traffic.

### Period 3: Closing the Books
The third period saw The Mafia add the final nail in the coffin. Daron Ross completed his monster night by recording a hat trick at 13:48, courtesy of a crafty feed from Tyler Waller. 

The Average Hoes, refusing to go home empty-handed, finally cracked the code with just 2:37 remaining. Nicole Baker stepped up and sniped one home to break the shutout bid, a gritty individual effort that served as a small moral victory in an otherwise long night at the office.

### The Breakdown
While the final buzzer signaled a 5-1 loss for the Average Hoes, the stat sheet tells a story of pure synergy for The Mafia. Ross (hat trick), Hellard (multiple assists), and Lamb (1G, 2A) were a three-headed monster that the Hoes simply had no answer for. 

**What this means for the standings:**
The Mafia is officially a force to be reckoned with. With this win, they improve to 2-0-0, leapfrogging the competition to solidify their place in the upper echelon of the league standings. For the Average Hoes, it’s a tough 0-2 start, but with plenty of hockey left in the season, they’ll need to tighten up the defensive zone if they want to avoid getting caught in the basement.
