# Valkyries Soar to Narrow Victory in Tuesday Night Thriller

**By: "Slap-Shot" Sammy, Hometown Beat Reporter**
**Date: May 26, 2026**

If you weren’t at the arena tonight, you missed a prime-time heater. The ice was crisp, the tension was palpable, and by the time the final buzzer rang out at 8:00 PM, the Valkyries had edged out Chix with Stix 3-2 in what can only be described as a classic Tuesday night barn burner.

### The First: Trading Leather
The Chix with Stix came out looking to keep their winning streak alive, and they wasted no time putting the pressure on. Just under seven minutes into the frame, Heather Cozad found the back of the net off a sweet dish from Elizabeth Edwards. It was a sniper’s goal, tucked away with precision.

But the Valkyries, fresh off a tough loss, weren't about to roll over. They started generating some serious traffic in front of the crease, and at the 6:43 mark, Alexandria Elrod answered the bell. Assisted by Kalea Maxwell and Jessica Hinkefent, Elrod hammered one home to knot things up at 1-1 heading into the first intermission.

### The Second: A Flurry of Action
The second period was pure adrenaline. With the pace quickening, the Valkyries’ Gabrielle Gibson decided to put on a clinic. At 9:19, she showcased some silky mitts to put the home team ahead 2-1. 

However, "Chix with Stix" lived up to their name, refusing to let the Valkyries pull away. Less than 30 seconds later, Angela Willoughby caught the Valkyries’ defense napping on a transition play, fed beautifully once again by Edwards. Just like that, we were tied again, 2-2. It was back-and-forth hockey at its finest—no room for error, and goalies on both ends were standing on their heads to keep the scoreline respectable.

### The Third: Rozell’s Rocket
The final frame turned into a defensive chess match, with both squads clogging the lanes and limiting clean looks. Just when it felt like we might be heading to a shootout, Cynthia Rozell decided to take matters into her own hands. 

At 4:20, Rozell found a sliver of daylight and ripped a wrister that went top shelf where mom keeps the cookies. It was an unassisted beauty that proved to be the dagger. The Valkyries hunkered down for the remaining minutes, playing a tight, defensive brand of hockey that made the Chix with Stix’s desperate attempts look like they were shooting at a wall of brick.

### The Takeaway
When the final horn sounded, the Valkyries stood tall, snatching a massive 3-2 win to stop their skid. For the Valkyries, this is a statement victory—a gritty, lunch-pail effort that proves they can hang with the hottest teams in the league. As for Chix with Stix, despite the loss, the duo of Edwards and Willoughby continues to be a handful for any defensive unit; they’ll be looking to rebound and get back in the win column immediately.

It’s nights like these that remind us why we love this game. Fast, physical, and decided by a single bounce. Catch you at the next one—and don't forget your earmuffs, because the goal horn was ringing loud tonight!
