# Kraken Cold Ones Pour It On in 8-1 Rout of Lite Beer Eagles

If you were looking for a high-octane offensive display on Tuesday night, the local rink was the place to be. The Kraken Cold Ones, who have spent most of the season struggling to find the back of the net, finally decided to stop counting their beverage options and start counting goals, dismantling the Lite Beer Eagles in an 8-1 victory.

For the Cold Ones, it was the kind of night where the puck seemed to have a gravitational pull toward the Eagles' net. Tommy Manning got the party started early in the first period, finding the back of the twine at the 4:33 mark off a feed from Kyle Murray. It was a simple play—no fancy dangling, just hard work and good positioning—that set the tone for a long, cold night for the Eagles.

The real fireworks, however, were saved for the second period, which turned into a bit of a clinic. The Kraken struck twice in a span of just 12 seconds midway through the frame, with Chase Stubblefield and Edgar Solorio turning a tight game into a runaway train. By the time Robert Tyree decided to put his own stamp on the contest with two quick goals in under a minute, the Eagles looked like they were ready to pack up their gear and head to the parking lot early.

Tyree, who entered the night riding a modest point streak, clearly decided that one point just wasn't enough. He completed his hat trick early in the third period, capitalizing on a pass from Walter Foddis to put the game well out of reach at 6-0.

"We just stopped chasing the play and started playing the game," one Kraken skater remarked in the locker room, likely enjoying the rare sensation of a multi-goal cushion. 

The Eagles, to their credit, didn’t completely fold, but they spent most of the night chasing shadows. A roughing penalty by Corey Phillips midway through the final frame didn't help their cause, and by the time Jeff Horvath finally broke the shutout for the Eagles off assists from Kevin Bailey-McGinnis and Michael Hilton with just under eight minutes remaining, the game had long been decided. Late goals by Randy Hatfield and Justin McKinney simply added salt to the wound, cementing an 8-1 final that saw the Kraken dominate in every facet of the game.

For the Lite Beer Eagles, this loss is a frustrating setback that drops them to 3-6-1, leaving them searching for answers as they hover near the bottom of the standings. Meanwhile, the Kraken Cold Ones move to 3-6-1, finally snapping a two-game skid and proving that even a team that’s been struggling can light up the scoreboard when the stars—and the bounces—align. 

It wasn't a game for the highlight reels of the pros, but for a Tuesday night at the local rink, it was a reminder that in this league, you’re only ever one good shift away from a breakout. The Cold Ones will look to bottle this energy; the Eagles, meanwhile, will likely look for a shorter memory.
