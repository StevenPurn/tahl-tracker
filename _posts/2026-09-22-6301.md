# Bar Down Boys Light the Lamp in Nine-Goal Offensive Explosion

If you like your hockey served with a side of high-octane offense, Monday night’s tilt at the rink was the place to be. The Bar Down Boys lived up to their name in a big way, dismantling Havok with a dominant 9-5 victory that had the scoreboard operator working overtime.

The Bar Down Boys came into the contest sitting pretty at second place, and they played like a team with a target on their back—and a laser focus on the net. The tone was set early in the first period when Nicholas Strle broke the ice at 3:17, followed shortly by a solo effort from Emil Hinkefent at 6:45. 

While Matthan Hillberg spent two minutes in the sin bin for tripping early on, the Havok power play couldn't find a rhythm to capitalize. Hinkefent proved he was the man with the hot stick, burying his second of the night at 10:16 of the second period to extend the lead. Havok’s Dominic Najjar finally got the home team on the board at 11:05, but it felt like the hockey equivalent of bringing a squirt gun to a house fire.

"We were just trying to keep the puck moving," said a grinning Hinkefent after the game. "When the chemistry is there, the puck finds the back of the net—sometimes it feels like the goal is the size of a garage door."

The third period turned into an absolute barnburner, featuring a flurry of goals that kept the crowd buzzing. Brandt Mueller kicked off the final frame by finding the twine just over ten minutes in. Despite a late-period slashing call on Benjamin Gartner, the Bar Down Boys’ offense refused to slow down. Even as Havok attempted a desperate rally—bolstered by goals from Zack Wade, Michael Bergstrom, Michael Pasque, and a late marker from Logan Singleton—it was too little, too late.

The highlight of the third was undoubtedly Garrett Williams, who went on a tear, recording a natural hat trick in the span of just a few minutes. "I don't know what they were feeding him at the intermission," joked a teammate from the bench, "but I'll take a double order of that."

For Havok, the loss is a tough pill to swallow, as they remain in the bottom half of the standings looking for their first win of the season. They showed serious grit by continuing to scrap until the final buzzer, with Logan Singleton extending his goal streak, but their defensive zone coverage simply couldn't contain the Bar Down Boys' relentless pressure.

With this high-scoring victory, the Bar Down Boys maintain their firm grip on second place, proving they have the firepower to run with the best of them. As for Havok, they’ll look to shake off the defensive lapses and find their footing before their next drop of the puck. One thing is for certain: if you missed this one, you missed a prime example of why we love local hockey.
