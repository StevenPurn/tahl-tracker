# Bronze Age Rebellion: Gold as Ice Bronze Stuns Yetis in 6-3 Thriller

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
**June 14, 2026**

If you thought the ice at the arena was only for skating, you missed the absolute fireworks show this evening. Coming into tonight’s tilt, the Yetis were sitting pretty at third in the standings, while Gold as Ice Bronze were looking for a spark at the bottom of the table. Well, consider the fuse lit: Gold as Ice Bronze walked into the barn and dismantled the Yetis 6-3, proving that on any given Sunday, the standings are just a piece of paper.

### Period 1: A Cold Start for the Yetis
The game began with a flurry of activity, and unfortunately for the Yetis, almost all of it was at the wrong end of the ice. Lance Levasseur kicked off the scoring barrage at the 10:40 mark with a solo effort that had the goalie scratching his head. Just over a minute later, Triston Wilson connected with Levasseur to double the lead.

The Yetis’ frustration began to boil over as the clock ticked down. Jason Fennell, usually a cool head, found himself in hot water with a 10-minute misconduct for verbal abuse of an official, followed quickly by an additional 2-minute minor for unsportsmanlike conduct. It was a chaotic end to a frame where the Yetis seemed to leave their composure in the locker room.

### Period 2: The Wilson Show
If the first period was a struggle, the second was a deluge. Triston Wilson decided he was going to take the game by the scruff of the neck, and he did just that. He notched a natural hat trick—and then some. 

Wilson buried his second of the night off a Jake Giove feed at 11:38, followed by a second marker from Levasseur. By the time Wilson buried his third and fourth goals of the game in rapid succession, the Gold as Ice Bronze lead had ballooned to a staggering 6-0. 

The Yetis finally found a pulse with just under seven minutes remaining in the frame. Michael Pacheco broke the shutout bid, redirecting a dish from Kirk Nielsen to put the Yetis on the board. It was a small consolation, but it signaled that the Yeti pride wasn't fully buried under the ice.

### Period 3: Too Little, Too Late
The third period saw the Yetis tighten up their defensive structure and start generating some real traffic in front of the net. They leaned on their veteran presence to spark a comeback, with Kirk Nielsen burying one from Corey Favier at the 14:45 mark to cut the deficit to four. 

Jason Fennell, looking to redeem his turbulent first period, managed to find the twine with just 2:35 remaining. The Yetis pressed hard in the closing minutes, throwing everything at the cage, but the mountain was simply too high to climb. When the final horn sounded, the scoreboard read 6-3 in favor of the visitors.

### The Aftermath
For Gold as Ice Bronze, this is a massive statement win. Coming in at the #10 spot, they displayed a clinical finish tonight, anchored by Triston Wilson’s heroic four-goal performance. They’ve proven they can punch above their weight class and should climb out of the basement with this kind of offensive output.

As for the Yetis, they’ll look to shake off the rust and the extracurricular drama. Sitting at 1-1-1 entering the night, this loss stings, but with a long season ahead, they’ll need to regroup quickly. 

The ice might be frozen, but the competition is heating up. We’ll see you at the rink next time, hockey fans—keep your sticks on the ice and your heads up!
