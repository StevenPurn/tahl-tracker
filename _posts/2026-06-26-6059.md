# Gold Rush: Undefeated Leaders Silence the Wendigos in 5-2 Masterclass

**Date:** June 25, 2026 | **Arena:** The Deep Freeze  
**Final Score:** Wendigos 2, Gold as Ice 5

If you were looking for a David vs. Goliath story on a Tuesday night, you might have walked into the rink expecting the Wendigos to finally find their footing. But standing across the center ice logo were the titans of the league, **Gold as Ice**, who arrived at the rink looking like they had an appointment with destiny—and they weren't about to be late.

Coming into the night 4-0-0, Gold as Ice proved exactly why they are sitting pretty at the top of the standings, dispatching the winless Wendigos 5-2 in a game that felt like a surgical strike.

### The First Period: Establishing the Territory
Gold as Ice came out with the speed of a runaway Zamboni. At 12:33, **Rachel Colombo** opened the floodgates with an unassisted tally that rattled the Wendigos’ confidence early. The pressure remained relentless, and at 4:38, **Daniel Hanning** hammered home a beauty off a crisp feed from **Joshua Hopwood**. By the time the buzzer sounded for the first intermission, the Wendigos were reeling, struggling to clear the heavy traffic in front of their crease.

### The Second Period: A Brief Flicker of Hope
The Wendigos, determined not to go quietly into that good night, finally found a pulse in the middle frame. **Taylor Chinowth**—riding a hot stick with a three-game point streak—tucked one home at 14:11, set up perfectly by **Dickie Williams**. For a fleeting moment, the barn was rocking. The Wendigos were skating with purpose, grinding out puck battles, and making the league leaders sweat just a little bit under the collar.

### The Third Period: The Closing Statement
The final frame started with a bang. **Zac Hughes** knotted things up early at 12:04 with a gritty, unassisted goal that had the Wendigos’ bench buzzing. But just as the upset seemed possible, Gold as Ice slammed the window shut.

**Mark DeSimone**, keeping his four-game point streak alive and kicking, reclaimed the lead at 2:07, again off the stick of the playmaking machine Joshua Hopwood. That goal seemed to break the dam. **Jessica Hopwood** added an insurance marker at 5:12, and DeSimone capped off his stellar night with a late exclamation point at 7:27, assisted by **Dylan Dillard**. 

When the dust settled, the Wendigos had learned a harsh lesson: against a team as deep as Gold as Ice, you can’t give them an inch, or they’ll take the whole rink.

### The Takeaway
For **Gold as Ice (5-0-0)**, the train keeps rolling. They are currently the undisputed kings of the mountain, playing a brand of hockey that is as cold and calculated as their name implies. They have the chemistry, the depth, and the momentum to keep this unbeaten streak well into the summer.

As for the **Wendigos (0-4-0)**, the search for that elusive first win continues. They showed flashes of brilliance tonight, but the road remains steep. They’ll need to tighten up the defensive zone and find a way to sustain that second-period energy for a full sixty minutes if they want to climb out of the basement.
