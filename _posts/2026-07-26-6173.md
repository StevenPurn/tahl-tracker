# Badgers Bite Back: Honey Badgers Claw Past Wolfpack in Thrilling Shootout

**By: Rinkside Reporter**
*July 26, 2026*

If you like your hockey served with a side of high-octane drama and a dash of “anything can happen,” you should’ve been at the barn this evening. In a matchup that looked like a simple mid-tier collision between the One Man Wolfpack and the Honey Badgers, we instead got an absolute barn burner that went the distance and then some. 

When the dust finally settled on this 5-4 thriller, it was the Honey Badgers—the underdogs of the league standings—who walked away with the W, proving that you should never count out an animal that’s known for being, well, pretty mean.

### Period 1: The Wolfpack Takes the Lead
The Wolfpack entered the evening looking to climb the ladder, and they came out of the gate with their fur standing on end. After killing off an early slashing minor to John Digges, they shifted into high gear. 

James Whitacre continued his hot hand, lighting the lamp at the 7:07 mark off a sweet feed from Bradley Fidler. Less than a minute later, Brandt Mueller hammered one home, assisted by Whitacre, to put the Pack up 2-0. It looked like they might run away with it, especially with the Badgers struggling to find their footing early, but a slashing penalty to Matthan Hillberg late in the period gave the Badgers a moment to breathe and reset their systems.

### Period 2: The Nunneley Show Begins
The second frame saw the ice tilt back toward the center. The Badgers needed a spark, and they found it in Marshall Nunneley. Fresh off his massive scoring streak, Nunneley broke the silence at 12:14, burying a helper from Hillberg and Drew Sleezer to cut the lead in half.

But just as the Badgers were building momentum, Bradley Fidler reminded everyone why he’s a threat, dangling through the zone to restore the two-goal cushion at 1:49. A hooking call on Fidler midway through the period threatened to derail the Pack, but they dug in their heels and played some gritty defense to head to the third with a 3-1 lead.

### Period 3: A Wild West Finish
If you left your seat to grab a hot dog, you missed the chaotic heart of this game. The Badgers came out with a level of ferocity that would make a honey badger proud. 

Nunneley struck again at 08.7 seconds into the period, followed by a clutch equalizer from Matthan Hillberg at 12:28 to tie it up 3-3. The tension was thick enough to cut with a skate blade. Whitacre refused to let the lead slip away entirely, answering back for the Wolfpack at 7:27 with a gritty goal from John Digges. 

However, with the game hanging in the balance, Adam Carter stepped up for the Badgers just 26 seconds later, knotting the score at 4-4. With the clock ticking down and hearts racing, both goaltenders stood tall, forcing the game into the high-stakes drama of a shootout.

### The Shootout: Cold Ice, Hot Hands
With the game on the line, the shootout proved that the Badgers had the sharper claws tonight. Joey Pachuta, riding a wave of confidence with a five-game point streak, stepped up and iced his attempt with the cool demeanor of a veteran. Marshall Nunneley, who had been a menace all night, finished off the masterpiece with the deciding goal, handing the Honey Badgers a 5-4 victory in a game for the ages.

### The Fallout
For the Honey Badgers, this win is a massive morale booster. They’ve been hunting for consistency all season, and tonight, they proved they have the depth and the grit to hang with anyone. They move to 2-6-0, but more importantly, they’ve got the momentum to start climbing out of the basement.

For the One Man Wolfpack (3-5-0), it’s a tough pill to swallow. They’ll look at the missed opportunities to put the game away, but they have to like the offensive output from Whitacre and Fidler. They remain in the hunt for a mid-table spot, but they’ll need to tighten up the defensive zone if they want to get back to their winning ways.

The league is tightening up, folks. If tonight is any indication, the race to the postseason is going to be one wild ride. See you at the rink!
