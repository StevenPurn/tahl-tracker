# Ice Cold to Red Hot: Wolfpack Claw Back to Edge Blizzard in Bottom-Half Battle

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
*July 20, 2026*

The mercury may have been rising outside, but the ice at the local barn was absolutely frigid on Monday night as the Blizzard (#6) and the One Man Wolfpack (#7) squared off in a classic "four-point game." Entering the night deadlocked in the standings with 4 points apiece, these two squads played like they were skating for their lives, resulting in a 2-1 thriller that lived up to every bit of the pre-game hype.

### Period 1: The Blizzard Takes the Lead
The Blizzard struck first at the 6:14 mark, and it was a beauty. Jared Conway channeled his inner sniper, burying a feed from Garrett Williams to put the Blizzard on the board. The Blizzard looked sharp early, buzzing around the zone and forcing the Wolfpack to chase the game. If you like your hockey with a little bit of grit and a lot of hustle, this opening frame was your brand of heaven.

### Period 2: Momentum Swings and Sin Bins
The second period saw the Wolfpack turn the tide—and turn up the heat. Just 67 seconds into the frame, Michael Bergstrom silenced the Blizzard faithful with an unassisted equalizer. 

The momentum turned into a bit of a chippy affair immediately after the goal. Jared Conway found himself in the box for tripping just as the celebration died down, and shortly after, Nolan Kwasny joined him in the "cool-down corner" for the same infraction. The Blizzard spent a chunk of the second period trying to keep their heads above water while killing off penalties, but the Wolfpack’s forecheck was relentless, turning the neutral zone into a minefield of turnovers.

### Period 3: Whitacre’s Late-Game Heroics
As the clock wound down, the tension in the building was thick enough to cut with a skate blade. With penalties to Bradley Fidler and John Digges piling up, it looked like this one might require a trip to the shootout lottery.

But James Whitacre had other plans. In the waning minutes of the third, Whitacre found a seam and punched home an unassisted game-winner at 7:35, sending the Wolfpack bench into a frenzy. It was a classic "garbage goal" effort—no flash, just pure grit and a nose for the net—that secured the 2-1 victory. 

### The Aftermath
This win is a massive statement for the One Man Wolfpack. By snatching these two points, they leapfrog the Blizzard in the standings, continuing their hot streak of two consecutive wins. The Wolfpack (3-4-0) are officially trending upward, while the Blizzard (2-5-0) head back to the drawing board to find their offensive spark once more.

With the middle of the pack tightening up, every shift matters. Tonight, the Wolfpack proved that when the game is on the line, they’re more than just a name—they’re a collective force to be reckoned with. 

**Final Score: Blizzard 1, One Man Wolfpack 2**
