# HODGES HAT-TRICK AND THEN SOME: MOOSEKNUCKLES MUSCLE PAST JACKALOPES IN 7-2 ROUT

**By: "Rinkside" Riley, Hometown Hockey Beat Writer**

**DATE:** June 14, 2026 | **TIME:** 9:45 AM

If you weren't at the rink this morning, you missed a clinic. The Mooseknuckles entered today’s tilt sitting pretty at #2 in the standings, and after a 7-2 shellacking of the Jackalopes, they’ve sent a message to the rest of the league: clear the tracks, because the freight train is picking up steam.

### First Period: The Jolt
The Jackalopes came out swinging, looking to derail the Mooseknuckles' momentum early. Tyler Wick drew first blood at 10:23, set up beautifully by James Whitacre. When Kyle Foster followed that up with an unassisted laser at 13:55, the ‘Lopes bench was buzzing, holding a 2-0 lead and looking like they might pull the upset of the year.

But the Mooseknuckles? They don't panic. They just tightened the laces and went to work. Jeffrey Kleehammer sparked the rally with 2:33 left in the frame, finding the back of the net off a gorgeous feed from Ben Savory and Jasen Lawwill. With the momentum shifting, Mark Hodges struck just moments later to knot it up at 2-2 heading into the break.

### Second Period: The Turning Tide
The second frame was all about the "Traffic Report." Both teams traded trips to the sin bin, but the Mooseknuckles’ special teams proved to be the difference-maker. Pete Fleming, who has been absolutely lights-out lately, finally broke the seal at 11:14, assisted by Lawwill, to give the 'Knuckles their first lead of the morning.

Mark Hodges, clearly feeling like he’d forgotten to eat breakfast and decided to feast on the Jackalopes' defense instead, potted his second of the game at 8:08. By the time the buzzer sounded on the second, the energy in the building had shifted entirely toward the Mooseknuckles' side of the ice.

### Third Period: The Hodges Highlight Reel
If the second period was a warning, the third was a total demolition. Mark Hodges turned this game into his own personal highlight reel. He completed the hat-trick, added a fourth, and then—just for good measure—potted a fifth goal with less than five minutes remaining. 

The chemistry between Hodges, Lawwill, and Fleming was surgical. Lawwill, in particular, was the engine room today, racking up assists like he was collecting library books. By the time the final horn blew, the Jackalopes looked like they’d just run a marathon in snowshoes, while the Mooseknuckles skated off the ice with a commanding 7-2 victory.

### The Breakdown
*   **The Hero:** Mark Hodges. Five goals. You read that right. He wasn't just finding the top shelf; he was taking the cookies, the jar, and the milk.
*   **The Playmaker:** Jasen Lawwill. Four assists. The man has eyes in the back of his head and the precision of a Swiss watch.
*   **The Standings:** The Mooseknuckles maintain their stronghold at #2, breathing down the necks of the Tulsa Murder. They look every bit like a championship contender. For the Jackalopes, it’s a tough morning at the office. Sitting at #4, they’ll need to regroup and find that defensive grit again if they want to climb back into the top three.

It was a cold morning at the rink, but the Mooseknuckles provided all the heat we needed. We’ll see you next game, folks—don't forget to keep your stick on the ice!
