# Dirty Birds Soar High in 10-3 Rout of Honey Badgers

If you came to the rink expecting a defensive stalemate on Saturday afternoon, you were likely watching the wrong game. The Dirty Birds treated the ice like their own personal playground, hanging a crooked number on the scoreboard and skating away with a decisive 10-3 victory over the winless Honey Badgers.

The Birds were flying early and often, turning this regular-season tilt into a highlight reel. By the time the final buzzer echoed through the rafters, the Dirty Birds had staked their claim as a force to be reckoned with in the standings, while the Badgers are left searching for a way to stop the bleeding.

The tone was set early in the first period when the Dirty Birds’ top line decided they weren't going home hungry. Triston Wilson and Nicklas Clark opened the account, reminding everyone why they came into this one riding scoring streaks. By the time the second frame rolled around, the Birds weren't just playing hockey; they were staging a clinic.

The second period was, quite frankly, a nightmare for the Badgers' bench. James Smith put on a masterclass that looked more like a video game on rookie difficulty. He found the back of the net with a relentless frequency that had the folks in the stands checking their watches to make sure they hadn't missed a period. Smith’s second-period outburst—a flurry of goals that kept the goal judge working overtime—effectively put the game out of reach before the third even began. 

"I think we just stopped checking the jersey colors and started checking the open space," joked one Dirty Birds supporter near the glass after the sixth goal went in. 

To their credit, the Honey Badgers refused to fold like a lawn chair in a windstorm. Down by a mountain of goals, Jack Brady finally broke the seal for the Badgers in the third, and Joseph Pachuta added a pair of respectable tallies to keep things interesting. But every time the Badgers seemed to find a spark, the Dirty Birds poured a bucket of cold water on it. Smith added yet another tally, and Clark rounded out his impressive afternoon with a late goal to finalize the double-digit scoreline.

For the Dirty Birds, this win is a massive statement. They’ve now secured their fourth win of the season, moving them firmly into the upper echelon of the league table. For the Honey Badgers, it’s a long, cold walk to the locker room. Sitting at 0-5-1, the team needs a serious internal audit if they hope to climb out of the basement.

As the boys cleared the ice, the consensus was clear: the Dirty Birds are hitting their stride at exactly the right time, while the Badgers are hoping that tomorrow’s practice involves a lot of puck-stopping drills. If the Birds keep playing like this, the rest of the league might want to start investing in better goaltending depth—because this flock has teeth.
