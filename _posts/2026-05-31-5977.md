# Buzzer-Beater Brilliance: Chix with Stix Sting the Bees in 3-2 Thriller

**By: "Hat-Trick" Harry, Beat Reporter**

**MAY 31, 2026** — If you weren’t at the rink this afternoon, you missed a classic barn burner. The air in the arena was crisp, the Zamboni had just finished its final polish, and the tension was thick enough to cut with a skate blade. In a pivotal mid-season clash, the Chix with Stix clawed their way out of the cellar, edging out the Bad Bees 3-2 in a game that had more twists than a playmaker’s saucer pass.

### First Period: Trading Punches
The Chix came into this one hungry, eager to scrub that zero from their points column. They didn’t waste any time finding the rhythm. At 5:35, **Elizabeth Edwards**—riding high on a two-game point streak—tucked one home off a crisp feed from **Ashley Ivanoff**. It was a classic "bread and butter" play that put the Chix up early.

The Bees, however, didn’t get their name by being pushovers. After **Remisha Blation** spent a two-minute vacation in the sin bin for a tripping infraction, the Bees flipped the script. While shorthanded, **Daphnie Peak** decided she’d had enough of the defensive grind, went coast-to-coast, and buried an unassisted beauty to knot the game at 1-1 heading into the first intermission.

### Second Period: The Tactical Chess Match
The second frame was a masterclass in neutral zone neutralizers. Both goaltenders decided that the back of the net was off-limits for the next fifteen minutes. We saw some heavy traffic in front of the net, but the goalies were stellar, turning away rebound opportunities with the calm of a veteran on a breakaway. It was a period of grit, grind, and spectacular glove saves that kept the fans on the edge of their seats.

### Third Period: The Allison Show
If the first two periods were the setup, the third was the punchline. With the clock winding down and the tension rising, **Mckenna N. Allison** decided to turn the arena into her personal highlight reel. 

At 13:31, Allison found a pocket of space and rifled one home to regain the lead for the Chix. But just as the clock dipped under five minutes, the Bees’ **Remisha Blation**—looking to atone for her earlier trip to the box—fired a rocket past the goalie to tie it up once again at 2-2. 

Just when we all thought we were heading for overtime, the Chix with Stix executed a textbook strike. With a mere 44.4 seconds remaining on the clock, **Ashley Ivanoff** dished her second assist of the night, feeding **Mckenna N. Allison** who buried it top-shelf where mom keeps the cookies. That game-winner sent the Chix faithful into a frenzy, icing a 3-2 victory in the dying seconds.

### The Aftermath
This result is a massive momentum shifter. The Chix with Stix (1-1-0) finally get their first two points of the season, vaulting them right into the thick of the standings. For the Bad Bees, it’s a tough pill to swallow after a gritty performance, but they showed that their scoring touch—led by the resilient Blation—is going to be a problem for every team in the league.

The standings are tightening up, folks. If tonight was any indication, the road to the championship is going to be paved with sweat, broken sticks, and a whole lot of heart. Catch you at the next puck drop!
