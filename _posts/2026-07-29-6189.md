# Wreck+ Rocks the Rink: Mayhem Topples Kraken in Late-Night Thriller

**By: "Slap-Shot" Sammy, Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: July 28, 2026**

There’s something about a 10:15 PM puck drop that just screams "gritty hockey," and tonight at the rink, the Kraken Cold Ones and Mayhem Wreck+ delivered a classic late-night barn burner. While the standings suggested a blowout, the ice told a different story. In a game that saw the lead trade hands like a hot potato, the Mayhem Wreck+ ultimately edged out the Kraken 4-3, keeping their momentum surging while the Cold Ones continue to search for that elusive spark.

### The Middle Frame Explosion
After a scoreless opening period—where the goalies were essentially playing a game of staring contests—the floodgates opened in the second.

The Mayhem drew first blood at 10:53 when **Andrew Burns** went coast-to-coast, burying a beauty to break the ice. But the Kraken, desperate to end their slide, answered back with a vengeance. **Gregory Boyer** found the back of the net just over a minute later, assisted by Ryan Martinez, to tie it up. Minutes later, **Corey Phillips** sent the Kraken bench into a frenzy with an unassisted laser, giving the Cold Ones a 2-1 lead.

Mayhem wasn’t about to let this be the Kraken’s night, though. **Ivy Emerson** knotted things back up at 6:03, capitalizing on a crisp setup from Jeff Rhodes and the red-hot Stephen Jeffery. The Kraken, refusing to go quietly, saw **Gregory Boyer** strike again at 9:26, snagging his second of the night off a Corey Phillips dish. We headed to the third with the Cold Ones clinging to a 3-2 lead, and the tension in the building was thick enough to cut with a skate blade.

### The Third Period Turnaround
The final frame was all about defensive grit and closing gaps. The Kraken held firm for as long as they could, but **Ivy Emerson**—playing the role of hero tonight—found the twine again with just 8.2 seconds left on the clock in a critical sequence, assisted once more by Stephen Jeffery. 

Just when the Kraken thought they’d salvaged a point to force overtime, **Justin DeMoss** played the spoiler. With only 4:42 remaining in the contest, DeMoss hammered home a feed from Forrest Beadle, snatching the lead for the Wreck+ and deflating the Kraken’s sails for good.

### The Aftermath
It’s a heartbreaker for the Kraken Cold Ones (1-7-0), who played their most spirited 45 minutes of the season but still find themselves stuck at the bottom of the table. They’ve got the pieces, but the "W" column remains stubbornly empty.

For the Mayhem Wreck+ (7-1-1), this win is exactly what the doctor ordered. Sitting comfortably at #4, they’ve proven they can handle the pressure when the game gets tight. They’re playing like a team that knows exactly where "the cookies are kept," and tonight, they snatched the whole jar.

The Wreck+ continue their climb toward the top three, while the Cold Ones will look to lick their wounds and hope the third time—or perhaps the next ten times—is the charm. 

**Three Stars of the Night:**
1. **Ivy Emerson (Mayhem):** The multi-goal night was the difference-maker.
2. **Gregory Boyer (Kraken):** A valiant two-goal effort that nearly willed his team to a win.
3. **Stephen Jeffery (Mayhem):** The playmaker, proving why he’s currently the league’s most reliable setup man.
