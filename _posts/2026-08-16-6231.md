# Valkyries Extend Winning Streak with Disciplined 5-2 Victory over Chix with Stix

The Valkyries reaffirmed their position at the top of the league standings on Saturday morning, dispatching the Chix with Stix 5-2 in a contest that showcased exactly why the frontrunners currently boast a commanding 9-1-0 record. While the Chix with Stix entered the game hovering at a .500 winning percentage, they found themselves unable to solve the tactical puzzle posed by a Valkyries squad that remains fundamentally sound in every zone.

The tone was set early, as the Valkyries leveraged their offensive depth to establish a commanding 3-0 lead before the first intermission. Gabrielle Gibson continued her blistering form, extending her goal-scoring streak to four games at the 10:30 mark of the first period, courtesy of a tidy feed from Jessica Hinkefent. 

If there is a stat that keeps opposing coaches up at night, it is the efficiency of Kalea Maxwell. She proved why, striking twice in the final three minutes of the opening frame—first on an unassisted effort, and then burying a helper from Haley Griffin. By the time the buzzer sounded on the first period, the Chix with Stix were staring at a high-danger shot volume that suggested a long morning ahead.

To their credit, the Chix with Stix clawed back a bit of momentum in the second period. Jordan Hawkins Baker finally solved the Valkyries’ defensive shell at 12:25, assisted by Rachel Gold, providing a momentary spark for the trailing side. However, the Valkyries’ transition game is a well-oiled machine this season. Haley Griffin restored the three-goal cushion just under two minutes later with an unassisted marker, effectively extinguishing any hope of a mid-game comeback.

The back-and-forth continued briefly when Jessica Hinkefent capitalized on a setup from Gibson at the 4:03 mark, but Carson See answered shortly after for the Chix with Stix. Despite See extending her goal-scoring streak to three games, the deficit proved too steep to climb.

"We knew we were facing a team that thrives on momentum," remarked one bench observer. "But when you give up three in the first, you’re basically playing against the clock as much as you are the opponent."

For the Valkyries, the win extends their current winning streak to five games, solidifying their hold on first place with 18 points. It was a textbook display of "spreadsheet hockey"—maximizing scoring chances while maintaining a firm grip on the neutral zone. For the Chix with Stix, the loss is a reminder that in a league this competitive, lapses in the early frames often render the math of the later periods moot.

The league standings remain tight, but as the Valkyries continue their relentless march through the schedule, they have clearly established themselves as the team to beat. If the Chix with Stix hope to climb higher than their current third-place position, they’ll need to find a way to mitigate these early-game surges that are currently defining the Valkyries' season.
