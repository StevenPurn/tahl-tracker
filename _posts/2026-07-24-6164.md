# Feathered Fury: Dirty Birds Clip the Goats in 6-4 Barn Burner

**By: "Slapshot" Sam, Beat Reporter**

If you were looking for a quiet Thursday night, you picked the wrong zip code. Last night at the rink, the air was thick with tension and the smell of fresh ice, as the top-seeded Killer Goats rolled in to defend their throne against the surging Dirty Birds. By the time the final buzzer rang, the Dirty Birds had proven one thing: you don't mess with the flock when they’re hungry.

In a back-and-forth thriller that saw the lead change hands like a hot potato, the Birds walked away with a 6-4 victory, handing the Goats a rare sting and tightening the stranglehold on the standings.

### First Period: A Tit-for-Tat Start
The game opened with the intensity of a playoff final. The Dirty Birds drew first blood at 10:50, as **Matthan Hillberg** snapped one home off some slick dish-work from Gabriel Kierath and Adam Carter. But you don't reach first place by rolling over; the Goats struck back at 14:17 when **Lucas Blanchard**—continuing his heater of a season—found the back of the net off a sweet feed from Michael Doyle. With a 1-1 tie and Kierath spending two minutes in the sin bin for holding, the teams headed into the intermission deadlocked and breathless.

### Second Period: The Hillberg-Moyer Show
If the first period was a handshake, the second was a brawl. Hillberg, looking like he was playing against traffic cones, notched his second of the night at 10:34 to put the Birds back in front. But Blanchard answered the bell again at 13:07, keeping the Goats within striking distance.

The momentum finally tipped heavily toward the Birds in the dying minutes of the frame. **Josh Moyer** injected some serious electricity into the building, burying a goal at 1:51 to reclaim the lead. The Goats headed to the locker room reeling, but the real fireworks were just around the corner.

### Third Period: Offensive Avalanche
The final frame turned into an absolute circus—in the best way possible. In a stunning display of "blink and you'll miss it" hockey, **Josh Moyer** tickled the twine with a literal heartbeat left on the clock (00.1 seconds!) to extend the lead. 

The Birds didn't let off the gas, with **Michael Bergstrom** finding the back of the net at 11:28 to make it 5-2. To their credit, the Goats showed the heart of a champion. Nelson Pina clawed one back at 3:09, and the red-hot **Lucas Blanchard** completed his hat-trick with just 39.7 seconds left, turning up the heat on the Birds' defense. 

Any dreams of a Goats comeback were extinguished, however, when **Matthan Hillberg**—capping off an absolute masterclass of a game—lasered home his third goal at 4:08. A late high-sticking penalty to Michael Doyle effectively put the icing on the Dirty Birds' cake.

### The Verdict
The Dirty Birds (6-1-0) are officially the team to watch, moving to 12 points and putting the entire league on notice. They aren't just hovering near the top; they’re flying formation. 

For the Killer Goats (5-2-1), it’s a tough pill to swallow, but they remain at the top of the pile for now. However, with the Birds flapping their wings this hard, the seat at the top of the standings is getting uncomfortably warm. 

The next time these two clubs meet, I’d suggest you arrive early—because if last night was any indication, the ice is going to be well worth the price of admission.
