# Havok Breaks Losing Skid with Nine-Goal Explosion Against Wolfpack

If you’ve been hanging around the rink lately, you know the boys on Havok have been wandering through a desert of bad luck, stuck on a five-game losing streak that had them looking more like a gentle breeze than a gale-force storm. But tonight, at the 5:30 slot, they finally found the light switch—and they didn't just turn it on, they blew the bulbs out with a 9-2 thrashing of the One Man Wolfpack.

It took Emil Hinkefent less than two minutes to signal that Havok wasn't interested in playing it safe. He opened the scoring at 1:27, set up by Steven Purn, and before the Wolfpack could even settle into their skates, Zachary Wade doubled the lead just forty seconds later. By the time the final buzzer rang, the scoreboard looked like a math equation gone wrong for the Wolfpack defense.

Hinkefent was the undisputed king of the castle tonight. He wasn't just playing; he was putting on a clinic, racking up a staggering four goals on the night. After his early opener, he tacked on two more in the second period—one even-strength and one shortly after a Seth Miller tripping penalty—to complete the hat trick. He wasn't done there, either, adding a fourth goal in the third period and picking up an assist on Dominic Najjar’s late marker just for good measure.

"I think Emil finally had his morning coffee today," joked one of the Havok bench staff after the game, wiping sweat from his brow. "Whatever he’s drinking, we’re putting it on the team budget for the rest of the season."

To their credit, the Wolfpack didn't just roll over and play dead. Jared Zellers managed to tickle the twine in the second period off a feed from Garrett Williams, and James Whitacre found the back of the net in the third to keep things semi-respectable. But for every step forward the Wolfpack took, Havok seemed to take three. Dominic Najjar buried two of his own in the final frame, and Zachary Wade grabbed his second of the night, turning the third period into a full-blown Havok parade.

The win is a massive sigh of relief for Havok, moving them to 4-5-1 and snapping that agonizing five-game slide. For the One Man Wolfpack, who fall to 3-6-0, it’s a night to toss the game tape in the trash and try to forget the smell of the locker room. 

Havok heads home tonight finally feeling like the team they expected to be at the start of the season. As for the Wolfpack, they’ll have to regroup quick—in this league, the ice is slippery, and your luck can turn just as fast as a bad bounce off the boards.
