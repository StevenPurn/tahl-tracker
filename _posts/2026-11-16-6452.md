# Bison Stampede: Tulsa Runs Wild in 9-0 Rout of The Syndicate

If you were looking for a quiet Friday night at the rink, you were clearly in the wrong zip code. The Tulsa Bison turned the ice into their personal playground on November 15, dismantling The Syndicate in a relentless 9-0 offensive masterclass that left the scoreboard operator wondering if they needed to install a secondary digit display just for safety.

The Bison came into the evening looking to climb the standings, and they arrived with the subtlety of a freight train. Joshua Hall opened the floodgates at the 10:28 mark of the first period, and before The Syndicate could find their skates, the Bison had already built a four-goal cushion by the end of the opening frame. Hall’s second goal of the period at the 9:31 mark, paired with strikes from Juan Padilla and Lucas Jameson, served as a grim reminder that when a herd of Bison decides to charge, you’d best be standing safely behind the glass.

If the first period was a tremor, the second was a full-blown tectonic shift, headlined by the absolute demolition crew that is Zac Hughes. Hughes didn’t just find the back of the net; he took up residence there, registering a natural hat trick in a span of under six minutes. "I think the puck just wanted to hang out with me today," Hughes joked after the game, looking as calm as a man who had just enjoyed a leisurely Sunday brunch rather than a three-goal rampage. 

The Syndicate struggled to find any traction, pinned deep in their own zone for long stretches of the evening. Even when they were granted a man advantage following Logan Pardue’s trip in the second, the Bison penalty kill was as impenetrable as a concrete wall.

The onslaught continued into the third, with Lucas Jameson netting his second of the night to cement the 9-0 final. By the time the final buzzer mercifully sounded, the Bison had extended several impressive individual streaks, with Jameson continuing his scorching two-game goal streak and providing the kind of momentum that makes locker rooms feel like heaven. 

For the Tulsa Bison, the victory is a massive statement of intent. Climbing to a 4-3-1 record, they’ve sent a clear message to the rest of the league that their offense has finally found a gear that most teams simply cannot shift into. As for The Syndicate, they drop to 3-4-1, but in a league this competitive, one bad night at the office is usually just the setup for a redemption arc. 

Tonight, however, the ice belonged to the Bison. They weren't just playing hockey; they were conducting a clinic on the fine art of the stampede.
