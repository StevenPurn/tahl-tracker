# Puckadelics Edge Skate Goats in Battle of the Unbeatens

The Puckadelics took a hard-fought step toward cementing their spot at the top of the standings on Wednesday night, knocking off the Skate Goats 5-3 in a fast-paced regular-season tilt that had just enough grit to keep the late-night crowd awake.

Coming into the 9:30 p.m. puck drop, both teams were looking to maintain their momentum after identical 1-0-1 starts to the season. By the final buzzer, it was the Puckadelics who managed to keep their composure, thanks in large part to a multi-point performance from James DePriest that effectively acted as the team’s offensive engine.

The 'Delics came out swinging in the first period, with Garrett Williams finding the back of the net just under a minute into the action off a helper from DePriest. DePriest then turned provider into production at the 7:29 mark, scoring his first of the night to extend the lead. The Skate Goats, showing the kind of stubbornness that makes them a tough night for any goalie, answered back quickly when Joshua Gonya capitalized on a Dustin Giebel feed to narrow the gap before the first intermission.

The second period turned into a tighter, grittier affair. Toby Temple pulled the Goats level at 2-2 with an unassisted effort that silenced the Puckadelics’ bench for a moment. However, the momentum shifted firmly in favor of the 'Delics midway through the frame. After a tripping penalty put Joshua Gonya in the box, the Puckadelics leaned into the extra space, with Cody Kern finding the twine off a nice feed from Triston Wilson to reclaim the lead.

"We knew they’d push back," said one anonymous veteran skater near the ice. "In this league, you’ve got about three seconds to celebrate a lead before someone comes barreling into your crease. We just kept our heads down and cycled the puck."

The final frame saw the Puckadelics pull away, with John White finding the mark at 12:57, followed by a dagger from DePriest at the 4:06 mark—his second of the game. While the Skate Goats managed one final push with a Tom Mortimer goal, assisted by Andrew Vandiver, the damage was already done. 

The Goats' frustration began to show in the closing minutes, punctuated by a slashing penalty to Gonya, who had a busy—if not entirely productive—night in the box. 

With this win, the Puckadelics distance themselves in the crowded middle-of-the-table scramble, proving that while the league is deep this year, they’re the ones currently setting the pace. For the Skate Goats, it’s back to the drawing board to fix the defensive lapses that allowed the 'Delics to convert in transition. 

Both teams head home knowing that in a league this tight, a two-goal cushion is rarely safe—but tonight, it was exactly what the doctor ordered for the Puckadelics.
