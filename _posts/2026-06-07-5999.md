# A Night of Grit and Guts: Dirty Birds Edge Out Hitmen Silver in a 5-4 Shootout Thriller

**By: "Slapshot" Sam, Rinkside Reporter**
*June 7, 2026*

If you weren’t at the arena tonight, you missed a classic. We witnessed a barn burner that had everything: heavy traffic in front of the crease, end-to-end rushes, and enough drama to keep the penalty box attendant sweating through his sweater. When the dust finally settled on the ice, the **Dirty Birds** emerged as the kings of the jungle, walking away with a hard-fought 5-4 shootout victory over a game Hitmen Silver squad.

### Period 1: The Feeling-Out Process
The game started with the intensity of a playoff tilt. The Dirty Birds drew first blood at the 7:52 mark when **Gabriel Kierath** sniped one home, courtesy of a slick dish from **Michael Bergstrom**. Kierath, riding a hot streak, looked like he was playing with a cheat code. But the Hitmen weren’t about to let the Birds fly away early. **Skylar Miller** answered back less than two minutes later, burying a feed from **Drew Sleezer** to level the score at 1-1 as we headed into the intermission.

### Period 2: Hitmen Turn Up the Heat
If the first period was a warm-up, the second was a full-blown inferno. The Hitmen Silver found their rhythm, putting the Dirty Birds on their heels. **Bobby Pickett** put the Hitmen ahead early in the frame, capitalizing on some stellar setup work by **Aaron Quinton** and **Josh Moyer**. 

The momentum stayed firmly in the Hitmen’s corner when **Christopher Gardner** hammered home a beauty off another helper from the playmaker, Sleezer. Just when it looked like the Hitmen were going to run away with it, **Michael Bergstrom** decided to play hero for the Birds, cutting the lead to 3-2 and reminding everyone that "Dirty" is just a state of mind, not a lack of skill.

### Period 3: Chaos and Clutches
The final frame was pure, unadulterated hockey mayhem. The Birds came out with their wings flapping, knotting the game at 3-3 behind **James Smith**, and then seizing the lead thanks to a **Joel Thorson** laser, assisted by the duo of Kierath and **Steven Purn**.

Just as the Hitmen’s prospects looked bleak—and after **Aaron Quinton** spent two minutes in the sin bin for a tripping infraction—**Josh Moyer** went into "hero mode." He clawed one back for the Hitmen, unassisted, to tie the game 4-4 and send the capacity crowd into a frenzy. We were heading to the shootout.

### The Shootout: Cold Ice, Hot Hands
The tension in the building was thick enough to cut with a skate blade. With both goalies having stood on their heads all night to keep the score tight, it came down to the one-on-one showdown. 

In the high-stakes silence of the shootout, **Gabriel Kierath** proved he has ice water in his veins. He stepped up, deked the Hitmen netminder out of his boots, and tucked it home. That goal sealed the deal, awarding the Dirty Birds the extra point in a contest that felt much bigger than a standard regular-season matchup.

### The Aftermath
For the **Dirty Birds**, this victory is a statement. Sitting high in the standings, they’ve proven they can handle a brawl and come out on the right side of the ledger. They remain the team to beat in this circuit, showing a poise that championship squads are made of.

For **Hitmen Silver**, despite the loss, there is plenty to be proud of. They matched the league leaders shift-for-shift and proved that they can compete with the best of them. If they keep finding that kind of traffic in front of the net, they won't be in the "N/A" column for long.

The league is tightening up, folks. Grab your coffee, keep your stick on the ice, and we’ll see you at the rink next week. It’s only getting better from here.
