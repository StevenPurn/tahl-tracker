# Laser Hold the Line in 4-4 Deadlock Against Puckedelics

If you came to the rink expecting the league-leading Puckedelics to roll over the bottom-dwelling Laser, you clearly haven’t spent enough time watching hockey. On a chilly Tuesday night at the local barn, the Laser reminded everyone that the standings are just numbers on a piece of paper, skating to a hard-fought 4-4 draw against the division titans.

The Puckedelics entered the night riding a five-game heater, looking every bit the part of a championship favorite. They drew first blood at the 5:58 mark of the opening frame when Joe Baker found the back of the net. It looked like the script was following the expected trajectory, especially after a tripping minor sent Laser’s Timothy N See to the box early. But the Laser had other plans.

In a flurry of activity toward the end of the first period, David Mansfield turned the game on its head. Mansfield potted two goals in the span of sixteen seconds—a sequence that left the Puckedelics’ defense looking like they were still warming up their coffee. With helpers from Nic Poverud, See, and Nathan Hogue, the Laser skated into the break with a surprising 2-1 lead.

The middle frame, however, belonged to the league leaders. The Puckedelics tightened their laces and regained control, with Triston Wilson and Kirk Nielsen both finding twine to reclaim the advantage, 3-2. Wilson, who has been filling the net with remarkable consistency of late, looked every bit the playmaker as he set up Nielsen and later finished his own work.

The third period turned into a proper dogfight. Jeremy Kuzmarov leveled the score at 10:17, and just a few minutes later, Ronald Herwig buried a feed from Kuzmarov to put the Laser back in front 4-3. For a moment, it felt like the upset of the season was brewing. But Triston Wilson wasn't about to see his team's streak snapped so easily. With 9:18 remaining, Wilson struck again—his second of the night—to knot the score at four apiece.

"We knew they were flying high," said one exasperated spectator, who may or may not have been nursing a lukewarm stadium hot dog. "But the Laser played with some actual grit tonight. You give them an inch, they’re going to try to take the whole rink."

Neither team could find the final blow in the closing minutes, leading to a stalemate that leaves the Puckedelics with a share of the points but surely feeling a bit frustrated. For the Laser, who have spent much of this campaign struggling in the basement, this result has to feel like a moral victory. They played disciplined, played hard, and most importantly, they didn't fold when the league’s top dogs started pushing.

The Puckedelics move to 5-0-2, maintaining their position near the top of the pack, while the Laser scrape together a point to move to 1-4-2. If tonight is any indication, the rest of the league shouldn't be so quick to pencil in a win when they see the Laser on the schedule. Hockey is played on ice, not on a clipboard, and tonight, the ice was as level as it gets.
