# Bombers Drop the Hammer: Blue Bombers Hang Ten on the Hoes

**By: "Slap-Shot" Sammy, Hometown Hockey Beat Writer**
*June 30, 2026 — 11:45 PM*

If you were looking for a defensive stalemate tonight at the rink, you were looking in the wrong place. If you were looking for a statement game, however, you found it. The Blue Bombers didn’t just skate past the Average Hoes tonight—they took them to the woodshed in a 10-0 shutout that will leave the Hoes looking for answers and the Bombers looking like legit contenders.

### Period 1: The Blitzkrieg Begins
The Bombers came out of the gate with their hair on fire. It didn’t take long for the ice to tilt entirely in their favor. Aaron West struck first at the 10:22 mark, courtesy of a slick feed from Tyler Waller. That seemed to open the floodgates. Waller—who would go on to have an absolute career night—netted one of his own at 12:45, followed shortly by Hayden Sixsmith. 

Even when the Bombers took a trip to the sin bin for a cross-checking minor, the Hoes couldn't find a spark. In fact, it was the Bombers’ penalty kill that looked like the power play, with Joshua Pula burying a shorthanded beauty while Chris Olsen was still serving his time. By the time the horn sounded for the first break, the Hoes were staring down a mountain they had no climbing gear for.

### Period 2: Waller’s World
The second frame was more of the same, only with more traffic in front of the net. Tyler Waller continued his masterclass, finding the back of the twine twice more to secure a rare hat trick—and then some. 

The most impressive display of the night? Despite an unsportsmanlike conduct penalty midway through the period, the Bombers’ momentum never dipped. Joshua Pula grabbed his second of the night, and Ken Bebak added a highlight-reel finish. The Bombers were moving the puck like they were on a string, cycling the zone with the kind of chemistry that makes a beer-league scout's heart soar.

### Period 3: The Finishing Touch
With a 9-0 lead heading into the final frame, the Bombers could have been forgiven for taking their foot off the gas and coasting toward the parking lot. Instead, they kept the pressure on. Daron Ross hammered the final nail in the coffin at 11:16, silencing any hope for a Hoes comeback.

Credit where credit is due: the Bombers' netminder was a brick wall. Facing a barrage of pucks and keeping the sheet clean for a full sixty minutes is no small feat. It was a flawless performance from the crease out.

### The Aftermath
With this decisive 10-0 drubbing, the **Blue Bombers (3-2-0)** vault up the standings, proving that their recent "win-loss" rollercoaster is a thing of the past. They’ve got the wind in their sails, and with Tyler Waller playing at this level, they’re a team nobody in the league wants to see on their schedule next week.

For the **Average Hoes (0-5-0)**, it’s back to the drawing board. It’s been a tough slog to start the season, and tonight was a harsh lesson in transition speed and defensive structure. They’ll need to tighten up the gap control and find some rhythm before they drop further into the basement.

**Final Score:** 
**Blue Bombers: 10**
**Average Hoes: 0**
