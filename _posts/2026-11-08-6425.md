# Skate Goats Survive Late Laser Surge to Secure 5-3 Victory

It’s been a while since we’ve seen the Skate Goats play with the kind of clinical efficiency they displayed in the opening frame this Saturday afternoon. Facing off against the struggling Laser at the local rink, the Goats looked like a team intent on breaking out of their mid-season malaise, jumping out to a three-goal lead before most of the fans had finished their first cup of lukewarm concession coffee.

The Skate Goats dominated the opening period, turning the ice into their personal playground. Alex Ruiz got the party started just forty seconds into the game, burying a feed from Justin Eppler to set the tone. Dustin Giebel added to the tally five minutes later, and before the Laser could even catch their breath, Seth Miller sniped an unassisted goal to make it 3-0. 

"We were skating like we actually had somewhere to be after the game," joked one Goats bench regular between periods. "It was a nice change of pace from our usual habit of standing around watching the puck go by."

However, the Laser, clearly tired of being the team that everyone else gets their confidence boost against, came out of the locker room for the second period with a visible chip on their shoulder. After Travis Miller was sent to the box for hooking, the Laser finally found their spark. Timothy N See opened the scoring for the visitors, and Ronald Herwig—who seems intent on scoring every single game—quickly followed up to cut the lead to one. When Nathan Hogue leveled the score at 3-3 halfway through the period, you could practically hear the collective sigh of anxiety from the Goats' bench.

But just as the game threatened to slip away, Robert Hoffmann settled things down. With 8:37 gone in the middle frame, Hoffmann took matters into his own hands with an unassisted effort that restored the Goats' lead and seemed to take the wind out of the Laser’s sails.

The third period turned into a tighter, grittier affair, exactly the kind of hockey that separates the contenders from the bottom-dwellers. JOSH MOSLEY provided some much-needed insurance with ten minutes remaining, burying a pass from Travis Miller to push the lead to 5-3. A late tripping penalty against Laser’s Ben Castagna effectively snuffed out any hopes of a final comeback, sealing the two points for the Goats.

For the Skate Goats, the win is a necessary morale booster that keeps them hovering in the mid-pack scramble at 3-2-3. Meanwhile, the Laser remain stuck in the basement at 1-5-2. While the Laser showed legitimate heart clawing back from that 3-0 deficit, they ultimately lacked the defensive discipline to keep the Goats contained for a full forty-five minutes. 

It wasn’t always pretty, and there were enough defensive lapses in that second period to give any coach a mild heart attack, but in this league, a win is a win. The Goats will take the points and head home, leaving the Laser to figure out why they keep spotting their opponents a three-goal head start.
