# Stix-y Situation: Chix with Stix Put on a Scoring Clinic in 8-1 Rout of Power Puck Girls

**By: "Slap-Shot" Sammy, Hometown Beat Reporter**
**Date: July 29, 2026**

If you were looking for a quiet Wednesday evening at the rink, you clearly didn't get the memo. The ice at the local arena was scorched tonight as the Chix with Stix put on a clinical display of offensive firepower, steamrolling the Power Puck Girls 8-1 in a contest that had the fans buzzing from the opening puck drop to the final siren.

### First Period: Stix Find Their Rhythm Early
The Chix with Stix entered tonight looking to rebound after a tough loss, and they wasted no time proving they weren’t interested in a repeat performance. At the 2:12 mark, **Heather Cozad** continued her scorching hot streak, burying a beauty off a feed from **Mckenna N. Allison**. 

The Power Puck Girls were scrambling, and just under two minutes later, **Nicole Baker** capitalized on some heavy traffic in front of the net, doubling the lead to 2-0. The Stix were skating with the wind at their backs, cycling the puck with the precision of a Swiss watch.

### Second Period: The Floodgates Open
If the first period was a warm-up, the second was a full-blown monsoon. Nicole Baker picked up right where she left off, tucking her second of the night into the bottom corner at the 1:09 mark. 

The Power Puck Girls finally found a pulse midway through the frame when **Unknown**—who has been a force of nature with a three-game goal streak—found the back of the net off a dish from **Addison Gaut**. For a brief moment, you could hear the Power Puck faithful roar, sensing a potential momentum shift. But alas, **Carson See** had other plans. See netted her first of the night shortly after, effectively silencing the comeback bid and keeping the Chix firmly in the driver’s seat.

### Third Period: Closing the Books
The third period was all about the Stix cementing their status as the third seed. Carson See completed a stellar night by adding her second and third goals of the game—her second coming off a crisp setup from Catherine Pearsall, and the third a solo effort that showcased her silky smooth mitts. 

With the clock winding down, **Jordan Hawkins Baker** hammered home a goal at 9:54 to keep the pressure on, and **Heather Cozad** put a ribbon on the package with just 49.8 seconds left to play, notching her second of the night and extending her goal streak to three games.

### The Breakdown
This was a night where everything went right for the Chix with Stix and almost everything went sideways for the Power Puck Girls. The Stix’s puck movement was top-tier, consistently finding the "soft ice" and creating odd-man rushes that left the defense guessing. 

For the Power Puck Girls, it’s back to the drawing board. Sitting at 1-7-0, they’ll need to find a way to shore up the defensive zone if they want to climb out of the basement. Meanwhile, the Chix with Stix (now 5-3-0) move to 10 points on the season, firmly securing their grip on that third-place spot and looking like a team that’s finally found their rhythm heading into the back half of the schedule.
