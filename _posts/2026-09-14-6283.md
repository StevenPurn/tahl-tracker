# Hot Garbage Cleans Up: Fossil Hockey Swept Aside in 7-3 Rout

If you looked at the standings before puck drop at the rink this Saturday, you’d have seen two teams hovering in the middle of the pack, both looking to shake off the rust of a season-opening draw. But by the time the final buzzer rang, it was clear that one team had arrived with a broom and the other was still looking for their skates. Hot Garbage lived up to anything but their namesake, steamrolling Fossil Hockey to the tune of a 7-3 victory.

The tone for the evening was set early in the first period. After an early trip to the sin bin for Fossil’s Kelly Rundle, the Hot Garbage offense started to hum like a well-oiled machine. John Bewley drew first blood with just under a minute left in the frame, followed quickly by a solo effort from Joshua Hall. By the time the Zamboni did its first loop, Fossil Hockey was staring down a 2-0 deficit and wondering where the puck had gone.

The second period turned into a full-blown barnburner. Zachary Hughes kept his hot hand moving, tucking away two goals to pad the Hot Garbage lead, while Aaron Whisner and Nicholas Brewer added markers of their own. To their credit, Fossil Hockey didn’t just roll over. Dave Griffiths finally broke the seal for them, and Kelly Rundle managed to erase some of the memory of his earlier penalty by burying one off a feed from Logan Pardue. Enoch Cox tacked on another for Fossil just before the buzzer, but it was a bit like trying to stop a freight train with a screen door.

"We just couldn't find our defensive rhythm," said one anonymous Fossil Hockey skater while untying his skates in the locker room. "Every time we looked up, the Garbage were finding a new lane. It was like they were playing on a larger sheet of ice than we were."

The third period settled into a more grind-it-out affair, characterized by a bit of extracurricular chippiness that saw LC Sinor and Enoch Cox trade seats in the penalty box for simultaneous infractions. With the game well in hand, Aaron Whisner put the cherry on top with his second goal of the night—his third point of the contest—sealing the 7-3 win.

For Hot Garbage, this victory is a massive confidence booster, proving they have the offensive depth to be a genuine nuisance for the top-tier teams in the league. As for Fossil Hockey, it’s back to the drawing board. They’ll need to tighten up the gaps and tighten their belts if they want to climb out of the basement of the standings. 

It was a tough night for the fossils, but that’s local hockey for you—sometimes you’re the hammer, and sometimes you’re the nail. And tonight, the Garbage took their turn swinging the hammer.
