# Reapers Reap the Rewards in 3-1 Win Over Ice Breakers Plus

If you like your hockey served with a side of hard work and a dash of grit, Sunday morning’s tilt between the Reapers and the Ice Breakers Plus was exactly what the doctor ordered. In a matchup that felt more like a chess match played on ice, the Reapers managed to grind out a 3-1 victory, leaving the Ice Breakers looking for answers as the season starts to heat up.

The game started with a frantic pace that suggested neither team had quite shaken off their morning coffee. It took less than eleven minutes for the scoreboard to start moving, with Jose Herrick finding the back of the net for the Reapers. The lead was short-lived, however. Just thirty-four seconds later, Tyler Waller—carrying his scoring momentum from his last outing—evened the score for the Ice Breakers, assisted by Benjamin Koelsch. It was a brief moment of offensive flair in a game that was otherwise destined to be won in the corners.

The defensive tone was set for the remainder of the first period, but the Reapers found the decisive edge at the 6:20 mark. Dickie Williams cleaned up a beautiful setup from Patrick Flannery and Sanford Silman, tucking it home to reclaim the lead. 

"We stopped chasing the puck and started playing the man," said one anonymous veteran on the Reapers' bench. "It wasn't pretty, but it was honest work."

The second period turned into a exercise in discipline—or the lack thereof. With the Ice Breakers fighting to stay within striking distance and the Reapers trying to stifle any momentum, the whistles began to blow. A tripping call on Jose Herrick gave the Ice Breakers a glimmer of hope, but they couldn't capitalize. Later, a bench minor for 'Too Many Men' on the Ice Breakers—a cardinal sin in any era—took the wind out of their sails just when they needed a push.

The third period was all about the Reapers’ ability to protect a lead. They clamped down defensively, turning the neutral zone into a graveyard for the Ice Breakers' rushes. Any hope for a comeback was effectively extinguished at the 13:08 mark, when Matthew Palmer fired an unassisted goal to push the lead to 3-1. 

By the time Sean Widdows headed to the box for roughing late in the third, the energy had largely drained from the rink. The Ice Breakers fought until the final buzzer, but they were consistently met with a wall of Reapers jerseys.

For the Reapers, the win is a much-needed shot in the arm, moving them up from the bottom of the table and snapping their winless skid. As for the Ice Breakers Plus, they’ll have to head back to the drawing board to find a way to generate more offense when the pressure is on. It’s a long season, and if today proved anything, it’s that in this league, you’re only as good as your next shift.
