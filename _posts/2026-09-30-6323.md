# Bone Zone Puts the Squeeze on Laser in High-Octane Affair

If you were looking for a quiet, defensive struggle at the rink on Tuesday night, you were in the wrong zip code. The Bone Zone and the Laser combined for an 11-goal fireworks show that left the ice feeling more like a pinball machine than a hockey rink. When the dust finally settled on this regular-season tilt, The Bone Zone walked away with a 7-4 victory, proving that when they find their rhythm, they can be as stubborn as a rusty bolt.

The Laser struck first, barely a minute into the game, when Matthew Rudick found the back of the net off feeds from Robert Verity and Nic Poverud. It was a beautiful start for the #12 seed, but it seems all they did was poke the bear. The Bone Zone responded with a vengeance, rattling off a string of goals that turned a shaky start into a commanding performance. Jonathan Lee kicked things off for the Bone Zone, followed by the first of two tallies from Jeffrey Greco, as the boys in the away sweaters started to dictate the flow of the game.

The middle frame was arguably the back-breaker. Tyler Wimpee continued his scoring streak with a clinical finish early in the period, and when Greco potted his second of the night just over a minute later, the Laser looked like they were skating through wet concrete. Garrett Williams added a dagger later in the period, extending the Bone Zone lead and forcing the Laser to spend the rest of the night playing catch-up.

To their credit, the Laser didn’t just pack their gear and head for the exits. Despite Robert Verity sitting in the box for a hooking minor early in the third, he managed to make an impact the moment he stepped back on the ice, lighting the lamp at the 1:26 mark. But every time the Laser threatened to spark a comeback, The Bone Zone extinguished it with cold efficiency. Williams notched his second of the night, and a late marker from the Bone Zone—credited to an anonymous hero—pretty much put this one on ice, despite a spirited two-goal surge from Kyle Verity and Matthew Rudick in the final minutes.

"We just kept finding the soft spots in the coverage," said one rinkside regular, sipping a lukewarm coffee while watching the teams shake hands. "The Bone Zone looked like a team that had a lunch date with the net."

For The Bone Zone, the win is a massive boost, keeping their momentum rolling as they navigate the mid-tier of the standings. The Laser, meanwhile, are left looking for answers after falling to 0-2-1. It’s early in the season, and as any veteran hockey fan knows, you can’t win the championship in September, but you sure can dig yourself a hole. The Laser will need to tighten the screws on defense if they want to stop letting these high-scoring affairs slip through their fingers. 

As for The Bone Zone? They’re heading home with a win and a whole lot of confidence. They played like a team that knows exactly where they’re going, and tonight, that was straight to the top of the stat sheet.
