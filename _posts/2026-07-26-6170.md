# Goat-ing for Gold: Killer Goats Pummel Puckheads in Sunday Morning Shellacking

**July 26, 2026** – If you were hoping for a sleepy Sunday morning skate, you clearly weren’t at the rink today. The Killer Goats rolled into the barn with a hunger for points, and the Puckheads unfortunately found themselves on the wrong side of a 6-1 massacre that showcased exactly why the Goats are currently sitting pretty near the top of the league standings.

### First Period: The DCD Show Begins
The Puckheads started the game looking to snap their four-game slide, but Dylan Criquet Danielson had other plans. At the 11:03 mark of the opening frame, Danielson found the back of the net off a crisp feed from Dustin Giebel, signaling that the Goats’ top scorers were feeling dangerous. 

The Goats kept the pressure on, turning the defensive zone into a pinball machine. With just 1:38 left in the period, the Lucas Blanchard-to-Danielson connection clicked, giving the Goats a 2-0 cushion heading into the break. While Brian Smith headed to the box for an interference minor at the 5:46 mark, the Puckheads’ power play looked as stagnant as a pond in mid-July, unable to capitalize on the man advantage.

### Second Period: The Floodgates Open
If the first period was a drizzle, the second was a monsoon. The Goats came out of the tunnel with their skates on fire. 

Travis Miller kicked off the scoring barrage at 14:36, thanks to some lovely setup work from Michael Bergstrom and Giebel. From there, it was a highlight reel of chemistry: Samuel Michaud buried one at 3:06, and just over a minute later, the irrepressible Danielson completed his hat trick at 4:34, courtesy of Blanchard and Giebel again. 

By the time Michael Doyle added his name to the scoresheet at 7:04—cashing in on a beautiful sequence from Danielson and Blanchard—the Goats had built an insurmountable 6-0 lead. The Puckheads’ goalies were under siege all period, facing a relentless barrage of traffic in front of the crease that would make even a seasoned veteran sweat through their sweater.

### Third Period: A Scrap of Honor
To their credit, the Puckheads didn’t pack their bags and head for the locker room early. They spent the third period grinding, and at 7:02, Gregory Douglas finally solved the puzzle, tipping home a pass from Jamie Halverson to put the Puckheads on the board. 

It was a goal of pride, preventing the shutout, but it was far too little, far too late. The final buzzer sounded with the scoreboard reading 6-1 in favor of the visitors.

### The Aftermath
For the Killer Goats, this victory is a statement. With a 6-2-1 record, they are proving they have the depth and the offensive firepower to hang with the Dirty Birds at the very top of the hierarchy. The chemistry between Giebel, Blanchard, and Danielson—who extended his point streak to a whopping seven games—is the stuff of nightmares for opposing defensive units.

For the Puckheads, it’s back to the drawing board. Falling to 3-6-0, the team is struggling to find consistency in their own end. They’ll need to tighten up their gaps and find a way to generate more offense than a single goal if they want to climb out of the basement of the standings. 

The Goats leave the rink today looking like contenders; the Puckheads leave looking for answers. That’s the beautiful, brutal reality of the game. We’ll see you at the next one—don't forget your earplugs; this league is only getting louder.
