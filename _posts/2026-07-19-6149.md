# A Sunday Morning Thriller: Power Puck Girls Finally Crack the Win Column!

**By: "Slap-Shot" Sammy, Hometown Beat Reporter**
*July 19, 2026 | 10:15 AM Puck Drop*

If you were sleeping in this Sunday morning, you missed the absolute barn burner of the season. The rink was icy, the coffee was strong, and the tension was thicker than a Zamboni’s wake. The Mother Puckers rolled in looking to climb the standings, but the Power Puck Girls had other plans, finally snapping their losing skid in a 4-3 heart-stopper that had the spectators on the edge of their seats until the final horn.

### First Period: Trading Punches
The game started with the intensity of a playoff final. The Mother Puckers drew first blood at the 1:11 mark when Joe Baker buried a beauty, courtesy of a sharp feed from Elizabeth Johnson. It looked like the Puckers were ready to cruise, but the Power Puck Girls—staring down an 0-6-0 record—showed the heart of a lion. They kept the crease crowded and eventually forced a breakthrough when "Unknown" lit the lamp at 7:28 to level the score. Both netminders settled in after that, turning away quality chances and keeping the score knotted at 1-1 heading into the second frame.

### Second Period: The Tactical Chess Match
The second period saw both squads tighten up their defensive zones. It was a classic "keep the biscuit out of the basket" affair. Neither side gave an inch, and the goalies were putting on a clinic, flashing the leather and keeping the game deadlocked. It was a defensive slog that left the fans breathless.

### Third Period: The Wild West
If the second period was a chess match, the third was a full-blown hockey brawl of scoring. 

The Mother Puckers regained the lead at 11:06 thanks to a Kalea Maxwell strike, with Nicole Baker doing the heavy lifting on the assist. But just as momentum seemed to swing toward the Puckers, Elizabeth Johnson headed to the sin bin for a high-sticking infraction. The Power Puck Girls capitalized on the chaos. Lauren A Mincey found the back of the net at 14:25 to tie it up, and before the Puckers could catch their breath, "Unknown" struck again for the Girls, handing them their first lead of the game!

The drama wasn't finished. Elizabeth Johnson, fresh off her penalty, redeemed herself in a big way by burying a feed from Maxwell and Baker at 2:21 to tie the game at 3-3. But the Power Puck Girls weren't going home empty-handed today. With the clock ticking down, "Unknown" completed their hat trick, burying the game-winner at 3:49 to seal the 4-3 victory.

### What It Means
The Power Puck Girls finally have a "W" in the win column! While they remain in sixth place, this victory breathes fresh air into their locker room and proves they can skate with the best of them. 

For the Mother Puckers (2-4-1), it’s a tough pill to swallow. They’ll need to tighten up their defensive discipline if they want to move up from their fourth-place perch. With the Valkyries and the Tulsa Riveters setting the pace at the top, every point in the standings is a battle. 

One thing is for sure: Sunday morning hockey never felt so electric. Until next time, keep your sticks on the ice and your eyes on the puck!
