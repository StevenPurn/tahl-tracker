# Bone Zone Digs Deep to Stifle Jackalopes in Late-Night Thriller

If you were looking for a quiet, defensive chess match on a Tuesday night, the 9:30 p.m. puck drop between The Bone Zone and the Jackalopes was not the place to be. It was a messy, high-octane affair that ultimately saw The Bone Zone claw their way back from a three-goal deficit to steal a 5-4 victory, proving once again that in this league, a lead is just a suggestion.

The Jackalopes came out looking like they’d had an extra cup of coffee before the opening whistle. Todd Thompson and James Whitacre were playing a two-man game that had the Bone Zone defenders chasing shadows for the better part of two periods. Thompson opened the scoring early, followed by a Whitacre tally at the 7:10 mark. When Whitacre buried his second of the night with just three seconds remaining in the second frame, the Jackalopes headed to the locker room with a comfortable 3-0 lead and a collective strut that suggested this one was in the bag.

"We were playing like we were skating in quicksand," said one Bone Zone skater, who requested anonymity—mostly because he was too embarrassed to admit how poorly they started. "We had to wake up or start looking for a new hobby."

The wakeup call arrived in the third period, and it was loud. The floodgates didn't just open; they were ripped off the hinges. Garrett Williams got the ball rolling just over a minute into the third, aided by some crisp passing from Jamie Halverson and Timothy N See. That goal seemed to rattle the Jackalopes, who suddenly found themselves spending more time in the penalty box than in the offensive zone. 

The momentum shift was palpable. James DePriest, looking to make up for a high-sticking minor earlier in the night, hammered one home at the 14:34 mark. Not two minutes later, Halverson—who was arguably the most dangerous man on the ice in the final frame—found the back of the net to pull the score to a 3-3 deadlock. 

The Jackalopes tried to reclaim the narrative when Todd Thompson snagged his second of the night, courtesy of a slick feed from Robert Verity, but the Bone Zone machine was already at full steam. Jonathan Lee provided the equalizer shortly thereafter, and with the clock winding down, the Bone Zone managed to lock things down defensively to secure the 5-4 comeback.

The loss is a tough pill to swallow for the Jackalopes, who saw their discipline crumble under the pressure of the third-period barrage. A late delay-of-game penalty by Joshua Kerr pretty much sealed their fate, effectively snuffing out any hope for a last-minute miracle.

For The Bone Zone, the win keeps their strong momentum alive as they maintain their position in the upper echelon of the standings. They aren't the flashiest team in the league, but they’ve got the grit to grind out wins when the chips are down. As for the Jackalopes, they’ll have to go back to the drawing board and figure out how to play a full 45 minutes of hockey, rather than just the first 30. 

It was a classic late-night barnburner that left the fans happy and the ice crew exhausted. Just another Tuesday in the local circuit.
