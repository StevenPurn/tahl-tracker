# Havok Storms Past Blizzard in a 4-3 Nail-Biter

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: June 22, 2026**

If you weren’t at the arena tonight, you missed a barn burner that had the ice steaming and the glass rattling from the opening drop. The Havok came into tonight looking to climb the standings, and they didn’t just survive the Blizzard—they weathered the storm to secure a 4-3 victory in a contest that had enough heart-pounding tension to last a full season.

### Period 1: The Early Strike
The Havok set the tone early, proving they weren’t here to skate in circles. With just 20.5 seconds remaining in the opening frame, Jered Pieschke struck gold. After a beautiful setup from the red-hot Zachary Wade, Pieschke found the back of the net to put Havok up 1-0. It was the kind of momentum-shifting play that sends a team into the locker room with a little extra pep in their step.

### Period 2: Heavy Hitters and Tactical Play
The middle frame was a scrappy affair. Havok’s Robert Verity found himself cooling his heels in the sin bin for a cross-checking minor at 11:29, but the Havok penalty kill unit looked like a brick wall, denying the Blizzard any breathing room. 

As the pressure mounted, Michael Bergstrom capitalized on a dish from Wade at 3:58, burying it to extend the lead to 2-0. It was a textbook display of "traffic in front of the net," with Bergstrom showing the grit required to finish the dirty work in the paint.

### Period 3: The Goal-Scoring Carousel
The third period? Absolute bedlam. 

Gary Hennenfent kicked things off for the Blizzard, slicing the lead in half at the 10:39 mark off a helper from Dayne Wall. But Havok’s Zachary Wade—who is currently playing like a man possessed—responded with a vengeance. He bagged his first of the night at 13:36, assisted by Bergstrom, only to find the twine again at 6:35 off a crisp pass from Ben Gartner. 

Just when the crowd thought the Havok were sailing home, Garrett Williams decided to put the Blizzard on his back. Williams went on a tear, scoring at 6:45 and then again at 9:41—the latter coming on the heels of a Jared Conway tripping penalty. With the score locked at 4-3, the final minutes were a pure heart-attack special, with bodies flying and sticks clattering as the Blizzard threw the kitchen sink at the Havok crease. 

Ultimately, the Havok defense held the line, suffocating the Blizzard’s final surge to walk away with the two points.

### The Aftermath
Tonight’s result is a massive statement for Havok. By moving to 3-0-1, they continue to solidify their position near the top of the standings, keeping the pressure on the Killer Goats and the Dirty Birds. Wade’s continued dominance—extending his point streak and showing that he’s arguably the most dangerous skater in the league right now—is the engine driving this Havok squad toward a serious title run.

For the Blizzard, it’s a tough pill to swallow. While Garrett Williams showed flashes of brilliance and pure scoring instinct, they’ll need to find more consistency in their own zone if they want to climb out of that #6 spot.

The ice has melted, the Zamboni has made its rounds, and the Havok are heading home with a win that proves they are a force to be reckoned with. Keep your skates sharp, folks—the road to the playoffs is just heating up!
