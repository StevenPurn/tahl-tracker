# Berzerkers Stave Off Syndicate Rally in High-Octane Thriller

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: June 21, 2026**

If you weren’t at the arena tonight, I’m sorry to tell you that you missed a genuine barn burner. The Syndicate came into tonight’s matchup hungry for their first win of the season, but the Berzerkers proved they aren't ready to yield an inch of ice to anyone. When the final buzzer sounded, it was the Berzerkers walking away with a gritty 4-3 victory, leaving the Syndicate still searching for that elusive first ‘W.’

### Period 1: The Hughes-Wilmeth Connection
The Berzerkers came out with their hair on fire. It was clear early on that Zac Hughes and Evert Wilmeth are currently playing a game of catch that nobody else on the ice can solve. At the 11:01 mark, Wilmeth feathered a gorgeous pass to Hughes, who buried it to draw first blood. 

The Syndicate looked a bit shell-shocked, and Hughes wasn’t done. With less than a minute remaining in the frame—at the 45.7-second mark, to be exact—the duo struck again, doubling the lead before the Zamboni even had a chance to think about the intermission. It looked like a long night ahead for the Syndicate, especially after Jeffrey Rosencutter headed to the box for tripping, but they couldn't capitalize on the power play before the horn.

### Period 2: Trading Paint
The Syndicate finally found their legs in the second. At 11:03, Logan Pardue managed to solve the Berzerkers’ defense, tapping in a beautiful feed from Daron Ross to cut the lead in half. 

The momentum shift was palpable, but the Berzerkers are a team that thrives on chaos. Just a few minutes later, the recently penalized Rosencutter made up for his trip to the sin bin by lighting the lamp himself, assisted by Dylan Calhoun. But credit where it’s due: The Syndicate refused to roll over. With just 58.9 seconds left in the period, Brett Mooney hammered home a John Hackett setup, dragging the score to 3-2 heading into the final frame.

### Period 3: The Hat-Trick Heroics
The third period was absolute carnage in the trenches. With tempers flaring and the game on the line, Edward Stern found himself serving two minutes for tripping. The Syndicate pressed, but they couldn’t find the equalizer. 

Instead, it was the Zac Hughes show yet again. At 13:26, Hughes managed a solo effort, weaving through traffic like a slalom skier and roofing it top shelf where mom keeps the cookies. That unassisted tally looked like the dagger. 

The Syndicate made a frantic push, and Tracy Lowe gave them a glimmer of hope with 5:42 left, assisted by Kacy Griffin. The final minutes were pure desperation—bodies flying, sticks hacking, and the Syndicate goalie pulled in a frantic search for the equalizer—but the Berzerkers’ defensive wall held firm.

### The Aftermath
For the Berzerkers, this win is vital. Sitting at 3-1-0 now, they are asserting themselves as a legitimate force in the middle of the pack, keeping pace with the upper echelon of the standings. 

As for The Syndicate? They move to 0-4-0. It’s a tough pill to swallow, especially with such a spirited third-period comeback effort, but the talent is clearly there. They just need to stop digging those early holes. 

The puck drops again soon, and if tonight was any indication, this league is only going to get tighter. Keep your sticks on the ice, folks—this season is just heating up!
