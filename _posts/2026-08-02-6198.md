# Riveting Performance: Tulsa Hammers the Power Puck Girls 7-2

**TULSA** – If you were looking for a masterclass in puck possession and surgical finishing, you should’ve been at the rink this afternoon. The Tulsa Riveters (7-0-2) reminded everyone why they’re the team nobody wants to face in the playoffs, dismantling the Power Puck Girls (1-8-0) in a 7-2 rout that felt every bit as dominant as the scoreboard suggests.

The Riveters, currently locked in a heated battle for the top seed, skated with the kind of confidence that makes you wonder if they’ve got magnets in their sticks.

### Period 1: The Offensive Fireworks
The game started with a frantic pace that would make a pinball machine blush. The Riveters struck first at the 10:11 mark when **Theresa Rose Rabara** buried a beauty, courtesy of a slick feed from the red-hot **Gabrielle Gibson**.

The Power Puck Girls, to their credit, didn’t roll over. "Unknown" (who brought a four-game goal streak into this tilt) answered back at 1:02, and after Gibson extended the lead for Tulsa, Kristin Brooks punched one in for the visitors to keep it tight at 3-2. However, **Ivy Emerson**—playing like she’s got rocket fuel in her skates—found the twine with two minutes left in the frame to give the Riveters a 3-2 lead heading into the first intermission. It was a barn burner, but the Riveters were just getting the engine warmed up.

### Period 2: Emerson Takes Control
The second period was a gritty, grinding affair. We saw a parade to the box with penalties on Bianka Diaz and Barbara Lepe Briones, but the special teams battle ultimately broke in Tulsa's favor. 

At the 9:56 mark, **Ivy Emerson** notched her second of the night, showing off some nifty hands in the "dirty areas." It was a classic "traffic in front of the net" goal—the kind that makes goalies lose their sleep and coaches lose their voices. That goal effectively took the wind out of the Power Puck Girls’ sails, pushing the lead to 4-2.

### Period 3: Closing the Curtain
If the first two periods were the appetizer, the third was an all-you-can-eat buffet of offense for Tulsa. 

**Gabrielle Gibson** opened the final frame with a silky-smooth finish at 1:24, followed by **Ivy Emerson** completing her hat-trick performance at 12:40, assisted by the ever-reliable Barbara Lepe Briones and Amanda Long. By the time **Amanda Long** went coast-to-coast for an unassisted beauty at 7:04, the Power Puck Girls were just looking for the final whistle.

### The Bottom Line
For the Riveters, this win is a massive statement. They remain unbeaten in regulation, keeping the pressure squarely on the first-place Valkyries. With **Gabrielle Gibson** extending her goal-scoring streak to a staggering seven games and **Ivy Emerson** finding the back of the net with terrifying consistency, the Riveters are looking less like a hockey team and more like a wrecking ball.

The Power Puck Girls, unfortunately, find themselves digging a deeper hole at the bottom of the standings. While they showed flashes of spark early on, they simply couldn't withstand the relentless pressure of a Tulsa team that seems to be hitting their stride right as the season enters its most critical stretch.

The ice might be melted, but the Riveters are still white-hot. See you at the next one!
