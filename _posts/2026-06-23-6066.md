# REAPERS HARVEST FIRST WIN: Ice Cold No More!

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: June 22, 2026**

If you were looking for a sign that the standings were about to get shaken up, you should’ve been at the barn tonight. Entering the 8:45 p.m. puck drop, the Reapers were the only ones still searching for their first tick in the "W" column, sitting at a hungry 0-3. But tonight, the Grim Reaper didn't just show up—he brought a hat trick of goals and a whole lot of bad news for the Kraken Cold Ones.

In a 5-2 victory that finally got the goose egg off their record, the Reapers proved that you can’t keep a good team down forever.

### The First: Opening the Floodgates
The Reapers came out of the gate skating like they had a fire under their laces. They established the "traffic in front of the net" early, making the Kraken’s crease look like rush hour on the highway. 

At 4:26, **Dennis Jackson** finally broke the seal, burying one off a nifty feed from **Lewis Prevost**. Not content with just being a playmaker, Prevost decided to take matters into his own hands at 8:40, finding the back of the net unassisted to put the Reapers up 2-0. The Kraken looked dazed, struggling to find an answer for the high-octane pressure the Reapers were throwing their way.

### The Second: Jackson’s Masterclass
If the first period was a wakeup call, the second was a masterclass. **Chance DeMier** kept the momentum rolling at 13:16, tucking one home thanks to a brilliant dish from Jackson. 

Then, at 14:31, the Jackson show continued. He linked up with **Dickie Williams** to put the Reapers up by four. By this point, the Reapers were playing "tic-tac-toe" in the offensive zone, leaving the Kraken defenders looking like they were trying to catch smoke with a butterfly net.

### The Third: A Late Surge and the Final Nail
To their credit, the Kraken Cold Ones didn’t just pack up their gear and head to the parking lot. They clawed back with a quick 1-2 punch in the third. **STEVEN CHERN**—riding that one-game goal streak—sniped one home at 12:54, and **Ryan Martinez** followed it up just 17 seconds later to narrow the gap to 4-2. 

For a heartbeat, the atmosphere in the arena shifted. Could the Kraken mount a comeback? 

The Reapers said, "Not tonight." With just 1.9 seconds remaining on the clock, **Robert Norris** put the cherry on top of the sundae, roofing a pass from Jackson to finalize the score at 5-2. It was the perfect ending to a night where the Reapers finally found their rhythm.

### The Bottom Line
For the Reapers, this is a massive morale boost. Breaking that 0-3 skid changes the locker room dynamic entirely; they finally know what it feels like to leave the ice as the victors. 

For the Kraken Cold Ones, it’s a tough pill to swallow. Sitting at 1-3, they’ll need to tighten up the defensive coverage if they want to climb out of the bottom half of the standings. As for the Reapers? They’ve officially entered the chat. Watch out, league—the reaper is back in the business of winning hockey games. 

**Three Stars of the Night:**
1. **Dennis Jackson (Reapers):** A goal and three assists. Need we say more?
2. **Lewis Prevost (Reapers):** A constant menace in the slot.
3. **STEVEN CHERN (Kraken):** Kept the Kraken dream alive, if only for a few minutes.
