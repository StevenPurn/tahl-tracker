# Gilded Dominance: Gold as Ice Crushes the Kraken in Late-Night Thriller

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: June 18, 2026**

If you found yourself at the rink at 10:15 PM on a Tuesday, you weren’t there for a quiet skate. You were there to witness the runaway freight train that is **Gold as Ice**. Entering tonight sitting pretty at the top of the standings, they didn't just defend their throne—they reinforced it with a 6-2 beatdown of the Kraken Cold Ones.

### Period 1: The Gold Rush Begins
The Kraken tried to bring the pressure early, but Gold as Ice looked like they were skating on a different surface entirely. Just 2:25 into the frame, **Dickie Williams** kept his hot streak alive, lighting the lamp off a crisp feed from **Taylor Chinowth**. 

The Kraken’s discipline started to fray shortly after, with Jeremy Wentworth heading to the box for tripping. Gold as Ice smelled blood in the water. With just under two minutes left in the period, the connection of **ADEN INGRAM** and **Tyler Mills** clicked, putting the puck home. Before the Kraken could even find their breath, **MARK DESIMONE**—who has been an absolute force of nature lately—potted another one just 27 seconds later, assisted by Williams. By the time the horn sounded, Gold as Ice had a 3-0 lead and all the momentum in the building.

### Period 2: Stretching the Lead
The second period was a defensive clinic, but Gold as Ice wasn't done on the scoreboard. **Daniel Maxwell** added to the tally at the 12:36 mark, once again finding the back of the net thanks to a pinpoint pass from Ingram. 

The Kraken netminder was doing his best to keep the score from becoming truly embarrassing, fighting through heavy traffic in front of the crease, but the Gold as Ice offense was like a swarm of hornets—relentless, buzzing, and stinging from every angle.

### Period 3: Fireworks and Finality
The final frame turned into a bit of a back-and-forth affair, giving the Kraken fans a flicker of hope. **Kirk Flemings** finally solved the Gold defense with less than a minute left in the period, followed by a **Steven Chern** goal midway through the third. 

But every time the Kraken dared to inch closer, Mark DeSimone reminded them who runs this league. DeSimone went on a tear, scoring two more goals in the final minutes—both aided by the dynamic duo of Ingram and Williams. DeSimone’s hat trick performance was the icing on a very golden cake, as he danced through the Kraken defense like they were standing in wet cement.

### The Aftermath
When the final buzzer sounded, the 6-2 scoreline told the story of a team playing with absolute confidence. 

For **Gold as Ice**, this win keeps them firmly in the driver’s seat. They’ve proven that their 3-0-0 start wasn't just beginner's luck; they are the team to beat in this league. With Ingram extending his points streak to four games and DeSimone playing at a legendary pace, they look like a team on a mission to hoist the trophy at the end of the year.

For the **Kraken Cold Ones**, tonight was a reality check. Sitting at 1-2-0, they’ll need to shore up their defensive coverage if they want to stop the bleeding and climb out of the bottom half of the standings. 

But tonight? Tonight belonged to the Gold. If you weren't there, you missed a masterclass in puck possession and finishing. See you at the rink!
