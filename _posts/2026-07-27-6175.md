# Anatomy of a Rout: The Bone Zone Dismantles Laser in 7-2 Barn Burner

**TULSA** – If you were looking for a quiet, defensive chess match on a Friday night, you picked the wrong barn. At the 8:30 p.m. puck drop, The Bone Zone and Laser entered the fray locked in a desperate battle for middle-of-the-pack supremacy. By the time the final horn sounded, the scoreboard told a story of pure, unadulterated offensive dominance. 

The Bone Zone didn’t just beat Laser 7-2; they put on a clinic in puck possession and transition speed, effectively turning the ice into their own personal playground.

### First Period: The Calm Before the Storm
The Bone Zone wasted zero time announcing their intentions. Just 78 seconds in, **Jerry Bates** found the back of the net off a beauty of a feed from **Richard Looney**. It was the kind of goal that takes the wind right out of a visitor’s sails. 

Laser, fighting to keep their three-game point streak alive, hunkered down. After a rough trip to the box for **Chad Pancoast**, Laser finally found a pulse late in the period. The league’s hottest hand, **Colton Taylor**, extended his personal goal-scoring streak to eight games with a sniper’s finish, knotting things up at 1-1. It was a momentum-shifting play that promised a tight, gritty remainder of the night. 

### Second Period: The Floodgates Open
If the first period was a skirmish, the second was an absolute barrage. The physical intensity spiked when The Bone Zone’s **Eric Roepke** took a seat for roughing, but the penalty kill backfired on Laser. Instead of capitalizing on the power play, Laser surrendered a shorthanded beauty to **John White**.

The game turned into a track meet immediately after. **Robert Verity** continued his personal tear by answering for Laser just eight seconds later, but that was the last gasp for the boys in Laser blue. The Bone Zone’s depth began to show as **Jonathan Lee** undressed the defense for an unassisted tally, followed shortly by a second strike from **John White**, who was feeling it like he was playing against traffic cones. By the end of the second, The Bone Zone had built a 4-2 cushion, and you could feel the energy being sucked out of the Laser bench.

### Third Period: Closing the Vault
With a two-goal lead, most teams might shift into a defensive shell. The Bone Zone? They stepped on the gas.

**Lance Levasseur** opened the third with a solo effort that effectively put the game on ice, and **Richard Looney** added to his multi-point night with a strike off a sweet dish from Roepke. To add insult to injury, Jerry Bates bagged his second of the night at the 6:52 mark. By the time the final buzzer rang, Laser’s defense looked like they’d been chasing shadows all night.

### The Aftermath
For The Bone Zone (5-3-1, 11 pts), this victory is a massive statement. They leapfrog their way up the standings, proving they have the offensive firepower to hang with anyone in the top tier. They’ve successfully climbed over Laser in the standings, creating a critical buffer as we head into the dog days of the season.

For Laser (4-4-1, 9 pts), the loss is a tough pill to swallow, especially with their momentum coming to a screeching halt. While Colton Taylor’s record-setting scoring streak remains the silver lining for the fans, Laser will need to tighten up the blue line before their next outing. 

The Bone Zone proved tonight that when they are clicking, they aren't just playing the game—they’re owning the ice. We’ll see if they can carry this momentum into their next tilt, but for tonight, the town is firmly in the Zone.
