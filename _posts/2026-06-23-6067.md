# Joes Go Berserk: Average Joes Turn The Rookery Into A Roast

**By: Our Hometown Hockey Beat Reporter**
*June 23, 2026*

If you caught the late-night action at the rink this past Monday, you might want to check your eyes—because what we witnessed in that 10:15 PM tilt was nothing short of a total offensive takeover. The **Average Joes** didn't just walk into The Rookery; they hung up a "Closed for Renovations" sign, putting up a nine-spot in a 9-2 demolition that left The Rookery scrambling for cover.

### First Period: The Calm Before the Storm
The game started with a bit of a back-and-forth rhythm. Joshua Hendrickson opened the account for the Joes at the 12:48 mark, set up by a crafty feed from Tracy Wilmes. The Rookery pushed back, answering at 2:54 when Joe Hubert redirected a beauty from Gage Walker and Travis Shuck to knot it at 1-1. 

However, Abigail Nisbet decided she wasn't done for the night, capitalizing on some traffic in front of the net at 8:24 to send the Joes into the break with a 2-1 lead. It looked like a tight contest, but little did we know, the dam was about to break.

### Second Period: The Joes’ Scoring Clinic
If you blinked, you missed a goal. The second frame was a masterclass in puck movement and sheer offensive willpower by the Average Joes. 

Tracy Wilmes kicked off the carnage at 13:41, followed quickly by Aidan Caldron at the 20.5-second mark (yes, you read that right, a literal blink-of-an-eye goal). Nisbet grabbed her second of the night at 2:47, and Sergio Castellanos joined the party just 30 seconds later. 

By the time Jordan Hawkins Baker—who extended his goal streak to three games—and Tracy Wilmes (who netted her second of the night) found the twine, the Joes had exploded for six goals in the second period alone. The Rookery’s defense looked like they were trying to skate in quicksand, and the Joes were finding the top shelf where mom keeps the cookies with reckless abandon.

### Third Period: Closing the Books
With the lead well in hand, the Joes stayed disciplined. Joshua Hendrickson tacked on his second of the night at 4:03, pushing the lead to 9-1. The Rookery managed to salvage a bit of pride at 5:42 when Gage Walker found the back of the net off a solid setup from Glenn Prouse and Christopher Guffey, but by then, the barn had already burned down.

### The Takeaway
The stats tell the story of a team hitting its stride. Jordan Hawkins Baker’s incredible goal-scoring form continues, and the playmaking synergy between the Wilmes family and the rest of the Joes' core is proving to be a nightmare for opposing goaltenders.
