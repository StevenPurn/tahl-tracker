# Bison Charge Past Danglers in Late-Night Barn Burner

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**

The clock struck 10:15 PM, the Zamboni had long since finished its dance, and the air inside the arena was thick with the scent of high-octane competitive hockey. On a chilly July 8th, the Tulsa Bison rolled into town looking to sharpen their horns, while the Dirty Danglers were desperate to climb out of the bottom half of the standings. 

When the final buzzer sounded, it was the Bison walking away with a 4-2 victory, proving once again why they are currently sitting pretty in the top three of the league.

### Period 1: The Danglers Draw Blood
The game opened with a frantic pace, both teams looking to establish a rhythm on the ice. The Danglers caught a break at the 11:12 mark when Tulsa’s Sean Widdows found himself heading to the sin bin for a tripping infraction. 

The Danglers capitalized on the man advantage with surgical precision. At 11:58, **Stephen Jeffery** cleaned up some garbage in front of the crease, tucking a saucer pass from **Travis Moore** home to light the lamp. It was a textbook power-play goal that had the Danglers’ bench buzzing. 

However, the Bison aren’t the third-ranked team in the league for nothing. As the period wound down, **JW DePriest**—riding a heater of a two-game goal streak—decided he wasn’t going into the locker room trailing. With 4:21 left on the clock, DePriest navigated through traffic and ripped a wrister that found twine, knotting the score at 1-1 heading into the intermission.

### Period 2: The Bison Stampede
The second frame was where the wheels started to turn for Tulsa. They began tilting the ice, pinning the Danglers deep in their own zone. 

Just as the period seemed destined to stay deadlocked, **JW DePriest** struck again. With only 7.3 seconds remaining, he capitalized on a feed from **Geoffery Rash** to bury his second of the night. It was a back-breaker of a goal, a classic "locker room momentum killer" that sent the Danglers back to the bench with their heads hanging.

Tulsa didn’t let their foot off the gas. At the 10:28 mark, **Brian Cuff** extended the lead, connecting on a gorgeous setup from **Joe Baker**. Suddenly, a tight game had turned into a 3-1 mountain for the Danglers to climb.

### Period 3: Too Little, Too Late
The third period saw the Danglers desperate to find a spark, but the Bison defensive shell was as sturdy as a seasoned veteran's cup. 

**Joe Baker**, who had been orchestrating the offense all night, added a goal of his own at 1:55, assisted by **David Josiah Guajardo**, pushing the lead to 4-1. The Danglers finally responded at 8:13 when **Jonathan McCormick** beat the Tulsa netminder after some crafty work by **Stephen Jeffery** and **Dylan Calhoun**, but by then, the Bison had the game firmly in their grip.

### The Aftermath
For the **Tulsa Bison (5-2-0)**, this win is a massive statement. They continue to sit comfortably in the third spot, proving they have the grit to handle business on the road. JW DePriest continues to be a highlight-reel machine; if he keeps this pace up, he’ll be the talk of the league office by the end of the month.

The **Dirty Danglers (2-5-0)** fall deeper into the standings, left to wonder what could have been if not for that late second-period collapse. While Stephen Jeffery’s multi-point effort was a bright spot, they’ll need to tighten up their defensive zone coverage if they want to stop the slide.

The ice may be cooling down, but the playoff race is just starting to boil. See you at the rink!
