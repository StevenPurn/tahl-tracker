# A Waller-coaster of Offense: Mighty Drunks Swamp Blue Bombers 7-2

**MAY 31, 2026** – If you caught the sunrise at the rink this morning, you didn't just get a workout—you got a front-row seat to an absolute clinic. The Mighty Drunks rolled into the building looking to pad their spot at the top of the standings, and by the time the final horn sounded, they’d turned the Blue Bombers’ morning into a nightmare. 

Final score: **Mighty Drunks 7, Blue Bombers 2.**

### First Period: Setting the Tone
The Drunks didn’t bother with a morning cup of coffee; they just got straight to the scoring. Justen Monroe opened the floodgates at 11:39, picking his spot to give the Drunks the early lead. While Daron Ross headed to the sin-bin for a cross-check, the Bombers couldn't find a spark, and Sean Laube capitalized shortly after, pushing the lead to 2-0. The Bombers looked like they were skating in cement, constantly fighting the "traffic in front of the net" that the Drunks were dictating all morning.

### Second Period: The Waller Arrival
The Drunks kept the pressure on in the second when Omar Awad fed off a clean pass from Cian Slattery to bury one, extending the gap to three. 

The Bombers finally showed some pulse midway through the frame. Samuel Northup and Anthony Rizzatto set the table, and Hayden Sixsmith finally broke the seal for the Bombers, burying a beauty to breathe some life into his team. But, just when you thought the momentum might shift, Daron Ross tripped his way into the box, and the Drunks punished the Bombers instantly. Tyler Waller—who decided the back of the net was his home for the day—scored the first of his monster performance off a feed from Remigiusz Ferenc. 

### Third Period: The Hat-Trick Heroics
The third period was all Tyler Waller. If there’s a place "where mom keeps the cookies," Waller was clearing out the entire pantry. He tacked on three more goals in the final frame, including a beautiful sequence assisted by Nathan Fahland and another pair of setups from his partner-in-crime, Ferenc.

To their credit, the Blue Bombers didn't go down without a fight. Late in the third, after some chippy play that sent Sixsmith and Awad to the box for matching roughing minors, Chris Olsen found the back of the net off a sweet dish from Northup. It was a nice goal for the highlight reel, but by then, the barn was already burned to the ground. 

### The Bottom Line
For the **Mighty Drunks (2-0-0)**, this is a statement win. They are firing on all cylinders, playing a physical, puck-possession game that is proving to be a headache for everyone else in the league. With Tyler Waller playing like he’s got a magnet in his stick, they are looking like the team to beat.

As for the **Blue Bombers (0-2-1)**, it’s back to the drawing board. They’ve struggled to find their footing in these early season tilts and will need to tighten up the defensive zone if they want to stop the bleeding. 

It was an 8:45 AM start, but the Mighty Drunks certainly didn’t snooze. They came, they saw, and they filled the net. See you at the next one!
