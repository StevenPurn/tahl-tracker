# Ice Breakers Keep the Streak Alive in a White-Knuckle Thriller Against The Rookery

**By: "Slap-Shot" Sammy, Hometown Beat Reporter**
*July 1st, 2026*

If you weren’t at the arena tonight, I hope you have a solid reason, because you missed a barn burner. The Ice Breakers looked to keep their unblemished record intact against a desperate Rookery squad hungry to climb out of the basement. What we got was sixty minutes of high-octane, teeth-rattling hockey that proved why this is the best game on earth.

When the dust finally settled, the scoreboard read **Ice Breakers 4, The Rookery 3**. But that scoreline doesn’t do justice to the sheer grit displayed on the ice tonight.

### Period 1: The Early Wake-Up Call
The Rookery came out of the gate like they were shot out of a cannon. **Gage Walker**, clearly feeling the heat, opened the account at 11:49 with an unassisted beauty, silencing the home crowd. It was a wake-up call for the Breakers, who looked a step slow early on. However, with just 2:48 left in the frame, **Luke Morley** decided he’d had enough of the Rookery’s joy, burying a solo effort to knot things up at 1-1 heading into the intermission.

### Period 2: The Breakers Find Their Rhythm
If the first was a skirmish, the second was a calculated assault. The Ice Breakers dialed up the pressure, forcing traffic in front of the net. At 2:07, **Justice Dick** hammered one home, set up by a silky-smooth feed from the unstoppable **Joshua Pula**. Pula, extending his point streak to a staggering six games, wasn't done yet. He decided to play hero himself at 7:11, finding the back of the net to stretch the lead to 3-1. You could feel the air leave the Rookery bench as the Breakers’ momentum became undeniable.

### Period 3: The Closing Act
The third period began with the Ice Breakers looking to put the game on ice. **Justice Dick** grabbed his second of the night at 11:39, thanks to a beautiful helper from **Shawn Ward**. With the score at 4-1, it felt like a wrap—but don't tell that to The Rookery.

They mounted a frantic, desperate comeback. **Aidan Caldron** responded almost immediately at 12:39 to keep the pulse beating. Then, at 4:44, **Chris Olsen** buried one off a crisp pass from **Tyler Waller**, pulling The Rookery within one. The final minutes were pure chaos—goalies diving, sticks clattering, and the crowd on their feet. The Rookery threw the kitchen sink at the Breakers' cage, but the defense held firm, weathering the storm until the final buzzer mercifully sounded.

### The Aftermath
For the Ice Breakers (4-0-0), this win is a statement. They aren't just winning; they are finding ways to close out tight games against teams playing with their backs against the wall. With 8 points, they are breathing down the necks of the Mighty Drunks and looking like a team destined for a deep playoff run.

For The Rookery (1-4-0), it’s a tough pill to swallow. They showed incredible heart in the third, but their defensive lapses in the second period proved to be the difference. They’ll need to tighten up the back end if they want to stop their slide and make a move back up the standings.

It’s back to the drawing board for both squads, but if tonight was any indication, the rest of the season is going to be an absolute rollercoaster. Keep your sticks on the ice, folks—it’s getting good.
