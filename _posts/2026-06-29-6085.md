# Average Joes Take the Tuskens to the Woodshed in 9-3 Rout

**By: "Slap-Shot" Sammy, Hometown Beat Reporter**

**TULSA** – If you were looking for defensive wizardry tonight at the local rink, you were looking in the wrong place. If you were looking for an absolute offensive clinic, the Average Joes had you covered. In a game that saw the scoreboard light up like a pinball machine, the Joes dismantled the Tulsa Tuskens 9-3, proving that while they may be "Average" by name, they were anything but on the ice this Thursday night.

### First Period: A Short-Handed Statement
The Tuskens came in desperate for their first win of the season, but the Joes decided to play spoiler before the Zamboni had even finished its first lap. Just five minutes into the game, Jordan Hawkins Baker sat for tripping, handing the Tuskens a prime power-play opportunity. 

But hold your horses—the Joes didn’t get the memo. **Frank Smith** absolutely torched the Tuskens’ defense, scoring a shorthanded beauty just five seconds into the power play. Smith wasn’t done, either; he doubled the lead just under a minute later off a feed from Aidan Caldron. 

By the time the dust settled on the first, the Joes had built a comfortable cushion. Despite individual efforts from Tuskens' **Gage Walker** and **Garrett Caraway**, the Joes' puck movement—orchestrated by the playmaking wizardry of **Ryan Martinez**—kept the Tulsa netminder under constant siege.

### Second Period: Stretching the Lead
If the first period was a skirmish, the second was a landslide. The Joes continued to win the battles along the boards, effectively turning the neutral zone into a toll booth that the Tuskens simply couldn't afford to pay. **James Caldron** extended the lead, and **Jordan Hawkins Baker** redeemed his early-game penalty by burying a heater off an assist from the ever-present Martinez. The Tuskens looked like they were skating in quicksand, struggling to find any sustained pressure as the Joes effectively cycled them into exhaustion.

### Third Period: Pouring It On
To their credit, the Tuskens refused to roll over. **Matthew Patrick** managed to find the twine midway through the third off a nice setup from **Dalton Sirmon**, giving the home crowd a brief flash of hope. But the Joes, sensing blood in the water, responded with a flurry. 

**Tracy Wilmes** and **Antonio Hurling** joined the scoring parade, and the irrepressible **Aidan Caldron** capped off his monster night with another goal to seal the 9-3 drubbing. Even a late penalty to Martinez couldn't stop the Joes' momentum; the Tuskens simply had no answer for the sheer volume of traffic in front of their crease.

### The Aftermath
For the Average Joes (3-1-0), this win is a massive statement. They look sharp, organized, and—dare I say it—dangerous. They move up the ladder to a solid 6 points, firmly planting themselves in the hunt for the league lead. 

For the Tuskens, it’s back to the drawing board. Still searching for that elusive first victory at 0-4-0, they’ll need to tighten up the gaps and find a way to stop the bleeding before the losing streak turns into a trend that’s impossible to reverse.

The puck drops again next week, folks. If tonight was any indication, you’d better grab your seat early—because when these Joes are rolling, they don’t just win; they put on a fireworks show.
