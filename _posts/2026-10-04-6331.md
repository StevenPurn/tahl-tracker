# Riveters Hammer Home a Win: Emerson Leads the Way in 5-1 Romp

If you were looking for a masterclass in offensive efficiency this Saturday morning, the Tulsa Riveters were happy to oblige. The Riveters marched into the rink and systematically dismantled the Chix with Stix, walking away with a decisive 5-1 victory that keeps their undefeated regulation record firmly intact.

The story of the morning was undoubtedly Ivy Emerson, who arrived at the rink with a hot stick and left with a hat trick. Emerson extended her goal-scoring streak to three games in emphatic fashion, opening the floodgates early in the first period at the 4:48 mark. From the drop of the puck, the Riveters dictated the pace, and Emerson’s clinical finish set the tone for a team that looks like it’s hitting its mid-season stride early.

While the Chix with Stix tried to weather the storm, the Riveters’ defense remained as stubborn as a jammed Zamboni door. In the second period, the momentum shifted heavily toward Tulsa. Emerson struck again at 7:31, courtesy of a slick feed from Theresa Rose Rabara, before Gabi Gibson decided to get in on the action less than two minutes later. Gibson’s unassisted tally at 9:20 sent the Riveters into the final frame with a comfortable 3-0 cushion.

The third period brought more of the same, with Emerson completing her three-goal masterpiece just 3:20 into the frame. Though the Chix with Stix finally broke the ice at 3:55 when Abigail Nisbet buried a shot off a pass from Hannah Atkins, the rally was short-lived. The goal provided a momentary spark for the Chix, but the Riveters quickly snuffed out any hopes of a comeback. Gabi Gibson added her second goal of the day at 6:47, sealing the 5-1 final and sending the Riveters’ faithful home happy.

"We just focused on keeping the puck moving and keeping our feet churning," said an exuberant Riveters player following the win. "When Ivy is finding the back of the net like that, it makes everyone’s job a whole lot easier."

For the Chix with Stix, it’s a tough result that leaves them searching for answers after dropping their second consecutive game. They’ll need to tighten up the defensive zone if they want to climb out of the middle of the pack. 

The Riveters, meanwhile, look like the team to beat. By staying disciplined and leaning on their red-hot scoring depth, they’ve solidified their standing in the upper echelon of the league. With a 2-0-2 record, Tulsa is proving that they aren’t just a team that shows up—they’re a team that shows off.
