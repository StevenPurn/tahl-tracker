# Silver Linings: Hitmen Dismantle Puckheads in 6-1 Rout

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
*June 21, 2026*

If you were looking for a quiet Sunday night skate at the arena, you definitely came to the wrong place. The late-night tilt between the Puckheads and Hitmen Silver turned into a total runaway, with the Hitmen turning the ice into their personal playground in a 6-1 statement victory.

### Period 1: A Flurry of Action
The Hitmen wasted zero time setting the tempo. Just 65 seconds into the opening frame, **Bobby Pickett** buried a feed from **Drew Sleezer**, silencing the Puckheads’ faithful early. 

The Puckheads, however, showed some grit. After **Andrew Retkowski** headed to the sin bin for slashing, you’d think the Hitmen would capitalize. Instead, **Gregory Douglas** turned the tables with a beautiful unassisted shorty at 8:17, weaving through the neutral zone like he was skating through traffic on a Friday afternoon to knot the score at 1-1. It felt like we were in for a barn burner, but that would be the last time the Puckheads’ offense tasted glory.

### Period 2: The Hitmen Pull Away
The second period was where the Hitmen truly separated the wheat from the chaff. **Calvin Baker** broke the deadlock at 10:31, firing a heater that found the back of the net. 

As the period wound down, the wheels fell off the bus for the Puckheads. Retkowski found himself in the box again—this time for unsportsmanlike conduct—and the Hitmen made them pay dearly. Bobby Pickett struck again with less than a minute left in the frame, followed by a late-period dagger from **Josh Moyer** with just 48 seconds on the clock. By the time the buzzer sounded, the Hitmen had a commanding 4-1 lead, leaving the Puckheads looking for answers in the locker room.

### Period 3: Closing the Curtain
Any hope of a Puckheads comeback was officially extinguished early in the third. Moyer netted his second of the night at 10:39, once again off a pristine setup from Sleezer. By the time **Christopher Gardner** slapped home the final goal at 13:42, it was clear: this wasn't just a win, it was a clinic.

The closing minutes were a bit of a chippy affair, with Retkowski and Pickett dancing in the corner, leading to coincidental roughing penalties at 9:58. But by then, the game was already in the freezer.

### Final Thoughts and Standings Impact
The Puckheads (2-2-0) entered the night holding the #4 spot, but this loss is going to sting. They struggled to contain the Hitmen's chemistry, particularly the Sleezer-Pickett-Moyer trio, who seemed to be playing a different game entirely.

Meanwhile, Hitmen Silver (2-1-0) are looking like a team on a mission. Having surged to a 2-1-0 record, they’ve officially announced their arrival as a force to be reckoned with. They’re climbing the ladder, and if they keep finding the top shelf where mom keeps the cookies like they did tonight, the rest of the league better watch their backs.

**Next time you’re at the rink, keep your head on a swivel—because if the Hitmen are on the ice, you’re in for a fast-paced, high-octane display of hockey.**
