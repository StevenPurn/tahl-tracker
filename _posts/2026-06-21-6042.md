# Danglers Turn the Tide: A Late-Night Barn Burner at the Rink

**By: Your Hometown Rink-Side Reporter**  
**June 21, 2026**

If you weren’t at the arena tonight, you missed a prime-time heater. The Dirty Danglers (#8) stepped onto the ice tonight looking to shake off a rough stretch, while the Lite Beer Eagles (#7) were riding a wave of momentum, looking to solidify their spot in the upper crust of the standings. 

What we got was 45 minutes of pure, unadulterated grit. When the final horn sounded, the Danglers had squeaked out a 4-3 victory in a game that felt like a heavyweight title fight on ice.

### Period 1: The Tactical Standoff
The opening frame was a masterclass in defensive congestion. Both squads were playing tight, clogging the neutral zone like a bad freeway exit. The only real drama came at the 10:01 mark when Kenneth Watkins headed to the sin bin for a tripping minor. The Danglers’ penalty kill unit, however, played with the tenacity of a junkyard dog, successfully frustrating the Eagles’ power play and heading into the break locked at zeroes.

### Period 2: The Goal-Scoring Carousel
If the first period was a chess match, the second was a bar fight in a phone booth. 

The Eagles drew first blood at 12:33 when Jeff Horvath buried one home, courtesy of a beautiful setup from the red-hot Jonathan McCormick, extending McCormick’s point streak to five games. 

But the Danglers didn't just bend; they snapped back. After a brief trip to the penalty box for Kalea Maxwell, the Danglers suddenly woke up. Dylan Dillard ignited the spark with 18.4 seconds left in the early frame, and then the floodgates blew wide open. Joshua Hopwood became the man of the hour, lighting the lamp twice in under two minutes! It was a flurry of activity that left the Eagles’ defense looking like they were trying to catch smoke with their bare hands.

Just as the Danglers seemed to be running away with it, the Eagles’ ETHAN HILTON reminded everyone why he’s on a scoring heater, burying a feed from Kevin Bailey-McGinnis to narrow the gap.

### Period 3: Hanging On for Dear Life
The third period was all about goaltending and nerves. The ice tilted back and forth as both teams traded heavy blows. Right at the opening buzzer of the third, Dylan Dillard notched his second of the night—a back-breaker for the Eagles—assisted once again by his partner-in-crime, Dylan Calhoun. 

The Eagles didn’t roll over. Jonathan McCormick hammered home a goal at 5:34, slicing the lead to one and setting the stage for a heart-pounding finish. The final minutes were pure "traffic in front of the net" chaos, with goalies on both ends turning into brick walls, swallowing rebounds and fighting through screens to keep the scoreline as is.

### The Aftermath
The 4-3 victory is a massive morale boost for the Dirty Danglers, who have been desperate to climb out of the bottom half of the table. Their ability to capitalize on transitions—specifically the Calhoun-to-Dillard connection—proved to be the difference-maker. 

For the Lite Beer Eagles, it’s a tough pill to swallow. While McCormick continues to produce at an elite level, they’ll want to review the defensive lapses that allowed the Danglers to strike twice in such quick succession during the second period.

As we look at the standings, the gap between the mid-pack squads is shrinking, and tonight’s result makes the upcoming schedule look like a complete bloodbath. If you’re a fan of hockey, grab your coffee and your lucky jersey—this season is just getting good.
