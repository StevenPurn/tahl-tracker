# Jackalopes Stomp the Bronze: Foster’s Hat-Trick Fuels Barn Burner Win

**TULSA** — If you weren’t at the rink on this humid August 5th evening, you missed a classic. In a contest that had more lead changes than a soap opera and enough tension to snap a carbon-fiber stick, the Jackalopes climbed out of the basement and punched back, taking down Gold as Ice Bronze by a final score of 5-4.

It was a classic "four-point" swing in the standings, and for the Jackalopes, this was the kind of grit-and-grind performance that turns a season around.

### First Period: The Feeling-Out Process
The game started at a frenetic pace, with both squads looking to prove that their spots in the bottom half of the standings were purely temporary. The Jackalopes drew first blood at the 10:15 mark when **Kyle Foster**—who clearly ate his Wheaties this morning—found the back of the net off a sweet feed from Donald Schleicher and Bradley Fidler.

But "Gold as Ice Bronze" isn't a team that goes down without a fight. Just over a minute later, **Ryan Brnardic** hammered home an equalizer off a helper from Triston Wilson. By the time the horn blared for the first intermission, it was a 1-1 stalemate, and the ice was already getting chewed up by the heavy skating.

### Second Period: The Foster Explosion
The middle frame saw the "Triston Wilson Show" begin. The Gold as Ice Bronze sniper put his team ahead 2-1 at 10:47, capitalizing on a crisp setup from Brnardic and Joshua Hopwood. 

Just when it looked like the Bronze might seize control, Kyle Foster decided he was done sharing the spotlight. In a dizzying 25-second span late in the period, Foster completed a natural hat-trick. First, he buried a pass from Ronald Herwig and Adam Schuh at 2:24, and then, showing the kind of hustle that makes coaches weep with joy, he forced a turnover and went coast-to-coast unassisted at 2:49. The Jackalopes headed into the break clinging to a 3-2 lead, having completely shifted the momentum.

### Third Period: The Wild West
If the second period was a chess match, the third was a full-blown bar fight. Ryan Brnardic struck early for the Bronze, knotting the game at 3-3 at the 1:09 mark, completing his own multi-goal night.

The teams traded blows in the trenches, with "traffic in front of the net" becoming the theme of the night. The Jackalopes finally broke the deadlock at 11:04 when **Donald Schleicher** snapped a beauty past the keeper off a Robert Verity feed. Then, with the Bronze reeling, **Todd Thompson** added a massive insurance marker at 13:28.

Gold as Ice Bronze showed incredible heart, with Wilson netting his second of the night at 9:51 to make it a one-goal game, but the Jackalopes’ defensive shell held firm in the final frantic minutes. 

### What It Means
The win moves the Jackalopes (3-4-2, 8 pts) out of the doldrums and into a tie with tonight's opponent, Gold as Ice Bronze (3-5-2, 8 pts). 

For the Jackalopes, this is the momentum they’ve been starving for after a rough start to the campaign. They proved tonight that when they cycle the puck and let snipers like Foster go to work, they can skate with anyone in this league. As for Gold as Ice Bronze, it’s a tough pill to swallow, but with a offense as potent as the Wilson-Brnardic connection, they’ll be looking to bounce back in their next outing.

The standings are tightening up, folks. Grab your popcorn—this season is just starting to get good.
