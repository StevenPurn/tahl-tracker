# Havok Wreaks Absolute… Well, Havok! Badgers Fall in 5-3 Slugfest

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: June 7, 2026**

If you weren’t at the arena tonight, you missed a classic. It was a 5:15 PM puck drop that felt more like a Saturday night main event, as the Honey Badgers and Havok squared off in a game that had more momentum swings than a pendulum in a hurricane. When the final horn sounded, the Havok faithful were walking a little taller, handing the Badgers a 5-3 loss in a high-octane barn burner.

### First Period: A Tit-for-Tat Tug of War
The Badgers came out of the tunnel with something to prove, looking to snap that early-season skid. They drew first blood at 11:15 when Adam Carter deposited a beauty off a crisp feed from Marshall Nunneley. It looked like the Badgers might finally be finding their rhythm, but Havok is a team that simply refuses to stay quiet.

Michael Henry, riding high on a point-per-game rhythm, leveled the score just two minutes later, thanks to a helper from Zachary Wade. Not to be outdone, Brandon Szuch decided to go "I’ll do it myself," putting the Badgers back on top at 2:56 with a gritty, unassisted effort. But that lead lasted about as long as a snowflake in a furnace. With 6:23 left in the opening frame, Wade took the torch, burying a helper from Henry to knot it up at 2-2 heading into the first intermission.

### Second Period: Havok Finds Their Gear
If the first period was a test of wills, the second was a clinic on persistence. Havok began to clog the lanes and establish the kind of traffic in front of the net that makes goalies lose their collective cool. 

Matthew Rudick put Havok in the lead for the first time at 1:21, cleaning up a gorgeous setup from the tandem of Henry and Steven Purn. From there, the pressure never let up. Just as the clock was winding down to the final minute, Seth Miller capitalized on a perfect dish from Emil Hinkefent. That late-period tally was a back-breaker, sending the Badgers into the dressing room trailing 4-2 and looking for answers.

### Third Period: Too Little, Too Late
The Badgers desperately needed a spark, but the Havok defense stood tall, turning away rushes and forcing the Badgers to the perimeter. It wasn’t until the final 30 seconds that Robert Tett finally found the twine, assisted by Szuch, but it was essentially "too little, too late" for a Badgers squad desperate for their first 'W'.

The dagger came midway through the frame when Zachary Wade—who is clearly playing on an entirely different level right now—netted his second of the night, fed by Robert Verity and Steven Purn. It was vintage Wade: finding the soft spot in the defense and tucking it top shelf where mom keeps the cookies.

### The Big Picture
For Havok, this 5-3 win is exactly the kind of fuel they need to climb the standings. They move to 1-0-1 on the young season, effectively silencing any questions about their consistency. Zachary Wade’s performance was nothing short of heroic, and with the chemistry building between him, Purn, and Henry, they are looking like a team that could cause major, well, *Havok* for the rest of the league.

As for the Honey Badgers, they drop to 0-2-0. It’s been a tough start to the campaign, and they’ll need to stop the bleeding before the rest of the field pulls away. The talent is there—we saw it in flashes tonight—but they need to lock down the defensive zone if they want to stop these streaks of bad luck.

The standings are tightening up, and the ice is only getting faster. Stick with us—the season is only just warming up!
