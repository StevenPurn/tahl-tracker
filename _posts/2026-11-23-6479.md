# Gold as Ice Heats Up in 9-5 Offensive Explosion Against Reapers

If you’re a fan of high-octane, pedal-to-the-metal hockey, you should have been at the rink late last night. Gold as Ice and the Reapers turned the ice into a total shootout, with Gold as Ice emerging from the chaos with a decisive 9-5 victory.

The story of the night was undoubtedly Aden Ingram. The Gold as Ice sniper came into the game riding a hot hand, and he didn’t just keep the streak alive—he put on a clinic. Ingram opened the scoring early in the first and refused to take his foot off the gas, completing a natural hat trick before the first period even hit the eight-minute mark. 

"I think the puck just had a GPS tracker set to the back of the net tonight," Ingram joked after the game. "My teammates were putting it on a tee, and I was just trying not to whiff it."

By the time the dust settled on a wild first period, Gold as Ice had surged to a commanding lead, highlighted by a scoring frenzy where they netted four goals in under five minutes. Mark DeSimone was the architect of the madness, facilitating the play with vision that made the Reapers' defense look like they were skating through wet cement. DeSimone capped off his own brilliant performance with a pair of goals, proving that he is truly the engine that makes this Gold as Ice machine hum.

The Reapers, never ones to roll over, tried to chip away at the deficit. Stephen Jeffery played the role of the spoiler, racking up two goals on the night, including a gutsy effort in the third period where he just put his head down and willed the puck past the goal line. Lewis Prevost also found the back of the net while shorthanded, showing the Reapers still had plenty of teeth even when they were down a skater.

However, every time the Reapers threatened to manufacture a comeback, Gold as Ice had an answer. Dylan Dillard provided the structural support with two goals of his own, and Taylor Chinowth kept his point streak alive in style, finding the back of the net in the final frame to effectively put the game out of reach.

For Gold as Ice, this win is a massive confidence booster as they look to climb out of the bottom half of the standings. The offense was firing on all cylinders, with Kalea Maxwell extending her point streak and the entire top line clicking like a well-oiled Swiss watch. 

"We’ve been working on our transition game," said a visibly relieved Gold as Ice coach behind the bench. "Tonight, the puck movement was crisp, the transitions were clean, and we finally saw the results reflect the effort we’ve been putting in at practice."

The Reapers will look to dust themselves off and get back to their winning ways, but tonight belonged to the visitors. Gold as Ice proved that when their chemistry is clicking, they can light the lamp with the best of them.
