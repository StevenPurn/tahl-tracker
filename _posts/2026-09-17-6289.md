# Mooseknuckles Run Wild in 14-3 Shellacking of Laser

If you left the rink early on Monday night to beat the traffic, you missed the equivalent of a hockey-themed demolition derby. The Mooseknuckles transformed the ice into their own personal playground, hanging a massive 14-3 scoreline on the Laser in a regular-season performance that can only be described as offensive overkill.

The Mooseknuckles wasted absolutely no time setting the tone. By the time the first period buzzer sounded, they had already rattled off five unanswered goals. Nicholas Strle lit the lamp first, but it was Garrett Williams who looked like he was playing a different sport entirely. Williams was a human highlight reel, finding the back of the net twice in the opening frame alone and contributing to a barrage of scoring that left the Laser defense looking like they were trying to catch smoke with a pair of chopsticks.

Things only got grimmer for the Laser when Timothy N. See headed to the box for a roster addition penalty midway through the first. Michael Pacheco promptly capitalized on the momentum, hammering home a goal while the man-advantage was still fresh to extend the lead. 

"I think at one point, I stopped checking the scoreboard and just started checking my watch to see if we were ever going to get a change of scenery," joked one Laser skater after the tilt.

The offensive onslaught continued unabated into the second period. Kyle Okurowski, Steven Belanger, and Jasen Lawwill all joined the party, with Pete Fleming putting on an absolute clinic by netting a hat trick all within the second frame. By the time the Laser finally broke their drought with a goal from Chad Pancoast, the deficit had already reached double digits. 

The Laser managed a bit of a pulse in the third period with tallies from Nic Poverud and Kyle Verity, but it was far too little, too late. Steven Belanger added his second of the night late in the final frame to put a bow on a night that the Mooseknuckles will remember for a long time, and the Laser will likely try to scrub from their memory by Tuesday morning.

For the Mooseknuckles, this dominant victory is a massive statement of intent as they climb out of the bottom half of the standings. They showcased a chemistry that most teams only dream of, with the likes of Garrett Williams and Jeff Kleehammer acting as the primary architects of a scoring symphony. 

The Laser, meanwhile, will look to reset after a game that saw them chasing shadows for the better part of three periods. It’s a long season in this league, but if the Mooseknuckles can keep this kind of offensive energy in the tank, the rest of the teams in the standings might want to start beefing up their blue lines immediately.
