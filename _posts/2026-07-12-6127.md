**Date: July 12, 2026**

If you caught the 11:30 AM matinee at the barn today, you didn’t just witness a hockey game; you witnessed a clinic. The Valkyries (#2) stepped onto the ice looking to keep pace with the Riveters, and they left the Bad Bees (#5) wondering if they’d accidentally wandered into a hornet’s nest of their own making. While the Bad Bees managed to find a lone goal at the 7:45 mark to cut the deficit, the Valkyries’ response was immediate and devastating: Gabrielle Gibson buried one just eleven seconds later to restore the two-goal cushion. 

### Period 2: Gibson Finds Her Groove
The second period was a gritty affair, but the Valkyries’ forecheck was relentless. Kalea Maxwell set up Gabrielle Gibson for her second tally of the afternoon at 14:09, essentially putting the game on ice before the second intermission even hit. 

### Period 3: Running Up the Score
If the Bad Bees hoped to find a spark in the third, they were met with a wet blanket. The Valkyries turned the final frame into a highlight reel. Kalea Maxwell notched a goal of her own at 2:57, and Jessica Hopwood followed suit shortly after. With Barbara Lepe Briones turning into an assist machine—picking up three helpers in the frame alone—Carson See rounded out her stellar day with her second goal of the match at 6:46. 

By the time the final horn blew, the Valkyries had effectively turned the game into a "Top Shelf Where Mom Keeps the Cookies" showcase.

This was a team effort that would make any coach smile. Barbara Lepe Briones was the engine tonight, orchestrating the offense with surgical precision, while Carson See and Gabrielle Gibson provided the finishing touch that left the Bad Bees scrambling in their own zone.
