# Power Puck Girls Snap Skid in Wild 5-4 Thriller Against Chix with Stix

If you were looking for a quiet Sunday morning at the rink, you were in the wrong zip code. The Power Puck Girls and the Chix with Stix traded blows in a back-and-forth affair that ended with a 5-4 victory for the Puck Girls, finally getting them into the win column for the first time this season.

The game started with the kind of frantic energy that usually suggests someone left their coffee in the locker room. The Power Puck Girls came out of the gate swinging, rattling off two quick goals in the first ninety seconds. While the score sheet might list them as "Unknown," the intent was clear: the Puck Girls were tired of their spot in the cellar. 

The Chix with Stix, however, aren’t the type to fold their cards early. Ashley Ivanoff settled the nerves midway through the first period, burying a feed from Elizabeth Edwards and Heather Cozad, followed shortly by Hannah Atkins knotting the game at 2-2. But just when it looked like the momentum had swung fully to the Chix, Maggie Marcum hammered one home off a lovely setup from Remisha Blation to restore the Puck Girls' lead before the first intermission.

The second period turned into a masterclass in how to draw the ire of a referee. With penalties for tripping and a "too many men" call on both sides, the game turned into a choppy, whistles-heavy slog. When the dust finally settled, the Power Puck Girls managed to extend their lead behind another unassisted effort, proving that sometimes, messy hockey is just as effective as the textbook stuff.

The third period brought the drama. Chix with Stix forward Haley Griffin sliced the deficit to one at the 3:55 mark, and when Abigail Nisbet tied it up later in the frame, it felt like the floor might fall out from under the Puck Girls. 

"We stopped looking at the clock and started looking at the puck," said one observer near the bench, which is exactly the kind of cliché that usually precedes a disaster—but not today. With the game deadlocked, the Power Puck Girls found one final spark, netting the deciding goal at the 9:43 mark to seal the 5-4 victory.

For the Power Puck Girls, the win is a massive sigh of relief after a rough start to the season. They looked faster, hungrier, and, perhaps most importantly, capable of closing out a tight game without hitting the panic button. For the Chix with Stix, it’s a frustrating stumble, but one they’ll likely look to put behind them quickly. 

At the end of the day, it was a reminder that in this league, the standings don't mean much when the puck drops. The Power Puck Girls walked out of the rink with their first two points of the season, and if they keep playing with that kind of grit, they might just climb their way out of the basement before the winter frost really sets in.
