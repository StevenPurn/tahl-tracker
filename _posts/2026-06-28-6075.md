# ABOMINABLE OFFENSE: Yetis Bury Leafs in 12-4 Goalfest

**TULSA** – If you blinked during this afternoon’s matinee at the rink, you probably missed a goal. In a game that felt more like a video game on rookie difficulty than a competitive league tilt, the Yetis absolutely dismantled the Tulsa Leafs by a final score of 12-4. 

Coming into today, the Yetis were looking to climb out of the middle-of-the-pack logjam, and they didn’t just climb—they brought a ladder and a snowblower.

### First Period: The Yetis Come Out Frosty
The Yetis set the tone early. Katherine Popoff drew first blood at the 14:24 mark, courtesy of a slick feed from the heater-hot Jason Fennell. From there, it was a display of pure offensive chemistry. Jered Pieschke went into "beast mode," netting two goals in rapid succession to put the Leafs on their heels. While Paul Atkins managed to find the back of the net to keep the Leafs’ pulse beating before the buzzer, it was all Yeti-snow-cones in the opening frame, capped off by another Fennell strike. The Yetis headed to the locker room holding a commanding 4-1 lead.

### Second Period: Traffic, Transitions, and Tremendous Offense
The middle frame turned into a bit of a track meet. The Yetis stretched their lead thanks to Brendan Burkman, who showed some serious poise in front of the crease. However, the Leafs refused to roll over. Paul Atkins completed a gritty shift to net his second of the day, and Skylar Miller followed it up just 26 seconds later, suddenly making it a 6-3 game. 

The Yetis, however, are a resilient bunch. Jason Bahr and Steven Belanger answered back with back-to-back strikes to restore order. The ice was tilted so hard toward the Leafs’ zone that I’m surprised the rink didn’t slide right off its foundation. 

### Third Period: The Avalanche Continues
If the Leafs had any hopes of a "Miracle on Ice" comeback, the third period snuffed them out. Despite another goal from Atkins—who finished with a hat trick in a valiant individual effort—the Yetis simply had too much firepower. 

Michael Pacheco provided the highlight of the period, scoring twice in under twenty seconds, proving that the Yetis' depth is as deep as a frozen lake. When Jason Fennell hammered home his second of the game with under ten minutes to play, the rout was officially on. The Leafs’ goaltending was under siege all afternoon, facing a barrage of high-danger chances that would make even the Vezina trophy winners sweat.

### The Big Picture
With this 12-4 statement win, the Yetis (3-2-1) vault themselves firmly into the upper tier of the standings. They proved today that when their transition game is clicking, they can outscore anyone in the league. 

For the Tulsa Leafs (1-1-2), it’s a tough afternoon to put in the rearview mirror. They’ll need to tighten up their defensive coverage in front of the net—the "traffic" today was far too heavy, and the Yetis were more than happy to drive through it all game long. 

The Yetis head home with momentum, while the Leafs look to regroup after a game that, quite frankly, felt like trying to stop an actual Yeti with nothing but a toothpick. 

**Three Stars of the Game:**
1. **Jason Fennell (Yetis):** A constant offensive catalyst.
2. **Jered Pieschke (Yetis):** Provided the early spark that broke the game open.
3. **Paul Atkins (Tulsa Leafs):** Kept his team within shouting distance for as long as he possibly could with a stellar hat trick.
