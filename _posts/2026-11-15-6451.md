# Hot Garbage Heats Up with 7-2 Rout of Ice Breakers Plus

If there’s one thing we learned on the ice this evening, it’s that "Hot Garbage" is a name that shouldn’t be taken literally. In a dominant display of offensive firepower, Hot Garbage dismantled the Ice Breakers Plus 7-2, putting an emphatic stop to their own recent slump while leaving the Ice Breakers still searching for that elusive first win of the season.

From the drop of the puck, Hot Garbage looked like a team on a mission. They wasted no time setting the tempo, with Zac Hughes—who would go on to have himself a night—opening the scoring just 90 seconds into the first period off a crisp feed from Joshua Hall. The Ice Breakers found themselves stuck in a cycle of defensive mishaps early on, and Hall added a second tally himself halfway through the opening frame to keep the pressure mounting.

"We just told the boys in the room to keep the puck moving and stop overthinking it," said one Hot Garbage squad member post-game, reportedly while trying to scrub a mysterious locker room stain off his jersey. "We played like we actually wanted to be there tonight."

The middle frame was where the floodgates really opened. Hot Garbage rattled off three unanswered goals in the second period, with Brett Mooney, Nicole Baker, and Justin Phillips all lighting the lamp. The Ice Breakers’ Jerry LaRue had a particularly long night, spending an impressive amount of time in the sin bin, which gave Hot Garbage all the breathing room they needed to rotate their lines and keep their legs fresh.

By the time Nicholas Brewer buried an unassisted goal early in the third, the game was well out of reach. To their credit, the Ice Breakers Plus didn’t pack it in. Daniel Reyes provided a flicker of hope with two goals in the third, showing some serious grit to find the back of the net twice. However, his efforts were sandwiched around another goal from Zac Hughes, who officially put a bow on his multi-goal performance to cement the 7-2 final.

For Hot Garbage, this win is a massive morale boost. Sitting at 3-4-1, they’ll look to carry this momentum forward as they climb out of the basement. Meanwhile, it’s back to the drawing board for the Ice Breakers Plus. They’ve had a rough go of it at 0-7-1, but if Reyes can keep finding the twine like he did tonight, they might just start turning those "L"s into something a bit more festive soon.

It wasn't a pretty night for the Ice Breakers, but that’s hockey—sometimes you’re the windshield, and sometimes you’re the bug. Tonight, Hot Garbage was clearly in the driver's seat.
