# A Rink-Side Rout: Oklahoma Precast Pours It On Against Tornadoes

If you were looking for a quiet Saturday afternoon at the rink, you clearly picked the wrong game to attend. The Oklahoma Precast squad decided to turn the ice into their personal playground this afternoon, dismantling the Tornadoes in a 12-2 shellacking that left just about everyone in the building—including the guy running the scoreboard—dizzy.

The Tornadoes actually managed to flicker for a moment. Christian Alvegran found the back of the net early in the first, courtesy of a tidy feed from Brandt Mueller, and for about thirty-nine seconds, the Tornadoes faithful might have dared to dream. Then, the dam broke.

James Stuckey, who was putting on a clinic that would make a professor blush, spearheaded the response. He rattled off two goals in short order, followed quickly by Bobby Watson, Joseph Pachuta, and Matthew Rankin. By the time the dust settled on the first period, the Precast had effectively put the game in the freezer, leaving the Tornadoes looking like they’d just tried to stop a freight train with a fishing net.

The second period was more of the same, with Bobby Watson seemingly intent on padding his own highlight reel. He netted a trio of goals across the second frame, proving that if you give him an inch, he’ll take the entire sheet of ice. Even when the Tornadoes managed a brief spark—another Alvegran goal midway through the second—the Precast answered right back with the kind of ruthless efficiency you rarely see outside of a professional practice drill.

The Tornadoes’ frustration started to bubble over, with a couple of trips to the sin bin for Bazil Cardamone and Christian Alvegran, but by then, the scoreboard was already well out of reach. The Precast finished their business in the third period with late tallies from Pachuta and Zack Wade, putting a final exclamation point on a 12-2 win that was, quite frankly, as lopsided as a stool with one short leg.

"It was just one of those afternoons where the puck felt like it was magnetized to our sticks," said one Oklahoma Precast player as he headed toward the parking lot, barely breaking a sweat. On the other side of the tunnel, the Tornadoes locker room was unsurprisingly quiet. You have to imagine they’ll be looking to turn the page on this one pretty quickly.

For Oklahoma Precast, the win is a massive momentum builder, pushing them forward in the standings as they look to leave their early-season jitters in the rearview mirror. As for the Tornadoes, they’ll need to regroup; you can’t win them all, but you certainly hope to keep them from looking like a track meet in reverse. Back to the drawing board, boys—it’s a long season, and there’s always another puck to drop next week.
