# A Stalemate in the City: Wendigos and Hot Garbage Battle to a Draw

**By: "Slapshot" Sam, Hometown Beat Writer**

**August 2, 2026** — They say you can’t predict hockey, and if you need proof, you only had to be at the rink this morning for the 10:15 a.m. puck drop. In a classic "garbage time" clash that proved to be anything but, the Wendigos and Hot Garbage locked horns in a gritty, defensive tug-of-war that ended in a 1-1 deadlock.

Coming into this one, the narratives couldn’t have been more different. Hot Garbage arrived riding a wave of confidence, sitting comfortably in sixth place, while the Wendigos were looking to find their footing in the bottom half of the standings. But as we’ve seen all season, once the steel hits the ice, the standings go out the window.

### First Period: The Garbage Gets In First
The game opened with the intensity of a championship final. Both teams were throwing the body around, turning the neutral zone into a congested parking lot. It was Hot Garbage who drew first blood at the 8:13 mark of the opening frame. 

LC III Sinor, showing the kind of hands that make goalies lose sleep, capitalized on a beautiful feed from Tyler Holmes. Sinor didn't waste time, burying it past the netminder to give his squad an early 1-0 lead. The Wendigos, however, didn't pack it in. They spent the remainder of the period clogging up the passing lanes and forcing the Garbage to play a dump-and-chase game that yielded little fruit.

### Second Period: Chinowth Finds the Equalizer
If the first period was a tactical chess match, the second was a frantic scramble. The Wendigos came out of the locker room with the bit between their teeth, desperate to snap their inconsistent L-W-L-W-L rhythm.

Their persistence paid off at 13:39. Taylor Chinowth, playing with the poise of a seasoned vet, stepped into a puck and snapped it home to square the ledger at 1-1. The primary assist went to Daniel Maxwell, who played a perfect dish through traffic to set the table. From that point on, it was a goaltender’s duel. Both netminders stood tall, turning away high-danger chances and keeping the tension high enough to snap a twig.

### Third Period: Disciplined Defense
The final frame turned into a battle of attrition. Neither team wanted to make the mistake that would end their morning in heartbreak. The biggest moment of the period came with just under eight minutes remaining when LC III Sinor—the goal scorer himself—found himself headed to the sin bin for a tripping minor at 12:42.

The Wendigos had a golden opportunity to steal the win on the power play, but the Hot Garbage penalty kill unit was a brick wall. They shut down the lanes, blocked shots with everything they had, and frustrated the Wendigos' special teams until the final buzzer sounded.

### The Takeaway
When the dust settled on this 1-1 barn burner, it was a fair result for both benches. 

For **Hot Garbage**, the point keeps them steady in the standings at 5-3-1, though they’ll surely be disappointed not to have extended their momentum. They’ll need to regroup before their next tilt if they want to climb higher than that sixth-place spot.

As for the **Wendigos**, this is a moral victory they can hang their hats on. Keeping a top-six team like Hot Garbage to a single goal is exactly the kind of defensive structure they’ve been searching for all season. While they remain near the bottom at 2-6-1, a point against a superior opponent is the kind of grit that builds a culture. 

It wasn't a high-scoring thriller, but for the early risers at the rink, it was a reminder that in this league, every inch of ice is earned, and nothing is ever given for free. We’ll see you at the next one—bring your coffee and your cheering voices.
