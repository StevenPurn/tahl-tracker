# Outsiders Put the Squeeze on Eagles in 9-1 Rout

If you were looking for a high-intensity defensive battle on Friday night, you walked into the wrong rink. The Outsiders turned what was expected to be a competitive tilt against the Lite Beer Eagles into a total demolition, walking away with a lopsided 9-1 victory that had the Eagles looking like they were skating through wet cement.

The story of the evening was undeniably Logan Pardue, who decided early on that he didn’t need much help to dismantle the Eagles' defensive structure. Pardue opened the scoring at the 10:04 mark of the first period, and by the time the dust settled, he had notched an impressive hat trick, proving that the most dangerous man on the ice is often the one who simply refuses to pass the puck. Even a brief trip to the sin bin for interference late in the first couldn't dampen his afternoon.

By the time the second period rolled around, the Eagles were struggling to find any sort of rhythm. The Outsiders kept the pressure high, with Pardue adding his third of the night at 13:39, followed shortly by a strike from Justice Dick. To their credit, the Eagles finally broke the goose egg at the 7:59 mark of the second when Ethan Hilton found the back of the net, but it was a lonely highlight in an otherwise grim evening for the Lite Beer faithful.

The third period served as a prolonged victory lap for The Outsiders. William Javier, Tyler Waller, Brett Lamb, and Cody Dinsmore all piled on the misery for the Eagles' goaltender, who likely left the ice dreaming of a much sturdier set of defensemen in front of him. 

"We just kept finding the soft spots," said one Outsider player, stifling a laugh in the parking lot. "When the puck is bouncing your way like that, you just stop asking questions and keep shooting."

For the Eagles, this is one they’ll want to bury in the back of the equipment bag and forget before their next shift. Having started the season with a draw, they now find themselves searching for answers after a loss that brings them back to earth with a thud. Meanwhile, The Outsiders make a statement early in the season, moving from the middle of the pack to a team that clearly has the wind at their back. 

It wasn’t pretty, it wasn’t balanced, and for the Eagles, it was a long 45 minutes of hockey. But in a league where momentum is everything, The Outsiders proved tonight that they have plenty of it.
