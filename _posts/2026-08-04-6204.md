# The Rookery Finds Their Wings: Tuskens Toppled in High-Octane Barn Burner

**TULSA** – If you were looking for defensive masterclasses and shutouts on this fine August evening, you clearly took a wrong turn at the arena entrance. What we got instead was a pure, unadulterated, end-to-end hockey frenzy. In a battle of the cellar-dwellers, The Rookery finally shook the cobwebs off their season, outlasting the Tulsa Tuskens 7-5 in a game that had more momentum shifts than a tilt-a-whirl.

### First Period: Tuskens Draw First Blood
The Tuskens, desperate to shed their 0-7-0 shackles, came out of the gate with their hair on fire. Garrett Caraway drew first blood at the 10:10 mark, proving that the Tuskens didn't come to play checkers. When Dylan Walker doubled the lead at 14:35, the home crowd was sensing a breakthrough. 

The Rookery looked a bit wobbly, even taking a tripping minor courtesy of Jerry Larue, but they found their footing late in the frame. Gage Walker finally solved the Tuskens’ crease at 7:53, converting a crisp feed from James Caldron and Glenn Prouse to cut the deficit to 2-1 heading into the first intermission.

### Second Period: The Waller Hurricane
If you stepped away to grab a hot dog at the start of the second period, you missed a meteor shower. The period opened with a penalty to Tulsa’s Tyler Rathe, and The Rookery smelled blood. Joshua Pula hammered one home on the power play, but the real story was the absolute offensive explosion from Tyler Waller. 

Waller went on a heater that would make an NHL scout blush. He found the twine at 12:28, followed it up with another at 12:51, and capped his personal hat-trick surge at 4:39. It was a dizzying display of puck possession that left the Tuskens scrambling. Dalton Sirmon managed a gritty response for Tulsa, burying a dish from Robert Millea at 4:52, but the damage was done. The Rookery walked into the locker room with a commanding 5-3 lead.

### Third Period: The Desperation Push
The Tuskens weren't about to go down without a fight. They came out in the third playing "must-win" hockey. Garrett Caraway notched his second of the night at 10:42, and when Cody Springfield sniped one past the keeper at 12:19, the game was knotted at 5-5. The barn was officially burning now.

However, The Rookery’s resilience won the day. Gage Walker tallied his second of the night at 4:30 to reclaim the lead, and Jerry Larue atoned for his earlier trip to the sin bin by potting an insurance goal at 7:54. A late high-sticking penalty to Travis Shuck gave the Tuskens a final breath of life, but The Rookery’s defense buckled down to lock up their first victory of the season.

### The Big Picture
For The Rookery (1-6-0), tonight is a massive psychological hurdle cleared. They move to 4 points on the season, finally breathing down the neck of the Rohan Riders. For the Tulsa Tuskens, the search for the elusive "W" continues. They remain anchored at the bottom of the standings at 0-8-0, but they showed tonight that they have the offensive firepower to hang with anyone when the chips are down.

The ice may have been cool, but the tempers and the tempo were high. This is why we love the game, folks—no matter where you sit in the standings, you’re always just one shift away from a highlight-reel goal. We’ll see if The Rookery can bottle this momentum for their next outing!
