# Gabi Gibson’s Hat Trick Leads Bad Bees Past Chix with Stix

If you weren’t at the rink on September 20th, you missed a masterclass in scoring efficiency. The Bad Bees took to the ice against the Chix with Stix and walked away with a dominant 4-1 victory, proving that when the Bees find their buzz, the rest of the league had better take cover. 

For the first frame, it looked like a classic defensive standoff. The Chix with Stix were disciplined, and when Ashley Ivanoff caught a tripping call at the 3:13 mark, the Bees were held in check by a stout defensive posture. The ice stayed frozen and scoreless until the second period, when things finally bubbled over.

The Chix drew first blood at 12:35 when Nicole Baker hammered one home off a sweet dish from Mckenna N. Allison. It was the spark the crowd was waiting for, but as it turned out, it only poked the hornet’s nest. A mere fifteen seconds later, Gabi Gibson answered back for the Bees with an unassisted laser, tying the game at 1-1 and swinging the momentum harder than a barn door in a tornado.

"We gave up the lead and I thought, 'Well, that’s just rude,'" Gibson joked after the game while nursing a well-deserved post-match water. "Decided it was time to put my head down and go to work."

Go to work she did. After a shaky high-sticking penalty against Remisha Blation forced the Bees to hunker down, Gibson struck again at 4:22 in the second, this time finding the back of the net off a beauty of a feed from Jessica Stoops. By the time the third period rolled around, Gibson was feeling unstoppable. She notched her third goal of the evening at 2:16, effectively putting the game out of reach for the Chix.

Even a "too many men on the ice" penalty against Suzanne Hawkins at the 5:05 mark couldn't cool the Bees' heels. Hawkins, perhaps feeling a bit sheepish about her trip to the box, returned to the ice with a vengeance, netting a goal of her own at 8:44—assisted, naturally, by the red-hot Gibson—to seal the 4-1 final.

The Chix with Stix tried to rally, but the Bad Bees’ defense held firm as the clock ticked down. The win sees the Bad Bees climb the ladder in the standings, moving to 2-0-1 on the season. For the Chix with Stix, it’s a tough pill to swallow after coming into the night with such high hopes, but in this league, the difference between a win and a loss is often just one Gabi Gibson on a mission. The Bees are looking like the team to beat right now, and if they keep playing with this kind of venom, they might just stay at the top of the hive for a while.
