# Precast Concrete: Oklahoma Survives a Tornado Scare to Secure 3-2 Win

The Tornadoes might be searching for their first win of the season, but they certainly brought the thunder to the rink on Tuesday night. In a back-and-forth thriller that had the local faithful gripping their railings, Oklahoma Precast narrowly escaped with a 3-2 victory, keeping the Tornadoes winless in a frustratingly close contest.

For the first frame, the goalies were the stars of the show, keeping the scoreboard as clean as a fresh sheet of ice. The real chaos, however, erupted in the second period, which saw four goals fly into the mesh in a span of less than eight minutes.

Oklahoma Precast drew first blood when Matthew Rankin kept his scoring streak alive, potting his latest tally at the 4:55 mark with a tidy setup from Nicholas Power and Andrew Doepp. Before the Tornadoes could even shake off the shock, Curtis Tucker doubled the lead just 37 seconds later, burying a feed from Zack Wade to put Oklahoma up 2-0. 

But if you thought the Tornadoes were going to blow away quietly, you don’t know Bazil Cardamone. With his team desperate for a spark, Cardamone went on an absolute tear. First, he struck unassisted at 12:17 to break the goose egg, and then, in a blink-and-you’ll-miss-it sequence, he hammered home his second goal of the night just 41 seconds later off an assist from Brendan Budnik. 

"I told the boys between periods that we were one bounce away from a totally different game," said a visibly winded Cardamone in the locker room. "I just wanted to make sure I found the open space. The puck was following me around like a lost puppy tonight."

With the score knotted at 2-2 heading into the final frame, the game turned into a chess match played at breakneck speed. The Tornadoes spent a significant portion of the second period fending off the penalty box, as Tyler Schulz caught the attention of the officials twice, but the defense held strong under the pressure.

The deadlock finally broke at the 12:31 mark of the third. Curtis Tucker, refusing to let the game slip, tallied his second goal of the night—this one assisted by Elliott Langford—to put Oklahoma Precast ahead for good. Despite a frantic late-game surge from the Tornadoes, Oklahoma’s defensive corps stood tall, slamming the door shut to preserve the 3-2 final.

The win gives Oklahoma Precast a much-needed boost, moving them to 3-2-2 as they continue to solidify their hold on second place in the standings. For the Tornadoes, the 0-5-3 record is a bitter pill to swallow, especially given how close they were to snagging those elusive first two points. But if they keep playing with the intensity they showed in the middle of the second period, the win column surely isn't far off.
