# The Mafia Orchestrate a Seven-Goal Shellacking of the Ice Breakers

If you were looking for a high-intensity, defensive masterpiece at the rink this morning, you were firmly in the wrong building. Instead, the Sunday morning crowd was treated to a total dismantling as The Mafia put on an offensive clinic, crushing the second-place Ice Breakers 7-2.

Coming into this one, the Ice Breakers looked like the team to beat, but they spent most of the morning chasing shadows. The Mafia, meanwhile, seem to have finally shaken off that three-game skid, looking less like a mid-table squad and more like the side that actually knows how to put the puck in the net.

The tone was set early, and frankly, it was relentless. Milton Campbell kicked things off just over two minutes in, followed shortly by a solo effort from Dylan Walker. By the time Luke Morley managed to claw one back for the Ice Breakers at the 4:33 mark, it felt less like a comeback and more like a brief interruption of The Mafia’s morning workout.

"We just decided to stop overthinking it," said a grinning Mafia winger after the dust settled. "Turns out, if you just shoot the puck instead of trying to pass it through three guys’ skates, it goes in more often. Who knew?"

The second period was where the wheels really came off for the Ice Breakers. Jose Herrick buried an unassisted goal, and Dylan Walker added his second of the day. By the time Gage Walker found the back of the net with less than a minute left in the middle frame, the Ice Breakers’ bench looked like they were already planning their brunch order rather than their defensive scheme. 

The Ice Breakers managed a small pulse in the third period when Aaron West scored to trim the lead, but The Mafia wasn't in a charitable mood. Jose Herrick snagged his second of the day, and Gage Walker followed suit to cement the 7-2 final.

The Ice Breakers, usually a disciplined group, were left visibly frustrated, ending the afternoon with a "Too Many Men" penalty that was a fitting metaphor for their defensive coverage—mostly just bodies running around in the wrong places.

For The Mafia, the win is a massive morale booster as they sit at 4-3-2, proving that when their scoring depth wakes up, they can hang with the league’s elite. The Ice Breakers, meanwhile, will look to put this performance behind them quickly. They remain in the top tier of the standings, but after today's performance, they’ll likely be spending a few extra hours on the practice ice this week working on the basics. 

It was a tough day at the office for the Breakers, but that’s the beauty of this league—there’s always another game, and usually, someone is waiting to serve up a slice of humble pie. Today, that pie was served seven times over.
