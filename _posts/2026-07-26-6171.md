# Bison Stampede: Tulsa Blanks Wendigos in Sunday Afternoon Masterclass

**TULSA** – If you were looking for a high-octane display of puck possession and defensive lockdown, you didn't have to look further than the BOK Center this Sunday. In a matinee clash that felt more like a calculated surgical procedure than a hockey game, the Tulsa Bison took the Wendigos out to the woodshed, skating away with a dominant 4-0 shutout victory.

Entering the day, the Bison were looking to climb the ladder from the fourth seed, and they played with the hunger of a team that knows the leaderboard is getting crowded.

### First Period: Peterson Sets the Tone
The puck dropped at 2:30 PM, and the energy was palpable. The Wendigos, sitting at 11th in the standings, were clearly hoping to play spoiler, but Taylor Peterson had other ideas. At the 13:47 mark, Peterson decided he didn't need any help from his friends, dangling through the neutral zone before burying a beauty to light the lamp. It was a solo mission that gave the Bison an early 1-0 cushion, effectively putting the Wendigos on their back heels before they could even get their skates warm.

### Second Period: The Floodgates Open
The second period was where the Bison truly tightened the screws. While Brent Brown found himself heading to the sin bin for a slashing minor at the 07.8 mark, the Bison penalty kill was absolute iron. They swarmed the zone, cleared the crease, and made life miserable for the Wendigos' shooters.

Once back at full strength, the Bison offense started clicking like a Swiss watch. 

*   **1:08:** Brett Campbell doubled the lead, finishing a crisp feed from Brett Mooney. It was the kind of goal that makes the highlight reels—Tic-Tac-Toe passing that left the Wendigos' goalie looking for his compass.
*   **12:18:** Matthew Palmer tried to halt the momentum with a trip, but he only invited more chaos. 
*   **2:03:** Geoffery Rash joined the party, potting a goal assisted by Campbell. At this point, the ice was tilted so hard in favor of Tulsa you’d think the arena was sinking on one side.
*   **6:40:** Payton Brewer hammered the final nail in the coffin, courtesy of another helper from the man of the hour, Brett Campbell. 

### The Aftermath
The third period was a clinic in "protect the lead." The Bison defense was a brick wall, suffocating any hope of a Wendigos comeback. The shutout—a testament to a complete team effort—kept the Bison’s netminder relatively calm, as the defense ensured most shots were kept to the perimeter. 

**What this means for the Standings:**
With this win, the Tulsa Bison (now 7-2-0) continue their scorching run. They are looking less like a mid-pack threat and more like a bona fide title contender, breathing down the necks of The Outsiders and Gold as Ice. Their four-game winning streak is the talk of the league, and they are playing with a swagger that suggests they won’t be cooling off anytime soon.

For the Wendigos, it’s a tough pill to swallow. Sitting at 2-6-0, they’ll need to find their bite quickly if they want to climb out of the bottom third of the table. 

As for the Bison? They head into the next week with the wind in their sails and the scent of the top three in their nostrils. If they keep moving the puck like they did today, the rest of the league had better keep their heads on a swivel.

**Final Score:** Tulsa Bison 4, Wendigos 0.
