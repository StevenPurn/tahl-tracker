# The Bone Zone Keeps Rolling With 5-2 Win Over Yetis

If there’s one thing I’ve learned after decades of watching guys chase frozen rubber around a rink, it’s that momentum is a fickle beast. The Yetis found that out the hard way this Saturday afternoon, as The Bone Zone extended their winning streak to six games with a clinical 5-2 victory, proving once again why they’re hovering at the top of the standings.

The Yetis actually drew first blood early, with Kirk Nielsen finding the back of the net just 65 seconds into the opening frame. It was a crisp, no-nonsense start that suggested we might be in for a barnburner. But The Bone Zone, a team that seems to treat every shift like it’s a math problem they’ve already solved, didn’t flinch. Garrett Williams leveled the score at the 13:12 mark, set up by Jerry Bates, and from that point on, it felt like the ice was tilted in one direction.

The second period was where the wheels started to wobble for the Yetis. The Bone Zone’s Bryce Perkins buried a beautiful feed from Williams and Bates midway through the frame, and when Jeffrey Greco added another shortly after, the Bone Zone bench looked like they were grabbing an early lunch. Even a tripping call on Jason Bahr late in the first and a hooking penalty on Greco in the second didn't disrupt the Bone Zone’s rhythm; they played a disciplined game that would have made a high school coach proud.

"We just stopped watching the puck and started playing it," Greco remarked while icing his shoulder after the game, clearly pleased with his multi-goal outing. "Sometimes you just have to go to the dirty areas and see what falls out."

The third period brought a brief glimmer of hope for the Yetis when Denis Blaquiere struck unassisted, but the deficit was simply too tall a mountain to climb. Garrett Williams added his second of the day with less than a minute remaining in the third, and Greco followed up shortly after to put an exclamation point on the afternoon.

While the Yetis put up a valiant effort, their recent stumble—dropping their last two—has them looking like a team in need of a reset. For The Bone Zone, however, the formula remains simple: keep the streak alive, keep the legs moving, and don't get fancy when simple will do. They entered the game as the number two seed, and after Saturday’s performance, they aren't showing any signs of slowing down as they continue to breathe down the necks of the league-leading Puckedelics.
