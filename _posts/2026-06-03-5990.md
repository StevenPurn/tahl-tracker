# Barn Burner in the Midnight Hour: Mojo Magic Edges Jackalopes in Shootout Thriller

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: June 3, 2026**

If you weren't at the rink for the 10:30 p.m. puck drop on Tuesday night, you missed a prime-time heater. The Jackalopes and the Mojo decided that sleep was overrated, opting instead to treat the bleachers to a high-octane, back-and-forth tilt that pushed the limits of the clock and our collective nerves. When the final dust settled, it was the Mojo skating away with a 5-4 victory, courtesy of some ice-cold nerves in the shootout.

### The Opening Act: Jackalopes Take Charge
The Jackalopes came out of the tunnel with their hair on fire. Kyle Verity set the tone at the 3:31 mark, burying a dish from Todd Thompson and Toby Temple to open the scoring. With the adrenaline still surging, James Whitacre—looking to keep his goal streak alive—took matters into his own hands at 9:51, dancing through the zone unassisted to put the Jackalopes up 2-0. It felt like the Jacks were looking to turn this into a runaway, but the Mojo had other plans.

### The Middle Frame: A Tale of Two Teams
The second period was a complete momentum shift. While James Whitacre added his second of the night at the 13:41 mark—cashing in on a sweet feed from Ronald Herwig—the Mojo began to find their rhythm. Stephen Jeffery sparked the Mojo engine at 11:33, and once the seal was broken, the floodgates rattled. 

The Jackalopes saw their discipline fray when Whitacre headed to the box for tripping at 1:10. The Mojo capitalized on the shift in energy, with Thomas White lighting the lamp at 6:07 and Austin Lowry knotting the game up just a minute later. By the end of the second, what started as a Jackalopes clinic had turned into a full-blown street fight.

### The Final Push and The Shootout
The third period was as tight as a goalie’s glove. Kyle Foster gave the Jackalopes a temporary lead with a gritty, unassisted tally at 1:52, but Austin Lowry—the man with the golden touch tonight—answered back for the Mojo at 4:43. 

Deadlocked at 4-4, regulation ended, and we were headed to the skills competition. The shootout was pure theatre. Todd Thompson kept hope alive for the Jacks with a clutch finish, but the Mojo duo of Matthew Allington and Stephen Jeffery proved too much to handle. Jeffery, fresh off his second-period goal, sealed the deal with the game-deciding shootout marker, silencing the Jackalopes’ bench and sending the Mojo faithful home happy well past midnight.

### The Bottom Line
It’s a tough pill for the Jackalopes, who looked to have the game in hand early, but they walk away with a hard-earned point in a loss. For the Mojo, this victory is a massive injection of confidence. After struggling with form earlier in the season, breaking the streak with a win over a hungry Jacks squad is exactly the kind of "mojo" they needed to climb out of the basement and into the thick of the standings. 

The league standings remain a crowded house, and with performances like these, the race for the top spot is officially shaping up to be a marathon, not a sprint. Keep your skates sharp, folks—the mid-season grind is just getting interesting.
