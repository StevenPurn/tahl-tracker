# The Outsiders Keep Rolling: Waller Hat-Trick Sinks Hot Garbage

If you showed up to the rink expecting a quiet morning skate, you were clearly in the wrong building. In a high-octane regular season tilt that lived up to the intensity of two teams looking to climb the standings, The Outsiders held off a gritty Hot Garbage squad to secure a 6-4 victory and maintain their grip on the top spot in the league.

The story of the morning was undoubtedly the man wearing the cape—or, in this case, the jersey—for The Outsiders. Tyler Waller was absolutely dialed in, putting on a clinic that would make a seasoned pro blush. Waller kicked off the scoring barrage just 12:59 into the first period, and he didn’t stop until he had completed a clinical hat-trick. By the time he netted his third of the day early in the second, the Hot Garbage bench looked like they were trying to solve a Rubik’s cube in a wind tunnel.

"When Waller gets that look in his eye, you just get out of the way and try to find an open lane," joked Outsiders captain J.W. DePriest after the game. "He was finding the back of the net like he was playing against empty buckets."

Hot Garbage, however, refused to be swept away. Despite falling behind early, they showcased the kind of resilience that makes this league so much fun to cover. Abigail Nisbet got the momentum swinging in the first period, capitalizing on some slick setup work from Joe Baker and Joshua Hall. When Zachary Hughes and Aaron Whisner rattled off back-to-back goals in the second, the tension in the arena was thick enough to cut with a skate blade, pulling the game to a nail-biting 3-3 deadlock.

Just as it seemed the momentum might tilt entirely toward the Hot Garbage, The Outsiders reminded everyone why they’re sitting at the top of the table. DePriest silenced the mini-rally with a critical goal late in the second, assisted by Michael Wixted and Brett Lamb, to regain the lead heading into the final frame.

The third period turned into a back-and-forth track meet. Cody Dinsmore extended the Outsiders' lead early, but Joshua Hall responded for Hot Garbage midway through, keeping the dream of a comeback alive with an assist from Nisbet and Baker. However, Scott Hellard finally slammed the door shut at the 9:01 mark, burying a feed from Logan Pardue to seal the 6-4 final.

While the scoreboard shows a loss for Hot Garbage, their refusal to go down without a fight was commendable. For The Outsiders, it’s another two points in the bank and proof that their current form is no fluke. As the dust settles on this 11:45 AM barn-burner, one thing is certain: if these two teams meet again, leave your phone in the car and your eyes on the ice—because if you blink, you’ll miss the next highlight.
