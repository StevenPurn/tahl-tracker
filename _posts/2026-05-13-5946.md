# The Syndicate Put the Cold Ones on Ice in 8-3 Shellacking

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: May 13, 2026**

If you were looking for a quiet Tuesday night at the rink, you picked the wrong zip code. Last night’s late-night tilt between the Kraken Cold Ones and The Syndicate was less of a hockey game and more of a clinic in offensive efficiency. When the final horn mercifully sounded at 9:30 PM, The Syndicate skated away with a commanding 8-3 victory, leaving the Cold Ones searching for answers in the locker room.

### Period 1: Syndicate Sets the Tempo
The Syndicate came out of the gate looking like they had rocket fuel in their skates. Brett Mooney kicked off the party at the 11:01 mark, courtesy of a tidy feed from Travis Moore. That seemed to wake the sleeping dragon, as the goals began to pile up faster than a snowstorm in December. 

Daron Ross buried one just moments later, and by the time Brett Mecham went solo to find the twine, the Cold Ones were reeling. Chase Stubblefield finally managed to light the lamp for the Kraken at 3:20, banking one in off an Edgar Solorio helper, but Mooney wasn’t done. He sniped his second of the night at 9:34, sending The Syndicate into the first intermission with a lead that felt insurmountable.

### Period 2: Penalty Trouble and Power Plays
The Cold Ones looked to claw their way back early in the second, with Randy Hatfield cutting the lead to 5-2. But the momentum shifted back to The Syndicate almost instantly when Michael Marshall struck back just thirty seconds later.

Discipline became a luxury the Cold Ones simply couldn’t afford. Paul Davis headed to the box for tripping, and The Syndicate turned the screw. Kacy Griffin capitalized on the power play, cleaning up the garbage in front of the net to make it 6-2. With Hatfield also spending time in the sin bin for high-sticking shortly after, the Cold Ones spent more time looking at the back of the penalty box glass than they did at the Syndicate net.

### Period 3: Closing the Barn Door
To their credit, the Kraken didn’t fold the tent. Edgar Solorio muscled home an unassisted tally late in the third, showing a spark of grit. However, The Syndicate’s transition game was just too sharp. Kalea Maxwell added to the tally, and Kacy Griffin notched his second of the night at 6:24, effectively closing the book on any hopes of a miracle comeback.

### The Bottom Line
For The Syndicate, this victory moves them to 4 points on the season, proving they are a force to be reckoned with. They moved the puck with surgical precision, rarely forced the play, and showed a knack for finding the "high-danger" areas that every coach dreams of.

For the Kraken Cold Ones, it’s a tough pill to swallow. Sitting at 0 points, the squad needs to clean up the defensive zone traffic and avoid the trips to the penalty box if they want to get their season off the ground. The road ahead is long, and as any veteran of the game will tell you, the best way to get over a loss is to lace them back up and get back to work. 

The ice might be cold, but the intensity is just heating up. We’ll see if the Cold Ones can thaw out their game before the next puck drop!
