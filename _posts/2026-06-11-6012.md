# Bison Stampede: Tulsa Overcomes Early Deficit to Corral the Berzerkers

**TULSA** – If you’re a fan of the “never-say-die” brand of hockey, last night at the rink was exactly what the doctor ordered. In a late-night barn burner that didn't wrap up until the clock struck well past midnight, the Tulsa Bison surged in the third period to snatch a 4-2 victory from the Berzerkers. 

Coming into the June 10th tilt, both squads were sitting at 2 points in a crowded middle-of-the-pack standings race. It was a game defined by two very different halves: the Berzerkers controlled the tempo early, but the Bison proved that when you keep grinding, the floodgates eventually burst open.

### Period 1: The Berzerkers Bite First
The Berzerkers came out looking like they’d had an extra shot of espresso before the puck drop. They clogged the neutral zone, forcing the Bison to dump and chase with little success. At the 10:17 mark, **Stephen Jeffery** proved why he’s been on a tear, finding the back of the net unassisted to put the visitors up 1-0. The Bison looked a bit sluggish, struggling to find any "traffic in front of the net" as the Berzerkers' defense kept the lanes cleaner than a fresh sheet of ice.

### Period 2: Digging the Hole
The second frame saw the Berzerkers double their lead when **Nikolas Kemmler** finished a clean feed from **Evert Wilmeth** at 12:25. Down 2-0, tempers began to flare. A tripping call on **Sean Widdows** followed shortly by an unsportsmanlike minor for the surging **Juan Padilla** put the Bison in a precarious spot. 

However, sometimes a little bit of "sin bin" time is exactly what a team needs to wake up. The Bison penalty kill was surgical, refusing to let the Berzerkers put the game on ice. The Bison skated into the locker room at the second intermission down by two, but they were finally starting to find their skating legs.

### Period 3: The Herd Stampedes
Whatever was said in the Bison locker room during the break, they should bottle it and sell it. The third period was, quite simply, an absolute masterclass in offensive pressure.

**Geoffery Rash** finally broke the seal at 12:48, courtesy of a slick setup from Padilla, cutting the lead to 2-1. Momentum shifted violently in Tulsa’s favor. After an interference penalty by **Edward Stern** left the Berzerkers shorthanded, the pressure intensified. With just under 30 seconds left on the clock in the third—in a moment of pure "clutch" heroics—**JW DePriest** buried an unassisted goal to tie the game.

But the Bison weren’t satisfied with overtime. With the Berzerkers reeling, **Justin Phillips** hammered home the go-ahead goal at the 7:30 mark (assisted by Rash), and just a minute later, **Juan Padilla** cemented his reputation as the man of the match, lighting the lamp to bring the final score to 4-2. The building was electric as the Bison completed a four-goal third-period comeback that will surely be the talk of the locker room for the next week.

### Looking at the Standings
With this win, the Tulsa Bison (2-1-0) leapfrog several teams, moving into the top half of the standings with 4 points. They’ve officially shaken off their recent form and are looking like a legitimate threat to climb into the top tier. 

For the Berzerkers (1-1-0), it’s a tough pill to swallow. They controlled the ice for 30 minutes, but in a league this competitive, you have to close the door—and tonight, the Bison kicked it right off the hinges.

The race for the top spot is tightening, and if last night was any indication, this season is going to be a real, honest-to-goodness dogfight. Stick with us, folks—this is exactly why we love this game.
