# Bar Down Boys Edge Puckheads in Sunday Morning Thriller

If you were looking for a reason to crawl out of bed at 9:45 on a Sunday morning, the Bar Down Boys and the Puckheads provided the perfect incentive. In a back-and-forth tilt that felt more like a street fight on ice than a mid-morning regular season contest, the Bar Down Boys managed to skate away with a 5-3 victory, putting their first 'W' of the season in the books.

The first period was a frantic affair that had both goalies looking like they’d just seen a ghost. Jeffrey Greco struck first for the Bar Down Boys, but the Puckheads responded with the kind of ferocity you usually only see when the coffee machine breaks. Matthew Cooper—who clearly had his Weetabix this morning—netted his first of the day, followed shortly by Cody Diehl. With Richard Retkowski playing the role of primary provider, the Puckheads looked to be hitting their stride. However, Tyler Wimpee decided he wasn't going to let the intermission happen without a response, scoring an unassisted beauty at 5:39 to keep the score knotted up.

If the first period was a sprint, the second felt like a chess match played with sledgehammers. Mark Hodges reclaimed the lead for the Boys off a smooth feed from Kirk Nielsen, but Cooper wasn't about to let that stand, grabbing his second goal of the morning to keep the Puckheads firmly in the hunt. By the time the buzzer sounded for the second break, the ice was chewed up and the tension in the stands was thick enough to cut with a dull skate blade.

The turning point arrived in the third, as the Bar Down Boys found another gear. Tyler Wimpee grabbed his second of the game at the 5:02 mark, and Jeffrey Greco mirrored that effort by potting his second goal of the day halfway through the period, putting the game out of reach for a scrappy Puckheads squad. 

Tempers flared late in the frame, with Kellen Nitz heading to the sin bin for hooking and Emil Hinkefent joining him shortly after for slashing. It made for a messy finish, but the Boys locked down the defensive zone to secure the 5-3 result.

"We just kept hitting the spots," Greco said, nursing a post-game Gatorade. "It’s early in the season, and you don’t want to be staring at a goose egg in the win column for too long. We found our rhythm, and the bounces finally went our way."

The win gives the Bar Down Boys a vital jump in the standings, moving them out of the pack of teams that opened the season with a solitary point. For the Puckheads, it’s a tough morning at the office, but with plenty of hockey left to play, they’ll have to tighten up the defensive shell before their next outing. All in all, not a bad way to spend a Sunday morning.
