# Perfection Personified: Shooting Blanks Stave Off Tornadoes in 6-3 Thriller

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
*August 2, 2026 — 8:45 PM Puck Drop*

If you were looking for a sign that the Shooting Blanks are human, tonight’s tilt at the barn wasn’t it. Entering the night as the undefeated kings of the mountain (8-0-0), the Blanks faced a desperate Tornadoes squad hungry to snap a five-game slide. For two periods, it looked like we might have a massive upset brewing, but when the dust settled, the league leaders remained perfect with a 6-3 victory.

### Period 1: A Seesaw Start
The game opened with the intensity of a rivalry match. **Hunter Best**—continuing his ridiculous eight-game point streak—struck first just 1:27 into the contest, burying a feed from Austin Tyler and Bret Kurth. 

But the Tornadoes weren’t about to roll over. They responded a mere 30 seconds later when **Tobias Alvegran** danced through traffic, leveling the score at 1-1. The Tornadoes kept their foot on the gas, even while shorthanded, when **Brendan Budnik** hammered one home off a crisp dish from Christian Alvegran at the 7:14 mark. Despite a "Too Many Men" penalty that had the bench buzzing, the Tornadoes skated into the locker room with a surprising 2-1 lead.

### Period 2: The Momentum Swings
The second frame began with the Tornadoes looking like the team that belonged in the top spot. **Alex Crowder** pushed the lead to 3-1 early, cleaning up a rebound created by the tandem of Christian Alvegran and Nicklas Clark.

That, however, was the wake-up call the Shooting Blanks needed. Shaking off the rust, the Blanks began to hem the Tornadoes in their own zone. **Bret Kurth** ignited the comeback at 4:57, and **Aaron Best**—channeling his inner sniper—knotted things up at 3-3 with just under 12 minutes to play in the period. The momentum shift was palpable; you could feel the breeze from the Blanks' skates as they transitioned from "struggling" to "unstoppable."

### Period 3: The Closing Act
The final frame was a clinic in discipline and finishing. The Blanks tightened the screws defensively, turning the neutral zone into a minefield. 

**Bret Kurth** played the hero, sniping his second of the night at 10:43 to give the Blanks a lead they wouldn’t relinquish. Even a late tripping penalty to Kurth couldn’t spark the Tornadoes. Instead, the Blanks capitalized on their transition game. **Aaron Best** doubled his tally at 13:52, and just 24 seconds later, **Bret Kurth** capped off a natural hat trick, sending a rocket into the top shelf where mom keeps the cookies. 

### The Aftermath
For the Shooting Blanks, this wasn't just a win; it was a statement. At 9-0-0, they are playing a brand of hockey that is as suffocating as it is creative. They’ve proven they can absorb an early punch and still dismantle an opponent.

For the Tornadoes, the 2-8-0 reality is harsh. Despite a high-energy performance from the likes of Clark and Alvegran, they couldn’t withstand the relentless pressure of a championship-caliber roster. They’ll head back to the drawing board hoping to find a way to finish the job, while the Blanks continue their march toward what looks like a flawless regular season.

**Final Score:** Shooting Blanks 6, Tornadoes 3.
