# Feathered Fury: Dirty Birds Clip Havok’s Wings in 10-7 Goal Fest

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
*July 28, 2026*

If you like your hockey served with a side of absolute chaos and a heap of defensive lapses, last night’s tilt at the rink was your personal buffet. In a game that felt less like a structured athletic contest and more like an organized street fight on ice, the Dirty Birds outlasted Havok 10-7 in a classic barn burner that surely gave both coaching staffs a collective migraine.

### Period 1: The Offensive Fireworks Start Early
The Birds came out of the gate looking like they’d been injected with rocket fuel. By the 13:28 mark of the first, Nicklas Clark had already notched two goals, sandwiching a silky smooth finish from Elliott Langford. 

But Havok, despite their recent slide, wasn’t about to let the Birds fly away with it. Adam Carter reminded everyone why he’s a dangerous threat, bagging a quick-fire pair of goals in under 30 seconds to slash the deficit. Langford responded with his second of the night, but Michael Pasque answered right back. By the end of the first, the scoreboard looked more like a grocery bill than a hockey score—a frantic 4-3 lead for the Birds.

### Period 2: Grinding Out the Gap
The second frame was where the Dirty Birds decided to tighten the screws. James Smith found the back of the net on a beautiful feed from Gabriel Kierath, and Langford completed his hat-trick magic shortly after. 

Havok’s Pasque continued his one-man wrecking crew act, netting his second of the night, but the Birds simply refused to stay grounded. Dylan Criquet Danielson added a solo effort, and despite a late high-sticking penalty to James Smith that threatened to give Havok life, the Birds maintained a firm grip on the momentum heading into the final intermission.

### Period 3: The Clockwork Comeback (and the Closing Door)
The third period was pure, unadulterated madness. Logan Singleton struck early for Havok to pull them closer, but the Birds’ top line had finally hit their stride. Between the 1:50 and 2:46 marks, the Birds exploded. Smith, Kierath, and Clark—who capped off an absolute monster game—turned on the red light three times in quick succession.

Havok tried to make it interesting late with Pasque’s hat-trick goal and a last-gasp effort from Marshall Nunneley, but the deficit was too steep to climb. As the final buzzer sounded, the Dirty Birds walked away with a 10-7 victory that proved one thing: they are the most explosive offense in the league right now.

### The Breakdown
**Dirty Birds (7-1-0):** They continue to push for the top spot, keeping the pressure squarely on the Killer Goats. Nicklas Clark was the man of the hour, orchestrating the offense with surgical precision. When you score ten goals, you don't need a Vezina-caliber performance in net—you just need to keep your skates moving, and the Birds did exactly that.

**Havok (3-4-1):** It’s a tough pill to swallow for the fourth-place squad. While Michael Pasque’s hat trick was a highlight reel performance, the defensive breakdowns in the third period turned a competitive game into a lopsided affair. They’ll need to stop the bleeding if they want to stay in the hunt for the upper echelon of the standings.

**Final Take:** 
The Dirty Birds are flying high, and they look every bit like a championship contender. As for Havok, they have the firepower, but they need to find a way to keep their opponents from finding the back of the net as often as they do. If you’re a fan of high-octane, "top shelf where mom keeps the cookies" hockey, this was the game of the year.

Stay tuned, folks. The playoff race is officially heating up, and it’s about to get a whole lot colder on the ice.
