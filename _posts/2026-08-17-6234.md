# Bison Stampede: Tulsa Dominates Battle of the Titans

In a matchup billed as the heavyweight clash of the season between the league’s top two squads, the Tulsa Bison emphatically proved that there is a significant distance between "very good" and "untouchable." The Bison extended their winning streak to six games on Sunday night, dismantling the Mayhem Wreck+ with a decisive 6-1 victory that wasn't even as close as the scoreline suggests.

The Mayhem Wreck+ arrived at the rink riding a hot streak of their own, and for the first 14 minutes, they looked the part. Justin McKinney ignited the offense early, burying a feed from Nick Hatfield at the 14:27 mark of the first period. For a fleeting moment, it appeared we were destined for a tight, tactical grind. 

Then, the spreadsheet broke.

At 4:53, a tripping penalty on McKinney opened the door for the Bison, and they didn't just walk through it; they kicked the frame off the hinges. Exactly one second later, Juan Padilla found the back of the net to equalize. Before the Wreck+ could recover their defensive structure, Brett Mooney struck at 7:35 to put Tulsa ahead for good. It was a momentum shift so violent it likely registered on local seismographs.

"We gave them an inch on the power play and they took a country mile," a visibly frustrated member of the Mayhem bench remarked after the final whistle, perhaps while trying to calculate the xG (expected goals) that clearly didn't factor in such a catastrophic defensive collapse.

The second period served as a masterclass in Bison efficiency. With the clock winding down, Joe Baker pushed the lead to 3-1 with just 24 seconds remaining in the frame—a back-breaking goal that essentially sucked the oxygen out of the Mayhem locker room. JW DePriest added insult to injury just over three minutes into the final frame, extending the lead to 4-1.

If the game wasn't already decided, the Bison’s special teams unit ensured it was. Brett Mooney added his second goal of the night at 11:02, and Brett Mecham capped the scoring at 3:46, turning the final ten minutes into a exercise in frustration for the Mayhem. The Wreck+ discipline completely eroded in the closing stages, with a flurry of penalties—including a double-minor for unsportsmanlike conduct to Ivy Emerson and multiple roughing calls—painting a picture of a team that had mentally checked out long before the final buzzer.

Statistically, the Bison were clinical. Brett Mooney’s multi-goal, multi-assist performance solidified his status as the current engine of this Tulsa juggernaut, while the Mayhem Wreck+ were left to wonder how a 1-0 lead transformed into a five-goal deficit. 

The standings are now clearly defined. At 10-2-0, the Tulsa Bison are playing a brand of hockey that borders on the unfair, widening the gap at the top of the table. For the 8-3-1 Wreck+, the task is simple: find the reset button, ignore the box score from August 16th, and hope the playoffs don't require them to face the Bison on a night when the puck is magnetically attracted to the Tulsa sticks.
