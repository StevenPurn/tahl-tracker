# Puckadelics Roll Over Gold as Ice Bronze in 5-1 Masterclass

If you were looking for a high-octane display of how to handle business on a Saturday afternoon, look no further than the ice at the local rink. The Puckadelics kept their momentum rolling, skating away with a dominant 5-1 victory over Gold as Ice Bronze. With this win, the Puckadelics are proving they aren't just here to participate; they’re here to make the rest of the league sweat.

For the first frame, the game was a defensive chess match that could’ve lulled a spectator into a mid-afternoon nap. Both squads were tight, the goalies were dialed in, and the puck was spending more time pinballing off skates than finding the back of the net. But once the second period hit, the Puckadelics decided it was time to turn the heat up. 

Brett Mooney became the architect of the afternoon, breaking the ice with a beauty of a goal at the 22.4 mark, courtesy of a slick setup from Cody Kern. Mooney wasn't done, though. After a tripping penalty forced Gold as Ice Bronze to play a man down, Mooney struck again at the 5:17 mark, unassisted, doubling the lead and effectively shifting the entire energy of the bench.

Gold as Ice Bronze didn't go quietly, mind you. Triston Wilson found the back of the net at the 7:45 mark, capitalizing on a crisp pass from Joshua Smith to keep his point streak alive and give the Bronze faithful a reason to cheer. For a moment, it felt like the comeback was brewing.

But the third period belonged entirely to the Puckadelics’ depth. Timothy N See started the late-game parade at the 22.9 mark, assisted by Mark Hodges. Once the floodgates opened, they stayed open. Kirk Nielsen added to the tally at the 9:00 mark, and before the dust had even settled, Mark Hodges hammered the final nail in the coffin just 29 seconds later. It was a 29-second flurry that turned a competitive game into a runaway train, with Pete Fleming providing the playmaking touch on both of those final strikes.

"We just stopped watching the puck and started chasing it," said one frustrated observer from the stands, but credit has to go to the Puckadelics’ persistence. They swarmed the zone and made the Bronze defense look like they were skating through wet cement in those final minutes.

With this result, the Puckadelics continue their climb, looking every bit the contender they were hyped to be. Meanwhile, Gold as Ice Bronze will have to head back to the drawing board to find some consistency after a tough afternoon. As the buzzer sounded, the message was clear: the Puckadelics are a team on a mission, and they aren't slowing down for anyone.
