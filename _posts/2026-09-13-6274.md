# Skate Goats Ram Through Gold as Ice Bronze in 8-1 Rout

If you wandered into the rink looking for a defensive stalemate this morning, you were likely in the wrong zip code. The Skate Goats arrived at the ice with their horns sharpened and a hunger that Gold as Ice Bronze simply couldn’t satisfy, skating away with a decisive 8-1 victory in what turned out to be a total clinic.

The tone was set early. Joshua Gonya wasted absolutely no time, tickling the twine just 10.7 seconds into the opening period. It was the kind of start that makes a goalie want to check if his equipment is actually strapped on right. To their credit, Gold as Ice Bronze didn't fold immediately; Mark DeSimone buried a beauty off a feed from Lance Levasseur near the 12-minute mark to knot it up at 1-1. For a fleeting moment, it looked like we had a hockey game on our hands.

That flicker of hope was quickly extinguished. Seth Miller answered back less than forty seconds later, and by the time Josh Mosley—who had spent a brief stint in the sin bin for hooking—returned to the ice to score a goal of his own at the 7:34 mark, the Goats were in full gallop. 

By the time we hit the second period, it wasn’t just a game; it was a full-blown stampede. The Skate Goats’ puck movement looked like they were playing keep-away with a bunch of toddlers. Andrew Vandiver and Alex Ruiz turned the second frame into their personal highlight reel, with Vandiver finding the back of the net and Ruiz adding an unassisted tally. By the time Travis Miller cleaned up a pass from Vandiver to make it 6-1, the Gold as Ice Bronze bench looked like they were ready to pack up the gear and head for an early lunch.

"I think the guys just had an extra scoop of oats in their breakfast bowl today," laughed one Skate Goats fan leaning against the glass after the final horn. "They didn't just skate; they stampeded."

The third period served as the victory lap. Andrew Vandiver, clearly not content with just one goal, decided to turn the final frame into a one-man wrecking crew. He notched two more goals in the span of less than a minute—one at 4:21 and another at 5:06—to firmly cement his status as the MVP of the morning. Even a couple of late-game trips by Erik Chillingworth and James DePriest couldn't stop the runaway train, as the Goats’ penalty kill held firm to keep the Bronze squad at bay.

With this result, the Skate Goats effectively shed their opening-day draw and are officially looking like the team to beat in this young season. As for Gold as Ice Bronze, it’s a tough morning at the office, but as they say in these parts: the ice is slick, the puck is round, and there’s always next week to lace them up and try again. For now, the Goats head home with a statement win, leaving the rest of the league to wonder if they’ve got a recipe to stop this herd.
