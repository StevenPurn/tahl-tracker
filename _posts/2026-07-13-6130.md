# Berzerkers Stave Off Kraken Comeback in Friday Night Nail-Biter

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: July 12, 2026**

The ice was barely dry at the arena tonight, but it didn’t take long for things to get greasy. In a classic battle of “haves and have-nots,” the fourth-place Berzerkers narrowly escaped the Kraken Cold Ones with a 3-2 victory in a game that proved why you never look past an opponent, no matter what the standings say.

### Period 1: A Rapid Response
The Kraken Cold Ones, looking to shake off a rough skid, drew first blood at the 13:47 mark. Justin McKinney managed to weave through some heavy traffic, finding the back of the net to give the visitors a stunning early lead. But if you blinked, you missed the response. Just 32 seconds later, Berzerkers’ own Mik Halgren—riding high on a point streak—fired back, burying an unassisted beauty to level the score at 1-1. It was the kind of quick-strike hockey that gets the blood pumping on a Friday night.

### Period 2: The Momentum Swing
The second frame was all about the Berzerkers finding their groove. After killing off a minor scare, the Kraken’s Justin McKinney found himself in the sin bin for a trip at 12:15. The Berzerkers didn't capitalize on the power play itself, but they used the momentum to dictate the pace.

Just as it looked like we were heading to the break tied, Evert Wilmeth—who is currently playing like a man possessed—found the twine with a mere 20 seconds remaining on the clock. Assisted by Edward Stern, Wilmeth’s heroics extended his point streak to a staggering seven games. Not content with a one-goal lead, Cody Stephens doubled the trouble for the Kraken at the 6:36 mark, hammering home a goal that had the home bench feeling mighty comfortable.

### Period 3: Hanging On for Dear Life
Trailing 3-1, the Kraken Cold Ones weren't ready to pack up their gear. They turned up the heat, and at 13:06, Robert Tyree tapped one in off beautiful setups from McKinney and Douglas Moore to bring it back to a one-goal game. 

The building went silent at 13:15 when LC III Sinor was whistled for a high-sticking penalty, putting the Berzerkers’ defense in the pressure cooker. With the Kraken smelling blood in the water, the Berzerkers’ goaltending stood tall, absorbing the flurry of shots and clogging the lanes to protect the house. When the final buzzer sounded, the Berzerkers had survived the late-game surge, walking away with a gritty 3-2 win.

### The Aftermath
With this victory, the Berzerkers (5-1-1) solidify their position in the top half of the standings, keeping the pressure on the league leaders. They proved tonight that they have the composure to lock down a lead when it matters most.

For the Kraken Cold Ones (1-4-1), the loss is a heartbreaker, but there is plenty of sunlight in their performance. They played a hard-nosed game and nearly forced a deadlock against one of the league's juggernauts. If they keep playing with this kind of desperation, that win column is bound to start filling up sooner rather than later.
