# Killer Goats Finally Find the Path to Victory, Topping Honey Badgers 5-2

If there was one thing the Killer Goats were sick of hearing about, it was their own inability to close the deal. After opening their season with a pair of frustrating ties, the Goats finally shook the monkey off their back Friday night, marching into the rink and handing the Honey Badgers a 5-2 defeat.

The Badgers, still looking for their first win of the season, found themselves chasing shadows early. Aaron Quinton kept his hot streak alive, lighting the lamp just 57 seconds into the opening frame to set the tone. It was a cold start for the Badgers, and things didn't get any warmer when Michael Bergstrom hammered one home off a crisp feed from Samuel Michaud at the 7:05 mark. By the time the horn sounded to end the first, the Badgers looked like they’d rather be anywhere else but between the pipes.

"We were moving the puck like we were trying to avoid a bee sting," one Badger told me near the benches after the game, shaking his head. "We gave them too much room, and they made us pay for every inch of it."

The second period was where the game really turned into a rollercoaster. Justin Houde provided a glimmer of hope for Honey Badgers fans, snapping one past the keeper at 11:19 thanks to a setup from Mathew Sproles. But the Goats weren't about to let this one slip away. Samuel Michaud, who was clearly the most dangerous man on the ice all night, decided to take matters into his own hands. He notched an unassisted goal at 2:05, and even after a tripping penalty sent Tyler McGarry to the box, Michaud didn't slow down. He struck again shorthanded at the 4:30 mark to effectively take the wind out of the Badgers' sails.

To their credit, the Honey Badgers refused to roll over and play dead. Houde snagged his second of the night—another nifty assist from Sproles—with just seconds left in the second period, keeping the pressure on. But that momentum evaporated in the third. 

The final nail in the coffin came halfway through the third period when Dylan Criquet Danielson found the twine, courtesy of a tidy pass from Lucas Blanchard, pushing the lead to 5-2. A late tripping penalty on Houde served as the exclamation point on a night where the Badgers just couldn't find an answer for the Goats' relentless forecheck.

For the Killer Goats, this 5-2 result is a massive relief. They finally climb out of the "draw-only" doldrums and get that first 'W' in the win column. As for the Honey Badgers, they’ll have to head back to the drawing board. They’re still hunting for that elusive first victory of the season, and if tonight was any indication, they’ve got a lot of work to do before they can start moving up the standings. 

The Goats looked hungry, the Badgers looked hesitant, and the scoreboard told the whole story. It wasn't always pretty, but for the folks in the Goats' locker room, it was exactly the kind of medicine they needed to keep their season on track.
