# Hot Garbage Stuns Undefeated Mayhem Wreck+ in 5-2 Upset

It’s been said that even a blind squirrel finds an acorn now and then, but on Saturday afternoon at the rink, Hot Garbage didn't just find an acorn—they marched into the house of the league’s second-ranked team and served up a cold reality check. Entering the day with a perfect 7-0-1 record, Mayhem Wreck+ looked primed to keep their momentum rolling, but they ran headlong into a determined Hot Garbage squad that played with the kind of grit usually reserved for teams fighting for their playoff lives. 

The final score read 5-2 in favor of Hot Garbage, a result that felt a whole lot less like an accident and more like a tactical masterclass.

The game started with Mayhem Wreck+ looking like the favorites they are. Sage Blubaugh opened the scoring at the 10:05 mark of the first period, set up beautifully by Ivy Emerson. It looked like the script was set. However, Hot Garbage answered back before the intermission when Nicholas Brewer, riding a hot stick with a two-game goal streak, buried an equalizer assisted by Justin Phillips.

The second period was where the wheels began to wobble for Mayhem. While Ivy Emerson reclaimed the lead for Wreck+ at 10:18—again, with Blubaugh playing the playmaker—the momentum swung decisively as the period wore on. John Bewley knotted things up at 3:35, and just three minutes later, Nicole Baker provided the go-ahead goal, unassisted. Baker’s hustle seemed to take the wind right out of the Mayhem sails.

"We told the boys if we just kept the puck moving and stopped trying to sauce it through three guys, we might actually see the back of the net," joked a Hot Garbage team spokesperson after the buzzer. "Usually, we live up to the name on our jerseys, but today, we actually looked like a hockey team."

The third period turned into a defensive struggle that Mayhem simply couldn't win. Hot Garbage kept the pressure high, with Joshua Hall extending his goal streak at 4:40 off a feed from Tyler Holmes, followed quickly by a dagger from Tyler Waller at the 6:04 mark to push the lead to 5-2. 

By the time Walter Foddis was sent to the box for hooking at 9:50, Mayhem Wreck+ appeared resigned to their fate. The once-mighty Wreck+ attack, which had been buzzing for five straight wins, found themselves stifled by a Hot Garbage defense that refused to give them any clean looks at the crease in the final frame.

For Mayhem Wreck+, the loss is a bitter pill to swallow, especially with such a strong standing in the league. For Hot Garbage, the win is a massive confidence booster for a team sitting in the lower half of the table. They proved that on any given Saturday, even the league leaders can be taken off their pedestal if you’re willing to put in the work. 

The schedule moves on, but for the rest of the league, this result serves as a reminder: don't look at the standings if you want to know who’s going to win the game. Just look at the scoreboard once the final horn blows.
