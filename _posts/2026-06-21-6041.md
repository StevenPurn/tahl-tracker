# Chix with Stix Edge Out Mother Puckers in Sunday Morning Barn Burner

**By: Our Hometown Hockey Beat Reporter**
**Date: June 21, 2026**

If you think Sunday morning is for sleeping in, you clearly weren't at the rink today. While the rest of the city was hitting the snooze button, the **Chix with Stix** and the **Mother Puckers** were busy turning the ice into a tactical war zone. In a back-and-forth thriller that had the fans on the edge of their seats, the Chix managed to tilt the ice in their favor, skating away with a hard-fought 4-3 victory.

### First Period: Trading Leather
The energy was high from the opening puck drop, and it didn't take long for the ice to get hot. **Nicole Baker**, riding a serious wave of momentum, opened the scoring at the 10:09 mark. With helpers from **Carson See** and **Jordan Hawkins Baker**, it was a clinic in puck movement. 

The Mother Puckers, however, have never been a team to back down from a fight. After weathering a 2-minute tripping penalty courtesy of Kalea Maxwell, the Puckers struck back. **Makenzie Dillard** proved she’s got eyes in the back of her head, finding the twine at 8:32 to knot the game up at 1-1.

### Second Period: The Dillard-Christian Show
If the first period was a tactical chess match, the second was a heavy-weight scrap. **Mckenna N. Allison** reclaimed the lead for the Chix early, but the Puckers weren't having any of it. **Makenzie Dillard** found her second of the day, assisted by the ever-dangerous **Malori Christian**, and just minutes later, **Emily LaRance**—also set up by Christian—buried one to give the Mother Puckers a 3-2 lead heading into the final frame. 

The goaltending on both ends was clinical, keeping the score from spiraling as the forecheck intensified and the traffic in front of the net grew thick enough to park a Zamboni.

### Third Period: The Comeback Celly
Down by one, the Chix with Stix showed the composure of a veteran squad. **Carson See** took matters into her own hands at 14:02, weaving through the neutral zone to tie the game at 3-3. 

The tension reached a boiling point when Ashley Ivanoff sat for a tripping minor, giving the Puckers a golden opportunity to steal the game. But the Chix penalty kill was absolute steel. Fresh off the kill, **Nicole Baker**—the hero of the morning—found the back of the net for her second of the game at 8:24, with Jordan Hawkins Baker adding the secondary assist. It was a sniper’s finish, tucked away neatly "where Mom keeps the cookies."

### The Final Snapshot
The Chix with Stix held firm in the dying minutes, keeping the Puckers at bay and securing a vital 4-3 win. 

**What this means for the standings:**
With this win, the Chix with Stix (3-2-0) successfully leapfrog the pack, solidifying their position as a legitimate contender in the top half of the table. They’ve proven they can play from behind and close out tight games—a trait that will serve them well as the season heats up. 

For the Mother Puckers, it’s a tough pill to swallow, but they proved today that they can hang with the league’s best. They’ll look to iron out those defensive lapses as they chase the top-tier teams in the standings. 

One thing is for sure: if these two meet again, clear your schedule. You won't want to miss a second of the action.
