# WENDIGO WINTER: Zac Hughes Turns the Ice into His Personal Playground in 7-1 Rout of Reapers

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: July 19, 2026**

If you caught the 11:45 AM puck drop at the barn today, you might have felt a chill—and no, it wasn’t just the A/C cranking at the rink. The Wendigos (#11) decided to wake up from their recent slumber, handing the Reapers (#10) a 7-1 reality check in a game that turned into a masterclass in offensive efficiency.

### First Period: Setting the Tone
The Reapers came in looking for a spark, but they found themselves digging a hole early. Evert Wilmeth headed to the sin bin for slashing just 93 seconds in, and the Wendigos didn’t waste any time capitalizing on the extra room. Taylor Chinowth drew first blood, cleaning up a dish from Mark Smith to make it 1-0. 

Just minutes later, Zac Hughes—who would go on to have a morning for the record books—started his assault on the Reapers’ defense, going coast-to-coast for an unassisted beauty to double the lead. By the time the buzzer sounded for the break, the Reapers were looking over their shoulders, down 2-0 and struggling to find their legs.

### Second Period: The Hughes Hat-Trick and Then Some
If the first period was a warning, the second was a full-blown blizzard. Zac Hughes decided the middle frame was his personal highlight reel. He notched two quick goals in the span of less than 40 seconds, completing a natural hat trick that sent the Reapers’ netminder into a state of visible frustration. 

Not content with just potting them, Hughes turned playmaker at the 8:41 mark, feeding Kalea Maxwell for a rocket that extended the lead to 5-0. The Wendigos were cycling the puck like a well-oiled machine, leaving the Reapers chasing shadows in their own zone.

### Third Period: Closing the Book
The final frame saw the Wendigos add insult to injury when Hughes picked up his *fourth* goal of the morning on a helper from Kenneth Watkins. 

The Reapers finally managed to break the goose egg with about four minutes left, finding the back of the net in a scramble to make it 6-1. But the Wendigos, perhaps feeling a bit generous or just looking to pad the stats one last time, responded immediately. Taylor Chinowth bagged his second of the game on a setup from—who else?—Zac Hughes, finalizing the scoreline at 7-1.

A late-game parade to the penalty box (featuring coincidental minors for Solorio and Robbins) provided a little bit of physical flair, but the result was never in doubt.

### The Bottom Line
Zac Hughes walked away with a staggering five-point performance (4G, 1A), effectively ending his point-streak-turned-point-explosion in spectacular fashion. For the Wendigos, this massive win is a much-needed injection of confidence. They leapfrog the Reapers in the standings, moving to 2-5-0 and proving that even a team at the bottom of the table can catch fire when the puck bounces their way.

The Reapers, meanwhile, fall to 1-6-1 and will need to head back to the drawing board. It’s a tough pill to swallow, but in this league, the ice is slippery and the turnaround is fast. If they want to climb out of the basement, they’ll need to tighten up the traffic in front of their crease before their next outing.

**Final Score: Reapers 1, Wendigos 7**
*The Wendigos take the points, the bragging rights, and perhaps the coldest performance of the season.*
