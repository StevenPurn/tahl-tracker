# ICE BREAKERS CRACK THE CODE: A 4-3 THRILLER AGAINST THE JOES

**DATE:** May 31, 2026  
**LOCATION:** The Rink  
**FINAL SCORE:** Ice Breakers 4, Average Joes 3

If you were looking for a Saturday morning wake-up call, you didn’t need a double-shot espresso—you just needed a ticket to this morning’s tilt. The Ice Breakers and Average Joes met on the ice at 11:45 AM and proceeded to turn the rink into a absolute barn burner. When the final horn sounded, the Ice Breakers walked away with a hard-fought 4-3 victory, proving that while these guys might be "Average," they certainly play with extraordinary heart.

### First Period: Trading Leather
The Ice Breakers came out of the gate looking like they’d already had their morning coffee. Just 17 seconds into the game, **Tobias Roeding** lit the lamp, assisted by **Justice Dick**, to put the Breakers up early. It was a sniper’s dream—a quick release that left the Joes’ netminder searching for his water bottle. 

But you can’t keep a good squad down for long. At the 8:53 mark, the Joes answered back. **Jordan Hawkins Baker** cashed in on a beautiful setup from **Sergio Castellanos** and **Elizabeth Johnson**, leveling the playing field and reminding everyone that this was going to be a 200-foot battle.

### Second Period: The Parade to the Sin Bin
The middle frame turned into a bit of a grudge match. The zebras were busy, as tempers flared and sticks got a little too high and a little too slashy. We saw offsetting penalties at 11:01, followed by another pair of coincidental minors at 12:52. 

The Ice Breakers managed to channel that chaotic energy into momentum, though. With the ice opening up, **Luke Morley**—riding a hot streak—extended his point streak by tucking one home at 5:31, fed perfectly by **Joshua Pula**. It was the kind of goal that makes you want to jump over the boards yourself, though my knees aren't what they used to be.

### Third Period: The Wild West
If you thought the first two periods were exciting, the final frame was pure mayhem. **Justice Dick** padded the lead to 3-1 at the 12:22 mark, again finding **Joshua Pula** for the helper. Pula is playing like a man possessed, and his vision on the ice today was nothing short of elite.

The Joes, however, weren't about to go home quietly. **Gage Walker** (1:57) and **Jordan Hawkins Baker** (4:18), who secured his brace for the day, clawed the Joes back to a tie. But just when it looked like we were headed for a stalemate, **Daniel Reyes** decided he wanted to be the hero. At 5:54, he went coast-to-coast, burying an unassisted beauty to restore the Ice Breakers' lead.

Despite a late-game roughing penalty to **Jason Rosengard** that gave the Joes a golden opportunity to force overtime, the Ice Breakers' defense held the line like a brick wall, turning away everything sent their way.

### The Bottom Line
The Ice Breakers keep their momentum rolling with this result, proving they have the grit to survive a back-and-forth affair. For the Average Joes, it’s a tough pill to swallow after such a spirited comeback effort, but they showed they can hang with anyone in this league.

As we look at the standings, the league is tightening up. The Ice Breakers look like legitimate contenders, while the Joes will be looking to get back into the win column next time they lace 'em up. It’s a long season, folks, and if today was any indication, we’re in for a wild ride.
