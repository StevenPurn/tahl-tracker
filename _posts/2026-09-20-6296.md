# Hat-Trick Heroics Lead Riveters to Dominant Shutout

If the Power Puck Girls were hoping to catch the Tulsa Riveters napping on a crisp September evening, they were sorely mistaken. In a 4-0 thrashing that saw the Riveters finally break their streak of stalemates and ascend the standings, Tulsa turned the ice into their personal launchpad, led by the absolute supernova that was Ivy Emerson.

For the first frame, the game felt like a polite dance—plenty of motion, but no one stepping on anyone’s toes. Then, the second period hit, and Ivy Emerson decided that "polite" was entirely overrated. At 11:08, she finally uncorked the bottle, burying a feed from Heather Cozad to break the scoreless deadlock. Less than five minutes later, Emerson showed she didn't need any help at all, dancing through the Power Puck defense at 6:16 to notch her second of the night unassisted. 

"I was just looking for a little space," Emerson said after the game, while still catching her breath. "Turns out, the ice was just feeling generous today."

The Power Puck Girls tried to tighten the screws in the third, but the Riveters were playing a brand of hockey that can only be described as 'industrial-strength.' Hope for a comeback dwindled when Cara Ahmadinia was sent to the sin bin for hooking at 2:18 of the third, putting the Power Puck defense under immense pressure. 

The dam didn't just break; it vanished. At 6:47, Emerson completed the rare natural hat trick, snapping a rocket past the keeper off a beautiful assist from Kim Wilmes. Just 45 seconds later, the duo reversed roles: Wilmes found the back of the net off a dish from Emerson, effectively putting the game into the freezer at 4-0.

While the offense was busy conducting a masterclass, the Riveters’ defense and goaltending were equally airtight, stonewalling the Power Puck Girls’ attempts to find a rhythm. The result marks a massive shift for Tulsa, moving them from the purgatory of back-to-back ties to their first decisive victory of the season. 

For the Power Puck Girls, it’s a tough night at the office, dropping them further into the basement as they struggle to find their footing. Meanwhile, the Riveters have clearly found their gear, proving that once they get rolling, they aren't just a team—they're a force of nature.
