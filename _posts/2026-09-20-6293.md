# The Syndicate Skates to Commanding 8-3 Victory Over Kraken Cold Ones

If you were hoping for a quiet, low-scoring morning skate at the rink this Saturday, you were clearly in the wrong building. The Syndicate absolutely dismantled the Kraken Cold Ones in an 8-3 shellacking that saw the scoreboard light up more often than a neon sign on the Vegas strip.

The offensive explosion was headlined by none other than Brett Mecham, who decided that one or even two goals simply weren't enough. Mecham put on a masterclass, completing a clinical hat trick that left the Kraken defense looking like they were trying to solve a Rubik’s cube in the dark. His first strike came just 36 seconds into the opening period off a feed from Brandon Johnson, and he never looked back, adding two more before the game was out.

The Kraken Cold Ones, who entered the contest riding high on a modest early-season streak, struggled to find their footing against a Syndicate squad that looked fueled by espresso and sheer determination. While Chase Stubblefield kept his point streak alive with a goal at 4:19 in the first, and Justin McKinney added another tally, the Kraken simply couldn't manufacture enough offense to keep pace with the avalanche of red jerseys.

"I think we just played like we were trying to skate in quicksand," said one Kraken Cold Ones fan in the stands, clutching a lukewarm coffee. "The Syndicate were playing like their lives depended on it. Or at least like they really wanted an early lunch."

The middle frame was where the wheels truly began to wobble for the Cold Ones. Logan Pardue capitalized on the chaos to find the back of the net, and the period was further punctuated by a flurry of whistles, as both teams traded trips to the sin bin for slashing, tripping, and high-sticking. It was a game of "anything you can do, I can do," but unfortunately for the Kraken, the Syndicate was doing it significantly faster.

By the time the third period rolled around, it was the Brett Mooney show. Mooney netted a hat trick of his own—a feat of such late-game efficiency it borders on rude—punctuated by a goal from Dylan Calhoun. Even when the Kraken clawed back with a goal from Paul Davis, the Syndicate had an answer waiting, wrapping up a definitive performance that surely sends a message to the rest of the league.

With this win, The Syndicate moves up from the bottom half of the standings, effectively shaking off the "slow starter" label with a performance that was as sharp as their blades. The Kraken Cold Ones, meanwhile, will look to rinse the taste of this defeat away, hoping to get back to the form that earned them their earlier points.

If there’s a lesson to be taken from this morning’s romp, it’s this: don't blink when Brett Mecham is on the ice. You might just miss a goal, and at this rate, you’ll be missing a lot of them.
