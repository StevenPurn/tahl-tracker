# Ice Cold Precision: Gold as Ice Edges Out Hot Garbage in 5-4 Thriller

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**

**July 28, 2026** – If you were looking for a way to beat the summer heat, the arena was the place to be tonight. In a battle of the heavyweights that lived up to every ounce of the hype, the league-leading **Gold as Ice** (8-1-0) narrowly escaped a surging **Hot Garbage** (5-3-0) squad with a 5-4 victory in what can only be described as a bona fide barn burner.

Coming into the night, Hot Garbage was riding a wave of momentum, but Gold as Ice reminded everyone why they’re sitting at the top of the mountain.

### Period 1: Trading Blows
The opening frame was a physical chess match. Both teams spent some time cooling their heels in the sin bin after tripping calls on Mark DeSimone and LC III Sinor, but the ice finally broke at the 2:16 mark. Zac Hughes found the back of the net for Hot Garbage, assisted by Nicole Baker, silencing the Gold as Ice faithful. 

However, the league leaders don’t stay down for long. With just 40.9 seconds remaining in the period, Mark DeSimone decided he’d seen enough, burying a shot off a feed from Stephen Jeffery to knot things up 1-1 heading into the intermission.

### Period 2: The Momentum Swings
Stephen Jeffery came out of the tunnel with fire in his skates, scoring an unassisted beauty at 4:18 to put Gold as Ice in the driver's seat. But don’t let the name fool you—Hot Garbage played anything but rubbish. Nicholas Brewer, continuing his white-hot scoring streak, tied the game at 7:34, capitalizing on a crisp setup from Jordan Hawkins Baker and Aaron Whisner. 

### Period 3: Total Chaos
The third period was absolute bedlam. Nicholas Brewer struck again at 11:57, his second of the night, giving Hot Garbage a 3-2 lead. When Aden Ingram headed to the box for high-sticking, it looked like the visitors might pull away.

Instead, Gold as Ice turned on the jets. Stephen Jeffery played hero again, finding twine with less than a minute left in the frame off an assist from Jessica Hopwood. With the ice tilted firmly toward the Hot Garbage crease, Aden Ingram atoned for his penalty just moments later, lighting the lamp off a dish from DeSimone to reclaim the lead.

The drama wasn’t over. Nicole Baker (assisted by Joe Baker and the ever-present Jordan Hawkins Baker) tied it yet again at 8:28. But in a game defined by grit, the final word went to the veterans. With time ticking away, Mark DeSimone went coast-to-coast for an unassisted tally at 9:30, burying the eventual game-winner.

### The Aftermath
It was a heartbreaking loss for Hot Garbage, who played a fantastic road game, but they’ll walk away knowing they can hang with the league’s best. For Gold as Ice, this win reinforces their grip on the top seed, keeping them clear of the surging Tulsa Bison and The Outsiders.

The "Gold" machine rolls on, but if tonight proved anything, it’s that there is no such thing as an easy night in this league. Keep your sticks on the ice, folks—the playoff race is just heating up!
