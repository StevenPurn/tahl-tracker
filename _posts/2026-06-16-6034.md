# GUTHRIE’S HAT TRICK GUIDES HITMEN SILVER TO THRILLING WIN OVER HONEY BADGERS

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: June 15, 2026**

If you found yourself at the rink at 10:30 PM on a Monday, you weren’t there for a nap—you were there for the barn burner that was the Hitmen Silver and the Honey Badgers. In a matchup of two teams hungry for their first tally in the win column, the Hitmen managed to claw their way to a 4-3 victory, leaving the ice with their first "W" of the season while the Honey Badgers continue their search for a breakthrough.

### The First: A Seesaw Start
The opening frame was a masterclass in trading jabs. The Hitmen drew first blood at 6:10 when Keegan Guthrie found the back of the net, thanks to a crisp setup from Josh Moyer. The Hitmen were buzzing, but the Badgers weren't about to go into hibernation. At 9:31, Marshall Nunneley snapped a beauty past the Hitmen keeper to knot things up at 1-1, carrying the deadlocked score into the intermission.

### The Second: A Scoring Frenzy
The second period saw the floodgates open. If you blinked, you missed a highlight-reel play. 

Josh Moyer, looking like he had eyes in the back of his head all night, put the Hitmen back on top at 11:40 with an unassisted solo mission. But credit to the Badgers—they have a "never-say-die" attitude that matches their name. Joey Pachuta answered the bell at 14:03, leveling the score once again.

Then came the "Keegan Guthrie Show." Guthrie found the twine at 5:01 to reclaim the lead, but before the Hitmen could even finish their high-fives, Adam Carter answered back for the Badgers just 18 seconds later, set up beautifully by Nunneley. 

With the tension thick enough to cut with a skate blade, Guthrie struck again at 8:02. Completing his hat trick, he buried a feed from Moyer and Curtis Connors to push the Hitmen ahead 4-3. It was top-shelf where mom keeps the cookies, and it proved to be the game-winner.

### The Third: Digging Deep
The final frame saw the Hitmen buckle down. Despite a late-game scare when Eric Guthrie headed to the sin bin for roughing at 12:59, the Hitmen's defensive unit played with their hearts on their sleeves, clogging up the neutral zone and keeping the traffic in front of the net to a manageable level to secure the narrow one-goal victory.

### The Aftermath
For the Hitmen Silver, this win is a massive sigh of relief. After a slow start, they’ve finally put points on the board, jumping from 6th to the middle of the pack and proving they have the firepower to trade blows with anyone in the league. 

For the Honey Badgers, the 0-5 start is a tough pill to swallow, but their resilience tonight shows they aren’t going down without a fight. If they can tighten up their defensive coverage, the wins will surely follow.

The standings are tightening up, and if tonight was any indication, the rest of the season is going to be a wild ride. Keep your sticks on the ice, folks—this league is just heating up!
