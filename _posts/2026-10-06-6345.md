# Outsiders Keep Rolling With Dominant 5-1 Win Over Syndicate

If you caught the breeze coming off the ice at the rink on Tuesday night, you might have felt a bit of a chill—or maybe that was just the Outsiders’ offense, which spent sixty minutes putting the Syndicate through a deep freeze. In a commanding 5-1 victory, the league-leading Outsiders reminded everyone why they’re sitting at the top of the mountain, extending their winning streak and keeping the Syndicate searching for answers.

The Outsiders came out with their skates on fire. It didn’t take long for the first tick of the scoreboard, with Justice Dick finding the back of the net just 1:39 into the opening frame, thanks to a clinical setup from Tyler Waller and William Javier. By the time the first period hit the nine-minute mark, Brett Lamb doubled the lead on a feed from Logan Pardue, who is currently playing like a man possessed.

"We knew they were sitting on a couple of wins, so we wanted to make sure they didn't get comfortable," said one Outsiders skater, wiping a bead of sweat from his brow after the final buzzer. "We figured if we kept the puck in their end, we’d stay out of trouble. Mostly, anyway."

The Syndicate tried to claw their way back in the second period, but their momentum was handcuffed by a pair of penalties—a trip at 10:59 and a hook at 4:07. The Outsiders took full advantage of the open ice when Tyler Holmes buried a beauty off another helper from the ever-reliable Tyler Waller at the 14:49 mark. By then, the Outsiders’ bench looked like they were enjoying a Sunday skate, while the Syndicate looked like they were trying to solve a Rubik's cube with boxing gloves on.

The third period was more of the same. Enoch Cox added to the misery for the visitors at 5:05, assisted by Cody Dinsmore. Things got a little chippy when Michael Wixted headed to the box for slashing halfway through the frame, but Justice Dick clearly wasn't interested in a comeback, potting his second goal of the night just seconds later on a setup from Michael Drach and Logan Pardue to put the game firmly out of reach at 5-0.

The Syndicate finally found a silver lining late in the third when their number 13 broke the shutout, but it was far too little, too late. 

For the Outsiders, this win confirms they aren't just a flash in the pan; they’re the team to beat in this league right now. They’ve managed to stay disciplined and clinical, turning good opportunities into goals with the efficiency of a Swiss watch. As for the Syndicate, they’ll have to head back to the drawing board and figure out how to stop the bleeding before their next tilt.

As the lights dimmed and the crowd filed out, it was clear: the Outsiders are playing a different brand of hockey, and they don't look like they’re planning on slowing down anytime soon.
