# A Total Takeover: The Mafia Blank the Tuskens in 7-0 Masterclass

**TULSA** – If you were looking for a high-octane offensive clinic on this fine June evening, the rink at the local barn was the only place to be. The Tulsa Tuskens stepped onto the ice looking to break their two-game slide, but they ran head-first into a buzzsaw known as The Mafia. When the final horn sounded, the scoreboard read 7-0, and frankly, it felt like the Tuskens were trying to clear snow during a blizzard.

### A Quiet Start, A Violent Finish
The first period was a tactical chess match, with both squads trading blows and battling for ice real estate. The goalies were standing tall, making the crease look like a fortress. But hockey, much like life, has a way of turning on a dime.

The deadlock finally broke at the 12:46 mark of the second period. **Tyler Winn** found the back of the net off a sweet dish from Benjamin Koelsch and Natalie Myers, sending the Mafia bench into a frenzy. It was the crack in the dam that the Tuskens desperately tried to patch, but they couldn't hold back the tide. With mere seconds left in the middle frame, **Nathan Garcia** danced through the defense, burying one home off an Aaron West feed to give The Mafia a 2-0 cushion heading into the final stanza.

### The Third Period Avalanche
If the second period was a warning, the third was a full-blown declaration of war. The Mafia came out with their skates on fire, and the Tuskens simply couldn't match the speed.

**Brett Lamb** took center stage, showing the kind of finish that makes scouts drool. He opened the third-period scoring at 11:15, assisted by Scott Hellard, before taking matters into his own hands just minutes later at the 3:17 mark—unassisted, pure hustle, and pure skill. 

The floodgates were well and truly open by that point. **Benjamin Koelsch** joined the party at 5:35, rifling one home off a lovely setup from Lamb and Myers. Before the Tuskens could even catch their breath, **Nathan Garcia** struck again—his second of the night—at 5:48, with Mark Blevins and Daron Ross helping him navigate the traffic in front of the net.

Finally, at the 7:43 mark, **Daron Ross** capped off the offensive onslaught with an unassisted laser, putting the finishing touches on a dominant 7-0 victory.

### The Bottom Line
For the Tuskens, this is a night to burn the tape and move on. They struggled to generate quality looks and found themselves chasing the puck against a team that was moving it with surgical precision.

For The Mafia, this is the kind of statement win that shifts the narrative of an entire season. After a sluggish start to their campaign, they looked cohesive, clinical, and absolutely lethal in the offensive zone. With key players like Garcia, Lamb, and Ross extending their point streaks, The Mafia look like they’ve finally found their rhythm. 

As we look at the standings, the league is starting to take shape. The Mighty Drunks remain the team to beat, but don't look now—The Mafia just sent a message to the rest of the pack: *Don't blink, or you'll be left in the dust.*

**Final Score:** Tulsa Tuskens 0, The Mafia 7.
