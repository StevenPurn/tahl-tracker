# Joes Get Their Mojo Back: Average Joes Blank Bombers in 4-0 Shutout

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: June 14, 2026**

If you caught the breeze coming off the ice at the local barn this morning, you might have felt a shift in the tectonic plates of the standings. The Average Joes, looking to climb out of the basement, laced up their skates and delivered a masterclass in defensive grit and clinical finishing, blanking the Blue Bombers 4-0 in a Sunday morning matinee that proved rankings are just numbers on a page.

### The Calm Before the Storm
The first period was a tactical chess match. Both squads spent the opening frame feeling each other out, trading heavy hits and puck battles along the boards. The goalies were standing tall, looking like brick walls in front of their respective cages, ensuring we headed into the intermission with nothing but zeros on the scoreboard and a whole lot of tension in the air.

### The Joes Explode in the Second
If the first period was a whisper, the second was a roar. The ice tilted heavily in favor of the Joes, and they made the Bombers pay for every inch of space. 

The dam finally broke at the 11.7-second mark of the period (yes, you read that right!). **Tracy Wilmes** buried a beauty, set up by the duo of Ryan Martinez and Jordan Hawkins Baker. That goal acted as a dam-breaker for the Joes’ offense.

Despite a brief hiccup—a "too many men" penalty that had the Joes bench looking sheepish—the offense stayed hungry. **Jordan Hawkins Baker** took matters into his own hands, weaving through the neutral zone to pot an unassisted goal at 1:26. Minutes later, **Frank Smith** joined the party, slamming one home off a crisp feed from Aidan Caldron to extend the lead to 3-0. 

The Bombers tried to generate some spark, but the Joes’ backline was tighter than a new pair of skates. **Tracy Wilmes** added his second of the night at the 8:56 mark, courtesy of Zachary Williams, effectively putting this one on ice.

### Staying Disciplined in the Third
With a four-goal cushion, the Joes focused on playing "keep away." The third period got a bit chippy—a parade to the sin bin for both sides—but the Joes’ penalty kill was stellar. Even when Olivia Pletcher and Frank Post tried to ramp up the physical pressure for the Bombers, the Joes stayed cool under pressure, protecting their shutout with the fervor of a goalie protecting a clean sheet.

### What This Means for the Table
Coming into the game as the #7 and #6 seeds, this result is a massive momentum swing. The Average Joes (0-1-0 entering the tilt) finally get their first mark in the "W" column, signaling to the rest of the league that they aren’t just here to fill space—they’re here to compete. 

For the Blue Bombers, it’s a tough morning at the office. After a seesaw start to their season, they’ll need to regroup quickly if they want to keep pace with the early-season juggernauts like The Mafia and the Mighty Drunks. 

The Joes leave the arena today with their heads held high, proving that on any given Sunday, you don't need to be an elite squad to play elite hockey—you just need to be the Average Joes.

**Final Score: Average Joes 4, Blue Bombers 0**
