# RIVETING COMEBACK: Gibson’s Quadruple Sinks Chix in Sunday Morning Thriller

**By: "Hat-Trick" Harry, Beat Reporter**
**Date: July 12, 2026**

If you weren’t at the rink this morning, you missed a barn burner that had more twists than a back-alley slapshot. In a clash of titans between the third-place *Chix with Stix* and the second-place *Tulsa Riveters*, the ice was absolutely screaming by the 10:00 AM puck drop. While the Chix looked poised to march into the second spot, the Riveters had other plans—specifically, a one-woman wrecking crew named Gabrielle Gibson.

### Period 1: Feeling Out the Ice
The game started at a breakneck pace, but it was the Riveters who struck first blood. At 14:28, **Gabrielle Gibson** opened her account for the day, dancing around the blue line and burying one home off a sweet dish from Barbara Lepe Briones. 

But these Chix with Stix are made of iron. At 9:59, **Mckenna N. Allison** decided she wasn't going to let the Riveters dictate the rhythm. She went coast-to-coast, fighting through a forest of skates to snipe one past the keeper, knotting the score at 1-1.

### Period 2: The Chix Take Charge
The middle frame saw the Chix with Stix turning the heat up to "broil." **Heather Cozad** showed off some silky mitts at 13:04, picking the corner with a shot that stayed top-shelf where mom keeps the cookies. 

Just a few minutes later, Mckenna N. Allison proved her first goal wasn't a fluke, finding the back of the net again at 6:06 to give the Chix a commanding 3-1 lead. It looked like the Riveters were reeling, but Gibson—ever the opportunist—cut the deficit to 3-2 at 9:09 after a clinical setup from Amanda Long. That goal felt like a lifeline, and as it turned out, it was the start of something special.

### Period 3: The Gibson Show
The final period was a masterclass in heart. The Riveters tightened up the defensive zone, suffocating the Chix’s transition game. At 2:55, Gibson completed her natural hat trick, leveling the score at 3-3 off a precise feed from McKenna K. Rhodes. 

The air in the arena was thick enough to cut with a skate blade. With tensions mounting, Elizabeth Edwards was whistled for a high-sticking penalty at 41.2 seconds, putting the Chix on the defensive in the dying moments. The Riveters smelled blood in the water. With just 9:18 on the clock, **Gabrielle Gibson** hammered the final nail into the coffin—her *fourth* goal of the game—snapping home a beauty from Kim Wilmes. 

The Riveters held firm in the final frantic minutes, weathering the Chix’s late-game kitchen sink attempts to seal a 4-3 comeback victory.

### The Aftermath
What a morning. The Riveters (5-0-1) remain the undisputed kings (or queens) of the comeback, maintaining their stranglehold on second place and keeping pace with the Valkyries. Gabrielle Gibson proved that she is currently the most dangerous skater in the league—a four-goal performance that will be talked about at the water cooler all week.

For the Chix with Stix (4-3-0), it’s a tough pill to swallow after leading by two in the second. They showed they have the talent to hang with the league’s elite, but they’ll need to figure out how to close out a lead if they want to move out of that third-place slot. 

Keep your sticks on the ice, folks—this season is just heating up!
