# A Golden Upset: Gold as Ice Bronze Shakes Up the Standings with Thriller Over the Lodge

**By: "Hat-Trick" Harry, Rinkside Beat Writer**

**TULSA – July 6, 2026** – If you’re a fan of high-octane hockey and upsets that keep the ice crew busy sweeping up the shattered expectations of the league leaders, you should’ve been at the barn tonight. In a classic "David vs. Goliath" tilt that lived up to every bit of the pre-game hype, the bottom-dwelling Gold as Ice Bronze (GAIB) squad reminded us all why they drop the puck: they clawed, they scrapped, and they skated away with a 3-2 victory over the Lodge.

### First Period: The Early Strike
The game started with the intensity of a double-espresso on a Tuesday morning. Gold as Ice Bronze didn't wait for the ice to settle, with **Trent Ohman** burying a beauty just 1:02 into the frame off a crisp feed from **Makenzie Dillard**. The Lodge looked a bit shell-shocked, and things nearly went from bad to worse when **Jonathon Anderson** headed to the sin bin for hooking at 5:50. GAIB moved the puck well, but the Lodge’s penalty kill stood tall, keeping the deficit at one heading into the break.

### Second Period: Chapin Turns Up the Heat
The second period was a tactical chess match played at a hundred miles an hour. Both teams were throwing heavy hits, but GAIB found the insurance goal they were hunting for at 13:06. **Christopher Chapin**, riding a two-game point streak, proved why he’s a man in form by finding the back of the net off a sweet dish from **Lance Levasseur**. Chapin’s composure in traffic was top-shelf—the kind of goal where mom keeps the cookies—putting GAIB in the driver’s seat 2-0.

### Third Period: A Rollercoaster Finish
Buckle up, because the final frame was pure chaos. 

The Lodge finally found their rhythm, fueled by the playmaking of **Jonathon Anderson**. After a roughing minor gave the Lodge a power-play opening, **Mark Stacy** capitalized at the 11:26 mark, cutting the lead in half. The momentum swung wildly, and just over four minutes later, **Scott Dorough** knotted the game at 2-2, tapping one in off an assist from **Seth Miller**. The Lodge faithful were roaring, fully expecting a comeback win.

But hold the phone! Just 24 seconds after the equalizer, **Joshua Smith**—who entered the night on a hot streak—played the role of the spoiler perfectly. Smith took matters into his own hands (literally, with an unassisted tally) at the 7:22 mark to reclaim the lead for Gold as Ice Bronze. 

The Lodge pushed the net hard in the dying minutes, trying to force a shootout, but GAIB’s defensive unit tightened the screws, weathering the storm to secure the 3-2 upset.

### The Aftermath
For Gold as Ice Bronze, this is a massive shot in the arm. Jumping from 3 points to 5, they’ve breathed new life into their season and proven they can hang with the league’s upper echelon. 

For the Lodge, it’s a tough pill to swallow. They entered the night tied for 5th, and this stumble sees them missing a golden opportunity to climb the ladder toward the Mooseknuckles at the top. 

As I walked out to the parking lot, the air was still buzzing. In this league, the standings might say one thing, but on any given night, the ice doesn't lie. Tonight, the ice belonged to the Bronze.
