# A Scoring Spree in the Ice Box: The Mafia Flex Their Muscle in 8-5 Win Over Rohan Riders

**By: "Hat-Trick" Harry, Hometown Hockey Beat Writer**

There’s nothing quite like the smell of fresh ice and the sound of vulcanized rubber pinging off the iron to get the blood pumping on a Wednesday night. Last night, July 1st, the rink was absolutely electric as the 6th-place Rohan Riders tried to play spoiler against the heavy-hitting Mafia. 

While the Riders brought the hustle, The Mafia brought the heat, turning an 8:30 PM puck drop into an absolute barn burner that ended with an 8-5 scoreline in favor of the visitors.

### Period 1: A Frenetic Dance
If you blinked, you missed a scoring chance. The Riders’ **Abigail Nisbet** came out of the gate with legs moving, lighting the lamp early at 12:30. But The Mafia—a team that knows how to lean on you—responded with a calculated strike from **Brett Lamb**, set up by the ever-dangerous **Daron Ross**. 

The momentum swung like a pendulum. Nisbet grabbed her second, and **Greg Barnes** buried one off a beautiful feed from **Samuel Northup** to give the Riders a 3-2 lead. But you can't keep a team like The Mafia down for long. Just before the intermission, the game turned on its head. **Gage Walker** found twine twice in rapid succession, and Daron Ross extended his personal point streak to a staggering six games, ending the period with The Mafia up 5-3.

### Period 2: The Mafia Tightens the Grip
The Riders were looking to establish some traffic in front of the net in the second, but The Mafia’s transition game was surgical. **Gage Walker** secured his hat trick early in the frame, proving he’s playing the best hockey of his life right now. 

Despite a gritty solo effort from Greg Barnes and a third goal of the night from the absolute warrior Abigail Nisbet—who was playing like she was possessed—the Riders just couldn’t bridge the gap. **Jacob Meyer** hammered home a helper from Walker to keep the scoreboard tilted in The Mafia's favor.

### Period 3: Closing the Curtains
With an 8-4 lead heading into the final frame, The Mafia went into lockdown mode. **Benjamin Koelsch** iced the cake early at the 1:53 mark, bringing the score to 8-5. The Riders kept pushing, searching for that top-shelf magic, but the defense held firm, and the goalies—despite the high-scoring affair—stood tall in the face of some relentless pressure.

### The Aftermath
For the **Rohan Riders (1-4-0)**, it’s back to the drawing board. They’ve got the offensive sparkplug in Nisbet, but they’ll need to tighten the seams defensively if they want to climb out of the basement.

**The Mafia (4-1-0)**, meanwhile, look like a freight train. They’ve now pulled themselves into a tie for the second seed in the standings, breathing down the necks of the league-leading Mighty Drunks. With **Daron Ross** and **Scott Hellard** playing at this level, and **Gage Walker** finding the back of the net with such consistency, the rest of the league had better take notice. 

It was a rough night for the Riders, but as we always say in this business: the best thing about hockey is that there’s always another game on the schedule. Until next time, keep your stick on the ice and your head on a swivel!
