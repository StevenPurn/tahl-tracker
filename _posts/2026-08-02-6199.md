# Stung: Bad Bees Swarm the Mother Puckers in 9-0 Rout

**By: Our Hometown Rink-Side Reporter**  
**August 2, 2026**

If you happened to be at the arena this afternoon, you might have noticed a peculiar phenomenon: the Bad Bees didn't just play hockey; they staged an absolute masterclass in offensive zone dominance. Entering the contest hungry for points, the Bad Bees lived up to their name, stinging the Mother Puckers early and often in a lopsided 9-0 victory that has the league office doing a double-take at the stat sheet.

### First Period: The Early Hive Mentality
The Bees came out of the gate with their hair on fire. It didn't take long for Ivy Emerson to announce her presence, lighting the lamp at the 12:40 mark off a nifty feed from Mary J Wolff. Emerson clearly had the hot stick today, adding a solo highlight-reel beauty just a few minutes later at 2:37. 

By the time Andrea Evans buried a GABRIELLE GIBSON setup at 4:55, the Mother Puckers looked like they were skating in quicksand. The Bees capped off a chaotic first frame with an "Unknown" goal—a mysterious snipe assisted by Jessica Stoops—sending the teams to the locker room with the Bees holding a commanding 4-0 lead.

### Second Period: The Gibson Show
The middle frame belonged entirely to GABRIELLE GIBSON, who transformed from playmaker to pure executioner. She put home an unassisted tally at 11:34, then immediately turned around to assist on another goal just 49 seconds later. 

The Mother Puckers attempted to tighten things up, but frustrations began to boil over when Malori Christian headed to the sin bin for tripping at 2:51. The Bees smelled blood in the water, and Gibson capitalized on the momentum, hammering home her second goal of the night at 6:16. The Mother Puckers' netminder was under a constant barrage, facing traffic in front of the net that looked more like rush hour on the freeway than a hockey game.

### Third Period: Closing the Doors
Despite the deficit, the Puckers fought to keep the Bees from hitting double digits, but the sting was relentless. Ivy Emerson completed her hat-trick performance at 5:26, burying another unassisted beauty to keep the crowd buzzing. Suzanne Hawkins rounded out the scoring party at 8:22, effectively putting the game on ice.

A late tripping penalty on Kaley Pate served as a final indignity for the Puckers, who spent the dying minutes of the game simply hoping to escape without further damage. 

### The Bottom Line
Coming into this one, the Mother Puckers (#4, 2-5-1) were hoping to distance themselves from the bottom of the standings, but tonight’s loss keeps them stagnant with 5 points. For the Bad Bees (#5, 2-6-1), this is the shot of adrenaline they desperately needed. They climb to 5 points, breathing down the necks of the Puckers and breathing new life into a season that looked to be slipping away.

The Mother Puckers will need to hit the reset button and shake off this shutout before their next tilt, while the Bad Bees leave the arena today knowing exactly how dangerous they can be when the offense is clicking on all cylinders.

**Final Score:**  
**Bad Bees: 9**
