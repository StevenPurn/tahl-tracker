# Murder on Ice: Tulsa Remains Unstoppable, Dismantling Knights 7-2

**TULSA** – If you were looking for a sign that the Tulsa Murder are the heavyweights of this league, you didn’t have to look further than the scoreboard this morning. In a 8:15 a.m. clash that felt more like a morning practice session for the Murder and a long, cold waking nightmare for the Knights, Tulsa cruised to a dominant 7-2 victory.

The Murder remain undefeated at 3-0-0, proving that early morning puck drops are just another opportunity to hunt. Meanwhile, the Knights find themselves in a bit of a tailspin, sliding to 0-3-0.

### Period 1: The Hodges Show
The game started with a bit of a hiccup for Tulsa when Jason Bigelow headed to the sin bin for interference at 1:54. But if the Knights thought they had an opening, they were dead wrong. Just 38 seconds after the penalty box door shut, **Mark Hodges** flipped the script with a short-handed masterclass, burying an unassisted goal to deflate the Knights' spirits.

Hodges wasn't done. At 5:56, he doubled down, finding the back of the net again off beautiful feeds from Spencer Charles and Pete Fleming. By the time the buzzer sounded for the first intermission, the Knights were chasing shadows and a two-goal deficit.

### Period 2: The Tulsa Tsunami
If the first period was a warning, the second was a deluge. While the Knights managed to get on the board with an unassisted effort from **Eron Richards** at 2:34, it served only to poke the bear.

Tulsa responded with a surgical strike. **Spencer Charles** capitalized on a Jeffrey Pruitt helper at 3:48, and a mere 18 seconds later, **Pete Fleming** went top-shelf where mom keeps the cookies, leaving the Knights' goalie shaking his head. The onslaught continued as **Curtis Connors** joined the party at 5:44, and Fleming notched his second of the game at the 9:03 mark. In the blink of an eye, the game had transformed from a hockey match into a clinic.

### Period 3: Closing the Curtains
The third period was all about managing the clock, though **Robert Hoffmann** managed to snag a consolation goal for the Knights at 14:40. 

But the Murder weren’t finished putting the icing on the cake. **Spencer Charles** hammered home his second of the contest at 5:15, officially turning the lights out on any hopes of a Knights comeback. A late tripping penalty on Justin Eppler was the final footnote in a game that was effectively decided before the zamboni even finished its second-period clean.

### The Takeaway
For the **Tulsa Murder**, this is business as usual. They continue to sit atop the standings, playing a brand of hockey that is as aggressive as it is clinical. They are firing on all cylinders, with depth scoring that would make any coach blush.

For the **Knights**, it’s back to the drawing board. Sitting at 0-3-0, they need to tighten up the traffic in front of their own net and find a way to generate sustained pressure before the gap between them and the rest of the pack becomes a canyon.

The Murder are off to a blistering 3-0-0 start—the question isn't whether they can make the playoffs, but who, if anyone, can stop them from claiming the throne.
