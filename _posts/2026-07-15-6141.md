# The Outsiders Run Wild: Wendigos Left in the Cold in 8-2 Rout

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: July 14, 2026**

If you were looking for a quiet Tuesday night at the rink, you picked the wrong zip code. Tonight’s tilt between the Wendigos and The Outsiders started as a scrappy affair, but by the time the final buzzer echoed through the rafters, The Outsiders had turned this game into a full-blown masterclass in offensive pressure.

The final scoreline? An emphatic **8-2 victory for The Outsiders.**

### Period 1: A Frenetic Dance
The game kicked off with the speed of a greased lightning bolt. It took only 1:20 for The Outsiders to draw first blood, with Zach Cox finding the back of the net off some nifty setup work from Tyler Waller and Andrew Lamparter. 

The Wendigos, sitting at the bottom of the standings, showed they weren't here to just take up space. Mark Smith knotted things up at the 14:30 mark, redirecting a Matthew Palmer feed to get the home crowd rocking. But Alan "The Architect" Dyke had other plans. Dyke went on a tear, scoring two back-to-back unassisted beauties to restore the lead. Aden Ingram managed a gritty goal for the Wendigos just before the period ended to keep them within striking distance, but the momentum was swinging hard toward the visitors.

### Period 2: The Waller-Dyke Show
If the first period was a skirmish, the second was an absolute blitzkrieg. The Outsiders came out of the locker room with their skates sharpened and their sights set on the top shelf.

Tyler Waller, riding a wave of confidence from his goal streak, was simply untouchable. He linked up with Dyke and Daron Ross to pile on the misery for the Wendigos’ defense. It was a clinic on transition play—quick passes, heavy traffic in front of the crease, and surgical finishes. Michael Drach joined the party midway through the frame, burying a pass from Zach Cox to cap off an incredible scoring surge.

The Wendigos’ netminder faced a barrage of rubber that would make a pinball machine blush. While they stood tall under immense pressure, the structural integrity of the Wendigos' zone defense simply evaporated under The Outsiders' relentless forecheck.

### The Breakdown
This was a tale of two teams trending in opposite directions. The Outsiders (#6) looked every bit a playoff contender tonight, displaying the kind of puck movement that forces opposing goalies to question their life choices. Tyler Waller’s multi-goal performance continues his scorching hot streak, while Alan Dyke’s hat-trick-plus-change effort serves as a reminder that this team has depth that most of the league hasn't yet figured out how to bottle up.

For the Wendigos, this is a tough pill to swallow. They’ve struggled to find their footing all season, and tonight the gaps in their coverage were exposed by a team that didn't miss when it mattered. 

**What’s Next?**
With this 8-2 win, The Outsiders continue to solidify their position in the middle of the pack, keeping the pressure on the teams above them in the standings. They’ve got the wind in their sails, and if they keep playing with this kind of synergy, the top-five clubs better start checking their rearview mirrors. 

For the Wendigos, it’s back to the drawing board. They’ll need to tighten up the neutral zone and find a way to stop the bleeding before the gap between them and the rest of the league becomes a canyon. 

But for tonight? Tip your cap to The Outsiders. They came, they saw, and they absolutely dominated the ice. That’s hockey, baby!
