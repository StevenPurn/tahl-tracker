Every high-danger shot was met with a glove save or a crisp pad deflection. The Bees were buzzing, but the Valkyries were playing a game of "keep-away" that would make a veteran blue-liner blush.

### Third Period: The Lockdown
The final frame was a masterclass in puck management. The Valkyries leaned into their defensive structure, clogging up the neutral zone and forcing the Bees to dump and chase—a strategy that yielded zero dividends against such a sturdy backline. 

With 1:11 remaining in the game, the Bees’ Jessica Stoops took a high-sticking penalty, effectively extinguishing any hopes of a late-game surge. The Valkyries held the fort, skated the final minute with poise, and walked away with the narrowest of victories.
