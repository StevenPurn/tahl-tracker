# Valkyries Launch Late-Game Offensive to Topple Chix with Stix in 4-2 Thriller

**By: Rinkside Reporter**

**August 2, 2026** – If you were looking for a Saturday evening snooze-fest, you definitely walked into the wrong arena. Under the bright lights at 5:30 PM, The Valkyries and Chix with Stix engaged in a tilt that started as a defensive grind and finished as an absolute barn burner. When the final buzzer sounded, it was The Valkyries standing tall with a 4-2 comeback victory, proving once again that you can’t count them out until the last drop of sweat hits the ice.

### First Period: The Chix Take Charge
The Chix with Stix arrived with a fire in their bellies, and they didn’t wait for the ice to settle to start testing the twine. Just 3:01 in, **Carson See** found the back of the net unassisted, silencing the home crowd. Not even two minutes later, **Nicole Baker** kept the momentum rolling, burying a helper from **Catherine Pearsall** to extend the lead to 2-0. 

At that point, it looked like the Valkyries might be headed for a long night, but **Kalea Maxwell** threw a wrench in those plans. At 5:58, Maxwell went to work, finding the back of the net to put the Valkyries on the board and sending us into the intermission with a 2-1 scoreline.

### Second Period: The Tactical Tug-of-War
If the first period was a track meet, the second was a chess match played at 90 mph. Both netminders locked the doors and threw away the keys. With the "traffic in front of the net" becoming an absolute nightmare for the forwards, the goaltenders turned into human walls. Every rebound was cleared, and every angle was covered, leaving both benches searching for a spark that wouldn't come—until the third.

### Third Period: The Valkyrie Awakening
Whatever was said in The Valkyries' locker room during the break, it worked. The team came out buzzing like a hive of hornets. 

At 12:57, **Makenzie Dillard**—continuing her scorching hot streak—found the equalizer off a crisp feed from **Jessica Hinkefent**. With the game knotted at 2-2, the tension was thick enough to cut with a skate blade. The Valkyries leaned on their depth, and with only 20 seconds remaining on the clock (a classic "heartbreak hotel" moment for the Chix), **Haley Griffin** hammered one home to give The Valkyries their first lead of the game!

Just to add an exclamation point to the night, **Gabrielle Gibson** struck gold at 3:39 (game clock adjusted), assisted by Dillard, to put the game on ice. Even a late high-sticking penalty to **Barbara Lepe Briones** at 6:08 couldn't stop the Valkyrie steamroller, as they killed off the power play to seal the 4-2 win.

### The Aftermath
Tonight’s result is a massive statement for the league standings. 

**The Valkyries (8-1-0)** improve to 16 points, keeping them deadlocked with the league-leading Tulsa Riveters. They are playing like a team that believes the trophy has their name on it. For the **Chix with Stix (5-4-0)**, it’s a tough pill to swallow after holding a two-goal lead early, but they remain a formidable third-place threat who proved they can punch well above their weight class.

The Valkyries have now extended their winning streak to three games, and if Dillard and company keep playing like this, the rest of the league had better start checking their rearview mirrors. The road to the championship is heating up, and we are just getting to the good stuff!
