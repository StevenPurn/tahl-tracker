# A Scoring Frenzy: Renegades Weather the Storm in 9-5 Barn Burner

**By: "Hat-Trick" Harry, Hometown Hockey Beat Writer**
*July 12, 2026*

If you like your hockey served with a side of high-octane offense and enough rubber flying to resurface a highway, last night’s tilt at the rink was the place to be. The Renegades (4-3-0) played host to the Tornadoes (2-6-0), and by the time the final horn sounded, the scoreboard looked more like a grocery bill than a hockey score: Renegades 9, Tornadoes 5. 

It was a classic “bend-but-don't-break” scenario that eventually turned into a total offensive onslaught. Grab your favorite beverage—we’ve got a lot of goals to cover.

### Period 1: The Tactical Opening
The Renegades came out of the gate with some serious jump, and **Alex Bateman** proved why he’s a hot hand, burying one at the 14:17 mark off a sweet feed from Caeleb Burch. Bateman has been on an absolute tear, extending his goal streak to four games, and he’s playing like he’s got a magnet in his stick blade.

But the Tornadoes weren’t about to roll over. They clawed back with a pair of snipes from **Christopher Cieslak** and **Nicklas Clark**, heading into the intermission with a 2-1 lead. It felt like a defensive chess match, but little did we know, the boards were about to start rattling.

### Period 2: The Clark Explosion
If you blinked, you missed it. The middle frame started with an offensive outburst from Nicklas Clark, who completed his hat-trick in a blink-and-you-miss-it span, putting the Tornadoes up 4-1. 

Down by three, the Renegades could have folded. Instead, they tapped into the "never say die" spirit. **Logan Singleton** sparked the engine at 12:44, and **Michael Pasque**—who was clearly channeling some inner magic—hammered one home shortly after. Suddenly, the deficit was cut to 4-3, and the momentum had swung harder than a beer leaguer’s backhand.

### Period 3: The Renegades' Power Surge
Whatever was said in the Renegades' locker room during the second intermission needs to be bottled and sold. The third period was a clinic. **Elliott Langford** tied it up early, and **Matthan Hillberg** followed suit to reclaim the lead. 

The Tornadoes tried to hold back the tide, with **Steven Purn** finding the net, but the Renegades were in full "top shelf where mom keeps the cookies" mode. Pasque went on a tear, adding two more goals to his stat line—giving him a natural hat-trick performance—while Bateman added his second of the night in the closing minutes. By the time the dust settled, the Renegades had racked up seven goals in the final frame alone. 

### The Final Stat Line
The victory propels the Renegades to a 4-3-0 record, keeping them firmly in the hunt for the top spot in the standings as they chase the undefeated Shooting Blanks. For the Tornadoes, it’s a tough pill to swallow; despite a massive performance from Nicklas Clark, the defensive dam burst, and they now find themselves staring down a 2-6-0 hole.

**Game Notes:**
*   **Caeleb Burch** deserves an assist on the night for his vision, racking up enough helpers to make a passing drill look easy.
*   **Michael Pasque** officially declared the third period his personal playground.
*   **Alex Bateman** continues his multi-game heater; he’s currently the most dangerous skater in the league.

The Renegades proved tonight that if you can match their intensity, you better be ready to play for sixty full minutes—because they certainly are. Same time next week, folks. Keep your sticks on the ice!
