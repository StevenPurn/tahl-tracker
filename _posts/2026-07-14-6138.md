# Goat-Powered Fireworks: Killer Goats Clip Puckheads in 8-5 High-Scoring Affair

**By: "Rinkside" Richie, Beat Reporter**

**July 13, 2026** – If you were looking for a quiet Monday night, you picked the wrong barn. Down at the local sheet, the Killer Goats and the Puckheads treated the late-night crowd to a classic barn burner that saw the red light get more exercise than a track star on a treadmill. When the final buzzer sounded on this 8-5 offensive showcase, the Killer Goats stood tall, proving why they are the team to watch as they hunt down the top spot in the standings.

### First Period: A Tit-for-Tat Start
The game kicked off with both sides looking for their legs, but the ice tilted early when Nelson Pina sat for two minutes on a tripping call. The Puckheads capitalized quickly, as **William Hofmeister** buried one off a nice feed from Jacob Sartin to draw first blood. 

But if you’ve been tracking this season, you know you don't poke the bear—or in this case, the Goat. Just 14 seconds after Hofmeister’s strike, the league’s hottest hand, **Dylan Criquet Danielson**, reminded everyone why he’s on a scoring heater, burying a feed from Lucas Blanchard to tie it up. Kyle Foster followed suit midway through the frame, and despite a late answer from the Puckheads' Matthew Cooper, we headed into the intermission knotted at 2-2.

### Second Period: The Goats Break Away
The middle frame turned into a track meet. The Goats, clearly fueled by a mission to move up the ladder, started clicking like a Swiss watch. **Michael Doyle** kept his point streak alive by cleaning up the trash in front of the net, and **Samuel Michaud** added another shortly after to stretch the lead.

The Puckheads didn't go quietly, though. **William Hofmeister** completed his own highlight reel with an unassisted beauty, and **Matthew Cooper** notched his second of the night to keep the pressure on. But just when it looked like the Puckheads might pull even, the Goats’ top line turned the knob to eleven. **Lucas Blanchard** and the seemingly unstoppable **Dylan Criquet Danielson** struck back-to-back, sending the Goats into the final frame with a comfortable—or so we thought—8-4 lead.

### Third Period: Closing the Gate
The final period was a masterclass in "bend but don't break" hockey. With the Goats protecting their cushion, the traffic in front of the net became thicker than a bowl of oatmeal. Despite a late rally attempt highlighted by a third goal from **William Hofmeister**—who was clearly playing like a man possessed—the Goats answered back one last time. **Lucas Blanchard** netted his second of the night, sealing the deal and ensuring the two points stayed with the boys in the Goat jerseys.

### The Takeaway
For the **Killer Goats**, this 8-5 win is a massive statement. At 5-1-1, they are breathing right down the necks of the league-leading Dirty Birds. Dylan Criquet Danielson’s scoring streak remains the talk of the town, and their ability to put up eight goals on a single night shows they have the depth to make a deep run. 

On the flip side, the **Puckheads** (2-5-0) continue to slide, struggling to find consistency despite a heroic effort from Hofmeister. They’ll need to tighten up the defensive zone if they want to stop the bleeding and climb out of the bottom half of the table.

As the zamboni floods the ice, one thing is for certain: if the Goats keep playing with this kind of offensive flair, the road to the championship is going to have a very hairy, four-legged obstacle in the way. 

**Three Stars of the Night:**
1. **Dylan Criquet Danielson (Goats):** The man is scoring for fun. 
2. **William Hofmeister (Puckheads):** A hat-trick performance in a losing effort.
3. **Lucas Blanchard (Goats):** A multi-goal night and pure hustle all game long.
