# Upset Alert: Riders Snap Bombers’ Win Streak in 4-3 Thriller

**By: "Rusty" Blades, Senior Hockey Correspondent**

**DATE:** August 9, 2026  
**LOCATION:** The Barn

If you were sleeping in this Saturday morning, you missed a classic. While the coffee was brewing at 8:45 AM, the Rohan Riders were busy serving up a cold dish of reality to the high-flying Blue Bombers, handing them a 4-3 loss in a game that had everything but the kitchen sink.

Coming into this tilt, the Bombers were the hottest ticket in town, riding a five-game heater. The Riders? They were sitting at the bottom half of the standings looking for a spark. Well, consider the fuse lit.

### First Period: The Riders Lay the Foundation
The Riders came out of the gate with more jump in their step than a kangaroo on an espresso bender. At the 3:36 mark, **Robert Millea** opened the scoring, burying a beauty off a setup from **Gage Walker**. The Bombers looked a bit sleepy, struggling to find their legs as the Riders dictated the pace early, clogging up the neutral zone and forcing the Bombers to earn every inch of ice.

### Second Period: A Total Barn Burner
The middle frame turned into an absolute track meet. **Sergio Castellanos** doubled the lead early on, finishing a silky play from Millea that left the Bombers’ defense looking like traffic cones. 

But you don’t stay in fourth place by rolling over. The Bombers finally woke up midway through the period. **Chris Olsen** extended his point streak, finding the back of the net off a feed from the ever-reliable **Samuel Northup** to cut the lead to 2-1. 

Just when you thought the Bombers had the momentum, Millea decided he wasn’t done. He sniped an unassisted marker—a real "where mom keeps the cookies" effort—to reclaim the two-goal cushion. Not 60 seconds later, **Dylan Walker** answered back for the Bombers, crashing the paint to put home a rebound from Sixsmith and Shuck. By the time the horn blew for the second intermission, the ice was tilted, the goalies were sweating, and the fans were breathless.

### Third Period: The Penalty Box Shuffle and the Final Hammer
The third was a gritty, grind-it-out affair. Tempers flared, leading to a trip to the box for **Dylan Walker** (high-sticking) and **Aivar Kant** (roughing). With the game opening up, **Samuel Northup** knotted things at 3-3 with a gritty goal assisted by **Frank Post**, sending the Bombers’ bench into a frenzy.

But the Riders had one last surprise in the bag. With the clock winding down, **Sergio Castellanos** played the hero, burying his second of the game at 8:36 with his partner-in-crime, **Gage Walker**, drawing the helper. The Riders’ defensive core held the fort for the final minutes, turning away a desperate Bombers barrage to seal the 4-3 victory.

### The Aftermath
This result shakes up the landscape of the standings significantly. For the **Blue Bombers (6-3-0)**, the loss snaps a golden five-game winning streak and halts their climb toward the top-three. They’ll need to regroup before they lose ground to the Mafia and the surging pack behind them.

As for the **Rohan Riders (3-6-1)**, this is the "W" they’ve been starving for. If they can build on this momentum and tighten up the gaps, they might just climb out of the basement before the season slips away. 

Next time you’re heading to the rink, don’t snooze—because this morning, the Riders proved that on any given Saturday, even the underdog can take a bite out of a title contender. See you at the next drop!
