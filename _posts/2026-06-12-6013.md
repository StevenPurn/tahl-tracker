# BOMBERS BLOW THE DOORS OFF: A 9-5 BARN BURNER IN TULSA

**Byline: “The Blue-Line Bard”**

If you didn’t have a heart monitor strapped to your chest for last night’s tilt, you missed the absolute definition of a momentum swing. The Blue Bombers rolled into the arena sitting at 0-1-0, looking to climb out of the basement, and boy, did they bring the shovel. By the time the final buzzer echoed through the rafters at 10:30 PM on June 11th, the Bombers had skated circles around the Tulsa Tuskens to the tune of a 9-5 victory.

### Period 1: Tulsa Starts the Engine, Bombers Find the Spark
The first twenty minutes were, frankly, a headache for the Bombers' bench. The Tuskens looked like they’d had an extra cup of coffee before puck drop. Garrett Caraway opened the account early, followed by a quick-fire double from Shawn Golden and Dylan Walker. When Caraway tucked his second of the night at the 6:20 mark, the scoreboard read 4-0 Tulsa, and the Bombers looked like they were trying to skate through quicksand.

But then? The tide turned. Tyler Waller answered back seconds later to keep the Bombers’ pulse, even as Travis Shuck found himself cooling his heels in the sin bin for a roughing minor. 4-1 heading into the break, but the Bombers had finally stopped the bleeding.

### Period 2: The Comeback for the Ages
Whatever the Bombers’ coaching staff said during the intermission, they should bottle it and sell it as performance fuel. The second period was a masterclass in offensive zone dominance. 

Chris Olsen—a man who is apparently allergic to *not* scoring—started the rally, assisted by Glenn Prouse. Daron Ross followed suit, and suddenly the deficit was just two. When Tulsa’s discipline dipped, the Bombers pounced. Hayden Sixsmith kept his point streak alive with a beauty, and Olsen wasn't done yet, ripping his second of the night past the Tuskens’ netminder to tie the game at 4-4.

With the momentum firmly in their pocket, the Bombers kept the pressure on. Daron Ross buried his second of the evening off a dime from Tyler Waller. By the time the horn sounded on the second, the Bombers had erased a four-goal deficit and held a 6-4 lead. The breeze from those skates? You could feel it in the nosebleeds.

### Period 3: Putting the Tuskens on Ice
Any hope the Tuskens had of a late-game surge was effectively dismantled by the Bombers' relentless traffic in front of the net. Chris Olsen completed his hat trick at 10:44, proving yet again that he knows exactly where mom keeps the cookies on that top shelf. 

Glenn Prouse added another for good measure before Caraway could snag a consolation hat-trick goal for Tulsa. Tyler Waller closed out the scoring marathon, icing the 9-5 victory. 

### The Aftermath
This was exactly the kick-start the Blue Bombers needed. Moving from 0-1-0 to a 1-1-0 record, they’ve clawed their way out of the bottom tier and sent a message to the rest of the league: don’t sleep on this squad when they’ve got their skating legs under them. 

As for the Tulsa Tuskens, they remain winless at 0-2-0. It’s a long season, but they’ll need to figure out how to stop the bleeding once the opposing momentum starts to build.

For the Bombers, the streaks continue: Sixsmith is looking like an absolute force, and the chemistry between Olsen and his line-mates is currently firing on all cylinders. If they keep playing with this kind of grit, the top of the standings better start checking their rearview mirrors.

**Final Score: Blue Bombers 9, Tulsa Tuskens 5**
