# Blanks Burn Bright: Shooting Blanks Put on an Offensive Clinic in 10-6 Thriller

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**June 16, 2026**

If you were looking for a quiet Tuesday night at the rink, you clearly took a wrong turn at the parking lot. Last night’s showdown between the undefeated league-leaders, **Shooting Blanks**, and the scrappy **Oklahoma Precast** was a real barn burner that left the ice scarred and the scoreboard operator’s fingers tired.

When the dust—and a whole lot of rubber—settled, the Blanks walked away with a resounding 10-6 victory, proving exactly why they hold the top spot in the standings.

### First Period: The Blanks Strike Early
The Blanks didn’t waste any time shaking off the rust. Mere seconds after the puck dropped (literally, 0.1 seconds in!), **Aaron Best** found the back of the net, set up by Bobby Watson and Elliott Langford. It was a statement goal that said, "We’re here, and we’re hungry." 

Despite a minor hiccup involving a hooking penalty on Langford, the Blanks kept their foot on the gas. Bobby Watson kept his scoring streak alive with a beauty at the 12-minute mark, and before the period was out, David Best added another. Marshall Nunneley managed to claw one back for Oklahoma Precast, but the period ended with the Blanks sitting pretty at 3-1.

### Second Period: The Goal-Scoring Carousel
If the first period was a warm-up, the second was an absolute firestorm. Both teams decided that defense was merely a suggestion. 

Alex Bateman and Christopher Cahill brought the heat for Oklahoma Precast, but the Blanks had an answer for every question asked. Brett Allen sniped a solo effort to keep the pressure on, and then the Blanks went on a spree. Bobby Watson and Austin Tyler scored back-to-back goals just eight seconds apart—a momentum swing that sucked the air right out of the Precast bench. 

Precast’s Corey Christian and Matthew Rankin kept the dream alive with late-period goals, but Joshua Phillips hammered one home for the Blanks to keep them in command, 7-4, heading into the final frame.

### Third Period: The Watson Show
The third period was all about closing the door. Hunter Best extended his scoring streak with a laser, and Bobby Watson—who was seemingly playing in his own zip code all night—netted two more to complete a monster performance. While Oklahoma Precast’s Christopher Cahill bagged his second of the night to keep things interesting, the deficit was simply too tall a mountain to climb.

### The Bottom Line
With this 10-6 victory, the **Shooting Blanks (3-0-0)** remain the undisputed kings of the mountain, extending their perfect record and continuing their absolute dominance of the league. It was a masterclass in puck movement and capitalizing on opportunities.

For **Oklahoma Precast (1-4-0)**, the scoreline is certainly tough to swallow, but their offensive resilience against the league’s top dogs shows they’ve got plenty of grit. They’ll need to tighten up the traffic in front of their net if they want to climb out of the cellar, but if they play with this much offensive intensity, the results will start to swing in their favor soon.

**Three Stars of the Game:**
1. **Bobby Watson (Shooting Blanks):** A masterclass in offense. He didn't just score; he controlled the tempo of the entire evening.
2. **Austin Tyler (Shooting Blanks):** A playmaker’s playmaker. His vision in the second period was the catalyst for the Blanks' offensive explosion.
3. **Christopher Cahill (Oklahoma Precast):** Never stopped fighting, providing two crucial goals for the Precast cause.

The league standings have tightened, but as of tonight, all roads in the league still lead through the Shooting Blanks. See you at the rink!
