# A Lethal Performance: Tulsa Murder Stings Gold as Ice Bronze in 7-4 Thriller

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
*August 9, 2026*

If you were looking for a quiet Sunday afternoon at the rink, you clearly missed the memo. The ice was smoking at 2:30 PM sharp as the #3-seeded Tulsa Murder put on a clinical display of offensive firepower, skating away with a 7-4 victory over a scrappy Gold as Ice Bronze squad.

While Gold as Ice Bronze fought valiantly to claw their way back in the final frame, the Murder proved exactly why they are sitting pretty in the top three of the standings.

### First Period: The Mortimer Opening
The game opened with the intensity of a playoff tilt, but it didn't take long for the Murder to draw first blood. With just 40 seconds gone, **Tom Mortimer** extended his red-hot goal-scoring streak to five games, burying a beauty off a crisp feed from **Justin Oxford**. It was a wake-up call for Gold as Ice Bronze, who spent the rest of the period trying to find their legs through the heavy traffic in front of the net. 

Gold as Ice Bronze’s **Eric Kolis** found himself in the box for a slashing call mid-way through, but the Bronze penalty killers managed to keep the Murder’s power play from doing further damage.

### Second Period: The Hodges Show
Trailing by one, Gold as Ice Bronze finally broke the seal at the 11:04 mark. **Triston Wilson** did the heavy lifting, capitalizing on a **Dylan Calhoun** setup to knot things up at 1-1. 

But then, the Tulsa Murder decided to remind everyone why they are a threat to take it all. After killing off a tripping penalty taken by Mortimer, the Murder went on a tear. **Mark Hodges** began his legendary afternoon at the 2:25 mark, followed shortly by a sniper shot from **Justin Oxford**. Before the Bronze could catch their breath, Hodges struck again at 7:06, turning a tight game into a 4-1 deficit. By the time the horn sounded for the second intermission, the Bronze were looking for a miracle in the dressing room.

### Third Period: The Late-Game Fireworks
The third period was absolute bedlam. The Murder looked like they were putting the game to bed early, with **Mark Hodges** completing his hat trick at 12:37, followed by an insurance tally from **Pete Fleming** just over a minute later. With the score at 6-1, most teams would have folded their tents.

Not the Bronze.

Proving they have the heart of a lion, Gold as Ice Bronze went on a vertical tear. **Triston Wilson** notched his second and third goals of the afternoon in quick succession, and **Lance Levasseur** followed up to make it a 6-4 game. Suddenly, the breeze from the skates was cold, and the tension in the rink was palpable. 

However, **Mark Hodges**—the undeniable star of the evening—slammed the door shut with his fourth goal of the game at the 6:46 mark, effectively silencing the Bronze comeback and finalizing the score at 7-4.

### The Aftermath
For the **Tulsa Murder (7-3-0)**, this victory is a statement. They continue to sit comfortably in the third seed, keeping the heat on the Mooseknuckles and The Bone Zone. The chemistry between **Luke Brandon** and **Mark Hodges** was nothing short of lethal today; if they keep finding each other in the slot like that, the rest of the league is going to have a very long season.

As for **Gold as Ice Bronze (3-6-2)**, despite the loss, they have to be encouraged by the late-game resilience shown by Triston Wilson. He’s putting the team on his back, and if they can tighten up their defensive zone coverage and stop the odd-man rushes, they’ll climb out of that #9 spot sooner rather than later.

The puck drops again next week, folks. Keep your sticks on the ice!
