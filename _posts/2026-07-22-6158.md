# Ice Breakers Edge Out Riders in a Seven-Goal Thriller

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**  
**Date: July 21, 2026**

If you weren’t at the arena tonight, you missed a bona fide barn burner. The Rohan Riders and the Ice Breakers treated us to a night of end-to-end action that felt more like a street fight on skates than a mid-season matchup. When the final horn blew, the scoreboard read 7-6 in favor of the Ice Breakers, but the real winner tonight was anybody who enjoys watching the twine rattle.

### Period 1: The Breakers Come Out Swinging
The Ice Breakers entered the night as one of the hottest teams in the league, and they showed exactly why early on. Daniel Reyes drew first blood at the 11:44 mark, set up beautifully by Luke Morley. With just under a minute left, Justice Dick decided he didn’t want to wait for the second period to make an impact, dancing through traffic to bury a solo effort. The Riders headed to the locker room down by two, looking for a pulse.

### Period 2: Chaos and Custodians
The middle frame was absolute bedlam. Justice Dick kicked things off with his second of the night just ten minutes in, but the Riders finally woke up. Glenn Prouse—who was a force of nature all night—put the Riders on the board shortly after, courtesy of a tidy feed from Nathan Lesnick.

The momentum swung like a pendulum. Nathan Klassen answered back for the Breakers a mere 15 seconds later to restore the lead. After a clumsy "Too Many Men" penalty put the Riders in a hole, the game turned into a scrap. A double-minor situation saw both Glenn Prouse and Dylan Walker head to the sin bin for some extracurricular roughing and cross-checking. Once the dust settled, Gage Walker fired a laser to keep the Riders within striking distance, and Prouse added his second of the night late in the period to make it a one-goal game heading into the final frame.

### Period 3: The Comeback Kid Mentality
The third period was pure, unadulterated high-octane hockey. The Riders clawed their way back with goals from Robert Millea and Joshua Hendrickson to briefly seize the lead! It looked like the underdogs might pull off the shocker, but the Ice Breakers proved why they’re sitting pretty in the standings. 

Daniel Reyes snatched the equalizer with under two minutes to play. Though Hendrickson responded with his second goal of the night—a real "top shelf where mom keeps the cookies" beauty—the relentless Justice Dick set up Reyes for his hat-trick clincher just seconds later.

### The Shootout: Cold Steel
Regulation couldn't settle it, so we went to the shootout. Gage Walker fired home for the Riders, but the Ice Breakers remained icy-veined. Justice Dick continued his dominant evening with a clinical finish, and Joshua Pula eventually stepped up to deliver the dagger, securing the 7-6 victory for the Breakers.

### Looking Ahead
For the **Ice Breakers (5-0-1)**, this win keeps the dream alive. They are playing with the swagger of a team that knows how to find the back of the net when the game is on the line. They remain firmly locked in the top tier of the standings, putting the league leaders on notice.

As for the **Rohan Riders (1-6-0)**, you have to feel for them. They showed heart, grit, and some serious firepower tonight. They didn't get the two points, but they pushed one of the league's best to the absolute brink. If they can clean up the defensive lapses and stop the parade to the penalty box, their luck is bound to change.

The ice might have melted under the intensity of this one, but the season is just heating up!
