# Garbage In, Victory Out: Hot Garbage Stifles the Kraken in Tuesday Night Tilt

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**June 29, 2026**

There’s an old saying in the locker room: “If you can’t stand the heat, stay out of the kitchen.” Tonight, the Kraken Cold Ones learned that lesson the hard way at the arena. In a matchup that pitted the gritty underdog Kraken against the mid-table tenacity of Hot Garbage, the scoreboard eventually read 4-2 in favor of the visitors, leaving the Cold Ones searching for answers in a season that has been colder than the name on their sweaters.

### Period 1: Garbage Takes the Driver’s Seat
The ice was barely groomed before Alan Dyke put the visitors on the board, finding the back of the net at the 13:29 mark to stun the home crowd. It was a cold start for the Cold Ones, and things only got frostier. 

Hot Garbage captain Aaron Whisner—who came into tonight riding a blistering five-game point streak—extended his personal heater at 3:21 with an assist from Jordan Hawkins Baker. Before the Cold Ones could even gather their breath, Dyke returned to the scene of the crime at 5:50, burying his second of the night off a crisp feed from Whisner. By the time the buzzer sounded, the Kraken were down 3-0 and looking like they were skating in quicksand.

### Period 2: The Battle for Breathing Room
The second period saw the Kraken tighten up their defensive structure, effectively turning the game into a trench war. The turning point nearly occurred at 7:29 when Randy Hatfield found himself in the box for a cross-checking minor. While the Kraken penalty kill unit held the fort, the lack of offense was becoming the story of the night. Every time the Kraken tried to cycle the puck, Hot Garbage’s defense was there, providing more "traffic in front of the net" than a Friday rush hour on the interstate.

### Period 3: The Kraken’s Desperate Surge
Down three, the Kraken finally uncorked some offense. Randy Hatfield redeemed his earlier trip to the sin bin, finding the twine at 13:29 courtesy of a beautiful dish from Jeremy Wentworth. 

Suddenly, the momentum shifted. The Kraken looked like a team possessed, buzzing the net with renewed intensity. With just under a minute left in the game, Steven Chern—continuing his absolute tear through the league with his third-consecutive game scoring—sniped one past the goaltender to make it a 3-2 nail-biter. 

The Kraken had their goalie pulled, eyeing an improbable equalizer, but it wasn't meant to be. With just over eight minutes showing on the clock, Brent Brown capitalized on a defensive breakdown, cleaning up the garbage (fittingly enough) off a Nicholas Brewer feed to ice the game at 4-2.

### The Aftermath
For Hot Garbage, this win is a crucial piece of the puzzle, keeping them anchored in the middle of the pack as they look to climb the ladder toward the top-tier squads. They played a disciplined, opportunistic game that proved why they sit comfortably at #7.

For the Kraken Cold Ones, the loss is a tough pill to swallow. Despite the late-game heroics from Hatfield and Chern, the team remains stuck in the bottom tier at #11. They’ve got the talent to score, but they need to stop spotting their opponents a three-goal head start if they hope to climb out of the basement.

The Cold Ones have the tools, but tonight, they found out that you can’t beat the clock, and you certainly can’t beat the Hot Garbage when you start the game on ice skates while they’re on rocket boosters. The season is still young, but the clock is ticking for the Kraken to turn this ship around.
