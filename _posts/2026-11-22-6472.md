# Laser Zap the Yetis in High-Scoring Thriller

If you showed up to the rink expecting a sleepy Saturday morning skate, you clearly didn't get the memo from the Laser. In an absolute barn-burner that kept the scoreboard operator busier than a one-armed goalie, the Laser surged to a 7-5 victory over the Yetis, proving that even a team at the bottom of the standings has enough electricity to spark a massive upset.

The tone was set early and never really cooled down. The Laser’s David Mansfield opened the scoring just minutes into the first, but the Yetis kept pace with a relentless Kirk Nielsen strike and an unassisted hustle goal from Alexander Hajduczok. Jeremy Kuzmarov proved to be the Laser’s secret weapon early on, netting a pair of goals in the opening frame to keep the Yetis back on their heels.

"We just decided to stop playing like it was a Sunday brunch and started playing like we actually wanted those two points," said a grinning Laser bench boss after the final buzzer. The decision seemed to pay dividends heading into the second period, as Chad Pancoast buried a beautiful feed from Kuzmarov just before the buzzer to give the Laser a cushion heading into the final frame.

The third period was pure, unadulterated hockey chaos. The Yetis, looking to capitalize on a hooking penalty to Ronald Herwig, saw Kirk Nielsen punch in his second of the night to pull closer. But every time the Yetis sniffed a comeback, the Laser responded with a haymaker. David Mansfield was the man of the hour, completing a hat-trick performance with two clinical finishes in the third that effectively took the wind out of the Yeti sails. 

The Yetis didn't go down without a fight, though. Derek Campbell and Alexander Hajduczok—who put on a masterclass with a multi-goal performance—cluttered up the crease and found the back of the net in the final minutes to make things interesting. Unfortunately for the Yetis, the Laser's defensive adjustments and a timely goal from Christopher King proved to be the difference-maker in a game that felt like it had enough offense for two separate contests.

The win is a massive morale boost for the Laser, who have struggled to find their rhythm this season. For the Yetis, it’s a tough pill to swallow; despite a high-octane offensive effort and strong vision from the likes of Hajduczok and Nielsen, they couldn't plug the holes in their defensive zone long enough to contain the Laser’s top lines.

As the ice clears and the teams head to the locker rooms, one thing is certain: the Laser showed us today that in this league, the standings are just numbers, but a well-timed goal celebration is forever. The Yetis will look to hit the reset button, while the Laser heads home knowing they finally found the winning frequency.
