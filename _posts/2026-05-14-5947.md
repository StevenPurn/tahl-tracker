# Danglers Leave the Berzerkers Out in the Cold in 4-0 Shutout

**By: Your Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: May 14, 2026**

If you were looking for a high-octane display of puck possession, you found it at the barn tonight. The Dirty Danglers skated into town looking to build on their early-season momentum, and let’s just say they didn’t leave the Berzerkers any crumbs. In a contest that felt like a masterclass in puck control, the Danglers blanked the home side 4-0, leaving the Berzerkers still searching for their first point of the campaign.

### First Period: Calhoun Sets the Tone
The puck dropped at 9:30 PM, and it didn't take long for the Danglers to find their rhythm. Seven minutes and nine seconds into the opening frame, **Dylan Calhoun** proved why he’s a nightmare for opposing blue-liners. Off a crisp feed from **Joshua Hopwood**, Calhoun buried a beauty that set the tone for the night. The Berzerkers looked a bit shell-shocked, struggling to find any sustained pressure as the Danglers clogged the neutral zone like a holiday traffic jam.

### Second Period: Penalties and Persistence
The middle frame turned into a bit of a gritty affair. The Danglers’ **Jonathan McCormick** found himself sitting for two minutes for roughing, giving the Berzerkers a golden ticket to climb back into the game. But the Berzerkers couldn't capitalize on the man advantage, and once the teams returned to full strength, the Danglers struck again. 

At the 4:25 mark, **Kalea Maxwell** put on a clinic, finding the back of the net unassisted. It was a heads-up play that took the wind right out of the Berzerkers' sails. The home team tried to generate some traffic in front of the net, but the Danglers’ defense was as stubborn as a goalie on a hot streak.

### Third Period: The Dangler Deep Freeze
The final period was all about icing the cake. Just as the clock ticked down to the absolute final buzzer, **Charles Webber** capitalized on a defensive lapse to make it 3-0. And if that wasn't enough, Kalea Maxwell struck again at the 1:14 mark, corralling a pass from Calhoun and McCormick to tuck one away and complete the multi-point night. 

Arthur Baisley did spend two minutes in the box for tripping midway through the third, but the Danglers’ penalty kill was stellar, refusing to give the Berzerkers even a sniff of a comeback.

### The Takeaway
With this victory, the Dirty Danglers move to a solid 4 points on the season, asserting themselves as an early-season force to be reckoned with. They played with a level of chemistry that made the ice look tilted in their favor all night long.

As for the Berzerkers? They’re still stuck at zero, and they’ll need to head back to the drawing board before their next tilt. They have the grit, but tonight, the Danglers had the polish. It’s early in the season, folks, but in this league, you can’t afford to let points slip away on home ice for too long.

**Final Score:**
*   **Dirty Danglers: 4**
*   **Berzerkers: 0**
