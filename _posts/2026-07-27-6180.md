# Riveting Performance: Tulsa Pummels Mother Puckers in 9-4 Offensive Masterclass

**TULSA** – If you were looking for a quiet Friday night, you picked the wrong barn. The Tulsa Riveters walked into the rink on July 26th looking like a team on a mission, and they left the Mother Puckers feeling like they’d just been hit by a freight train. In a high-octane 9-4 victory, the Riveters proved exactly why they are the team to watch in the standings.

### The First: A Shaky Start, A Swift Response
The game didn’t start exactly how the Riveters drew it up on the whiteboard. Mother Puckers’ Makenzie Dillard drew first blood at 10:37 of the first period, silencing the crowd briefly. But if you’ve watched this Riveters squad, you know they don’t stay down for long. 

Gabrielle Gibson—who extended her goal-scoring streak to a staggering six games—answered back just under two minutes later. That ignited the fuse. Ivy Emerson then decided to take the game by the scruff of the neck, tallying two goals before the intermission, including a beautiful feed from Hannah Atkins. By the time the horn sounded for the break, Tulsa had flipped the script to a 3-1 lead.

### The Second: Emerson’s Hat Trick and the Gibson Show
The second period was a clinic in puck possession. Ivy Emerson completed her hat trick early in the frame, capitalizing on a crisp helper from Barbara Lepe Briones. 

Not to be outdone, the dynamic duo of Gabrielle Gibson and Kim Wilmes went to work. At 4:07, Gibson hammered home her second of the night, putting the game firmly out of reach. While Kalea Maxwell managed to claw one back for the Mother Puckers late in the period, the Riveters’ forecheck was suffocating, forcing turnovers and keeping the Mother Puckers' netminder under a constant barrage of rubber.

### The Third: A Goal-Scoring Fever Dream
If the first two periods were a warm-up, the third was a full-blown hockey highlight reel. Holly Hutto opened the scoring immediately, and although Dillard snatched a second goal for the Puckers, it was all Tulsa from there.

Ivy Emerson added her *fourth* goal of the night—a performance that will be talked about at the water cooler for weeks. Remisha Blation joined the party shortly after, and Gabrielle Gibson capped off her own hat trick with a vintage finish off a Wilmes feed. Dillard managed to complete her own hat trick late, but at 9-4, the "Mother Puckers" were simply looking for the final buzzer to mercy their defense.

### The Takeaway
This win pushes the Tulsa Riveters to 6-0-2, keeping them locked in that high-pressure second-place spot, breathing down the necks of the league-leading Valkyries. With an offense that is clicking at every level—from the persistence of Gibson to the surgical precision of Emerson—Tulsa is looking like the scariest team on the ice right now.

For the Mother Puckers, tonight was a tough pill to swallow. Sitting at 2-5-1, they’ll need to tighten up the traffic in front of their own net if they want to climb out of the basement. But tonight? Tonight belonged to the Riveters, who showed us that when they get going, the cookies aren't just on the top shelf—they're being taken home.

**Three Stars of the Game:**
1. **Ivy Emerson (Riveters):** Four goals. Enough said.
2. **Gabrielle Gibson (Riveters):** A hat trick to keep the streak alive.
3. **Makenzie Dillard (Mother Puckers):** A valiant effort, fighting for a hat trick in a losing cause.
