# BERZERKERS BATTLE BACK: A Hard-Fought Victory Over Fossil Hockey

**By: The Rinkside Reporter**
**Date: July 9, 2026**

If you weren't at the barn tonight, you missed a classic. The 8:45 PM puck drop between the Berzerkers and Fossil Hockey was exactly what we crave in this league: high-octane action, a bit of chippy physicality, and a scoreboard that kept the fans on the edge of their seats until the final buzzer. When the dust settled, the Berzerkers walked away with a 6-4 victory, proving they have the grit to grind out a win when the pressure is on.

### First Period: The Berzerkers Come Out Swinging
The Berzerkers clearly had their coffee before hitting the ice. Evert Wilmeth got the party started early, snapping one home just 1:18 into the contest. It wasn't long before Stephen Jeffery doubled the lead, burying a feed from Kevin Keller and Mik Halgren. 

Things got a bit heated midway through when Wilmeth and Fossil’s Michael Hilton decided to exchange pleasantries, resulting in matching minors. Once the ice cleared, the Berzerkers kept the pedal down, with the red-hot Zac Hughes extending his goal streak to four games at the 7:59 mark to put the Berzerkers up 3-0 heading into the intermission.

### Second Period: Fossil Hockey Mounts a Rally
Edward Stern kept the momentum rolling early in the second, dangling his way to a goal at the 14:10 mark. But you can never count Fossil Hockey out. As the Berzerkers started spending more time in the penalty box—courtesy of a couple of tripping calls and a hooking minor—the Fossil squad found their rhythm.

Jose Medina broke the seal for Fossil at 3:26, and Michael Hoey followed that up just minutes later, capitalizing on a power play shift. Suddenly, a comfortable lead was looking a bit fragile at 4-2. The Fossil defense tightened up, and the Berzerkers found themselves doing a lot of defensive heavy lifting as the period closed.

### Third Period: A Nail-Biter to the Finish
The final frame turned into an absolute barn burner. Robert Norris trimmed the lead to one early, and when Andrew Lamparter found the back of the net at the 14:26 mark, we had ourselves a brand new hockey game at 4-4. 

The air in the arena was thick with tension. Every dump-in was contested; every transition was a battle. With Fossil Hockey forced to play a man down after a delay of game penalty, the Berzerkers found their second wind. Stephen Jeffery stepped up with a massive unassisted goal with under two minutes to go, and Zac Hughes sealed the deal shortly after, lighting the lamp for his second of the night and fifth of the series.

### The Aftermath
It was a statement win for the Berzerkers (4-1-1), who continue to solidify their place in the middle of a crowded standings board. By handing Fossil Hockey (3-3-0) their third consecutive loss, the Berzerkers create some much-needed breathing room. 

The Fossil squad showed tremendous heart clawing back from that 4-0 deficit, but tonight belonged to the Berzerkers. They managed the traffic in front of the net, survived the parade to the penalty box, and delivered in the clutch. As we head into the next slate of games, the Berzerkers look like a team that’s finding its stride, while Fossil Hockey will be looking to head back to the drawing board to snap their skid.
