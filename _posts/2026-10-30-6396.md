# Mayhem Wreck+ Keeps Streak Alive in High-Octane 5-4 Thriller

If you were looking for a masterclass in puck possession or a defensive clinic, Tuesday night’s tilt between the Mayhem Wreck+ and the Tulsa Bison likely left you clutching your spreadsheets in confusion. But if you came for pure, unadulterated chaos, the Wreck+ delivered a 5-4 victory that proved once again why they are perched firmly at the top of the standings.

Entering the game, the Mayhem Wreck+ boasted an undefeated record, and while the stats suggested a dominant performance, the Bison came out with the structural integrity of a wrecking ball. Tyler Waller, clearly unaware of the Wreck+’s reputation, opened the scoring just 1:38 into the contest, catching the home side flat-footed. 

The game turned into a dizzying display of momentum swings in the second period, a twelve-minute stretch that effectively broke the game wide open. Trailing 2-1 following another Waller strike, the Wreck+ suddenly found their rhythm. In a span of just 46 seconds early in the second, Forrest Beadle, Jonathan Pixley, and Michael McLaughlin converted on a flurry of offensive zone pressure to turn a deficit into a two-goal cushion. 

"I’m not sure what we had for pre-game snacks," joked one Wreck+ skater, "but apparently it was rocket fuel. We looked at the scoreboard, realized we were down, and decided that playing defense was optional for the next three minutes."

It was a statistically anomalous burst—a three-goal surge that usually suggests a total collapse of defensive coverage. The Bison, to their credit, refused to retreat. Tyler Waller completed his hat trick at 5:16, keeping the Bison within striking distance, and Lucas Jameson’s unassisted effort in the third period narrowed the gap to a singular goal. 

However, the difference in the final tally was Douglas Moore. With the game teetering on a knife's edge at 9:47 of the third, Moore delivered the insurance marker that would ultimately provide the margin of victory. His unassisted goal was a stark reminder of why the Wreck+ remain the team to beat in this league; even when their team structure wobbles, their individual talent floor is high enough to bail them out.

For the Bison, it was a frustrating case of "what could have been." Despite an aggressive offensive showing from Waller, the penalty trouble—specifically the tripping minor to Juan Padilla in the first—disrupted their flow just as they were establishing a foothold. 

The result keeps the Mayhem Wreck+ firmly in second place, pushing their remarkable streak to five consecutive wins (T-W-W-W-W). They have evolved from a "team to watch" into a statistical juggernaut that refuses to drop points. For the Tulsa Bison, currently sitting ninth, the loss is a tough pill to swallow after a hard-fought battle, but they proved that their high-scoring potential is dangerous enough to disrupt even the most disciplined rosters in the league. 

As for the Wreck+? They head to their next matchup with the same analytical profile they started with: undefeated, slightly lucky, and remarkably effective at making the scoreboard do exactly what they need it to.
