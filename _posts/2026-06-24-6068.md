# Ice Breakers Keep the Streak Frozen: A 5-1 Masterclass at the Rink

**CITY ICE ARENA —** If you were looking for a late-night cure for your insomnia on the night of June 23rd, the 10:15 PM puck drop between the Ice Breakers and the Average Hoes probably wasn’t it—unless, of course, you’re an Ice Breakers fan, in which case the action was pure adrenaline. 

In a clash that saw the league’s hottest squad face off against a team still searching for their first spark, the Ice Breakers flexed their muscles, skating away with a dominant 5-1 victory. While the scoreboard might suggest a blowout, the atmosphere under the arena lights was nothing short of electric.

### A Defensive Standoff
For the better part of the first half, this game was a classic "mud-wrestling in the crease" affair. Both goaltenders were locked in, turning away shots like bouncers at a sold-out concert. It wasn't until the second period that the seal finally broke. 

At the 12:15 mark of the second, **Dylan Walker** of the Ice Breakers decided he was done waiting. He found a pocket of space and buried one, officially snapping the scoreless tie and shifting the momentum firmly into the Breakers' corner. The middle frame was a gritty, chippy affair, featuring a parade to the sin-bin. Jason Rosengard spent some quality time in the box for the Breakers, but the Average Hoes couldn't capitalize on the man-advantage, leaving the Ice Breakers’ penalty kill units looking like a brick wall.

### The Third Period Explosion
If the second period was the appetizer, the third was a full-course buffet of goals. 

The Average Hoes managed to stir the pot early, with **Ashley Ivanoff** finding the back of the net at 11:06, courtesy of some slick setup work from Suzanne Hawkins and Mckenna Allison. For a fleeting moment, it looked like the Hoes might stage a comeback. 

Then, the Ice Breakers slammed the door shut. 

It started with a goal from the surging **Daniel Reyes** at 13:45, followed immediately by **Joshua Pula**—who extended his point streak to a staggering five games—putting one home just 53 seconds later. With the floodgates open, **Connor Unruh** and **Haley Griffin** joined the party, padding the lead and putting the game squarely out of reach. A special shout-out to Daniel Reyes, who finished his night with a multi-point performance that left the Hoes' defense chasing shadows.

### The Aftermath
For the Ice Breakers (now 3-0-0), this win is a statement. They aren't just winning games; they’re steamrolling the competition and climbing the ladder to challenge the league’s titans at the top of the standings. The ice has melted, the Zamboni has done its rounds, and the Ice Breakers march on. If you’re a fan of high-octane hockey, keep your eyes on this team—they’re currently the coldest thing on the ice and the hottest thing in the standings.
