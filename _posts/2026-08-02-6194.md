# BISON BASH: Tulsa Steamrolls Mayhem in Statement Shutout

**TULSA** – If you were looking for a high-octane offensive clinic this morning, you didn't need to look any further than the ice at 11:45 AM. In a clash between the league’s third and fourth-seeded heavyweights, the Tulsa Bison didn't just walk into the barn—they burned it down. 

While the Mayhem Wreck+ arrived with a 7-1-1 record and a heavy-hitting reputation, they left with nothing but a bruised ego and a 4-0 shutout loss. The Bison are officially on a heater, pushing their winning streak to a cool six games and proving that this wasn't just a win; it was a hostile takeover of the standings.

### First Period: The Mooney Momentum
The game opened with the intensity of a playoff tilt, but it didn't take long for the Bison to find their groove. Just 4:39 into the opening frame, **Brett Mooney** decided he was done waiting around. He broke the deadlock with a solo effort that sent the Mayhem defense scrambling, tucking the puck into the net to draw first blood. It was a statement goal that forced the Wreck+ to play catch-up for the remainder of the afternoon—a position they were clearly uncomfortable in.

### Second Period: Mooney Makes It Rain
If the first period was a warning, the second was an absolute deluge. The Bison kept the pressure in the Mayhem zone, creating enough traffic in front of the net to make a downtown commute look like a walk in the park. 

At 4:47, **Juan Padilla** polished off a feed from Mooney to double the lead. The Wreck+ looked shell-shocked, struggling to find any flow as the Bison defense turned their blue line into a literal brick wall. Just when it looked like Mayhem might escape to the locker room down only by two, Mooney struck again with just 46.4 seconds left on the clock. Assisted by Brian Cuff and Padilla, Mooney netted his second of the day, effectively putting the game on ice before the Zamboni even had a chance to think about the intermission.

### Third Period: Rash Decisions and Rough Stuff
The third period served mostly as a victory lap for the Bison, though it wasn't without some fireworks. **Geoffery Rash** hammered the final nail in the coffin at the 14:44 mark, courtesy of a beautiful dish from Spencer Sheets, extending the lead to 4-0.

As the clock ticked down, frustration began to boil over for the Mayhem. Stephen Jeffery found himself sitting for two minutes after a hooking infraction at 8:13, but the game took a spicy turn just 16 seconds later. Tulsa’s LC III Sinor was handed a 10-minute misconduct for verbal abuse of an official. It was a bizarre moment in an otherwise clinical performance, proving that even in a four-goal romp, there’s no love lost between these two squads.

### The Takeaway
Let’s talk about that Bison goaltending—simply lights out. They weathered the storm, cleaned up the crease, and ensured that not a single puck found the back of their net. 

**What does this mean for the table?**
The tides are turning. With this victory, the Tulsa Bison (now 8-2-0) leapfrog the Mayhem Wreck+ (7-2-1) in the standings. The Bison have officially inserted themselves into the top-tier conversation, sitting pretty at 16 points and riding a wave of momentum that has to have the rest of the league looking over their shoulders. 

For the Mayhem, it’s back to the drawing board. They’ve been rock solid all season, but today, they were simply out-skated and out-worked. The good news? It’s a long season, and if these two meet in the postseason, you can bet the Wreck+ will be looking for a serious bit of revenge.

Until next time, keep your sticks on the ice and your eyes on the rafters!
