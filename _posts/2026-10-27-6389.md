# Laser Finally Find the Light with 7-4 Win Over the Lodge

If you’ve been waiting for the Laser to finally figure out which way the puck is supposed to go, Monday night at the rink provided the answer. In a matchup between two teams desperate for a spark, the Laser finally broke their winless drought, skating away with a 7-4 victory over the Lodge.

Coming into the contest with an 0-4-1 record, the Laser played like a squad tired of being the league’s doormat. James Whitacre set the tone early, opening the scoring just under a minute into the first period. It was the kind of start that usually signals a long night for the opposition, but the Lodge showed some grit, hanging around long enough to keep the Laser honest.

The first period was a frantic, end-to-end affair that would give any traditionalist a heart attack. After LOUIS LUONGO knotted it up for the Lodge off a feed from Dylan Calhoun, the Laser responded with a flurry. Timothy N See, Bryce Perkins, and NATHAN HOGUE all found the back of the net before the intermission, effectively turning what was a tight game into a significant mountain for the Lodge to climb.

"We finally stopped playing like we were wearing skates for the first time," said one anonymous Laser team source near the snack bar. "I think we just realized that the goal horn actually sounds better when it's for us."

The Laser momentum carried into the second, where Whitacre notched his second of the night, followed by a tally from Chad Pancoast. By the time the third period rolled around, the Laser were sitting on a comfortable lead, though the Lodge refused to pack it in. Jered Pieschke and LUONGO—who secured his second goal of the night—managed to chip away at the deficit, briefly making the Laser bench look a little nervous.

However, any hope of a Lodge comeback was extinguished at the 9:13 mark of the final frame when Whitacre buried his third goal of the game, completing the hat trick and putting the game firmly out of reach. 

The game featured the usual suspects for the Lodge, who saw their respective point streaks continue: Jered Pieschke extended his run to three games, while JONATHON ANDERSON kept his own three-game streak alive with an assist on LUONGO’s second goal. On the other side, RONALD HERWIG continued his own productive stretch, contributing two assists despite spending two minutes in the box for a tripping minor.

The final buzzer marked a sigh of relief for the Laser, who move to 1-4-1 on the season. For the Lodge, the loss drops them to 1-4-1, leaving them looking for answers as they head into the next slate of games. It wasn’t always pretty—the third period featured a parade to the box for slashing penalties—but for the Laser, it was a necessary reminder that even the teams at the bottom of the standings can find the back of the net if they keep moving their feet.
