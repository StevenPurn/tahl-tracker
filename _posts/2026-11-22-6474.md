# The Outsiders Stave Off Bison Rally in Disciplined Thriller

If you were looking for a masterclass in how to win while spending more time in the penalty box than a repeat offender at a traffic court, look no further than The Outsiders' 4-3 victory over the Tulsa Bison this Saturday afternoon.

Entering the matchup with a 7-1-1 record, The Outsiders solidified their hold on the top spot in the standings, but they did it the hard way. The narrative of this game wasn't just the scoring; it was the peculiar, almost obsessive relationship between The Outsiders’ Enoch Cox and the referee’s whistle. Cox managed to flirt with the rulebook all afternoon, culminating in a third-period sequence that defies typical box-score logic—including a zero-minute "Excessive Penalties" notation that surely sent the league’s scorekeepers scrambling to find the rulebook.

The Outsiders struck first in the opening frame. Michael Drach broke the deadlock at the 4:19 mark off a feed from Logan Pardue, followed shortly by a solo effort from Tyler Waller at 7:12. At that point, it looked like the league leaders were set to cruise. 

However, the Bison began to tilt the ice in the second period. James DePriest finally solved The Outsiders' defense at 2:43, snapping a lengthy scoreless drought for Tulsa. Just as the Bison smelled blood, The Outsiders reminded everyone why they sit at the top of the standings. Logan Pardue notched his first of the afternoon just sixty seconds later, reclaiming the two-goal cushion. 

The middle frame also saw a valiant effort from Geoffery Rash, who continued his two-game point streak with an unassisted goal at 9:32, narrowing the gap to 3-2. 

The third period turned into a chaotic affair of special teams, minor infractions, and pure desperation. As the clock ticked down, the Bison found a surge of energy. Brett Campbell managed to find the back of the net with a laser at the 17.3-second mark of the period, assisted by DePriest, bringing the game to a fever pitch. 

But consistency is the hallmark of a first-place team, and The Outsiders proved they are nothing if not opportunistic. Despite being besieged by penalties—including multiple trips, unsportsmanlike conduct, and interference calls against Cox—The Outsiders managed to execute when it mattered most. Logan Pardue, showcasing the kind of finishing touch that keeps his squad atop the standings, buried his second goal of the game at the 6:50 mark off a setup from Bryan Simms. That strike provided just enough breathing room to withstand the final Bison push.

For The Outsiders, the win is another notch in a dominant season, further distancing them from the pack and proving that even a game marred by trips to the sin bin can be managed with high-end offensive efficiency. The Bison, meanwhile, proved they are a gritty, resilient group that can hang with the best in the league, even if the math didn't quite fall in their favor this afternoon. 

As for the league's statisticians? They’ll likely be studying the game sheet for a while, trying to determine exactly how a team can collect enough penalties to trigger an "Excessive Penalties" entry and still walk away with the two points. That, folks, is the beauty of this sport: sometimes, the spreadsheet just isn't prepared for what happens on the ice.
