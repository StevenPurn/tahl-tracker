# One Man Wolfpack Snaps Skid with Dominant Win Over Honey Badgers

In a battle between two teams looking to crawl out of the bottom of the standings, the One Man Wolfpack finally found their bite on Monday night, dismantling the Honey Badgers 5-1 at the rink. 

For the Wolfpack, who entered the night riding a frustrating five-game winless streak, the victory was less about fancy highlight-reel plays and more about the kind of gritty, lunch-pail hockey that keeps a coach from having a coronary. 

The tone was set early when Alexander Hajduczok opened the scoring at the 10:59 mark of the first period. It was a classic "no-nonsense" goal—no assists, no apologies, just the puck hitting the back of the net. While Todd Thompson spent a brief vacation in the penalty box for roughing late in the frame, the Wolfpack’s defensive structure held firm, and James Whitacre extended the lead to 2-0 with under two minutes left in the opening period, courtesy of a tidy setup from Brandt Mueller and Kyle Foster.

The second period was where the Wolfpack truly pulled away, effectively turning the game into a controlled exercise. Jared Zellers found the twine midway through the period, followed quickly by Hajduczok’s second of the night at the 9:52 mark. By the time the buzzer sounded for the second intermission, the Wolfpack held a commanding 4-0 lead, leaving the Honey Badgers looking like a team wondering if they’d left their skates in the parking lot.

Hajduczok capped off his stellar evening by completing the hat trick early in the third, snapping home his third goal of the night off a crisp feed from Benjamin Gartner. It was a clinical performance from a player who clearly decided he was tired of losing.

To their credit, the Honey Badgers didn’t fold completely. With the game well out of reach, they managed to break the shutout at the 8:19 mark of the third period when Justin Houde beat the Wolfpack netminder, assisted by Michael Mosca. It was a small moral victory for the Badgers, but it was far too little, too late. 

"We stopped overthinking it and just started putting pucks on net," said one Wolfpack player after the game, while dodging a rogue water bottle in the locker room. "Usually, we make things complicated. Tonight, we kept it simple, and it turns out, that actually works."

The win marks the first victory of the season for the One Man Wolfpack, moving them up to two points on the year. For the Honey Badgers, the loss is another tough pill to swallow as they continue to search for their first "W" of the campaign. Both teams have a long road ahead if they want to climb out of the basement, but tonight, at least, the Wolfpack looked like a team that remembered how to play the game.
